The big idea

Predictions come when the collective has built enough resonance, and some details are withheld so as not to tip off the opposition. The teachings in this talk are practical: learn real history so you aren't a pawn of one narrative; avoid what lowers your frequency (trauma, alcohol, drugs) because it opens you to negative entities; let children explore, since their natural state is genius. New technologies exist, but systems need a step-by-step transition, not a collapse. Prepare sensibly, look at the facts, and help others.

#48.1Why information comes when it does

Predictions often arrive unasked, when the collective has mobilized enough resonance. The Council knows the end result, but the path depends on everyone's choices. Details are withheld "because of protection": certain things are revealed at a certain moment so the opposition isn't tipped off.

#48.2Know your history

When history is erased from schools, people can't connect the dots and simply follow the mainstream hologram, pointing fingers like chess pawns. She urges learning how many peoples were enslaved and manipulated throughout history, not one group's story alone, and seeing a single globalist system manipulating all sides. "I'm not pro this or pro that. I'm pro humanity."

#48.3What lowers your frequency

When someone is abused to the point of passing out, the light of consciousness leaves the body and negative entities can grab it; that is how mind control works. In the same way, alcohol and drugs lower your frequency to a different resonance and allow negative entities to enter. Everything is etheric first; they manipulate the field to maintain their existence.

#48.4Children are born geniuses

She cites a study finding that most young children tested at genius level in creative thinking and the share fell sharply as they went through school. Allowed to explore, a child expands its consciousness and creates new realities, because consciousness is always moving, spiralling to new ideas. Protect that.

#48.5Transition, not collapse

Free energy and advanced technology exist (she cites Tesla's work), but you can't shut everything down overnight: think of all the people working in factories and current systems. First free ourselves of the international cartel, then transition systematically, with the right people, through oil, gas and electricity if needed, to avoid a global collapse including banking.
