#14.3Her water-fasting protocol

"Do not be afraid": fast on water on the seventh day of the week. Build up over three months:

  1. Month 1: water only on the seventh day, Sunday, every week.
  2. Month 2: two days a week, Saturday and Sunday.
  3. Month 3: three days a week, Friday, Saturday and Sunday.
  4. Only pure water on fasting days, distilled: nothing the gut flora must process; on the other six days you may eat and juice.
  5. Have a doctor's supervision and blood work if you can: "a lot of people's body is different". If you're diabetic, you need supervision "because it fluctuates"; she points to the Tree of Life clinic in Arizona, where a doctor treats diabetes through diet and juicing.
  6. Adjust: stress, poor sleep or a cold can drop your frequency; then you may need to go back, for example to eat fish for a week.
Added safety note

Repeated multi-day water fasts can be dangerous, especially with diabetes, low weight, pregnancy, heart or kidney disease, or an eating disorder. Please do this only with your doctor's approval and monitoring.

No alcohol, no chemical substances: chemicals are so potent in frequency that they override your molecular resonance, so the body can't regenerate naturally; your DNA, RNA and consciousness are a code designed to shift you into the new timeline, and modifying it means you can't use the frequency coming from consciousness. Natural substances are different. Then: follow the detox protocol the hybrids taught her and the elimination diet, eliminating a food every two weeks, slowly; "do not drink more than one cup" of juice; use Visbiome probiotics, if you wish, to restore the gut flora (bloating means inflammation); eat raw food and juice, but not with gastrointestinal problems: repair the flora first with probiotics. She had "botox-like", elastic skin by day 11 of her diet (see S3 38 and the Detox and Juicing Guide).

Look into UV light treatments for the blood ("it purifies the blood... does miracles"). Oils: research which oil for skin regeneration has the highest frequency; test one drop at a time for allergy, and test each oil before mixing. She also suggested fasting at Easter and researching what was done at Easter in Babylonian times: "to transform the darkness, you have to shine it in a light". "I'm not a doctor... I'm just telling you what I would do."

Added safety note

UV blood irradiation is not an approved or proven treatment, and carries risks; ask your doctor. Natural doesn't always mean safe: check supplements and herbs with a doctor or pharmacist, especially if you take medication.

Can the Council explain sleep paralysis?

The other is a pamphlet of reality laid over your body hologram, or an entity trying to enter without permission: the shock is a shock for it too. As your vibration rises you gain an underlayer of protection; anything trying to penetrate shows itself in front of you, and you recognize at once who you're dealing with.
Are manifestations happening faster now?
Yes: the frequency from the centre of consciousness is increasing, and the 7% increase is fundamental (14.1).
I sometimes hear my heartbeat like an ultrasound. Is that the shift?
Partly, combined with your situations and the galactic increase. No need to worry: keep a proper diet, proper sleep and proper sun, to use better what's coming in.
If one is used to fasting, can one just fast Friday, Saturday and Sunday?
Yes, with supervision. Unless you have direct guidance, check your body and blood work to see where you are (14.3); the hybrids guided her through the fluctuations ("sometimes more spinach in a juice, or less").
Is it only water fasting, or can it include juicing?
The seventh day is pure water only; juice on the other days (14.3).
Must the water always be distilled?
"The most important, from our point of view." Distilled, non-conductive water recognizes and flushes out anything off balance, and conducts purely what consciousness gives, without the substances added to the water supply (see S4 3, distilled water).
During a water fast, may we take homeopathic remedies? Vitamin supplements?
Homeopathic and herbal remedies in a natural state, yes: plants' code is in synchrony with the body. Supplements: natural substances, yes.
How is salt connected to water and the sun?
Salt crystallizes and splits frequency with the water molecule ("1.13373" in the holographic imprint), conducting nutrients to their positions so the body resonates fully. Processed or modified salt gives a different code, depletes the cells of water and disturbs the sun's signal. Use salt that is crystal clear, pure, "no more than a little spoon". Celery or Swiss chard juice is full of natural salt: "you'll spit it out."
What about natural plants like cannabis?
Plants are being genetically mixed; find the original structures. For inflammation or cancer it must be designed specifically for you: "do not take one pill for every sickness". Take it drop by drop, slowly but surely; once healed, stop. Taken a lot, you become "druggy": "do not become a puppet for a substance... you are good already."
Is that why salty sea water heals the body?
Yes: it regenerates cells; a cut heals quickly in it (a hint for a regenerating ointment). Breathing the salty beach air heals the lungs, especially at 5 a.m. at sunrise; walk barefoot in the dew and inhale: "the highest potency resonance".
Added safety note

Sea water can infect open wounds; clean cuts with clean water. Cannabis can affect the mind, interacts with medicines and may be illegal where you live; talk to a doctor first.

In homeopathic books on oils and their combinations; find the oils of skin rejuvenation with the highest frequency. One drop first, to check you're not allergic.
