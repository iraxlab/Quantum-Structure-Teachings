At students' request, how she applied the quantum structure from "ground zero": what each of her real situations teaches about money, emergencies, anger, guidance, giving and even the weather; the Council's cancer analogy; and questions on the order of the protocols, healing, and waking up family (live talk, September 2021)

The big idea

Manifestation is not a theory you watch; it's what you do in the moment a situation hits. Every example comes back to the same moves: recognize it's a hologram, neutralize your emotion, know you already have the result, and act until you find it, without fear and without expectation of how. "Nobody is saving you, but you're saving yourself." The stories are hers; the lessons are for you.

#Key teachings

The message: think of the negative as a cancer

"If you ignore and you don't want to get rid of it, nothing may happen and you will be overtaken." First find it and determine what type it is; "crystallize the fact in your consciousness that there is a cancer", without denying it. Then find a way to get rid of it: trust, put motion into it, "and motion with something stronger": action and the quantum manifestation. "You cannot have a healthy body if you do not know there is a cancer in it." "How many of you will deny that they have cancer because of ignorance?"

They never told you where the reflection comes from: that you project it. "I'm not your power, I'm a projection within your structure... If I did it, so did you, if I'm another version of you." (Practical: prepare for three months as advised, because prices will keep rising; the move to crypto will come but is delayed, and the real monetary system that will help humanity is still secret.)

#36.1Money, from ground zero

  1. Embed "I have it" now. When there was nothing to pay the mortgage, she stayed home learning and held the feeling that she had the money; within three or four days an unexpected cheque arrived for more than the mortgage.
  2. Change the collective frequency. With $14 in the bank on her son's birthday, she smiled, told everyone to dress up, and took the family to the mall as if all was well; a stranger offered paid surveys, enough for an organic birthday meal. "People go to the mall to spend money, I went to get money."
  3. Stop the self-pity. Crying in the car with $17, she heard: "Stop. It's an illusion... It doesn't matter if you need a $1 or a $1 million. What matters is what you need right now"; the million will come through the structure when it's needed.
  4. Drop the expectation, and it can be instant. Needing $10 for a school trip, she felt good, saw $10 in her mind, and found a crisp $10 in a wooden box beside her. It happened when it was for her children, "because I have no expectations": expectation blocks the signal; without it, the frequency is so precise it needs no time.

#36.2In an emergency

  1. Name it a game. When no taxi would take a drugged young woman, she told herself, "this is a hologram... a virtual reality game", and she was its creator.
  2. Play hide-and-seek. She knew the solution existed and looked for it by acting (see S1 1.7).
  3. Treat each refusal as a door that doesn't resonate. Three drivers and two officers refused; she stayed neutral, "I wasn't mad", tried a different approach, then moved on.
  4. Ask the structure. "I need help in a hologram": within a minute, someone found a bicycle cab.
  5. Keep raising the feeling on the way, even joking, so the projection stays positive; don't think about the money. The guides later told her it had been a test, and she passed: higher beings watch your energetic field and how you use the knowledge for others.

#36.3When you are angry

Furious, ready to fight her husband, she heard: "Marina, be careful... remember the fluctuating realities." Acting from that anger would collapse the fluctuation negative. So she removed herself ("I can't talk right now"), sat by the pool, and changed the frequency physically, here by watching one seven-minute video on realities over and over for 45 minutes, until she reached calm, the zero point. Then she chose the positive, went back, smiled and apologized, and left the goal open. Two months later the way appeared: an online meeting with the scientist she needed to reach, paid with a friend's loan. "Never take an action from a state of huge anger." Other ways to release it: positive music, nature, running, screaming, a punching bag.

#36.4Trusting guidance and timing

  1. Leave an unhealthy place, and trust the process: she quit a school job where she was caught in others' fights.
  2. Accept "not yet". Told she would move "in four years", every attempt before then hit a dead end; they don't tell you how, so you don't take the wrong turn early. When it was time, she sold everything without fear.
  3. Convert each hit, one after another. On moving day the car broke down. She closed her eyes, breathed, used the Galactic Meditation with the car instead of the galactic family, and felt that all was perfect, with no reaction to the panic around her. A grateful caller sent a $500 donation; a repair shop with a free slot was found within 40 minutes; the bill came to less than the donation. "I know he's there": the end result, not "maybe".
  4. Follow the guidance even when it delays you. Told not to involve a lawyer in a family legal matter ("a family lineage karma"), she watched; the delay kept her from buying when prices jumped, and the new home ended up fully paid off, "like a jigsaw puzzle".

#36.5Giving without condition

When six people in a row received a session and gave nothing, she was hurt, then calmed down; the same day a stranger booked a session and sent $600. "When you project the frequency of giving... without a condition", it comes back, if not from these people, from another structure. "That one person is your entire universe": give each the best of yourself, and love people enough to allow their actions. It isn't easy, "because you still have to pay the bills." (She also warned that she never asks for payment to watch her videos: any such page is not her.)

#36.6Even the weather

At a pool party, with a storm turning afternoon into night, she gathered the group, because more people make it more potent. She stayed neutral ("thank you, storm, for clearing the sky"), since the storm is a living part of the structure and can't be rejected, only mutated; then, with the Galactic Meditation, everyone felt the sun on their skin, and held it even when heavy drops began. Neighbours had to pull their cars over in the downpour; only their two houses stayed dry, "a vacuum within the structure itself". At a rainy funeral, holding "it's beautiful" inside, the rain cleared within 15 minutes. "Don't have complete expectations how that would be."

#Questions answered

Must we repeat your guided meditation each time, or can we do the Galactic Meditation on our own?
On your own. The order: Season 1, episodes 1–3 (fluctuating realities); the Galactic Meditation, then put anything in place of the galactic family; the Protocol to Manifest Potency (S4 9), which explains why it works; then the nine-day protocol (S3 8), to train for when you're hit again and again. Give the end result, "not maybe", and feel it: "you don't wait for something to happen, you know it already is." If you drop, change the emotion again, even if it takes 45 minutes.
Is there guidance on tapping into healing powers?
Only if permission is given through your contracts, which even she isn't told. The hybrids guided her into her own DNA to change it so she could live on water (see S4 18.2). Water is a conductor: frequency from a spacecraft moves through it into the whole DNA and RNA and transforms the body.
Her instructions: weekly water fasting

If you're not diabetic (diabetics only with a doctor's supervision): month 1, water only every Sunday; month 2, Saturday and Sunday; month 3, Friday to Sunday (see S4 14.3). Watch the detox video on what happens in the first 48 hours. To go further, first a year of the detox protocol: "a lot of commitment and a lot of discipline."

Added safety note

Fasting can be dangerous with diabetes, medication, pregnancy, low weight or a history of eating disorders. Talk to your doctor first.

Should we keep trying to wake up family members who aren't open, like my daughter?
What you project into your collective becomes a structure with a life of its own, free to reject. If you speak from the heart, you have planted the seed. Later, something small (two seconds on TV, a remark at school) resonates with it and they recognize it, because everyone is being activated. (Her son woke up when an official told his school, "You are meant to be workers.") Nourish children with real knowledge: Tesla, Walter Russell, Jacque Fresco, Spirit Science for children, the scientists in her Freedom Awakening playlist. Not every school or teacher is bad; but know your rights, and act without fear. "Never give up."
