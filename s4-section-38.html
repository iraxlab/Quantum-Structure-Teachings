The big idea

Souls who exit the hologram, even those who never saw the manipulation, did so by agreement: they become etheric legions, holding the positive vibration around the planet like a shield and clearing their karma. So never call them stupid; thank them. And use the geometric meditation: hold the picture of a free Earth, increase the heart's feeling seven times, and open a timeline stargate at 47 degrees.

#38.1Three timelines, and why the legions are needed

One timeline is the negative side's: an artificial, technological reality for those who consent, which can't hold the heart's vibration and will eventually be unable to reproduce itself (see S4 30). A second is yours: as you wake up and act from the heart, you shift into a new reality where you find food, another job, an exemption, whatever you need, while consciousness itself moves up. Some on the negative side, seeing their timeline fail, activate their own souls to come across and help. The third is the souls who exit: they rise into the etheric, another parallel timeline, and project frequency into the planet so its pulsation becomes potent enough to stay in the 5D, balancing the ether and the matter. "Not one dot is left behind" (see HR): ETs, consciousness, those here and those who left all build the magnetic and gravitational field together.

She added that what is being injected is, in her view, a mutation of DNA and RNA, and that no single fix can suit everyone because every DNA is different. She is not a doctor and can't predict what happens to anyone who consents.

#38.2The quantum physics of the meditation

What you picture becomes a hologram in the ether in front of you. To make ether and matter one magnetic and gravitational unit, you activate the heart: its feeling is a second frequency, a laser that makes the hologram pulse. Each increase adds a layer, "almost like we're trying to pop a balloon into matter structure"; the Council asked for seven. You look into the centre of the drawing so the coordinates penetrate your consciousness. "Even if you see this video a year later, you can do the same thing."

#The method — The Geometric Meditation

  1. Gaze at the centre of the geometry, taking in the whole picture, and begin to feel joy: the portal must be created from the frequency of joy.
  2. Picture, line by line, as the prayer is read: God in heaven and earth, ether and matter; all souls free, those leaving and those staying, celebrating; the joy seen; your light rippling from east to west and from south to north across the planet.
  3. See the matrix form in the star: the 5D matrix, the new earth; enter at 47 degrees and see the stargate open, holding the souls, the people and the new earth.
  4. Increase: while holding the whole image, increase the feeling of joy, love and compassion from the heart and send it as a wave into the image, until you feel goose bumps and even tears.
  5. Repeat the increase seven times, each wave stronger than the last, staring at the centre; breathe out as if sending the signal.
  6. Seal it ("and so it is; already it's there"), then let it go.
  7. Hold the line afterwards: positive actions, no consent to manipulation, peaceful non-compliance from the heart, and your legal rights.

#Questions answered

Are the spinning star and pentagons the same as our merkaba?
There are different merkabas; different geometries and angles create different realities, at different latitudes and altitudes in time-space. This one is specific: spun at 47 degrees by the frequency, it creates a 360-degree matrix with its own frequency, opening a stargate and a timeline. The occult may use geometry for negative ends; "the intention and the wish with the emotion" decide what is created.
How do we forgive, according to the quantum structure?
Each time you choose and move, you shift to a brand-new earth, with another version of yourself and of everyone in it (see S1 2). "So why would you be upset?" If someone keeps returning with the same behaviour, it's because you never shut that program down, so it leaks into each new reality. "If I tell you this person did that 10,000 years ago... okay. But if I tell you this happened yesterday, you panic": there is no time in the quantum structure, only perception. What happened was by agreement, to make you stronger; now put positive frequency into it, and they no longer exist in your timeline. It takes the protocol and practice (Season 1).
