The big idea

The planet's structure, and you with it, is spinning faster and faster, so choices collapse quicker: you'll be presented with more situations, positive and negative, every day, and each one goes whichever way your resonance is in that moment. That's why action now has to be quick and conscious. Those who keep choosing the positive will see better and better synchronicities; those who keep choosing the negative will spin the other way. Full restructuring of the planet was given as a span of about 20 years, and how fast it goes depends on how much people act.

#Key teachings

#29.1Everything is speeding up

As the ascension proceeds, the molecular structure spins faster, and you, being the structure, spin faster through every parallel reality. So the collapse between two choices (1 + 1 = 3) comes quicker: "if you don't catch it on time, it's going to go based on what your resonance is at that particular moment." Expect to be "bombarded with challenges", positive or negative, in daily life as well as in the world. Stay in the eye of the storm and act quickly from the knowledge (see S3 8).

#29.2Seven layers in every signal

A signal of emotion is geometry and a sound wave, and within it are seven layers of sub-resonance joined in one (see the Harmonic Reactor on subtones). Its potency is rising, connected with the sun's movement: as the sun reaches certain degrees, the light spectrum and the sound frequency of the body change with the sound of consciousness. "We are literally being activated": the body, and with it the speed of your timelines (a sequence was given for this frequency). Everything around you shows what you resonate on and what you need to learn.

#29.3Two directions, and 20 years

Those acting positively are moving into the fifth-dimensional structure by their own choices. Those choosing negative behaviour, or blindly supporting it, spin faster the opposite way, collapse inward, separate from the 5D waveform and in time "self-recycle". Those blinded by manipulation are activated again and again through synchronicity (a friend, a video, something bizarre enough to make them say "wait a minute"); ignore it, and the situations get harder, until you may be stuck. Full restructuring for the positive (new systems, cities, free energy, healthy medicine) "will take a span of 20 years globally"; why not sooner? "More action you take, more you're collapsing the momentum."

#29.4There's no delay, only a collapse

"There is no delay. It's a collapse." Two waveforms running at once merge and mutate into one, spiralling inward and outward. The greater their potency, the closer they come and the faster they collapse; lower potency takes longer. "This potency is the reason why you call it time" (see S3 26.2). Listened to as a whole, consciousness "has a humming sound": the subtotality of all that is.

#29.5Your other fractals live other choices

Do other fractals of you still live the old timelines? "Yes, indeed." Versions of you can look completely different, be another gender, or even choose to be a villain and go back to source, while another chooses the positive and ascends. Even in a single moment, being angry and not being angry both exist; you see the one you acted on. Consciousness may choose an orientation or identity in one life to balance its experience: "there is no judgement; you chose that. Love to all." Some children already see, in dreams, the probability of a choice; but you still have to act now to crystallize it.

#29.6What counts as action

Anything that shifts your emotion from negative to positive, "such as watering the garden". Faced with something that angers you: gratitude that it's showing you what doesn't resonate, "I will not allow this", and move on to something positive. But if the same situation keeps coming and you're physically suffering, your higher self is usually pushing you to take physical action: to stop someone, or to move away from that place. "Read your holograms." Holding a frequency long enough is simply consistency: same situation, same positive choice, again and again (see S3 8, S3 18.6).

#29.7Don't follow idols

Where do masters such as Buddha or Jesus operate from? "Your existence": if you believe it exists, you'll find their teachings. "Do not attach yourself completely to certain idolized figures: they are a frequency resonance of knowledge." Learn from them and make it your own, but follow no idol, "because there's nobody outside of you". Being given idols to follow "was how you were manipulated".

#29.8Soul contracts can change

Can a soul contract be cancelled? "Yes", as a collective decision of your guides and councils, based on how well you're doing. Something may be replaced; or you may be given an imprint to continue what must be completed; or a test may come "exactly when you least expect it", to see the true core of your vibration. "The biggest warriors at this time will never be left alone or left behind."

#29.9Children are tuning forks

Children are "very perceptive, very much like a tuning fork": what they take in early becomes permanent, because they're laying down their first resonances. Many influences now are negative. "You are the filters. Teach them right": be aware of what they see, do and talk about (see S3 24.3).

#The method — Meeting a Faster Stream of Choices

  1. Expect more situations, faster: don't take them as signs that you're failing.
  2. Catch each one early: pause before reacting.
  3. Give gratitude for what it shows you; say "I will not allow this."
  4. Choose a positive action, however small: watering the garden counts (29.6).
  5. If it keeps repeating and you suffer physically, consider a physical change.
  6. Repeat with consistency; use the 9-day protocol (see S3 8).
