The big idea

The Council of Nine said the collective has reached a higher resonance, so what was hidden will be exposed, in politics and in spiritual circles alike, like a spotlight in a dark theater: you can only restructure what you see. Stay strong as steel and compassionate, because compassion runs through the heart's toroidal field and lets you see clearly. Physical action is the glue that bonds manifestation into 3D: a utopia where people only take, without acting, is an entrapment that loops into the negative, while communities that act and help each other bond into a fifth-dimensional timeline. Two parallel timelines are splitting; don't get caught in the noisy middle, and leave the self-pity field, which keeps reprinting the same reality.

#Key teachings

The message: the spotlight (condensed, as received)

"The human recovery of consciousness is more predominant when the field of the collective is really in a sound wave of coherent resonance." "You will find a lot of fractal expressions of different politicians... literally serving [a] different side of whatever they've been speaking." "You will be the spotlight... if you're in the theater everything is dark and all of a sudden is a spotlight and you're going to see something that you never saw in the darkness. And [it] may shock you... The only way you'll be able to restructure it is when you see it." The same will be shown among channels and spiritual teachers: whether they serve humanity "or [act] simply out of ego to be more popular".

#44.1Steel and compassion

"Your trajectory for ascension already had happened." Stay in your power, strong as steel and compassionate, because compassion is connected to the heart field: the heart's toroidal field converts everything and sends it back as a signal 360 degrees through your plasma field, so the subatomic particles mobilize with such potency "that there will be no mistake": you see exactly where the light points.

The message: money and the utopia trap (condensed)

The money structure is embedded in every structure of the collective, often through fear, so for now expansion includes it, until resonance is high enough for a utopian society. Meanwhile, many "sit and wait completely to be spoonfed... without taking physical action to support community, person or a structure"; with no action, nothing bonds in the field; "it actually propel[s] in [the] opposite direction", like a welfare loop. Beware the illusion of a utopia "like you will have everything and you will not need anything to construct." "Yes, you can have everything, but you also have to take the physical action... Your action is actually the physical glue bondage to create... the structure of three-dimensional." Even in a utopian community, you still help your neighbor put food on the table, do the crops, water the lawn.

The message: two signals splitting (condensed)

Two parallel timelines look almost alike at first, but their subtonality differs at the core: one converts through entitlement, without physical action, and loops into a negative input; the other takes physical action ("we're not talking here only money... by volunteering work, by giving to somebody, by... creating a localized community"), and the bond becomes "very strong... like a superglue" toward a fifth-dimensional timeline. In the middle there's so much chaotic information that you'll tire of it, "background noise"; that's a natural push toward the reality you've chosen. "If you stay within the heart... you will always choose to go through [the] ascension protocol."

#44.2Charge with reason; leave self-pity

If you are the main provider, charge, but with reason, so you don't deplete someone else's livelihood. If you feel you can't make it because of family drama, you'll keep reprojecting that field: pull away from the self-pity field, which keeps projecting events that return you to self-pity. Study the structure; it's your safe place. Then switch the signal through awareness and take the physical step, even without seeing the solution yet; you see the outcome only after you've projected it, because there are 360 degrees of parallel versions. Stay in the moment, converting it knowingly.

#The method — Switching the signal (as described)

  1. Notice which signal you keep vibrating in (fear, self-pity, waiting to be helped).
  2. Study the structure so you know it's a hologram you can convert.
  3. Choose the next moment through awareness, from the heart.
  4. Take one physical step that supports yourself or someone else, even without seeing the solution.
  5. Stay in the moment; let the outcome appear after you've projected it.

#Questions answered

Are negative beings triggering volcanic eruptions, and are positive ETs intervening? What about clones, and what percentage of humanity has awakened?
Sound technology can, they said, ignite a volcano's plasma structure, stop a fire, or redirect water; and a stronger layered signal can subside such a combustion slowly. Whoever does harm stamps it into their own consciousness to carry. On clones: some public figures have clones embedded with a negative consciousness; separately, they said, 73% of humanity has given permission, through the DNA ascension protocol, for the original sequence to be restructured and amplified (no junk DNA), as each human is a small copy of God consciousness holding the knowledge of conversion in the nucleus. To download such things precisely, the sound in your heart must be pure: act for humanity, not ego.
Is the interstellar object a ship of helpers from all over the universe?
They see two spectrums: a negative one (reptoid structures and super-soldier programs) and a more predominant positive one. What each channel downloads depends on their own vibration; some honestly download the negative probability. It will become what you focus on, so stay above it.
When we raise our vibration, do we light up on the grid and get targeted?
Yes: the artificial fragment recognizes you and sends signals to pull you into fear. But they can only send the signal; it becomes your reality only if you accept it through fear or misunderstanding. Stay centered, strong and compassionate, use the protocols, and keep overriding: pull back, analyze, then choose where your attention and physical action go. It can take a couple of years when it's close to home, and the hide-and-seek game still brings the next step.
I lost time twice during my meditation and exercise. What happened?
Practice made your creation so precise that you collapsed the field into zero point; time is a construct of the false hologram that delays conversion. Next, practice bilocating, for example from bedroom to kitchen: you'd be creating a new kitchen, in a new timeline, with a new version of yourself.
How can trauma, karma and reincarnation become traps?
Trauma is when you can't stop vibrating on a negative signal projected to you; you then reprint its codes. Karma is not knowing how to reconstruct it with a new signal; acting from the pain (eye for an eye) creates the same and loops you. Subside the pain through understanding, tell yourself you are a quantum field, and convert through compassion and the heart. The true history can't be erased; as you leave the loop, you'll download it, often in dreams: write them down and cross-reference.
