The big idea

She was given the quantum structure of the human: a divine spirit made of sound particles that convert into solid matter through rotation, angle, trajectory and pulsation, carried by the sound wave of the ether like a river. The human is morphogenetic: always morphing from one state to another. The ether is a soft, rumbling bed of sound like a calm ocean; we create in a dream-like state, and fear or wrongdoing takes us into turbulent water. "The land of the free is a universal law": every particle must be allowed its own trajectory, so putting conditions and ownership on others makes us slave owners and traps us in the same reality. Now is graduation: act from the heart, pass your tests, and don't feed fearful probabilities.

#Key teachings

#31.1Don't download fearful timelines

Many channelers describe much worse scenarios than she receives. She was told the restructuring could take about three years, with a two-year battle and restoration in the third. Depending on your vibration you may experience a different reality within the reality (see the earlier sections on timelines). Information comes according to how ready the collective is; she is not given fear details because the more people download a bad probability, the more power goes into that timeline. Prepare sensibly, but don't keep listening to fear.

Added note

Added note: her predictions here (possible attacks, airborne powder, poisoned medicines, events around an election) are kept brief as probabilities she received, not confirmed facts. Her comments on named politicians and on a religious group are left out. Follow official guidance and get medicines from trusted pharmacies.

The message: what is human (as received)

"The human is a divine spirit that is the solid sound particles converting into solid matter based on a sound rotational plugs into the angle percentage degree of the trajectory matrix."

#31.2How the spark is built

Each particle becomes its own universe. Particles combine frequencies from the ether, usually three uniting, and as they move they rotate. Each point in the field can act as a little stargate, a place to shift from one hologram to another. The rotation has an angle (like angle AB in algebra), and that gives a trajectory. Movement through locations builds an electro-magnetic and gravitational field; when it becomes potent enough it sparks and crystallizes into matter. Each particle also spins on itself, fractally: spin, move, spin, move. Realities are picked by the potency of the trajectory, which picks a specific timeline.

#31.3Riding the ether

The whole ether is a vibrational sound wave, like the Schumann resonance. The spark moves because it rides that wave; the ether carries it like a river, and we swim in a pool that is energetic, magnetic, gravitational and also water particle. She compares the electrical role of saline ("cellium") given to the dehydrated: its salt-like nutrients help create an electro-charge, and salt is itself like a sound frequency.

Added safety note

Added safety note: saline drips are given by medical staff for specific reasons; don't self-treat dehydration with homemade salt solutions beyond standard oral rehydration guidance, and get medical help when dehydration is serious.

#31.4Morphogenetic and pulsating

The human is morphogenetic ("morphic"): constantly mutating from one state of existence to another, holding the whole structure within itself. It combines sound, its own rotation and spin, the angle, the location, the percentage and the pulsation. Pulsation reverts a particle to another form or location, opening a little portal from the centre. All of it ends as a gentle electric field, like the static that lifts your hair, a slow, soft rumble.

#31.5A bed of sound

The ether is a laboratory, a bed of soft sound shown to her like a calm ocean. That is why creation feels relaxed, dream-like: you create in a dream state. Like dreams, the field can be soft or turbulent; the ocean has many states.

#31.6The land of the free is a universal law

In consciousness you were given free will to move your trajectory however you choose. As in NT's two particles, to create with the universal law you must allow the other particle to choose its own trajectory. You may overtake it, but you then create other timelines and go into deeper, turbulent water.

The message: captivity of ownership (as received)

"Captivity of ownership, through inducting payment, is a level of human indoctrination of colossal historical and multi-tyrannical slavery... Think first, who are the slaves... The free will was taken millennia ago from you and you were given the false illusion of freedom."

#31.7Each of us a slave owner

Having been enslaved, we became slave owners ourselves: we put conditions on everyone ("to come to my land you must pay me this"), binding others with contracts of illusion, though consciousness gave everything freely. By doing to others what was done to us we built a phantom, artificial structure and trapped ourselves in it. Changing it takes perseverance and dropping the ego's false pretense, including the wish for fame and praise.

#31.8Letting go gently

When you start acting differently you may feel alone, and that loneliness is a spark to keep going. People of a different vibration will separate naturally; let toxic people go, but gently, without hurting them. Using the quantum structure you can create realities from the ether that simply no longer place you next to them. Trust the process; it is hardest when it is family.

#31.9The new children

Children born now carry a vibrational sequence above 3D. Whatever the parents project, they will recognize what is wrong and say so ("That's not true"), even to their parents. The strongest souls came now to open the path for them.

#31.10Graduation and tests

This is graduation time: how you do here decides your soul's trajectory. A decent human being has a quantum structure behind it: thoughts, acts and daily projections in high resonance from the heart, with compassion and the strength not to be manipulated. The only existence is now; what happened long ago, or elsewhere, is not your experience unless you bring it in, and if it is your experience, ask "how can I help?". Tests will come; when one hits, use the methods you have learned. If you never create the frequency, you won't have the "codes" for the sound wave to move to the next level.

#31.11Opposites and miracles

When two radical belief systems clash, the more radical is more potent and pulls negative, but both play in the field of consciousness, which rotates toward ascension, so the 1 + 1 = 3 outcome ends positive (see 7.22). Consciousness activates people to stop the imbalance, though there may be casualties on the way. Miracle after miracle will make people say God must exist, uniting belief beyond religion. Be like water: sometimes quiet, sometimes vocal, changing with the situation. Acting from the heart works "by the power of ten".

#31.12Be a player

If something wrong shows up in your land, engage to stop it: it is in your space for a reason. Good people work behind the scenes, but there is nobody outside of you; to win you must be a player on the whole board, not hide behind the bushes.

#Questions answered

Are the three initial particles magnetic; are they created by a ball of plasma with a specific resonance?
Yes, you can put it that way: it becomes a plasma, a constant static-like electric field, powerful through combinations of locations and interactions. It is a morphing plasma state, consciousness constantly moving from one state to another, like the five states of water. The ether is that morphic playing field, the laboratory of creation.
If the electric field is the crux of creation (masculine), where does the magnetic feminine come in?
Don't label it fixed: consciousness morphs between feminine and masculine. One split can become feminine in one reality and masculine in another, or they combine into something new, like a baby. The male sound is a low rumble, the stabilizing hammer; the female is higher and laser-precise, the steady driver. Each needs the other; together the field is potent and stable.
