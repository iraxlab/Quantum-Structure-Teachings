The War On Humanity

Why things are escalating and how you'll be tested to the core, how a divided people is controlled, why passivity can't change your reality, a received message ("the war is between realities now") on balancing every negative with a positive action, and questions on helping the awake, lockdowns, and grief after a disaster

The big idea

The negative must keep its frequency low to survive, so it will escalate, and you will be tested by how you react from the core of your heart when no one is watching. The way to control the most powerful species is to divide it; your answer is unity and action. Prayer without action won't crystallize anything: share the truth, give, help. The message: the war is between realities now. Answer every negative sound with a positive action so the matrix stays in balance, create your own new world order from the heart, and don't comply with what you know harms others.

#Key teachings

#33.1Tested to the core

If you find yourself in a disaster, ask why you're there: perhaps to help others out with your knowledge, so be the warrior. In such moments you react from the core of the soul, not for popularity; she has seen both the best and the worst of humanity, people profiting or competing for glory while others suffered. Those who know the structure can run on a sub-platform: like watching it happen on Mars, never to you.

#33.2Divide and control

She read out a text describing how to control a species: explain everything so they never think for themselves, humiliate dissent, show only tragedy so they fear each other, teach that existence is random and purposeless, give idols of artificial beauty, rank them by followers, "completely divide [them] within themselves". That is the agenda. Don't waste time on those whose analytical processing is shut down; focus on those open to facts. There is no middle ground: complying with what you know harms others makes you part of the problem.

#33.3Passivity doesn't crystallize

"Can I be praying and not doing?" Spreading information plasters a frequency in the field and plants seeds that sprout later. Being afraid of what family and friends will say means you're not ready: speaking the truth carries the true vibration. Ask yourself daily what you physically did for humanity. If you want abundance, learn to give it. If family reject you, make new friends: your best friend could be a brother from another timeline.

The message: the war is between realities (condensed)

"Lotus in rivers, springs will be lifted up, and the new sky will rain with cleanest water," its droplets full of life to repopulate depleted soil; new seeds will begin again. "The new era is now. The war is between realities now." "Stop all social negative media. Focus only to family, neighbors, local farms... Repopulate your soil with new seeds... Be trustworthy and support your neighbors. Smile... and whisper words of encouragement." "Use the negative words in reverse... with positive actions. This way you will create complete balance in the matrix." Make your own new world order with your visions and actions: "be the leader." "Do not comply... Only prayer? Where is the action?... In the end, only your actions will truly determine who you really are." "The war is not real. It is an illusion of your mind."

Her instructions: Balancing the matrix

When they stop food: give food to someone. Negative music: play positive music. "Total collapse": say "we have everything we need". Negative teaching: "not my child"; teach your child to be a lady or gentleman. Their hologram meets your counter-frequency, and yin and yang balance.

Added note

She also predicted further escalation in America, with the people eventually calling on the military for order, after enough have woken up to avoid civil conflict. As with every prediction in this book, it is given as received; disaster-specific claims and named people are left out.

#Questions answered

How do I help people who are awake but don't know the quantum and have no faith in change?
Speak your truth with facts; you may become the black sheep. If they won't accept facts, move on: "do not look for the people. People will find you" (also through the Quantum Manifestation Network). As your frequency grows, strangers will start the very conversation. "The truth will set you free": repeating it carries its vibration and draws people who think alike.
Will there be another lockdown?
Yes, in certain areas, of something minor: a bank, an area's food supply, linked to building controlled "mini-cities". But why even ask? The question carries fear and gives that reality a probability; you can create a reality where it never touches you.
A friend lost her home in a disaster and I'm grieving. What can I do?
Recovery of the home won't be soon: legal obstacles were placed strategically, but legally it will be restored in time, and negative structures will be exposed. Such souls chose by free will to be there so humanity sees the evil and acts. Act: in creating, the path of what you can do will show itself.
