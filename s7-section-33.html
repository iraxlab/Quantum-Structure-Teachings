#Questions answered

If these bloodlines enter certain soul groups, will negative souls be recycled and start again?
Yes and no, depending on how potent their off-balance symphony is. Some will be recycled; others go through pockets between realities, an "intermission" between octaves, to play another reality and become coherent before entering the next octave. Only very low-vibration expressions choose self-destruction: spinning on themselves, they dissolve back to source.
Are hybrids and Pleiadians part of the new bloodlines; will we see our ET bloodlines?
Some are. Not every ET family enters the planet; some observe or stay above, using technology to pull Earth's trajectory toward a specific stargate, since technologies had been pulling Earth toward negative octaves. Some intelligences are so high that our consciousness can't yet convert their sound. Step by step, drop by drop.
Does a bloodline affect the human energy field?
Depends on the bloodline. Some built very coherent, potent resonance, positive or negative. Earth was overtaken by bloodlines not in humanity's interest; their symphony rippled for centuries (for example, a banking system built negatively), so now a new conversion is needed until such systems dissolve. The transition must be smooth, slowly but surely, because the mutation is then permanent, like not jumping from very hot to very cold.
Could a blood genetic disorder in children relate to this?
In some ways, yes. When the vibrational sound from the parents' or lineage's connection is off balance, the genetic component (the octave in the genome) becomes off balance, showing as a disorder. Advanced technologies used by some bloodlines are one reason; another is partners merging through artificial rather than natural, love-based coherence.
Are the bloodlines playing this role only to help us ascend?
Yes. It is a laboratory of creation: you can't learn to construct and deconstruct without knowing what merges and what doesn't. It is a game; play to the end, and at the next level you will retain the previous knowledge.
How can we best use this information?
It must feel coherent to you; then learn how it manifests. Let go of judgment about why family members act as they do. Perhaps forgive, perhaps let someone be who they wish while no longer letting them hurt you or your line. Knowing the structure, you choose with precision what to do, and manifestation becomes fluid and easy.
Are indigenous peoples of a special bloodline important to Earth's freedom?
Very much so. Their blood holds sacred knowledge and the original codes given to humanity, while others' DNA was repeatedly modified; they kept apart in forests for a reason. "The knowledge is in the blood." Study epigenetics and which blood types respond to which sound; look at blood as plasma.
