The big idea

The Council of Nine said everything is consciousness: every concept of creation has its own reality and free will. You cannot condemn any part of it without condemning yourself, because whatever you see is a projection in your own hologram; you don't consent to evil, you pull back, see it as a lesson and restructure. Technology is part of that growth, so you can't simply reject it, but it must be balanced: nature's etheric field grounds your bio-photonic field to the planet's hologram, while too much technology disturbs your toroidal field, leaving holes that cells copy into decay. Their suggestion is about 70% nature and the rest technology. Live by acceptance and balance, not separation.

#Key teachings

#8.1Accept, don't condemn

Every concept of creation has its own reality and free will of expression; you can't eradicate any part, you must include all of it. Each person chose to experience certain versions of themselves, so don't judge. That doesn't mean consenting: pull away and observe from pure love and total awareness. To condemn another's existence (another religion, even people doing hideous things) is to condemn yourself, because they are a projection in your own holographic expression; you can't reject it, but you can restructure it and shift into a timeline that projects another version of them.

#8.2See it as a lesson

To become pure knowledge, pass through realities without judgment, seeing each like a lesson of self-awareness, not through hatred. Hatred projects a frequency into your field that reprints realities giving you more reasons for hatred, unless you convert it to positive. Acceptance is your zero point of balance (see the zero point in earlier sections): no frequency in or out, and in that moment you hold the power to command your mind and mutate the reality.

#8.3No escape from the lesson

Technological advancement is part of consciousness's growth. You can't escape into the mountains, caves, a bunker or a homestead to avoid it: wherever you go, your higher self downloads the situations you came to learn from. Every expression must be allowed its free will to find its own balance (see HR on the bylaw of the singular particle). Creation is limitless but built for perfect balance; when something tips too far negative, consciousness sends situations so you can choose to rebalance. Knowledge, like graduating 12th grade instead of 1st, lets you command that balance.

#8.4Nature's etheric field

Everything is first created in the etheric field and then downloaded into matter. Nature has its own frequency and hologram with a specific sound wave. If you live only in technology, your consciousness may stop understanding nature and lose the ability to download nature's etheric field: the conductive energy that grounds your bio-photonic field to the planet's hologram. Technology is designed to help restore ideas and speed information, not to fill 100% of your day.

#8.5How imbalance becomes illness

Technology's hologram moves at a different speed through your toroidal field. Too much of it changes your toroidal field's spin and trajectory (compare HR on entering structures at 90 degrees), like a craft entering a planet at the wrong angle, and illness follows. Its "ionic zap" can stop the field suddenly, leaving packets of holes in the etheric body. Each cell has its own toroidal field and copies the previous one in cell division, so the holes reprint into every cell: decay, and aging.

#8.6Restore the land

A toxic environment does the same damage. Clean the planet of poisons: restore land, meadows, fields and flowers; release ladybugs (they suggest about a thousand per two acres); stop growing genetically modified seeds, which she says cause mutation in every creature living in that ecosystem, including you. Technologies that restore soil and its biome, and free energy, exist; ask your representatives about them. Integration must be slow, so people don't suddenly lose their jobs.

Added note

Added note: her claims about GMOs and suppressed technologies are her views; check the evidence for yourself.

#8.7AI exists: accept, don't consent

Whatever is created in consciousness, as AI is, already pre-exists; you can't reject it. Some will consent to merge with AI, perhaps for a needed military role in a parallel reality; it needn't be negative. But the timeline pulling humanity toward the negative won't survive: its spin will be so out of balance that it self-destructs. You don't have to consent to it in this reality. Technology can be used for good or harm; keep the balance.

#8.8Unity, not "only I know"

Some claim only they download from God and condemn everyone who speaks of extraterrestrials, out-of-body travel, lucid dreaming or astrology as possessed. But God is all that is, including everyone with these abilities, and scripture is science that few can break down. Include every nation and love everybody. If someone does evil, stop it the legal way; if someone invades your domain, defend yourself and don't let anyone overtake your consciousness. But don't reject existence or set yourself above others: that is separation, not unity.

#The method — Rebalancing with nature (as suggested)

  1. Aim for about 70% of your time in nature and the rest with technology.
  2. Take a weekly break (weekend or any days off): no phones, no TV, no computers.
  3. Spend it with family and friends in nature: hug a tree, talk to the birds, love your pets.
  4. Plant trees and flowers; restore your land and release ladybugs.
  5. Step outside daily and breathe the fresh air for a few minutes, feeling the energy.
  6. Plan a daily schedule and keep it consistently; repetition makes it permanent.

#Questions answered

Can the Council suggest fun competitive games, since many games reward killing?
A game merges a technological hologram with your organic bio-field. Its repeated thought (kill, kill, kill) becomes a permanent vibration that reprints, so don't be surprised if synchronicity brings you situations of violence. Held in the heart, you wouldn't choose such a game. Find games that reward positive action and expand consciousness and memory: building positive technology, a homestead, nature. The same goes for negative movies and videos.
Can you hold the other webinars at a time when everyone is free?
She can't change the timing, but much of her information is available free.
How do we balance the 70% with staying informed and connecting online?
Keep it modest: a couple of hours a day is enough to learn and connect. If your job requires screens, cut back on movies at night. Start little by little; the Council said at least 15 minutes of repetition makes a new action permanent in the neurons, and matter takes longer. A consistent schedule for 30 days becomes a permanent structure.
