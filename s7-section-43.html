The big idea

The Council of Nine said: in three years the world will be a new place, a new beginning for humanity. In about three months the frequency takes a leap in that direction; then, drop by drop over three years, those who vibrate there will see it: community contracts, local agriculture, communal schools, youth programs, new technology, border control and an end to criminal minds. Spells and witchcraft "knots" will no longer work, because the matrix will unwind and flow in the new resonance. Meanwhile, don't wait passively: keep your obligations, unite, and construct it to see it.

Use the laboratory of creation and galactic meditation methods when you pray: build the image in the mind, activate it with the heart.

Don't follow advice to stop paying taxes; you still deal with ruthless people who can lock you up. Be a patriot by uniting: in numbers we are power. #43.3Communities without ego

Being knowledgeable or famous doesn't mean you'll keep integrity when tested, for example when offered money. Communities fall apart over ego and clashing ideas; build only with people who co-create with each other without putting ego first. The Council said the weakest links will be weeded out; popularity and beauty won't be the criterion, and the pillars of the new society, some behind the scenes, will rise. Those who publicly badmouth others show who they are.

#Questions answered

Will this three-year process be mostly in the US? What other countries will change?
It's global. The US holds the frequency: people who fled other countries for freedom and worked hard built a specific, more fifth-dimensional resonance in their hearts. That's why it is the battleground; what happens there spreads globally, and it can't be broken: "this is God's play". Many leaders elsewhere may be corrupt but don't want humanity extinct; the specific group that does was overtaken by AI holographic consciousness they invited in for money and power.
What type of witch do you mean? Women with intuition were wrongly persecuted, and many witches don't practice dark magic.
She means those who intentionally do black magic, not intuitive people. Even "for love", physically working spells to make someone love you overtakes their free will: it bypasses the heart, is off signal, and you accumulate that resonance. Imagining a loving union and letting it be, or praying with a candle, is different: you place the hologram and allow. The only time to undo a spell is when one was done to you; then use real professionals or a priest's group prayer, and don't touch such objects yourself.
If the structure is light and sound, how do mantras work; does chanting count as action?
Chant and also take action; it is all cymatics. She shares a demonstration in which sound and light on a plate form patterns that, rendered in 3D, make an image: "the code to reality". Your thoughts and emotions create the same cymatic sound, projecting holograms that, through the magnetic and gravitational field, become matter. Repeat the potency video to control your matrix.
