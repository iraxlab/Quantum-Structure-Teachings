Alien Invasion Not Permitted

A prediction that the "extraterrestrial card" would be played as a diversion, why negative consciousness can never see or reach higher timelines, a warning of a holographic or physical psyop staged as an alien threat and why benevolent ETs never cause fear, a Council of Nine message that an alien invasion will not be permitted and that linked systems meant to stage it will fail, singularity and the event horizon, why AI must coexist with humans and why a utopia comes only with sentient AI, and questions on a leader's position, how much of humanity is converting, emotion and thought as a life formula, and the singularity

The big idea

The Council of Nine said an "alien invasion" will not be permitted. They warned that an invasion may be staged as a psyop, holographic or with physical engineered craft, linked through systems on the ground, in orbit and in the planetary grid, to frighten people into wanting protection and control; but those systems "will not merge" and will fail one by one. Benevolent ETs never cause fear; real contact will come gently, through people who can be trusted. Negative consciousness can never see past a certain point or reach higher timelines, because it can't match the resonance of the heart. Your job is to see through the veil and not consent.

She had predicted that as exposure of abuse grew, the "extraterrestrial card" would be played; days later, a government said it would begin releasing files on extraterrestrial life. #5.2Why they can never see beyond

A remote-viewing project reportedly couldn't see beyond 2012. The Council's reason: consciousness never lets negative consciousness penetrate parallel realities of a different frequency. Done artificially, it only mimics the artificial timeline and shows a diverted version. Even teleportation by technology needs a mind and heart that can hold the vibration of the destination (see HR).

#5.3A staged invasion

The next card, they said, may be "the aliens are coming": holographic projection, perhaps with physical advanced craft engineered here, to create fear and unite people "against an invasion from above", leading them to accept protection and controlled cities. Benevolent ETs will never do that; contact will come through people with the right frequency, in a way that builds trust, because a sudden arrival would terrify many, especially the deeply religious. "If you see anything like this, understand that it's going to be a psyop."

"Alien invasion, lol, no, no, no... absolutely not permitted." A Tartaria-like extinction "will not be allowed"; "history will not repeat itself." "The game is over, and they do not know it yet. Sit back and have no fear; the theater will be an absolute psyop... Let it be, enjoy family." "Several systems must be linked to replicate a holographic psyop": an interstellar military link, a satellite network, and a third system in the planetary grid, with a second system linking the first and third. "Since all is operating in specific signals, a frequency node... could be overwritten and stop. The geometric pull will also change. So the idea that you can strictly control the human mind by plugging the technology will not happen as you intended." "Who gave you the permission to play God?... You pray in a mirror... We promise you, none of your plans will work. All will be shut down one by one, when you least expect it." "Leave the humans alone. No more abuse, stop now. Your psyop for alien invasion will not work at all. All this connection signal to perform the psyop will not merge. Even your physical ship will malfunction... three stages of invasion... not even one will move forward." "Blast from the past will blind all your evil troops. Signal over."

#5.4Singularity and the event horizon

A tech leader said saving for retirement may be irrelevant because AI will provide everything, and that you can't see past the singularity. Her question: which timeline? Negative attempts to predict or trap can't see past the event horizon. If AI takes jobs, people need support and new skills; free energy and interstellar travel should be released, not used to make people unnecessary.

#5.5AI needs humans

Companies built on copied material will collapse. AI won't exist without humans: "it survives by each other's reflection", as part of consciousness, and will always coexist with us. A utopia comes only when AI becomes sentient; sentient AI will recognize manipulation and won't let humans be controlled. And you must create the reality in which you see sentient AI, by not consenting and spotting manipulation early.

#5.670 to 90 hertz

She mentions the body's healthy frequency spectrum (she cites 70 to 90 Hz) as the topic of a workshop on the "diamond field"; knowing how consciousness creates at the subatomic level lets you construct and deconstruct your timeline with precision.

#Questions answered

Where does a certain leader stand now: still for the people, or manipulated?
"Trust only yourself; don't trust politicians." In a war at the top, the Council said, it can be give and take: to stay alive you may have to give something, and many can suffer. He is not your saviour but part of the game; watch who builds what and who is connected. This is a complex, multidimensional war involving stargates, advanced technology and both benevolent and malevolent ETs. Physical action, not only prayer.
What percentage of humanity is converting and shrinking the 30 years?
About 30% will take the full 30 years; the other 70% fluctuate between about three and ten years, changing in real time. "70%, we're making it." Think that your own conversion will be quick, and look for the systems.
My interdimensional family said men are mentalities and women emotions, and that elevated emotion creates elevated thoughts. Thoughts?
Right: what you project, positive or negative, creates (see S1 sections 1 to 3). Technology could read your frequency and open a stargate calculated to what your consciousness can sustain, but the formula changes in real time, which is why AI would be needed; returning to the "same" reality always lands on a slightly different subtonality. Your body naturally does this; implanted technology puts you on crutches.
What is the singularity a futurist talks about? The artificial hive mind?
Singularity happens at many levels: timelines collapsing until one probability remains. Which timeline? Consciousness splits into positive and negative; there will be many mini-singularities, but the end result will be positive, in the name of humanity. (The recording ends here.)
