Q & A: The Zero-Point Vacuum, Healing and Frequency

Why manifestation is instant, illness and remedies as frequency, not judging, and meeting storms from neutrality (live Q&A)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

When two realities merge into the third (1 + 1 = 3), the merge is a compression so intense it reaches infinity, and infinity equals the zero point: a vacuum where no frequency comes in or goes out, only awareness. Neutralize yourself into that vacuum, then project a potent positive frequency; because the vacuum holds the energy of the whole structure, the stronger frequency becomes your new hologram instantly. Illness is treated the same way, as a frequency carried from reality to reality, and every remedy is a vibration. Don't judge anyone: change comes from within, through neutrality, the heart and action.

#Key teachings

#12.1The shift has been made

This session opens by saying a new era has begun: enough frequency was built up to crystallize the timeline of ascension, and that is worth celebrating (see 2.8).

#12.2How two realities become a third: compression

Each reality is a hologram of your own Earth, with everyone imprinted inside (see 11.5). Two timelines on different platforms can't see each other; you see the other one only when you merge back and forth at a similar frequency (see 10.3). As your emotion fluctuates, the two merge into a third, 1 + 1 = 3 (see 2.2), like a tug of war in which the more potent side wins.

New in this session: to make the third, the two realities undergo a compression of the energetic field, vibrating so fast that it is effectively infinite speed. And infinity equals the zero point. The zero point is a vacuum: no frequency comes in or goes out of anywhere. You are just there, as awareness. All manifestation comes from it.

From the booksZero point is where "each structure you create a vacuum of stillness so you will be able to shift and choose the new implementation of realities" (SD). The books also name a "'0' point Portal Vacuum" (NT).

#12.3Why manifestation is instantaneous

First neutralize everything: no positive, no negative, nothing in or out. Neutrality is what enters the vacuum; you become the awareness. Then project, through your mind, a very potent frequency. If one side is negative and one positive, overwrite with the positive (see 2.6). Whatever is stronger is projected into the vacuum.

Because the vacuum is under infinite pressure, holding the energy of the entire multi-universe, the projection is instantaneous: there is no time, only flickering. You think of something and it is projected in front of you. Even moving across a room, you aren't really moving; you are projecting everyone back in, instantly (see 11.5). What comes out of that compression is your final hologram, the new reality you shift into.

#12.4Illness in the quantum field

Question: how do you perceive illness in the quantum field?

Illness is described as an energetic momentum of a frequency, matching your conscious understanding. If you keep the belief system that holds that frequency, you carry its vibrational sequence from one reality to the next and keep re-imprinting the same illness.

The teaching is that you can neutralize it and shift your consciousness, through the vacuum, into a reality that no longer holds it, using Stop · Transform · Complete (see 7.1). A further method is to teach your consciousness to go into your DNA: recall the momentum of that frequency, let consciousness lead you to the DNA fractal, see it, then stop and transform it. This needs a belief that manifestation is simultaneous; you are extracting yourself from one timeline and co-creating a new one, where the "dots of vibration" carrying the illness are no longer present.

From the books"Healing comes at '0' point," and the body "will recognize within itself the DNA code and re-adjust to perfect harmony"; the books link healing to sound and the heart's tone (SD).
Important health note

Use these practices alongside medical care, never instead of it. If you are ill or in pain, see a doctor; don't stop or change any treatment or medication without your doctor's advice.

#12.5Remedies, sound and frequency

Question: as a practitioner, how can I help heal someone?

Everything comes down to frequency and sound; every flower and plant carries its own vibrational sequence. A practitioner using remedies is choosing vibrations and giving them to the person. In that sense the healing is "an illusion": the plant is a vibration, and you are learning to work with vibrations, through matter, so you can have the experience. Combine them well and you are "playing like a chemist" with the structure.

Each frequency has a meaning. From the session: 3 Hz prepares the vacuum before a new frequency comes in; 5 Hz stabilizes, and without it the other frequencies can't merge; higher numbers mean more potency. The same holds for music: tones carry frequencies into your consciousness, so choose consciously what you listen to.

The talk also claims that published frequency lists for some oils, and for older frequency-healing machines, were altered and are no longer accurate, so it advises: don't assume, measure. These are the talk's claims, not checked facts. Its broader point is that knowledge is power: study the science, and you gain the awareness to decide your next step.

From the booksIn the books, 3 Hz "prepares all the energy for operation for all the fields (the base)", and 5 Hz "stays neutral" and "is needed when you are mixing the different tones" (HR). So the books describe 5 Hz as neutral and needed for mixing, where the talk calls it stabilizing; both agree the merge needs it.

#12.6Don't judge; change comes from within

Signs of disclosure are appearing because those in control have less and less choice (see 6.8). Still, don't judge a consciousness that has lived for centuries by taking others' energy; it is how it learned to survive. Many of us do something similar to animals, which are conscious beings, and then complain when it is done to us (see 9.7).

Change happens from within: by neutralizing, switching realities and timelines, manifesting and moving on, through forgiveness, neutrality and the heart. Support each other through Energy Philanthropy in any form, money or gifts (see 5.1). And take action: thinking and feeling bring the frequency, but action is the infuser of your reality (see 11.4).

#12.7Weather, storms and contracts

Question: are the intense weather events a result of the new reality crystallizing in the collective?

Weather events are described as part of our evolution; don't take them as negative. The talk adds claims that some weather may be used to clear chemicals sprayed from the air, which it says can make people act like antennas for frequencies, and recommends drinking distilled water to flush them out. These are claims, not established facts.

The teaching itself: whatever you see, neutralize it at zero point with gratitude for being shown where you are, then manifest the positive, again and again. Shift your timeline and it is no longer part of your reality. If you go through a storm or a hard experience, pull out and see the whole picture: you are there through a contract, perhaps to experience it, or to help or save someone. Stay neutral and act positively through the heart (see 5.7).

From the booksA reader challenged the distilled-water claim as chemically false, since pure water is neutral. The books' answer agrees it is "technically" neutral, but says it takes on a charge in the body and pulls out toxins (SD).
Important safety note

In severe weather, follow official emergency guidance first; pulling out is an inner practice, not a substitute for staying safe. Distilled water lacks minerals, so if you plan to make it your only drinking water, check with a doctor first.

#The method — Through the Vacuum

Use it when what you see and what you want are pulling against each other:

  1. Name the two realities: the one in front of you, and the one you choose.
  2. Pull out and neutralize everything. No positive, no negative. Gratitude for being shown where you are. Be the awareness, with nothing going in or out.
  3. Project a potent positive frequency from that stillness, stronger than the other side (see 2.6).
  4. Know it is instant. There is no time; it is projected now. Hold it.
  5. Take action. Action infuses the new hologram.
  6. For illness or danger, add the practical step: see your doctor, follow safety guidance, and use this alongside.
