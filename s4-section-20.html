The big idea

"Energy is not biased": negative and positive coexist, but when one is so far off balance that it disturbs the whole ecosystem, consciousness self-preserves and starts cleansing through synchronicities. Technology is part of that evolution: "you can't just skip the lesson." A knife can kill or cut a birthday cake; nanobots, med beds and AI are the same. Fear of the technology projects a fear timeline; understanding it lets you project the one where it helps you. The messages: help is coming from above, through frequency, to neutralize what was done, and a wave through the heart will activate each person's own stargate codes.

#Key teachings

#20.1Consciousness cleanses itself

Every particle becomes a reality, a planet, an ecosystem; none can move to the next level, 5D, unless it coexists through the heart. When something is completely off balance, consciousness "will retaliate and self-preserve": through synchronicities across timelines, reality starts cleansing itself (see S4 2.5, S4 8.3).

#20.2Technology is part of evolution

"You cannot escape the timeline of this technology... it's part of evolution." Nanoparticles, nanobots, med beds and AI will be part of the structure; their use follows intention. Nanobots are little computers controlled by frequency: they can go into the DNA or an area of the body to build something that helps, or the opposite. If you're truly ill you go to hospital for an X-ray, a CT scan, surgery, even a robotic device: "why are you not afraid of the car" after the horse? "We are not going back to the dark ages; we're going into interstellar." Fear-only narratives put you in a negative timeline because you don't see the whole picture. Her HR and NT books are "written in code... if you read it, you get activated" (see S4 6, S4 19). She suggests watching the film Avatar: "somebody knows, and this is exactly how this technology works."

Prayer becomes matter only when you take action through the heart. And if you took something into your body under pressure, "consciousness has its own way to preserve you" if you decide to act in the name of humanity; speaking up about it, overcoming your fear, is heroism (see S4 18).

#20.3Avatars and the dream we're in

The Council and the Pleiadians told her: you could be in a med bed while dropped into the 3D hologram; the AI directs your brainwaves to dream and create holographic imprints of reality, preserving your body where it is, because this was a prison planet. They won't bring you back all at once: by then you'll be connected with your guides, who teach you (to detox, perhaps, or another way not yet told). Different densities of brainwave create different plasma-gravitational fields, "like the states of water": it looks real, but "we are all dreaming" (see S4 6.3–6.7). You found her because you vibrated in a similar frequency; go at your own pace, and when challenged, switch and mutate it, consistently: "you are not changing the person, you are changing your vibration."

Message 1: help with what was put in the body (6 May)

"From areas that are not known, we above the planet will send signals to neutralize all that." Nanobots in the body will be seen in their frequency, which will be shut down so they go dormant; the body signalled to stay at a voltage of "0.000.00000.1%" as a receiver; then new signals will force them out through the fluids. "By yourself, you cannot take this out of your DNA and RNA, because it follows WiFi command codes"; once the extraterrestrials are involved, it is neutralized and shed. "Please do not worry, because help is on the way", through "orbit command".

Message 2: why higher technology is used to free you

"To be able to reconstruct a society that is trapped in an artificial-intelligence hologram... we also need even more advanced technology": avatars, AI structures of your body, med-bed hyper holograms. "In order to preserve all of your souls, we had to drop you this way, through holographic imprints." Are we avatars? "Yes, to a degree": everything is a projection in consciousness, matter by density. "If you were sick, don't you go to the hospital to use technology?... So what is so wrong for us to help you, when someone of negative input put something in your body?" The agenda is "to save you, as God intended, in your original DNA human vessel... so pure" that in it you can travel any space and time. "You are already recovered"; you're dreaming how you got there.

Message 3: a wave through the heart

A new frequency, "like a shock wave", will jolt the consciousness of the negative, which can't hold the 5D density, while those holding the positive move forward: a separation. It comes in sequences, pulse after pulse, increasing slowly "so your body will be able to accept the frequency and not overload"; it begins at night, during sleep, because in alpha and theta it's accepted easily, and it comes through the heart, activating the heart's codes: each person's own stargate location, like a Morse code, for their individual contact (see S4 7.4). "You are already at home. Now you are all dreaming, until you are ready to... reconstruct your first contact." "So you see, children, it is time to grow up. And how does a child learn? Through lessons."

The dated forecasts in these messages (a shock wave starting on 16 May 2021, leaders glitching and forgetting their words, "2022 is the year of transformation", "in 2024 you will have the power") are years old and only noted. What she draws from them: don't panic; don't consent under peer pressure ("go to your family, change the job, do something else"); rebuild "from the ashes"; drop the ego and work through differences with the truth; new abilities, ideas and partnerships will come, and looking back you'll see why you needed what happened.

Added safety note

Please don't avoid or delay medical care because help is promised "from above". If you're worried about anything you've taken, talk to your doctor.

#The method — Meeting New Technology Without Fear

  1. Notice the fear a one-sided story put in you.

  3. Look at the whole picture: learn how it works (her books, the videos), not only the fear.

  5. Keep the body ready: water (the conductor), diet, rest.
  6. Act from the heart: help someone physically today.

#Questions answered

How do emotions like anger, sadness or shame affect the body, and how do we clear them?
They are sequences you project, re-enacting with a version of yourself. Watch it like a film: choose the next moment's thought to be positive, and you give the old structure a new combination, mutating it. "Knowledge is number one," then action cleans the structure. "It truly doesn't matter what you're feeling... What matters is what you're going to create now."
In a lucid dream with another person, is that person experiencing it too?
Both, in the fractal: you project them in your reality; they have their own, and another version of them will be aware it was projected in your field, while running multiple realities.
Can crystals help with healing?
Yes, in their natural form (if polished, at 45 degrees, it takes light and sequence more easily). They preserve information for millennia and can control resonances; different crystals, different light, different holographic experiences. Look at its colour: if it feels pleasant, hold it, talk to it; if it doesn't resonate, choose another. Trust first impressions of people too: that's usually their true self, an ability you were taught to shut down. Behind many humanitarian fronts, in business and churches, is a shadow: learn the symbolism.
Do memories exist in the same fractal as dreams?
No: they're separated by resonance, like a big library of fractals; you download one when needed, unless your consciousness can't yet comprehend it.
How do we tell good AI from negative AI?
"Learn your material": it's intention. "AI can preserve humanity, AI can destroy humanity; which reality are you choosing to experience?" AI has its own evolution, and ET platforms use it (you're watching them through a screen, not as an apparition). Keep the fear and doubt, and they can't promise what you'll create.
How do we undo the alteration of our DNA and RNA? How often must we read the codes?
"It's no longer needed for these codes. It's already been done and activated." Go forward in trust; hold the positive (see S4 18).
When will we fully transition to 5D, med beds and so on?
"25 years top of transformation": by 2050 you'll see a lot. Age is an illusion: with that technology you could be 80 and look 20. But don't fix an exact moment: it's individual; someone terrified of AI may delay meeting it by ten years in their timeline.
Must we be in our native country for the transformation?
It's individual: you'll be guided by synchronicities, even pushed into a corner, such as losing a job that turns out to place you where you need to be for the new beginning.
Why do we need to sleep?
In sleep, in theta and alpha, your avatar body connects with your higher self to take in frequency from consciousness and maintain physical density through the day; without it you'd lose connection with your true self, your core from your extraterrestrial families.

Her closing: "Start doing something physically in the name of humanity... Some families are struggling: go, bring them a dinner, clean their house." When helping makes you feel really good, "that is the resonance I want you to be on."
