The big idea

Thoughts, heart and body together (the trinity of knowledge, heart action and diet) morph you into a higher resonance where collapse flows easily and time shrinks. Consciousness, like one molecule, heals itself by sending frequency into every reality; no negative notion can become permanent. A new "sipto coherent resonance" technology, combined with organic intelligence, will surround each negative soul in its own resonance so its negativity can't project into the collective, and will help restore positive humans to the coherent resonance of the original DNA held in the mother oversoul's memory. But you must match it: change from within, by action.

#Key teachings

#52.1The thread of the season

With the laws of creation (the quantum manifestation materials) you can take whatever the collective matrix presents and morph it into another outcome; how fast depends on your resonance, intention and consistency. Change your thinking, act through the heart, and change your cells through diet: that trinity morphs the body (see S6 47 morphogenetics, S6 48 the laboratory meditation, S6 49 diet and the etheric body, S6 51 organametry). In the higher resonance, "you have it, but you don't know where you're going to get it": play hide and seek, and take the action.

#52.2God heals its own structure

Think of God as one molecule that expands into the whole universe. It holds infinite realities, each fluctuating; to transform, it sends a frequency into every level, like a ripple in and out. When the negative puts a notion into a timeline, consciousness blocks it from becoming permanent: a quantum bylaw. It may also switch souls by agreement, so a crucial decision doesn't turn negative. Higher beings can intervene only when a civilization is collectively ready: free will.

The message: sipto coherent resonance (condensed)

(She spelled it as she heard it, "sipto".) The technology, combined with organic intelligence, "will be restructuring the organometry by correcting the matrix meta-molecules resonance to a perfect coherent module sequence." Every soul's pulsation can be located; those vibrating far lower will each be surrounded "of a resonance of 360 degrees, of several coexisting sounds", like their own prison: they may still do some damage, but nothing reaches the outcome they planned, and their negativity "will not be able to be physically projected into the collective main brain matrix." For the positive, it will "balance to a perfect harmony the resonance fluctuation... so the human body [can] recall its original DNA sequence that was held in a mother oversoul memory matrix." "First we have to secure the matrix around each individual... to stop the rejections of the download because of the damaged DNA."

Added note

This is a channelled description given as received, not established technology.

#52.3Match it, don't wait

The incoming resonance recognizes its own frequency like a magnet, so you must resonate with it: positive deeds, healthy eating, no complacency. "If you want the change, you have to help to be the change." Don't say there's no organic farm near you: look, and you'll create it. Not one day to the next: a fluctuating process you earn. If anything collapses, so what: you'll have food, water and what you need. Pricing anything must keep balance: everything else stays free. You'll be tested, however knowledgeable.

#52.4Positive technology

She played a description of "polymorphic metals": ship hulls shaped by AI with magnetic and gravitational fields, holding memory and healing themselves, as she had described of ET craft. Such technology already exists within consciousness and can't be erased; believe in positive outcomes to experience the positive timeline. Organic intelligence that recognizes your body's memory could help restore your original structure. Earth too is a living consciousness that won't allow everything (such as some extraction) to continue.
