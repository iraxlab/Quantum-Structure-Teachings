Q & A: Evil, Insanity and the Year's Lessons

The difference between evil and insanity, switching out of fear, lucid-dream control, guides and agreements, solar flares, why belief comes before seeing, a look back at the year, and many questions (live talk)

The big idea

Evil has its own logic, a belief system you don't judge; insanity has none, and that is when higher intervention comes. A structure that no longer resonates with expanding consciousness cannot sustain itself. Your part is to keep switching out of fear, and it only works when action and feeling happen together: thinking "I'm happy" without feeling it builds nothing. The same control works in a lucid dream, which is no different from this reality except in perception. You have to believe it to see it, not the other way round. And the toughest lessons are with family: transform them through the structure.

#Key teachings

#27.1You can't reject the fluctuations

Some information is held back so you don't go into fear. One structure goes down as another goes to 5D, and both coexist while the timelines split, so you can't say "this doesn't exist": it's all part of consciousness. Learn to build the bridges through challenges with the quantum manifestation protocol, keeping yourself in high vibrational resonance (see S2 24.2).

Added note

Political predictions and commentary about named politicians and a travel warning for one region are left out; the book stays neutral on politics.

#27.2Evil has reason; insanity has none

"It's not evil, it's insanity." Evil has its own logic: it's someone's belief system, and we don't judge that. But when it goes into no reasoning, doing harm "just because", or "if I go down, you go down", that is insanity, and that is when higher beings get involved, because it's beyond any reason. They can't stop everything, because free will remains, but humanity will be restored.

When contacted by a negative collective, the teacher stayed neutral and compassionate. They told her: "You cannot ask us to extinguish ourselves; that's all we know", comparing it to telling all humanity to stop eating animals overnight. Such a structure, no longer resonating with expanding consciousness, cannot sustain itself: it self-sabotages and goes back to recycling. Continuing to take what it can, even when it could move to its own timeline, "goes into insanity".

From the booksDP: "Evil no more. Insanity in play. Evil has reason. Insanity has not."

#27.3Meditation: the protocol

The teacher meditates little, because she is always connected: her brain has learned to hold several structures at once, talking while receiving an image or a voice. For everyone else, the given protocol is the galactic meditation (see S2 16): put in the blueprint, energize it with emotion and frequency to a high summit, let it go, then act. It's generic: replace the galactic focus with anything you wish. Every emotion and thought goes into the field, and you shift into the next moment on that vibration; doing more of the same crystallizes the outcome.

#27.4Regenerating cells

Regeneration through frequency and sound is in HR: AI merged with frequency and sound measures the cell and reconstructs it back to normal, designed individually, "not the same pill for everybody" (see S2 24.14). Without technology it's very complex: you'd raise your consciousness and mutate your molecular structure through diet, almost to living without food, which is only for those already connected and guided individually, "because you can die from something like that" (see S2 25.7).

Added warning

Do not attempt to live without food. It is dangerous, and especially so with diabetes or any medical condition.

#27.5Switching out of fear: action and feeling together

There's no shortcut: practise. A negative thought or emotion is your consciousness working in perfect synchronicity, showing you that your vibration dropped. Do you want to stay there? Then act: switch your attention to something positive, and at the same time feel the joy. "If I tell you action, I want you to feel it: those two have to go simultaneously"; otherwise you don't build the magnetic and gravitational field. Embed that frequency as if nothing else exists in the now, and hold the emotion and action through the day so it crystallizes into matter. Mastered, you shift not only parallel realities but timelines, without technology, always matching structures similar to your own resonance (see S2 25.9).

#27.6Self-responsibility for what our children know

Look at the whole picture, not one outlet; many people, confronted with something new, attack rather than research. Much of history isn't taught. Don't just point at institutions: "we should point the finger at ourselves and how much we did" to expand our children's thinking. Give them things to read and research. "Self-responsibility, I think, is number one."

#27.7Contacting your guides

Practice and knowledge of the quantum structure come first. Contact comes when your resonance reaches a certain level, usually after testing (see S2 25.2), and differently for everyone: out of body, in dreams, or sometimes a being of light appearing briefly. Stay on target, be the best of yourself, and do more for others: "when you do something for others without a condition, you do it for another version of yourself". That builds the potency, and your guides decide how and when.

#27.8The physical heart is changing

Physical and energetic are one structure. As you take in the new resonances, and act through the heart for others with loving compassion, the heart's frequency rises. Make it your daily practice: what did I do for others today? What did I convert from negative to positive? The more you accumulate, the higher the heart vibrates, until it becomes like a portal: "the heart holds the codes for your particular timeline" (see S2 25.8).

#27.9Solar flares

What some call light blasts are solar flares, which have happened for centuries. When a new energetic field comes from the galactic centre (the zero point of the universal magma), it ripples out like waves of sound, transforming each platform, and each star releases energy as it readjusts. A planet vibrating lower than its neighbours can find this hard to sustain: floods, tsunamis, storms. Earth was slightly off axis compared with the central sun; flares and frequency technologies were used to pull it back into alignment for the ascension protocol, and your awareness helps that too.

From the booksDP: "we can not stop the levels of frequencies that are integrating in your systems because it is coming from the central sun".

#27.10"Nobody is following you"

Asked about entities following someone: "The concept of following you is a false pretense. You're projecting a structure that gives that illusion." If you believe you're followed, you'll see more of it; there's a positive and a negative reality, so which do you choose? "We're never going to give you an answer that gives you fear" (see S2 9.6). One exception was noted: contracts made with the negative side must be finished or cancelled, a process not discussed here. Questions about possession and the astral are not the teacher's area.

Added note

If you feel watched or followed and it's distressing, please talk with a doctor or someone you trust.

#27.11Taking control in lucid dreams

Wild, vivid dreams? "Then learn to control them." Be calm and focused. Find your key code: the sign that keeps recurring when you're lucid (for the teacher, everything going black). Once you recognize it, ask yourself to open a portal, fully focused, not in panic; on her second focused attempt it opened and she went through into another reality. "There's no difference between this reality and the lucid one; only the perception." To control this reality the same way, you must embed it 100%, which takes practice and mastery. If dreams jump from scene to scene, pull back and start directing them. And you may still be sent out of some realities (see S2 21.11, S2 22).

#27.12Guides, agreements and what you can accept

Are messages from guides just us talking to ourselves? "Yes and no, depending on agreements." You project the form you can accept: for some it's angels, for others extraterrestrials, because some would panic at one and not the other. A teacher may say "God tells me" when the source might be another kind of being, so as not to frighten people. Your agreements decide which form you receive. When messages come, write them down; with practice you'll find the line between your own thoughts and the ones that come immediately. Catch those, however strange: belief or fear can interfere and blur the signal, so stay open, without judgement.

#27.13Believe it to see it

Asked for proof, or why the source doesn't simply materialize: "I'm here to present, not to convince"; everyone comes by free will. A being of much higher frequency can't materialize before you until you've embedded that frequency, and doubt keeps anything from materializing. "You don't see it to believe it: you have to believe it to see it. That is the quantum structure." Waiting for proof means watching the mirror image control your reality.

#27.14Everyone has a soul

"Everybody has a soul": what differs is potency and vibration. A consciousness that did very negative things resonates at a certain sequence; never having accumulated positive structures through action, it can't match 5D naturally. It can only create artificial timelines through technology. A positive consciousness can lower itself to meet, unpleasantly, in a space created between the two realities: 1 + 1 = 3 again.

#27.15Roadblocks expand your understanding

"The roadblocks are expansion of your understanding." Each teaches a lesson; meet it with more commitment to learning. Without challenges you'd never learn how to construct, or how to shift timelines. Always ask: what can I do to transform this into positive? Then use the protocol (see S1 1–3 and Stop, Transform, Move On, S1 7).

#27.16Looking back at the year

It brought a great awakening for many, and a great separation of belief systems. Even when everything seemed to be going downhill, redirecting thoughts and emotions to the positive transformed it. The toughest lessons are with family, because they're close to home and hard to step back from; a co-worker is easier. Tests not passed will be repeated: "don't worry about it", transform them through the structure. Detox is fundamental for everyone. Much will be released step by step, as people can understand it; each person has their own ascension experience. "Stay on target, be your best."

#The method — Switching Out of Fear

  1. Notice the negative thought or emotion: it's the signal your vibration dropped (27.5).
  2. Choose: do you want to stay there? If not, act now.
  3. Act: turn your attention to something positive, something you watch or do.
  4. Feel it at the same time: the joy, not just the thought.
  5. Hold it through the day, as if nothing else exists in the now.
  6. Repeat every time it drops: practice is the only way.
