Channeling the Council of Nine: Q & A

A direct channel: motherhood as a fractal, power used two ways, why meditation doesn't replace action, a seed practice for your own food, what "above your reality" means, how lessons find your weakest link, and how to keep overriding a loved one's negative version (live talk)

The big idea

The council kept returning to the same correction: many questions "miss the structure". Fear asked as a question creates the feared reality; meditation does not replace physical action; and you never change another person, you change your frequency and reprint a new version of them, again and again, until it collapses into the positive (often through someone else's words). Power itself is neutral: greed for more giving is still power, used well. And the lessons that come to each person target their weakest link.

#Key teachings

#13.1Motherhood is a fractal

"In a daily basis you are expressing the motherhood that is coming from your higher self." It is connected with the one particle, expressing itself fractally: Mother Planet, Mother Nature, your mother, you as a mother. To honour it, don't expect what others will do for you; ask how you can be the best of yourself for your family today, "because when you give to others, it's another version of you."

#13.2Power, used two ways

Someone felt a pleasure of power in reacting meanly. Recognizing that split is already the choice to rebuild the behaviour: next time, choose the positive. "In power you can have greed, or positive power: to give more, to share more, to love more. That's greedy, but in a positive way." The intention decides (see S3 10.8).

#13.3Meditation doesn't replace action

Can a high enough vibration turn unhealthy food healthy? "Are you trying not to do your physical work? You must take an action. Then why are you here?" You accumulate resonance, but consciousness has to choose not to put in what isn't beneficial. Decalcifying the pineal gland likewise takes both: gradual detox suited to your body (juicing, water, herbalism, elimination diets) together with conscious understanding, so the new codes work with your action.

#13.4Love doesn't go through fear

A grandmother anxious for her grandson: "Fear will create more fear." Go through it as an experience, with understanding, and the lessons complete with ease and the next step appears. "Love does not have to go through fear. Caring does not go through fear. Just be." Asking from fear (about any feared future) projects it: pull away, create the reality you want, and if you act, act from understanding and your own power, because action taken from fear "comes back in vengeance".

#13.5Knowing the dark to master the light

Many humans have lineage in old galactic wars: "you needed to go to the darkness to understand what light is", so you can be a master who holds the frequency in neutrality. Now release it, and act on beauty, joy and freedom. A dark message received in automatic writing works the same way: it matched your vibration at that moment, like a wrong number; you don't have to take it as the answer. Note it and move on.

#13.6A seed carries your DNA

A practice for growing food: hold a pure, organic, non-modified seed under your tongue for at least a minute, so it takes your saliva and DNA, then plant it with water. The plant is said to carry a particle of your DNA, so what it grows has healing properties designed for you. For dry climates: good irrigation, pure water, and technologies that collect water from the air.

#13.7What "above your reality" means

It means density: the magnetic and gravitational structure. One dream is light, and you move easily; another is dense. "Above" is a higher spin of the molecular structure of your understanding. When the gap is large, it's hard to mutate or bilocate to that level, which is why you need "absolute, 100% knowledge" of how your field works: splitting, collapsing and combining realities (see S2 4).

#13.8How you are chosen, and tested

Contact and guidance follow the energy you accumulate through your lessons: what you did in your hardest moments, and what you did when you had power to destroy and chose the heart instead. If you break a contract (greed, money), the information drops or another takes the place; teachers switch according to how you do. "Each individual is going to get the lesson that is the weakest link in their personality", without warning (see S3 4.2). Guidance gives little about your galactic family until it can be proven, to keep its integrity.

#13.9Keep overriding a loved one's version

If your spouse stays in the negative, remember it's a version you projected. Imagine, without conditions, that he understands everything, and hold it. When you see the old behaviour again, don't panic: project again, and again; each time you override and collapse toward the positive. Often the change arrives through someone else (his mother, a co-worker): stay neutral about where it comes from. "You are not changing him; you are changing your frequency" (see S3 10.4). And the same principle frees you from the past: the version of reality in which someone harmed you "has nothing to do with who you are right now."

Important

This is not for abuse. The teacher was clear: if someone is physically or mentally abusing you, it's your higher self telling you to wake up and take your sovereignty back: take action and get out (see 13.10).

#13.10When you feel trapped and afraid

Asked by someone who feels physically threatened: "then leave". Fear stops you seeing the solutions; knowing 100% that you will gain your freedom, and starting to look for solutions, creates situations that help. Practical support: watch stories of people who freed themselves, to take in that resonance; call the helplines for people in this situation; and get legal advice, especially where children are involved.

Support

If you feel threatened by a partner, in the US call the National Domestic Violence Hotline, 1-800-799-7233 (or text START to 88788); in danger, call 911. Elsewhere, contact your local domestic-abuse service.

#The method — Reprinting a Loved One

  1. Remember: the person you see is a version you're projecting.
  2. Imagine, without conditions, that they already understand.
  3. Hold that feeling in meditation and through the day.
  4. When the old behaviour returns, don't panic: project again (13.9).
  5. Repeat, over and over; let the change come from anywhere.
  6. If there is abuse, act: safety first (13.10).

See 13.3.
How do we wake others without spreading negativity?
Once awake, study the quantum structure: hold your vibration from the higher view while you override the old holograms. "You have to know it, feel it, create it to see it."
Will those who don't wake up suffer?
Each experiences what they project; but you can project a positive version of them, while their other version stays where it chose (see S3 10.4).
Is a new web of satellites good or bad?
Everything unfolds as it needs to and humanity moves forward; believe it's negative and you project that.

See 13.2.
Who put an implant in me?

Sensations behind my heart, and high tones?
Massage for nodules and stretching yoga; the tones are higher-self activation coming down the spine.
What do old galactic wars have to do with my path?
See 13.5.
Why am I so anxious about my grandson's health?
See 13.4.
Can meditation make unhealthy food healthy?
See 13.3.
What is the origin of Rh-negative blood?
Described as DNA of extraterrestrial design, a fast-spinning resonance above the planet's 3D frequency; a blood cleansing is coming for many, with frequent urination or joint aches as signs of flushing.
Added note

Frequent urination or persistent joint pain can have medical causes: check with a doctor.

Since my near-death experience I remember more. How do I open further?
"Trust the process. Have no fear."
Why do my views and emotions swing to extremes?
Fluctuation: find your centre of neutrality by observing, and train to hold one emotion steadily (see S3 11.3).
How can I have a smoother experience on water-only days?
It's a training normally guided by your own guides; the teacher detoxed for a year and juiced before water. Without that, "take it with caution or medical supervision".
Added safety note

Water-only fasting can be dangerous; talk to your doctor first, and don't attempt to live without food.

Should I learn from star beings or my higher self?
Whatever you truly wish: expansion is always good.

See 13.6.

See 13.7.
Why is life stricter in one country than another?
A nation under constant restriction takes on that resonance; one given choices projects freedom. Stronger souls are often born where it's harder, to lead the rest.
In a dream I merged with a little girl. What was it?
Another version of herself: go back into it for more.
I can't go deep in meditation and was told I'm not connected to my body.
Taking that as true makes it your resonance: know and feel that you are connected, and create it as you go.
How can I connect with the council myself?
See 13.8.
My son struggles with anxiety and self-worth.
See him healthy and whole, take every practical step, and see the lesson rather than self-pity. Any change to his medication must be with his doctor: stopping can shut the system down.
Since the hourly practice I see more number sequences and animals near me.
Don't hold on to the numbers; notice what you're doing when they appear: they show your location in the structure, designed for you.
Some days I barely eat and feel amazing. Is that normal?
Daily giving raises your resonance, so appetite can drop; give more, and you grab more of the same.
Added note

Keep eating enough to stay healthy; if appetite stays low, or eating feels hard, talk to a doctor.
