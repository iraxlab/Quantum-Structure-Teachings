The big idea

Knowing this mechanism is knowing how you operate your own reality, and with total understanding you pass through the zero point with precision and manifest faster.

Yes. The smallest holds the same potency as the largest; size is an illusion. Zooming in, all is balanced in one singular particle.

From the field of your own consciousness. To interpret, use gematria, or an AI to turn numbers into words or sound. Meanings vary; if one seems negative, don't take it as negative: switch it and create anew.
