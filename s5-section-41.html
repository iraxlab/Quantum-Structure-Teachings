Solitary Distribution / Energy Philanthropy

The quantum structure of giving: the torus field that feeds back whatever you project, why a price on a person slows what returns to you, what "solitary distribution" means, why only taking depletes the taker, and questions on giving with the right attitude and on when to stop giving (talk)

The big idea

Everything you project from the heart and mind is a sound wave that becomes a hologram; your field is a torus that feeds the same frequency back to you, endlessly. Energy philanthropy means giving freely into the field without putting a price or condition on any one person; the return then comes from anywhere, in any form, and faster. Solitary distribution is the other half: when something is given to you freely, you give back to it of your own free will when you can. Only taking leaves an open hole in your own field, so abundance cannot return to you.

#Key teachings

#41.1The torus field feeds back what you project

Between the heart and the mind each thought, feeling and deed becomes a sound wave, then a hologram. From one particle it expands into your field and the collective, and the field mimics that frequency back to you. Each time, you shift into a universe that copies the previous vibration, then collapse it. Separated out, the motion is a torus: it twists out from you at the centre and returns, a perpetual motion that never stops. Negative or positive, it feeds back the same. (On the hologram and reprinting, see S5 34.)

#41.2Energy philanthropy: no price on a person

Money is also an energy field and a hologram. If you give from the heart into the field, the return can come back as money too, but you don't know from where. Putting a fixed price on a person plasters a condition onto your field: the return must then pass through that same money hologram, so it comes slower and harder. Without the condition, giving from the heart is a higher-resonance timeline that shrinks the time of manifestation. If the person you gave to chooses not to give back, it returns through someone else. This is also how you raise your frequency: feed the field without condition, and support what you see doing good.

#41.3Don't picture only money

Imagine having it: when you imagine abundance, imagine that you have what you need, not only the money for it. It may arrive as the thing itself: a couch, a car, a house given by family, the same value in matter. The quantum field holds infinite probabilities for how it comes. Building that potency took her two years of constant giving without a price on anyone.

#41.4Solitary distribution

Her Council of Nine's definition: you willingly take part in a platform that is freely given, and you contribute back of your free will. To have full access to it in your field, you must give the same frequency back; this engages the hologram to reflect the same resonance to you, back and forth through the torus, and the potency keeps building. Only taking, without ever giving back when you have, depletes your own frequency: "Do not be surprised that you do not have abundance when you never proclaim the giving feedback first." They measured that only about 3% of the collective support what is freely given.

#41.5Entitlement and the one who starts

Their image: one person works the field and feeds the whole family while the others eat the fruit and never help. Any system where people learn only to receive and never give back teaches entitlement and waiting for "somebody else" to act. But that somebody else is a projection in your own hologram: waiting gives your power away. The lesson she drew from a story: when one person starts doing something good for all (a garden in front of a neglected building), others join one by one, and the whole place changes.

Added note

She also illustrated this with comments on welfare systems and on one country's government; they are kept here only as the teaching they illustrate, in keeping with this book's neutral approach.

#41.6Taking only harms the taker

When you keep taking from someone who keeps giving, you deplete your own structure, not theirs: it becomes "vampiric", the same as being used yourself. Meditation alone isn't enough: distribution has to be a physical action, done consistently from the heart, to the people and platforms that help you, "at least once a month or at least once a year." If you have nothing to give, do something physical and positive for someone. Your individual ascension is how you help the collective, because the one particle expands into the whole.

#41.7Stop, transform and move on

When she panicked and felt sorry for herself, they stopped her mid-moment: self-pity feeds your field with a lower frequency. "It does not matter if you need $1 or $1 million. It's an illusion, it's a hologram." Stop the thought, replace it with awareness, calm down, take a different action. Consistent action mobilizes the right situation, people and money (S1). Even when the opportunity arrives, reality can still split if one party acts differently, so keep your integrity and keep your promises through the challenges.

#The method — giving without condition

  1. Give: into the field from the heart (knowledge, help, time), without putting a price or condition on any one person.
  2. Don't watch: who gives back; attaching to it lowers your vibration.
  3. Imagine having it: when you imagine abundance, imagine having what you need, not only the money; let it come in any form.
  4. Support: when something is given to you freely and helps you, give back to it of your free will when you can.
  5. Act: if you have no money, do something physical and positive for someone, consistently.
  6. Stop self-pity: when it starts, stop, transform, move on (41.7).
  7. Feel it: notice the goosebumps of joy when you give freely; that is the resonance to accumulate (Questions).
Her instructions for starting energy philanthropy

If you are the main provider, don't jump straight into it. Keep a job, done with gratitude, to support daily life, and once or twice a week use energy philanthropy: give without knowing whether people will donate, leaving them their free will. Over time, the structure "from the free will" builds in you and overrides the need to charge; it can take about two years of non-stop work from the heart. If you do charge, "charge with reason": never so much that a person's family suffers, because "you cannot put a price in a human suffering." For beginners she points to the manifestation potency protocol (S4 E9) and Quantum Manifestation episodes 1–5.

#Questions answered

Does the giver's attitude matter: giving begrudgingly versus lovingly?
Giving still has a positive effect, but the frequency to accumulate is the one you feel when you give freely out of love and appreciation: goosebumps of joy, moving like a torus from your feet and heart into your hands. That resonance opens the portals of the heart into the ascension timeline that already exists, and you match it. When a person gives what they can, without pressure, giver and receiver are coherent and both move into a better vibration; the receiver becomes a giver too. If you don't have money, distribute to society.
Can you deplete yourself by constantly giving? When should you stop giving to someone?
Don't look at who gives back; she was told to keep feeding the field without checking, because it hurt her and attached her to the hologram. She still does her job for everyone, but she no longer works with people who are disrespectful to her platform or who come again and again into her personal space while only taking. Money isn't negative; the way it's used to extort people is. Charge, if you must, with reason (see the box above). Some will learn and some never will; the loudest critics often give nothing to society.
