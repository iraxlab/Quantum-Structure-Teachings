The big idea

A code read forwards and then backwards erases a hologram back to zero point, a clean state, and a new code is then put in its place. This one is for the collective, to erase structures put in without your knowledge or permission: "We give you a clean state, and now create as you wish." As things are cleansed, what's off balance will come up in front of you, so you can fix it.

#Key teachings

#39.1Backwards erases to zero

The new instruction: read the sequence, then reverse it, reading backwards to the beginning. "This way you're actually erasing the hologram, and it becomes zero point, like a clean state." Inside such codes, 3 Hz cleans the holographic imprint in front of you (see S3 36.3); with potency and consistency it cleans the entire structure. So where you see threes, something is being cleaned out and something new put in. Then a new code is implemented on the clean slate.

"Sector one is ready. In the next 13 days this code will penetrate and erase all the structures that were implemented without your knowledge or permission."

#39.3After a cleanse, a challenge may come

When things are cleansed, "you may have some challenge coming in front of you": whatever is off balance is presented so you can fix it. Don't panic; hold your ground; use the quantum manifestation knowledge, and transform it with positive action, thought and deed, so you neutralize it and clean the state. Then ask, as the council taught her: What did I learn? Did I overcome it? "How can I express that in the name of others?" Not for fame or money: those come by themselves when you stay in the heart.

#39.4Squeezed to survive, a society imprints

Where people are left only a couple of options to survive, a society can become conniving: stealing and lying turn normal, even admired, and accumulate karma (see S3 31.7). "And again, it's an illusion: you always have a choice." Knowing that you create your reality is what lets you choose otherwise.

#39.5Don't ignore your wake-up cue

Consciousness is raising everyone's vibration; those still asleep will get their own awakening situation. When it comes, "it's almost like waking up out of a deep trance". "That is your cue for moving forward." Don't push it under the rug because it concerns someone you admire or a side you prefer: do the research, peel the layers, set aside ego, and "choose through the heart" (see S3 36.7). Ignoring it turns you the wrong way.

#39.6Numbers work in any language

"Mathematics is universal": the moment you see the number, its geometry penetrates; that's why codes are given as numbers rather than words, which would carry a different numerology in each language (see S3 34). Repeat only if you wish; don't worry if you misread one: once you've consented, it's in your hologram, "because you're the one putting the numbers there". You're projecting her, too.

#39.7A burst of merging realities

Asked about a much-discussed date: when two realities merge and reach zero-point synchronicity, they collapse into one timeline with "a burst of energetic field, like sparks" that penetrates every particle (see S3 31.8). The high resonance is downloaded and at the same time pulls you up. It's an activation, "like awakening out of a deep trance", followed by codes after codes; and it's "subtle, very subtle", so the body can handle it.

#39.8Practising lucid dreaming

Dreams are "simply another reality"; what you can do in a lucid dream you can do here: "the only difference is your belief system". What you construct there can be carried over into now. Her tips:

  1. Study daily for a couple of weeks, from both books and videos: the words and the voice each imprint.
  2. Train waking naturally: meditate before sleep on waking every two and a half hours, so you move from alpha toward the dream state aware.
  3. If you leave the body, go forward, never backwards: the angle decides which reality you enter.
  4. Stay calm and in the heart: not stressed, not over-excited, so you hold the resonance.
  5. Ask your questions there; a portal may open, or someone may appear.
  6. If you're thrown out, it wasn't your time: don't be discouraged.

Shutting a program in a lucid dream needs the councils' approval. Practising this, they said, can raise your trust in what you're capable of by about 30% (see S3 36).

#39.9Perception, and observing in neutrality

How do you find your purpose? Purpose and perception are illusion: what's beautiful in one culture isn't in another, and skin colour is "just a colour, designed from different extraterrestrial races", used to separate. To see clearly, embrace your whole hologram daily: watch the people around you with love and "observe. Observation is neutrality": no judgement, only understanding. Let each "molecule" express itself and progress naturally; "create through the beauty of understanding, not through conquering and overtaking".

#39.10Everyone is learning

There's always someone who knows less and someone who knows more: she learns from the council, the council from a higher order (the priesthoods), extraterrestrials from the council, "and do you really think we didn't learn from your experiences?" Information is given drop by drop and sometimes modified to suit your understanding. Even an answer in code is an answer: "you got your answer, you don't even know it".

#The method — Making a Clean State

  1. Consent, then read the forward code aloud (39.2).
  2. Read it backwards, slowly, to the end: a full stop at zero.
  3. Read the new code: 333 777 444 111 777 999, complete.
  4. Allow about 13 days for it to penetrate.
  5. Meet any challenge that comes up calmly: fix what is off balance (39.3).
  6. Create as you wish on the clean state: thought, action, deed, from the heart.
