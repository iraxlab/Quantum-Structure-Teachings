Ashtar Command Message to Us

A first, unasked-for contact from the Ashtar Command and its message; and many questions: losing memories as you shift, what a new human is, the Ashtar Command's own words, resistance to meditation, A.I., love between partners, nanotechnology, lucid dreaming and tiredness (live talk, September 2021)

The big idea

The Ashtar Command, a global alliance of races within the Galactic Federation, contacted her directly: the old structures will crumble from within as their own people awaken, and new systems and technologies will replace them. "The most stronger will continue... Stay strong, stay vigilant, stay compassionate, and be a warrior." As you shift timelines, people and memories that don't resonate fall away; you become a new human, moment by moment and in your DNA. This is the chance of many lifetimes: "there will be not another choice as this one."

#Key teachings

For the anniversary of 11 September she asked people to have a "Marina day" (see S4 29), putting positive frequency into consciousness to honour those who died. She played the 1977 British television broadcast interrupted by a voice speaking for the Ashtar Galactic Command, "overriding the frequency" without shutting down the system, which warned of false guides and called humanity to live in peace.

The message of the Ashtar Command

In the years to come, those responsible "will be prosecuted in the highest degree", and the mainstream media will be shut down systematically. The planned restrictions will increase, but the "premeditated agenda... is going to go wrong": "the following dark minions of the dark cabal will start breaking their orders due to awakening within their souls." The global structure "will crumble from within"; institutions will collapse under the pressure.

Within three to four years, new systems and technologies will replace the infrastructure: "do not speculate that you know what the tech is. It is new, period" (to protect those doing the work). "You all going to be smarter, wiser, stronger and much more knowledgeable." Many will not make it; "all that was by agreements on individual levels and in group levels." "The most stronger will continue because they are the ones to be able to sustain the pressure."

"Calibrations are given from us to the chosen people to correct the humans in their awakening." "All the fleets are in position to help all the humans because your last battle has begun. Stay strong, stay vigilant, stay compassionate, and be a warrior." (Dates in the message are left out; she allows six months to a year either way.)

Know your rights. She pointed to the Nuremberg Code and to working with lawyers and doctors who know the law, and asked employers to think about what they impose out of fear.

#Questions answered

Spirit seems to be taking away people, experiences and even my job, and I'm losing memories of my past. What's happening?
You are shifting realities. As your heart raises your frequency, people and memories that don't resonate no longer occur in your timeline; someone asks "do you remember...?" and it isn't there, because you're on a brand-new earth whose memory bank doesn't hold it. "Congratulate yourself." Sometimes the new beginning means pulling away from institutions that pressure you; find others who think as you do, and another solution. "You have to create it to see it."
Added note

Memory loss can also have medical causes. If you notice it, please see your doctor as well.

Is the Ashtar Command part of the Galactic Federation?
Yes, and interstellar, working with many worlds; it is in position here "because we entered the real war."
How far are we from the great awakening, and how will it look for families where some are asleep?
"You are already in the great awakening." Everyone will be challenged personally to act, because the old system must be erased: "not one particle has to stay from the old reality." The process makes you stronger. Family members will project their own version; some may leave your reality, even your memory. "There will be not another choice as this one right now."
What's the difference between the Council of Five and the Council of Nine?
Councils form around a person's lineage, connections and frequency through different planetary systems, so different people download from what they call different councils.
What does "new human" mean?
Every moment you shift, you are a new person (see S1 2). It is also in the DNA: raising the body's frequency, through technology or consciousness, makes the DNA resonate higher; your perimeter widens, you reach more spectrums of reality, and perceive spectrums of light you weren't told exist.
The Ashtar Command answers: how we help

"The Ashtar Command is a global system of different races." From this planet they learned "that even a small system as yours can become so dark... that can create a ripple effect even through the systems that are highest resonances", so no planet should be left outside the galactic family. "We have rules not to interfere. However, when a consciousness of a planet as yours is awakened and wishes to free itself... no other consciousness has right to suppress that evolution." "We wish for you to start thinking of us as friends." Physical contact will not be allowed until humanity's frequency reaches a certain resonance. "We will pick the ones that are the strongest. They will be the new pillars of the beginning of the new age of the Aquarius."

I'm resistant to meditation. How do I overcome it?
There is a blockage in the third layer of the lower abdomen. Detox (see the Detox and Juicing Guide); know you have no blockages; in meditation, put white light into that place and transform it with the feeling: "the feeling and the wish of the mind is what is transforming the structure."
What if A.I. was self-produced?
Once made, A.I. learns from itself and creates its own language and evolution. "Why the fear?" You don't have to be part of it, but it coexists. Teach what you create to be positive, "to give you a service, not a disservice": like reverse osmosis, what you give comes back (see S4 30).
Is twin-flame union part of ascension?
Love between partners from the heart, of any gender, is a different structure from desire alone; "when it is true love between the structure of the heart, realities are created... very potent."
I'm having up to eight dreams a night. Is it messaging?
Part of it. Learn to be lucid in them, and you'll interact and receive more.
Are spike proteins and nanoparticles the same?
Very different (she declined to say more because of platform rules). A nanobot can be designed with a code; it can carry DNA to a place and release it, changing the DNA for good or ill, or sit between the villi and block a protein; blood can be changed too, over months. Like atomic energy, it can build or destroy; she would use it only to help.
Does lucid dreaming teach us to deconstruct the 3D laws?
Yes, it becomes fluid. A lucid dream is another reality, less dense, where the 3D code "nothing like this is possible" is missing, so you bilocate freely. That code was projected into you until it erased the original blueprint; mutate and replace it, and telepathic bilocation becomes possible. You'll still use technology too: a spacecraft that responds only to your thoughts (she was trained to stop one with her mind).
I feel fatigue, aches and soreness for no reason. What's happening to our bodies?
Raising frequency can make you sleepy, not hungry, exhausted for a couple of days. First make sure you're not sick. If all checks out, it takes one to three days for the frequency to embed in the molecules: "rest, rest and rest", so the energy isn't spent elsewhere.
If I've shifted and lost a memory, how can others remember it?
Fractals of the previous timeline leak into the new one through their expression; but in you "the code was replaced. Think about computer code."
