And In The End Of Times / Angels

A message about the angels, read like a page from a book of the end of times, the spear of destiny as every warrior's own spirit, a prayer and code for the angelic portal, and questions on knowing your frequency is enough, finding your mission, and whether the angels are of God or ET, ending in a guided visualization

The big idea

The angelic realm is mobilizing: after the ETs declared war, the angels "came from the heaven and gave to all its soldiers the spear of destiny." The spear of destiny isn't an object: it's your own spirit, your pulsation held like steel and pointed like a laser, and every warrior holds one. Angels exist between the packets of consciousness, an unchanging backup reality that keeps records and protects you when it isn't your time to exit. Prayer reaches them directly, but only prayer joined with action becomes matter: "we are not there to win the battle for you."

#Key teachings

#45.1Being tested to lead

This time is a testing ground to find the strongest souls, from whom leaders will be chosen for the next level of evolution. Bank and system changes will come in fluctuation, one bank down and another up, because the positive side won't allow a global collapse (be cautious with anyone telling you what or how to invest). Each person's experience differs with their vibration. See her two earlier spear-of-destiny talks (Season 4 Episode 8; Season 5 Episode 29).

#45.2The spear of destiny

It isn't a metal relic: it's the human spirit, your vibration held like steel, which with thoughts, actions and deeds becomes a very strong laser. Not one spear but the spears of destiny: every individual who stands their ground is one, and together the collective is one unit. Look at such things esoterically: the literal reading is part of the manipulation.

The message: in the end of times (condensed)

"The angels came from the heaven and gave to all its soldiers the spear of destiny. Each soldier had a power of lightning that pierced through the darkness and created the new destiny for the human race." True humans preserve God's laws: "do not steal, do not kill, do not lie, do not manipulate. Love is the answer." The evil felt the spear's ripple and began "to feel the pain that they caused." "The angels stood and waited and protected all the humans... the humans woke up... the children were the new age of dawn... The sick became healthy. The evil became awaken. The love spread through the land." "The golden age has started... Armor of angels shield you all have... prepare for your final battle."

Her instructions: The prayer to the angels (as given)

"Dear angels, we ask for your assistance now. It is time to take our freedom back, to lighten our souls again with the true power of God. We ask you, angels, to come to each true human soul and protect us with your power and wisdom, so our consciousness and our thoughts become one with yours, so it is one that is the creator. We pray for you, angels, to give us all the strength of yours, to merge with ours. We pray, and we pray, and we pray, and so it is."

Her instructions: The closing visualization (as led)

"We are already here, dear ones." Spread the wings they gave you: flap once, twice, three times, and feel the power. Breathe it in. Take your spear and slam it into the darkness; see light spread until the whole globe is bright white-blue, pulsating. Hold your wings like a warrior around the planet and keep projecting that power. When you wake, know you carry their power: "you are the spear of destiny."

#Questions answered

How do we know our frequency is high enough?
Don't ask whether it's enough: you simply are it. Embed the thought that you have the power now, and act from that moment: "so it is."
How can an angel help me know my mission?
Merge your thoughts with them instead of only asking. Write down the first thought that comes. Pray to them three times a day for ten days: you're already connected, but you must create the belief that it's there.
Are these angels from God's angelic realm, or galactic ETs?
Two parts: an etheric realm, and ETs whose higher consciousness may be angelic. The angelic realm lives in the steady pockets between consciousness's spinning timelines: ancient, unchanging, holding the memory of all consciousness. Each person has an assigned angel, who may delay you five minutes or lift you from a car accident when it isn't your time. They see everything, even secrets, down to your DNA's past, and must cleanse after entering dense realms. "The prayer from you, only the prayer without the action, can reach us. But in order your prayer to become reality... you must connect that prayer with the action."

Left out: platform notices, political remarks, and banking speculation beyond its caution.
