The big idea

You are creators, and you can restore any reality simply by putting the intention: replace the thought and let nothing else exist; imagine it, make it true in your mind, and believe 100%. Only 28% of the collective can do this now, but it will spread "like a wildfire" that warms rather than burns. Even a small act of love or a smile can change your whole reality, because one particle holds the universe. Even evil has its place: to teach you to choose and convert resonances until you are a master. You have made it: come home to the stars.

#Key teachings

#15.1Traditions and the field

She respects all beliefs, but suggests looking into the origins of some traditions and celebrating in your own way with family, so you don't feed old Archon frequencies.

#15.2The shield around Earth

She was shown a shield around the Earth in three layers, with tiny particles between them. Whatever penetrates from a solar flare is grabbed and converted by the particles, layer by layer; what leaks through becomes droplets of a water-like substance that clean the outer ozone layer by a chain reaction. "This is why you have not gotten so sick." Asked about a flat Earth, the Council's answer was: "the Earth is a 3D holographic energetic field." Stop worrying about solar flares and flat earth: you'll experience what you believe.

#15.3How she receives

Sometimes pictures, sometimes a whole download from one question, sometimes direct channeling. "What I do, you can do. I'm just ahead of the class"; don't put her on a pedestal.

The message: children of the new dawn (condensed)

"Into the horizon, history contributed to all of the realities... Commander, you can restore all people, what they do, by just putting the intention. How is this possible? Simply replace the thought and let nothing else exist. This way 100% precision will be in the holographic reality that you project... All is in the now... Imagine, create a story and visualize it, make it true in your mind, and then believe 100% that it is true. Practice, practice, practice." Only 28% can do this collectively now, but in time it will spread like a wildfire, "comforting and warm... It will never burn you, only if you let it go free." The mutation from one resonance to another is the challenging part, only in the beginning. Love has infinite frequencies: potent, light, flame, peace. "Sometimes the smallest love and slight smile can change the world." Even evil is in alignment within itself: "to learn, to see, to become as strong as steel... When you master it, then and only then can you call yourself a master. Children of the New Dawn, please relax and take your rightful place among the stars... You have made it. Congratulations."

#15.4Why you still have to act

As the frequency increases, a body that can't hold it starts to decay; you'll be pushed by signs (bloating, inflammation). Keep trying with the diet; your higher self will guide a protocol specific to you. She was told that within 30 days of such a diet the cells can look 20 years younger. Stop worrying about ageing.

#Questions answered

Does the chemical reaction also purify our lungs?
It makes the atmosphere more breathable, not directly healing; "thanks to that, you are preserved."
Does water have its own consciousness? Can it answer questions?
Water has three particles: one receives, one memorizes, one conducts and projects. It mutates in the field and interacts with your body, a living waveform battery. Purified water without charge converts according to your field and flushes out what isn't positive; it's a recommendation, not to be drunk exclusively or all the time. Other waters (with a little selenium, say) are fine as professionals advise.
Describe the astral body and how the physical body adjusts to the astral light body.
They are synchronized frequencies, one fractal converting into another, always switching. The astral can open portals if your consciousness can hold it, but be cautious which realities you enter: some aren't positive for you. Learn lucid dreaming first: you stay linked by a gold cord and snap back. Bilocation needs mastery and 100% precision; the bond feels physical.
What's the difference between neutrality and positive emotion?
First recognize the hologram by your emotion; then observe it like a film (a shield), until calm brings zero point. Then analyse, and only then bring your thoughts, emotions and actions back in to convert it. If you can't calm down, step away, sing, listen to a song. A sudden blast may make you react strongly; don't resist it: allow, understand, mutate. Body, mind and consciousness in one unit become a laser that links with your higher self, so you sense ahead what's good for you. 440 Hz music combined with negative imagery is especially harmful.
Are we here to learn a new way of creating, by intention?
Yes: you are the creator, a painter making a different picture each time.
How do I know when to let go instead of keep trying?
Never stop trying, but don't be too harsh: take a breather, enjoy the scenery, recharge, then go again with more power.
How can we read the present energy and predict for ourselves?
Predictions are probabilities; even the who and how can shift (five or six months may become a year). Leave room for playfulness: it's a game. Fear puts branches across your own path.
What's the difference between the astral world and out-of-body experience?
The astral body is above your physical structure; "out of reality" is the multiple realities running at once in your field, which you don't see because you can't yet convert their sequences (as some see the dead). Be cautious where you travel.
My adult son is very disconnected from his higher self. Any advice?
Difficult experiences may continue for up to about three years. Love him enough to let him choose, even with bruises; "allow the little bird to become an eagle." Age is an illusion; education doesn't equal awareness. Help and observe, but leave room to learn.
