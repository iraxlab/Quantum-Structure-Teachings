The big idea

The hybrids who first contacted her said: "Be careful what you think; it becomes a hologram in front of us." They are benevolent but largely mechanical: their race became so technological it could no longer hold a soul or draw energy from source, so they help humanity regain original DNA. Other species have moved and stripped souls and used Earth as a farm, harvesting human soul energy like a battery. The way out is to raise the body's frequency (detox ascension, diet, behavior and the protocols) so you no longer match their timeline and can draw from prana yourself. Create above the structure, stop looping in fear, and command your mind.

#Key teachings

#39.1How her contact began

In 2010 the hybrids contacted her first: "Marina, be careful what you're thinking, because whatever you think becomes like a hologram in front of us." They awakened her, taught her telepathy and an elimination diet; then came Ra, the Council of Nine and the Pleiadians, each teaching what she needed at the time.

#39.2Detox ascension

Detox ascension restructures your molecules above the 3D structure: DNA takes more from what you consume, crystallizes and spins faster, drawing more energy from the quantum field and becoming more incandescent, with the heart and bio-field converting to plasma energy. Then the body can hold the vibration of high-dimensional beings: channeling, out-of-body experience. The heart holds the codes for your predetermined stargates; she describes her heart chakra being renewed after a juice period.

Added safety note

Added safety note: she describes her own regimen (an elimination diet over eight months down to juice and then water, a 14-day distilled-water fast she credits with dissolving gallstones, a daily green-juice protocol, and daily castor oil), stating it is not a recommendation. Water-only and long juice fasts can be dangerous; gallstones need medical care. Check with a doctor before any fast or detox.

#39.3The hybrids

They told her they are more mechanical, built partly from technology, but benevolent, and everything they taught her was for ascension. A race from a future timeline became so technological it could no longer hold a soul or reproduce, unable to draw enough energy from source. Different channelers connect with different parallel timelines, so experiences of hybrids vary.

#39.4Souls moved and stripped

As in S5 (the hybrids' message about abused children), a soul can be lifted out and held floating. She connects this with others' accounts of "soul stripping": fragments of souls taken and embedded in other bodies, including people with reptilian structure, and Earth used as a farm whose soul energy is harvested like a battery. Films she recommends as showing this: Jupiter Ascending and The Host.

#39.5Free will over origin

Some people carry a reptilian spark, but by free will can choose not to behave that way. In consciousness nothing is simply good or evil; it learns, and to know the light you must know the dark. The positive works by the power of ten because when evil gets off balance, consciousness rebalances it.

#39.6Why raise the frequency

Those feeding on us survive only in this timeline, while consciousness moves to a higher one. The grid around the planet was shut down and you are free to ascend, but without the protocols, a proper diet (no animal products, in her teaching) and refusing to consent to manipulation, the body can't convert the resonance. Raise your DNA's frequency (epigenetics, morphogenetics) and the body draws from prana; you won't need to be part of it. The human body is the hardest place to raise frequency from; that's why it's mastery.

The message: how the hybrids regain a spark (as received)

"A contour of seven layers of sequences is embedded into their peripheral bioenergetic field that runs in constant spark. When they interact with people of positive souls, that exchange of energy through a good intention is always omnipotent and constant spark." Through positive intention and action their bio-field is restructured, and "it will take energy now from the localized field as prana", so they no longer need a host body. That is why they needed human DNA and the spark: the hybridization program.

#39.7When guides say "don't act yet"

Guides can't bypass free will, so they may say "don't do that" without explaining. Operating above the structure, they see how your choices' fluctuations collapse. Ignore them and you may get "smacked in the face" until you return to the track of your contract. Choose carefully whom you channel: preparation takes years, and malevolent beings can hurt you.

The message: create above the structure (Council of Nine, condensed)

"Stop, look above the structure and start creating above the structure; coexist with it, but [don't] start tuning into negative stuff constantly... then you're re-projecting that frequency and you're looping in their timeline. We want you to start creating above the structure: positive, perfect, positive actions, and stop fluctuating what could be, what could not be." Fear projects the very thing you wanted to protect yourself from. And: "Marina, command your mind."

#39.8Command your mind

While attention is kept busy, agendas pass quietly. If your mind can't imagine a reality, you can't shift into it. In the galactic meditation the mind constructs the image, the heart is the laser that activates it into a magnetic and gravitational field, and action crystallizes it. Download 100%, not 99%: absorbing their programmed "tell-a-vision" runs you on their code and loops you in their timeline. Keep paying the bills and living in the reality within the reality; that is the building block of step-by-step ascension.
