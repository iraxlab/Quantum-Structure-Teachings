The big idea

Karma is out-of-balance frequency collected in previous realities. Every choice collapses two fluctuating probabilities into a third, which lands positive or negative according to what predominates in your thoughts, feelings and actions. #28.2How a collapse decides

Two probabilities run in parallel, fluctuating and spinning more potently until they collapse into a third structure (plasma: 1 + 1 = 3, and the three is one unit; see 8.27). #28.3The echo

Unresolved, it rides on your positive hologram like a little virus, a reality within your reality, keeping you out of full coherence. As consciousness raises its vibration, time shrinks and the echo returns faster and faster, until you deal with it.

#28.4Your part, their part

Do your best: talk, correct your own mistake, reach out. If they refuse, it's no longer in your court; they must learn to forgive. Forgiveness neutralizes your field to a clean state: nothing in, nothing out. Let it go 100%, not 99%, and it stops bothering you, or resolves on its own. Every situation's hologram has a core where it started. Pull back and analyze like peeling an onion: did you start it? Honesty matches the true field of the heart, adding no extra tone; if you won't admit a mistake, you plaster a subtone over the foundation and nothing works, however "5D" you think you are. #The method — Clearing an echo (as given)

  1. Notice what keeps repeating: that is the echo.
  2. Pull back and find its core: where, with whom, and how it began.
  3. Be honest: peel off the ego and admit any mistake that was yours.
  4. Correct it: reach out, talk it out, do everything possible.
  5. Each time it repeats, override it with a predominant positive action, thought and love; try again.
  6. If the other person refuses, send them positive light, forgive 100% and let go.
