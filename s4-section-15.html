The big idea

"The devious signals are stopped. New realities will arise from the new frequency. The shift already happened." Now your job is to raise the frequency and keep it up, steady, by what you take in: positive TV, positive food, positive channelling, positive thoughts and actions. #Key teachings

#15.1The body must be light enough

Her guidance is that her own diet be permanent, "almost like a vegetarian"; she never recommends this to anyone: "you have to be connected with your guides first", go through the elimination diet perhaps for a year, and your body must reach a certain resonance. Extraterrestrial races coexist in the matrix at a different resonance (you wouldn't even see their planet); to reach them, consciousness rises first, then the body's cellular structure. When the DNA is pure, frequencies "shoot like a laser" through it, carried by water to every corner of the field, and the body vibrates above 4D–5D. Physical contact is then a different resonance of matter, "the way water has different states of matter" (see S4 7.3, S4 14.2).

What her lucid dream showed. Realizing a dream place was "completely empty, just a hologram", she let go and tried to rise, but her body consciousness was heavy and dropped down; then, in control and without fear, she travelled body and consciousness together through twisting portals, until she was pulled back to bed, tingling. They explained: they were showing her what it takes to expand her body into their reality; it was not an out-of-body experience, and the heaviness came from the food. Cleaned, the body becomes "more silica, more light", "like a spacecraft". The heart holds the codes of the portals; the body must be able to follow (see S4 7.4). Everyone's trigger for becoming lucid differs (a brick wall, pitch black): "I keep recommending you do lucid dreaming."

Put negatively into the DNA, the same technology becomes a negative genetic alteration. Scientists who administer what is against humanity comply with the negative structure, "and that reality will be their future". Can it be reversed? Through a very advanced technology, yes, by each person's agreements, through the councils, for those who received something without consent. "Have no fear, but have the knowledge" (see S4 8.6).

#Questions answered

Is it okay to watch negative history now and then, if the purpose is positive learning?
Yes: learning history in the collective is positive. History repeats only when you ignore the facts and comply. Those who won't educate themselves will think the non-compliant are crazy, and realize too late, "the frog in boiling water".
Why do I never remember a dream?
Training: the synapse must form a specific resonance to keep it. Practise lucid dreaming consistently; give it two weeks (see S4 10, dream recall).
Earth's frequencies are high and fluctuate a lot, affecting many of us. Is this ascension?
Yes. Fluctuation (Season 1) is fundamental, not negative: it's how you mutate what's off balance, like tuning a radio. Train yourself to choose more wisely among the holograms that pop up; download more of the resonance you want and it becomes potent (see S1 3).
Do moon cycles affect our frequency positively?
Yes, by cycle, degree and potency, which is why some use them for the negative. At the full moon a stargate of frequency is wide open: use it for positive transformation. Learn about tides and waves, and the ancient knowledge of planting by the moon cycles: "your plants could be triple the size and the nutrients".
What frequency is best for lucid dreaming?
Different for each of you: try soothing sounds and choose the one that gives you a wave of joy and goosebumps, so your hair feels electric. That is your resonance (see S4 14, 432 Hz).
How can we practise seeing our own timelines?
Any thought is a holographic imprint of a version of you that already exists, and that you're creating now. To make it matter, put thoughts, emotion and action into it consistently, with 100% knowing; it locks into location, "like a jigsaw puzzle", and pops into reality.
Can we expect another activation like last night's? I had a similar lucid dream.
Practise, and don't be disappointed if it doesn't come at once: it needs consciousness and the body. "Think of yourself as a chemist, as an alchemist." Stop watching the projection of millions being manipulated: "who are these millions? You are projecting all the millions." Think that humanity is awake, and you'll create people who understand you.
How do we handle people who hijack our emotions, especially those close to us?
Your emotion is a choice. Pull back, see it as your own hologram, a virtual-reality game, and do something different from what that image expects of you. Repeat, and the image transforms, maybe not as you expected: leave it open. "Shut this door and open another."
How many times should we repeat a new code?
As many as you wish; with full trust, once is enough. But for humans, 15 minutes at a time of repetition builds the neural bridges for permanent understanding.
Are portal access codes permanent, or do they change?
Once downloaded into your DNA, it's permanent, but you have to hold its vibration through consistent positive actions, thoughts and deeds.
Are the white hats still winning? Things seem worse despite our prayers.
Go to the mountains, with your garden and birds and no social media: what is your reality then? Stop consenting to the frequency projected through the screens; the more you tune in, the more of the same you project.
What is today's code for?
A portal download, to help you through what's being done to you. "Did you ever think that we will leave you alone?" If you don't consent to it, that's fine.
If we hold a high vibration, how can 3D things like injections affect us?
You live in a meta structure: what you administer there creates that reality for you. Ask what its intention is, who created it, and for what purpose; "the truth will set you free".

In a dream I heard "human galactic information centre". What does it mean?
There are many galactic information centres: not only one federation, but many races with a positive interest in humanity. You exist in parallel realities and can download from your galactic family in dreams.
With so many in a negative frequency passing and high-frequency children being born, does that help us ascend?
It's the natural process. Each has free will, and from their perspective you are the crazy one. "We have no right to stop anybody's free will." Focus on what you wish to do and move forward.
Does anger serve a purpose, or must it be eliminated?
Both. Sometimes you need a full stop, full force, against what's coming to annihilate your resonance. But stop it as soon as you can: held too long, it penetrates your next moments and loops.
If we protest, are we creating a negative timeline?
It depends on the intention: is it in the name of humanity? Protest for your belief, with a clear mind and through the heart, "but do not ever engage in destruction" (self-protection when your life is threatened is different). From consciousness's view both sides are true for themselves; but overtaking another's consciousness without permission is off balance. If you've been manipulated, it will come in front of your face as a wake-up call: "no middle ground anymore".
