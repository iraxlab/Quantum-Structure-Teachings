The big idea

The Council of Nine said Christ consciousness is the breath of God: an etheric field that responds to your thoughts and reflects your emotions, flows into you and becomes you. It was never meant to be a business; building wealth on it distorts the pure flow of love. Gather freely (in homes, under a tree, by the beach, around a fire) and speak and sing from your heart, because the original text is within you like a code. If something you read puts you in fear and feels off, that is your soul telling you; don't ignore it under peer pressure: research. Not all churches or teachers are false, but use discernment and follow the money. You don't need anyone between you and Christ consciousness.

#Key teachings

#33.1Breaking down what's out of balance

To move to the next level, you must break down every structure out of balance with the original Christ consciousness of God, the breath of God (see 8.32). Christ consciousness is a quantum structure, fractalized across the experiences of different realities. Some institutions rode on sacred scripture with their own agendas, and religion became a way to control and separate people, who then clash, each thinking they're more right.

Added note

Added note: her references to specific religious groups, historical movements, named individuals and videos are left out. No faith or people should be blamed or targeted; she says herself that there are good people in every religion and that this applies to all religions.

#33.2Questioning is not betrayal

Texts have been rewritten over time; how do you know you hold the original? If everyone has been manipulated somehow, and we are all children of God, why judge someone of another faith? Raising such questions often brings attack. She doesn't ask you to believe her: do your own research across all sources, without ego, and decide for yourself.

#33.3Sacred sound in stone

Researchers decoded carved patterns in an old chapel as cymatic shapes and played them as a melody; great cathedrals follow musical ratios (the octave 2:1, the fifth 3:2) as sacred proportions, "divine order in stone". Such knowledge, like children's natural abilities, is rarely taught, and is often called "voodoo".

#33.4Love can carry you through fear

Love and fear can mix: you can go through fear with love, step by step, until it subsides and shows the truth. Digging into this may shake your belief system and strain family relationships, so be strong. She speaks her truth from the heart and lets each person find theirs.

#Questions answered

Prophets pop up everywhere and some damage others' reputations. Where is the truth?
She won't discuss individuals. Ask with reason (see energy philanthropy): if teaching is priced so only the rich can afford it, what are you really doing? Pointing fingers isn't Christ consciousness. You don't have to trust anyone, even her: go to a quiet place, pray, quiet down, and speak your truth. You are Christ consciousness within; you're here to experience, not to be told you're a sinner or that another faith is lower.
Will anything happen on the blood moon?
Globally she sees floods and disturbances as the planets' alignment shifts something, and the negative may try to use the energy; you can ride it by holding the positive. Your experience can be sitting on the porch watching it. Fear-driven content is an algorithm, not the truth: gather with family and friends instead.
What about those of us with no religion?
That's fine. Are you kind? How you behave daily still carries God's consciousness; speaking from the heart is the quantum structure, whatever you name it. Each religion teaches a behavior, which is a sound code that creates a timeline; don't separate humanity by religion, and respect others' beliefs without imposing yours.
Money is energy in the round; once you understand manifestation, you see it.
True: you can't reject money, but implement it with purity, in a flow people can afford, while noticing those who only take and never give.
