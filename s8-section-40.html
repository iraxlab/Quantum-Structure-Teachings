The big idea

The Council of Nine said that before the history usually told, Earth was a high-vibration world of crystalline trees and silica grass, with interdimensional stargates, inner-Earth societies and a benevolent interstellar civilization based here. A war over the stargates (the Zaharaiah war, between celestial beings and reptoid and other groups) used crystalline-combustion technology that vanishes everything, leaving no remains; the great flood was an artificial reset. Humanity's vibration was lowered and shifted onto a false, artificial timeline. Downloading this history now reactivates your DNA toward the original blueprint. They invite you to meditate, ask about it, write what comes, and compare stories with others: when three or more agree, you're on the right path.

There's no time: ten million years ago or two seconds ago, you only need to tune into that etheric field and reprint it with repetition (see the protocol to manifest potency).

You can't think of something whose vibration doesn't already exist; with no time, you create it to see it. #The method — Downloading the lost history (as suggested)

  1. Meditate (for example with the subatomic meditations or in a theta state).
  2. Imagine yourself standing before the etheric plasma field that holds the record.
  3. Ask your question about the Zaharaiah war, the celestial beings or the stargates.
  4. Write down the first thought that comes, without judging it (automatic writing).
  5. Share it with others in the community and compare stories.
  6. When three or more stories agree on names and events, you know you're on the right path.

#Questions answered

Is the Zaharaiah war related to crystal technologies or skulls, the same as the Orion wars, or connected to a recent interstellar object? When did it happen?
Long before the Orion wars, when the planet held vibration through stargates. The information is held in a seven-dimensional, organic, plasma-like technology: a "web etheric plasma field" in an isolated stargate chamber. When you project a thought, it mimics your frequency, reaches through parallel timelines, and downloads the answer back to you. It responds only to organic consciousness: a recorded or AI-mimicked voice can't extract anything, so no infiltration is possible.
So if our vibration hadn't been lowered, they couldn't have taken our reality?
Exactly. They needed specific subtonalities and portals. Your consciousness can open portals; lowered, it opens pockets through which low entities enter: possession. The more evil done, the more portals; that's why the vessel of the innocent was targeted, to open portals and feed the 3D field.
What happened to the celestial beings? Is there a parallel Earth where none of this happened?
Many lives vanished in technological wars down to the subatomic level. You can't change that field, but you can escape it and create a new, parallel reality on the fifth-dimensional field; gathering and sharing remembrance opens positive stargate pockets.
How will DNA reactivation affect us?
Every thought, moment and act of love shifts the subatomic fields that hold your consciousness. Believe creation is in the now, mobilize it in your field, and you change your near future, which follows what you tune into daily. The heart collects the sequence that opens the portals.
How will these lost memories help daily life?
There'll be challenges as you clear negative inputs (family, situations), but you can restructure each one. Hold the truth "like steel", compassionate, and don't let negative sequences keep reprinting; weak links will either reprint as new versions or leave your field. Learn to let go.
