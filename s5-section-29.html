The big idea

The human spirit is the resonance of consciousness itself in each moment, carried by every fractal of it: when consciousness raises its frequency, the spark ripples into every person, the negative included. It cannot be stopped. As the crescendo comes, some will be pressed against the wall until that spark ignites and they act of their own free will; that awakening is the point. It is short: consciousness allows the boiling point no more than about three months, then restoration begins. Prepare, maneuver like a chess player, keep living and building. The "spear of destiny" the negative sought as an object is this spirit, held by each of us.

But watch the pattern: in a crisis, a "solution" arrives that pretends to save you and traps you (as when disaster funds came tied to school standards). Re-watch "What is Direct Programming?" How much you feel the crescendo depends on what you have accumulated through action, thought and behaviour.

But people needed to see it, to experience it... so you never ever again give your freedom away... You've been always already free."

#29.2What the human spirit is

The oversoul is consciousness expressing its versions (S5 28). The human spirit is the collective resonance of the collapse of all that frequency in the now: "the resonance of consciousness itself in every given moment." Your actions ripple through consciousness and shape that moment's resonance; you carry the frequency of the whole of consciousness in each moment. When it rises, you carry the new sequences and your spark. The negative forgot that they, too, are part of it.

The message: the hybrids on the crescendo

"In the month[s], [on the] 7 day[s], pay attention [to] the news" (the 7th, 17th, 27th, or a month's seventh day). "Prep for new restrictions and maneuver accordingly. What do you do when you are off-grid? Supplies?" "In one to two months, all will be resolved." "For some of you [it] will be easy and for some... big lessons... so you can ignite the spark of your spirit to take the physical awakening [and] action. It will be [by] free will then." Why America? "It holds the highest frequency on the heart resonance", accumulated through generations of giving and compassion, the highest probability for freedom. How long? "Three months. It will not be allowed more than that, because consciousness will preserve its own expressions": like water that takes long to reach the boil but holds it only briefly. Then restoration, with everyone having "a piece of the pie." "Do not rush... You all know the outcome. Humanity wins."

Her instructions: during the crescendo
  • Prepare supplies in case you are off-grid.
  • Watch what is announced around the 7th, 17th and 27th.
  • Don't put everything into the stock market; if you bought something, wait before building; avoid getting stuck in contracts or banks.
  • Businesses: don't give everything away; secure your position strategically. Many will still profit.
  • Don't travel abroad unless you can provide for yourself there if borders close.
  • Don't stop living: continue your projects, desires and businesses, maneuvering "like you are in a game".

#29.3Play the game to win

"Strategically plot all your moves and counter-moves... Who has the fear will never win. Who has the doubts will never see. Who has the position and the passion will see." For the best chess players it will be "like an echo in the wind": food, place and money already positioned. Some were strategically placed ahead (a contract signed two weeks before a shutdown, land bought) so they can pull others after them. Reality within the reality: in a storm that flooded homes nearby, she found everything she needed in a nearly empty store. "Trust the plan, because this is in God's hands."

#29.4The spear of destiny

The negative searched for the spear of destiny (the Roman soldier's spear) as an object. "Checkmate": it is not matter but the energetic field coming from God consciousness: the human spirit. It is not held by one person: each of you holds it, sparked by consciousness itself.

By perception each is right; by resonance, consciousness rebalances what is off.

It is a lack of vocabulary: each shift is quicker than the blink of an eye, instantaneous. Think of a glass of water: it already exists in the matrix and you create it at the same time.
When does the soul enter the body? Is soul different from spirit?
Different resonances and densities, each a spark of consciousness. A soul can have twelve versions in different timelines, collecting from them in real time, which also split further: the oversoul, feeding the mother consciousness. The spirit is the collective energy of all that collapse, consciousness itself, splitting and re-collapsing, entering matter and DNA (12, then 24 strands). The timing of entry was not answered.
