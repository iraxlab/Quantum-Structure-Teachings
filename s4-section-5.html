How to Clear Karma

Why 3D and 5D run together; why every imbalance now pops up in front of you; taking your sovereignty with integrity; why there is no white lie; how karma travels through families and marriages; and questions on knowing when it's cleared, karma versus contract, and harder tests (live talk, January 2021)

The big idea

Under the umbrella of ascension, your 5D reality and the 3D reality run at once, so you carry signals from both, and "consciousness is not going to leave one particle behind": every imbalance you accumulated, or are connected to, will pop up in front of your face to be cleared. Karma is accumulated, out-of-balance frequency. You clear it not by revenge ("eye for an eye" locks you in) and not by passively meditating, but by taking action for your freedom through the heart, with integrity, override after override, until the last particle is cleared and you shift to an earth where that situation no longer exists.

#Key teachings

#5.13D and 5D are merging

Between the 5D (positive heart emotion) and the 3D you fluctuate and collapse, 1+1=3 (see S1 2, S1 3), and both are under the umbrella of the 5D ascension protocol, Mother Gaia. Imagine 5D at 5 Hz and 3D at 3 Hz: as you act more and more in the positive, the 3D molecule of your earth starts mutating into 5D, and your outcomes start collapsing in a 5D structure, partially coexisting until you lock in permanently. Mutation takes a curvature of time; you can't jump there without having learned to mutate 3D through actions, emotions, thoughts and deeds. Meanwhile you still carry signals of coexisting realities: "you're not only going to see the positive, you're also going to see the negative." Learning to switch the emotion is the challenge (see S3 8).

#5.2Coexisting with someone in the 3D

When you combine your structure with a spouse, family or friends, you have a contract to coexist. If you hold the heart while they keep acting in the negative (greed, abuse, manipulation), their imbalance "is going to pop up right in front of your face", because you're in the ascension protocol. Then you must mutate it by taking positive action.

#5.3Take your sovereignty with integrity

If you can't take it anymore (say, an abusive partner), take action for your freedom: call a lawyer, speak the truth, hold your ground, find the papers and the proof, "fight like a soldier, but with integrity": no manipulating, no lying, no "get as much money as you can". Why? Your heart collects the vibration of your actions; accumulate positive action and it starts vibrating on a 5D platform. Each time they act negatively, you override with a positive action and collapse a new platform.

The key point

"You are not changing that person. You are changing and accumulating the heart vibration through your action", and so you project another version of them, in a brand-new earth, where they can no longer continue the negative. Sovereignty "does not mean I'm just going to meditate and allow this person to do whatever he wants". "When you're good, you have the most power": be clever, play the chess game, with positivity and integrity.

Consciousness, like an immune system, is slightly tilted toward the positive so that it can't self-destruct; so if you act with integrity and heart, it supports you, and "in the end, you always win". If instead you let yourself be the puppet, you vibrate on the abuser's frequency and loop in the 3D without knowing.

#5.4The one who harms accumulates too

The person intentionally hurting, lying and manipulating (not the blind, but those who know) is accumulating negative frequency in the heart, and their reality vibrates on it daily, "even when it's giving you the illusion everything is fine". Because their reality is also under the 5D umbrella, it eventually collapses: consciousness will never allow it to continue (see S4 2.5).

#5.5You carry their lineage too

Marry someone, and you coexist with their structure, and through it with their family's accumulated karma (stealing, crimes, generations of deception). Until it is cleared, "everything is going to start popping right in front of your face". You can't download 99% (see S2 1.10): you move fully to 5D only by acting positively again and again. Every challenge now comes from that fluctuation and from what you collected through the people you're connected with, and through your own daily actions, in every reality you coexist in.

#5.6Clearing to the last particle

Each time they attack, you take a positive action with integrity and erase one negative situation; again, and again, "and all of a sudden it's going to be the last one". Then that person will no longer exist in your reality: through infinite probabilities you shift to another earth, free of the karma accumulated through them. But not before the last particle, because one particle is a hologram of the entire universe. "God you are."

Nothing is wrong with you. Every person, situation, politician and collective out of balance will be in front of you (spouse, parents, children) because everyone you project is being challenged to go forward or stay behind. "There's nobody outside of you": even the people you deal with are your own ascension protocol. And it can be collective: a couple can hold the heart together to clear a negative family structure (in-laws, say) as a family.

#5.7There is no white lie

They told her "there is no such thing as a white lie": it isn't the truth, so it accumulates a negative structure, and the quantum field mirrors back to you what you project like a laser, from the same person or someone else. Better: "I wish not to discuss this, if you don't mind." Rudeness is also a frequency: be a lady or a gentleman, or someone else will do it to you. Whatever you wish to get, get it through integrity, without a lie.

#5.8Let it out, then transform it

"You're human... you're going to go ballistic sometimes, you're going to say negative words. So what? But transform it." Yes, scream for five minutes if you want to let it out; then pull away, "it's a hologram", and do something positive: take an ice bath, go for a walk, go to a restaurant, watch something very positive. Each time it takes a shorter time to calm down; as you shift faster, the solution is there faster, "because it's not time in the first place".

Added safety note

Ice baths can be dangerous with heart conditions or high blood pressure. Start with a cool shower, and ask your doctor before cold immersion.

#5.9Eye for an eye locks you in

"Eye for an eye, how clever": you kill my brother, I kill you, and that is exactly what the negative side wants, because doing the same as was done to you accumulates the same structure and keeps you in the 3D negative platform. If you did harm before, consciousness gives you the opportunity to correct it: "you played that game in a hologram, let's fix it here, because you're part of me." You'll be given the same or a similar situation, with the power to destroy, and the choice not to. That is the test (see S4 1.3). Choose integrity and the heart, and consciousness will collapse it so that person no longer coexists in your structure. (Self-defence is different from intentional harm.)

#The method — Clearing Karma

  1. Recognize the cue: an imbalance pops up (a friend turns spiteful, a family fight). "Thank you, I understand: this needs to be cleared."
  2. Peel the ego with brutal honesty: is it them, or is it me? If it's you, apologize and act positively.
  3. Let it out if you must (five minutes), then pull back: "it's a hologram".
  4. Shift the emotion: something positive (the Season 3, episode 8 training; the Galactic Meditation Short Version with your own scenario).
  5. Take action for your freedom, with integrity: the truth, the proof, the proper channels. No lies, no revenge.
  6. Repeat with each new attack, override after override, until the last particle clears.

#Questions answered

How do we know we've cleared our karma, or how much is left?
It pops up in front of you, often out of the blue: a 15-year friend is suddenly rude and spiteful. That's the cue that realities are splitting: you move toward the positive while they stay behind. But it could be you acting in the negative, so strip out the ego and peel the layers: were you showing off, spiteful, wanting attention? Those layers are illusion covering the pure core of your heart. Find the core, see the truth, and if it's you, fix it by apologizing and acting in the positive.
How do we tell karma from a contract? Can positive karma dissolve contracts?
A contract is written before you drop into the 3D matrix, with your councils: who your mother, father and brother will be, what happens at age two or five, a script that gives you the knowledge you came for, however negative or positive. Psychics can't give you the details (you might marry the first person when the contract was for the fifth). Karma is what you accumulate by free will in daily actions within it: if a contract held a terrible experience so you could learn and later help others, karma would be your going after that person to destroy them. Revenge accumulates; taking your sovereignty "through the heart, through the proper channels" doesn't.
As we clear more, does life bring us harder situations?
The intensity depends on what those you coexist with have accumulated, such as a family that built wealth on lies through generations. Marry into it, and it's in your structure too; it will be much more challenging to get out. So choose your partner wisely, and "do not give your body freely": intimacy and orgasm create a heart-to-heart, DNA-like frequency of creation that merges your resonances. What you co-create flows into your children; if you don't clear it, "your kids may have to clear this out". When you refuse to destroy the person who wronged you, even with the power to, and help instead, you break the chain of karma running through the timelines; she was told her children started "a new karma, good karma". "When I let go, then was given" (see S1 14).

Left out: her personal stories (only their lessons are kept) and names.

Remember
  • Everything out of balance pops up: nothing is wrong with you.
  • You don't change them; you change your frequency by action.
  • Sovereignty with integrity: no lies, no revenge.
  • No white lie: "I wish not to discuss this."
  • Choose your partner wisely; break the chain for your children.
