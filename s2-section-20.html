Q & A: Dimensions, Auras and the Meaning You Give

Why higher dimensions aren't a matter of years, the meaning you give to signs, what happens when auras meet, movement as geometry, change across a generation, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

The fifth, sixth and seventh dimensions aren't stages a calendar will bring: they run simultaneously, and you move between them by embodying their frequency, creating a third reality between yours and theirs. Ascension already exists; you're constructing it. Signs, numbers and inner images mean what you assign to them: you are the mantra. When two energy fields meet, a positive and a negative balance into a zero point; two of the same push apart, which is why consciousness, seeking balance, brings whatever is off balance to the surface. Deep change in a society can take a generation, carried by those doing the work and by children born into the new frequency. Through it all, train a steady mind, and help each other.

In keeping with this book's approach to politics and to claims about groups and religions, that survey is left out. The teachings below are kept.

#Key teachings

#20.1Dimensions run at the same time

"In which years will we reach 6D and 7D?" It's a misunderstanding: 5D, 6D and 7D, like all timelines, run simultaneously in the quantum hologram. What matters is the state of your consciousness, the frequency and geometry you embody. Your body is a vessel, "like a living spacecraft": as you call in a frequency and become it, moment to moment, more and more potently, you create a third structure between the two (1 + 1 = 3; see S1 2.2). You're not travelling from one reality to another; you're switching timelines by embodying a new frequency. Mastering bilocation, you can shift back and forth, some realities only with permission.

Higher beings do the same: they can appear in a form your consciousness can receive (angelic, Pleiadian), matching your frequency so you recognize them. Contact is individual: you'll have your own platform of contact "while everybody else is watching the sky". "Ascension is fractal", and your ascension already exists; you're constructing it into matter (see S2 9.7).

#20.2Dreams are leaking platforms

Short dream scenes (levitating, for example) are sequences leaking from realities that coexist with this one, giving your consciousness a platform to start embedding new frequencies. There is no real difference between one state and another. Recall the frequency of the dream, and it slowly begins to manifest in matter (see S2 17.9).

#20.3You are the mantra: meaning is yours

Asked about beautiful mosaics seen at night, the answer was a question: why connect your reality with a mantra outside you, when "your reality is a mantra itself"? You projected it, so you already know what it means. "Why are you asking for us to create your platform? You are the master; even we are in your reality." Whatever meaning you choose is what manifests.

So with repeated numbers and signs, assign the meaning yourself rather than borrowing someone else's: the same sequence can mean different things to different people. Build your own dictionary: the teacher has learned from her dreams, for example, that certain colours, clothing or clear versus muddy water tell her what kind of day is coming. Write it down; it's designed for you. Colours and smells are generally significant.

That's also why there are "no predictions": she can read your frequency now, not what you'll do tomorrow, and guidance won't reveal a tragedy you came to learn from. What is given is a bylaw, an end result already "written in a code" (see S2 10.1).

#20.4When two energy fields meet

The aura is your vibrational sequence, projected from your consciousness and emotion into your field; it holds your geometry and resonance. When two people meet, it's a merging of quantum fields, with an enormous effect. A positive and a negative together create balance, a zero point. Two of the same (negative and negative, or positive and positive) push apart like same-pole magnets: there's no balance, and the vibration drops toward density, where manifestation is difficult.

Consciousness, now ascending, does everything it can to restore balance: it creates situations so that whatever is off balance comes to the surface for you to deal with (see S2 18.1). Keep choosing the negative as frequency rises, and timelines separate. A second bylaw applies to love: fields very similar in loving resonance become one unit.

From the booksSD: "Sub sound, (mirror sound), the opposite (+,-) to complete for perfect balance '0' point."

#20.5Hands, movement and geometry

An electrical zap through the palms in a dream was a higher-self activation, "almost like a walk-in", changing the body's frequency. Hands matter: the first thing you do is touch; through the hands you draw more energy from the ether, in and out. And the movements of the body create geometric patterns: yoga, and especially tai chi, which is based on sacred geometry, redirect the flow of energetic force from the ether into your matrix. Look for the original poses: some have been changed, though they were meant for all of humanity.

#20.6Change can take a generation

When a society is suppressed for a long time, a frequency of survival becomes embedded, sometimes with negative intention (cutting corners, even corruption, can come to seem normal, "even cool"). That frequency has to be balanced and cleared through choosing positive actions. For some places the time given was 20 to 25 years. The real transformation comes through the children: those born from 2020 come from the new beginning and will refuse manipulation ("I'm sorry, this is wrong"), shutting the program down. And through people doing the work now, lifting the frequency (see S2 10.5).

Timelines are portals: think of a radio channel; nothing in a lower vibration can enter. The heart holds the frequency fractally, moment to moment, and more codes are given as you rise, so you don't "spin out of orbit" (see NT on the spinning of particles).

#20.7Painful history: acknowledge, don't relive

When a date tied to a collective tragedy keeps appearing, it's embedded in your consciousness. Acknowledge it, honour and thank those who were lost or who sacrificed, then neutralize it: that happened in a different Earth, a different frequency, and it doesn't have to be part of your structure now. Returning to it again and again brings its frequency back. "Acknowledgement to neutrality"; then move forward, in the now, toward the new beginning.

#20.8Change from the ground up

Asked about political turmoil: "in the end, it's going to be in the name of the people"; what happens behind the scenes differs from what's shown, and media often confuse. Seeing manipulation helps people wake up and reclaim their sovereignty. "Nobody is going to give it to us": speak out, take action, hold your ground, non-violently, and help others. Change comes from the ground up (see S2 16.4).

#20.9Know it's benevolent

When you begin channelling, don't keep questioning whether it's benevolent from fear: both a positive and a negative connection exist; which one becomes real is the one you embed. Neutralize doubt, smile, and choose. The teacher has seen the most unlikely people turn out to help her. Likewise, a shaman or a spirit-clearer may help if it gives you a physical boost of belief, and you'll find one by acting; but "don't give the power to somebody else to tell you that you have the power: you are it."

#20.10Train a steady mind

Practising the quantum manifestation is how you master the chaos: shift negative emotion to positive and stay there; give and share from the heart, because "the heart has the codes that switch the timelines." A training exercise: hold one image (a photo of someone) with steady positive thought and emotion for one minute, without drifting to tomorrow. Steadiness in the now becomes potent enough to pop into matter. It's normal to go back to the material when you go off balance, and to seek help physically when you need it.

#20.11Help each other

Connect with each other, exchange skills and ideas, and support one another (a doctor helping someone, others helping in return). Many people have no one; build a community of helping, whatever form it takes (see S2 15.11).

#The method — One Steady Minute

  1. Choose one image: a photo of someone, or a picture of what you want.
  2. Calm to zero for a few breaths.
  3. Hold the image with a positive thought and a warm feeling, for one full minute.
  4. Stay in the now: if your mind jumps to tomorrow or to doubt, gently come back.
  5. Notice the steadiness: thought plus emotion is the magnetic and gravitational field.
  6. Repeat daily, lengthening the time; steadiness builds potency.

Not years: see 20.1.

See 20.3: you are the mantra; you assign the meaning.

"Major, enormous": see 20.4.
I've noticed markings near my ankle and a lumpy area below my elbow. Are aliens probing me?
"No, I don't get the aliens; I get more medical." See a doctor, have blood work and have the lumps tested.

See 20.9: you can manifest one by acting, if it helps your belief; but you are the power.
Political turmoil and heavy energy where I live: should I move abroad?
"In the end, it's going to be in the name of the people": see 20.8.
Are the quantum manifestation videos the only way to learn it, and how can every level of me embody it?
When you connect with this, you're connecting with your higher self: the teacher is a projection in your own reality, giving you information from your own structure, because you're ready. Being the frequency projects the hologram, not the other way round (see S2 15.3).
I'm starting a company with two others, and one seems unsure. Should I include him?
Undecided for now: within three months a situation will help you decide ("fluctuating realities").
I've begun channelling what seems to be an advanced group. Any advice?
See 20.9: let go of the mind, and know it's benevolent.
A beam of light from a clear night sky hit us. What was it?
Not allowed to be told yet: "three or more realities on top of it." It's an activation, connected with your belief system.
Does practising quantum manifestation help in a chaotic reality?
Yes: see 20.10.
Are negative attachments only imagination? Do they dissolve when you say "stop"?
For you, it's embedded deeply in your thoughts; someone experienced in clearing negative spirits could help, because it helps your belief system.
