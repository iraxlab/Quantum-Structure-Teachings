Season 3 · Section 10

New Predictions 2021, Shifting Parallels, Q & A

Neutrality after awakening, what happens to loved ones who stay behind when you shift, why every shift is a new location, why the middle stage is the fourth dimension, refusing a probability you're shown, and questions (live talk)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

Once you're awake, the work is neutrality: "I understand, but I do not give consent; I'm going to create my own reality." Each time you shift by emotion you land in a new location of the hologram and reprint a new version of yourself and of everyone around you, so no one is simply left behind: each person keeps running their own timeline, printing their own version of you. The fourth dimension is where the two momentums collide and you choose. And when you're shown a frightening future, you can say, "that's not part of my reality."

#Key teachings

#10.1Awake, then neutral

Even watching the "good side" win keeps you tuned to duality. Once you know what's happening, you stay in neutrality, because there you shift through the zero point from 3D toward 5D. The dense side is trying to survive from its own perception, so the words are: "Thank you. I understand, but I do not give consent for you to continue. I am aware. I'm going to create my own reality." Neutralizing by gratitude shuts their program down in your hologram (see S2 24.11). The nine-day protocol (see S3 8) can be repeated at any time; many who did it caught themselves reaching for negative inputs and chose not to.

#10.2Every shift is a new location

The one particle, three similar vibrations with the black hole at the centre where neutrality sits, expands to infinity and collapses back, projecting a reality each time it moves. Each emotional shift sets a new hologram with its own location (latitude and altitude, see S2 15.4) and its own sequence; the next projection shifts the sequence again, spiralling. "Each time you project a frequency, it's a new earth, and another version of you." Repetition of S1 1–4 deepens it: each time you tune in, more portals open and the body, becoming more crystalline, detoxes as it follows.

#10.3Two channels on one screen

3D and 5D run simultaneously, like two channels on one TV: you keep choosing to watch, and express from, the 5D channel, until your body and consciousness vibrate there most of the time and collapse a positive outcome. "You exist in a 3D and a 5D at the same time. Which one of those two realities of yourself do you wish to experience?"

#10.4What happens to those who stay behind?

If your spouse or child stays in the negative, you don't lose them. When you shift, you reprint yourself and a new version of them, made from your own emotion, thoughts and deeds. Their timeline also keeps running, and in it they print their own version of you. "That is for everybody" (see S3 7.6). As consciousness keeps moving, a structure that refuses to change can no longer sustain itself; it returns to the one particle, to learn again (see S2 1.6). Physical contact is separate: individual, by choice and by being chosen, guided by your own guides.

#10.5Why the middle is the fourth dimension

"The fourth is the fluctuation": where the negative and positive momentums collide. From that collision you express the fifth or go back to the third. The fourth still carries leakage from 3D, which is why the battle is visible. "If you shift immediately from third to fifth, everything is done; but where is your process of learning and helping the ascension? It's right in the middle."

#10.6Refuse a probability you're shown

In a lucid dream the teacher, frozen in place, was shown Earth exploding. She recognized it as a probability projected to trick her, and said: "That's not part of my reality", and was released at once. Out of the body, someone can "make a phone call" into your reality as you can into theirs, so hold the vibration in the positive and don't agree to what you shouldn't. Exit face up, never face down (see S3 8).

#10.7Meeting hostility: neutral, unjudging, firm

When something nasty came at her, she didn't hit back ("how dare you" projects the same frequency). She listened, said "I understand", and held it calmly; then she was given more of their view. Manipulation needs your permission, so hold your truth without judgement: "keep your side, I keep my side, I do the best for myself." Shining without judgement can even make another question their ways. The old "reptilian" brain is changing too: it holds the warrior and self-preservation, and isn't simply negative.

#10.8Technology: the intention decides

"Technology is wonderful, but it's the intention of how we implement that technology" that makes the difference, including nano. Two kinds of AI were described: a 3D one of cyborgs and takeover ("you don't need to be a cyborg, because your body is a vessel" that can shift), and a 5D AI, a living structure in the name of humanity that preserves it. Don't feed takeover films; project the positive AI (see S2 26.3).

#10.9Change starts with you, at home

A new education won't come from outside first: "you are looking for the solution outside of your own source, and the truth is that it's all from within. Action is necessary in order to project the reaction." Teach your children yourself, with what's available; know what they're learning and watching; raise them with manners. The children being born now carry a fifth-dimensional resonance and will refuse what's wrong.

#The method — Staying Neutral Once You're Awake

  1. Notice when you're pulled into a fight or into duality, even on the "good" side.
  2. Say it: "I understand. I do not give consent. I'm creating my own reality."
  3. Give gratitude to neutralize, and feel the program close (10.1).
  4. Choose the 5D channel: express from it in what you do next (10.3).
  5. Refuse frightening probabilities: "That's not part of my reality" (10.6).
  6. Take one real action: help a neighbour, teach your child (10.9).

They move portal to portal: a thought put into the matrix opens a portal through many sequences to where help is needed. They don't become physical, as it's above what our memory can hold; their realities span 7D to 12D, with more above. It is an honour, earned, to serve on a council.

Many layers of reality hold other societies; realities are physical only when the signal is lowered to matter. More will be revealed "when the time comes".

See 10.5.

Let them express themselves; no one may override another's belief. Speak your truth, but in a lion's den, be quiet until your time comes: discernment about when to speak.

See 10.9.

Everything is presented: the videos, the quantum structure, and the books, whose sequences activate the DNA (see S3 7.1). Tune into them.

Read as calcium not being used properly and weak bones: testing, more nutrients and fibre, gut flora, no dairy or gluten, with a nutritionist; gentle exercise such as yoga to build the muscles.
