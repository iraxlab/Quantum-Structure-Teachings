The big idea

The weekly codes are locations, stargates, like a star map that guides your consciousness step by step to a shared "quadrant". There, in about three months, galactic help gathers everyone who resonates in the heart for a collective quantum leap; on the way, manifestation and revelation speed up. You make the journey with your own consciousness, as the vessel, not by being carried in a craft: "this is how you become a master manipulator" of your reality. The codes only suggest; your actions keep you on the platform. And the hardest mastery is to force yourself to be compassionate even when you feel anger.

#Key teachings

Her instructions: using the codes

1. Watch or read each week's code video, then repeat it seven times in a row on the same day (a five-minute video becomes 35 minutes): it takes up to seven repetitions to crystallize in the synapses. One day is enough; it holds for seven days.

2. Do them in order if you are new, starting with the first video on holographic imprints (S5 34), so you know what they do.

3. Stay awake and aware; you may lie down and relax. No music, only the voice and the codes; not on a loop while sleeping.

4. Family may read them too (more potency); for small children you may say them on their behalf.

5. Don't do them in depression or anger: first calm yourself and lift into joy (a friend, nature, dressing up for a walk).

6. For the rest of the week, act from the heart.

#35.1The codes are a star map

Each code is a location, latitude and longitude in the holographic matrix: a stargate. Like a journey to a mountaintop through countries and rivers, each step has coordinates; you reach one this week, hold its vibration all week, and the next code takes you on. It happens through consciousness, not technology in the body (she is for organic intelligence, not transhumanism). It is an inner dimension: realities within the parallel timeline. The strongest souls came to hold this vibration for the collective; there will be several such leaps.

#35.2Mastery: compassion by force

"You have to force yourself to be the most compassionate, even when you feel so much anger and you want to punch the wall. And that is mastery." What was done on this planet is beyond imagination; to transform evil you must face it, understand it and transform it. Have fun on the journey; overwhelm makes you unproductive.

#Questions answered

Do the codes work while we sleep?
Once they crystallize in the synapses they become a permanent waveform that keeps vibrating and, with daily heart action, pulls you like a magnet toward the intended timeline. But read them awake.
In which dimension does the change of location happen?
An inner dimension: realities within a parallel timeline, layer after layer. The codes are star maps to the collective quadrant meeting.
Why is there no number two in the codes?
Two Hz is not compatible with where they are going now; it would flip the switching "upside down". Two is also linked to duality in balance and the opening of portals (see the frequency list in the Harmonic Reactor).
From the booksFrom her books (HR): the Harmonic Reactor lists what each frequency (1 Hz, 2 Hz, 3 Hz and so on) does in the quantum field, and how combinations build or dissolve realities; such work must be done in a protected environment.
What happens to those who don't receive the codes? Must I listen to all the talk too?
They are in the reality, but perhaps not yours; everyone moves at their own speed; someone finding this a year later will still be in the right place. Ideally learn the first video's quantum structure; the codes readjust like a magnet even if taken out of order. "I'm not your savior... just ahead of the class."
If my husband doesn't read them, is he in a different reality?
Either way: he may live a different reality, or you may project him with you and one day he says "you were right". Hold the vibration without expectations; never try to override someone's consciousness.
Do I still need the codes if I already know how to maneuver the hologram?
No, if you don't wish to; they add potency to the collective.
Can anything block the codes?
Yes: your ego.
If I do something unkind out of anger, do I get left behind?
No, unless it is truly evil. You'll get it back quickly, a lesson that puts you back on track. If something really bad happens, calm down and repeat the codes.
What does faster manifestation mean?
Potency: repeating the same heart-led actions, thoughts and deeds makes the waveform stronger, so the reflection, the synchronicity, the right people come back quickly (see "Protocol to Manifest Potency" and "Examples of Manifestations", S4).
When will the shift happen? Is it gradual and individual?
"The shift already happened... now you're dreaming how you made it." A positive future connected with our now; we are building that reality.
I get dizzy at work. Is it the shift?
It could be the frequencies, stress, environment, or a physical cause such as the thyroid. She is not a doctor: get medical checks and blood work regularly.
Are the codes stargates in space and time? Should I avoid social media?
Yes, very specific locations. And yes.
Are there signs before activation?
You are already activated. Don't push beyond what you desire; the hardest part will be other people's ignorance close to home. Have joy in the journey.
Is the coming gathering a physical place?
No, it is multidimensional: a stargate. Personally, everything will speed up: answers, revelations, truth.
How can I reach zero point in a heavily negative workplace?
You may need to move. Use the galactic meditation's method for a new job; feel that you have it; thank those who make it hard, because they show you what is off balance; and every day keep looking ("hide and seek"). When told no: "Thank you, you're not resonating in my hologram." See the nine-day Council of Nine protocol (S3 8).
