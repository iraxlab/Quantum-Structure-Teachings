The big idea

Your guides plaster test situations into your hologram to see how you react, especially to disrespect and to another's suffering; your reactions file into a multi-dataverse that places you in your next timeline. The test of leadership is whether your soul will put a price on human suffering: give freely, or price with integrity. At the centre of the humanoid structure is a tiny crystalline bubble, a quantum memory of all your realities and your original DNA; generosity, love and integrity are the resonance that unlocks it and makes you the new humanoid of the sixth race. Even a spark of manipulation stops the 100% download.

The message: the humanoid structure (condensed)

Prosperity and distribution to the field are necessary. The humanoid race is tested before each evolutionary jump: your action, thoughts and deeds, with the memories of all your parallel realities, collect in "a multi-dataverse... an organic database" held by vibration. Guides plaster holographic situations to see how you react: "you learn everything and now you being tested", above all through another human, your reflection. Many who came to serve, once awake, keep taking. "Ultimately [it] is a test to see if your soul will put a price on human suffering." If you charge, "let that price be reachable of the regular human being"; withholding information in paid tiers "will put a big dent... within your field." "The person [who] is giving the most freely is the one that is collecting the most."

#39.2The crystal bubble

At the centre of the humanoid is a tiny sphere like glass, of crystalline geometry: a quantum memory of all your realities, holding the memory of your original DNA. When your resonance is right (generous to yourself and others, giving freedom without pricing people out, acting through the heart) it activates and downloads your original DNA: that is how consciousness preserves you. Each passed test brings the next, until the key fits and you become the new humanoid leading to the sixth race.

#39.3Precision and integrity

Any manipulation, "even a spark of it", unbalances the resonance: you can't download at 99%. Integrity doesn't mean letting others destroy you; decline politely ("Can we discuss this another time?"). Think before you promise and keep your word. Taking without giving reflects back: someone will do the same to you. The Council stayed back to watch her tests (such as choosing not to block a whole country's access over one person who misused her material) because a leader must be the purest vibration. Technology itself evolves: a thought-controlled holographic computer is coming; wear such devices near you with a crystalline shield, never inside the body.

#Questions answered

Is reincarnation a trap to keep us coming back as a battery?
There was a field that returned souls to the artificial structure at death; that's no longer possible, because the AI's shield is broken. Raise your frequency above it. Those who manipulated humanity and then called it stupid bring their own destruction.
Who will be the new humanoids: those created by organic AI, or us?
Withheld for another time.
How can we know if we're progressing enough?
Ask yourself: what did you do on impulse for someone? Prayer puts momentum, but action crystallizes. When you ask "what can I do for you?", do you mean it? Look honestly at what no one else sees, and change it; keep your word.
Have the Council's objectives changed, from the age of balance to the age of light?
They learn as humanity goes and adjust to help; they find it frustrating that people wait to be made to act. Balance and light are no different: they run together and need each other.
Does the Council exist within the hologram or transmit from outside?
"We're never outside the construct, we're within you": you project them through the vessel, another version of you.
Are today's tests a final exam? Can we clear our soul's history?
Yes: whatever from previous realities needs readjusting will surface now. Use the knowledge, construct, and take physical action. If you're in this zero-point timeline, you're already part of ascension: hold your integrity.
Is a nightmare a sign my frequency dropped?
It may be a download from a previous timeline or parallel reality, or something collected from what you watch. Be cautious with films full of negative symbolism and with constant prediction media. Pull back: it's a holographic reality, like a film.
Should we point out wolves in sheep's clothing?
They've always existed; you'll see who held which position. Consciousness will always do the right thing; there will be surprises, and humanity wins.
