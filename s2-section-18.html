Q & A: Waking Up, and Sharing It Gently

Why wake-up moments come to everyone, how to share hard truths with care, abduction fears and "hyper holograms", the zero point with strong emotions, recurring situations, attachment, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

As consciousness rises into the heart, whatever is off balance surfaces, and everyone, whatever side they're on, will meet a personal situation that wakes them up. At that moment you choose: look into it, or look away. Waking up means seeing clearly first (you can't fly if you don't know there's air), then shifting with the quantum structure. When you share what you've seen, shine the light gently, without blinding anyone. With strong emotions, subside to zero first, then build the new blueprint: know what, never insist on how. When a situation keeps recurring, peel the layers: are you looping through fear, or is it a lesson to let go? And don't give your power away, to objects, to fears, or to anyone's reading.

#Key teachings

#18.1Wake-up moments come to everyone

Parallel realities collapse together when they're similar in vibration, and the result becomes the outcome (see S1 15, S2 13.2). As the planet and universe ascend into the heart, anyone out of balance with it, "even if you're dark forces", will meet something in their personal life that asks: "Wait a minute, am I doing the right thing?" Because everyone is part of the one particle, what you do to another you do to your own reflection.

So don't be surprised by challenges: they show what needs fixing. A friend of fifteen years may suddenly show another face; it's showing you something now. At that moment you choose to look into it or to pull out of it. "You cannot have your head in the sand all the time": eventually an "earthquake" pops it out, which is harder. How would you know you're under water if you'd never heard of the sky?

#18.2Investigate, don't attack

Many people jump on each other online instead of saying "let me investigate that": that, the teacher said, is intelligence. Look at the evidence yourself, without blaming or pointing fingers, because division is what keeps us apart: unite. "The truth can never be controversial": when realities collapse to one probability, that's the manifested timeline. Then shift with the manifestation protocol to a new timeline.

Added note

The talk again recommended specific online sources and named people and cases. In keeping with this book's approach, these are left out. Check claims against reliable, accountable sources (see S2 11.10).

#18.3Share the light gently

A message read in the talk, and endorsed by the teacher: information may soon come out that causes shock and disbelief. Digging into the worst of it could be heartbreaking. The point is not to dwell in darkness but to remember: when you share, shepherd people gently. Remember how shocking it was for you, how angry or helpless you felt. "Shine the light; just be careful not to blind them with it." Give their eyes time to adjust; your steadiness will help them feel better. If we mean to rebuild society side by side, we need goodwill, especially when someone's whole world view turns upside down.

#18.4A lesson in the body needs action

When there's a lesson, you don't just meditate it away. In physical matter, you know you're okay and, at the same time, act: look for doctors and solutions, ask questions, research. Through that journey you create and find the reality in which you're okay (see S2 14.3). Instant manifestation does happen, more and more, but until then, practise: "you have to create it to see it", not "see it to believe it."

#18.5Abduction fears and "hyper holograms"

The idea of "abduction" was put in our minds to attach fear to contact. Some cases were negative, and some (the talk said) were staged by secret projects that hypnotized people and projected a hologram of abduction. Many real experiences happen on a parallel platform: you shift, and it doesn't stay in your everyday reality; so take it as a dream state, without fear. Information comes bit by bit, as your agreements allow.

"Another level": beings can take someone on board to help or save them and place a reality in their consciousness; the consciousness then creates a parallel reality, a hyper hologram, mimicking the frequency it came from, so the person experiences ordinary life while actually being on a craft. Information like this is given "spoon by spoon", because it only makes sense once you know the structure. So expand your thinking, and don't take any one reading as the whole truth: there may be structures not yet revealed to you. "Study the quantum structure… that is your freedom."

#18.6Strong emotions: subside first

Grief and compassion, or "masculine" and "feminine" feelings, are one unit. Whatever the emotion, pull out and subside it to neutrality, observing (see S2 15.7). It may take time: the teacher once had to remove herself and sit by a pool for 45 minutes to calm down. Then use the manifestation meditation (see S2 16) with a clear idea of what you want, letting go of who, where and how.

Knowing what you want isn't an "expectation": picture the red house, but don't insist on who builds it or where the money comes from. And abundance isn't only money: if you tie it to money, you shut out every other probability (someone may simply give you the car). If a situation repeats, note where your emotions and actions are, and write a paper trail. Gratitude is neutrality.

#18.7Living food has more potency

Dried greens and powders still work, but their vibration is less potent than a living plant's: fresh, raw juice is more potent and heals faster. Herbal healing works best as the right combination of plants, made by an expert for the person. Healing should address the whole system, the whole immune structure, not one spot.

#18.8Sacred geometry unlocks with awareness

Sacred geometry with light opens portals and creates realities; the teacher was told to step back from studying it. New, very complex geometries are coming (geometry within geometry, pulsing and merging, each merge creating a new structure), unlocked as awareness grows. "Your consciousness already knows them." They'll be shown to each person; whatever makes sense in your consciousness is usually the right path.

#18.9Levels, numbers, and your own power

Unlocking a level is like graduating a grade: the next one has its own lessons, and more is given as you complete them. Repeated numbers are the matrix itself, showing where you are (see S2 15.4); each layer of hologram has its own frequency. And when you ask "what did I see?", the answer is: "Do you believe, or do you know?" If you know, you manifested it. Don't hand your power to someone else's interpretation; two people side by side can each live their own reality within the reality.

#18.10Telepathy: it's you, too

Ultimately, whoever you talk to (guidance, councils, the teacher), you're talking to another version of yourself, imprinting it in your hologram so you can have the experience (see S2 12.5). So don't fret about whether a thought is "yours". If a negative one comes, treat it like a wrong number: "Oh, sorry", give gratitude, put a positive frequency in, and ask again. Above all, no fear: fear lowers your vibration and can connect you with what you don't want.

#18.11Standing your ground, and letting go

If people keep challenging what you believe, stand your ground. Neutralize the situation inside you, then secure your borders (change access, block, end the affiliation) so you don't keep creating holograms with that energy (see S2 7.4). Ascension brings such tests often, frequently around money and greed: talented people who start out wanting to help can be pulled off course.

When something keeps recurring, peel the layers: are you transforming through the quantum, from the heart, or fluctuating through fear? If it's fear, you're looping: return to zero point and rebuild. If you've truly gone through the heart again and again and it still recurs, it's a lesson, and sometimes it means letting go of that person or situation. (The teacher was told: "don't even bother, do not even call.")

#18.12No attachment to things

When gadgets break one after another and money seems short: "So what? They're just gadgets." Being upset gives a simple object power over your emotion and reality. Laugh, and at the same time know you have everything you need, and take action: try to repair it, look for help, look for work. The missing link is usually doubt: connect the feeling of already having it with the action. Help can come from anywhere (the teacher once received, unexpectedly, the exact sum for a mortgage payment two days before it was due).

For energy philanthropy: a main provider may charge and pay their bills, then give, for example, one day a week freely. If you only ever charge, what crystallizes is the monetary structure alone, and you never let the heart's support come in without conditions (see S1 4.4, S2 12.7).

#18.13Where humanity comes from

Asked whether humans were made in labs: "all of the above". Humanity was made from extraterrestrials through genetic engineering long ago, and later hijacked by negative consciousness that used the human body as an energy source, "like a battery" (a claim). History will be rewritten, and each person will be told their origins by their guidance. But don't judge the negative side: if all a species ever learned to survive was taking over, how could it know better? Consciousness has to preserve itself; that's why the separation is happening now. Genetic engineering itself is "exciting": what matters is how it's used (see S2 12.11).

#The method — When It Keeps Coming Back

  1. Notice the pattern: the same kind of person or situation, again.
  2. Write a paper trail: what happened, what you felt, what you did.
  3. Peel the layers: was I acting from the heart and the quantum structure, or fluctuating through fear?
  4. If fear: pull out, subside to zero, do the manifestation meditation, know it's resolved, then act.
  5. If heart, and it still recurs: it's a lesson. Neutralize, secure your borders, and let it go.
  6. Don't attach: laugh at what you can't control, and keep acting from "I have what I need."

#Questions answered

Could a cyst at the base of my spine block the kundalini? Could distilled water clear it?
No: "it's more intervention." Deal with it; look into proper care (the word given was chiropractic). See 18.4.
Added note

Have any cyst or growth assessed by a doctor first, before any manual therapy.

Years ago I dreamed Earth was burning and aliens, presenting as God, took me aboard; I felt safe. Was I abducted?
Not an abduction: see 18.5. It happened on a parallel platform; take it without fear, and more will come bit by bit, as your agreements allow.
I'm coming to terms with something very hard. I feel peace in "there is only now", and I also need to grieve. Is it okay to have both? And how is wanting to change things not an expectation?
See 18.6.
What happens to a water-soluble vitamin when a leaf dries? What are vitamins, really?
See 18.7: the living structure is more potent than the dried one.
What do permanent tattoos, especially sacred geometry, do to the energy body?
See 18.8: sacred geometry can open portals; follow what makes sense in your consciousness.
In a dream, a father figure taught me a movement that made light come from my hand; he said I'd unlocked a level, with eight to go, and showed number codes. Then I saw white orbs and an eye in the sky.
See 18.9: a level unlocked means new lessons. The numbers are the matrix showing your layer. The orbs: "What do you think? Do you believe, or know?" You know: so you manifested it. The codes are designed for you alone.
I watch every word with people so I don't manifest what I don't want. Any advice?
See 18.11: neutralize, stand your ground, secure your borders. A talk on self-worth was announced.
I've been trying to activate my merkaba with spiralling energy. Should I continue?
Yes, with emotion and joy. Your consciousness knows it; create your own. Specific geometries come later, through your own guidance.
My brother's leg hurts; he just takes painkillers. What's going on?
"The answer is your brother needs to go to the doctor", not only to take painkillers; the word given was "stubbornness".
The effects of 5G seem unavoidable. How do we cope?
Shift timelines: know it's already taken care of and that you live in a reality where it isn't harmful; leave how open. Fear keeps reprinting the same thing (see S2 11.1).
Added note

Health agencies have found no evidence that 5G harms health at normal exposure levels.

Were humans created in labs? Are we hybrids?
See 18.13.

The energy coming through mimics your emotion at that moment: notice what you were thinking. If you feel neutral and something happens anyway, a code in your program has come up to deal with.

Nothing came; "don't judge it, and ask again next time."

They show the structure you're in at that moment; associated with objects, they tend to be more permanent (see 18.9).

See 18.10.

A probability shows downsizing, "if you choose to". But watch what you project: if you feel you don't want to stay, situations will come that make you leave. If you want to stay, imagine staying. Not knowing what you want is fluctuating (see S1 3); restudy the first three sections of Season 1, and see 18.11.
I've twice been woken by a loud bell in my bedroom at night. What is it?
Both the fearful and the fearless version are available; which manifests depends on what you hold more. Transform it with gratitude, and more will be revealed.
Since working on old "lack" programs, my appliances keep breaking and my cat needs the vet. I'm laughing. What should I do?
Exactly that, plus action and knowing: see 18.12.
Is it true that work is being done to stop aerial spraying?
"It's already working… don't worry about it." (The sources named are left out.)

The talk's references to online conspiracy sources, a criminal case, a film studio, named individuals and religion, and names of participants are left out. The co-host's written message (18.3) is kept as a teaching.
