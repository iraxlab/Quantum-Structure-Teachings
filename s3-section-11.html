Shrinking the Time Continuum for Faster Manifestation

Why a steady, unbroken positive frequency makes manifestation faster, the "puppy" way to meet someone who hurts you, finding what you love, knowing your limits, and questions on soul versions, shifting alone and panic (live talk)

The big idea

Manifestation is slow when your frequency keeps dipping into the negative and has to be rebuilt by repetition. When you hold the same positive frequency steadily, without breaks, thought, feeling and action become one continuous signal, a linear potency. The hologram then vibrates so strongly so quickly that it pops into matter much sooner: you shrink the time continuum, until, with training, manifestation comes almost in the now. The nine-day protocol was training for exactly this.

#Key teachings

#11.1The message

"To move forward, it will take bravery to face your fears and recognize the evil, to wake up, and then let go, to be able to transform in order to continue your journey; to understand that you are part of the one, the omnipotent, omnipresent intellect unit of the one particle consciousness." The material is getting more complex on purpose: the vocabulary itself holds a frequency, and learning it is part of building the structure.

#11.2How a thought becomes a timeline

In the quantum field, lines of frequency are infinite and combine by similar vibration. Give them motion (a thought, an emotion) and they become a waveform with a speed, moving in a spiral. Three similar vibrations combine into the one particle with the zero point at its centre (see S3 1.2); from there each movement lays a layer of hologram, like pages of a book running simultaneously. The guidance called the whole sequence a linear line, and spoke of "correctors" of it, because it corrects how all these frequencies work together. Combined, they form the magnetic and gravitational field, and action brings it into matter by density.

#11.3Shrinking the time continuum

While you fluctuate between negative and positive, the third reality (1 + 1 = 3, see S1 2.2) needs repetition after repetition to build. But if you keep choosing the positive through every fluctuation, the frequency becomes linear: non-stop, steady, very high, through the heart and a positive intention. "Focus only by neutralizing and recreating the thought in one non-stop, continuous frequency motion with steady intention", and the new hologram becomes potent very quickly, "and shrink the time rotation to now". It is "perception, potency and intention". Manifestations come faster and faster, by routes you don't know, so stay neutral about the how.

#11.4When something truly disturbing happens

Sometimes you're already positive and just stay there by knowing. But a horrific or deeply upsetting event can knock you out of control; then shift quickly by recognizing "you are in a hologram, almost like a virtual reality game, and you are the main character". That thought, held constantly, is "the generic platform for absolutely anything you want to do."

#11.5The puppy: meeting someone who hurts you

After being humiliated, the teacher was given an image by the council: if a little puppy scratches and bites you, you say "hush, silly" and move it gently away. A grown person can have the understanding of a puppy: "with no disrespect to the person", act with them as you would with a puppy or a small child. It made her smile, and neutralized it. From their perspective they're right, and from yours you are: both realities are true for themselves. If you're very angry, first remove yourself to another room and calm down completely (music, dance, a calming talk) before any action, or you'll loop the same timeline. Rejecting family, who are part of your structure, loops you back; transform it.

#11.6Finding what you love

Your purpose here is to hold the vibration for ascension, and you are "the most significant in your own hologram", whatever your work: your frequency carries into everyone near you, and the heart structure is far more potent than the negative. To find what you love: write down two or three things you're excited to try, practise each, see which truly makes you happy, then act on it consistently with the quantum structure.

#11.7Know your limits

Working at high frequency takes energy; the body has to handle it. "Find how much you are able to give", which is not the same as how much you're willing to give: the teacher limits her sessions and takes regular breaks. Don't feel guilty when you can't. Go back to nature: walking and hiking neutralize and heal. Body exhaustion was described as dense frequencies accumulating in a lower field until fractals shut down and you can't project properly.

#11.8You change yourself, not them

Hoping your grown children will change? Drop the expectation and let them continue in their momentum. Sitting in neutrality, you shift to a new earth and reprint them in it, thinking and talking differently (see S3 10.4). "You have nothing to do with their structure. What you have to do is your own." The same works for a partner in pain: be there, and meditate on him happy and healed, overriding with the positive whenever you see sadness.

#The method — Holding a Linear Signal

  1. Choose one thing to manifest, and feel that you already have it.
  2. Keep the same feeling all day: thought, emotion and action as one.
  3. Catch every dip at once: neutralize, thank, and return to the same feeling (11.3).
  4. Act from it, moment to moment, without waiting to see it.
  5. When something disturbing happens: "I'm the main character in a hologram" (11.4).
  6. Stay neutral about the how, and notice it arriving faster.

#Questions answered

Read as pressure on lymphatic connections between the muscles: deep circular massage on the knots, every other day, until sharp turns numb. "Always have a doctor's supervision": doctor first, then a massage therapist.
Is there a new monetary system?
Something is in place, digital and new, built so money can't disappear; it comes when needed. Don't reject the money structure, transform it (see S3 4.3).
I'm a starseed with healing gifts, but I'm always out of body and neglect my business. Any blocks?
"The blockage is only your perception": you're free, so act from that. Out of body, hold a positive frequency: the portal mimics your body's frequency. You can also open a portal with focused thought and clear intention.
Can we silence the mind? Where do thoughts come from?
"I am the source": thought comes from you, the one particle; look within, not outside.
Does an imprinted version of someone have a different soul?
Not another soul: another aspect of the one soul, which splits into many fractals to experience different timelines, all running at once.
Could I shift to a timeline and end up alone?
Energy is always there, but you choose whom to imprint. If you imprint no one, you'd be by yourself, in the void, because you chose it.
Does "no death in 5D" mean no one will die?
No. It means that over time the body and consciousness can be raised until, with contact, you shift rather than die; this is far off, step by step, and the heart holds the codes for contact.
