The big idea

We don't change the negative timeline; we create a reality above its vibration and shift into it, reprinting everyone in it. The quantum bylaw she was given: what you do from the heart (love, compassion, action) is held by consciousness "by the power of 10", plus 10% and more, because the heart's frequency is clear and precise while density is like swimming through mud. So if you keep taking positive action and hold your ground without stopping, you will always convert the negative; uniting multiplies it further. The negative ruled only because people didn't unite or act. A received warning about several cities is given in outline. No one is the messiah: you are, together with God consciousness.

#Key teachings

#12.1Create a reality above theirs

Negative structures must keep a negative frequency to reach their own timeline (transhumanism, a synthetic brain). You won't change them; you raise your frequency and shift into a new world. Shifting is fluid, like watercolor, so you see both tracks for a while and must keep maneuvering.

#12.2Protect the children

Look for alternatives outside the system; online schools and platforms already exist once you take action to find them. Read what is actually planned for children's education. "There will be no excuse" once you know. Neighbors, stay-at-home parents and small payments can help: in each reality you'll find help once you start creating it.

The message: the power of 10 (condensed)

Consciousness doesn't separate negative and positive; it corrects whatever tilts off balance. To shift levels, the heart multiplies: whatever your final frequency, it must go "times 10", collectively, "then we have 10 times 10", and at about 1000 a portal opens above the structure. "Love and compassion is always stronger on vibration than the negative density... whatever you do... from the heart is always going to be by the power of 10." "Positive light frequency from the heart is by 10 equals infinity to zero point, and this is when you shift." No expectations of how: know you already won. Uniting makes it "thousand times more" than one person alone. Even the word "potency" carries the 10.

#12.3Why the negative won before

They united through fear and strict rules in secret societies, while we didn't unite and left others to suffer alone, so our potency was far lower. Hiding behind the bushes doesn't help the collective in your own consciousness. When you act from the heart, the field gives back with a force of transformation, "but you're never going to know from where".

The message: the cities (condensed, as received)

From the hybrids, then the Council of Nine: be careful in New York City; disorder by design in several cities (Chicago, Los Angeles, Seattle); "in three steps all the negative will be stopped", not all at once, after "all negative on deck"; "it will be stopped from the American people". "The spark of light from love and compassion is stronger in potency than the dense negative impulses... 10% and more, if you act in your truth from your heart and stand your ground." "Transformation is not easy but it's absolutely necessary... The victory is already predetermined." Also: three days of disruption for New York and seven for Chicago and Seattle as probabilities, and bank changes without drama: "do not worry".

The message: no negotiating with a snake (condensed)

"You're in power and you must own it. Now is the time. Step up, own it, act on it, hold it... Unwilling to compromise. There cannot be a negotiation with a sick, dismantled mind... The snake is always a snake. You cannot change its nature." Dismantling their narrative is what saves you: stop following it, unite, and "all the deceptions of their illusion will crumble". (Peacefully: see 7.5.)

#12.4In the final test

Consciousness will test each person, sometimes subtly tempting you to do something against humanity to survive. Do the right thing, and around the corner something will wait for you. Being kind isn't being stupid; that ridicule is how the heart was separated.

#12.5You are the messiah

Never make one person your savior, however important their role. You are a fractal of God consciousness and hold the power yourself; follow your heart (see the "Marina's Day" video), and help pull others through in your own way.

#12.6Love your child

If a child wants to be a different gender, love them and don't reject them; at a soul level it may be part of their path. She regards pushing this onto very young children who can't choose as the wrong. She also learned the hard way how much a child's gut flora matters and had to educate herself.

#Questions answered

Is there a way to know what frequency we resonate at right now?
No single number: you flicker to a slightly different reality every moment, each a new planet with its own symphony in which you are one subtone. From moment to moment within one timeline the readings are very similar (to keep seeing the same room you reprint a nearly identical picture), yet you've already shifted to a new world.
Will the April 8th solar eclipse realign the ionosphere and help Earth rise?
Yes and no, depending on each person's perception and reality. Both sides have plans for that time and there will be fear and confusion. Stay positive and away from negative content: in a 5D reality you may notice nothing at all. If you find yourself in a challenge, thank the hologram and neutralize it; have fun with it rather than rejecting it.
