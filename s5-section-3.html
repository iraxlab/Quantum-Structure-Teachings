Shifting Timelines Through Neutrality

Why neutrality is not passivity; how a location's compliance locks it into the negative; acting through awareness, not fear; be the abundance you want to see; and questions on positive action, choosing the right action, and family who don't see (live talk, January 2022)

The big idea

Neutrality is the zero point: you see clearly what is happening, without anger or fear, and understand it. It is not sitting still. "Passiveness does not mean that you shouldn't take an action": if you see and do nothing, the frequency projected at you overtakes you, and your location's compliance locks you into their timeline. So the order is: see it, neutralize it by understanding, then act physically, through awareness, to mutate it. "In the heart through love, but at the same time strong as steel."

#Key teachings

Study first: S4 22 (direct programming, the analytical brain), then S4 2 (you are the field, the collapse of timelines). This talk builds on both. The Council named it: she wanted to call it "converting reality with power", and they said "shifting timelines through neutrality".

#3.1How you were locked in

The negative consciousness cannot exist above 3D, because it went so far into the negative it lost balance with consciousness. So it needs your body's pulsation, the resonance of your original codons: "you are like a live battery." It projects its narrative nonstop (TV, schools, rewritten history) so you vibrate in its frequency; when you project, you create a new version of reality mimicking that fear, and you stay locked in. Then the connection to your higher self shuts down, "and now they have you" (see S4 22). They also use technology, the Archon A.I. (see S4 40). But consciousness is ascending and will always find a way into your heart to wake you.

#3.2Neutrality is not passivity

When you wake up, a new hologram appears in your field showing what the negative is doing. If you think "that doesn't make sense... but I'm not going to say anything", you can't convert it: "whatever you see, you have to neutralize it by understanding what's really happening. But if you don't take an action to transform it, you won't manifest by shifting." Being passive from fear is compliance. It's a tug-of-war: when they change their attack, you change your maneuver. You don't sit on a battlefield saying "I love everybody, shoot at me." Pointing people to information is not spreading fear: "you project to create awareness."

#3.3A compliant location locks in

Each location in the field has its latitude and altitude (X and Y). If the people there, your peers, co-workers and bosses, comply, that location resonates in the negative and is reprogrammed; they can no longer see it, while you can. If you stay, the bombardment grows more potent and you sink "deeper into the mud", and later it is ten times harder to manifest out. So act: change job, relocate, gather people peacefully, call the right lawyer or doctor. Prepare food not from fear, but "so you mutate the structure without fear". Physical action is vital: without it you don't build the magnetic and gravitational field, you shift only to a similar timeline and keep looping.

#3.4Acting through awareness, not fear

She too went through fear, crying, anger and shock, then calmed down, researched and acted: that is how she projected the other version of herself. "I'm playing the game": "I see what you're doing in my hologram. I understand you. Thank you so much, I'm going to create my own reality." Then redirect attention to new solutions; if ten people shut the door, persist from the heart, and your actions start to come from your higher self. Building a bunker from fear of "the end of time" is their program: "it's the end of their time" and the beginning of new time. Consciousness will never self-destruct, "so that's why I understand we already made it."

#3.5Be the abundance

Whatever you put out comes back the same; the confusion is expecting how. When she wanted help for her son and was asked for huge sums, she became the opposite and started giving. "If you want to be abundance and see abundance, then be the abundance." Don't just take: give, with balance, whatever you can, not only money. What you project at another person (another version of you) is what returns. Glitches, such as a narrative suddenly changing on the news, are signs you've shifted timelines. Agreeing is consenting: "if you agree and you're compliant, they have you." Train new neural connections (neuroplasticity), so you recognize a projection instantly: "stop right there. I understand what you're doing, I'm in neutrality."

Tested by fame. Many who want to help change when fame and money come; all will be "tested to the core". The Council said awareness had reached 80% (left brief). Separation by gender, religion and ethnicity separates you from the heart.

#The method — Neutrality, Then Action

  1. See it: let the new hologram show you what the negative is doing.
  2. Calm down to the zero point: "I understand. I am going to calm down."
  3. Analyze with the analytical brain; peel the layers (S4 22).
  4. Don't consent: "I see what you're doing... I'm going to create my own reality."
  5. Act physically, through awareness: relocate, prepare, gather, call the right people, spread awareness.
  6. Persist from the heart, and change your maneuver each time they change theirs.

#Questions answered

Must the positive action relate to the specific goal, such as money?
Yes, and more: feel now, "like an actress", that it's already paid off; use the geometric meditation (S4 38). Embed that you have it, believe it 100% now, and act daily. A shut door means that situation didn't match the frequency: switch. Consistency shrinks the time to manifestation.
How do I know which action to take, heart or ego?
Through experience, the analytical brain, and peeling the layers. Ask whether you hurt the person; talk it out, without ego, not in anger but in neutrality. If they keep hurting you, back off and find new friends. If you're angry, calm down first: an action taken in anger shifts you into an angry outcome. "There is never a big lie or a little lie": your intention is what you accumulate. Be honest, kindly; take a punch or two, then stand your ground.
I share with family who don't see; is that action enough?
Project your truth and let them choose; you can't overtake their consciousness. Stay in neutrality: "not that you don't care", but you respect their choice (see S4 38, the etheric legions). It hurts, but it's their choice.
