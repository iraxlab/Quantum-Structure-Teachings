The big idea

A message called it a chess game, "cat and mouse", on God's board: the hidden "evil eye" at the top of power structures uses the innocent people of nations as pawns, making a nation suffer and then blaming others, to create wars; "all the world will see that it was not the nation, but the eye using the nation." The answer is from within: "to awaken me to come as your Messiah was all from within." God said: "I cannot just arise and help you... I need to see you physically doing the work." Whatever you project returns: "You ask, you do, I answer as the same." Hold your ground without consenting and without fear, act from the heart, use the system wisely, and synchronicity will bring what you need. The end result: "God wins, checkmate."

#Key teachings

Added note

Note: the recording repeats fragments of itself after the 6G message and ends there; only the first, complete pass is used.

#25.1Know what the dark does, without fear

She calls this a war between good and evil: some souls connect with dark forces to extract information through terrible deeds. To understand the structure, you must know what the evil is doing; she points to survivor testimonies of ritual abuse and mind-control programs, and films showing technology that controls soldiers. She prepared people step by step so they wouldn't go into fear.

Added note

Added note: named people and the specific accusations in this talk are left out. They are allegations from testimonies and documentaries, not established facts, and no person or group should be targeted.

#25.2Above the field

As you raise your frequency, you become a vibration above whatever is projected into the collective; even if they deploy technology, you hold the high resonance and shift into a hologram above their field, finding solutions by not consenting, as before. If you took something out of fear, don't worry: consciousness has its own position; simply don't consent to what comes next. You'll be shown the way moment to moment, but only when you take physical action. What can be put in the body by nanotechnology can, in her teaching, be taken out; the same system can be turned to the positive.

The message: the triple I (condensed, as received)

"The triple I... Protect the people of I. They are innocent." "The evil eye... is using the people of this country." "The secrecy will detonate in steps: confusion, chaos, evil words, blames, all shared, all lies, all using people as pawns on the chessboard." "People are replaceable... Who is to blame? Look at the one eye. This is all by design, all for control." "The snake has three heads. One is out, two more to go." "Order through chaos." "Soon you will see it all; the veil will be lifted": a nation is made to suffer and others blamed, "but... all the world will see that was not the nation but was the eye using the nation." "Stand up, unite and see how you were all lied to... Rise up, and help is coming." "New laws will be written and you will see the Messiah, but the Messiah will be in the body, eyes and souls only of the righteous ones."

Added note

Added note: this is a received message about contested world events, condensed and kept neutral. It says the people of these nations are innocent; in her teaching, no nation, religion or people is to blame. Predictions are probabilities, not certainties.

The message: the Messiah within (condensed, as received)

"You hold my heart spark, my breath you speak, my tears you shed, and I felt your pain... so you are part of me and I am part of you." "After your spoken breath of sound of pain, I have... answered your plea. So I was born from that energy." "And then you will know that to awaken me to come as your Messiah was all from within." Asked how long: "Seven... I can't tell you when... multiple ups and downs. It's a chessboard... Chess game, cat and mouse." "Remember that the board is mine. In the end humanity wins."

#25.3What it means

The Messiah isn't someone out there: consciousness mobilizes through you when you act from your heart, without causing pain; then you download the higher frequency that lights the path through the chaos. Ascension is singular: you don't change others; you change your vibration and shift into a pre-existing better field, which can include your family. Help will come in every form you need (money, shelter, food) if you don't consent.

#25.4Use the system; let synchronicity provide

Play the game: if you must drive a certain car or use a certain system, do so; if money is blocked, look for another system, a farmer, a neighbor. When she couldn't furnish her home in hard times, a neighbor moving away gave exactly what she needed, down to the table in the corner: "like a jigsaw, perfect synchronicity". Be a leader and an example; you'll feel fear, but move through it.

The message: "I cannot just arise" (condensed, from God)

"Who is really in power? Are the deceiver entities in power or God?" God is in power only when you shine through it. "I cannot just arise and help... I need to see you physically doing the work... when you move through some tests, then I am going to come in." "You need to experience it to learn... you need to raise the vibration to match these realities in order to exist in my high-dimensional spectrums." "It is the only way: through the heart, through compassion, through strong power." "How would you know what evil is... if you have never seen it, experienced it and freed yourself of it? Only then will I come."

#25.5Alchemy: white light holds every color

Paint white is made by mixing every color, even black: in pure light, all exists. To transform darkness you must know its vibration, like a painter who knows each color.

The message: the law of reciprocation (condensed, from God)

"If you did evil in my name, same to you. If you did lies in my name, same to you." It isn't condemnation but quantum structure: what you project returns. "Reciprocation is mimicking whatever you did... biofeedback, osmosis, like a toroidal field." "You ask, you do, I answer as the same... I collect your deeds, your thoughts, your words, mimic them, and then answer to you as the same." "I answer your true frequency field": your heart's true feeling, not the words. "Are you greedy, taking only for you? Are you a liar, speaking lies to gain more?"

#25.6Forgive; let them learn

She doesn't wish evil on anyone who harmed her: "Let them learn." There are no harmless lies; rather than lie, say "Can we table this and revisit it later?", because what accumulates is your true heart feeling. Those who took her work will in time be exposed; she holds her ground.

#25.7Inverted symbols

In her teaching, the dark took the quantum structure, inverted it and gave only fragments. A star symbol can be changed in spectrum, vectors and degrees so prayers summon a different frequency; the symbol itself can still be used positively. All religions were used to manipulate, whatever your faith.

Added note

Added note: these are her interpretations of religious and historical symbols; respect every faith and people.

The message: a field the Council will shut down (condensed, as received)

"No matter what frequency somebody puts, the frequency could be shut down. But it will not be shut down until we physically start doing something about it." A message warned of a plague, of technology placed in water so "the body will become like an antenna" for a 6G field, and of illness for those of low density. Asked what they will do: "No, it is not dangerous... The moment this technology is deployed... it will be shut down"; those awake and not consenting will vibrate in a protective shield, "hologram within the hologram". "The savior is from within."

Added safety note

Added safety note: these claims about 6G, water supplies, tumors, organ failure or violence are her account and received messages ("a fiction", in her words), not established medical or scientific fact. For any health concern, consult a qualified doctor, and rely on official guidance about water and telecoms safety.

#The method — Holding your ground in the chess game (as given)

  1. Know what the dark does, so it doesn't shock you into fear.
  2. Don't consent to what you know is harmful; stay calm.
  3. Act from the heart physically: the Messiah rises from within.
  4. Use the system as needed; look for other routes when one closes.
  5. Let synchronicity bring what you need; notice it.
  6. Remember the law: what you project comes back the same.
