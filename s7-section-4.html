You're doing it now: learn and share the information. Each positive act inflates the balloon of consciousness. If you feel negative, convert it; have the healthy, even heated, discussion until anger is released. Speak your truth with integrity.
How do I overcome the fear of speaking my truth?
Underneath it is fear of rejection. Speak from the heart, politely; if people reject you, that's their free will, so love them enough to let them be. Your truth is a potent frequency, a magnet: like-minded people come in and replace those who left. "Until you express your true self, you will never find your own pack."
Does her being further along mean less resistance for us?
It's an illusion of separation: you are projecting her and the guides, so you are downloading this because you're ready. Practice and express it yourself without expectations. "Shh, it's a secret." What she does, you can do, with consistency.
