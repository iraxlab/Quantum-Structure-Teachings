The message: the request for passage (condensed, as received)

An entity said she was one of the souls ruling a region: "Humans will not be for consumption anymore. It was a long tradition we were trained to do... this was our way of life." The Council of Nine interrupted: "We see you, Queen Selma." Asked whether she could stop the others, she said honestly: "No, I do not have the power to stop all of them... I can only access my quarters." She compared it to humans eating animals: "Would you stop at once?" The Council said: "Commander Marina, let us handle it from here... back off."

#52.5The lesson in it

Be very careful what you agree to; it is easy to be tricked. Stay objective: observe, analyze, and decide without too much emotion. Her answer was "I cannot make that decision", and she left it to the Council. "This is not an excuse; this is observe, analyze."

"We have huge methods to stop them... None of their acts will work... Mission accomplished... trust." "Look into interceptive DNA."
