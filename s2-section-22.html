Intimacy, Lust and the Heart Structure

A new download on intimacy, self-pleasure and lust; why romance keeps a couple's reality potent; separating honestly; freedom built by acts; and many questions (live talk)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

Sexual energy is creative energy, and it follows the same bylaw as everything else: create with pure intention. When intimacy comes through the heart, with the being you truly love, it builds a structure: two fields make a third, and at the summit the energy is potent enough to manifest the next reality. Lust is separated from the heart: its energy scatters across the matrix, builds nothing and slowly depletes you, which is why it ends in emptiness. A couple who stop the romance and only perform tasks become two separate, fluctuating structures. And in every situation, freedom isn't given to you: you construct it by your acts, moment by moment.

#Key teachings

#22.1Downloads follow what the collective understands

Information comes as a group learns. When the first quantum-structure talk wasn't understood, the next download explained the zero point and mobilizing structures, and then it clicked (see S1 1, S1 2). This download came unexpectedly, while reading, and it was given in their own wording, "a little bit twisted". Some structures the teacher is being shown now are beyond what she can explain, and she won't teach them until she has achieved and can show them: "I was taught quantum manifestation from scratch", so no one can tell her it's impossible. The teaching here is that we've been shown intimacy in a way that separates it from the heart (through films, books, social media and TV), and so loops us into artificial structures.

Safety note

The teacher mentioned that her next protocol involves an extreme diet close to living without food. Do not attempt this: going without food is dangerous and can be fatal. Any change of diet belongs under a doctor's supervision.

#22.2Self-pleasure: only with pure intention

Self-pleasure should be engaged with pure intention, with the being you truly love; then the frequency is pure. Used only as a release, the energy has no intention and no cause: it becomes artificial, does not fulfil you, and depletes you, because nothing returns as a manifestation. So whatever you imagine, let the image be of the being you truly love. If not, the energy scatters without potency and never becomes a targeted frequency that grows into a reality hologram, and you'll find it hard to manifest a person who truly loves you. "When you create, create with pure intention. It is a bylaw you all must learn."

From the booksDP records this download almost word for word under "Intimacy And Self Pleasuring / Lust": "In order for the structure to be precise it must come from pure love and only love."

#22.3Lust is emptiness

"Lust is emptiness, and it will never manifest your love." It loops the same energy you keep projecting, never satisfies, and can lead into a downward spiral of ever emptier self-indulgent acts "that eventually may finish you". The body's and the brain's reaction "has nothing to do with the purity of your heart love structure": you must learn to see and feel the difference (see also S1 3.8). This isn't a judgement of pleasure ("that doesn't mean it's wrong"); it's that lust is a different structure.

#22.4Two structures: love builds, lust scatters

The love structure. It begins with foreplay, which is fractal: talking, enjoying each other, for example over dinner. Each moment is a parallel reality building a potent frequency (see S1 2.7). Feeling goosebumps when you talk is the sign of that energetic field. In lovemaking, his structure and hers create a third structure (see S1 2.2, 1 + 1 = 3). At the summit, the combination of the magnetic and gravitational fields is so potent that the moment can manifest much of what you want, and it projects the next reality: you stay a strong family whatever you go through.

The lust structure. Attraction to the body alone ("oh my god, he's gorgeous") never develops the frequency connected to the heart, so it is artificial, even when it feels potent. Its energy at the summit doesn't go into a structure: it scatters all over the matrix. Repeated, it accumulates in scattered sequences and depletes the heart structure. That is why people with many partners, even attractive or successful ones, can feel blank, unhappy and unable to find the right person: they never projected a heart frequency into their hologram to find someone who truly loves them. In her picture: love goes through the heart to the 5D platform; lust stays in 3D.

#22.5Keep the romance alive

Couples should never stop loving and respecting each other, and should keep the romance going: go out, talk, learn to please each other, say something pleasurable to each other. Without it, the marriage becomes tasks (kids, work, bills) performed by two people who no longer connect; the family's base is only "collective energy", and the platform of the reality you were creating together becomes "non-existent". Then the two become separate structures that fluctuate, one going up as the other goes down, never synchronizing, and "this is how we end up in divorce, how you end up looking for somebody else". When you keep making love with passion, the structures rebuild over and over, and the frequency of that reality stays potent. Life can be spiced up between the two of you; it doesn't have to be anything extreme.

#22.6Falling in love with someone else

If feelings fade in a long marriage and you fall in love with another being, then for it to be pure and to manifest, that other being must love you in equal amount. Whether to stay or move on depends on the individual situation: children, obligations and more. If the love is pure on both sides, it creates a potent energy that, with guidance, through platforms, agreements and parallel realities, will manifest; at this level it must be true purity, or it won't work. If you stay in an unhappy marriage for the children or other obligations, revisit what is truly important (children, family) and whether there is abuse; this is decided individually, with the higher councils (see S1 1.8). Keep gratitude to your family: many leave for the rush of a new feeling and hurt children and a partner who may still love them.

From the booksDP gives both questions and the council's answers in the same words, adding that you print everyone into your own hologram and matrix.

#22.7Books and films: put the one you love in the picture

A romantic or erotic story can make the energy shatter if your heart goes to the character. Instead, imagine the main character as the person you truly love. Switching that in your mind sets your blueprint on the heart structure; this is how you switch. It applies to imagination only: it doesn't mean being with one person while thinking of another, which is a physical act and a completely different energetic field.

#22.8A marriage built on the heart, and an honest parting

A marriage built on money has a weak base: when challenges come, the partner who married for money won't fight for it, and everyone gets hurt, collecting karma that passes to the children. A marriage built on true love can always find bridges: talk, reconnect, go to counselling if you must, and keep the heart frequency up. It's a lesson, and if you collapse with it, you'll keep repeating it (see S1 1.5).

If you no longer love the person, discuss it and separate as friends, not as enemies: "we'll be great parents and best friends". Honesty makes it pure, and there is no harm done. Staying while you don't love the person, and lying or acting out from boredom, is deception and a completely different structure. Much of what keeps people in marriages is the contract structure of society; imagine a caring society where housing, food and work were no longer at stake: marriage would no longer be a contract to feel obliged by.

#22.9You are already a master

"You are a master already": you manifest every day, projecting your hologram even now; the limitation is only your own perspective. What makes it hard is how deeply the wrong idea of structure is embedded: the brain needs time to crystallize a new concept that's the opposite of what we were told. Bilocating the whole body into another reality is possible, but it needs the protocol of mastery: diet, conscious contact with your guidance, and a lighter molecular structure that takes in more frequency (see S2 4, S2 21.3).

From the booksSD: to "become a multidimensional body vessel" you increase the body's molecular vibration, and "then you can easily connect with full memory" of less dense realities.

#22.10Freedom is constructed by your acts

Asked about the planets and humanity's development, the council said the structures are individual. Many platforms promote fear and self-indulgence; choosing those media inflicts holographic imprints you then reprint daily (see S1 9.9). "You never understood that you are already free." You can create a new Earth, but only by knowing the structures and projecting them daily by your acts: "you cannot simply wait for somebody else to do your freedom." Action creates the potency that shifts you, and the planet is already ascended: you're experiencing it moment by moment, and through that fractal momentum you become a master of creation.

From the booksDP: "As you expand your multi-dimensional understanding so is your personal holographic expression and the people that are able to exist on it."

#22.11In chaos, be the best of yourself

For anyone in a hard or chaotic situation: observe, and when a situation presents itself, be the best of yourself in the name of humanity. Hold a knowing with no fear, and you'll be shown the bridge to safety: food, help and a positive outcome. Train your consciousness to see that you are in a hologram, a game in which you are the main character and can open doors. Whatever you do in this moment is what you project into the next: "this is a quantum bylaw." Fear is a stronger vibration than joy and creates more of the same: "to escape the forest, you end up in a forest" (see S2 15.2, S2 15.6).

#22.12Releasing a lingering connection

After a deep relationship ends, you may still feel the person. There are two modalities. If the love was mutual and you separated because of obligations, the other person can tune their frequency toward you, and you can feel it. If it isn't returned, you are re-projecting your own frequency because you loved so much: an illusion you keep looping. To stop it: pull yourself away emotionally, pull out and call it a hologram, give gratitude, calm yourself completely in that understanding, then act on it and change your emotion, until you're no longer looping and are free of the pain (see S1 7). It's challenging and takes time, especially if you really loved the person.

#22.13A wise person transforms themselves

In a family conflict: "A wise man does not fight with evil; a wise man transforms himself." You cannot change them; change yourself. It's a lesson: pass through it and don't let it pull you down. There is nobody outside you; this is your hologram (see S2 9.6). Protect the vulnerable from being drawn in, and make your own decision.

#The method — Keep the Heart in Intimacy

  1. Know the difference: notice whether you're moved by the heart or by the body alone (22.3).
  2. Build it before: go out, talk, enjoy each other; foreplay begins the structure (22.4).
  3. Keep the romance in a long relationship: please each other, not only tasks (22.5).
  4. Imagine only the one you truly love, alone or with a story or film (22.2, 22.7).
  5. At the summit, feel love and joy: that is the frequency you project.
  6. If love has ended, say so honestly, and part as friends (22.8).
