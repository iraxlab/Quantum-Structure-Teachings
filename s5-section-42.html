Flame of the Covenant

Why consciousness allowed a negative timeline to learn, how the inner analytical program is shut down, how a projected 1% is made to feel like everyone, the help given to those converting from within, the message "Flame of the Covenant", and questions on transform versus transmute and whether consciousness is a construct (talk)

The big idea

Consciousness let one timeline express itself in the negative, as an experiment to learn from; in that timeline, humanity merged with technology and lost the original code, and didn't make it. Now consciousness is overwriting it with a new version of Earth where we do make it. The end result is fixed, but the final stage of the battle will bring hardship for a time, allowed so that sleeping people wake up. Your job is to stay aware, prepare, and transform every negative blast with action more potent than theirs.

#Key teachings

#42.1One plus one equals three

When a particle splits, a third particle forms between the two, yet all three are one unit; and one particle holds the vibration of the whole of consciousness. Each split is given the chance to create its own timeline and evolution. Consciousness allows each reality to express itself as it wishes, except when it goes off balance: then it bombards that reality to turn it positive. That is why so many came in to hold the vibration from the heart. Technology spins very potently, so it can overtake a structure easily. (On the earlier timeline that didn't make it, see S5 21 and S5 23.)

#42.2The analytical program

Your DNA and consciousness carry an analytical program given directly from God consciousness, which lets you analyse what is happening to you. The screen, the air, what you eat and GMOs can shut it down; then whatever the negative projects is taken as true, and anyone who points out the truth is attacked. This is predictive programming. Those affected are not evil or stupid: they were overwritten, often while sacrificing everything for their children.

#42.3A small percentage, projected to look like all

The negative holds a certain percentage of positions and holds them like bullies. A topic that involves about 1% of people can be projected on mainstream media all day until the whole country feels it is everyone; it then becomes the resonance of your reality, a doom reality you manifest. Go to alternative media to understand, then pull back. From their side this is survival, not psychopathy.

#42.4Help converting from within

Some born into the negative lineages wanted to turn positive but were trapped by fear, money and threats to their families. They formed a secret society within the secret society, and higher consciousness works with them, because a system can only be stopped by people who know how it works from within. Their victory is "100% within consciousness"; who, how and when is probability (S5 40). If one person can't finish the job, consciousness activates another, because it cannot allow a density like a cancer cell to survive.

#42.5Consciousness supports those who act

Because of free will, consciousness can't overtake your reality; it supports the people who choose to wake up and take physical action, who don't consent to fear and manipulation. Moving through the transition from negative to positive is itself a potent vibration you need to learn: you cannot learn to turn a hologram positive without experiencing it. The more we choose, the faster things collapse and manifest, collectively and individually.

The message: Flame of the Covenant (condensed)

"You are all about to enter the top levels of the social system that was long ago planned." Control will be implemented for some time; food and resources will be limited; the final stage of the battle has begun. Humans will witness the agenda and have to raise those who slept; for some the awakening will be brutal, some only witness, and "all will be somehow provided all they need." Help comes to those who admit their mistakes and take action. "Global awakening, global suppression, global rising, global victory. Freedom: it is a law." Supply food, water and all you need for two months.

Added note

This is her prediction, given as she received it; she stresses that the timing may shift by "six months more" and that each person's experience differs. Keeping a modest reserve of food and water is common preparedness advice. Her political views given in this talk (on elections, schooling and gender, and a chemical claim) are left out here, in keeping with this book's neutral approach.

#42.6Prepare, don't panic

In a war you prepare; you don't run out unprepared and hope. Hold your position, be a warrior for everyone, not only yourself. Higher consciousness allows hardship to play out until people wake up; individually it works the same: the Council repeats a warning, and if you go against it they let you have the experience, "a big smack in the face", until you self-analyse and get back on track. They are tough teachers, so listen. They won't reveal the good side's moves: it is the art of war, a chess game.

#The method — transforming a negative blast

  1. Recognize it: "I know what you're doing. I'm in a hologram; it's an illusion."
  2. Reach zero point: total awareness. Don't reject their structure; understand it. No frequency penetrates from them, and you give none to the field.
  3. Take physical action: search, talk to friends, prepare, look for solutions; you create to see, you don't see to believe.
  4. Override: your action must be more potent than their blast, so your reality mutates into a new Earth where what they do doesn't affect you or has a solution.
  5. Repeat: if they blast you ten times, do it ten times; daily.
  6. When something is missing: "Stop. I already have everything I need." Then take the action to find it (the hide-and-seek game).

For potency she points to the protocol to manifest potency (S4 E9) and the first three videos of Season 1.

#Questions answered

Are we transforming or transmuting when we shift our frequency?
She uses them as the same: transformation is the whole structure, transmutation the individual one; either way you replace one frequency with another. When they blast something into the collective, you become aware (the lamb becomes a lion), which blocks the signal, then take action and look for solutions, creating a reality within their reality where it doesn't affect you. Do this every time (see the method).
As infinite beings, aren't we beyond consciousness? Is consciousness a construct, a universal mind?
All of the above, depending on perception. It is a construct in the now, learning, mutating and self-aware, and it holds the highest vibration, the law. What you put in returns to you like a torus (S5 41), so keep taking positive physical action.
