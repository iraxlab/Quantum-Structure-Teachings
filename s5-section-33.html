Message From The Hybrids / The Help For The Abused Children

A difficult "dark to light" talk: why a structure that cut off the heart must harvest energy through fear and pain; how abuse fragments a soul; the hybrids' promise that every abused child's soul spark will be restored, and how; brief predictions; and questions on helping, healing abuse, memory, why the dark shares our timeline, and supporting friends (live talk)

Content note

This talk deals with the abuse of children. It is summarized here at the level of teaching only, without details. If you were harmed, or know a child at risk, please reach out to local child-protection services, a crisis line or a trusted professional.

The big idea

The heart is the key that opens portals and draws life force from God consciousness. A consciousness that cuts itself off from the heart and compassion cannot sustain itself, so it can only survive by harvesting the energy of others through fear and pain; it locks itself in a loop of its own dark reality. Such cruelty can fragment a child's soul, producing split personalities. The hybrids' promise: "Not one single soul will be lost." Each fragmented spark will rest in a healing medium and be re-ignited by mother consciousness, rebuilding awareness slowly and gently. And those who harmed carry a frequency that consciousness tracks "like a laser": no one can hide.

#Key teachings

#33.1Why the dark must harvest

The body is like a battery holding a spark of God consciousness. Positive actions engage the heart (its geometry, the chestahedron, S5 25), which downloads prana and opens stargates to other realities. Engage in dark actions and you open dark pockets in consciousness, where entities teach how to harvest energy. Having cut off the heart, the only way to sustain that timeline is to take energy through fear and pain; and what they consume is the reflection of another version of themselves, a loop that "pokes a hole" in the field and reprints the same dark reality (S5 28). It gives the illusion of invincibility.

#33.2How abuse splits a soul

Extreme suffering can make the spark pop out of its fractal moment, where it can be captured and the protective layers of the field broken (see "What is Direct Programming?", S4 22), leaving split personalities that can be switched by a trigger word. In effect, separate parallel realities were created for one soul. Children's brains are most malleable, which is why they were targeted. Such groups infiltrated religions, parties and celebrities; many in these bloodlines were born into it and conditioned as children. "No judgment": but no one can hide.

Added note

Some claims she repeats from videos she recommends (for example, about "adrenochrome" harvested from victims) are not supported by evidence. The teaching here is about energy, fear and the heart; please treat specific allegations about named people or groups with care.

The message: the restoration of the children

"Three months from now, your human societies will know the truth" about how the planet was used "like a farm" to harvest energy; "the shock will be tremendous... dark to light." "Blood was shed to free you... Even we weep tears for you." Those who harmed are tracked: what they took "had a code that was restored into their DNA... like a tracking device... we know each one of them." "The human children that sacrificed themselves for you, so you can awaken, will be held in [a] hyper-simulative stage of aquarium... silica water, the recovery in one single spark of the soul consciousness. There you will sleep for some time, and the new collective... mother God consciousness will give... a little electrical frequency spark to ignite your soul... and your soul will recover and will start again... create first small memories and... parallel singular realities." "It will be short and easy... none will cause a stress into the soul spark." "Let us promise you that not one, not one single soul will be lost. All children will be restored in God consciousness. You are all loved, you are all God sparks."

#33.3How a spark rebuilds

She saw a gentle, luminous light-blue liquid, "like a little spark of light of a baby", many of them, like a nursery. Stage one: just a pulse of awareness ("I'm here"), held until it is comfortable and potent. Stage two: more single memories, each shift a new one, held again. Then a short linear timeline, until it can hold a location in the matrix and know who and where it is. A soul needs enough potency to hold each new hologram it shifts into.

The message: the crescendo (brief)

A planned "blasting within the field" (she was given the 28th) "is not going to work". Hidden "true followers" in any party will be revealed; "November, December... wins the battle, and in January is a victory" (she gives end results, not who or how). Don't watch mainstream media: "total garbage." From March 2023 the evidence will be given to everyone, "not turning back." Beware AI-faked videos of people; trust your sources. "The fifth-dimensional Earth is fully... constructed and... ready for all human souls."

#Questions answered

What can I do to help these children, besides spreading the word? Do meditation and prayer help?
Meditation and prayer spark the collective frequency; physical action crystallizes. Even choosing to listen, without rejecting it, was an action; it creates synchronicities for the next action (sharing, speaking), a chain reaction. You transform your own world from the single particle into your collective.
Will the soul spark be applied to all abused souls, including those abused by parents? Will it clear entities?
Yes: "a major cleanup." The extreme cases need this method. For others, there is no past: the version of you that was abused no longer exists, and you can create a new lineage of reality (also for drinking or alcohol). Meditation alone won't do it: forgive yourself first, then take real physical action for your recovery; repeating the same drama makes recovery very hard.
Will these souls remember other lifetimes?
Not in the beginning: first only a pulse of existence. Later perhaps, but a memory of something long ago, in a different frequency, won't destroy them.
Why do dark ones occupy the same timeline as we do?
Consciousness was learning: each split particle may create what it wishes, so the negative had to be allowed. When it took so much energy, like a black hole, consciousness, which never self-destructs, created a parallel timeline to fix it. It has learned and will never again allow this: it will recognize the pattern and shut it down, while keeping it in the archives, because you learn through history. That is why the negative erased history in schools.
Friends are losing their freedom through money, partners and jobs. I support them; what else can I do?
"You've done what you need to do." Allow their experiences: being brought to one's knees can be a cleaning out and a new start, like a business owner who learns and rises again. Be present and give as you wish, in your own talent: paint a house, build for someone, fund a community. Change happens through action, not only posting and wishing.
Added safety note

If you have experienced abuse or are struggling with drinking, please consider support from a qualified therapist, a doctor or a support group alongside these practices.
