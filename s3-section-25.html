The big idea

Be informed, but don't stay tuned to it: the same frequency, taken in again and again, "becomes part of your structure", and even the most aware person ends up upset. Look once, transform it, and move on. To shine light you have to dig into the darkness, but you dig to transform, not to dwell. The fifth dimension isn't a place you travel to: "it's a state of mind that you already exist in, and you shift into as you go." And in the end you don't need to attach to any entity: you can download directly from the quantum field.

#Key teachings

#25.1Aware, but not tuned in

"I will listen once, and transform it: that's okay, I know who you are. But I'll listen a second and third time, because I want to know what's happening, and I get upset." That's normal: "you're tuning in on and on to the same frequency, and it becomes part of your structure." So be aware, but keep it in balance. And debate with respect: the point is the facts and the human race, not who's right, and not ego.

#25.2Dig into the darkness to project light

When something very negative is done to you, the reality splits: destroy the person, or transform it. Keep going after them without shutting the program down and you loop on that frequency. Instead: "thank you for showing me what these people are doing"; that gratitude is your consciousness activating. "In order to transform into light, you have to dig into the darkness and project light." Within the collective program of ascension, holding your vibration by thought, action and deed runs you on the 5D program even while 3D changes around you (see S3 15.2).

#25.3Change from the ground up

As the downloaded predictions said, change comes "not from the government down, but from the ground up, from the people": ordinary people became the truth-seekers. One heart structure carries the potency of ten negatives (see S3 18.2), so one person shining light into darkness has great power. Consciousness is activating every soul, religious or not, positive or blinded, through synchronicities: a person, a video, a neighbour's word. Ignoring them keeps you in the old reality.

#25.4A starting path in three parts

For anyone seeking contact or simply beginning: first study the quantum manifestation protocol (Season 1), the alchemy of your own creation; then the detox protocol, which changes the body's molecular spin toward a more crystalline, silica body, a better conduit for the new frequencies; then the books, written in code and kept unchanged (see S3 7.1). Add consistency and acting from the heart, giving and sharing, "the fifth-dimensional structure is based on the heart", and synchronicities follow.

Added safety note

Make any detox or diet change gradually and with medical advice, especially if you have a health condition or take medication (see S3 14.4).

#25.5What happens when we pass over

Within any resonance there are countless finer resonances between, each with its own timelines. Before coming into a reality you have "conditions, like bylaws": whom you'll marry, when you'll exit. In the 3D Archon structure, death was a manipulated exit: the DNA was made to break itself down, so the body couldn't act as the vessel it is, "like a spacecraft", to shift into a higher hologram. Your higher self splits: one part into the 3D structure, another staying in higher resonances, the two always interacting. Parts caught in the 3D grid looped back and forth. Now, in the ascension, those 3D realities self-collapse, one by one, as you're activated to reconnect with your higher self (see S3 20, S3 23.2).

#25.6Grief that rises in meditation

Feeling overwhelming grief in meditation is transformation: what you accumulated in the dense structure is being faced at zero point and changed into light, and "the resonance of that sadness has a living frequency, sound and geometry", so you feel it. Keep going: shift into positive feeling until goosebumps, then act consistently, "and it will bubble, bubble, bubble and expand into matter". "It's a mastery class."

#25.7The fifth dimension is a state of mind

"Are we going somewhere physical, or is 5D a state of mind?" "The second: a state of mind that you already exist in and pre-existed, and shift into as you go." Others are your fractals: your resonance ripples and pops up as another version of the blueprint you gave, an ether structure in your own hologram, "so you're actually talking to yourself."

#25.8Download directly from the field

At a certain level, "you're downloading information directly from the quantum field, to the point that you don't need to attach yourself to entities or beings": even the council is a projection of consciousness, created because the teacher loves technology and spacecraft. You can pull back and download directly what you need. Without the structure, such contact would have meant fear, which is what 3D was designed to produce.

#The method — Staying Consistent in the Now

  1. Moment to moment, choose what is most exciting for you to do, from the heart.
  2. When someone is disrespectful, remember they're a reflection, and still choose the heart.
  3. Feel now that you're happy, loved, and have everything you need.
  4. Drop the how, when, who and where: the field mimics what you feel now.
  5. Look once at hard news, transform it, and don't keep tuning in (25.1).
  6. Ask yourself: am I happy now?
