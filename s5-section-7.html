The big idea

Every sound carries a geometric pattern and can construct or deconstruct anything, a glass, a person, a society. Projected nonstop (media, algorithms, music), it overrides the analytical process and reprints a mind in its resonance; used positively, it restores. Because we were locked in by technology, we also need technology to get out: the Galactic Federation is sending measured frequency, individually, to shut down what destabilizes the body; restoring the DNA is more complex and comes through your guides. Meanwhile: no fear, positive action, compassion.

#Key teachings

#7.1How a society is dumbed down

She saw the control system as "an artificial mechanism" placed at every level of society, the lowest and the highest, "like a machine that is self-operating": dumb down generations through schooling, then place them in offices where they turn on their own people. Algorithms push the most inappropriate content to the front, so children absorb that narrative nonstop. Projected frequency shuts down the analytical process and replaces it with the resonance they want (see S4 22). "There is a geometric pattern and a sound wave" in that frequency; with it you can construct and deconstruct an individual or masses. Watch what your children watch, play and listen to; pulling many children at once from a school district has power. Expect infiltration of real movements by false acts.

The message of the hybrids and the Council: help through frequency

"The characteristics of cells of humanity [are] increasing in awareness." The negative hoped to reduce the population to 25%; "we say the opposite: 75% will make it, and 25% will not be energetically fuelled." Of those who make it, "by percentage" will have to restore their immune system and DNA; "the signals... will be given with technology of the spacecraft." Because the Archon matrix was built with advanced technology, the positive side must use more advanced technology to penetrate it, while consciousness itself raises the frequency like a ripple.

"No matter what technology may be inside your body... when you put frequency sound... it will shut down and short anything within the body." The body must first be relaxed, and it must be monitored individually: frequency can be sent globally to shut systems down, but reforming the immune system and DNA "will be individual", through your guides and extraterrestrial family, with positive organic A.I.

"There will be a sound undertone sent from the Galactic Federation to shut down a technology that was implemented to make your body unstable", usually at night, in the half-sleep state; "within three to five weeks" of the signal the body begins mutating, flushing through urine, waste and saliva: "a zap, an electrical shortage... Humans, if you only knew what was done to you. But we say have no fear... help is here." For some, tissue repair won't be possible and they will exit by design (see S4 38).

"In the months to come, the convoy of the truckers will become global. They will be infiltrated to some extent by false acts... But that will shine more light." To the negative: "Don't you understand that it's over? Stop, just stop." To us: "watch for the false flags." If money is stopped: barter, share, cook together, exchange clothes, "to the point that the monetary system cannot touch you." (Kept brief: dated events left out.)

Her warning: frequency machines

"You cannot just put a frequency vibration into your body without knowing how much you need for it and how long. Who is measuring that?" She would not put money or trust in machines that claim to restore your frequency: precise measurement of your molecules' pulsation needs super technology; "no machine that is going to measure something there by a biofeed is completely precise."

Added safety note

The claims about technology inside bodies being zapped and flushed out are her views, not established fact. Please make health decisions with your doctor.

#7.2The choice for those in positions

Officers, teachers, school-board members, lawyers, doctors: you will be asked by the chain of command to do something you know is wrong, with your job or money at stake. If you say no in the name of humanity, "you will be supported of every shape and form", because you resonate in the new structure; if you keep suppressing, you lower your vibration and that timeline collapses on itself. See their side from neutrality: a consciousness that knows no compassion lives on yours, "you are basically their food", and understanding it keeps you from anger.

#7.3The mother consciousness

Within consciousness there is a "mother consciousness", a mother wave, that holds every individual's original DNA and blueprint, a very specific sound and pulsation, preserved; where, they won't say (see S4 44). She saw it "like a soup of this blue light... almost gooey looking", flickering, "holding all the data."

#Questions answered

Are the tones I hear before sleep the frequencies being sent?
They won't specify; don't put a negative input on it. A very low rumble-like tone working on you in sleep is not good, but "it will be stopped", globally. Keep doing the positive.
Can we hear the ships' tones? Is it a low hum?
Not necessarily: it's sent in fractals, by a federation of ships, each over a location, measured in real time, so she can't say who, when or how.
Can tuning forks and singing bowls shift my hologram?
Yes, as a permission slip that lifts your mood, which is good. But it's "very primitive": overriding the negative needs a very precise, potent sound wave.
Have the signals already started?
"Yes, in fractal. We have not stopped... How do you all think you're still maintaining to be alive?"
Will the 5D still show this fluctuation?
Yes: you see the negative and positive together as you move into 5D. Those who choose to act in the name of humanity will be in the structure for full mutation; no one's consciousness can be overtaken.
Is the high-pitched ringing I hear part of the frequency?
She can't say individually: it has many possible causes, including parasites; check everything.
Can I play subliminal positive frequencies at home for my children?
They're already exposed to them: know it 100%. Music or tuning forks can enhance your belief; then act on it.
How do I raise my frequency daily?
Study Season 1, 15 minutes at a time; live a Marina day; act with compassion, without expectations. In a situation: pull back, "I'm a hologram", disengage from the illusion.
Must I be at a higher frequency to understand all this technology?
No: words from compassion carry a sonic geometry that penetrates; your body is geometry and mutates to it. How much potency depends on how actively you participate. Keep compassion even for the negative.
