The big idea

"Consciousness is physics": separating the two was the manipulation. Sound and frequency create geometry; geometry is a live ether structure; at a certain density it becomes matter. So the number codes she downloads are not random: each number is a frequency, and combinations build geometry, up to a whole torus. #Key teachings

#33.1The prophecy: a free mind

A prophecy was given in symbolic language: an awakening call ("a trumpet") sounds through the land; the awakened "are sheep no more" and become lions, while those who choose to stay blind to the facts stay in darkness by their own choice. Its key line: "Your mind must also be free. The free mind will change your perception, and your perception will change your reality." "It is God's choice, and God you are." She says you may interpret it as you wish; choosing ignorance is a choice that brings what you wish for.

#33.2Sound makes geometry

She showed videos as "scientific proof": water moving in set patterns at around 24 Hz and reversing when the frequency changes; a single particle vibrating, pulsating and turning into geometry as the frequency changes; objects lifting and metal breaking under sound. The lesson: everything is pulsation; the one particle is ether surrounded by a matrix; frequency shapes it. That is why, in her books, sound can be used to control water and fire. "Teach your children sacred geometry."

Healing with frequency and sound: "teach your children and look into the information of Dr. Raymond Rife's frequency machine". She points to the full documentary (about five hours), in the Freedom Awakening sub-channel of her Harmonic Reactor channel. In her account, Rife could measure the frequency of a cancer cell and neutralize and heal it through frequency sound, and was destroyed for it; she showed a recent video of a machine whose sound frequency kills cancer cells such as leukaemia cells under the microscope: "that was discovered from Raymond Rife years ago".

Added safety note

No frequency or Rife machine is a proven treatment for cancer in people. Killing cells in a dish is not the same as curing cancer in a body. Please keep to your oncologist's care; anything else, only alongside it and with their knowledge.

#33.3Numbers form a torus

In vortex mathematics each corner of a figure is a number, and number patterns build a torus, constantly moving, like the cones she draws (see S3 31.2). That is what her downloads do: each code is a set of "point locations" that forms a live geometry in your field. If her codes were measured by technology, "you would see the geometry of that code". Listened to with trust, it becomes live structure in you anyway.

From the booksFrom her books (HR): the double torus is "the two molecules of the one"; the torus is strong only in combination with the URON (the atom at higher vibration). The whole of HR is "download codes": some sentences don't make sense because they're laid out as geometry to activate DNA and RNA.

#33.4What each hertz does

In HR ("Frequency and sound") each number has an action, and combinations carry meaning: 2 opens the field and 8 stabilizes it, so 28 Hz shows a certain potency; a hum such as 528 makes a different geometry again. Change the frequency and you get a completely different geometry. This builds on S1 19.

#33.5How self-healing works

A healing body needs light and resonance flowing in and out "with no blockage", a constant yin-and-yang cleansing ("reverse osmosis"). Technologies lowered human resonance so that vortices spun only inward; with no outflow, cells degenerate and can't regenerate, unlike an octopus regrowing an arm. As you change your perception, the new resonances from the galactic central sun layer into your body; cleansing and water as a conductor (see S3 30.2, S3 31.2) carry them through the cells, and you become "a live torch" of frequency. Beings who hold higher vibration stay youthful for thousands of years.

#33.6A fractal of days and years

Do we have a 5D body? "Yes indeed", running at the same time; for now you get glimpses as you fluctuate. Each day is a fractal of your week, each week of your month, each month of your year, each year of your hundred years, and so on, "until all the combination of vortexes become one particle". At a certain understanding you'll "download the entire information in one unit": manifestation is in the now.

#33.7Pain is a frequency to recognize

"You cannot skip certain lessons" (see S3 19.3): pain is a frequency to be recognized. Let it go through the body "without rejection", so you can bring its resonance into awareness and transform it. At the same time take actions to look for solutions: you'll create a synchronicity that brings the cure or an easing. "You're already healed", but you have to create the reality to see it. If one platform doesn't work, don't be discouraged: "you don't give up, you don't give up".

Added note

Constant pain needs medical care. Her teaching is to keep looking for solutions as well as working with the frequency: keep seeing doctors, and try another if one doesn't help (see S3 32.7).

#33.8Bilocation and portals

Portals are based on perception and understanding of your quantum field: every shift of thought, emotion and action creates an ether geometry that can shift "like a spacecraft". Bilocation: recall or create the field of a place, know it already exists, return to the now, signal that you want to be there, and act "in simply knowing it". It needs knowing it's "100% possible" (see S3 20). In time you may even teleport, from bedroom to backyard without the kitchen. Higher contact asks for diet, understanding, and a heart without conditions: "the heart has the codes to open the portals". Earth guides will be needed as a bridge (see S2 4).

#33.9Your children's holograms

Both at once. Your reality and your child's sit under the one particle. In yours, you project a version of your child; in theirs, they project a version of you, with a completely different perception. Hold the heart and keep overriding: you create a new version of them that interacts as you wish. The more your positive action outweighs the negative, the more the fluctuation collapses into a positive outcome. Study S1 1.

#33.10Letting go of the past

The one particle shifts so fast it appears next to itself, a new earth and a new version of you each time. If you don't change your perception after a hard experience, you reprint the same resonance into the next earth: a permanent negative timeline. But there is no time: something 10,000 years ago or yesterday "is just a perception". Change the perception, think and act positively, and "you shut down one program and replace it with a brand new one". Practise, as a surgeon must (see S3 14.4).

#The method — Shutting Down an Old Program

  1. Recognize the old resonance still running (a hurt, a fear, a pain).
  2. Let it pass through without rejecting it; feel what it is (33.7).
  3. Drop the time label: yesterday or long ago, it's only perception (33.10).
  4. Change the perception: "I'm whole; I'm already healed."
  5. Act positively in thought, action and deed, and look for real solutions.
  6. Repeat daily until the positive outweighs it and the new program holds.
