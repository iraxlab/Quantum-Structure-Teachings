Praying Mantis Contact Story

A warning about people who claim to speak for her; how real contact comes; Earth as the school; beings who look like animals; her account of a telepathic contact with mantis beings and what she learned from it; and questions answered partly by them (live talk, June 2021)

The big idea

In ascension we are vulnerable, searching for truth and not knowing whom to trust, and some will take advantage. Real contact is individual: it comes to you when your own resonance is ready, through dreams, visions, thoughts or a physical proof, never through someone who says only they can be trusted. When it comes, meet it from the zero point, without fear and without judgment, telling the truth. That is what she did when beings who look like praying mantises contacted her, and why, in her account, they agreed to change their ways.

#Key teachings

#24.1Beware of those who claim to speak for her

"Be cautious when you are connecting with people that are claiming that they're working with me", or know her "in a spacecraft". She was told the opposite: to stand alone. Her material is free for all of humanity, with no restrictions; sharing it on Patreon or elsewhere for money is without her consent (see S4 7.8). Anyone who says they know better than you because they work with her, and that you can trust no one but them, "is not in a heart structure". Report it to her. "Follow the money, follow what they say."

#24.2Why she doesn't tell you who your galactic family is

In sessions you get little information about who you're connected with, because at this point nothing can prove it, "and they're watching exactly the people who can manipulate you". What she teaches is the quantum structure, which physics can back. Your contact will be your own: lucid dreaming, out of body, "literally physical, pulled up to a spacecraft", waking in a med pod. If it ever comes through a psychic, "it will be a physical proof". "I'm not your saviour. I'm another version of you... How can I control you, when you projected me?" When you're at the proper level, those who lied to you will be exposed (see S4 6, S4 7).

Star children? "Look at yourself in a mirror." Star children usually know and say something, but some will lie about it to control you; synchronicity will expose them. Listen to others too: "my truth is my truth, it doesn't have to be your truth."

#24.3Earth is the school

She was told to tell the truth about her own role, which she had held back so no one would misuse it: as a Pleiadian consciousness she has, in her account, command responsibilities in a fleet overseeing planets. The lesson for all: "this is the playground, this is the school. Do the best of yourself... it's almost like a graduation." How well you do here, what you do with the knowledge in the name of humanity, opens more responsibility in the timelines of your galactic family; "if you achieve high honours... more opportunities will be given." Earth is one of the toughest planets to transform, so the toughest and starseed souls came, though even they can be locked in the illusion. Don't rush contact: "it happens exactly when it needs to happen"; meanwhile practise lucid dreaming and telepathy, "to start expanding your senses that are being suppressed on purpose" (see S4 7, the automatic-writing method).

#24.4Beings who look like animals

"What makes you think there's not a race like the lions? A race that looks like cats... like a snake or a praying mantis?" The Council showed her that the timelines of these animals also evolved into other structures, telepathic, with their own genomes and races: "a big melting pot"; "we call them extraterrestrials, but in reality they're just different beings", with different beliefs, agendas and goods to exchange.

Her account of the contact (as she tells it; she calls it her "fiction story")

Out of the blue, beings that look like praying mantises connected telepathically: they knew who she is and asked to meet when she is in her position on her craft. "We are ready to negotiate for the human race." Asked, staying neutral, whether they used children, they answered honestly: yes, mostly for experiments "like you do to the rats and the bunnies", for slave work in tunnels, and food, "but not to the extent of... your satanic culture. Even for us that is appalling." They said those here who use children as an energy source only take and get the negative back, with no positive return: yin and yang must balance. Their original agenda was cheap labour, traded with governments for technology, "the way you also use other nations". She asked them to consider artificial food and to stop; they agreed: "It is time", and spoke of cloning programmes and of learning "to be truly more human". She answered: "We should never ever use each other as slaves, but have respect and be fair to each other's societies." The Council said afterwards they never thought it would happen: mantis beings are hard to negotiate with, "but you reached out to them and not judging them, they seem to respect you."

The lessons she draws. Meet contact at a stable zero point, "trying to understand, not to judge, and not to panic". Tell the truth: "the truth will set you free." And look at ourselves: we package animals in pretty words ("free range... organic"), yet a slaughterhouse would make most of us sick: "so why should we go and blame some other entity that is doing the same thing to us, when we are doing the same to others?" "Without pointing fingers, because we have all done crimes", we can work together and change for the better. She also announced an interview with Mary Rodwell, who works with hybrid children who speak of med beds.

#The method — Meeting a Contact

  1. Don't chase it: build your resonance; it comes when it needs to.
  2. Check the source: does it claim you can trust only them? Is money involved?
  3. Stay at the zero point: no fear, no judgment, curiosity.
  4. Ask honest questions, and tell the truth yourself.
  5. Speak from the heart: for respect, fairness, an end to slavery.
  6. Leave it open: no expectations of when or how.

#Questions answered

She was still connected to the mantis beings while answering; some answers are theirs, some the Council's.

My galactic family are mantis beings. How do I connect telepathically?
"We will choose to connect." With no disrespect: parts of their society still see humans as very immature, primitive, "almost like bees".
Are the praying mantis beings the Greys? Do they have human slaves, the reason for missing people?
"Yes, but not in the way you think. We are very much in control of your groups. We wish to reveal more in time... you are not ready yet for us." This is just the beginning of how they move forward, "so you can be free".
Am I Arcturian, a starseed, with a strong Pleiadian connection?
You are surrounded by energy fields, entities holding a vibration around you; to free yourself, "nature is the best way".
How can we make contact with our star family?
You're making it now: let the frequency penetrate; when it reaches a certain velocity, portals for contact open in many ways.
I often see mantises on my car and in my lift. Are they trying to contact me?
"You received a spark, yes."
I feel deep love from mantis beings, like a parent's. Could I be a mantis-human hybrid?
If you are connected, those realities will open; how is yet to be determined, since their society is now making decisions for change.
Are the little praying mantises on Earth connected to the ET ones?
"A spark of it, but not exactly; early stages of mutation."
Is meditation a big part of raising our frequency?
From the mantis beings: they simply know and move into it; their telepathy is a floating resonance merging into a "buzzing tone" so precise it can levitate; many of them together create very potent realities, "like your ants": their strength is in their numbers. For you, meditation can be used at other levels if you know how.
Where are the praying mantis beings from?
A cluster of three stars. A war made them extinct but for a mother left on part of a planet; they multiplied into a major fleet, then, through power and greed, traded other species as commodities, "command of wars and command of power", including with your military. Now, evolving, "we wish to move forward, so we can continue our ascension, in a more humane way."
Am I a starseed? Am I Pleiadian? How can I have contact?
"If you were not activated, you wouldn't even be here." Keep learning and exploring; when your frequency is ready, contact may come in various ways. Don't expect when and how; stay neutral, with curiosity rather than fear, so that reality can be preserved, as with her contact.
Should we now focus on the new Earth rather than waking people with hard truths?
Both, hand in hand: as you ascend, you mutate everything around you. "You are here, yes, to wake everybody up", but tell your truth and let them express their reality: "you do not control anyone; what you control is your own expression."
I dreamed of driving off a cliff with my children, telling them we were going to another timeline. What does it mean?
Dreams are built on your own belief system; keep a record, and learn what certain colours and things mean for you. Here: "be more cautious."

Left out: names and greetings.

Remember
  • Real contact is your own: beware of go-betweens.
  • Earth is the school: do your best here.
  • Meet any being at the zero point, without judgment.
  • We do to animals what we blame others for.
  • Tell the truth: it sets you free.
