Stabilizing Your Matrix with the New Codes #3

The big idea

A code doesn't land all at once: like a nanoparticle, your structure has to absorb, heal and mutate by itself, and that takes a different time for each person, from instantly to about a week. This third code strengthens your own ability to recognize manipulation in your personal reality. Once you recognize it, be honest: don't consent, act from the heart, and overwrite it day after day until it tips.

#Key teachings

#36.1A new land reprints your codes

Each nation you're born into carries its own signal, codes designed for the learning in your agreements. If you move to another country, it's a different timeline: the old codons have to collapse and shut down so you can be "reborn" in a new holographic matrix, decided with your higher self. You came with amnesia so you could take in new structures without decoding why you came. Many reading this incarnated "to hold the vibration for the new beginning"; that's why you're magnetically drawn to such material.

#36.2Two structures, no judgement

The 3D molecule "is simply a resonance", whatever produced it. Negative and positive structures coexist in consciousness, so "you should have no notion that one reality is better than the other. It simply is. Stay in neutrality." What falls off balance from the original purity of consciousness self-destructs, with the help of your higher self. The planet is liberated as you mutate your belief system: that gives a completely new resonance. Don't support manipulation because it comes from "your" side: "support the truth, not the side".

#36.3Why a code takes days to settle

After a strong period of downloads, it's "absolutely normal" for nothing to happen for a while. A code shuts one program and brings in another, and that needs potency. In each code, the number 3 clears the previous structure "like a zero point" so the new resonance can come in (see S3 33.4). Then the structure must "self-absorb, self-heal and self-mutate by its own doing": for one person a couple of days, for another instantly, for another a week. "Don't think it isn't being embedded." Consent is a faster route, "an easy loophole", but still takes some time, since your resonance shifts daily with your location.

From the booksFrom her books (NT): after a nanoparticle receives a new frequency and starts to split, "do not recall the Hz again. Let it stay and let it stabilize by itself for 3 days. Let its own consciousness build the calling for the next unit, 1+1=3."

#36.4How the councils see you

"We see both" body and energy; there's no separation. They can break it down into molecular structure, matrix and timelines (easier through technology), or simply feel your signal and the timeline pops up in front of them. Often they recognize you as an energy field showing the resonance you've accumulated through behaviour, and from its purity decide whether to work with you. Agreements can change with how you behave (see S3 34.1).

#36.5New codes change who's around you

Yes, depending on the person. "You are not changing other people, you're changing your resonance", projecting a new earth with new versions of those you stay with. Others leave through synchronicity: a move, an illness, a passing, or you simply stop calling. "A very smooth transition." Use time at home as activation. To decide whether to stay or go, peel all the layers (see S3 17.5) and switch off the ego; even discovering that you feel selfish is honest, and that honesty is your real foundation. Then act positively.

#36.6What this code does

The last downloads shut two programs that needed outside help to break (see S3 32, S3 34). This one "enhances your own structure to recognize" manipulation in your personal reality: it holds subtones, "multiple codes within the codes", so the moment something is off balance, you'll see it, recognize it, and move forward. The Archon timeline (a prison reality built by highly evolved negative consciousness) was so clever that "you wouldn't know for millennia" you were trapped.

#36.7Recognizing manipulation takes courage

"It's not subtle. It's a perception that is subtle." Seeing the truth shatters every belief system you had, and that is frightening: "it takes bravery". If your mind keeps inventing stories not to follow your guidance, it's because confusing sub-signals were placed in the matrix and you were trained to think in it. Practise tuning in "like a radio" (see S3 35.5); pull away from negative media. And don't say "it's not my problem": you'd be waiting for the mirror image to fix it. "The changes are happening from within." Act, bravely, daily, until it tips into "a rebirth of the new frequency" (see S3 35.2).

#36.8Why there's no dramatic change

Expecting something tremendous is the illusion. A smooth, slow change "is actually what we usually are looking for": overwhelm a being with what it doesn't yet understand and it blocks everything that comes next. "Every single material we gave you is like a drop in the sea", so you can drink it drop by drop.

The codes for this talk: 7338373 · 11001 · 384 · 4444 · 71373; an infinite signal is activated at the top of the hologram, by your latitude and altitude, to complete the matrix: help "non-stop" through the process.

#The method — Letting a Code Settle

  1. Consent, then read the code aloud once or a few times (see S3 34).
  2. Let it settle: allow days, even a week; don't keep forcing it (36.3).
  3. Expect no drama: drop by drop (36.8).
  4. Watch for what's off balance in your own life (36.6).
  5. Be honest about what you see, even in yourself; peel the layers (36.5).
  6. Don't consent to manipulation: act from the heart, daily, with courage (36.7).
