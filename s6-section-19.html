The big idea

Staying positive never meant staying blind: knowing what the negative structure is doing lets you use the quantum structure to rise above it and run your own program. Whatever out-of-balance situation arrives in your hologram is not a punishment but a test drive showing you what to fix, so pull back, analyze and convert it through the heart. The heart, not the mind, is the centre of your consciousness: it converts frequency and holds the codes of the portals.

#Key teachings

#19.1See it, then rise above it

Learn the quantum structure of manifestation so you can see what the negative does in the collective hologram and still run on your own program, fractally, moment to moment. Information is sometimes withheld and given only weeks before an event. Bylaw: no one can tap into another person's bylaws. If you go to another reader through fear, you may create realities that aren't beneficial, because you create reality in the first place; and Akashic readings vary with location and code, so stay in your heart and dig deeper.

Added note

She received a brief collective message called "three systems in place", linked to corruption being exposed, negative portals closing, judicial systems being replaced and people in high positions suddenly changing their decisions or no longer able to operate. Only the principle is kept here; the coded names and places are left out. As with every prediction in this book, it is given as received, not as fact.

#19.2A test drive, not a mistake

When a challenge arises, don't ask what you did wrong: be glad it came, because it shows you what is out of balance with your resonance. Like a test drive revealing a faulty wheel, you fix it. Neutralize it analytically, understand it, and use the quantum structure to overturn it, sometimes over and over, especially close to home (relocating, or a toxic relative moving away through synchronicity). Some have ten small things to fix, others one big one. Being good in the heart means holding your ground, strong as steel and compassionate.

#19.3The heart is the centre

Mistakes are natural: each one shows a hologram not compatible with you, so your next decision is precise and shrinks time. She shows the path; you walk it (individual ascension). Pull back when emotional, ground barefoot, greet the sunrise without sunglasses, sleep by ten, and live "Marina's day" with joy moment to moment. The heart converts the resonance and holds the codes of the proper portals, which is why compassion raises your frequency.

Added safety note: never look directly at the sun except at the very edge of sunrise or sunset, and stop at any discomfort; looking at the sun can permanently damage the eyes.

#Questions answered

Has our timeline jumped backwards in time?
There's no such thing as back in time: each shift is a new hologram, reality within reality, running simultaneously, as consciousness rises. Engage your attention and physical action with what is positive for humanity, oppose peacefully through law and petitions, and stay away from the negative. Some see only after getting burned, like a child touching a hot iron.
Can the negatives put your consciousness in a clone body?
Yes: a spark of resonance can be cloned into a synthetic body, though it can't hold it long. The original human DNA mimics God consciousness and can shift through timelines, so preserve it. Experiences you consented to are honoured; overtaking a soul by manipulation violates the bylaws. Words and sounds were engineered to lower vibration; fear made people compliant and set them against each other, which is why unity is your power.
Can we be harmed while astral travelling?
Yes, depending on where you exit or enter. The Archon AI shield created deceptive pockets of reality. Advanced souls do it naturally; otherwise hold the vibration of compassion in the heart, because negative resonance can't hold that frequency.
How can we help children through the changes?
They already hold the resonance. Early-age resonance is imprinted and keeps reprinting, which is why trauma persists. Teach respect and love, history, and nature: sand, mud, planting. The soil's bacteria strengthen immunity; children putting soil in their mouths reconnect their DNA with Mother Earth. (Added: she also mentioned holding an organic, non-GMO seed under the tongue for one minute before planting it, so it carries your healing sequences.)
