The big idea

By a bylaw of consciousness, each new particle must be free to choose its own position and trajectory (about three days, in our time, to code its place), just as children must be free to play and learn. Overtaking a soul without permission breaks it, which is why the black-goo AI reality had to be overridden. She received an account of the distant past: a group of beings tampered with the DNA of early humans to create workforces, leaving about 10% to evolve naturally (and these advanced spiritually), and their appointed rulers came to see themselves as gods, keeping humanity's vibration low through fear. Now consciousness and organic intelligence are pressing against that. The outcome is known; it could be stopped "in 3 days, 3 months, 3 years" if we woke up. Don't judge: suppression pushes any being into negative ways to survive.

#27.2The bylaw of free evolution

In the NT material: when a particle vibrates and reprints a new version of itself, each new reality has its own vibration and free will over its trajectory; the second particle takes about three days of our time to code its own position. Overtaking another's consciousness (through programming, media, schooling) violates this, so it must be cancelled and overwritten. Consciousness, learning too, let the experiment run, not knowing it would go so far; it will never allow that again.

The message: History 1.0 (condensed, as received)

A group of seven tampered with the DNA of early humans (originally more like the being called Sasquatch) to create workforces: different groups designed for different tasks. About 90% were genetically altered and 10% left to evolve naturally; "the ones that natural evolved... moved spiritually ahead". The engineered became cleverer but were manipulated. Their appointed "generals" became the first bloodline, worshipped them as gods (religions under a sun god), and came to believe themselves gods ruling everyone. Those who did the tampering were later asked to leave so humans could evolve, but the bloodlines kept control, using sacred knowledge of how reality works and fear to keep the vibration low; unable to convert through the heart, they now turn to cyborg technology. Not all of that race agreed with what was done.

Many people were trapped by fear or compromise. So pull back from judgment, yet speak up and correct what's off balance now, or it returns like a bomb in your face.

Stay close to family; forgive and fix differences, and if that fails, new people will become your family. An adopted child can be your true soul line. Give people a chance on the facts, not on one thing you dislike.
