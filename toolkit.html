#Daily practice

  • Morning: give thanks for what you have; write what you love and choose the most exciting thing to act on.
  • When upset: run the Pull Out method (Section 1, Fig. D); for strong negatives, the Overwrite Protocol (Section 2, Fig. I).
  • When the news or others tell you what will happen: "That's their projection." Give gratitude, project what you choose (2.5).
  • Affirm in the now: "I am", "I know", never "I will" (2.9).
  • Before saying yes: run the Truth Check (Section 3). Kind honesty, no white lies.
  • After work: spend time on your passion and give something from the heart, with no condition (Section 4).
  • Feeling low: run Shift Your Location (Section 5): thank, neutralize, potent gratitude and action.
  • Learning: study in short pieces of about 15 minutes, and rewatch (5.2).
  • After a hard day: gratitude for why you were there, then something you enjoy; move your body (6.7).
  • An old memory or belief returns: forgive, start from zero, replace it with a positive picture (6.6).
  • In an argument or replay: Stop · Transform · Complete (Section 7).
  • Before a decision: the Heart Check: heart, fear or ego? (Section 8).
  • Feeling flat: an uplifting film, music that gives goosebumps, or a kindness for a stranger (8.13).
  • Money worry: "I have everything I need." Give something non-monetary today (9.1).
  • Old memory replaying: Layer a New You (Section 9).
  • Wanting something specific: Leave It Open: strip the details, "I have everything I need", act (Section 11).
  • Someone keeps causing problems: neutralize and forgive; don't go after them (11.6).
  • Two realities pulling at you: Through the Vacuum: neutralize, project potently, act (Section 12).
  • Ill or in pain: see your doctor; use Stop · Transform · Complete alongside (12.4).
  • Choosing music: pick what lifts you; tones carry frequencies (12.5).
  • New guidance or a new health idea: check it with research and a professional before acting (13.1).
  • Have something useful: share it; pool small amounts with others (13.2).
  • Watch your words: positive, from the heart (3.9).
  • After a big shift (dizziness, nausea): rest, integrate, and check your body (3.7).
  • Forgot why you came in? Recall the last room's frequency (2.7).
  • With difficult people: kind tone, no judgment, accept the outcome.
  • When something is missing: know you have it, take action, watch for synchronicity.
  • Self-check: Am I doubting or scripting the how? Do my feelings match my words? Am I holding steady? Then what do I actually need right now?
