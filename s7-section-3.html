President Trump / Trump 2 / Elections

A Council of Nine message on the US elections given in outline and neutrally, how the guides decide when a prediction can be given (probabilities over 90%), why every being's record is visible like a hologram, and questions on South America, the portals, Canada, what winning looks like and a dream of a currency change

The big idea

Much of this talk is political commentary and a received prediction about the US elections; it is given here only in outline, as received. The teachings underneath: predictions are probabilities, and the guides speak only when a sequence is more than 90% into a permanent, coherent resonance with a stable tone. Some questions are not answered, so as not to interfere with your own growth. Expect back-and-forth "like a ping pong", hold your ground and step back to analyze. Humanity wins, but how you experience it is your own creation: be the driving force, help people and proceed with caution.

#Key teachings

#3.1Not every question can be answered

Sometimes she is shown only a vision, or told not to share. Private concerns are better sent privately than posted in public. Information that arrives without being asked deserves attention.

The message: the elections (condensed, as received)

"We have a lot of delays. It will have changes in the system, big changes. People will prevail." There would be deception and doubles; new systems and laws of the land, local coordination, systems prepared in advance that "appear to emerge out of nowhere", and the central bank's reprinting and grip loosening. "Big, big fluctuations... back and forth like a chess game... In the end, we're winning, but hold the ground." "Never let your guard down... Step back and analyze your positions. Be focused." Later: "Do you not trust us?... Stay put and allow to play it out."

Added note

Added note: this is a received political prediction, kept short and neutral. It names politicians, parties and institutions and makes claims about fraud, doubles, cloning and arrests that are not verified; those details are left out. Form your own views from reliable sources.

#3.2How predictions are given

"Predictions are probabilities, but when the probable sequence is more than 90% into the permanent coherent resonance and we see stable tonality of sound, then we know that it will manifest into matter. So this is when we give you information, and not when you wish to hear it." "Some things you must see for yourself... we cannot interfere with that."

#3.3Your record is a hologram

Every being, positive or negative, carries a specific pulsation of everything accumulated through consciousness. Consciousness can zoom in like a movie screen and see every reality and thought. The hybrids' first words to her: "Be careful what you're thinking, because everything you think comes in front of us like a hologram."

#3.4Forgive and unite

Every culture has harmed someone in history; nobody is perfect. Step back, forgive one another and unite as a human race instead of pointing fingers, which is exactly what division wants.

#Questions answered

How will the new system come to other countries, such as South America?
With a slight delay. Most of the world is on standby, waiting to see who wins, and some act like chameleons, trying to stay in favor with both sides. She was told something moves around the end of 2025 into 2026.
What happened when the portals opened recently?
They spoke of one portal: "Portal 9 is open."
Where are things in Canada?
As received: what is being attempted won't work; stay on standby and don't worry. "Nothing is as it seems"; many are being stopped and replaced in a very big game. (Names and personal claims are left out.)
What is the ultimate goal for humanity, and what does winning look like?
Humanity wins, but how you experience it is up to you: how you conduct yourself, what you think, whom you connect with and what you physically do. Don't ask others to create your reality; she is a projection, another version of you. Use the laboratory of creation, be good and loving in the physical, and you crystallize it daily. In a battlefield proceed with caution: if the "wild dogs" come, take cover. Be patient when basics are repeated for newcomers: someone must lead those who can't yet find their way home, and in time you become the leader.
I dreamed the currency suddenly changed and everyone was confused. What does it mean?
There will be changes, but she wasn't told where or how: stay where you are and don't do anything drastic. When a door closes (for example a bank transfer suddenly blocked), don't panic; another solution appears.

Left out: video clips she shared, named politicians, commentators, organizations and documentaries, claims about rituals, abuse and medical products, and channel notices.
