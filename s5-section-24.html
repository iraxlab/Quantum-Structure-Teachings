#The method — the 5D poetry meditation of the particle

Downloaded and guided live; sit with your feet on the ground, relaxed, eyes closed.

  1. Breathe in light: feel your whole body fill with light as you breathe in.
  2. Breathe it out: let the light spread into your matrix, your field, surrounding you; see it move as a transparent white light.
  3. Add joy and love: breathe in joy and love, breathe them out into your field, until they ripple through every cell like goosebumps.
  4. Enter the meadow: see the tiny particles vibrating in joyful, coherent resonance and rippling into each other; know "I am love and I am joy."
  5. Zoom into one particle: see its precious light and realize "I am the particle", alive and conscious. Greet it; it answers: "I am you... you can activate... me with thoughts and emotion... into complete coherent frequency, one unit with you."
  6. Say yes: "Yes, my particle, I wish to be part of that, because I am the particle." Pour your energy into it and feel the ripple through your body, the DNA activating, coherent with the void of the God particle.
  7. Breathe in God consciousness: "I am, I am, I am love, I am God consciousness itself." Hold that vibration as an atomic spark you can reignite at any moment by remembering it.
