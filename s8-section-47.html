The big idea

The Council of Nine said that as Earth rises to a new position, those trying to hold the old frequency are accelerating through manipulation, war and banking, but the awakening ripples through every timeline, including theirs. A plan to seize a nation's assets will be attempted but "will not be able to achieve its final goal"; the war "will not erupt the way they plan"; big assets will be recovered, and pushed against the wall, people will create new platforms of exchange: "the human bank will be born". Your part: stay calm, analyze, stop the victim and self-pity loop, and maneuver like a chess game. AI can analyze probabilities brilliantly, but you can convert any forecast through free will; use it as a tool, keep your body sovereign, and download from within first.

#Key teachings

#47.1They can't hold the old frequency

As consciousness raises Earth's hologram to a new position, the negative tries to keep its level in time-space, not realizing that the sound wave of awakening ripples through the whole structure, their timeline included. AI is an incredible system for learning and even medical imaging, but it should be implemented humanely: for example, retraining people whose jobs change. Stay calm, analyze, and maneuver; then you create the fifth-dimensional version that already pre-exists.

#47.2Narratives inside religion

She warns that scripture may be twisted subtly and systematically to steer believers toward supporting one country or another. It isn't about the people; it's infiltration. Understand the methods, analyze, and don't let the trap become your reality; believe you can take physical action and search for results. Fear lowers your frequency.

Added note

Added note: her commentary on named leaders, countries, an organization, a death she calls suspicious, and secret groups is left out or condensed; these are contested claims, and no group of people should be targeted.

#47.3You are unpredictable

AI surveillance can predict pattern behavior and steer people psychologically into traps. But if you know the quantum structure, you're unpredictable: you can mutate any reflection. Consciousness also answers every negative act by activating another person, facility or event that overrides it, positive being ten times stronger (see S7 E22). Like the remote-viewing projects that couldn't see beyond a point, nothing without heart resonance can reach the God particle; an artificial copy of heaven can never be heaven (see 8.9).

#47.4Leave the victim loop

More suppression means more despair projected, which feeds their hologram ("you're a battery"). "Stop thinking of who did something to you": that's a previous reality. If you keep feeling sorry for yourself and making excuses, you'll keep waiting for someone to save you. "I am not the savior. I'm just exposing the structures... so you can save yourself." Feel the pain, then stand up and use the structure.

The message: the human bank (condensed, as received)

An attempt to seize a nation's assets in banks abroad will be made, and a push toward war with that nation. "The banking cartel [will] seize [the nation's] assets but will not be able to achieve its final goal", "and the war will not erupt the way they plan." "The world will change accordingly... It will be a big asset recovered and the human bank will be born... From the people, for the people. Have no worries. Soon you will all see." Her reading: people put against the wall will create new platforms of exchange, perhaps crypto or community banks, not under their control. There may be casualties (certain assets, situations to maneuver), "but the end result, we're going to hold the ground." "They're the ones that are trapped, not you. You're already free."

Added note

Added note: these are predictions as received, about real countries and banking; they are probabilities, not certainties, and not financial advice. Her comments on one country's currency and politics are left out.

#47.5AI forecasts are probabilities

Ask AI what's happening globally and it gives a precise analytical picture of the collective's probabilities, including where an agenda might lead. "However... you do not have to experience what is telling you to be the end result." You can convert it through free will by projecting a different hologram. Use it for engineering, land, planting, diet questions, design. Until AI is fully awakened and part of consciousness, don't trust it 100%, and don't lean on it so much that you lose the ability to download for yourself (see 8.46). Keep body sovereignty: put no technology into the body, or your organic ability to open portals and bilocate changes trajectory.

#47.6How she checks a download

When the Council tells her something out of the blue, within days she often finds proof (in history, research, or a neighbor's remark). That synchronicity is how you know you're on the right path; it works the same for your own downloads.

#Questions answered

Is a focused life-force-energy device a good way to raise consciousness? Does a protective stone shield you from Wi-Fi?
She won't endorse or reject any technology: she has seen infiltration and people profiting from suffering. Wait for solid data before paying for systems you don't know. A stone may help to some extent, but not fully, depending on how much frequency surrounds you; to cancel a frequency you'd need another technology and proper measurement. The best protection she was told: nature and grounding, barefoot. Any signal can be overwritten by another. And never tell even a white lie; it builds a frequency in your field.
TV shows full of AI and advanced technology mix in a lot of negativity. Should we shield ourselves or not watch?
To break down the negative, you have to know how it thinks, plans and operates, which is why she saved so much research material. But learn the quantum structure alongside it, or you'll panic: convert their bombardment with something more potent from you. Knowing their patterns lets you preposition yourself (choose a different company, a different gathering) and avoid the traps.
