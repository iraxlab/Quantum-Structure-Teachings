Q & A: Oils and Skin-Cell Regeneration

Combining pure oils for skin, a simple homemade balm, why fear of waves keeps them hitting you, letting people go, how permission is given away, money without a price, the conscious and subconscious as one, and tapping into a past experience (live talk)

The big idea

The session opened with oils: certain pure oils, in combination and in tiny amounts, were said to work together to open, hold and regenerate the skin's cells (with an important safety note below). The rest returned to the structure from new angles: fear of something keeps projecting it at you, while knowing you're healthy shifts you; no one can override your consciousness, but you give permission whenever you refuse to look at the facts; and a past experience can be recovered by recreating its feeling and "trying again" until the picture is clear.

#Key teachings

#12.1Oils work in combination

Each oil has a resonance and a property, and in combination they act together at the cell: one opens the pores, one holds the molecule so it doesn't open too far, and one, entering through that opening, regenerates the cell. Chamomile was one; the full combination was withheld on guidance, to prevent profiteering. Each oil is used by itself, not diluted, in combination with the others, in very small amounts: never more than three drops at a time, and try one or two drops first, on the face only, to see that you're not allergic; the oils should be pure, organic and non-GMO. Train the skin slowly: she used too much at first and her face swelled for two days. Coconut oil as a carrier was said to block the skin from opening. A general rule: "If you cannot eat it, it shouldn't be on your skin."

Added safety note

Essential oils are usually diluted for good reason: undiluted oils can burn, irritate or cause allergic reactions, as the swelling described here shows. Patch-test any oil on a small area first, keep oils away from the eyes, and ask a dermatologist or qualified aromatherapist before using them on your face.

#12.2A simple balm

For a shaving cream or skin balm: half a cup of organic shea butter and half a cup of organic raw coconut butter, whipped together with a mixer, plus a little lavender or chamomile (lavender antiseptic, chamomile soothing).

#12.3Fear keeps the waves hitting you

Asked whether electromagnetic waves are harming us: there will always be some, but "if you keep yourself in fear, you're going to keep projecting" a hologram in which they keep hitting you. Neutralize, shut the program down (see S2 24.11), and the next moment shifts you to a new earth where the wave subsides or stops mattering, by a route you can't foresee. Know you're healthy, imagine the planet free, and act on it. One frequency can be neutralized by another.

#12.4Letting people go

When many people fall away, you're recreating your hologram, but "don't have the expectation to recreate the same people": some you may never see again, even close friends. Leave the structure open and stay neutral.

#12.5How permission is given away

Beings can place a holographic imprint in your consciousness so you have an experience, but only by your higher self's agreement. "Nobody is overtaking your consciousness, except you allow to be manipulated." You allow it when you keep giving your attention to what deceives you and refuse to look at the facts out of loyalty or fear of the truth. Those who were compromised and then do something heroic for humanity are given another timeline to continue ascending; your higher self keeps placing situations in front of you to wake you up.

#12.6Resentment reflects back

Anger at what was done is understandable, but "at the moment you blame somebody, you're projecting frequency into your quantum field and it's going to reflect the same to you, with vengeance". Say: "I know what you're doing; from your perspective it's right, but I say no to that. I'm my own person", and imagine the positive (a healthy planet, a free humanity) instead (see S3 10.1).

#12.7Money without a price

To an artist blocked from receiving money: "You're not blocked from money; you're projecting situations that block you" because you don't know 100% that you already have it. Feel now the joy of already being successful, keep working, giving and sharing, and leave the structure open without putting a price on people (see S1 4.2); if you're the main provider, charging is fine (see S1 4.4). A dollar or a million: "there is no difference" in the field, only in resonance.

#12.8Conscious and subconscious are one

"There's no difference between your conscious and subconscious mind: it's one unit." The split is a fractal separation made by old beliefs that reject what comes from the higher self. Let it flow in neutrality, and higher self, subconscious and conscious receive at once, as a whole blueprint you can then unpack.

#12.9Tapping into a past experience

To recover an experience (a sighting, a contact): sit down, go back into it and recreate its emotion, so you resonate on that frequency; then ask what really happened and watch the images. If it's scary, neutralize; if it doesn't sound right, "try again, and try again": you're tuning the frequency until the picture is proper, as the teacher was taught on the mothership (see S3 9). In time it becomes knowing.

#The method — Tapping Into an Experience

  1. Sit quietly and bring the experience to mind.
  2. Recreate the feeling you had then, until you resonate with it.
  3. Ask: what really happened? Watch the images that come.
  4. Neutralize anything frightening; don't reject it.
  5. If it doesn't feel right, try again: you're tuning the signal (12.9).
  6. Write it down, and repeat until it becomes knowing.
