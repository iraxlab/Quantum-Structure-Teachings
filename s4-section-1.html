The big idea

Above the dimensions we know, there is a very high order of consciousness, which she calls the priesthood, where a thought "becomes a structure instantaneously": there is no bilocation, "it's simply there". To download from such a reality, the condition is absolute purity of the heart. For us, the same law works step by step: you don't see it to believe it, you create it to see it, by refusing manipulation and taking action toward solutions, while the emotion you hold activates the blueprint until it pops into matter.

#Key teachings

#1.1A new teacher from the priesthood

She has a new teacher, Sanjar (her spelling is uncertain), from what she was told is "the priesthood": not a priesthood as we know it, but "one of the highest orders of consciousness". There are hierarchies of realities: our timeline, then the fourth and fifth dimensions, then extraterrestrial platforms, each with its own bylaws. Over all of them stands the universal bylaw of the quantum structure, quantum manifestation itself. Within it you co-create realities by your choosing, through the alchemy of creation.

Matter itself follows this: as in nano material, a molecule in one structure changes to a completely different structure as soon as you give it different resonances and conditions: "from proton can become atom, from atom could be something else"; matter can bilocate. The Harmonic Reactor and Nano Technology books are written in code, and contain formulas a quantum physicist or nano scientist can work from.

From the booksFrom her books (NT): a nanoparticle that receives a new frequency splits and mutates; "let it stabilize by itself for 3 days" (see S3 36.3).

#1.2Where thought is instant law

He explained it like this: imagine floating in a thought that is all-knowing and all-existing, at a very high frequency. You always exist as part of consciousness and also as an individual thought, and "at the moment you want to project something, it becomes a reality instantaneously", with no barriers. Create matter, and it's matter; wish to be somewhere, and you zoom right into that platform. Their thoughts become "instantaneous law", needing no approval, unlike the Council of Nine, whom she asks before acting.

#1.3The heart is the condition

To download from such a reality, or even to have contact, "you have to have absolutely purity in the heart". When the Council of Nine first connected with her, they looked not at her body but at her energy field, and above all at her heart: whatever was done to her through a lifetime of abuse, she created her own reality to move forward and never intentionally hurt anyone, even when she had the power to. That resonance is the only way to these levels; your body couldn't otherwise hold the vibration.

#1.4Permission, and proof

He first asked her permission to work with her, and she gave it with the Council of Nine assisting: "you do not want to give a permission to a consciousness that you don't know who you're dealing with." Everything she did was through proof: only when she saw that their suggestions helped her through the process did she start doing what they asked (see S3 32.2).

#1.5Her body protocol, and why

He came to help her complete her diet. She is now in a protocol of three days only distilled water, then three days distilled water and honey, given by the priesthood; at the seventh day she will be told what to do next, because her body is monitored. As the molecules spin faster and reach a certain trajectory, they shift to a higher resonance, closer to the ETs and high consciousness, where you can download information: so consciousness and body must be one unit (see S3 38.1). Food, she was told, became like an addiction, and GMOs, poison and chemicals in it damaged the DNA and RNA so the body couldn't mutate and bilocate through timelines.

Her instruction

"Never, ever do what I do right now, because you have to first be connected with your guides." "I'm not suggesting anyone to do this diet." To learn detox, go to her YouTube channel The Harmonic Reactor and search "Marina Jacobi detox", and the elimination-diet video from 2020: "it's a slow process" (see S3 38 and the Detox and Juicing Guide).

Added safety note

Water-only and honey-water regimes can be dangerous, especially with diabetes, kidney disease or medication. Don't attempt them; any fast needs a doctor's supervision.

#1.6A message for channelers and oracles

"Oracles will come and go, and several will stay true to the reality." Those blinded by fame and money will find their information is no longer clear, and people will find the truth elsewhere, because they read a timeline not connected with the frequency consciousness is moving into. Her request to channelers: support your family, but "after you pay the bills, please at least introduce energy philanthropy a couple of times a week", so those who have nothing can see your material. In the quantum field there's no condition: she accepts any gift with gratitude, "even a million dollars", because it comes from the heart (see S1 5).

#1.7Pushed against the wall: create the solution

Through synchronicity you may be pushed against the wall: "I can't believe I lost my job". But if you stay in the heart and don't go into fear, other realities open: your partner finds work elsewhere, a business opens online. When you see something that makes no sense, first neutralize it, then transform it: "I know what you're doing. I do not consent with that. If you wish to explore that reality, be my guest, but I'm going to start looking for solutions." In the quantum structure you don't only see the solution, "you create the solution": you create as you go. Study Season 1, Sections 1, 2 and 3 again on fluctuating realities: "it's fundamental for you now to hold the vibration."

#1.8The emotion activates the blueprint

The moment you think of something, such as the sun, "you create the matrix of reality in your consciousness", with its numbers and geometry. But a blueprint is only a blueprint: for it to become your reality, "you have to feel it. You have to have the emotion", because the emotion connects the geometry to your field. Then you hold that vibration and trust it until it is "so potent to pop into matter". When the council guides a meditation in answer to a question, close your eyes and feel what is described (see S1 1).

The predictions given by the priesthood (an election outcome, a hard recovery for New York, workplace vaccine mandates, communities restructuring around family within seven years, new technologies within seven years) were said to be probabilities, "it could be a chess game". They are years old now and are only noted here. She points to the website stopworldcontrol for the vaccine information. "Stay out of the politics": do your research and go through the heart.

Added note

Vaccines don't change your DNA. For any medical decision, talk to your own doctor.

#The method — When You're Pushed Against the Wall

  1. Neutralize first: see it, know it's there, don't go into fear.
  2. Name it: "I know what you're doing."
  3. Withhold consent: "I do not consent with that. If you wish to explore that reality, be my guest."
  4. Start looking for solutions: a new job, a new idea, a new place.
  5. Feel the reality you want: the emotion activates its blueprint (1.8).
  6. Take the action, and follow the synchronicities as they come: you create as you go.

#Questions answered

When we die, does our soul always choose how and when, or are some deaths accidental?
When you exit through the bylaws you signed, the body can no longer hold the higher vibration and slowly reverses its structure, disintegrating. In the quantum field you create a double reality: the old structure stays as a fractal hologram that doesn't move forward, and a new version of you pops up in a higher resonance. Before you split into the new reality, your guidance, higher self and higher orders decide your next trajectory, based on pre-existing agreements; many will go to galactic family. "It's always a new momentum": you co-create even after leaving the body.
Is the sun inside a crystal-ball-like structure, like the other planets?
"All of the above." The sun is a portal and a zero point where many stargates come in and out; some hold crystalline structures. Crystalline can be matter, antimatter or ether: "it's more fluid; you don't have to reconstruct it into matter."
How do the Fibonacci sequence and the golden ratio fit into fifth-dimensional teachings?
Picture the cone: one twist expanding, one collapsing, creating the spin and the zero point. The Fibonacci becomes fluid and spins faster, which is how time shrinks to the zero point. Science thinks it never stops; in a fractal of instantaneous manifestation, "that specific location collapses in a single number".
I see floating triangles, not orbs. Is this contact?
"Yes, in a way, if you wish to be so." Zoom into the image, feel it, explore it: from there you create the structure for a form of contact (see S3 30.8).
Can prayer help our deceased loved ones reach higher realms?
In your reality, yes: your prayer raises their vibrational sequence and helps them, if they choose. In their own chosen structure they may still need to go through realities to learn, for example someone who took a life. Whatever goes against the structure eventually collapses under its own weight; when they choose to express from the heart, they've learned the lesson.
Will there be new lands in the new world?
"It's all new": new realities, platforms and resonances, like radio stations at 0.11 and 0.22 (see S1 15).
What happens now to the Archons?
"Whatever they wish to happen." A reality built on manipulation doesn't run through the heart, so it will self-destruct: the whole Archon timeline can no longer sustain the vibration consciousness is moving to (see S3 30.7). "We wish for you to choose wisely."
Do bees carry the vibration of the central sun, and does their honey connect us to it?
They connect with the planetary field and change with it; they aren't separate. If you choose to believe they're in synchronicity with the sun, let it be so: when you see bees, look at the sun, feel the vibration, enjoy the moment, and you're in resonance with it.
When will we get a new message from the sun?
When you tune in to it: a guided meditation followed. Look at the sun, feel the rays, "I'm a ray of the sun", zoom and vibrate; you are the glow. Breathe through it, give gratitude that it sustains your body (even your vitamin D), imagine a palm tree, the beach and the water. "Stay in the joy, feel it."
