How Consciousness Activates It's Own Realities/America

How consciousness reads the collective sound of a nation and sends a ripple to activate it, what that means for America in the coming year, why those who stay asleep or compliant will be pushed until they act, and questions on other countries, examples of action, a "simple insignificant life", working while keeping a high frequency, and being called abroad (the recording ends mid-sentence)

The big idea

Every individual's reactions collapse into one collective sound, and God consciousness reads it in the now. Where a nation's counter-reaction is not yet potent enough to override the negative (if it does something 10 times, you must answer 11 or 12), consciousness sends a ripple that activates each person according to their resonance. The awake meet challenges but move through them easily; the sleeping and the compliant will be pushed into situations that leave no choice but to act. Like a wound being cleaned, the healing stings, but the end result is that America wins.

#Key teachings

#28.1Reading the collective sound

Each person's collapse of positive and negative becomes a sound, and the collective sound of a country is recognized by consciousness. Where it needs more activation to shift from negative to positive, consciousness creates a ripple through every individual. Bylaw of potency (S4 9): your counter-reaction must be more potent than the negative's action. Hold your light like steel so consciousness can ride the wave through you as an example.

#28.2The healing stings

Consciousness found the cancer and is cleaning it: cleaning a cut hurts, and surgery has a recovery period. Those who chose to stay asleep, afraid, quiet or compliant while witnessing evil will be pushed into a corner by synchronicity, like cold water splashed in the face: shock, then reaction. Some will become examples that wake others. Even professionals who half-saw and denied will witness something they can no longer deny. Being awake doesn't exempt you from hits, but you'll see them and act at once.

The message: America in the year to come (condensed)

"The human resources will be depleted in America to one year... But the attempt will be colossally stopped. Not one human will be left alone in hunger. To restore the true Republic, it is important for the humans of America to truly, truly see who, how and when was all done, who is the true enemy." The collective still needs more action: don't sit back, counter peacefully and cleverly, nonstop, without consenting. The asleep will awaken "accordingly to their own vibration".

Added note

She saw this as coordinated, seemingly random disruptions to supplies of goods in strategic places, intended to lower the collective vibration, which will fail as consciousness also activates people in positive positions. As with every prediction in this book, it is given as received; named people and political remarks are left out.

#28.3In waves

Awakening comes in waves, first, second, third, so the structure doesn't collapse (not everyone leaves a city at once). The first wave often loses much, including friends still in the matrix. "All of us will be brought to our knees"; the warriors wake then and pave the way. Whatever your role, a clerk or a top professional, there is no middle ground: compliance sinks your hologram deeper. You cannot just pray: act, and hold your position from the heart.

#Questions answered

Will similar things happen in other countries?
Mostly in America, where the big push is. Elsewhere some things were tried but didn't build momentum because collective awareness was higher, for instance where people lived through communism and see manipulation quickly.
Give some examples of taking action.
Through your children: a mother asked her school board for the curriculum, was refused and bullied, and then fought back legally. You have rights; learn them. When one stands up publicly, more are activated.
I can't think of an action. What can someone with a simple, insignificant life do?
Your existence is most significant: without you the structure wouldn't exist. Expose what's happening, talk to friends and family, join a helping group. One click on a post can lead, through physical action, to something huge. Instead of "I'll pray for you", ask "what can I do? Can I send ten dollars?" One step holds the power of the new reality.
If I work hard while keeping a high frequency, is that being active?
Relax: act from compassion and joy and don't monitor every mood swing. Live like Marina's day; help the stranger crossing the street spontaneously, not to be seen. Once it's who you are, synchronicity brings what you need. Compassion is not weakness.
If this is mainly in America, why am I called to move abroad?
Your resonance may be needed in another location; through synchronicity you are placed where your signal must resonate. Those meant to stay in America will stay.

Left out: political commentary on named people and countries, personal history and anecdotes beyond their lessons. The recording ends mid-answer.
