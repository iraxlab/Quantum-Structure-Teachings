The big idea

Your consciousness has a natural way of judging what it sees: an analytical process of four steps, running through brain and heart together, that compares each hologram with your own matrix and lets you neutralize and mutate what is off balance. Constant programming can shut that process down; then a person takes in only the image they are given and acts on it "like a robot", without the heart's protective shield. That is direct programming, and it's why some people can be shown every fact and still not see. Protect the process: stay off the programming, keep analyzing, and act from the heart.

#Key teachings

#22.1The four steps of the analytical brain

Your body is "a very sophisticated organic machine" of frequency, sound and colour; everything you see is a reality you project within your own, a geometric pattern of sound, light and colour. As the Council explained it, judging what you see runs in four steps:

  1. Combine: your analytical brainwaves combine all the brain's memory imagery of the hologram in front of you.
  2. Compare and accept the reflection: what you project comes back to you; you compare it, by synchronized frequency, and accept that you see it.
  3. Analyze what's off balance, further and further, until your consciousness can accept the proper frequency, and put what doesn't resonate into the zero point of neutrality: "Oh, thank you so much" (not rejecting it).
  4. Read from the heart: let consciousness feel which frequency causes discomfort; then project a new frequency, act, and construct new ways to balance, so the next moment's shift (1+1=3) comes out in the heart's structure.

This is why you need a pure resonance and to stay in the heart: it is your natural process. Change the spin of the body's frequency ("any robotics"), and you lock yourself in a different timeline where you can't do this practice (see S4 18, S4 20).

#22.2Direct programming

The former KGB officer she played again (see S4 4.2) said a demoralized person "is unable to assess true information". The quantum reason: through deception and constant negative exposure, the four steps, "the meta-hologram compared with your own matrix", are slowly shut down, and the protective energetic field of the heart with them. Then you react only to the image put in front of you, without comparison: "you follow orders without questioning", even toward harm, "like a robot". The 1+1=3 of fluctuation disappears. Doctors, lawyers, financiers can all be programmed "through the ears". This is why humanity is separating, and why "we can't judge these people": the manipulation is genius. Anyone caught in it can still be activated (see S4 21.4).

Children first. What children are given to read and watch in school can shut the process down early (a book left her son unable to sleep for two days). Look for solutions outside the system: online schools, a few parents sharing one teacher, a mother teaching up to six or ten children at home; she points to Dr. Duke Pesta's online school. Let them learn about Tesla, technology, frequency and music; Spirit Science on YouTube and the film The Last Mimzy are good for children.
