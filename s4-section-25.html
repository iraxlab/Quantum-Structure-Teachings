Restructuring Your DNA / High Tech

How she checks a claim against her own connection; the Council's correction to a claim about nano sprayed in the air; the "blueprint molecular DNA restoration"; why help needs your consent; and questions on giving to someone who takes advantage, and finding your purpose (live talk, June 2021; part of the recording is missing)

The big idea

Hear a claim, even from a channeller, and look for the layer beneath it. Told that ETs would spray nano into the air to change the DNA, the Council answered: never. Spraying more nano on top of what's already there would make "a nano war in everyone's body". Help comes by frequency: a precise wave shuts existing nano down to zero, a new resonance flushes it out, and a blueprint molecular DNA restoration reprints the cell to its original print. But help needs your consent to the positive: if you keep consenting to the manipulation, "there's nothing we are going to do to interfere".

#Key teachings

#25.1Check it against your own connection

"The information is a vast amount... you will not find it only from one source of media or only from me." When someone makes a statement, a politician or a channeller, she asks for confirmation through her own connection; when she points to a person, it's because she was told something similar. A download can be true at its top layer and still need the layer under it: "I'm not saying the person is wrong... it's certainly another layer under." Do the same: research many sources, and check what resonates (see S4 16, whom to trust).

#25.2Nano will not be sprayed by them

As she understood it, nano designed for a function can reach the genome and change the code, and spread to every organ; a channeller had said extraterrestrials would spray nano into the air to change the DNA. The Council's answer: "In no circumstances... no way will we ever take the chance with that." If anyone sprays something, "it is not going to be the ETs or consciousness itself." Each body and each DNA is different; a second layer of nano with a new code, while the first is still working, would be "absolutely dangerous... a war zone in there".

The message: how help comes

A frequency wave into the collective hologram, "so specific and precise" that whatever nano is doing in the DNA "is going to shut down... like an electric shock... stop to zero". At the zero point another resonance gives the nanobots a new code, so they're flushed out of the body (see S4 15, S4 20).

Then blueprint molecular DNA restoration: "the singular oval stimulant of the cell... is reconstructed to the original print by bursting into one singular motion. Then it will get restructured by new DNA codon TPT singular frequency, decoded and reprinted": one of many processes, "technology that is beyond your imagination. We are thousands of years ahead of you. Have faith." They won't reveal their moves: "you do not give, in a chess game, your moves."

Added note

mRNA vaccines don't change your DNA, and "shedding" from vaccinated people has not been shown. Please make health decisions with your own doctor.

#25.3Help needs your consent

"Fear is what makes you consent to the manipulation. That is why you must recognize the darkness, then shine the light to see through it." "Trust the plan: no one will be left behind" who was manipulated without consent, or received something without knowing: that gives the positive side permission to help. But if you reject the positive and keep consenting, staying in the ego ("I'm a doctor... I know better") even when pushed against the wall, "then you consented to their timeline, and there's nothing we are going to do to interfere", because otherwise they would be overtaking your consciousness. "A level playing field: don't touch my guys, I will not touch your guys."

When what you feel and what you do split apart. If you feel one thing in the heart and say and do another, the structure separates and the fluctuation spins toward a negative outcome, from the polarity you created against the heart. Daily, switch your frequency from negative to positive and mutate what is flashed on top of you (see S1 3). "Who is in power here, me or you? It's you projecting me, because you are it."

#The method — Giving Without Being Used

  1. Give from the heart, without expectation (energy philanthropy, see S1 5 and S1 4).
  2. Know your boundaries: "do not give more than you can chew".
  3. Notice disrespect: someone who keeps taking, can give, and gives nothing back while they see you struggle.
  4. Say it honestly: "I'm sorry, I will not be available to you, due to my decision."
  5. Keep giving to new people, and help again anyone who is truly in a tough situation.
  6. If you charge, charge with reason: let the limit be felt in the heart.

#Questions answered

If I give from the heart and the other person takes advantage, should I keep giving to them?
Give without expectation, but you can't let someone keep grabbing: it will deplete you. If they can give and don't, "they quite frankly disrespect you": it's not about the money, it's about respect for your work. She has refused such people, and the Council has twice shut her programme down to stop someone using her work for their own gain. "That is why I stand alone." Charging $500 an hour while people suffer "is connected with the ego". When you give and feel good simply because you gave, "that is the resonance to accumulate"; when you need it, it comes. Money is the core of the structure now (even to build an eco-city), until it becomes obsolete and skills become the commodity.
I'm a nurse practitioner with cancer patients, also a Reiki master and hypnotist, overwhelmed. What am I here to do?
You're fluctuating too much to pinpoint one reality. Practise each thing you wish to do for 10 days each; pick the one that brings the highest joy: that is your highest-resonance probability, which will expand into the collective to support you.

She recommended Mary Rodwell's books (from her interview) and a book by a girl from North Korea, about abuse, for teenagers if appropriate: "that should be educational in the school system".
