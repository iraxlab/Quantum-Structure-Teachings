The big idea

The Council of Nine said the Earth is both: a sphere in the etheric field that, through frequencies given to humanity's consciousness, projects in matter as a flat land under a dome, a shield of sonic frequency that once locked the soul in. The soul is vital because it projects frequency, and it is also "your ticket back home". Extraterrestrials removed that shield so you could project the reality you choose. Their technologies (the hyperspace hologram, avatar downloads, thought-piloted craft through stargates) show what your own consciousness can do. Suppressed free energy already exists and will emerge, but future technology mimics your plasma biofield, so it can't be sustained by ego. Hold your vibration, unite and open-source.

#11.1The shield and the walk-ins

Years ago, the Council said they and the extraterrestrials were shutting down an artificial grid: a frequency sound shooting around Earth. In her account (see S8 E36, 9.1), after explorers found the black goo, they recorded the signal consciousness gave and used it to download part of a consciousness from a spacecraft into the 3D frame, keeping a link with the craft: a walk-in, as she describes herself. Travel isn't by propulsion: reading a planet's frequency, a craft folds space-time and "pokes a hole" through a vortex, a stargate.

#11.2Suppressed technology

Free-energy devices have been demonstrated by various inventors (she shows a magnetic generator), yet suppressed, while the world fights over oil. In her view, negative ETs and those they influence used advanced technology to keep consciousness feeding their frequency. Remote-viewing programs recruited gifted children to predict how reality would collapse, but couldn't see past 2012, when consciousness gave a sequence that reactivated all its realities.

#11.3Protecting your field

When out of body you need a shield of resonance, or you can be trapped in parallel belief systems. Once, lucid, she was shown Earth exploding, with a reptoid beside her; she said "That's not my reality", and was popped back into her body. Once the shield came down, the protocols were released so you can create your own grid, "reality within the reality", still seeing the collective while holding your heart frequency.

#11.4The hyperspace hologram

In a spacecraft, a sentient organic intelligence, like a plasma at the centre, reads your thoughts and heart and builds a virtual hologram that becomes matter; given a planet's coordinates, it sends fractal signals to your brain so you can download into that reality, coexisting in both. She was taught to pilot a craft by thought, including how to stop it.

#Questions answered

If you're on a spaceship projecting to different realities, is it reality?
Each moment you shift into a slightly different Earth, a new version of you. When ETs download part of your consciousness from a hyperspace hologram, two versions coexist (one on the craft, one on Earth) and can exchange information telepathically, recognized by resonance. Holding your vibration without consenting through fear, you begin to vibrate in a fifth-dimensional platform while still seeing the 3D collective. You came here to help humanity ascend.
(On the ocean.)
The ocean holds another, very intelligent world; whales and dolphins use sonar that creates imagery. In her teaching, humans were genetically upgraded rather than descended from monkeys, and that history was erased.
How do the holographic imprints that shield and hide us work?
Fear and hunger are downloaded massively through screens, so people project them and you see them in your mirror. Recognize it, subside the negative into positive moment to moment, even when everything you've collected "smacks you in the face". Steady, repetitive vibration builds the potency that opens a timeline above 3D.
A hypnosis case said the body was engineered from prehistoric humans and acts as an avatar for the soul. Have you heard this?
Yes: long ago Orion beings told her "we are from you... your body is like an avatar". When told she was already on a spacecraft, she was depressed for days until a synchronicity confirmed it. Beings may be seen but not remembered, because your field can't yet hold the imagery. Ascension takes the body too, so diet and meditation clear negative frequency from the nucleus. The key is consistency: challenging moments are your consciousness telling you it's working.
