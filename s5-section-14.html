The big idea

What you take in over and over is a consent, and repeated consent builds potency: it crystallizes your permanent timeline. Something you were exposed to once, without consenting again and again, cannot hold its potency and will collapse. Knowing you can "transform" an energy is not the same as being able to; you transform by not complying with it. Use common sense: don't take in what you don't understand, and follow the practical steps you were given years ago (distilled water, no GMO, no chemicals).

#Key teachings

#14.1The potency of repeated consent

Every choice of action and thought accumulates a magnetic and gravitational field. Consent to the same negative field over and over and it gathers enough potency to construct a parallel reality, and you lock yourself in. Too much of it and your consciousness is overwritten: you now project reality from their perception, not yours, and your mind will "create reality to give you the okay" for whatever they want. This is how deception starts: it looks like the right thing from inside the locked timeline. Pull back, analyse and peel the layers (see S4 13).

From the booksFrom her books (HR): the Harmonic Reactor explains positive and negative charge and the "Metatronic conversion" of sound frequency, which she points to as the science behind this talk.

#14.2Transform by not complying

"I transform it by not complying to their energy." Understand what they are doing and their survival, without hate; there is "no judgment here, it is simply existence" (you eat animals; something else may want to consume you). What upset her was people who tell everyone it is fine to take in anything because "it's just energy and I can transform it." You can, only with the knowledge of potency and accumulation; without it you consent to the structure. "You always have choice because you are the creator": create the probabilities that pull you away (another job, a way out) instead of fearing.

The message: the reptile (her report)

She relayed a message from "the animal" whose venom she was told had been used synthetically without its permission: those who consented over and over "have chosen to experience that"; the responsibility is theirs. "Do not hate the reptiles. They actually can help us." In 2018 the Council told her that a substance in Komodo dragon venom, spun at high velocity, breaks down into sequences, one of which could help the DNA slow and repair damage. She later saw lab work on Komodo-blood research. "I was never given the entire truth... let that be your decision."

Added safety note

This is her report, not established fact. Approved vaccines and medicines do not contain snake or reptile venom, and Komodo-dragon research is early laboratory work, not a treatment. Please make health decisions with your doctor.

Her instructions: the body and water

Drink distilled water to flush out; stop eating GMO; stop using chemicals; avoid fluoride ("fluoride is poison... why are you buying toothpaste with fluoride?"). A good home water system is fine. "All that is action from the person itself." She has said for years that she drinks distilled water.

From the booksFrom her books (DP): Downloads & Predictions (around page 46) covers frequency, sound and distilled water, including a scientist's explanation of what happens in the molecular structure when water, a conductor, carries no charge and flushes things out.
Added safety note

Her view on fluoride and distilled water is her own. Health authorities consider fluoride at recommended levels safe for teeth, and long-term use of only distilled water may need attention to minerals in your diet. Ask your doctor or dentist before changing.

#14.3Don't play with frequencies

Neutralizing something precise in water needs a precise technology: you must measure what is there and know it. Crystallizing water depends on how long, what time, how potent, the duration and how many times. "Don't go around and start playing with frequencies now because you think you're going to transform it." Anything that reached water supplies without entering your genetics will not hold enough potency to keep reprinting, and subsides.

Do the unawakened realize what is going on after they pass?
Yes (see the Etheric Legions, S4 38). Don't judge anyone: most who spread misinformation truly believe it; it is part of the information, locked in by accumulated consent. Even those who chose to take it in chose from the higher self, to show the rest what is true and not; without references you can't know where you are. If you were wrong, so what: drop the ego and move forward.
Can distilling and blessing water clear the negative structure?
Something that evil needs a precise frequency technology. If it is in water supplies, it will eventually not hold enough potency to reprint: "don't worry about it." She drinks distilled water; the Council never told her yes or no on this belief, she presents the facts and the quantum structure.
Will consciousness allow our galaxy to be pulled 100% into a fallen system?
She won't comment on another teacher's material. The Council: consciousness never allows itself to self-destruct; it always reprints itself to survive, because it is ascension. How and how long is fluctuation. In Project Looking Glass, consciousness will not show the true outcome to those who can't hold the vibration above the structure, or it could be used to sabotage ascension. Don't chase final predictions; tuning into an event with a dropped vibration can create it. The end result: humanity wins, but the players change by daily choice. Stay neutral, observe, peel the layers, check whether a messenger has changed the narrative or gone into money or ego.
