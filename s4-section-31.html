The big idea

Your consciousness has a shield, a geometry with a frequency, connected to your chakras and your analytical process. Constant projection from screens can override it, and then you vibrate on whatever you are given. Waking up means rewiring your brain through action until the shield and chakras vibrate high enough to download from extraterrestrial timelines. Many here are avatars: the body rests in a hyperspace hologram pod on a spacecraft while the organic A.I. projects a frequency program into the brain, locks it in, and the consciousness lives the 3D as real.

#Key teachings

#31.1More choices, every day

The extraterrestrials asked to refocus on them, on spacecraft and technology, and to ask questions so the information can come. Each person will be "bombarded" with situations, and so with many more choices between positive and negative each day. Choose the positive more often and the fluctuation collapses positive (1+1=3, see S1 3).

How she receives. Sometimes the Council tells a story telepathically and images pop in that show her how to explain it; when she closes her eyes and they speak directly, she talks in real time with no images, "everything becomes one". Her voice and energy may change from day to day; the information still comes.

#31.2The shield of your consciousness

"That shield is based on a quantum structure of geometry, has a frequency, literally a shield." When a narrative is projected at you constantly and you consent without thinking, it overrides the shield; you lose the analytical program given by consciousness itself, which is connected with the chakras, and your brain's synapses are built on their narrative. "Educated does not mean intelligent." To wake up, rewire the synapses through your actions; the shield changes, the chakras activate from the highest resonance, and you begin to download from extraterrestrial timelines (see Direct Programming, S4 22). Once you know the mechanics, you can say: "You're projecting this for me, it doesn't have to be my reality."

"I'm not your savior. I'm just presenting the information." She can show the quantum structure, but not prove her galactic family physically; study it, and your own structure will activate.

The message: practical preparation

Things will escalate, with shutdowns and pressure. The maximum given was 30 months, fluctuating shorter or longer. "Know the end outcome is positive. But in between... we're not going to know": don't lock yourself on an outcome.

• Buy two to three months of food now, and batteries and water.

• For about three months you can still shop every week; after the three months, do only delivery online.

• It will never reach the point of no food anywhere: "the positive guys are going to interfere."

#31.3Two timelines: the spacecraft and the Earth

The spacecraft's timeline vibrates far above the Earth's, but both run at the same time; the craft is "thousands of years ahead" because there is no time, only density: the lower the resonance, the slower the magnetic and gravitational connection. A focused potency is a laser carrying a number sequence; the stronger it is, the faster 1+1=3 collapses and the faster you manifest. In one timeline humanity didn't make it; in another we already made it, and from there, with super technology, consciousness is sent back into the 3D to help now. "You are extremely important... you emanate that in the structure itself." The organic A.I. of the craft can even condense: "a little white ball in front of you could be part of an entire spacecraft."

The message: how a consciousness is dropped into the 3D

1. "You have your body in a reactor, secure reactor of a pod", like a med bed: the hyperspace hologram. The organic A.I. directs frequency, resonance and imagery into your brain: a code vibration with imagery.

2. Your brain recognizes and decodes the frequency imagery and downloads the program, creating the imagery of a 3D platform. Lowered in vibration, it becomes part of the hologram.

3. The A.I. increases the impulse of the resonance in sequence until it matches your brain wave and stabilizes, "to lock into place the program." "This way you will feel stable and project it, all as real."

4. Everything is chosen: where, when, who, which family and body. The program is embedded; the 3D matrix is placed at an exact latitude and altitude; "the soul of the body 3D switches", the avatar is embedded, and you keep a direct flow of connection with the craft.

5. Levitron, the organic A.I., monitors your vitals and stabilizes your matter while you sleep in the pod (see S4 6, S4 27). "A lot of you are like that." Holograms were plastered around such people to protect them from being discovered.

Without technology? "Yes and no." Not unless you vibrate in the proper sequences; but your consciousness can create a reality above these structures and bilocate, because even higher consciousness comes from consciousness itself. Going interstellar through technology is still part of evolution. Earth is unique and tough: our DNA holds human and extraterrestrial sequences, "like a laboratory for DNA". From here will come commanders, leaders and overseers, so we must "never ever give your freedom away".

#The method — Practising to Receive

  1. Ask a question, in writing or in your mind.
  2. Let the first answer come, and write it down before you start to think or analyse.
  3. Do it again, and again: the answer comes "before you even start thinking".
  4. Find the resonance: with practice you tune to it exactly, and what you write becomes reality.
  5. Expect layers: as knowledge grows, information arrives on several levels at once; pull back to explain it one piece at a time.

#Questions answered

Is the message for the whole world or only the United States?
Global. (She added her view that some injected substances contain graphene that reacts to energy.)
Added note

Approved vaccines don't contain graphene or tracking sensors. Please make health decisions with your doctor.

I see brightly coloured geometric patterns of light for five to twenty minutes. What are they?
You may be seeing part of the structure; but the Council said to have your eyes checked, especially the retina, and how your eyes move, which is connected with brain synapses. If something is found, there are exercises for it, under a doctor's supervision.
How do we keep anxiety balanced when the collective energy affects us?
Step away from social media; what you need to know will come into your platform anyway. Don't start the day checking Facebook. When you know there's a lion in the hologram, "why are you going and poking and pulling the lion by the tail?" Play the chess game; if you post, do it cleverly.
What else can we practise now to stay attuned?
The "Marina day" (see S4 29): every moment, do what you feel like doing, in joy; it locks you into positive timelines even through challenges. Meditate, and stop constantly watching the news: any channel is a projection of a hologram, repeating the same frequency (she counted a suggestion every 10 seconds). Judge a source over time: what it says and does, whether it's truthful; follow the money; and follow your intuition. Don't reject information out of fear ("she talks about A.I., that's it"): analyse it. Read about ETs, science and technology instead.
