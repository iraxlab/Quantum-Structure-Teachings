The big idea

The Archon structure reprinted itself endlessly, like a factory mimicking consciousness, so the higher realms placed pure vessels of intention inside it to track its shifts in real time. Once more than 70% of humanity awoke in some way, a loophole in non-interference opened, and the higher realms declared war on the negative structure, a multidimensional war using technology far superior to theirs. Expect exposure, arrests and new positive technologies, often settled quietly; how you experience it depends on your resonance. Your part: be the best of yourself for humanity daily, unite without picking sides, and act as your higher version.

#Key teachings

The message: the declaration (condensed)

"Consciousness itself has to have... balance within its own field." The artificial structure "became very potent... like a cancer cell": each world cleaned revealed another, "almost like a never-ending" reprinting, "genius... the way [it] mimic[ked] the original structure", yet also an offspring of consciousness. So "in the core of that archon structure" they placed "a pure vessel of intention", shifting with it to track it in real time. Its manipulation implanted look-alike sequences in your belief system, so you believed the mirror image. "You reach more than 70% of awakening... we found a loophole through the bylaws... of not interfere[nce]." Bylaw: whatever cannot restructure because its signal is off balance with the original blueprint, consciousness "has every right to restructure it... repopulate with the new sequences... and move forward." "Because they cannot convert it, from the higher realms we declare war on them... this war is multidimensional."

#44.1Technology, not rejection

Whatever consciousness has created can't be rejected, but it can be restructured: the loophole is that artificial structures can be restructured by their own higher, sentient version (organic intelligence). They kept humanity primitive; to win, the higher realms use far superior technology. Never mimic your brain with synthetic nano: your original neural pathways are the circuitry consciousness downloads through.

#44.2What to expect

In coming years: accountability without diplomatic immunity, arrests of politicians, doctors and lawyers, blackmail exposed through recordings or old data, and "glitches" where one hologram replaces another. Wars they declare won't reach the momentum they want, though casualties may wake people. New technology, banking, hospital systems and free energy will appear. Much will be settled quietly: "by the time you turn around... it's all played out", experienced according to your resonance; some will face strange predicaments as tests. Consciousness preserves even negative entities as a memory in its many-layered Akashic library.

Added note

These are channelled predictions given as received; specific claims are not presented as fact.

#44.3Your part

Ascension is singular: friends and family may not understand: that's their free will. Be compassionate and love humanity at its best; delay in compassion can leave you unable to match the resonance that pulls you through. Your one particle projects the collective. The balance: 1 + 1 = 3 = 1, with zero at the centre between negative and positive.

#Questions answered

Will downloading our higher versions reduce the need for walk-ins?
It will increase them: as you open to the higher realms through the heart, more souls exit and enter to take over the negative platform.
How can 70% be awake? Where I live 90% are clueless.
That's your own reality: switch it. Say every moment "humanity is awakened", even seeing the opposite, because what you see is a mirror image projected into your field. In her reality, nearly everyone she talks with is awake: her resonance created it.
How can we send peace and unity to a war region?
Collective prayer creates a potent resonance; pray without duality, for everyone at once. Don't pick a side: everyone was manipulated.
How do we move through the dark night of the soul while staying balanced?
You couldn't be here if you weren't ready to transform it; the confusion comes from giving your power to the mirror image. Don't comply; restructure with your own beliefs and unite simply because we exist. Be consistent: your actions must be more potent than what you see. You are the alchemist.
With access to our etheric records, why can't we change certain things?
In the Archon structure the original records were withheld; there were double records of an illusion. Readers may not know which record they read. The end result is fixed (humanity made it); how changes every nanosecond.
What does "new radical structure" mean?
Very quick, advanced changes, possibly with technology or organic intelligence, to stop harm: portals and platforms long kept from you.
How can I unlearn all the manipulation?
Practice: dilute one density with another, a new resonance that keeps penetrating the old until the reality changes. It's the hardest part, because everything you see is the opposite of how it works.
Is there anything extra we can do, or is holding our ground enough?
You coexist in the higher realms: express yourself as your higher version: "I am my higher version through the heart." Ask what it would think and do. Steel plus compassion is the balancing combination.
