#The method — the wrong phone call

When you reach out telepathically and something negative answers:

  1. Know what it is: a thought is like an etheric phone call; each reality holds a different frequency, and the resonance you send can reach a negative one, since both exist at once.
  2. No fear, no anger: keep your heart and mind positive; fear projects a new frequency that overrides and dissolves a real download.
  3. Thank and neutralize: "Thank you, zero point... I reached [a wrong number]... that was not what I wish to connect with. Thank you." This shuts down that signal.
  4. Call again: if it happens ten times, override it eleven.
  5. Practise seeing: look calmly at what is shown in your third eye; ask again and zoom in (as she learned to recognize the motherboard). "You shall create to see it, not see it to believe it."

For deeper interference (black magic and the like), she doesn't work at that level; she points to shamanic practitioners.
