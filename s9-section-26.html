The big idea

A painful memory from years ago is a hologram from a completely different version of Earth; you are a new version of yourself. But when you recall it, it activates your heart and becomes "now", and if you keep vibrating with it, you shift into the same negative sequence. Two ways out: immediately do something positive and engaging in the moment, or acknowledge the memory ("I recognize you") and create a new one, like making your own movie, until the new version becomes predominant. The old one still exists somewhere, but you no longer carry it, and its echo stops leaking into your reality, even across generations.

#Key teachings

#26.1"Stop" and synchronicity

When she had almost nothing and felt she was losing her mind, she heard "Stop". Saying it firmly shocks you out of the sound sequence in your mind so new information can enter (see 9.24). Her guides: it's an illusion; "when you need a million dollars, you are going to have it; when you need one dollar, you are going to have it." Everything comes through synchronicity, as her own work did over the years. We're still on the trajectory through money to utopia; implement it with integrity.

#26.2Reaching another version of you

It is very possible, often through dreams, where you may meet a version of yourself in another timeline. She trained in lucid dreaming and by the second week became lucid: a laser from her third eye drew a circle that first failed (red), then completed (green) and opened a portal. Another time she was told "you are not allowed to be here" and found herself as a child in her old kitchen. This reality is another dream state that feels real; you control it by implementing the knowledge.

#26.3A memory is a different Earth

If something negative happened 20 years ago, that hologram is on a completely different parallel timeline, a different version of Earth with nothing to do with you now. But the memory is a vibration in your field; recalled, it reflects into the heart and becomes "now", and if you keep holding it, you shift into the same negative sequence, so whatever you project ends negative.

#26.4Rewriting the memory

Replace the sequence: acknowledge it ("I recognize you, I know you exist in this parallel"), then create a brand-new memory, as if making your own movie, in which that experience was positive ("my childhood was beautiful"). You create a parallel version with your own consciousness; once it's potent, you connect only with the new one. This isn't denying what happened, but you no longer carry its negative spectrum. A frequency never converted keeps echoing through timelines, which is why karma can pass through generations; converting it in real time stops the echo.

Added safety note

Added safety note: for trauma or abuse, she encourages replacing the memory with positive experience; this practice doesn't replace support from a qualified therapist or counsellor.

#The method — Rewriting a painful memory (as given)

  1. Notice the memory pulling you into its feeling now.
  2. Say "Stop": pull out of that sound in your mind.
  3. Either do something positive right away (Marina's day).
  4. Or acknowledge it: "I recognize you; you belong to another Earth."
  5. Create a new memory, like your own movie, in which it went well.
  6. Repeat until the new version is predominant and the echo fades.

Yes, often through dreams and lucid dreaming, and you can restructure a past experience from the now, as described above. She calls this advanced knowledge that needs the earlier teachings and practice.
