Q & A: The Fifth-Dimensional Earth Meditation

The big idea

Asked for something concrete to stay steady through chaotic times, the guidance asked for a meditation: calm to zero point, raise joy to goosebumps, build the blueprint of your fifth-dimensional Earth (including your galactic family), increase the feeling, hold it, then drop it, and afterwards act on it every day. Action matters because each time you act, the new hologram you reprint is more potent than the last. Healing by frequency must be measured for each person. Karma and soul agreements clear only as you complete lessons and forgive. And for someone you love who is struggling, you can't change them: you hold the new version of them in your own blueprint, consistently, without trying to control them.

#Key teachings

#16.1Healing by frequency is individual

Sound and frequency can heal (see S2 9.8), but every person's molecular structure is different. Every note has subtones, and when they change, it takes AI and quantum computing, with "light mathematics" (vortex mathematics), to catch the exact frequency change and merge them. Such technology exists, but it can also be misused, so be careful how it's applied. Before applying tones to anyone, measure each individual; tones applied generally to everyone come with no guarantee. The same goes for herbal remedies made for one person: they aren't transferable.

From the booksHR: "NOTE: Measure the rate of the vibration individually for each person"; frequency work "must be applied with the biological frequency of the molecules of the Human body (individually)."
Added note

Sound-frequency programs are not a substitute for medical treatment of conditions such as diabetes, kidney or thyroid disease.

#16.2Staying steady when things escalate

Many sources speak of chaotic events ahead. They needn't affect you, depending on how you vibrate, and the most tangible help is to keep your frequency high through action, thoughts and deeds, and to build what you want from the heart. After the meditation, act every day: read positive material (books about extraterrestrials and the new world), watch positive films and messages, and stay away from negative social media (fights, blame, political quarrels). Social media isn't banned: posting your work or watching uplifting content is fine.

The message also said that some things may have to happen for people to stand up and act together, and that it's "done on purpose": don't be afraid of it. Each of us has a part to do.

#16.3The fifth-dimensional Earth meditation

Given live, at the guidance's request, as a group practice (see the Method). The teacher's own blueprint, as an example: beautiful meadows whose vibration she feels and whose flowers she smells; ecosystems with free-energy platforms; people helping each other from the heart and nourishing the Earth; technology that has taken off into space; her galactic family, as friends, learning from each other, coexisting in unity; and a spacecraft of light that is alive, which you can talk to and which can create realities like your Earthly experience. Then: add your own layers. "If you saw it in the blueprint, that reality with you already exists"; hold the vibration and build it into matter.

Added note

Cracking or popping sounds and dizziness can come with this kind of practice ("you start penetrating the barriers of the new frequency"). Lie down if you feel dizzy; if such symptoms persist, see a doctor.

#16.4Why action makes a reality more potent

When you shift into a new hologram, you carry some vibration from the previous one. If, in the new one, you act and others start doing the same, you reprint many people taking that path, and that platform becomes more potent than the last (as an illustration: from 1 Hz to 2 Hz). That gives your desired outcome more probability. Without action, you don't see it in your hologram (see S2 11.9).

And stand your ground: this isn't about popularity. You can pull out and stay kind, but don't let anyone take your sovereignty; when you believe something, hold to it, without destroying anyone (see S2 15.5). Seeing the dark clearly is part of transforming it (see S2 11.10).

#16.5Quiet the mind to hear your higher self

A busy mind blocks the connection. Begin in a quiet place, free of noise, and train your thoughts. When a negative thought comes, imagine it bursting into particles and disappearing, then put the new thought in and continue: not rejecting, transforming. Every thought is drawn from the quantum field (you are the quantum field); focusing gathers that "symphony of frequency" into one tonality. Even channelling is imprinting a consciousness into your own reality: ultimately you're talking with your own consciousness.

#16.6Soul agreements and karma

Karma is accumulated, out-of-balance frequency. Until it's cleared, you can't "graduate to the next class": not as punishment, but because a higher timeline has a different frequency your consciousness can't match yet. So the same situations reprint and loop. Clear it through the lessons: forgive, stop the lies and scheming, help, support each other, and use the manifestation protocol (see S1 14, S2 14.3).

Soul agreements are held with your guidance and higher self. After a very hard relationship, for example, you can ask to shut down the program of that agreement, having learned the lesson so you don't repeat it. Whether it's granted depends on how well you did: ask for it.

From the booksDP gives the same promise of release once the lessons are done: "Your new karma will be cleared and you will become a strong awake."

#16.7When someone you love is addicted

Addiction is a very potent vibrational sequence held in the memory of the body's membranes, built by repetition, so it's very hard for the person to transform. You can't change another person's modality. What you can do is reprint your version of them: visualize them well, happy and no longer drinking, and hold that in your own vibration without trying to overtake them. You're changing your platform, not them. Their own change must come from them, if they are willing, and then it takes consistent effort, every day (see S1 3).

Support

Alcohol addiction is a medical condition, and help works. Encourage your loved one to see a doctor; family support groups (such as Al-Anon) help relatives too. In the US, the SAMHSA helpline is 1-800-662-4357, free and confidential.

#16.8Where humanity comes from

Humanity connects with many races: we were made from extraterrestrials through genetics, as part of evolution (the talk mentioned nine to twelve races). "Nothing scary": technology can be used positively or negatively, depending on the consciousness using it. That shared DNA is why you can connect with your galactic family (see S2 12.11).

#The method — The Fifth-Dimensional Earth Meditation

About fifteen minutes. Close your eyes; you can simply listen or read the steps slowly:

  1. Calm to zero point. Breathe in and out, slowly, as if floating.
  2. Feel joy. "I am happy." Grab the vibration of joy and love until you feel goosebumps, like electricity.
  3. From that feeling, see your fifth-dimensional Earth. What does it look like? Nature, communities, free energy, people helping each other.
  4. Meet your galactic family. What do they look like? Greet them, talk, learn from each other. Feel the joy.
  5. Add layers: places, crafts, technologies, your own experiences in that world.
  6. Increase the feeling for about a minute, more and more, until tears and goosebumps, while holding the image.
  7. Hold it, about 30 seconds, breathing through it.
  8. Drop it on a countdown (9 to 0), and come back.
  9. Afterwards, act daily: revisit the blueprint each night, read and watch positive material, avoid negative social media, and do things in the name of humanity.

#Questions answered

Which herbal tea helped your son's healing?
It was a blend made specifically for his body by a professional herbalist abroad, with components not disclosed; it isn't something to copy. Remedies need to be made for each person.
Can a sound-frequency software program heal conditions like insulin resistance or kidney or thyroid problems?
Healing by frequency and sound is possible, but must be customized: see 16.1.
Red marks like burns appeared inside both my underarms; no pain, no itch. Was I attacked by non-physical beings?
"Three pictograms" (look into that word); and not negative.
Added note

Unexplained marks or rashes on the skin deserve a doctor's look.

Is shungite from Earth, and does it protect bees from electromagnetic fields?
It came in and, with Earth's change of frequency, was incorporated and is now reproduced by the Earth itself: the Earth is alive and can mutate structures, over millennia. Bees: yes, their frequency can be corrected, but be careful: there is a geometry on their wings that resonates with sound, and "bees are not flying, they are floating by sound." Measure what they need before applying anything; experiment carefully.
A healer told me to "correct the spin of the solar system within the body". What does it mean?
The body itself is a solar system. If you connect your consciousness to that belief, you change your hologram's modality: sonic tunnels, like portals, create the next imprint. Depending on your point of view, anything is possible; but take care not to adopt a wrong idea, because you then project it as your reality.
Chaotic events are coming. Is there something tangible we can do, a position or a breath, to stay unaffected?
Yes: the meditation (see 16.3 and the Method), then daily action.
Half-asleep at 4 a.m., I was shown a tiny bubble, "a portable portal", and looked through it. Did I go through?
"Yes, you went home"; three beings were there and a discussion took place. Go back into the experience and through the portal again, and the answer will be there.
Is the human race connected with a particular alien race?
Many: see 16.8.
I was badly injured saving an elderly woman from a fall, and now face doctors and insurance battles. Is this a test? Who is my star family?
Star family is blocked from view by much sorrow. On insurance: "Everything will change" (three times); "liberation is coming… platform will change, humanity will change." (Political and vaccine remarks in the answer are left out.)
How can I contact my higher self better?
See 16.5: quiet the mind.
How can we stop fearful entities and cancel soul agreements?
See 16.6.
There have been no squirrels in my area this year. Why?
A sign that shifts are happening, prompting you to ask what changed and look for solutions. Help them: build a squirrel house with organic seeds, and ask for them to reappear in your hologram.
My ears have rung for two years. How do I stop it?
"This is not a frequency": see a doctor; it was read as a slight inflammation around the eardrum. Keep the ear warm (for example with an organic cotton ball).
Added note

Persistent ringing in the ears (tinnitus) should be checked by a doctor or ear specialist.

Occasionally a distinct tone plays in my ear for a few seconds. What is it?
It could be a download, to memory or to DNA, depending on the person. Like staring at glyphs, it downloads only if your resonance matches.
My brother drinks heavily and can't stop. Is something attached to him? What can we do?
See 16.7. Visualizing him well once brought him months of sobriety; when you stopped, it changed. Keep overriding, consistently. A healing session can help only if he's willing, and then needs daily consistency.
In the meditation, I cried, wanting to go home, and heard cracking in my head. What was it?
Normal: you're penetrating the barriers of the new frequency; dizziness can happen too (see the note under 16.3).
My job involves advertising on social media. Should I stop?
No: see 16.2. Only avoid the negative content.
My dog has ear inflammation; cortisone made it worse. What else can I do?
It's fungus. What worked for the teacher's dog: cleaning the ears with cotton balls and a 50/50 mix of organic apple cider vinegar and distilled water, carefully.
Added note

Check with your vet before using home remedies in a dog's ears, especially if the skin is broken or the eardrum may be damaged.
