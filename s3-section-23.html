The big idea

An agreement is how a spark of the one particle chooses what to experience: it splits into fractals, each carrying its own signal, and before coming into a world like this one it agrees with its guides and galactic family on a blueprint, written as a living light-language script. How well you do here can change that blueprint, and until an agreement is complete you may not move on. In the dense 3D structure many lost the cord to that blueprint; that is why the knowledge is being restored. Some records and memories stay closed to you, not because they're negative, but because you need to go through your lessons without interference.

#Key teachings

#23.1How agreements are made

From the one particle, fractals split off, each a symphony of signals, creating universes within universes. To experience resonances fully in matter, consciousness learns how they work together: a thought is a pulse, "almost like electricity", rippling through a hologram until a universe forms for it. You can experience being a rock, a river, a flower or an entity; each such spark has its own specific resonance, "think of it as a soul creation". Fractals of one origin may evolve differently (one with Arcturians, one with Pleiadians), and agreements are made between your guides about what you will experience: for example, a Pleiadian being splitting a spark of itself into the 3D structure to help humanity.

#23.2The agreement is a living script

Each agreement "has a specific resonance script, and it actually looks like a live structure, light language": looking at it, one knows it all, "like a blueprint for what's going to be". It is seldom changed, but it can be, depending on how well you do. "Until you complete that agreement, you may not move forward to the next level." You're always connected to your galactic family; but in the dense Archon structure some lost track of "the cord holding the blueprint of your agreements" and began creating realities far from the original (see S3 14.3). A spark that disengages completely collapses from within and returns to source.

#23.3Many Akashic records

There isn't one record: "there are a lot of Akashic records", global, interstellar and planetary, within realities within realities. What you see in them depends on what you're connected to and allowed to see in that moment, because you must stay on the track of your original agreement.

#23.4When information is closed

Sometimes even the council can't see something: "we don't see the beings; we just see the information is not available", like "a reflection: it's not allowed to enter". It isn't because it's negative; it's because it isn't needed in that moment, and the person will have their own experience of it. The same is why many don't remember much of their childhood: "you need to go through the lessons without any interference." Complete them in neutrality, or you may have to repeat them, which you don't want in the ascension.

#23.5Dreams are realities too

In a dream your consciousness shifts into another frequency, while you still know at some level that you're asleep in this reality: two parallel realities at once. "The dream state is just another reality, like this one." Why can't we do here what we do in a lucid dream? "It's all your belief system": you are creating it here too. But not everything you wish is allowed in a dream: you can't enter a reality you're not yet allowed to, as the teacher was once turned away from her galactic family's craft (see S3 9).

#23.6Signs in your hologram

Repeating numbers are codes in your holographic imprint: "when you look at that number, look at your location and remember who you are." The same can come as the same bird, the same truck, the same object again and again. Pay attention: they are designed for you, to help you through the process (see S3 13).

#23.7Giving from the heart, and boundaries

How do you give without expectation and still protect yourself? "You give the answer when you have the feeling that you've been taken advantage of: put up the boundaries. Very simple." Giving from the heart never means giving up your sovereignty.

#23.8Preparing, in neutrality

Is prepping necessary? It is, in the quantum sense: stay at zero point in neutrality, understanding how you are modifying your structures as things change globally. Hold your vibration, act toward the positive, and "even if something occurs, you will be able to survive through the process, because you're going to project the realities that give you what you need." The innate abilities people hope technology will restore are already in you: "start taking choices from the heart."

#The method — Reading the Signs in Your Hologram

  1. Notice a repeat: a number, a bird, a vehicle, an object.
  2. Pause and look at where you are and what you're doing.
  3. Ask: what am I thinking and feeling right now?
  4. Remember who you are: the creator of this hologram.
  5. Adjust: if you were drifting, switch to the positive.
  6. Write it down; patterns become clearer over time (23.6).

#Questions answered

A tingling in my back, perhaps a cord to a family member?
Read as a pinching in the lower back: see a medical doctor, then a qualified massage therapist.
Will my abilities open, and what are they?
Use everything you know first; build your structures with potency; they'll show as situations to choose from.
I come from a planet with a particular name. What are the other two?
That name isn't recognized as a planet; perhaps a sound or a location frequency.
I dreamed of Marina giving numbers, then of a ship on a glowing sea.
A parallel universe you created from your consciousness, from what you'd been listening to.
Is a blackout coming? Should we prepare?
Not seen. See 23.8.
Is the magnetic pole reversal connected to the shift?
Yes, changing with the new frequencies, with much help to keep the planet's angle right for the portals.
Can a walk-in come while the original soul remains?
Yes, often, by permission of the original soul, to help in a situation or to bring you back on track with your agreement.
How can I prevent and release spirit attachments?
"You cannot be a healer if you don't control your reality": step off the belief that something is attached, and know 100% that you've shifted to a new reality where it isn't.
When will healing technologies be available?
Not soon; meanwhile "you already have them": choose from the heart. Choose carefully what you put into your body, following the facts.
How do I tell giving from the heart from needing boundaries?
See 23.7.
How, when and with whom do we make agreements?
See 23.1 and 23.2.
My divorce is dragging on.
Much stubbornness and ego keep it looping; act from the heart, stay neutral, don't hurt yourself, move forward.
I sway and circle in meditation. Why?
A blockage in the middle of your back: yoga, kundalini work, or an energy healer such as Reiki can open it. Rocking also helps: "you're never stationary".
I want to leave a secure job for my dream.
Feel you already have it; give gratitude for the job you have now; act daily, "brick by brick".
What are dreams, and what happens to the body?
See 23.5.
Who creates the scenes in my dreams?
"You are the one creating it. There's nobody outside of you."
How am I related to the numbers 3 and 13?
See 23.6.
Is my marriage agreement karmic? I feel stuck.
Stay neutral and override: thank the experience and build positive outcomes, without giving up your sovereignty; for some that means staying, for others leaving.
What is a specific plan for unity?
Your own creation; but look at flowers and trees, and find the one whose peace resonates in you.
What about solar flares?
No harm: the Earth's angle was adjusted and grids were reinforced. "Your sun is actually a portal itself"; there are craft larger than your sun.
Is birth control safe?
Proceed with caution: dosage and duration matter, and the body changes constantly.
