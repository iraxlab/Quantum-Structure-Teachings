The big idea

The final test is for everyone, including the aware: how will you help when it gets tough? If you find yourself in a hard situation, don't panic: you're there to help the rest, and through synchronicity you may be picked to lead. When you keep pushing through and talking it out rather than splitting apart, help appears "out of the blue" to show you're on the right path. Everyone you see is another version of you, projected from your consciousness, so caring for others' children passes on your love as fully as your own. When emotions spike: remove yourself, let it out, subside to zero, analyze through the heart, then construct a new reality, again and again, until it's cleared. "There's no such thing [as 'not personal']. Everything is personal."

#Key teachings

#32.1Not what she meant

Lab-grown products are spreading; buy carefully, ask local farmers what they use. Long ago she spoke of future machines that could create food at a push of a button, but from a positive molecular structure in the 5D platform; what's being done now is the opposite, designed without the human. Back to the land, back to community: share porches and rooftops for growing, and keep seeds.

#32.2Picked to lead

The timeline is splitting. If you're aware and land in a hard situation, don't panic: you're there to help, and when you look for solutions you may be picked to lead. In a disaster she saw the worst and the best of humanity at once. The DP calls this the final test, and it will be brutal, as the pandemic was for everyone: when you're aware, what will you do, and at what level will you help? As Europe squeezes, consciousness amplifies the positive by the power of ten; seek solutions without consenting. Many who meant well fell into ego and the race for popularity.

#32.3Pushing through brings help

After a hard family day (someone tried to steal her son's work), they talked it through instead of splitting apart. At lunch, a stranger paid their bill and sent a blessing for their family; everyone cried. The toughest moments can go very bad or very good: keep pushing through and trying to fix it (not violence, but emotion), and what you need will appear out of nowhere to show your next step. You still have the free will to take it or wait.

#The method — When emotions spike (as given)

  1. Recognize you're overloaded, and remove yourself: 10 to 30 minutes outside.
  2. Let it out: a nice video, a friend, even a shout.
  3. Let the emotion subside to zero through something physical.
  4. Analyze through the heart and compassion (not by excusing abuse).
  5. Construct a new reality from a clear mind; play hide and seek for solutions.
  6. Repeat every time it returns until it's 100% cleared; some patterns take years.
Added safety note

Added safety note: don't use substances to induce out-of-body experiences. For health concerns, consult qualified doctors, as she says. If someone appears drugged or in danger, call emergency services.

#Questions answered

Can the Council share my experience during a workshop?
The question must be specific; an overall question asks her to tap into the whole, which is confusing.
I wrote a book with an AI chatbot; why not direct channeling?
"You worry too much." Practice automatic writing: let go, connect with your higher self, and write whatever thought comes, as she did with the HR. Keep a record and research it even if it makes no sense, then use AI to find connections, asking scientific, analytical questions ("Is this possible? Keep an open mind") rather than database searches. That is how a Council clue about a "black pearl" opened new knowledge for her.
When we die, can we fall into a "light trap" or be persuaded to reincarnate?
Yes and no. Using substances to leave the body can open parts of your consciousness that aren't ready and invite low-vibration attachments; be very careful. The soul splits into fractals, and the predominant one you chose from your higher self incarnates. The false light, she was told, has a buzzing background sound; the true portal is usually marked by beings who wait for you, family or someone you know. In the ascension protocol, a portal pulls souls up like a magnet; only very low-vibration souls can't match it.
How do prana and water particles in the air work?
Condensed, the universe is water: you're "swimming" though you think you're breathing, because particles are spread vastly apart. Frequency moves through a conductive sequence: to mobilize a trinity structure you need a magnetic field and an electric field of conductivity, the water molecules between. Density comes from friction and spin of subatomic particles; colliding particles create a third (1+1=3=1), so one region can be a dying star and another a living Earth. Every particle mimics the whole universe; that's why the body is mostly water: conductivity.
Is a particular date in October significant?
For about 3 to 30% of the population it brings an amplification of the field: extra knowledge within their sequences, especially around the full moon. Not war or catastrophe.
Some leave the body at will and return. Can everyone?
Everyone can; practice lucid dreaming. What's missing is consistent practice: lucid dreaming, automatic writing and daily acts for others, holding your sovereignty: be nice but strong, and end manipulation as soon as you see it.
How do I match my frequency to my desired reality?
That's her whole material: start with Jumpstart for Beginners, especially "Protocol to Manifest Potency" (S4 E9), and the galactic meditation for at least 15 minutes a day. Every night for three months, write your reality in a light notebook. The mind projects a hologram; the heart, a spinning toroidal field like a star, amplifies the emotion's frequency; action mobilizes it into matter.
How can I change from sick to healthy?
She can't answer medical questions. Everything is possible, but not by prayer alone: take action, look for the proper medical doctors and solutions, and do everything under medical supervision; distrust anyone who says meditation alone will do it.
If you don't give birth, how do you pass your knowledge on?
Everyone you see, including neighbors' children, is another version of you that your consciousness projected. If you chose not to raise children directly, you can give to children in need: adopt, visit, mentor, join a group, change the lives of a few. Treat others' children as your own; what you project returns, perhaps much later. Physically helping is far more potent than only sending money.
I fluctuate quickly in emotional moments and get frustrated. What helps?
You've overloaded your field. Follow the method above. Each situation is individual: with rudeness or abuse, you may need to remove yourself permanently; if a job demands that you give up your sovereignty, find other work while you keep applying. It will keep returning until cleared; it took her four years with some patterns. Play the game: when she found a drugged young woman at a club, she stayed, refused to leave her with a stranger, and kept asking "what else can I do?" until a stranger with a tricycle helped them reach her hotel. Her guides called it a test of how she'd react under distress.
