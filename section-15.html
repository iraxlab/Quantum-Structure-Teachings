Season 1 · Section 15

Shifting From The Parallel Realities By Your Choosing

Why freedom doesn't have to come through money, how everyone channels, what to do with someone else's version of the truth, and questions on cleansing (live Q&A)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

Money is neither good nor bad; what matters is how you use it. But believing that freedom can only come through money locks you into one narrow, conditioned timeline. Feel that you already have everything, the life itself and not the money, and let the structure choose the route. Everyone is always channelling: you receive the "stations" you match, and living from the heart raises you to higher ones. And you don't have to take anyone's truth as yours: neutralize it with thanks, don't condemn, and switch the channel.

#Key teachings

#15.1Freedom isn't only money

Question: I've studied the markets and I enjoy trading, though I believe money shouldn't need to exist. My goal is freedom: time to grow and to live on my own terms, not by the clock of a normal job. Am I limiting myself?

The heart vibration and the money vibration are completely different timelines (see 4.8). Money runs on conditions, so it is denser and has limits; the heart has no condition, so it opens infinite probabilities. Money itself is not negative or positive; it is how you use it. You could earn a great deal and put it to work for humanity, and if it is truly from your heart, nothing is wrong with it.

The illusion is the belief that the only way to freedom is a lot of money. That gives power to a piece of paper and blocks the rest of the structure. In the structure you always have freedom; how it comes is infinite, perhaps money, perhaps an inheritance, perhaps something you would never guess. It doesn't matter whether you have $1 or $1 million: using this knowledge, what you need comes when you need it. So ask honestly whether the want comes from ego, and don't make "a lot of money" your permanent goal, or you lock yourself into a timeline controlled by money.

The practice, from the very first teaching: don't imagine having a lot of money; bypass it. Imagine already having what you want: the holiday, the home, the person beside you, the free time. Feel the happiness of having it now, and let the structure decide the shape and route. Then play the hide-and-seek game: you know you have it, even though you don't see it yet, and you keep projecting it until it crystallizes (see 1.7). Our three-dimensional thinking can't work out how; consciousness is infinite.

From the booksAbundance comes "through any form, not only money" (SD, NT; see 4.2).

#15.2You are always channelling

Question: how do people channel through the quantum structure?

You are always channelling. Even the question that came into your mind was information you drew from the field, because you are the field. Realities run on different vibrational levels, all at the same time, a reality within a reality. It is like radio stations: you can tune into channel three, but if you don't know the frequency for channel five, you'll never find it. What you can receive depends on what you match.

As you raise your consciousness and act from the heart, you move up, like a ladder, into less dense, faster levels. Stay in dense thinking and behaviour, and that information isn't available to you, just as believing that everything must come through money hides all the other routes (see 15.1).

From the booksThe teachings are said to open "the synoptical channels in your brain to become familiar with the quantum structures" (SD).

#15.3Why some people channel easily and others need a trance

Question: why can some people access information instantly, while others need a trance or deep meditation?

First, agreement: some came with an agreement to do this. Second, sticking with it: even with an agreement, the teacher had to practise, starting with automatic writing as a child, and pass many lessons. Everyone came with this knowledge; it is your choice whether to tap into it. When your guidance sees you passing your lessons and going into the heart, they decide to interact more. Not everyone sticks with it, because the transformation is hard, and someone who isn't consciously ready can't be connected with yet. Guidance can show you what to do, but nobody makes you do it (see 5.7, 6.5).

#15.4Cleansing questions: juices, distilled water, baking soda

Several questions were about the body. The answers given, reported here as the talk's own advice and claims:

• Fruit-only versus vegetable juices: the advice was to combine both, because each has different properties, and to prefer juicing (with green vegetables) over blended shakes, as more potent; bitter greens were described as part of the cleansing. It also said a fruit-only cleanse might feed a fungal problem; that is the talk's claim.

• Distilled water: the talk repeats its preference for distilled water and says you can make your own by evaporating water in a stainless-steel pot (see 12.7).

• Baking soda: the talk suggests aluminium-free baking soda for washing externally after using the bathroom or after intimacy, and for haemorrhoids, and mentions once taking a teaspoon in water for an upset stomach. It also claims this kills cancer cells; that claim is not supported by medical evidence.

From the booksA raw diet with fresh vegetable and fruit juices is described as raising your frequency (HR).
Important health note

These are reported as the talk's advice, not medical guidance. Talk to a doctor before any detox, fast or cleanse, especially with a health condition or medication. Don't use baking soda as a treatment, and don't drink it regularly or in large amounts (it can be harmful, especially with heart, kidney or blood-pressure problems). For haemorrhoids, infections, persistent stomach problems, or any concern about cancer, see a doctor.

#15.5Someone else's truth doesn't have to be yours

The session closes with a question about information that seems valid but feels heavy or lower. In short: everyone downloads from the field according to their own vibration, so their truth is true from their point of view; but the moment you accept something as 100% true, you start creating from it. Neutralize it with thanks, don't condemn the person, and switch the channel. This answer was also released as its own video, so it is taught in full in Section 16.

#The method — Open the Whole Field

For money worries, or for heavy information you've taken in:

  1. Notice the narrow belief: "Only money can give me this," or "what they said must be true."
  2. Pull out and say thank you to the situation or the person. Don't condemn it.
  3. Imagine the life, not the money: the freedom, the home, the holiday, the time, as yours now.
  4. Feel it until you're happy now, as if it has already happened.
  5. Switch the channel: hold the positive version, and let the structure decide how it comes.
  6. Keep doing your work and keep playing hide-and-seek: you know you have it, so keep projecting it.

#Questions answered

Will I limit myself by conditioning things through money? I trade the markets because I enjoy the game and want freedom from a normal job.
Money isn't negative or positive, and if trading truly comes from your heart, nothing is wrong with it; you might even use what you earn for humanity. The limit is the belief that freedom can only come through a lot of money: that locks you into one conditioned timeline and blocks every other route. Keep doing what you do, but imagine the freedom itself, not the money, feel that you have it now, and let the structure decide whether it comes as money, an inheritance or something unexpected. Check honestly whether the want comes from ego (see 15.1).
Can you describe how people channel through the quantum structure?
You are always channelling: even your question was drawn from the field, because you are the field. Realities run at different vibrational levels, like radio stations, and you receive the ones you match. Living from the heart raises you to higher, less dense levels; staying in dense behaviour keeps that information out of reach (see 15.2).
Why do some people get information instantly, while others need a trance or meditation?
Partly agreement, partly sticking with it. Everyone came with the knowledge, but tapping into it takes practice, passing lessons and going into the heart; as you do, your guidance interacts more. Many people don't stick with it because the transformation is hard. Nobody makes you do it; it's a choice (see 15.3).
I only have a blender, not a juicer, and blended vegetables taste bad. Can I detox with fruit only?
The talk's advice was to combine fruit and vegetables rather than fruit alone, and it preferred juicing to blending as more potent. It also said bitter greens help the cleanse, and that a fruit-only cleanse might feed a fungal problem, which is its claim. Before any detox or fast, talk to a doctor, especially if you have a health condition or take medication (see 15.4).
Does baking soda help with detoxing? Should I drink it?
The talk said to apply aluminium-free baking soda externally (after the bathroom, after intimacy, or for haemorrhoids), not to drink it, though it mentioned once taking a teaspoon in water for an upset stomach. Its claim that this kills cancer cells is not supported by medical evidence. Don't use it as a treatment, and see a doctor for any medical problem (see 15.4 and its health note).
Some people share information that seems valid, but their tone feels heavy and lower. What do you recommend?
Their truth is true from their own channel, but it doesn't have to be yours. Thank it, don't condemn the person, and switch the channel by overriding it with the positive. The full answer is in Section 16.
