The big idea

The one particle moves so fast that it looks still, printing itself next to itself endlessly, and every print is a whole universe at its own location (latitude and altitude) in the quantum field. Restoration is how you jump from one hologram to another: change your sound equilibrium, the frequency of your emotion, thoughts and deeds, and the angle shifts by a few degrees into a different reality. The correction comes from the heart: heart-felt thoughts are the precise angle that connects you to 5D and above, while separating from the heart builds synthetic, fear-controlled timelines. Because every moment is a new print, there is no past: old pain is only a vibration you keep carrying. Stop it, thank it, transform it, and it no longer affects you.

#Key teachings

#3.1Every particle is a universe, at its own location

A recap of part 1 (see S2 2.5): sound creates a whirlpool, like a wormhole, that moves back and forth like an elastic and keeps reprinting itself in the hologram; that is why everything repeats and looks the same wherever you cut into it. Each particle becomes a holographic universe for itself, holding infinite realities in every direction, all through 360 degrees.

Each one has a location, like X and Y: latitude and altitude (see S1 5.3). How the whirlpool twists gives an angle of geometry, and that angle shows which parallel reality you are printing and moving into. It is always moving, so even each parallel reality is at a different latitude and altitude the next moment, and the next. It never stops: infinite imprints, constantly merging with each other, so you keep shifting into another hologram, without end.

#3.2There is no past and no future

So when people say they "went into the past", they didn't. They created another holographic reality, at another latitude and altitude, in which they could reprint something they imagined. It is never the past and never the future: you create and shift in the now. Everything is now. What happened "before" has nothing to do with what is happening right now.

If you have accumulated energy you want to clear, you can always clear it: pull out of the hologram and build a brand-new one in the next moment (see S1 1, Method; S1 2.1). Pulling out changes your latitude and altitude. Whether the new reality is positive or negative depends on your emotion, because emotion is the frequency vibration (see S1 1.6).

The download: restoration happens when you need to jump from one sonic cymatic hologram to another. You do it by changing the sound equilibrium, which means changing the frequency of your emotion, thoughts and deeds, to a slight angle in the matrix, at a new latitude and altitude, usually measured in degrees. Every degree of angle is a different sonic cymatic whirlpool, a different reality hologram.

The correction happens when you know the angular frequency, and that is the sound created from the heart connection thoughts. Heart-felt thoughts, listening to the heart's love, are the precise angle that connects you to the higher realms. The teaching here points to the fifth-dimensional structure: the heart is 5D and above, where benevolent extraterrestrials, celestial beings and every heart-based structure vibrate, at a completely different frequency from dense 3D.

From the booksSD and DP print this download: "RESTORATION happens when you need a jump from one hologram sonic cymatic sound Hz to another when you change the sound equilibrium Hz to a slight angle into the matrix (X, Y) Latitude and altitude… Every degree of angular expression has different sound sonic cymatic whirlpool reality holograms. Correction happens when you know the angular Hz and that is the sound created from the heart connection thoughts."

#3.6Why old pain keeps returning, and how to end it

The one particle vibrates so fast that it seems to stand still, printing itself next to itself endlessly (see S1 1.1). Each print is a parallel reality and a whole universe. Say that in one print, which could be a whole 3D lifetime, you had a negative experience. The next print is a brand-new reality, and the negative experience doesn't have to come with it.

If you are still upset about something someone did long ago, ask: which reality are you talking about? That was a different hologram, with another version of you; the two prints have nothing to do with each other. It only follows you because you carry its memory vibration (we carry some vibration from print to print so that we can have an experience at all).

So stop it, transform it, move on (see S1 7.1): give it gratitude, whatever happened ("I'm moving forward"), and you neutralize it. Its vibration stops coming into the new print, you stop looping, and the imprint in your record changes; it still exists somewhere on the platform, but it no longer affects you. If you don't let go, you carry it into every next moment and keep meeting it, until you shut it down (see S1 14.5).

#The method — Restore to the Heart Angle

When you want to leave a reality, or an old hurt, behind:

  1. Notice your emotion. It is your frequency, and it shows where you are.
  2. Pull out of the hologram. Go to zero point: "This is one print, not all of me."
  3. Ask: which reality is this? If it's an old hurt, it belongs to another print, another version of you.
  4. Thank it and neutralize it. "Thank you for showing me. I'm moving forward."
  5. Stop carrying it. Don't talk about it again; don't bring its vibration into the next moment.
  6. Shift the angle with your heart. Feel love and think from the heart: that is the precise angle to 5D.
  7. Choose how you see what's around you. With gratitude, not fear, again and again through the day.

#Questions answered

So the past doesn't exist; it's just a hologram of something we experienced that we keep holding on to?
Yes, and keep co-creating. Each moment is its own hologram, a new Earth. A negative experience in one print doesn't have to be in the next. If you learn to shut it down and replace it, it has nothing to do with you any more; but if you keep carrying its vibration, you keep meeting it. Stop, transform, move on (see 3.6).
My four-year-old has type 1 diabetes, so we can't do a detox or a strict diet. Can blessing our food make up for it?
The answer was that blessing alone is not enough: you have to take action. Pray if you wish, but we came here to experience, and you can't reject the experience; go through it with the best of your abilities. It urged the parent to work with professional doctors and nutritionists, and said this is the right age to start. It added that many new children react strongly to processed food, and pointed to gut health (gut flora, probiotics) as very important. It also claimed that this kind of diabetes can be fixed through diet under supervision, and urged the parent to call the clinic now, before the body deteriorates more: the Tree of Life clinic in Arizona, from the video "Reversing Diabetic in 30 Days" ("most people don't know about this clinic"). It raised doubts about vaccines while saying it is not a doctor and the parent must decide carefully.
Added health note

Type 1 diabetes cannot be reversed or cured by diet. A child with type 1 diabetes needs insulin every day for life, and stopping or reducing insulin can quickly become life-threatening. Make every change to diet or treatment with the child's diabetes team. The talk's claims linking vaccines to diabetes and other illnesses are not supported by evidence; for children with diabetes, doctors usually advise keeping vaccinations up to date. Please decide with your paediatrician.

I have a mental-health diagnosis. I've been off my medication for a year and a half, but I've been suicidal the whole time, and I can't seem to control my thoughts and emotions. Should I go back on medication?
The answer stressed that it is not a doctor and could not tell anyone what to do. It suggested looking into homeopathic doctors and doing research; said that pharmaceuticals can be needed when someone is very ill, while also believing the body should be helped to heal itself; and voiced its own doubts about electric-shock therapy, saying it would not choose it for itself.
If you are having thoughts of suicide

Please don't go through this alone, and please don't rely on this book or any talk for this decision. Talk to your doctor or psychiatrist about medication; never stop or avoid treatment without them. Treatments such as medication and ECT are established and can save lives; your doctor can explain the benefits and risks for you. If you are in danger now, call your local emergency number. In the US you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline); elsewhere, contact your local crisis line. Reaching out is not a failure of these teachings; it is taking action, which the teachings themselves ask for.

So déjà vu is a memory of a timeline that you are creating at the same time: you already exist in it while you are creating it. Both at once: that is the quantum.
