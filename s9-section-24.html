The big idea

When she worried about the world, her guides said: stop. You forget that your consciousness entered 3D and created this existence by its own frequency. In a story they told, a cylinder pod gives a code that projects the mind to the location where it must be born; the mind constructs a simulation that looks real and feels real. What is truly real? "A perception of beliefs." So don't fight systems: don't engage with those you've researched, localize, create communities, and take unfamiliar action, because awareness without action isn't enough. "Are you going to allow a bit of setback to determine your future?" No one can make you do anything: "They will threaten, punish. So what?"

#Key teachings

#24.1Why worry is forgetting

Anger and pain over what she saw made her worry; her guides stopped her: your consciousness was sent into the 3D platform and created this existence within Earth's hologram by its frequency, so "what do you worry about?" To hold a level above 3D, every negative situation must be subsided and replaced, moment to moment; meditation alone can't erase accumulated negative sequences.

The message: the cylinder pod (condensed; given as fiction)

"The mind is given": in a pod, a vibrational sequence measures and knows the longitude and altitude of a stargate location; that signal enters your consciousness, your brain projects it, and your consciousness is downloaded into the place where you need to be born, to the mother and father you need. "The mind is giving a signal of semantic cognitive molecular sound with fractals, and your mind constructs the longitude [and] altitude of the reality where exactly you need to be." "Your mind is constructing the simulation that looks real, feels real to you. But what is truly real? It is a perception of beliefs." Your "counter female", another version of you, can coexist in a parallel timeline; consciousness splits into such fragments.

Added note

Added note: she presents the pod as "for entertainment purposes only... a fiction"; it is not established fact.

#24.2A new hologram moment to moment

Consciousness is omnipresent and always changing position: each moment you're in a new hologram with a new location, so even a laser line is not truly straight; your mind constructs simulated realities moment to moment.

#24.3Simulation technology: help or trap

Technology will soon offer simulations people will want to experience, for rest or to slow the frequency of trauma; but any game can carry subtonalities and hidden messages. Don't see it as negative; see it as technology. Her predictions prepare you so you aren't trapped during the five or six years of transition: research who controls a system, and decide what you'll allow into your body and consciousness.

The message: what to do (condensed, as received)

"Do not fight what is already in the field as a system... it could be used for positive, could be used [for] negative, depending who's controlling the system." If you've researched who controls it, don't engage; you have free will: plant the forest. Technology will advance; "to what level you wish to engage and what to allow into your own God-given system is by free will." Asked about digital money control: "Oh, money... Do you really need the money? When is your society [going to] learn to live freely?" Asked about being bulldozed by powerful interests: "You actually gave a piece of paper and a pen to control you... Wise up. Create local courts, communal [structures], local communal banks that are proven, and private exchange. Localize it." "To leave one's comfort zone is proving very challenging." "The mind has to be clear, and the persona has to stop its own ideology and allow room for possibilities." "Fear of the unknown causes discomfort... and the human... chooses the familiar pattern even if that pattern causes them pain." "How many of you wish to learn something new every day?"

Added note

#24.4Comfort keeps you in pain

When comfortable, people don't want to do the work, yet want to manifest a new reality; physical action, consistently, is the glue. If you don't act to shift anything for humanity, what you ignore eventually comes to your doorstep, because the negative must do more and more to hold its position as consciousness moves. Start small: join a garden club, knock on a gardening neighbour's door and ask for help.

#24.5Don't let a setback decide your future

When she complained that every time she tried to exercise something went wrong (vertigo), her guides said: "So? It happens. Push through it and move on... Are you going to allow a bit of setback to determine your future?" Complaining reprints the negative until it becomes potent and repeats; people fall into depression this way. Awareness "is the first part... but the awareness is not enough if you do not take a new, unfamiliar action of perseverance and dedication." Then your coherent field pulls you like a magnet to higher frequencies. "You know what to do, so do it." Even if the body recoils, it is rebooting.

Added safety note

Added safety note: dizziness, vertigo or a struggling body need medical assessment; she says her juicing protocol requires medical supervision and blood work, and is her own experience, not advice.

"Reboot evolution, reset from subtones in levels of sequences in mega silica omniton particles fusion." "Level two is contact to self-replica... level three: mimic the new evolution code and the new human rebirth occurs. 2030 is the new era... the time is syncing with God's fractal. Enigma gene on."

Added note

#The method — When a setback or worry grips you (as given)

  1. Hear the word "Stop": pull out of the hologram you're in.
  2. Ask: "What do I see? What do I hear?": that's your reality.
  3. Reach the zero point: the anger or worry subsides.
  4. Choose an unfamiliar action of perseverance, not the familiar comfort.
  5. Do something you love right now, to convert the negative.
  6. Repeat, in different ways, until it crystallizes.

#Questions answered

Is the Zeon part of hidden plasma elements and technology?
She never gave a date for it. In the HR, the Zeon is the zero point where everything combines: true neutrality, awareness, where nothing comes in or out. From there you place new sequences and shift into a new field. You are always co-creating, reprinting everyone within your closed system moment to moment.
Is there anything about the particle collider we need to know?
See 9.21. What they attempt there won't complete. Systems are being built for total control, and some will consent from fear, but it won't finish as planned because aware people will do something else. Research lab-grown food yourself, go local and produce your own.
Are we all in pods, projecting our consciousness into 3D?
Most, depending on the level you come from. In sessions, she has projected (like remote viewing) and later had it confirmed by a person's dreams; not everyone can do this.
It sounds like we need to get back to the fundamentals.
Exactly. Knowing much, bombarded with information and personal situations, the path becomes lonely, because you need pure frequency to move on. When she was fuming over a disaster she believed was deliberate, she heard: "Stop... What do you see?" Trees. "What do you hear?" Birds and crickets. "This is your reality." Watch, listen and read until you grasp it, reach the zero point, then take a new action. She says "stop" firmly on purpose: it pulls you out of the negative hologram. Prayer is the field; action crystallizes it. A sharp word once made her quit a habit instantly: "Are you going to allow this to control you, destroy you?" That is the Zeon moment.
