The big idea

Children's minds are pure and sponge-like, so they are the first target: sub-frames and sub-tonalities in films and music can programme the subconscious, and you are responsible for what they are exposed to. Exposure of hidden abuses is coming, starting with what was done to children, which will wake even the sleeping. Raise children by example, correct disrespect at once, and stand united as parents.

#Key teachings

#30.1Subliminal programming

Film runs frame after frame; at speed it looks continuous, as reality does hologram after hologram. A hidden frame (she calls it the "13th frame") is missed by the eye but caught by the subconscious, which works at a different speed, especially in children. Music can carry hidden sub-tonalities, and colours and geometry add to it. Two hours of that, then the school system, and a child resonates with the negative platform without anyone knowing why. Watch what your children watch. Her guides also emphasized that male and female, yin and yang, are a strong balance: don't let media teach children that either isn't needed.

Her codes: The new stargate sequence

R11371, 11371, 11371, repeated in three sub-tonalities, in the stargate 173 (Pleiadian installation), stargate 13313. Through individual permission, those resonating with it will shift easily through the portal, and life will begin to get easier, while still seeing what happens.

The message: pardoned, but not in the soul (condensed)

Exposure "will start with the children... when you find out the truth, what your kids was used for... anyone that is asleep will wake up." Medical insiders will reveal what they witnessed. Some may be pardoned for revealing the truth, but "in your soul [you] can never be pardoned": what you accumulate knowingly through fear stays in your oversoul until a similar hologram tests you and you don't consent. Some consciousness is so dense it returns to the void and to source, starting again from zero.

#30.2What a guide must be

Spirit guides and guardian angels are different groups. Your guide usually shared similar conditions with you in parallel realities. You can't be a master or teacher unless you completed the same test yourself: being in service to another being is the highest honour in consciousness.

#30.3Confusion as a weapon

When everything right is called wrong and vice versa, your consciousness is neutralized into stillness and you don't know whom to trust, the same principle as neutralizing a frequency with its opposite. Question carefully who people are, including in the truth community; be wary of anyone who pushes only negatives and won't listen to solutions.

#Questions answered

How do we influence children positively or redirect them from subliminal messages, if they already show negative signs?
Pull them away from platforms that carry such material. Teach them to be ladies and gentlemen, to respect and give; don't feed entitlement: let them earn. Stop disrespect at that moment with a stern, calm explanation; ignored, it accumulates until they no longer respect you. Parents must be united, not one good and one bad. Teach by example; follow their natural interests (painting, nature, science). Every child chose its parents, adoptive and same-sex couples included: love is the same. There's no excuse of tiredness: ask and you will find solutions (another school, home schooling, small groups).
