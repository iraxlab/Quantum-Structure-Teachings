The big idea

Consciousness reflects itself: to express, it becomes duality (you and your reflection), and the two always collapse back into one unit. Sound plus magnetism (positive and negative) plus that collapse equals the zero point: stillness, the void, "the most power energy, the God-consciousness power within you." You reach it by acceptance: knowing everything in your field is there for a reason, without rejecting the negative, "no hatred, just understanding", then acting. Reject it, and you reprint your rejection. "Zero point equals acceptance equals stillness equals action."

#Key teachings

#11.1Duality, and back to one

The Council repeated (see S5 10): consciousness and its self-reflection are fractal, singular and collective; "it becomes duality when it chooses to express itself... This mutation is infinite." When you put thought and emotion into the field, it flickers and reprints a new Earth of similar vibration, or, if you drop, a different outcome. 1 + 1 = 3, "and equals one again": each collapse is one structure, one molecule, one universe in the now, stacked on top of each other. The reprint follows the light's curvature (see S4 47), each at a new latitude and altitude.

#11.2How the zero point forms

"The sound, plus the magnetism, which is negative and positive in the now unit... plus the collapse of this structure, 1 + 1 = 3, equals [the] zero point of a stillness. [It] becomes the void, becomes knowingness in the now." Holding all of it in one unit through understanding, you hold "the power and the potency of consciousness itself": then it becomes a free-energy field. Two structures spin in opposite directions, one in, one out, like HR's two pyramids twisting opposite ways, collapsing into one. In the small, this makes the double helix of DNA, and a third, etheric strand: the combined sound of the two (see HR's heart sound and its undertone, S4 45).

Sound is the core. "Sound, speed equals potency, power, energy, focus, consistency and repetition." "Include the magnetism, light, sound, the speed, and you will also learn position in space-time" (a lead for scientists).

#The method — Acceptance, Stillness, Action

  1. Recognize: "I'm in a game. This is the structure of the game."
  2. Accept: it exists for a reason; no hatred, no rejection; understanding.
  3. Be still: observe, analyze; if you're in the lion's den, "you don't roar in front of the lion": retreat quietly.
  4. Calm down: neutrality; listen to something that lifts you.
  5. Act: choose what to engage in, by the protocol; if they hit 10 times, transform 20.
  6. Repeat moment to moment, daily (S3 8, S4 38), until the potency shifts you.

#Questions answered

Should we meet the negative with acceptance, stillness and action, even with corrupt parties involved?
"Does it matter?" You project your structure: hold the vibration knowing that it's okay, and collapse everything from the positive. Being "brought to your knees" means seeing the dark, not extinction. If you're in a negative place, retreat, stay still and aware, then act and move away. "Do not confuse the indulgence with weakness."
How long is a unit?
It's in the now. A thought immediately makes a blueprint, its own timeline; you couldn't think it unless its vibration already existed, yet you must create it now to see it. Collapse it with action, thought and emotion into matter. With coherent, consistent frequency, time shrinks; sometimes it's instant (see S4 36).
What does "30 units" mean?
About 30 reflections or collapses of what the negative is doing: then so much negative potency that it self-combusts, because consciousness keeps sending holograms to collapse it. "We already won."
