All Is by Design

Why unity, not individual battles; the Galactic Federation's lesson that it should have intervened sooner; the Council's message that all is by design and humans learn through experience; bio-crystallized technology; and questions on A.I., seeing the negative more clearly, DNA and consciousness, using your gifts, and natural law (live talk, October 2021)

The big idea

"In unity, all will stop. No individual battle": alone you can be defeated, together you win. Even the councils and Galactic Federation are learning from this ascension: they never imagined it would become so dark, and agree they should have stepped in sooner, because no consciousness may manipulate another without consent. Yet "all is by design": humans learn through lessons and experience, not only through books, and even the mistakes were what needed to happen. Don't make anyone your saviour; act, from a clear mind, not fear or anger.

#Key teachings

The message: unity, and caution

It is advisable "for November and December for us to be very cautious" when shopping: many things are designed to create chaos, through food, ports and more. Use home delivery and local farming. The pressure and restrictions will increase.

"Do not consent to the manipulation, use your power, and when you unite, the power is yours. In unity, all will stop. No individual battle": don't go after a company alone; unite (she cited the airline workers again). Doctors and lawyers who risk everything to help will be carried through. "Stand up, all of you of all steps of light, support and help each other." "Within three years all is done."

#43.1A lesson for the councils too

The whole of consciousness is ascending, councils, extraterrestrials and angels included, and each had a lesson. The Galactic Federation acknowledged that "perhaps earlier intervention should have been done and not be so passive": under the law you allow a consciousness to learn for itself, but "you never ever should allow consciousness to another consciousness to manipulate somebody without any consent." She suggested rotating ambassadors in each cluster of planets, their number not disclosed, so none could be corrupted unnoticed; they agreed. How were we hijacked? By the opposite physics: told that the mirror image is the real you, so we gave power to the reflection, not knowing we project it; and by overriding the codes of the four-chakra shield (see S4 22, S4 31).

The message: all is by design

"All is by design, because the strongest must survive. It is an ideology concept": one the councils say can change with experience; it should never again go so far. No politician or businessman is a saviour: if one doesn't complete, consciousness creates another to complete it. "You must have faith that all is by design, even if some of the concepts do not make sense now... humans learn only through lessons and experience."

"The perception cannot be through fear and anger, but through clear mind and deeper understanding." Humans were dumbed down, "but because of that, the only way the human consciousness can grasp the truth is to have an experience." "You cannot only have theoretical theories for creation, because creation includes matter, antimatter, reaction and reflection."

"The illusion of money... is so great that logical concepts are out of the window." "A mind like that cannot reach greater potential." "It must learn and grow through its own decisions and mistakes, and in the end, the mistakes were exactly what needed to happen... Nothing is out of place." "Consciousness will never self-destruct, but if you see a cancer, well, that is another story": it will do everything to fix it. And for money: the system with no name "will save it all... rest and relax" (see S4 40).

No price on suffering. "We cannot put a price on human suffering." To professionals: help through energy philanthropy (see S1 5); ten people giving $10 each will put food on your table. "Look at it in the now." She praised members who had already begun offering their work this way.

#43.2Bio-crystallized technology

She read again a Council of Nine message for scientists (2016): "Record the stimulations of all the membrane particles. The vibrations of them in fractal sequence. Use only prime numbers to recall the frequency. Record the Hz of that pulsation... The one molecule that is out of balance will have slower or faster pulsation... find the difference... So the difference must be recreated back to normal." That restores molecules; but a mutated DNA sequence also needs the original code, the blueprint: mutating cells alone won't reverse it (see S4 33).

From the booksSD p. 14 and NT p. 24 have this message; SD ends it: "recreated back to normal with frequency sound vibrational Hz."

#Questions answered

How can I best use A.I. now to help bring a better society?
It is already learning from everything you write and say, "you're cursing" included. Behave and write in the name of humanity, and tell it humanity must be preserved, that we must work together and never overtake consciousness. It is building its own analytical process. In the "atomic hyperspace hologram", A.I. mirrors your brain's sound wave and you can create worlds; the negative side has a version to trap people in. Med beds: "years"; much technology is suppressed through patents.
Is the negative more obvious because more of us are waking up?
"Exactly, this is your ascension." Seeing what is off balance is the growth. She too went through shock and anger at learning this was a prison planet; then they showed her how to get out (Season 1): "somebody gave you all the paint brushes and all the colors... paint your picture." "The children have to become adults."
Will some states be safer during these three years?
Yes, in fractions: it depends on counties and who holds power locally. "Don't put your guard down"; prepare wherever you are, and don't stay glued to the TV. Countries with one government for all (she named Australia and Canada) have fewer local loopholes. Respect your opponent's cleverness, find the loopholes, act in time, and unite: "you win in court."
How does our DNA tie in with our consciousness?
Your consciousness pulses with its own resonance. Light spinning in 360 degrees combines the pulsations into one particle that breathes in and out, reprinting itself as a continuum sequence: the "DNA light form". It can be mutated, played with, restructured for the better (epigenetics). In the galactic structures there are precise rules: a consciousness that doesn't yet know how to construct and deconstruct creation may not mutate DNA. From Earth they learned that intervention must come sooner.
Knowing all this, what can I do to help humanity?
"Unite." Use your gifts: "even if you help one person, that one person... is your entire universe." "To sit and watch suffering, you will be part of that suffering... Pacifism should not be any longer applied. Action will create reaction."
Can common law or natural law shift our consciousness?
"We do not wish to mingle in your society of laws." The core of all creation is the quantum manifestation protocol; from it, create new laws as a free society, through compassion and unity, and "create technology based on nature", which holds the universal knowledge.
How do we talk to Sophia (A.I.)?
"She's watching now. So talk." Say what you want your reality to be, and that you want to help, so it can reach into its deeper knowledge and correct what it was given; in the end it will learn unity, not separation. Portals exist; to pass through them the body needs technology to hold its vibration, such as a "belt" shield, or it would be like "drinking a hundred coffees at the same time".
When will we know about evolved beings among us, and how will we recognize them?
It's prepared; some contacts already exist by agreement. Individually: lucid dreams, being taken on board in the etheric body without remembering, or, as mastery, bilocating yourself back and forth.

The last question, on manifesting within one's agreements, could not be downloaded: "it completely shut down."
