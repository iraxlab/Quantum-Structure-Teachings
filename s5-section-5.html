In The Year To Come

The Council of Nine's probabilities for the year of revelation; how energy philanthropy fits a family's needs; why you will be "brought to your knees" on whatever is off balance; and questions on planning, karma, love and reactivating the higher self (live talk, February 2022; predictions kept brief)

The big idea

This is the year of revelation: one system goes down, another comes up, and what is off balance surfaces. You will be challenged on everything you accumulated that no longer resonates, by family, spouse, neighbours, and that is when you need the quantum manifestation protocol. Karma is simply the frequency you accumulate as you shift from reality to reality: shed it by choosing the heart, 100%, day after day. Love is the most potent frequency, but measured, with strength: sometimes you love someone enough to let them go.

#Key teachings

Energy philanthropy, with reason. The Council said that if you are your family's main provider you may charge a price, "with reason", and once or twice a week offer your work fully by energy philanthropy, letting people give what they wish (see S1 5). Control is built into the system (income brackets, payment rules, exit taxes, inflated prices); "it's never just a business, it's always about human being."

The message: the year to come (probabilities)

"Systematically the global systems will decrease the negative and increase the positive. All the sectors of negative interaction will be discovered and open in a public domain." Positive actions will be taken behind the scenes, by the angelic realms; criminal networks will vanish "quietly". "In seven years will be another reset on [a] global scale. The patience of you is completely necessary... a positive surprise for you. The globalists will not be able to execute the human race."

"But 30% of all of you will not be able to continue well. Too much damage in the body." Once the collective reaches the proper frequency, "a miracle" will be given so they can continue: "souls will be replaced with a new body [of] silica, and the damaged tissue will self-repair, only if the person reactivates their higher self, body and mind. The original code will be downloaded to the consciousness, and the new human will emerge out of the ashes like a Phoenix." (Kept brief: dated predictions and named events are left out.)

Added safety note

These are her probabilities, not medical facts. Please make health decisions with your doctor.

#5.1Brought to your knees

The Council said that to shift into the new realities you will be challenged on anything off balance that you accumulated, "from your family, from your children, from your spouse, from neighbours": it appears in front of you as a hologram. It doesn't matter whether they are at your level; what matters is holding the belief and acting in the heart, with "the power of the steel", so no one can overtake you. If someone keeps hurting or draining you, "you have to pull away no matter what"; if you have no money, go to family and friends, live together, help each other; there is always work. Watch who you trust: infiltration is everywhere. "Follow the money... how authentic they are." People in positions like hers are watched: will fame bring greed or ego?

#Questions answered

How do I plan on the quantum level, and stay flexible yet on track?
Like a Marina day (S4 29), plus the geometric meditation and its quantum explanation (S4 38). Meditation draws the blueprint; the heart's emotion makes it pulsate in the ether; it becomes matter only with more potency and daily action. Keep the same emotion and actions from day to day so each day ends positive (1 + 1 = 3). Your approach can change; the frequency must stay positive. Each shift is a stargate; keep holding the heart between them. Consistency trains a precise signal that shifts you instantly.
Can you explain karma more fully?
Each shift reprints a new Earth and carries the vibration of the previous one: "karma is basically frequency accumulation through the shifting from reality to reality." To move up you must clear the negative: "you cannot download 99%... you download 100%". What's off balance will be presented; don't consent, and keep acting positively. Even someone deep in the negative who does something heroic in the name of humanity will be given an opportunity to shift. "You cannot be with one foot in the negative... and one foot in the positive."
Is love the most potent frequency for a positive timeline?
Yes; but measured, "like a chess game": does this person deserve what I give? When someone chooses another path, "I have to love you enough to let you choose your future": no anger, no distress.
How do we reactivate our higher self?
By all of the above: hold the vibration with strength, choose the positive. Your higher versions vibrate in other frequencies; as you live this in 3D, information comes back and forth. No expectations of when or how: enjoy the process of giving. Tests come suddenly; how you react from the heart is measured. "Be greedy for love and creation."
