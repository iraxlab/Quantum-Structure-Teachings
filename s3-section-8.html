The 9-Day Quantum Protocol from the Council of Nine

A nine-day practice of switching from negative to positive at shorter and shorter intervals, why it copies the quantum structure, what to stop feeding during it, and questions on the third DNA, out-of-body travel, discernment and soul agreements (live talk)

The big idea

The council gave a concrete, collective practice: for nine days, set your phone to remind you to check where your attention is and switch it from negative to positive, every three hours for days 1–3, every two hours for days 4–6 and every hour for days 7–9. Each check is a small version of the manifestation meditation, so the shrinking intervals raise the vibration fractally until the positive hologram is potent enough to crystallize. During the nine days, feed nothing negative: no political or crisis media, no violent films. Done together, it builds a collective momentum.

#Key teachings

#8.1The third reality is built by your attention

3D and 5D vibrate at completely different frequencies, and you fluctuate between them. The third structure that forms between them (one plus one equals three, see S1 2.2) is shaped by how many times you put your attention on the negative or the positive. That is why repetition matters so much now: the teacher learned the structure by constant repetition, often reluctantly, before she fully understood it.

#8.2The 9-day protocol

Start at the same time each morning, so the frequency hologram builds in one steady rhythm, and set the phone: "don't think you're going to remember. You will forget, I promise you." At each reminder, ask: where is my attention? If it's on doubt, complaint or fear, neutralize it at zero point and switch: give gratitude (for your home, your children, being safe) and feel it as joy, then take a positive action. If you're in a bad mood, act anyway (dance, put on a good film): "the action is infusing the potency of your hologram." Thoughts, emotions and actions must be one unit.

#8.3Why it works: a copy of the quantum structure

The protocol mimics the manifestation meditation (see S2 16): picture it, add the feeling, and keep increasing the feeling until the resonance is very potent. The nine days do the same in daily life, three stages that each push higher, so the blueprint you want becomes "more potent and more potent" until it can crystallize into matter. That meditation is a template: replace the galactic family with anything you want to create.

#8.4What not to feed

For the nine days: no social media about politics or the crisis, and no films or games full of killing and war, which "crystallize a certain frequency in your body" and become part of your hologram. Choose cooking shows, dancing, sport, gentle period films. Even those who expose wrongdoing, if they stay on it constantly, keep projecting that timeline and never see the positive side. Expose once, so people can choose freely, "not by convincing them that you're right", and then transform it.

#8.5Fear feeds a dense hologram

A consciousness that can't rise above a dense resonance needs that resonance held in place, and fear is what holds it: "your body is a battery", and panic projects the frequency it needs. A collective switch to the positive does the opposite. Even an artificial timeline is still part of consciousness, and as consciousness ascends through every platform, a resonance that can't follow can no longer hold (see S3 7.9).

#8.6Unity multiplies the potency

"The potency is very powerful when it's in a unity structure": doing it together creates a collective momentum, "a massive portal into the structure of positivity". Stay in touch with others doing it, sharing only uplifting things. And it is a free choice: nothing breaks if you don't, but staying in a negative structure, even as a kind person, can mean a negative experience on the way.

#8.7Holding a home full of conflict

Keeping your frequency in a home of fighting is "probably the most difficult thing you're going to be doing", because it runs through your children and family, and you may look like the crazy one. Stay out of the fights, keep switching to the positive and taking action, "transformation, transformation, transformation": you are transforming one hologram and creating a new one. For the teacher this took about three years.

Support

If fighting at home turns to threats or violence, your safety comes first. In the US, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233 (or text START to 88788).

#The method — The 9-Day Protocol

  1. Pick a start time and keep it each morning; set phone reminders.
  2. Days 1–3: every 3 hours. Days 4–6: every 2 hours. Days 7–9: every hour.
  3. At each reminder, check: where is my attention, in thought, action and what I'm watching?
  4. Neutralize and switch: give gratitude and feel it as joy (8.2).
  5. Take a positive action, especially if your mood is low.
  6. Feed nothing negative for the nine days (8.4), and then let it go.

#Questions answered

How can you tell if the third strand of DNA is activated?
It's active in most of us now. It isn't physical: it's a frequency created between the two strands as the code changes through your actions; only a resonance device could show it, as a picture made by sound waves.
For the 3, 70 and 90 Hz water practice, is 28 minutes better?
No: 20 minutes each is the generic duration that sustains your frequency; anything more is individual (see S1 19).
Blissful energy rises up my spine, stronger at night. Am I being activated?
You were activated long ago; the frequency is increasing, as it is for many people now.
Is the galactic meditation the one to use?
Yes, as a template: see 8.3.
Does the Earth have a message for us now?
Her new name, Aria, means a new beginning, which won't allow more harm (see S3 3.8). Meditate for the planet's healing, or use the template toward her.
How do I keep my vibration high in a home with a lot of fighting?
See 8.7.
I have no access to health care. How do I stay healthy?
Take more care of yourself: diet, plant-based eating and herbalism; meat in the diet "will fade out".
I have nightmares of being hurt. Do they mean something?
Dreams are another platform with its own frequency, and they become codes in this reality: write them down, and you'll start to see what was always there (see S3 3.4).
What causes the feeling of electricity or pins and needles?
It's individual: in some people sharp pain from the spine, in others resonance in the muscles and veins that brings spasms.
Added note

Ongoing numbness, tingling or sharp pain should be checked by a doctor.

Am I making the right decisions for my path?
Turn your attention toward others: projecting positive force toward them doubles your frequency, because in the end all is you, and it dissolves the illusion of ego, leaving the pure heart.
My teenage son has insomnia and depression. Can he be balanced?
From now on, think of him as healthy even when he acts out: you project the hologram. Because the pattern is deeply embedded, the change is slow and fractal, within about a year. (A personal sound sequence was given as a one-time push.)
Added note

Please keep working with his doctor or a mental-health professional; in a crisis, call or text 988.

Strong ringing in my ears: what does it mean?
A transformation, not something negative; it's also connected with how the ear takes frequencies, and will stop soon. Medical care may not give the whole answer.
Added note

New or persistent ringing in the ears is worth checking with a doctor.

How can I have an out-of-body experience safely?
Leave the body at 90 degrees, upward, never backward or face down: it matters for the portals. Keep a steady, positive vibration, or you may loop into a reality that matches a lower signal or meet entities that aren't positive. Learn from someone experienced.
A channel claims messages from star beings online. Are they true?
Those who manipulate for fame, glory or money will find their information no longer accurate, because they don't extend above the fifth-dimensional resonance (see S3 4.2). You'll see it through experience.
What about discernment?
Every belief is true from its own latitude and altitude (see S2 15). But in the new structure, whatever is off balance is exposed in the open: you'll find the truth through synchronicities.
Should I wear a face mask?
Yes: take your precautions, because the structure needs it, and transform it without fear.
Noisy neighbours are driving me crazy at home.
Music; redirect your attention; transform the experience.
What about bad surprises when I'm in a positive vibration?
The protocol is the same at every scale: use the knowledge and transform it.
My four-year-old has sudden outbursts of anger, and hives.
He's very sensitive to waveforms around him: he needs nature. Let him let it out on walks and hikes.
I'm a doctor with misconduct on my record and an addiction. Should I stay in medicine?
"You have the strength to transform it": write, teach, turn the experience into help for others. Forgiveness heals: it transforms every cell and expands as love into new synchronicities. "Forgive yourself. You have been forgiven already."
Support

For substance use, in the US the SAMHSA National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357 (free, 24/7); in a crisis call or text 988.

Stage 5 kidney disease: can distilled water or frequencies help, or wait for a transplant?
"No, no, no: the body is too weak. You need medical help." Herbalism only after recovery, to support the tissue.
I feel a big change is coming but I'm confused which way to go.
"See your beauty, know that you are it": you are creative and manifest easily.
How can I best help people with psychosis?
Write the stories and results. It was described as a mind taking fractal realities from several platforms at once, like electrical shortages, so change is slow; deep hypnosis under supervision was suggested.
Added note

Psychosis needs psychiatric care; any other approach should be alongside it, with the treating team.

How can we help rescued children heal?
See them as beautiful; asking the question already leads you to ways to help. Any child you care for may be a soul from another lifetime with you, by agreement, so adoption is as much your own family.
Is feeling grief for humanity negative?
No, it's natural. Let it flow and cry as much as you need, until there is a point where you cry no more; settled there, transform it through understanding and positive action.
I lost my job. How do I prepare for after the shutdown?
"There is no other side. It's just now": focus on something positive now, and that creates your next momentum.
How much is predetermined by soul agreements, and how much is free will?
The over-soul splits, by agreement, to experience many realities, even several lifetimes on Earth at once. An agreement is a bylaw (who your parents are, who you may marry); how well you complete it is your free will. You can stay in "first grade" as long as you like, but you'll eventually choose something more exciting.
Where do you go when the council speaks directly?
"I'm here but not here": still present, in the background, hearing it; a collective thought comes through. Everyone can do this eventually.

Left out: participants' names, a guest's promotion, named organizations and channels, political and military claims, claims about vaccines, wireless networks and a research facility, and remarks on a region.
