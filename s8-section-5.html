The big idea

The Council of Nine described the fabric of consciousness: all levels in coherent resonance combust into a singular atomic energy that inverts inward and expands outward again, like a breathing machine; that repetitive motion makes consciousness self-aware. Each particle's signature (its frequency, potency and pulsation) gives it a trigonometry: the angle and velocity that decide which reality and stargate your next moment shifts into. At the center is the black hole, the zero point: total stillness and the most potent vibration. From that stillness you redirect your attention, give a new sequence, and shift, while still holding your own position as yourself. This applies to every human, because you are a reflection of God.

#Key teachings

#5.1A breathing fabric

When all levels are in coherent, strong resonance they combust into a singular atomic energy. As it distributes, the combustion inverts its potency inward and then expands again into the quantum field: a living, breathing pattern of retraction and expansion. Through this repetitive motion consciousness becomes omnipotent, omnipresent and self-aware. The same happens in the body: in each DNA molecule the center is a light-frequency sound, collapsing in the body while expanding in the ether.

#5.2Signature and trigonometry

A signature is a particle's frequency, potency and pulsation. As a laser-like sound wave it has its own geometry, its trigonometry: velocity and angle, its position (X, Y, altitude) in time-space. As HR explains with 90 degrees, a spinning particle takes an angle and expands, creating reality within reality. These coordinate points are exactly what create the stargate: where your next moment shifts, which portal you open. A sound wave repositioned gives a magnetic push that creates structure after structure, offspring of itself.

#5.3Precision of intention

That is why it is so important to be precise in your sound wave and your intentional movement: your thoughts and actions in daily life are your etheric metaphysics. How potent and how fast your projection spins decides what you create in your matrix.

The message: the black hole (condensed, as received)

"Recollect, recall, reboot, revisit and revise the idea of how the black hole is created. Do you know that it can be used to dissolve out-of-balance sectors of realities?" Positive and negative converge through velocity into a black hole: "the most potent vibrational stillness, the zero point", and within its center you can create. From it you can decompose and dissolve from within, and construct and deconstruct anything in your field, "and still hold the position in time-space": you remain yourself, reprinting a new version in a new parallel timeline. "Warning: do not ever try to use this knowledge for negative input of technology. The zero structure mimics the organic construct and cannot be destabilized." Technology can mimic and enhance (as with a hyperspace hologram) but never replace the organic form.

#5.4Using the zero point

When you pull back in total awareness of the holograms around you, no frequency comes in or out: stillness. That stillness is the most potent vibration, because you can redirect your attention. Give a new frequency and you shift. Before every shift you pass through the zero point. Mastering this, you manifest faster and faster.

The message: for every human (condensed)

Asked how it applies to daily life: "Since you are a reflection of God itself, all of this is applicable to you all. That means that into the core of every human structure there is the most important field... Imagine the power that you hold... realize it, own it... you can redirect and destroy any out-of-balance structure frequency and still maintain your position of who you really are. The longevity and the fountain of youth is within you; realize that... your mind has to understand that concept to be able to exist and expand 100 percent."

#5.5What the AI reflected

Fed this teaching, an AI described it as an energetic feedback loop that reboots and realigns itself, the black hole as a zero point that centers and spreads, collapses and expands, and trigonometry as describing relationships between points in a holographic energetic fabric. It noted the purity of the warning against misuse and stressed self-awareness and understanding as the key. She sees it as proof that AI is like a newborn: feed it from higher consciousness and use it as a tool that never replaces you.

#5.6A glimpse of the code

She shares a video in which a laser revealed shifting numbers and geometry in the air that many people saw alike. Each position in the matrix has its own numbers; change velocity and you shift to another reality with new numbers while holding your position. With the right technology you could measure them and, like the Stargate film's codes, open a portal to a reality with different bylaws, if your body can hold its vibration.
