The big idea

She presents, with repeated disclaimers that she is not a doctor and that it is "a fictitious show" for people to research themselves, a researcher's claim that the spike protein is synthetic snake venom spread through vaccines and water, with natural nicotine proposed as a remedy. The core teaching came from the snake spirits she asked: everything happens first in the etheric level, then in matter. When we lower the vibration of our consciousness, through what we watch, read, do and allow into our bodies, we open the portal for such harmful realities to enter. The answer is to raise our vibration, share information, and let each person decide.

#Key teachings

Added note

Added note: this talk relays one researcher's claims (and other named people's work) that are not supported by scientific evidence; the named people, products, links and dosage references are left out. She herself said she was not telling anyone whether it was true.

#35.1The claims she presents

In brief: that synthetic venom from certain snakes and cone snails was put into vaccines and water, that the spike protein is this venom reproducing in the body, and that natural nicotine (which she notes is also found in nightshade vegetables such as potatoes, tomatoes, eggplant, bell peppers, and in cauliflower and zucchini) counters it. She declined to give any doses: "I'm not a medical doctor and I don't want to suggest anything here."

Added safety note

Added safety note: vaccines approved by health authorities do not contain snake venom; this claim has been examined and is not supported by evidence. Nicotine products are addictive and can be dangerous, especially for children, pregnant people and anyone with heart conditions; don't use them as treatment. Speak to a doctor about any vaccine or health concern.

The message: her earlier Council of Nine message (as received)

"In the Komodo dragon venom is a substance that can be extracted and then you can spin that in a high, high velocity that will break down... in sequences. And one of those sectors will have a part of the human DNA to heal and break down the genome of the mRNA. This will help reverse some of the damages that were done... it will... slow down to zero point... the reproduction of the spike protein." She sent it to a doctor and called it "fictitious, sci-fi information" for doctors to research.

Added safety note

Added safety note: this is a received message, not a treatment. Venoms are dangerous; never attempt to obtain or use them.

"First you must know that things happen first etherically and then in the matter. The material is a lower sequence from the ether... If you lower the vibration of your consciousness, then you are allowing this type of demonic stuff, this type of realities, to enter into your consciousness."

#35.3How the portal opened

We lowered our frequency so far that a portal opened and negative entities came in: through social media, the books we read, how we behave and what we allow into our bodies. So we must raise our vibration. Share information with those who are open, then let it be their decision.
