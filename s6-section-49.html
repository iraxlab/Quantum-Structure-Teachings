The big idea

You are always connected; what you need is to increase your light etheric body until you can receive the next level, like graduating. Intelligence alone isn't enough: the heart and mind together create the current, and ego blocks it. The body's part follows three stages: cleanse the cells and balance the biome and pH; then frequency flows in and the DNA activates; then the etheric body is restored, the heart downloads its stargate codes, and the body becomes luminous. She tells her own path only with her disclaimer: everything here must be done under a doctor's supervision.

#Key teachings

Added safety note

Added safety note, read first: this section reports her personal regime and a channelled protocol, including extended water-only fasting and stopping medicine. These can be dangerous, even fatal (for example with diabetes, heart, kidney or eating-disorder history, pregnancy, or for children). She repeatedly said "you must have a doctor's supervision at any cost" and "I would not advise you to do what I'm talking about." Never fast on water alone or stop prescribed medication (such as antibiotics) without your doctor.

#49.1Heart and mind

Being very educated doesn't guarantee connection: ego creeps in. You need a pure heart, compassionate yet strong as steel, and a heart-mind current flowing most of the time. Ascension combines diet, mind, heart, actions and every interaction; the common denominator must be positive. Fluctuations and down days are normal: recharge and continue.

#49.2Her elimination path (as she described it)

Taught by the hybrids: list everything you eat and remove one food every two weeks, heaviest first (beef, then other meats), while juicing and taking shakes from her detox videos, with a probiotic and distilled water. By the third month her skin cleared. When she dropped fish her energy fell, so she was told to return to fish for a week. Later only salad, potatoes, juices and shakes; then juices and shakes; then juices only. Juices supply minerals (Swiss chard is very salty); avoid anything you're allergic to. Have blood work at the start and checks about every six weeks. You may lose friends who drink; real friends adapt.

#49.3Heart activation

After 15 days on juices only, she was told she'd be activated: she felt a hand take out her heart, found herself fully aware above her body, then felt it put back: "you've been activated". The new heart holds the codes for the stargate location of your star family. On the eleventh day of distilled water her face had no wrinkles. Cleansing the cells switches the DNA on, which then draws prana from the ether: the start of longevity.

The message: the ascension diet (condensed)

When the body cleanses through juicing and then distilled water, it transforms. Hardened salt deposits dissolve. After fruit every four hours with distilled water, then water only, "the hemorrhoidal cells will start shrinking in 10 days" and the cells' portals open: within about 20 days the cells and etheric body begin cleansing. The water protocol alternates: three days distilled water only, then three days water with 3 to 4 tablespoons of honey (so blood sugar doesn't drop), repeating. The gut biome becomes stronger and corrects the cells; then frequency flows into the cells, the DNA coordinates and activates the genome, the etheric field is restored, the heart downloads its codes, and "the body becomes luminous." "Only then you are ready to activate your etheric body."

Added note

Health claims here (dissolving stones, shrinking hemorrhoids, clearing pneumonia with water, gum treatment with baking soda) are as she said them, not established medicine. For gum disease see a dentist; for kidney or gallstones, infection or any serious symptom, see a doctor.

#49.4Sleep and intimacy

Go to bed early enough to sleep through the hours before midnight (she said this is when the body draws its melatonin and energy for the next day); dreams and thoughts become clearer. Avoid alcohol and smoking. Intimacy without love depletes your energy: you exchange genetic and karmic information through it, which is why a partner's karma can affect you and your children. Retreats in nature with like-minded people help; treat yourself well.
