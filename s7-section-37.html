The big idea

Neutrality is observation: be informed, but don't get pulled into the daily drama, because your reaction to a hologram creates a sound wave that sets your next position in longitude and latitude, and so your parallel timeline. The Council of Nine called the building blocks of consciousness "community-cove" particles: one particle, rotating in two directions through 360 degrees, takes every position in the matrix, so blue becomes black and black becomes blue; that is why everyone you see is your mirror image. Rotation is the bridge from hologram to hologram; only a similar sound wave lets you flow across, which is why a shock freezes you. Every shift passes through neutrality, and action is what crystallizes it into matter.

Every hologram you watch carries a specific resonance; learning what the negative does, you start vibrating in its sequence. The outcome is positive, but the fluctuation produces many unexpected packets of reality: when the positive stops something, the negative switches a new hologram in. Be vigilant: she would never tell anyone to invest in anything.

#37.4Community-cove particles

As in HR, every particle is a reality, a hologram, fluctuating. Seen from above it spirals like a Fibonacci, rotating one way, and it also rotates in a second direction. As it reprints and shifts it takes the previous position in time-space: blue becomes black and black becomes blue, and through constant reprinting both are in the same location at once. That is your zero point and the mirror image you see in others: "we are all the same". One particle holds all positions in the matrix; what differs is its sound. Particles communicate with their own reflections, a "cove", a community of conversion.

#37.5Emotion sets your position

Saying "oh" or "yay", feeling negative or positive, puts you in a different position in time-space and a different outcome. You see the hologram, but your reaction changes your position, seen from both the positive and negative realities within the reality.

#37.6Rotation is the bridge

"The bridge is the rotation movement in the quantum field, and that builds fluid motion from one hologram to another." The fractal flow of sound carries you through portals of almost the same sound wave; a completely different sound wave and you'd vanish into a completely different reality. That is why sudden screaming or an event freezes you: a lower frequency passes through your hologram and you must restructure before you can react.

#37.7Action crystallizes

"The hologram is a fractal fluid reprint that gives the illusion of motion." You can have the hologram and the emotion's sound wave, but the action crystallizes it into matter; action is the bridge between sound and the states of matter. Light has curvature, she adds.

#37.8Every shift passes through neutrality

To shift from one hologram to another you must pass through neutrality, a point of complete stillness, and then the new frequency enters based on your reaction and position. Reprinted particles with their own electric and magnetic charge bounce off each other like magnets; that creates the constant sound and motion of the always-present field. You are a fractal of God holding that charge. The negative lowers it by provoking reactions that match their sound, which changes your DNA code, the code of the superhuman with a sixth sense who opens portals.

Don't let the mirror image you dropped into control your reaction. Protect your children: the youth is our future.

#The method — Switching an emotion (her steps)

  1. Catch the reaction before you speak; hold your words.
  2. Walk away and physically do something calming: breathe in and out.
  3. Use something uplifting until the anger falls to zero.
  4. Put the outcome in neutrality: fine either way, knowing you already have it.
  5. Return and level it: smile, say something kind, apologize if needed.
  6. When an opportunity appears, act on it and accept what is given from the heart.

#Questions answered

Does switching your emotion count as taking an action, or must a separate action follow?
Both are needed. (Her personal story is condensed into its lesson.) When about to explode in anger, she held her words, walked away, breathed and watched something uplifting until the anger fell to zero, then switched to the positive, put the outcome in neutrality (okay whether or not it happens, while knowing it already exists) and apologized. Later, when an opportunity appeared, she spoke up about the situation without asking, and help came through someone else. So: first a physical action to bring emotion to zero and avoid an argument; then a physical action when the opportunity appears; accept what is given from the heart, with no ego. Don't put conditions on how it comes. Prayer alone won't do it: to protect children, act to stop what harms them. Repeat the potency, predictive programming and examples of manifestation videos to build potency. Bigger goals involve more people and more layers to maneuver.
