Utilization of the Quantum Structure

How consciousness recognizes each of us by a unique signal, why 100% knowing gives 100% manifestation, how to test any information by the feeling it gives you, a perception trick for old pain, and a channelled round of questions (live talk)

The big idea

Every being has a unique pulse, with its own colour and geometry, recognizable among infinite realities; its location changes constantly as its frequency changes. Peel away the layers of pretence and that location becomes precise, so your next moment is clear. To use the structure: 100% knowing brings 100% manifestation, shown live when a reality was shifted simply by changing perception; information is tested by the feeling it gives you; and old pain loses its hold when you change your perception of when it happened.

#Key teachings

#20.1Your unique signal

When you become a fractal of consciousness, you carry a pulse "so unique that between the billions and infinite probabilities of realities, it will be recognizable", with its own colour and geometry. That is how higher consciousness recognizes you on any platform. Your location (latitude and altitude, see S2 15.4) keeps changing, "like a huge soup of resonances, constantly merging", so the next moment is never the same place. Consciousness knows all and learns all at once: that is the now; a slight delay, the curvature, is what lets you experience realities one by one (see S3 16.2).

#20.2Peel the layers for precision

Go "to the bottom of the true essence of who you are, without pretence, without ego", which is a false hologram plastered onto your field. With that pulled away, your location is precise "and confirms what your next moment is going to be"; understanding the next step becomes "the frequency within your heart". Earlier teachings gave only fractals of the whole; now the whole is being given as you can take it (see S3 17.5).

#20.3100% knowing, 100% manifestation

Asked about suffering in one country, the council declared, "Program stops", then explained: it meant that from that moment the teacher's own timeline would show positive outcomes there, step by step. "So you see, it's all individual perception. We changed the hologram; we just gave an example of how that works." The questioner could do the same: "shift your perception of knowingness to 100%, and you shift the timeline. 100% knowingness is what utilizes into 100% manifestation." Seeming repetition is an illusion.

#20.4Test information by its feeling

Truth is never held by one being: it was scattered on purpose, across many people and places, to keep the field in balance. So: read or hear something a few times, then notice how you feel. If it brings fear, "the resonance is not recognizable yet": move to other information. Keep searching until a wave of knowing comes, "you 100% know that this is it", then expand from there. If later it no longer sustains you, explore further. In the end, even the search was what you needed in order to awaken.

#20.5Old pain: change when it happened

Childhood trauma keeps looping because each new hologram reprints a similar frequency, though every moment is "literally a completely different earth". A perception that helps: "If I tell you that whatever happened to you happened 10,000 years ago, what are you going to say? Oh, okay." Told it was two days ago, your frequency drops at once. So place it, in your perception, far back, and the attachment to the frequency loosens; the program can shut down, and you switch to the positive daily, four or five times if needed, until the new outcome collapses (see S3 18.5).

Support

Working with a trauma-informed therapist can help alongside this. In a crisis in the US, call or text 988.

#20.6Receiving through the heart

Many gifted people are held back by the belief that exchange can only be through money. Money received through "the philanthropy of the person's heart", by their choosing, builds a different timeline: it comes exactly when you need it. "Neutrality and acceptance of the whole structure, without condition" holds the 5D. You may pay your bills; after that, let the harmonics bring more in, "easy, breezy" (see S1 4).

#20.7Grab back the resonance of joy

To connect with the higher realms of love: let everything be from the heart, and when your action brings someone joy, "grab that resonance back from them". Accumulated, it draws more of the same chord, until you merge into a community where beautiful outcomes come daily. You are one particle carrying the whole collective's signal: "expect from yourself the best, be the love, be the vibration, and so it is."

#20.8Perception, not dates

Fear attached to a date or an event (an unlucky day, an eclipse) projects that day: "if somebody told me the eclipse was going to be a disaster and I 100% believed it, I wasn't going to have a beautiful day." Both realities exist; you choose. Some will have their hardest times through the ascension, others their best, by the resonance they carry.

#The method — Testing Information by Feeling

  1. Take in the information a few times, calmly.
  2. Turn inward: what emotion does it bring?
  3. If it's fear, the resonance isn't yours yet: set it aside and look elsewhere.
  4. If a wave of knowing comes, take it as your next step and learn more.
  5. Keep checking: when it no longer sustains you, explore further.
  6. Remember: truth is scattered, never in one person (20.4).

#Questions answered

Between sleep and waking I saw pyramids lighting up. Will they be active again?
Built at about 45 degrees, slightly off, to connect photons; much older than they appear. History will be rewritten fractally over about 50 years, as you create it; you may find your memory of things has changed.
Should I invest in stocks?
No stocks are recommended. A new system is said to be prepared and on standby.
How will a reversal of the magnetic poles affect us?
"What poles, and which reality?" Holding the higher resonance, you'd feel any change "as simply a breeze through your body".
Many are going hungry in my country. Will it improve?
See 20.3.
Should I stay in my relationship or leave?
Leave if you wish, but don't rush: revisit it within three months; you may not think the same way.
A force flipped me over in my sleep. What was it?
Three entities trying to enter at a certain angle; it wasn't allowed. You're safe.
I keep seeing the number 28.
It activates attention: notice what you're doing when it appears; more number patterns will come as awareness grows (see S3 13).
What happens at death and reincarnation?
The body was designed to expand its DNA and shift by bilocation, but the 3D structure was hijacked so that after death souls returned into the same hologram, believing it was heaven. A union of councils and beings acted to free humanity: "no consciousness has the right to overtake another without permission." Hence the quantum protocol: "we gave you your freedom. So create" (see S3 12, S3 14.2).
I don't remember any past lives. Am I a walk-in?
Yes, from a certain era, and caught in the loop; only the strongest held their vibration. See 20.6.
Will new laws protect people?
Many laws were worded to confuse; new bylaws must come, pro-life, pro-future and in the name of humanity, and change will come slowly, not by everything shutting down at once.
Old friends have deserted me.
"Smile, child. When old friends go, new friends come." It's part of your transformation.
Energies in my home seem to block my gifts.
A looping portal was closed: "Go sleep normally now. It's done" (see S3 17).
Must I make a legal claim to be sovereign?
"There is no need to claim sovereignty when you're already free"; change comes through new bylaws and new people in positions, slowly but surely.
Added note

For any legal step, consult a qualified lawyer; see the note in S3 15.

Will anything be done about student debt?
In the new beginning, once certain people in office change.
I had a vivid dream of soldiers in a helicopter at my window.
A dream is another reality in different vibration: don't hold it in fear; look at it as a dream and move on.
How do I clear a belief I have no evidence for?
"Your confusion is also a belief": change it. And see 20.4.
I lack motivation to act in the world.
Rejections became insecurity, and insecurity fear: let go and trust who you are.
How can we connect with the higher realms of love?
See 20.7.
Some nice people make me deeply uncomfortable.
Let them express themselves; respect their space and hold neutrality. You change your vibration, and reprint a new version of them, or they drift away. Love them, "even if you have to say no more."
How do I heal childhood trauma that feels stuck in my back?
See 20.5.
Is a certain date a sign of one side or the other?
See 20.8.
