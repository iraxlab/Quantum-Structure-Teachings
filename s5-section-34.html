Going up and down shifts the signal of the hologram you enter, landing you somewhere else. #The method — the weekly holographic imprint

Codes are given weekly (on Wednesdays); this is the first.

  1. Begin at zero point: relax; become aware and accept the new.
  2. Sit in quiet, with no music in the background: music carries sub-tonalities that interfere with the signal.
  3. Read the code (aloud or silently) three to seven times; reading it even months later still activates it, since there is no time.
  4. Accept it as true for you, or it simply passes by.
  5. Keep your vibration similar to the imprint in actions, thoughts and feelings for the days it reprints.
  6. Do your 99%: act from the heart daily; notice the small pushes and follow them.
