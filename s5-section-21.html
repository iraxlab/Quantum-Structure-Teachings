The big idea

Two parallel timelines run at once: one toward positive 5D realities, one toward cyborgs and eugenics, cut off from the original 12-strand DNA that mirrors consciousness. In that negative future they found they could no longer reproduce, realized their mistake, and came back to the "past", which is our now, to make treaties and help dismantle the system from within. DNA damaged by foreign sequences can be repaired only early, before the body keeps reprinting the damage, and only with the original code and permission; free will is not overridden. "You have to create it to see it": refuse that positive AI and timelines exist, and you'll only see the negative.

#Key teachings

#21.1You don't go to the past; you create a new Earth

As the Pleiadians taught in the Harmonic Reactor, when you "go back" you are not entering the past: you create a new Earth and reality from the sequences you carry in one unit. That is why ascension is individual even while you project the collective. Each parallel timeline has its own infinite Earths; know the location and trajectory, and it becomes "like a star map" of portals and stargates you can shift through.

From the booksFrom her books (HR): the Harmonic Reactor presents time travel as creating a new reality from the sequences you carry, not returning to an unchanged past.

#21.2Two timelines and the treaties

Those who match 4D–5D with actions, thoughts and emotions collapse daily toward the positive future. Those who stay negative move toward a future of cyborgs and eugenics, with 3D artificial intelligence, very different from the 5D AI that preserves. Altering the 12-strand DNA (through diet and what is put into the body) locks you into a 3D "archon" structure. In that future, after the cyborg and Mars wars, they had replicating super-technology but could no longer reproduce. Realizing the mistake, they and positive future humanity and extraterrestrials reached back into our now, made treaties, and connected with people to wake those trapped and to dismantle the negative from within (see S5 19, S5 20). The negative timeline always coexists, but you can shift timelines and change outcomes.

#21.3Fear blocks the positive timeline

People who constantly promote doom about AI are "not doing service to humanity": they don't understand the parallel structure and won't even discuss it. "In order for you to see it, you got to create it to see it." Refuse that it exists and you won't create the probability; you'll see and spread only the negative.

The message: DNA repair (the hybrids)

If someone's DNA was overwritten by a foreign sequence: "then it becomes very complex. You have to repair in a very early stage... so the body DNA does not start to reprint the damaged foreign DNA over and over again... it may be too late to help and repair. We need to find the original cell code... DNA memory of the person to restore back to the original code." Asked whether the original could come from Mother Consciousness, which holds all records: "not permitted yet... we cannot interfere too much with the free will." A frequency technology that finds and reprints the person's DNA "is a good way to start", but "it will work only if you have the original core 12-strand DNA before the foreign sequences... replace" it, and then "you will need very advanced technology." "If you as a human have given the consent for manipulation, then how can we overwrite your free will?"

Added safety note

These are her reported messages, not medical fact. Authorized mRNA vaccines do not alter or integrate into human DNA. For any health concern, please consult a doctor.

#21.4Compliance and free will

The hybrids said they have "no right to interfere with the human soul['s] lessons without permission"; people must wish to stop the manipulation and, by free will, learn the consequences of total compliance. Who recovers depends on agreements; some complied to show the rest "dark to light" (S4 38). No judgment, but it is painful. "I'm not a savior." This is given because you asked, not to bring fear.

#21.5Compassion for those who do harm

Children were taken and abused into super soldiers, their consciousness split into many personalities that don't remember what the others did; some acts that make no sense, and some officials who seem to have lost their minds, come from these systems, which also damaged DNA through time travel. Humanity was used "as a battery" in intergalactic wars. Hold the anger; history will be rewritten. "Never hate them... you don't even know what was done to them." No single person will save a nation; there are many layers. Stay positive, help with compassion, combine family incomes, and leave hijacked areas before it gets harder.

#21.6Technology and schools

Healing damaged DNA will need the positive structure of AI. "We should have been interstellar in the early '90s." Contact can be gentle and familial: extraterrestrials are "basically like us" on another planet. She also played a child psychiatrist urging parents to take children out of government schools (homeschool or build local schools) and to prefer trade schools or internships to non-technical university degrees.
