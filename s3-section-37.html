The Regeneration of the Human Body: New DNA Code

The third, etheric strand of DNA, a preserved original code, why change takes years, cloned bodies, why you don't "earn" a low vibration, how to serve, what to do with intense energy, loving everyone, food and water, and how silence becomes consent (live talk with channelling)

The big idea

As consciousness expands, the new codes will activate a third, etheric strand of DNA, the invisible life force, and the original human code, kept safe by consciousness itself, will be downloaded to everyone so the body can rejuvenate and regenerate. Mutation takes years, so help it: clean food, detox, nature. And remember that not acting is also consent: you always have a choice.

#Key teachings

#37.1The third strand activates

Under a microscope DNA shows two strands; put certain frequency in and you'd see the third (see S3 30.11). The new codes from the natural expansion of consciousness will make the genetic code "pop up like a balloon" and activate this third etheric codon, the life force "invisible until now". The original human DNA and RNA was "secretly preserved" in a vault reality that only consciousness itself could open, so no one could tamper with it. Now it's being released to all, to restore DNA that was altered without consent. Then "all of you will start to self-rejuvenate and self-regenerate".

#37.2The code, and why it takes years

Only a fraction of the code was given, to protect it: 3371 · 33371, and, in answer to a question, 33713333371. It activates the body and begins mutating the DNA codon back to the original; it was described as restoring the original, balanced design of male and female. "Mutation takes years", but it's easier with the right food, detox and not too much meat (see S3 30.2; her detox talk is Section 38). History, erased and restarted more than once, "will be rewritten from consciousness itself".

From the booksFrom her books (NT): the same knowledge applied to all cells "means that you can rebuild and regenerate all cells, all systems back to normal. This is longevity."

#37.3Cloned bodies hold only a fraction

Is the soul inside the body? There's no separation, only a different projection. A cloned body can be grown and a fraction of a consciousness shifted into it, but that soul doesn't hold "the original helium of the frequency DNA" to sustain the body. So cloned bodies aren't self-sustaining, can't mutate to the next level, and die off after a time.

#37.4You don't "earn" a low vibration

Did a hard childhood mean you earned a low vibration in a past life? "We wouldn't say you earn it": you run a program from a previous timeline, to complete lessons you accumulated, and you build new frequencies here to mutate it. But it's free will: "you always have the choice to say no" and convert negative to positive. You set the conditions, through your emotions, so you reproduce a new version of yourself (1 + 1 = 3; see S1 2.2).

#37.5How to serve humanity

"Be the best expression of yourself on a daily basis, moment to moment": those moments combine into a potency that expresses a better you later. Ask: "What can I do now to be the best of myself, whatever the situation? How can I act positively, even if somebody is negative?" Sometimes let others be until they realize who they are; sometimes step in. Peel each situation's layers (see S3 17.5): the feeling it brings shows the density you're on, and you choose to stay or to mutate.

#37.6When energy runs strong

Intense lucid dreams and energy through the body are the small activation push taking momentum, different for each soul. Have no fear: meditate and breathe through it, and go to nature. Trees and fresh air hold the balance of yin and yang and release what you've accumulated; your body connects differently there than in a home. Walk barefoot on grass or soil, ideally early in the morning, at sunrise (see S3 30.11).

#37.7Watching it like a screen

Feeling that the world looks like a screen you're observing is part of the process: you've "had enough", you understand what's happening, and you choose a positive video, a positive act, something in the name of humanity. Many will stop wanting to talk politics or hear speculation. "The true core of your reality is your belief system."

#37.8Love everyone; never override

To help as many as possible: share what you know, but "you can never overtake somebody's consciousness to make them believe you". Let them express who they are, and "love them for who they are": everyone chose to come here for a reason; give that space to all, with no judgement. "Forgiveness, expression of love, giving and sharing should be the core of your behaviour" (see S3 27.6).

#37.9Food, water and intention

Can intention change your food? Yes: you can shift reality so that a new organic shop or farm appears nearby, "a reality within a reality running specifically for you". It's a process, so also act: detox, eat carefully, drink distilled water as a conductor (see S2 8). Don't drink structured or energized water for long periods, "like you wouldn't take the same pill all the time": do it with supervision. Most of you have one to three guides helping (see S3 32.1).

#37.10Not acting is consent

Why do the councils need permission when the negative doesn't? "It happened with your consent." What was done was shown openly, in films and media, and staying passive ("there's nothing I can do") is agreement. "It's always something to do." Look at what your children are learning; read books; ask questions; then take action. The glitch in their design: they're part of consciousness too, so acting against it ends in self-sabotage (see S3 30.7).

#The method — Supporting Your Body's Regeneration

  1. Consent to the code and say it once (37.2); no need to repeat all day.
  2. Eat cleanly: more natural food, less meat; detox gradually with supervision (see S3 30.2).
  3. Drink water as a conductor; don't use structured water long-term (37.9).
  4. Go to nature: barefoot on the ground, ideally at sunrise (37.6).
  5. Breathe through strong energy or dreams; meditate; have no fear.
  6. Be patient: mutation takes years; act from the heart every day.
