The big idea

She can't see into possession or dark matter; for that, seek others who work in that field. The message came unrequested, so she treats it as already collapsing into the field: within about three months, a global dystopia in places and, at the same time, "medical, political and domestic" winnings for humanity, all coordinated by consciousness. The evil must come out into the open field to be seen and stopped. Don't focus day to day; know you are already free, stay calm and act with precision. The true awakening is awareness.

#Key teachings

#19.1What she can't do

Many write about possession or curses. Her guides show her a brick wall there; it's beyond her abilities and she won't take that responsibility. Some practitioners (deep hypnosis, those who work with such cases) may help.

#19.2Everything has its place

However chaotic it looks, everyone will receive what they deserve by their frequency. What's being rolled out (another pandemic, digital controls) will be tried and won't roll over; people won't listen. In conflicts, don't pick a side: everyone is manipulated, instigated against each other by those maneuvering both sides. "What is right they promote as wrong and vice versa."

The message: victories (condensed)

"The hologram of our reality will expand into a new trajectory within three months. You will see a big, big dystopia in some global rejection, and at the same time big, big winnings of the global positive side of humanity." "Everything in a medical, political and domestic will be all winnings... and yet know that all is coordinated from consciousness itself." To the negative: "Checkmate. "You are always protected... I'm in charge." On money: "All is in good time... all you need, when you need, will miraculously appear."

Added note

Added note: these are received predictions, kept brief. Named public figures and families, and claims about groups and conflicts, are left out or kept general.

#19.3Your soul is the target

Rich or poor makes no difference: "they're after your souls", to ride on your energy and keep the hologram on their trajectory, which is why transhumanism and synthetic brains are pushed. "Your soul, your energy is the true power." Feel your heart's power, as a torus, then act like a warrior. Gold coins won't feed you in a crisis; your community within a mile will. Anyone hoarding to profit from others' need fails the test.

#19.4The moment a warrior is born

At some point something will shock you, in front of you or on a screen. Let that moment remind you of these teachings: "a warrior will be born in that moment". You'll learn what you're capable of when challenged; that is your mission. "You are it. All the power of God is within you... But you gave your power away, and now it is time to take it back... The true awakening is the awareness."

#19.5Countries move at different speeds

Within about three years you'll see differences in countries that now look dystopian. Each country, like a person, is a pulsation of what it accumulated over centuries, and some must cleanse more. Some old wealthy families broke away from the destructive agenda because they want their descendants to live. Keep paying your taxes: many people in institutions are honest workers who don't know.

#19.6Let children develop naturally

Each child is like a free particle that must find its own strong position. Let them play outside, climb trees, fall, get dirty and learn, in balance with technology. Avoid spectacles that may carry hidden frames. When you look for alternatives, you'll find schools and clinics that seem to have existed for years: you have shifted into that reality.

She didn't give those codes, so she couldn't answer.
