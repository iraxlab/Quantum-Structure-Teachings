The big idea

The Council of Nine said you don't need hours of meditation, lucid dreaming or out-of-body experience to leave this timeline. When the knowledge of the protocols and a clean diet make your body and mind one unit, luminous and at the right signature, you can split your consciousness into two parallel realities running at once (here and with your galactic family) and remember both, until you choose to live in one. To enter a stargate, a soul's signature must match its codes: the knowledge of the universal bylaws plus a heart full of positive transformation; the ego-driven cannot enter. To move forward you must also break down the misinformation embedded in history and religion, without attaching to any one belief system.

#Key teachings

#7.1Workshops led by questions

The Council asked that workshops follow your questions rather than new topics, because a missing link (something misunderstood) keeps you from penetrating to a higher resonance. The knowledge is a code; each piece must be in place. The books will be built step by step for the same reason.

#7.2Break down the deception

To shift into the new reality, every frequency downloaded into our consciousness through deception must be restructured: it comes in front of you so you can break it down. She was guided (through synchronicity, a 15th-century reference she couldn't find until she searched precisely) to history showing how original teachings were altered over centuries and infiltrated into many religions and secret societies, for example removing teachings on fasting. Fear of the words "extraterrestrial" or "psychic" was designed to keep people from the knowledge.

Added note

Added note: her account of specific historical religious movements, groups and figures is left out. It is her reading of others' material; no faith or people should be blamed or targeted.

#7.3Why the knowledge opens stargates

Proper information opens stargates. The Egyptian structures, like the Halls of Amenti, open when you resonate with certain knowledge; in some chambers people found themselves in another reality. But you don't need a pyramid: with the knowledge you can access it anywhere. No time exists, just as you can phone someone in another time zone now. Prophets were downloading from parallel realities or the quantum field.

#7.4Why the original DNA was targeted

In her account, those who manipulated humanity knew our DNA is like a spaceship: the original blueprint that can open stargates at will. They lowered our vibration and pushed practices that wiped out human DNA lineages so their own remained predominant and their artificial timeline stayed in place.

#7.5Out of body, or the shortcut

Out-of-body experience is good and real, but takes long training. The Council said the shortcut is the quantum manifestation knowledge plus the diet: when mind and body share a similar resonance, at will you can say "I'm seeing my extraterrestrial family" and, matching that frequency, be guided step by step. She trained lucid dreaming in two weeks but was refused entry to a craft: "you don't have to do it this way". During a juice cleanse she felt her heart replaced with one holding the stargate codes, and floated above her body.

Added safety note

Added safety note: she ended up on juices and plans water only, saying "never do anything like this without a doctor's supervision" and that she had blood work. Long fasts are dangerous for many people; do them only with medical supervision.

#7.6Two realities at once

Out of body, your body stays in Earth's planetary density while you travel, as in Avatar. With the protocols and a luminous body you split your consciousness into two parallel realities running simultaneously (one perhaps with extraterrestrials, even "ahead" in time) and hold both memories. If you choose to leave permanently, your body stays like a picture frame in time-space, and family and friends project their own version of you, as agreed.

The vicious... souls that have measured themselves without the heart and with the ego will never be able to enter these platforms. #7.7Everyone has a piece

Many through history held parts of this knowledge; no one knows it all. Share it so people can grasp it, without charging so much they can't breathe. Some take to out-of-body work naturally; learn from those who know it, but you still need the quantum structure. Children should one day learn these skills in school.
