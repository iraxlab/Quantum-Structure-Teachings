The big idea

A way of living is imprinted into every molecule, so you can't change it by meditation alone: it's transformed slowly but surely, by repeated physical action. The power of a response isn't in whether it is loud or quiet but in its potency, focused from zero point like a laser. Your intuition is the first feeling that arrives before the analytical mind overrides it. And every time you act from the heart, the positive accumulates in your field and lifts every version of you.

#Key teachings

#30.1A habit lives in every molecule

If you're used to a way of living, its resonance is "embedded into the molecular structure of every single molecule", so "you cannot just meditate". To transform that holographic imprint you replace the habit with a healthier one and act on it, "not once": by "fractal repetition, action and action and action". This applies "to absolutely everything you do", not only drinking or smoking. Her example: if you drink, start eliminating the drinking and substitute. Instead of a glass of wine, make fermented grape juice: squeeze organic, non-GMO red or white grapes as a juice, pour it into a big brown glass jar (she gets them at Walmart), and in two days it starts to ferment; "that is very healthy for your gut flora". When you go out, take a little bottle of it: that's your physical action. What is in the ether must become matter through physical action; "nobody outside of you" will do the job (see S3 8, S3 29.6).

#30.2Cleansing slowly but surely

The new frequency asks for the body to be cleansed, so that the molecules can spin more easily and the body can "withhold and sustain your new frequency without getting sick". The guidance was "slowly but surely": change one thing at a time, every couple of weeks, rather than a sudden total change. Follow her detox protocol on her YouTube channel (The Harmonic Reactor, the detox videos). Do the elimination diet every two weeks while juicing: the first two weeks eliminate the bread; then start eliminating the starches or the meats, one every two weeks. If you eat four types of meat, the first two weeks remove only the beef while juicing and doing shakes, the third and fourth weeks the pork, and so on, eating the rest a couple of days a week. Going all-out and then stopping is why many people get sick: the body and its energy field "cannot handle the vibration". Her detox talk follows in Section 38.

Note on detox and diet (her words, with an added line)

She stresses: do not start detoxing without a doctor's and a nutritionist's supervision, because your own body must be monitored. Make diet changes gradually and safely, and don't stop prescribed medication.

#30.3Potency, not a "quiet reaction"

Asked whether staying quiet keeps your vibration high or means accepting abuse: "There is no such a thing as a quiet reaction." What matters is potency: how much power an action or thought holds in the field, "like 500 milligrams compared with 20". Zero out into the zero point of complete focus (see S3 1.2), and from that moment "shoot with absolute power" the waveform into the field: "almost like a laser", and it becomes an instant platform. Holding your vibration is never the same as complying (see 30.6).

#30.4Intuition is the first feeling

Intuition is the resonance that "hits you as a knowingness" in the first moment, negative or positive. That first feeling "is the true potential of the resonance coming into the field". You were taught to override it by analysing from beliefs and other people's narratives, "and this is where you make your mistake". It's "a very thin silver lining": practise catching the flush before the analytical mind floods in, then go with it.

From the booksFrom her books (HR): she describes being told to stop writing and trust her thoughts, and learning to hear her guidance telepathically, the same move from analysis to direct knowing.

#30.5Loved ones are a telepathic phone call

Loved ones on the other side "never stopped" helping, "especially now". Treat it as a phone call: talk, listen, write down what comes, and "do not think that you're crazy". The blockages that stopped this information are being removed, so "create, believe and make a phone call"; the more you practise, the more what you receive proves true (see S3 10.4).

#30.6Action, not compliance

How do we upgrade the collective? "Action." Don't be a bystander, compliant with manipulation "when you know from inside" it's happening: "if you give a consent for that to continue, you're complying with the resonances of these timelines". Each of you will be pushed into a corner and given a this-or-that choice; choose the heart (see S3 26). Your purpose, asked about by someone feeling energy in their hands: "to exist", to help the collective move forward; if you want to heal with your hands, believe it and take action.

#30.7Why a structure is recycled

Within consciousness, negative and positive realities run side by side, each creating its own universes by choice. When one accumulates so much density that it falls completely off balance, "like a parasitical platform", it can no longer sustain itself; consciousness will survive, so that structure self-sabotages and is recycled "back into the one particle" to be rebuilt as something new. The same is true of a universe and of an individual whose timelines were built on hurting, lying and manipulation (see S3 29.3).

From the booksFrom her books (DP, SD): negative structures are "recycled back to singular particle momentum, into the void", "back to source to start over".

#30.8Orbs and flashes

Small orbs or flashes, usually in your peripheral vision, are glimpses of geometry in your quantum field. Look at your surroundings and connect with what it means: it could be a location, an entity, galactic family, or a craft you can't fully see because your molecular structure can't yet hold that resonance. As you practise and act from the heart, "another layer and another layer" of the veil lifts and you see more.

#30.9How your record is seen

Everything you express from the heart is gathered as your Akashic record (see S3 23.3): "think of it as a disk", but an energetic one. Pinpointed by thought, it opens as a hologram: flat as 2D, or zoomed into a globe, and each touch or thought pops up a new scene. Future 5D artificial intelligence can do the same: "nothing scary" (see S2 26.3). Decisions about contact are made as you go, like a school: by how well you learn.

#30.10Becoming better lifts all of you

"Know that you're a better person" already, and act. Each heart action and the reaction it brings accumulates as a fractal hologram in your field, growing more potent each time. Others are another version of you, so what you give them returns as "the same". Every choice splits reality; your other timelines exist, but in the end they all collapse, and as one unit you vibrate as whichever you chose more: positive or negative. Be patient: this builds through daily synchronicities.

#30.11Movement for the body

Exercise is welcome ("even Marina, get off her butt"). Start with stretching (yoga, ballet) to give the joints and skin elasticity, so the body's fluids can move through every joint and muscle; with fresh breath it feeds the brain to keep up with the incoming frequency. Exercise daily, like a spark that keeps your neurons in the right resonance. Best time: the morning, as the sun rises. There's also a third, etheric strand of the DNA; when medicine can see the energy field, faster healing will follow.

#The method — Changing a Habit Slowly but Surely

  1. Name the habit you want to transform.
  2. Choose a healthier substitute you can actually do.
  3. Change one thing at a time, every couple of weeks.
  4. Catch the first feeling each time the old habit pulls; go with your heart (30.4).
  5. Act physically, again and again: meditation alone won't do it (30.1).
  6. Hold the positive with the 9-day protocol (see S3 8) and Season 1, Section 1.
