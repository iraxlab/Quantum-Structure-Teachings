The big idea

Everyone downloads information from parallel fields, but only according to their predominant sequence, often set in childhood. So each belief system is right for itself, yet locking yourself into one spectrum ("only my way; anyone who speaks of other life is possessed") is itself a judgment and a loop. Perception is raw data filtered by your beliefs; perspective is where you stand: step back and you see more. The Council of Nine explained that every particle has a unique frequency, so beings from planets with different chemistry and light look different; that doesn't make them evil. Demons are something else: low-vibration consciousness that can only take form parasitically. "What do you see right now? That is your reality."

#Key teachings

#27.1You download only your sequence

What you were taught as a child becomes the predominant sequence in your field, and you can download only what resonates with that pulsation. A higher vibration can hold more potency and download from fields above the structure. A prophet may truly see a probability (for example, one reality going negative and one positive), limited by what their consciousness can handle, their agreements and their location.

#27.2Why belief systems collide

The Council of Nine has permission to oversee clusters of realities. Within each belief system, people are right for themselves according to what they downloaded; when someone wants to control the narrative, they teach each group to point at the other, and conflict follows. A person locked into one spectrum puts a shield around themselves that nothing else exists. The quantum structure was given so you don't let fear control you: open up and don't reject others' experiences, which they chose. Even the role of victim was chosen so others could see dark become light.

#27.3Perception and perspective

Perception is the raw data your senses receive, but your beliefs filter it before it reaches awareness: two people see the same event differently. Perspective is the position you observe from: when you step back, you see more.

#27.4Everyone channels

Everyone downloads from some timeline, even in daily thinking; distancing yourself from "channelers" doesn't change that. A person who calls every psychic gift demonic (even one that found a lost child) may be the one blinded, by not opening the heart to all that exists. Higher beings, angels and etheric legions help everyone. Gather in your churches, but don't judge other belief systems; science and religion are the same knowledge given in different ways.

#27.5Why she was "not allowed"

When she left her body and reached a spacecraft, a voice said "You're not allowed to be here", and she found herself in her childhood home: it wasn't her time. To hold the vibration of other groups, you become that vibration: "instead [of] 110 volts, you are going to hold 10,000 volts", slowly, by learning the mechanics so your body can hold the field. That's total mutation. She is juicing to detox her body again.

The message: "who cares?" (condensed, Council of Nine)

When she worried about someone with a large platform calling ETs demons: "Who cares what is going on with somebody else's hologram?... What do you see now?" Trees, birds, peace. "This is exactly what you need to think of." "Why do you continuously in your mind [think] of what evil is doing?" "Your location now is your and only your reality that you are creating... that is the vibrational location in the now [that] your consciousness holds." "Anyone that holds negative information is truly [in] their vibrational plug of probability trajectory." "Do not apply the fear, stop it now. Take care of your diet [and] body; knowledge is feeding the soul; the mind plugs the positive frequency through videos [and] information." Then "anything that is vital for you to see or experience will pop up in synchronicity." A hard experience that forces you to move may be protecting you from something worse. "The goal is to stay focused in the present." "So what? Let them talk... This is their reality... it doesn't have to be yours."

The message: why life forms look different (condensed, Council of Nine)

"Each molecule of creation has its own personal vibration. It is unique." By free will, each particle chooses its position, so each shift reprints it with a new frequency: "new vibration, new earth", a new universe. So whatever mutates into matter "will have a similar or completely different makeup [and] visual looks". On planets with different chemistry and light spectra, beings may have more silica, fly, look like birds, communicate differently: "That doesn't mean that they are evil." Human races and even the lungs of high-altitude peoples differ by location; diet tolerance varies by origin; DNA too close doesn't combine well. "That is complete amnesia" to fear other worlds while accepting differences here. "Demons are completely different... low vibrational consciousness energy that can take a physical form only by altering its soul energy to matter density... like a fuel of a low frequency": parasitic, unable to convert. They may stage a fake "evil ET" threat so humanity fights an outside enemy in perpetual war. "Where is your free will then?"

Added note: these are her teachings and received messages; claims about ETs, demons and staged events are not established fact. Examples about adaptation and diet are simplified.

#27.6Getting rid of parasites

"What do you need to do to get rid of parasites? First you need to detox": understand how reality works and push away what doesn't resonate. Don't put anything in your body that takes your senses. Don't let AI tell you how your reality should be: "You're playing with fire, but the fire can warm your home and... destroy you." Trust yourself first; AI can help, but the source must be you. Gather with joy and laughter, build community, make friends, garden.

#The method — Staying in your own reality (as given)

  1. When someone else's belief disturbs you, ask: "Who cares about their hologram?"
  2. Ask: "What do I see right now? What do I hear?"
  3. Take that as your reality; release fear: "Stop it now."
  4. Body: diet. Soul: knowledge. Mind: positive videos and information.
  5. Trust synchronicity to bring what you need to know.
  6. Gather, laugh, garden, and judge no one's path.
