Q & A: Predictions and the Council of Nine Answers

New predictions, how to hold them without fear, a coming wave of activation, and a long round of questions answered as a channelled download (live talk)

The big idea

A prediction is the end result of a program that is already running: the end result is fixed, but the who, how and when are only probabilities, so don't attach to dates. The predictions describe exposure and arrests, withheld technology being released step by step, new structures built by and for the people, and contact with our galactic families. Your part is not to watch the news in fear but to hold a positive frequency moment to moment, because you create your own reality. A wave of activation is coming (out-of-body experiences, lucid dreams, contact) in a different way for each person. Help comes only with your permission, and you still have to take action.

#Key teachings

#8.1Don't take the fear

When a new law, a news story or a frightening event appears, don't go into fear: fear projects that same reality into your hologram (see S1 9.9, S1 8.12). You create your own reality, so what happens on someone else's platform doesn't have to be yours. Hold the positive, moment to moment, and the timeline you live on shifts with it (see S1 14.8). Watching the news is not the problem; taking its frequency in is.

#8.2A wave of activation

Many people are about to have out-of-body experiences, lucid dreams and activations, and contact with their galactic families will come in infinite ways: in dreams, in meditation, through synchronicity, and for some in person. Each person receives it in their own way, so don't compare. The talk said that three people in the group were to be activated for contact.

#8.3What a prediction is

A prediction is the end result of a program that has already been set in motion. The end result is the fixed part; who, how and when are probabilities, because realities keep fluctuating with everyone's choices (see S2 5.1, S2 6.2). So hold the end result and let go of the timing. The downloads are also tested by guidance: answer from the heart, and let the outcome show what was true.

#8.4The predictions

As given in the talk, the program includes: wrongdoing exposed, followed by arrests around the world; a new alliance working behind the scenes (described in the talk as "white hats" within the military); withheld technologies released, including healing technology ("med beds") and tissue regrowth; a new elite formed from the people, for the people; new structures built globally; a move toward digital currency; and, over time, contact with galactic families through portals. The release happens step by step, so as not to cause panic.

From the booksDP prints the predictions given on 2/6/2019, which match this talk: criminals "globally arrested within three years", a new global elite "formed from the people, for the people" that "will declassify all the free technology", "people will be liberated", new structures built globally, portals to reconnect with galactic families, and "no negative structure can see this platform". It sets the frame: about 20 years of rebuilding, and in 30 years humanity is ready to connect with its galactic families through the portals.
Added note

These are predictions and claims, not established facts. No "med bed" or tooth- and tissue-regrowth technology exists for public use today; for any health problem, see a doctor and use proven treatment. The book reports the political and military claims as the talk gave them, without taking a side.

#8.5Help comes with permission

Help from the galactic families is individual and personal, and it works through your permission: nothing is done to you without your agreement (see S1 13.4). The intention is a smooth transition, without suffering. But help doesn't replace your part: you still take action, and you stay alert. "Don't be naive."

#8.6Divine neutrality

Divine neutrality is zero point: from there, timelines collapse into the end result (see S1 2.1). How you experience the change depends on your beliefs and your agreements, so stay in the heart. "You want to get out of the Matrix? Create your own."

#The method — The manifestation meditation

Given as the answer on how to meditate deeply to manifest:

  1. Get comfortable in your own way: music, nature, a quiet room. There is no one right way.
  2. Imagine the blueprint: see clearly what you want to create.
  3. Feel it from the heart. The emotion is the frequency; an image without feeling has no pull.
  4. Increase both together: let the picture and the feeling grow stronger.
  5. Hold it as long as you can, then drop it. Release it completely; don't keep checking.
  6. Act, relentlessly. After the meditation, take steps in the physical world.

#Questions answered

The answers in this part were given as a channelled download (the "Council of Nine").

I keep seeing purple eyes and rainbow crystals. What does it mean?
Three platforms are merging in you. The message: you're ready for manifestation; stop practising and start creating.
Can the effects of vaccines be reversed?
The talk claimed vaccines carry nanoparticles and that intervention is possible to reverse them.
Added safety note

This claim is false. Approved vaccines do not contain nanoparticles that harm or control you, and there is nothing to "reverse". If you have questions about a vaccine or a reaction, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.

Why are some of us awake when others aren't?
It is an activation at the soul level. Many of us carry the DNA of more than 20 races, described in the talk as "the DNA of three heliums"; many artistic children carry it too.
What should we expect from the revaluation (the change in the money system)?
A smooth transition is intended. Still be practical: keep a little cash at home and one to two weeks of food (see S2 6, on preparing).
When will the healing beds be available?
Fully operational within 30 years, with glimpses sooner. (A claim; see the note under 8.4.)
Can we regrow teeth with our thoughts?
The talk said yes, that it can be done through thought. (A claim; see a dentist for your teeth.)
Will the planet be destroyed?
Which Earth? There are parallel Earths; the one you experience depends on your frequency (see S2 4.2).
Can I freeze fresh juice?
"Yes, but it's not going to be that potent, because it's oxidizing": drinking within 20 minutes of juicing is best, "max 20 minutes".
The detox videos say to prepare juice for the whole day. Does it lose its nutritional value?
Yes, if it oxidizes. For the real potency, drink it five to ten minutes after juicing. "But you can, if you close it really well and leave it in the fridge": you still get nutrition, the potency is slightly different, and it tastes a little odd. (Her detox guide keeps half of the day's green juice in the fridge for later the same day.)
Is the earth flat?
Respect the belief; everything comes "full circle". Each person experiences the reality they believe in.
Are there corridors with the Arcturians?
Yes. There are three of them that humans may not enter.
I saw ships in the sky. What did I see?
Many ships within a ship. Your wish was granted.
I keep seeing 555. What does it mean?
5 + 5 + 5 = 15, and 1 + 5 = 6: the merkaba, manifestation. But don't attach to numbers; they are a permission slip (see S1 8.9, S2 2.10).
Besides detox, what else can I do?
Automatic writing: let your guidance write through you.
When will the new platform become tangible?
Don't attach to timing; realities are fluctuating (see 8.3).
How do rainbows relate to the chakras?
Chakras are vibrational tonalities, played like a piano. Play with colours.
Am I doing enough?
Follow your desire, and take action.
How do we recognise the new elite?
It isn't only politicians; it is people. Don't judge.
What about chlorine and fluoride in water?
Be cautious and drink clean water. "Don't be naive" and "follow the money".
Added note

Treated tap water in most countries is tested and safe to drink; a filter is a reasonable choice if you prefer it. Check your local water report rather than avoiding water.

How can I help humanity?
Through the joy of expression, from the heart chakra.

Whatever resonates with you: you'll feel it as goosebumps. Everyone is different.
Is all this only our timeline?
It depends on your belief.

Will a 5D portal open on March 21, 2019?
A date is a permission slip. If you fear something like a power outage, you project it (see S1 18.6).
Can we accelerate this with intention?
Yes. (Asked about water: distilled water in plastic bottles is fine.)

Stay positive; practise lucid dreaming and out-of-body work; think positive thoughts as you wake up.
Why is this happening now?
The threshold has been passed. Take action. (The answer also repeated the vaccine claims; see the safety note above.)

Positive: an activation, done with your permission.

See 8.6.
