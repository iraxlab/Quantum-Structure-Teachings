The big idea

Through the Council of Nine, members of an association of connective worlds said that when an attempt was made to reverse the spin of Earth's field and push it down into a lower frequency (the only spectrum where those forces can survive), a seventh-dimensional earth-ship gave three layers of signals that overrode it, so the grid realigned, the planet's field rebooted stronger, and its axis held to avoid great cataclysms. Help is always present; such manipulation "is not allowed any longer". Your part is to stay positive, stay out of the negative feed, meditate for the planet with love, and physically help in your own reality, because the collective and you are one projection.

#Key teachings

#35.1Read it from the plasma

Look at everything from the quantum field, from plasma energy; matter derives from plasma. As DP and S7 E22 said, when something very negative happens on Earth during ascension, consciousness activates everything possible, on or off planet, to help, because Earth, even its artificial timeline (see 8.9), is under consciousness's umbrella. Any frequency can be overridden.

#35.2Portals and their dangers

In her view, high-energy experiments can open and close portals to other dimensions by acting on the plasma core of the magnetic and gravitational fields, and some have tried to summon negative entities that way without knowing what they were doing. The negative needs Earth's 3D field in a certain position, like a radio signal, because it can only survive in that spectrum; the human is its frequency source.

#35.3Why it stays hard

Those forces can survive only in a certain spectrum, so they won't stop; they will keep bombarding. If at a given moment their potency outweighs the collective's, hard events can still happen; awareness and mass awakening then override them. Never think your opponent isn't clever: they make people fight each other while they don't.

#Questions answered

My wife and I reset the blueprint of Earth barefoot. Why does it matter?
You're working in the plasmatic field of consciousness; with positive intention you amplify the collective toward a positive trajectory. Meditate for the planet with the emotion of love (as in the subatomic and galactic meditations, replacing the galactic family with Earth), and your higher self will show you what to do. There's no one outside you: helping the collective helps you.
What are the best daily practices now?
Stay positive and away from negative mainstream and social feeds: each is a download of sequences that becomes a probability in you. Do your positive things all day. If you're in a hard situation, ask why you're there, call it a hologram to shut down fear, step back, then help where you can.
(Her example) How do we change the world?
By physically helping in your own reality: she noticed a teenager with ruined shoes and offered help, while others froze. Until we take a chance and act, nothing changes; sitting behind the bushes waiting for someone else lets the negative overtake your reality.
When you shift timelines, do the people around you shift too?
Both are true. They continue in another parallel reality as their old version, while you project a new version of them in your new reality, though it may take time as you mutate the frequencies you collected from them. You don't change them; you create a new version of yourself and project new versions of them. (The recording ends here.)
