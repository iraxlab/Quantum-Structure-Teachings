The big idea

#Key teachings

#10.1A prediction is a bylaw

Consciousness runs on a protocol of specific guidelines and frequency timelines (see S2 9.5). When something must be completed, "it's like a bylaw: it's written, this is definitely happening." That is the prediction: the end result. In between there are infinite probabilities, so the people, places and steps can change, and there may be a diversion, "the art of war": you aren't told every step (see S2 8.3, S2 9.3). So don't take any named person as 100%; they show you where the frequency is going.

Predictions come unasked, in their own time, and are passed on in the order given, not in date order. If someone given a task gets scared and doesn't complete it, there is always a plan B: ascending consciousness creates another holographic reality to complete the task (a topic, "hyper reality", announced for a later talk).

#10.2Your higher self stops what goes against humanity

Each soul splits into infinite versions, and also exists as a higher being in another reality. Because this is a sentient process, if you keep acting against humanity, you no longer match the frequency of the timeline, and your own higher self and guidance will sabotage that path in this reality, to make you stop and wake up. So anyone who continues to manipulate will find it doesn't work: they are blocked both by the structure itself and from within (see S2 7.6).

What do we bring back to source? You are the universe, so the experience is consciousness expanding itself: a game of creating something new every moment. Told of a breathtaking new planet, you'd want to go first; that is what consciousness does.

#10.10You are never in the same situation twice

Many questions miss the same link. Every moment is a new fractal reality, a new hologram of Earth (see S1 2.7). Going back to a place where things went badly, you are not going back to the same place: it is a brand-new version, built from the frequency you hold now. So why expect the old outcome? Expect the positive one: smile, enjoy, take the joy into it.

You fluctuate between a positive and a negative reality all day; in the end, whichever you hold more becomes the outcome (see S1 2.2, S1 3.3). Take it from the negative, and "don't be surprised if you end up in the same situation." Go back and study the first three sections of Season 1; it's time to practise.

#10.11Training is number one

Knowing the structure is the code; then you have to do it physically, because you are the matter structure, and "we can't just think about it without taking the action." Your next moment is the frequency of what you felt two seconds ago, so manage it all day. Catch a mood: notice the dense frequency, then make yourself do something to shift it (music, a smile, going out, a good thought). When someone attacks or criticizes you, catch the reaction, neutralize it (they are right from their own perspective), and re-set your thinking and action. Done every day, it builds a positive timeline for that day.

#10.12Neutralize the fear, don't feed it

Worried about harmful technologies or chemicals? Shift timelines instead of fighting: everything is frequency, and any chemical's frequency can be neutralized; the councils said they are doing this. You can't reject another reality, but you can neutralize it, give thanks, and construct a brand-new one, printing your reality from the new beginning, not from fear. Stop believing in the new and you project fear, "and don't be surprised if more things you see happening." Take practical action for your peace of mind, do everything you can from the heart for humanity, and let the end result take care of itself.

If you see a photo or video of a craft and know from within that it's real, you are tuning into its vibration: it is in your hologram because you are ready, and that is one form of contact. They are preparing us individually: one person sees something, another a video, another hears something, another has a personal encounter (see S2 9.2).

#The method — Daily Frequency Training

  1. Set reminders every 20 to 30 minutes: a phone alarm or stickers ("I'm happy, I'm joy").
  2. Check yourself: what am I thinking? What am I feeling? Am I happy?
  3. If you're low, shift it physically: music, a smile, a walk, a positive thought. Make yourself do it.
  4. Imagine the positive outcome for the situation you face, as a brand-new hologram, not the old one.
  5. Act from the positive.
  6. Repeat all day. Whatever you hold more becomes the outcome; at day's end, look back at the timeline you built.
  7. Celebrate: a happy dance after each session of study.
