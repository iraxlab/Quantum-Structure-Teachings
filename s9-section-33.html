The field of emotion is the number-one cause of poor manifestation and the hardest part to control (an October workshop goes deeper). The body is sovereign, a fractal of God: it can bilocate, construct and deconstruct, and leave the body to see other worlds without machinery. Consciousness experiments with other routes, like alchemy, but choose consciously. Surveillance infrastructure will be presented as safe and futuristic; she loves technology, and it can catch real wrongdoers, but ask: to what extent, and to whom, are you giving your sovereignty?

#33.2Who Nimrod was, and what the archetype means

In Genesis 10:8–12, Nimrod, great-grandson of Noah, is "a mighty hunter before the Lord", the first powerful ruler, founder of Babel and other Mesopotamian cities, traditionally credited with the Tower of Babel, when languages were confused. Language is sound and vibration: distort it and the original field becomes synthetic, as dark forces do now by reprinting the synthetic into your field. Tradition also calls him a hunter of men's souls. "Nimrod will be out soon" refers to "the spirit of Nimrod, the archetype of total dominion and rebellion against the divine order": centralized control over communication, commerce, movement and thought; "the modern Tower of Babel built not from bricks, but from information", data centers, AI and satellites. It could be a law, a mayor, a person or a system.

#33.3Hold them responsible

Watch who implements these structures (municipal offices, officials) and hold them responsible: that is acting from the Christ within. True people of any religion never commit harm; no faith justifies killing another version of you. Crises in different places aren't random: economic pressure, food, water control.

#33.4How truths are hijacked

Every truth has two spectrums. Compassion for animals can be hijacked to sell synthetic food the body doesn't recognize; "you don't need a car" can become a squeeze. To trap beings who can bilocate, you'd need a signal and a body that receives it; the same technology could create food forests and medicine. That's why the HR and nano information were given ahead: so you recognize the conversion. Her concern isn't technology but a few years in which it could be used to upload and entrap. Your own body can build neural bridges without a device; the choice is your free will.

#33.5Anger dissolves in the structure

Those at the top fight for control of the planet whoever is in office, feeding on humanity's etheric field and emotion, and won't let benevolent ETs free us. Consciousness is infiltrating from within: souls trapped through generations want out. Seen through the quantum structure, anger dissolves and you process analytically. When the evil grabs at you, are you strong enough to say no and build from the ground up? Then consciousness lifts you like a magnet. Accept those who chose otherwise: some sacrificed themselves as examples and help later as etheric legions, which is what's happening with families.

#33.6No one can tell your future 100%

Ascension activates the body and the 12-strand DNA through sequences; you begin to see other versions and realities, perhaps an ET experience in a forest, and only you will know. Don't give your power to anyone to tell you how your reality should be: a psychic's vision can fractally change with what you do tomorrow. She can see a percentage ("70%; you need to do more here"), but promising 100% would be a lie.

#The method — Navigating the transition (as given)

  1. Preserve your God-given body; do not consent from fear.
  2. Ask of every convenience: to whom am I giving my sovereignty?
  3. Find who implements control locally, and hold them responsible.
  4. Look at the structure until anger dissolves; analyze calmly.
  5. Say no when grabbed, and build from the ground up.
  6. Don't give your future to any psychic; act and shift it yourself.

#Questions answered

Why is lucid dreaming so difficult, and why does it throw off my concentration?
It took her years to perfect automatic writing; lucid dreaming can take months, and stress or family drama makes concentration hard. Don't be discouraged; keep practicing until the potency builds. Record automatic writing with date and time, without expectations: early downloads may not manifest. Do the nightly practice for three months and "something will manifest"; you don't know when, how or from where, because there's no time. Precision comes from constructing and deconstructing, again and again.
How do you collapse your desired timeline?
It is very complex: start with Jumpstart for Beginners and the galactic meditation for manifestation. In short, the collapse happens positive or negative according to your thoughts, emotions and what you choose to manifest.
