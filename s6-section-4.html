#Key teachings

#4.1What happened

After eating bad fish she became very sick. In the shower (water washes off frequency) she used the galactic meditation method on her body: she saw her body as a perfect light hologram, raised love and joy, affirmed "I'm healthy, I'm okay" and breathed that frequency in and out like a torus. Because she is now trained to be precise, she drew in far too much: within a minute she heard "it's too much", started shaking, and her temperature rose from 37 to 38 °C. The fever was her body rebalancing to the higher resonance; it settled within an hour. She drank water, took a probiotic and vitamin D. She had tapped her original (Pleiadian) blueprint, much higher than her current Earth resonance.

The message: holographic imagery replicator technology

"In the secondary body of your new hologram expression, we will replicate your body frequency in a parallel structural hologram. That will mimic all your biophotonic resonance to a perfect replica. And then slowly, we will integrate that secondary version of you into this body right now, 3% every day until it's 100% complete. That last 1% will build total readjustment to make sure that nothing is off balance. That is why when you use the holographic replicator imagery, you do it slowly, and then you allow 1% to adjust in fluctuation, fractally."

#4.2Why three

33 days at 3% makes 99%; the last 1% is fitted to the person's frequency. Three is fundamental: after three days on water the body turns ketogenic and starts healing; the trinity structure is very powerful and balancing. Compare green juice: start with half a cup, because two cups at once are so potent you get nauseous; energy works the same way.

#4.3Other ways it's done

ETs can also take the frequency of a healthy earlier version of you (as a child, say) from your "frames" and replicate it now; or copy a percentage of your biophotonic structure into a hologram on a spacecraft, work on it, and integrate it back (1 + 1 into one new version). Organic intelligence helps hold the image in the field; you already use AI in an MRI. Accept technology used positively, but never synthetic implants that make you a cyborg (S5 44).

#4.4Water as the conductor

She went onto water only, because each water molecule carries and embeds the new frequency through every cell like an electrical current, turning the body into an antenna.

Added safety note

Fever, shaking and food poisoning can be serious. Don't replace medical care with this practice: see a doctor if symptoms are severe or persist, and stay hydrated.

#The method — drawing on your perfect hologram, gently

  1. Prepare: water helps; a shower washes off frequency.
  2. See your perfect body as a light hologram (the galactic meditation method, your body in place of the galactic family).
  3. Raise love and joy and know you are healthy.
  4. Breathe it in and out like a torus, but in small doses: if you hear or feel "too much", stop.
  5. Repeat daily, slowly: like 3% a day, letting the body adjust.
  6. Support the body: water, rest, and medical help when needed.

#Questions answered

What are dimensions and densities, simply? In what realm does dreaming happen?
Your thoughts, emotions and deeds (a trinity) give a resonance, a location in time-space. Simply: one reality is like swimming through water, another like flying through air. Where you dream depends on your vibration as you fall asleep; each dream shift is a different Earth. If lucid, stay in the heart and just observe, because one burst of excitement wakes you; then shift thought and emotion to open a portal.
When I visualize, I affirm and imagine but can't feel it physically. How do I raise my body's vibration?
Start the day with gratitude for everything you have (even a job that isn't ideal: it's your stepping stone), which levels the field. Then do Marina's day (S6 1): one joyful thing after another, all day, consistently, so the heart's resonance becomes permanent. Without action, your negative holograms are more potent and block what you call in.
