The big idea

Your body is created by a sound wave in the ether, at a location; so you can reconstruct it. You don't need a machine: in deep relaxation, with total knowing and a strong feeling of love and joy, you go to the location of the cells, recall your original DNA and restore it. Then you must act as that new version of yourself (clean food, no consent to what harms you), or you lower the frequency and recall the old program. Combine it with the diet, the galactic meditation and the potency protocol.

#Key teachings

Everything is sound and frequency; when a density is put into a location you shift timelines. All the geometry and sound waves already exist in the ether above the 3D matter structure, and your job is to download them. But you can't tap in if you don't think it is possible. #1.2How she was taught

For a year the hybrids took her through an elimination diet, then 15 days only on fresh juices and shakes (not powders alone: live juice holds a higher resonance; powders can be added). Energy goes up and down the first month; avoid heavy workouts so the energy goes to rebuilding. After the 15 days the hybrids had her lie down, relax, and "switch your DNA from food to water": she saw a structure with hieroglyph-like numbers dissolve into a white-blue liquid mist. Earlier, the Council of Nine had restructured her heart: she felt it lifted out, hovered above her body for a few seconds, and was told "this is how death is"; her heart now vibrates above the 3D structure, while she still sees the 3D.

#1.3The heart opens the portals

Your heart accumulates resonance from the ether and will know the sequences that open a portal to a location (for example, your galactic family). That is why diet matters: food from killed animals is a low sequence. She recommends Frank Chester's work on the chestahedron. Your DNA is a vessel of sequences; the heart converts and opens the structure so you can bilocate.

Her instructions: her fasting sequence ("what I did, not what you should do")

After the elimination diet: three days only distilled water; then three days of distilled water with about a tablespoon of honey whenever hungry (the Council said honey levels the blood sugar); then three more days of distilled water only. Diabetics only under clinical supervision (she names the Tree of Life clinic in Arizona). Always a holistic, open-minded MD and blood work. Listen to your body: if the frequency drops, the hybrids told her to add spinach, a banana water shake, or go back to fish for two weeks.

Added safety note

Juice-only phases and water fasts are risky, especially with diabetes, pregnancy, an eating disorder, low weight or medication. She insists on medical supervision and blood work; please follow it, and stop if you feel unwell.

#1.4Marina's day

If you find it hard to bring up love and joy: get up and do whatever you most feel like doing in that moment (coffee, a call, the park, making ice cream), physically, until you no longer feel like it; then the next thing. All day you hold and accumulate your highest excitement, without obligations. Express love when you feel it; if it's rejected, that person isn't for you; don't try to overtake anyone's consciousness. Learn to switch into strong love on demand, like an actor.
