The big idea

The Galactic Meditation taught by the Council of Nine is the structure of manifestation itself. The mind builds a blueprint in your hologram; the heart, at the same time, zaps it with feeling like a laser, so the two frequencies form a magnetic and gravitational field (1+1=3); you inflate it with more and more joy to a summit, hold it, then drop to zero and leave it vibrating in the ether, ready to manifest. What brings it into matter is daily action from the knowing that it already exists, without expectation and without ever giving up. Most manifestations fail because the heart wasn't included, or the vibration was let subside to zero.

#Key teachings

#9.1A wake-up moment for everyone

The Council said that in the months ahead the negative and positive would clash with such potency that, at the zero point between them, many still asleep would be "smacked in the face" by a situation: "that moment is to wake you up". Put the ego aside, even as a scientist or doctor, and question what doesn't make sense. Knowing something is manipulation and still doing it out of fear is consenting, and being "a puppet": "that consciousness does not recognize compassion". "If you go into power and you say I will not consent... and we do this as a collective, game over for the negative side." (A years-old forecast, noted briefly.)

#9.2The structure of the Galactic Meditation

Behind the meditation is the quantum structure: how the magnetic and gravitational field combine for manifestation in the ether. It uses the galactic family as its example; once you've learned it, "remove the galactic family" and put in whatever you wish to manifest: marriage, love, money, anything (see S3 8).

  1. The blueprint. Be calm and somewhere quiet (in nature, by a waterfall, at the beach). From your mind, create the scenario in your own hologram, "like a movie in your mind".
  2. Include the heart. While holding the imagery, start feeling, putting emotion into the image at the same time. The two resonances together create a magnetic and gravitational field in the blueprint; the feeling zaps it "like a laser", and it starts to vibrate, a geometry holding numbers (7, 3, 9, 4...).
  3. Increase the vibration. Keep the image and keep adding more and more potent feeling (joy, love, gratitude), until goosebumps come and tears of joy run down. Each increase inflates the hologram bigger, "like a balloon".
  4. Hold the summit. When you can no longer increase the feeling, hold that highest potency, with the image, for about a minute or 30 seconds.
  5. Drop it. When you can't hold it any more, drop the frequency "literally to zero". The ether structure you activated keeps vibrating in your hologram: "now it is ready for manifestation."

Then live it. After you drop it, let it vibrate as much as it wants, but to keep it from subsiding you must continue the motion by taking action daily, knowing it already exists ("you cannot see this reality if it doesn't exist with you already") and that you're creating it at the same time (see S1 1, S2 13). How long, who, and through whom, you don't know: infinite probabilities. Play hide and seek: even if somebody denies you 10 times, override it 11 (see S4 2.5).

#9.3Why manifestations fail

"I was manifesting, but nothing is going on." Because you never held the vibration: you thought or meditated about it, never included the heart, never held with consistency the belief that it's already there, and dropped your frequency, so the blueprint subsided to zero; and you never took action. Don't have expectations or conditions (see S1 5). When a person rejects your idea, it's because they don't resonate with your blueprint: "Thank you so much. I'm going to try a different approach. Who do I call next?" "Even if it takes you five months."

#9.4How practice shrinks the time

When something important was lost at home, she didn't panic: she went to zero point, neutral, focused like a laser, saw in the blueprint where it had gone, and projected a reality in which it was found, without knowing who would find it or how. It was found within a minute. Panic would have lowered her vibration and projected a negative next earth. Because she has practised so long, the potency of her feeling is now like a laser and her belief is 100%, so small things manifest almost at once; something very big may take a year of practice, of meeting the right people and learning more. "More you practise, more your consciousness is resonating in that potency", until a thought becomes reality easily.

Stay in the positive in small ways. Bored? "Why are you complaining about it? Take an action": she took up crochet from YouTube videos. Your body is the vessel, and the state of your heart pulses 360 degrees through your field non-stop; by its potency, your reality is determined.

#Questions answered

In step three, while increasing joy and love, are we still imagining what we pictured in step one?
"Absolutely." Through all the steps hold the image and the emotion together: the mind puts the blueprint into the field, the heart activates it like a laser to pulse in the ether: 1+1=3. To become matter it then needs consistent action, never giving up.
How do I know which step I'm on?
Step one: you imagine. Two: you feel toward the image (love, joy, gratitude). Three: you increase it, "like an actor", engaging your whole body until goosebumps and tears of joy. Then hold, and drop. To practise, put a photo of someone you love (or a house, or money) in front of you and focus on it, feeling how much you love them, for as long as you can; you don't need the picture, but use one if it helps. "The focus is the potency."
What if I can't bring up joy on command? Life has been hard. Will staying relatively positive work?
Practise daily: stay away from negative media, films and thoughts, and shift anything negative. Learn to feel it like an actor. Go back to an image of real happiness (someone you truly love, a childhood moment when everyone was laughing) and pull that frequency into the now: "every single one of us already knows how to be happy"; it's in your memory bank. Capture it and train with it.
Do we need to reach the summit, drop it and act more than once, or is once enough?
Once could be enough, if you then act daily without giving up and without doubt or conditions: it must be 100% belief. Otherwise repeat it, and practise with Season 3, episode 8, until you get it. But repetition isn't enough without physical action: "Oh, I pray for you... how is that going to crystallize when you didn't take the action for it?" Action changes your inner reality and the collective in your reality.
Does it work for a state of mind, a high level of consciousness, without specific circumstances?
Yes. Visualize yourself extremely happy, call in "right now I have everything I need", feel it, and see the earth and people happy too. Then, daily, keep switching your emotion to positive through challenges: "thank you very much, this is just a hologram... what can I do right now to switch it?" Practised, it becomes the normal state of your heart, holding the 5D frequency. Respect those in fear, and don't judge or dictate what others should do: judging is rejecting another version of yourself, and your reality mirrors it.
