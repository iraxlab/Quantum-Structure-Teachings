The big idea

Every moment you split into two realities and collapse a third from them (see S1 2), positive or negative by what you choose and feel. A negative structure can only survive with a host, so it works by redirecting your attention into chaos and fear: drop your frequency and you've been pulled into its timeline. It can win a moment, but it can never win in the end, because it is also part of consciousness, and consciousness keeps plastering new holograms to sabotage it until the positive collapses by its own weight. Your job: hold the ground, fractal moment by fractal moment.

#Key teachings

#2.1The fractal collapse, again

Whatever you create in the now becomes a platform of a hologram, with its own geometry, numbers and sound vibration. Put thought and emotion into it and it splits into a second holographic imprint; the two run simultaneously, fluctuating, and between them you collapse a third structure: "1+1 is not 2" (see S1 2, S2 1). If you choose to stay in the negative emotion, the outcome you see is on the negative platform. This is only one moment; the next moment you shift again, and again. "More positive it goes, more it goes up."

#2.2Permanent timelines, and how to climb out

Consciousness that behaves constantly in the negative creates a permanent timeline, which still has infinite layers of probability realities fluctuating within it. To get out, you create positive fractal moments: choose positive in this moment, then in the next, then the next, so that the collapses keep shifting you toward the positive timeline, and toward the fifth-dimensional structure consciousness is moving into. "No matter what you see... stay in the positive, in neutrality" (see S1 3, S3 11).

#2.3How a timeline is hijacked

When a positive outcome has collapsed and many people hold it, someone who knows the structure does something shocking. Your attention goes into chaos, the emotion of the commotion drops your frequency, "and what happened? They're bringing you into their timeline of reality". A negative consciousness can survive only parasitically, with a host, and the host is the human, vibrating as a holographic imprint. In the past, people who chose black magic opened portals through which such consciousness came in, set the positive soul aside, took over, and taught technology to hijack. So when you panic without knowing this, you can get locked into their structure: "that's the goal". "You cannot judge it": it's how they survive. But you can maneuver yourself by knowing the structure.

#2.4Your body is the biggest technology

"The biggest technology is not free energy. The biggest technology is your human body", made from resonances of the magnetic and gravitational field, a whole specific code. Implanting things in it and changing its DNA changes the vessel you shift through the timelines with. Cut off from consciousness, the negative builds artificial timelines through technology, opening portals into the field where only it can survive, "but in order for them to survive, they need you" (see S2 1.12).

#2.5Consciousness sabotages the negative

The negative side can never succeed in the end, "because you are also part of consciousness", and consciousness can never self-destruct. If the negative collapses something, the next time consciousness plasters another hologram into its field: somebody finds the laptops, somebody finds the proof. "You may try 10 times and you may succeed. But the 11th time, everything is going to collapse" to the positive. So it goes, plan B, plan C, plan D, to infinity, until the positive has such potency that the negative collapses by its own weight (see S3 30.7). The end result is known; "we don't know fractally who and how, where": that is why predictions can seem to fail when a collapse is hijacked, and why she asks you not to lose trust in the material.

#2.6If you are on the negative side

"If you are seeing my video and if you're being trapped... you've been given the choice." Have no fear: what you need to save is your soul. Your body structure may mutate, but your soul can go through the lineage of the fifth-dimensional structure; she was told they will "plaster holograms" for those who were manipulated and choose to switch to the positive, so they can continue ascension. A hijack captures "the lineage of the timelines you are creating": if you never clear it, the next time you're born you're on the negative platform. So clear every platform, daily, moment to moment, to build potency toward the fifth dimension.

#2.7Stay off the battlefield of the media

Constant talk of who will be president and "it's over now" is frequency manipulation; the social media matrix is "a holographic imprint from the negative side... put in daily through light". The moment you watch and get upset, you drop to negative density. Instead, shift the emotion with a positive action: "Thank you very much, I know what you're doing, but you know what? I'm going to take my family, go walk in a park", have dinner with them, go to your hometown, gather your money and pay off a home "so I am not part of the system anymore". Ask: in the woods with no Wi-Fi, would you even know? "Do you see who controls you?"

#The method — Holding the Ground

  1. Notice the hook: news or a shock that pulls your attention into chaos and fear.
  2. Pull back: "I know what you're doing"; step away from the media (2.7).
  3. Release if needed: cry it out to the numb, neutral zero point (see the grief question).
  4. Shift the emotion: watch something positive for at least 15 minutes at a time, then disengage from social media so the vibration stays continuous.
  5. Take a positive action: a walk, a dinner with family, a step toward freedom from the system.
  6. Feel the outcome now, and repeat moment to moment: positive fractal after positive fractal.

The field, from your consciousness into positive emotional sequences. Shift the emotion by watching something positive, a minimum of 15 minutes at a time, then disengage from social media so you hold the vibration continuously for a positive outcome.
