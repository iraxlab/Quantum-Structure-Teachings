Q & A: Distilled Water and the Council of Nine

The big idea

A scientist asked how distilled water could be "negatively charged" when chemically it's neutral. The council agreed it is neutral, and explained that in the body it mimics the stronger frequency (such as toxins), which changes its charge and lets it carry them out; water, they said, has memory. Structured water, by contrast, must be measured for each person. Around this, the talk repeats its own limits: it shares the teacher's experience, not advice; work with doctors; never rely on it instead of medical care. It also gives a short message on money (a secure new system; "keep it cool"), and explains that during sleep you co-create in other realities, which you can learn to recall.

Read this first

This section reports a teaching that contradicts mainstream science. Distilled water is safe to drink, but there is no scientific evidence that it pulls toxins or medicines out of the body, cures infections or reverses disease. Never stop or change a prescribed medicine (for thyroid, asthma or anything else) without your doctor, and never use fasting or water in place of treatment for an infection, cancer or any serious illness. The talk itself repeatedly says: "this is not a substitute for professional medical advice."

#Key teachings

#17.1The question, and the council's answer

The question, sent by a scientist: distilled water is pure H2O; its positive and negative charges cancel, so it is neutral. What is missing? The council: "You are correct: the water molecule appears to be neutral." However, with the spinning of each person's molecular structure, distilled water "becomes negative" when it meets unnatural toxins in the body: a ripple effect, as it mimics the stronger frequency. "What humans are failing to understand is that water has memory and is able to reconstruct the system of a new platform of structures." Because distilled water starts neutral, it corrects and pulls out "only the toxins", whose frequency is stronger than the body's natural compounds.

The council proposed an experiment: shine light through two glasses of water, one with a substance in it; within days, they said, the plain glass will mimic the other. A second scientist, asked by the teacher, agreed from his perspective: the spin rate of the distilled water's atoms "rejects that which does not belong." The teacher also pointed to research on water's memory.

From the booksDP and SD both print this exact exchange, including the two-glass experiment and the second scientist's reply. HR had earlier stated: "Distilled water has (-) charge and is measurement match for the portal."

#17.2Distilled or structured water?

Distilled water is said to pick up exactly what needs to come out of your body. Structured, magnetized or spun water is "a different implementation": you'd need to measure your molecular structure first to know whether you need it and how much (the earlier structured-water example used set frequencies for a fixed duration, for everyone, to keep the body in a normal state; toxins still have to come out). And no water replaces responsibility: eat well, detox carefully, and don't keep taking in toxins expecting water to fix it. Keep distilled water pure; you can pray over it or meditate with crystals around it, since it mimics your thoughts.

#17.3The teacher's own experience

With pneumonia, she was told by her guidance to drink only distilled water for three days and eat nothing, and she recovered. Her understanding: the bacteria had the stronger frequency, and the neutral water mimicked it and carried it out. Her homeopath and doctors disagreed ("it will deplete your nutrients"); she researched, experimented on herself under medical supervision, and shares it only as her experience: "I am in no case advising anybody to do what I did."

Added safety note

Pneumonia can be life-threatening; it needs prompt medical care and often antibiotics. Don't fast or rely on water to treat an infection.

#17.4Medicines and repairing the body

Her belief: a strong pharmaceutical compound can sustain the body without helping it repair its own tissue, so some people stay on medication for life; she chose, with her doctors monitoring her blood tests, to try to repair tissue through detox and diet. But she is clear: in a life-threatening situation, use the medication; do what's necessary to survive. Her hope is that medical and natural medicine work "in coalition, as one unit", and that we stay cautious and informed about side effects.

Added safety note

Thyroid medicine, inhalers and other prescribed treatments must not be stopped or reduced without your doctor; doing so can be dangerous.

#17.5Research creates the answers

When something doesn't make sense, search sincerely, and you create a reality in which the answer appears. (When her son was ill and advice contradicted what her family had always known, she went to a library; her hand fell on a book that addressed exactly her question.) "The truth sets you free, because it's the higher vibration." But use logic: "anybody can claim anything. Even in this video, I can claim anything." Research, test, and make your own decisions (see S2 11.10).

#17.6Working with doctors

Many people feel doctors dismiss them. The teacher still urges working with them, because only a medical doctor can order the tests that show what's really happening; she found an open-minded doctor willing to run the tests she asked about, and monitored every change. Natural practitioners and doctors each have a part.

#17.7Stones in their purest form

Before 2012, her guidance asked her to buy uncut stones for energetic work, not pre-cut or shaped ones: the potency is in the purest form, and shaping may change a stone's properties. Likewise, keep water pure rather than adding compounds (such as shungite) to distilled water.

#17.8A new money message

"Propaganda will not be allowed." Money will be gold-backed, with a reset "soon": "keep it cool", don't worry. Whatever the economy does, don't panic; "the economy is going to be safe." A new, secure system is already in place; money will be returned to the people. "No one knows" how it will play out (see S2 10.3).

Added note

These are predictions, not financial advice. Remarks about a criminal case and an online conspiracy source are left out.

#17.9What happens while you sleep

Realities run like different channels. When you sleep, the structure splits: while your body keeps sleeping, your higher self co-creates another reality (for example, with your galactic family), and both run at once. Your memory may not hold it, because you're not allowed yet or it isn't time. Lucid dreaming creates yet another reality in between; even then, you may not be allowed into certain places (the teacher was twice sent back from a spacecraft: "you are not allowed to be here") until it's your time and your frequency matches (see S2 15).

To remember: on waking, write down whatever you recall, then sit back and let your thoughts guide you ("what were you doing? Try again"). You couldn't see those structures if you hadn't experienced them; you're recalling what you co-created.

#The method — Recalling Your Night

  1. Before you move, on waking, notice any image or feeling left from the night.
  2. Write it down, even a fragment.
  3. Sit back quietly and ask: "What was I doing? Where was I?"
  4. Let the thoughts come; if it's vague, "try again", tuning in more each time.
  5. Ask what it was for, and how you might use it.
  6. Trust it: you are recalling something you co-created, not inventing it.

#Questions answered

Isn't structured and magnetized water better for self-healing?
A different implementation: see 17.2. It needs measuring for you.
Does distilled water pull out toxins from food and pollution, if there's more of it in the body?
That is the council's answer (see 17.1). The teacher added her own view on thyroid medicines and tissue repair (see 17.4).
If water takes on the stronger frequency, wouldn't it take on the toxin?
Yes: that's the point: it takes the toxin's frequency and carries it out.
Doesn't distilled water deplete nutrients?
The talk says it pulls out what has the stronger frequency, meaning toxins, not nutrients.
Added note

Drinking distilled water is generally safe, but it contains no minerals; a balanced diet provides them.

Can I purify rainwater by spinning it clockwise with my finger and intention?
It depends on how long and how you spin it; it would need measuring. That's why distilled water was recommended: it's neutral.
Added note

Rainwater can carry bacteria and pollutants; boil, filter or treat it before drinking.

I structure my distilled water and add quartz crystals. Should I stop?
Keep it distilled, "because it's a conductor". You can pray over it or meditate with crystals around it; it mimics the wave of your thoughts. Any structured water has to be measured: the structured-water protocol of 3 Hz, 70 Hz and 90 Hz, 20 minutes each, is the duration for everybody, to keep the body in a normal state. On water's memory she points to the Japanese scientist Masaru Emoto, who showed that water is a conductor and has memory.
Should a cleanse be supported with distilled rather than filtered water?
"This is what I did."
How much of distilled water's effect is intention, as with homeopathy?
It depends on the person, which is why the teacher had both a homeopathic and a medical doctor checking her blood work. When her thyroid results improved, her doctor wouldn't claim it was possible: doctors can't endorse what they haven't studied.
Will distilled water pull out medications?
"Yes, slowly, within ten days", with no food for the first three days. (A claim; see the note at the top of this section, and never stop medication without your doctor.)
I charge my water with a plate device and sunlight, write "love" on the jug and add lemon, lime and ginger. Any suggestions?
"That is completely doable. Keep going with that; it works for you."
Unless we cleanse, are we all walking around with toxins?
Not necessarily; everyone is different. But with toxins in the air and food, most of us carry some. Eat healthily and consider detoxing; and structured water only with personal measurement. As with a painkiller, you don't keep taking something once the body no longer needs it.
Isn't there "distilled water" in fruit, such as watermelon and apple?
Yes, but it carries the vibration of the fruit's own molecular structure.
I had hypothyroidism, now hyperthyroidism, and take no medicine. What water could help?
"You definitely need a doctor's supervision." Get tested. The teacher found her own thyroid problem early through a specific antibody test (generic tests looked normal), and improved with an elimination diet, juicing and distilled water over about a year, under monitoring. If the gland is already destroyed, she can't promise repair, and medication may be necessary.
I have cervical cancer. Is a three-day distilled-water fast enough?
"No." Medical intervention and supervision are fundamental; there are clinics with both medical and homeopathic doctors. "Don't do it only by yourself."
Added safety note

Cancer needs treatment from an oncology team. Fasting or water cannot treat it, and delaying treatment can cost lives.

What does drinking distilled water with shungite do? Does it change DNA?
"Very much so", but shungite also neutralizes, being a different compound. Not recommended: keep the water pure, so it mimics what you need (see 17.7).
Are the carbon filters in water distillers dangerous?
It depends on manufacturing: chemical compounds can leak into the water. Use the purest carbon. Carbon and sand in layers purify water but don't distil it.
Doctors often dismiss me. Why go?
See 17.6: you need them for proper tests.
How does the structure work while we sleep? Some days I wake to drama.
See 17.9.
