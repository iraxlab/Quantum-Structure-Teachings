#5.2Why some souls exit

When Earth rises in resonance, those who couldn't hold or transform the new frequency, because they failed too many lessons, stay low and lock into the 3D platform; they choose by synchronicity to leave. Above the platform they shield and send frequency to help Earth's ascension, appear in dreams, or place thoughts for family members. Ascension then happens from within (those who stay) and from outside (those who left): a coherent yin and yang. Don't judge them: "thank you for your service." (See her video on the etheric legions, S4.)

#5.3Hold 5D while seeing 3D

Through daily acts for humanity, one person at a time, your consciousness moves up fractally until it holds the 5D structure permanently; you'll still see what 3D does, but you maneuver so your outcomes land in 5D. People who didn't consent and created reality within the reality were often left alone. When the negative is brewing around you, she was told to "stay undercover": don't provoke; pull away and build your solutions.

#5.4Be brave and share

No more hiding for fear of losing your job: transformation must be 100%, not 99%. Share what you know; even one person reached holds your whole universe. Allow those who don't get it, or who chose to comply, to exist; you can't overtake anyone's consciousness. Calm yourself by understanding the structure.

#Questions answered

How does what people believe play into their experience?
(Channelled.) Preconditioning splits the consciousness like a double personality: one part connected to the soul, one artificial; the more potent one takes over the belief system and the higher self becomes secondary, disconnected. "You call it brainwashing; we call it disengaging from the system of the higher self."
When we move to the fifth density, will we leave this planet and come back later?
All realities run at once. Some will have hard experiences, some will glide through. Connected with your higher self, you'll be given platforms; some will bilocate to their galactic family and return. Going from one country to another is the same as reaching your galactic family; only the resonance differs. The diet and protocols prepare body and consciousness to hold it. There is no future; it's all now.
How can we heal ourselves energetically? Is it the manifestation protocol?
Yes. It is given fractally, bit by bit, so you don't "pop out of orbit": each lesson builds bridges between neurons (wave genetics) until they form a tree of life; then manifesting gets easier. Study from the beginning; dizziness or sleepiness means take it slower.
When someone joins the etheric legions, where do they go?
Those with great trauma first rest in special chambers, like a coma, receiving a slow murmur frequency for a day or a few weeks. Then they meet their galactic family, higher selves or councils by resonance, review their life, and decide how to help (downloads, dream appearances, placed thoughts). Those fully disengaged from consciousness return to dust: consciousness recycles. Only the strongest continue into 5D to rebuild from the ground up. "Have no fear."
Are we all fractals of one oversoul or soul family?
Of one consciousness, which reprints itself into infinite realities that also split into similar and different frequencies. A soul can hold higher-dimensional downloads by accumulation and lineage. Everything, even a computer, is a different modality of consciousness; don't reject it through fear.
