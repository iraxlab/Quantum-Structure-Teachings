#Questions answered

Will time speed up and manifestation get faster?
Yes: time becomes like a laser or warp hole. The more coherent your daily expressions, the more precise the collapse, faster and faster; you collapse in time, because time is an illusion.
How do we share truth when 99% aren't open?
Always through compassion, never taking anyone's free will. Express it politely; if rejected, let it be. At home you can simply watch a documentary in the shared space; it penetrates their field. Later someone else, elsewhere, will say something similar, and it will match like a magnet. Family members usually accept it more easily from someone outside the family.
What is real? Everything seems planned, like a game.
Observe in neutrality, where no input or output goes into the field; then analyse and act with compassion. It is a game in the sense of the quantum structure, but respect your field: if you don't take it seriously, nothing gains the potency to manifest. Balance.
What is the purpose of this collective experiment?
Consciousness learning what is possible, like an alchemist. Each split gets its own free will; when one drifts so far it believes itself disconnected, consciousness mobilizes to correct it. You are another version even of the darkest; don't point fingers, mutate. A strong, precise resonance can't be overtaken.
What can we do daily to align with Earth's new vibration?
Expression from the heart: compassion, giving, sharing, Marina's day, and "What can I do for humanity today?" You'll see the ugliness too, but everything will line up, and people of similar resonance will come.
Some of us are opened to love and life is lovely. What is our part?
You chose to hold a stabilized pocket of positive resonance, like a metal rod in the ground, amid the fluctuation. Be proud of it: every expression has its place.
Why does it have to be a hide-and-seek game?
Because the negative bombards your hologram, sometimes you must retreat to preserve your field and sometimes come forward to mutate; you wouldn't shout the opposite in the middle of a riot. Be cautious and aware: a chess game.
Is there a difference between living in your heart centre and in front of it?
A big one. You project from within; what's in front is the hologram, the illusion that someone outside controls you. Check how you feel first; that knowing never changes.
Why do we forget past lives? How do we reopen the connection?
Connection comes when your accumulated resonance matches your higher connections (for example Pleiadian family); then knowledge comes in droplets until a lucid dream or an epiphany proves it. More than 80% are praying for something different. Whether they let ego stop it is free will.
Do we create our ascension through positive thinking?
Every thought, even negative, becomes a reality. As consciousness moves up, negative structures are no longer compatible; each is given chance after chance to mutate, "to the last of its breath"; by their free will they will be their undoing. That is a bylaw. Don't fear takeover.
When we protest and share truth, people think it's hate. What happens to the vibration?
Truth is a resonance: true for one, ludicrous for another. Present it and let them choose; your heart's coherence shows you whether it's right for you now.
I live more from peace than love. Is there a difference?
Very similar resonances with subtle subtonalities: love is the ultimate power of all resonances in coherence, and peace is a modality of pure love.
