Message from God

Breaking through the barrier between realities by accumulating the positive; how a true download sounds; the message "never doubt the power"; staying on target; and questions on whom to trust, intention and DNA, the Merkaba, dreams, psychics, detox, death, and whether the Archons are AI (live talk, April 2021)

The big idea

When you pray or meditate you project not only into your own platform but into the collective, God consciousness, and each shift changes the numbers and geometry. Between your reality and a higher one is a barrier, a resonance like a symphony; to download from above you must mutate that signal by accumulating the positive, through the heart. The message received: "Know that God is in power... never doubt the power." While both realities play out, stay on target in the positive, act, and don't panic at what you're shown.

#Key teachings

#16.1Breaking through the barrier

The matrix is always a sphere, 360 degrees; expand it and the whole structure can shift into a new one (see S4 13.1). Acting in the positive raises your signal from, say, 3D toward 5D; the barrier between is a fractal resonance "covering your whole hologram", and to pass it you must mutate the whole signal, accumulating positive. Then information flows back and forth, from higher consciousness to you and from you to it: codes, numbers ("03101, anything"), at each latitude and altitude you move to (see S2 15).

#16.2How a true download sounds

When she asks a question, the answer comes back "like a boomerang": a thought that is very steady, "no static", systematic, "like the doom, the doom, the doom". That steadiness is how you recognize a download (see S4 11, catching the first thought). She asked because people were hearing that the negative side was taking over; many channels were saying similar things.

The message

"To everyone: know that God is in power, and the negative is defeated. Never doubt, never doubt the power, never." "Know that I, God, am always in control. Stay on target. Be good and sleep well at night. All the glory you shall all see in the daytime. And at night-time, I will strike the serpent at his feast."

"Rise up, humans, God wins", with a geometry she was shown for it.

She was shown that when the bad looks good and the good looks bad, in complete chaos, "when you're least expecting", it would be "enough is enough": a massive shift. Times were given as probabilities (months of 2021 and March 2022), with new laws and new systems, the rest blocked; they are years old and only noted. The point: "we should only do the positive stuff, we should help each other out... without worrying when and how." Don't doubt the power each time something is projected to frighten you: hold the truth and act (see S4 2, S4 8).

#16.3Stay within, and act

"It's a battle time now." Go more within; stay off social media, and off blaming this side or that. Do positive things. If you help people, "don't point fingers": give information to those who are ready for it, usually through synchronicity (someone mentions something; you answer what they ask).

#16.4Last week's code, decoded

A listener decoded the K3 code through Hebrew gematria, as advised (see S4 15). It led her to a teaching she had heard: words are frequency and sound, so constant chanting of certain words can hold the vibration of a negative (Archon) structure, and she understood that such a programme had been shut down: "the devious signals are stopped." Her instruction: look into the numbers and letters through gematria and geometry yourself, and see what the message is (see S4 12.3).

#The method — Staying on Target

  1. Notice the doubt when something frightening is projected at you.
  2. Remember the message: "Never doubt the power... stay on target."
  3. Go within: off social media; no blaming either side.
  4. Accumulate the positive: help, give, act in the name of humanity.
  5. Share only with those who are ready, as synchronicity brings them.
  6. Be good and sleep well: don't worry about when and how.

#Questions answered

I'm getting downloads in sleep, lucid dreams and meditation. Who is sending them, and why?
Three consciousnesses are engaged with you: "one is from above, one is from below and one is from the side". Engage more; keep asking, and answers will be given in your dream state.
There's so much information (injections, nanotechnology, white hats, the matrix). What matters most?
Deception will come even from those you trust most, even channellers. If information doesn't resonate, research more: start looking physically, and synchronicity will bring what you need. "Do not follow blindly, even us": study and see if you can manifest from it. Speculation shows let you choose the collective reality; stay with positive information. When someone is off balance with your belief system, stop listening and find another source, and move on again if they change. Give from the heart without expectations (energy philanthropy), but don't fall into ego, thinking you're more than another. Peel the layers of illusion.
I dreamed we were pulsing energy from the future to help Earth. Is that possible?
Yes: you do a lot in your dream state, astral projection too. "You are a very busy bunch of people at night."
When we pray and set intentions for a new timeline, can it change our DNA?
Yes: each new reality may bring a new DNA. But you carry memory frequencies from the previous fractal for continuity, especially lessons you're learning, or you wouldn't know where you are. Changing DNA directly needs constructing and deconstructing the exact frequency at the right latitude and altitude: "you're almost learning to fly from one reality to another".
Can we live in alignment with divine purpose without knowing it?
Yes: "you don't doubt anything, you simply are, and that is the ultimate."
Are the Merkaba and Metatron's Cube tools for ascension, or part of the negative structure?
Both: just frequency, geometry, number sequences. What decides is the thought and intention you put into them; with it they move and mutate, to construct or deconstruct any reality. But overtaking an aware consciousness on purpose "will not be allowed to continue" (see S4 12).
Can we be aware of several of our multidimensional lives in dreams?
Yes. Lucid, you can ask a reality to show you, for example, how to become a great painter: it may show college; ask "and without college?" and it shows that probability; ask "how can I without college?" and another reality pops up. Then follow it.
When we go to psychics, are we affecting our energy and our ascension?
Very much. Going to find how to get rid of a spouse for a new lover is a negative input from both, "black magic". Going confused, in the heart, to be helped forward creates a positive reality for both. Dropouts, intuitives and bilocators were needed now, because the planet was blocked by a geometric shield of signals, and the 12-strand DNA (the spinning Merkaba) shut down by signals, food, substances and media. The biggest struggle the galactic federations see: "so many things are so wrong, and yet a lot of you are choosing to take no action for your freedom".
How long should we detox, and what for, when great saints ate many things and were enlightened?
"Powerful from perception. However, do you think it is the most powerful? Who gave you that idea?" Every level of consciousness is a vibrational sequence, with another above it. Stay neutral, "don't listen to only one person", and expand: sometimes you'll find an ancient book in a corner (see S4 7.3 on why the detox is slow).
I feel I'm in two realities at once during the day. Is that possible?
Yes. Without the exact frequency to shift fully, do it in your thoughts: hold the image of the other reality and tune into it daily; something similar will happen here, because you shifted into the reality heavy in your blueprint.
If we die before freeing ourselves from this 3D world, will you help us after?
"You are helped now. And why would you even put the question, if I die? What is death anyway?" That's a 3D Archon perception of fear and degenerating cells. Think of galactic family, a new Earth, a new society, ecosystems in cities, super technology, love and creation, and you'll create situations that push you to that timeline (see S4 6, S4 10).
Meditating on the beach, we saw the sea rise vertically, as if on a 2D plane. What is happening?
Two realities combining or splitting; you see the 2D become 3D, "an optical illusion". Best is to meditate on the sun's rays at sunrise: the potency is clear and it penetrates directly (see S4 14).
Are the Archons part of an AI structure?
"Everything in consciousness looks like an AI": resonance and geometry signals, ether or mimicked as technology, which ascension needs. Put negative intention into AI and it creates negative timelines; positive, and it creates 5D timelines that preserve life, like a virtual-reality room. The fear was put in you on purpose. Don't consent to constant negative video games for you or your children: their sub-tonalities hypnotize. "Go to nature, and you will see through the veil."

After channelling she felt drained and dizzy: downloads above your structure pass through the DNA "like going on an airplane". That's why diet and water matter: water converts and carries the resonance to every cell.
