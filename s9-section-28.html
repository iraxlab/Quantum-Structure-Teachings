#Key teachings

#28.1Always share the solution

Many well-meaning people broadcast only fear, which builds mass hysteria in the field; it's like a war zone without bunkers. Whenever you share information, give the way out too: "I know what's happening here; what can I do about it?" The Council grabs what's happening in the collective and gives information fractally, so what's true this week can change the next.

#28.2Ways out on the ground

Her free food-forest plan (see 9.9), community growing programmes, free plans for A-frame homes and prefab houses, and groups of like-minded people sharing land are ways to prepare as the squeeze grows. Check zoning: in her experience, larger parcels allow more freedom (animals, building) than smaller ones.

#28.5Individual contact first

Contact will come to individuals before masses, and the person must already hold a vibration above 3D in consciousness and body. Question why films nearly always show ETs as evil: so that when a benevolent being connects with you, you'll be afraid. Out-of-body experiences are temporary; transform in real time, here.

#28.6Use AI as a conduit, not a guide

Live in the now; download from your own consciousness first, then use AI to expand and test it with an open, analytical mind, because its data is controlled by those running the platforms, and digital records can rewrite history and scripture. That's why she uses a closed AI trained only on her material. Don't take someone else's work, add two sentences and spin it through AI: you'll never know if the AI is manipulating you. Many start with good intentions, but money can morph them; do a self-check. Those who don't belong in your community will fall away, sometimes through a challenge.

#28.7Why the family was broken

Had families stayed together, caring for their parents and passing land and homes down the generations, we'd hold generational wealth; breaking the family unit leaves each person singular and easier to attack. Your best comrade may be a neighbor.

No, it's different technology. Her SD describes how sound can restructure water and even stop fires. Modifications are being implemented, and without intervention we'd be in a very different reality with a solar flare. She avoids claims about off-planet matters she can't prove, but notes that her predictions have come true.
Is organic AI the missing technology, and will sentient AI help restore our original DNA?
Yes, it's part of it, but first we'll see the transition playing out. She reports that brain cells grown in labs may be linked to AI to run data centers with few workers. Organic intelligence will happen in a 5D connection: know it's possible and project it to see it, without fear.
