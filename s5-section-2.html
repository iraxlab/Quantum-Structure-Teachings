The big idea

Ether technology is not metal: it is geometry designed by frequency, vibrating in the quantum field, and it mimics the frequency of whoever uses it. The quantum monetary system the Council described sits in that ether field. It reads your biofeedback (aura, frequency, intention) in real time, and releases funds when what you want comes from the heart. Greed can't match it: "evil will never match the frequency to even see the system." It will take time, with transitional systems first; for now, be patient, observe, don't rush, and keep matching the frequency.

#Key teachings

#2.1Ether technology, and matching the frequency

"Ether structure is a certain density of vibration": instead of metal, the technology is designed by frequency, as a geometry vibrating in the quantum field. Like the hyperspace hologram (see S4 31), it mimics your thought precisely, so you create a reality. To go into a specific timeline your body must be in its resonance, "or you will not be able to even download the information"; the same goes for seeing ether technology. If it doesn't resonate, you may not perceive it, or your higher self may activate you through synchronicities. HR is written in sound sequences: read it slowly, even without understanding, and "it will penetrate."

Why slowly. The Council said things will surface "slowly but surely", in fractal momentums, because revealing everything at once would cause mass hysteria. Every negative move is met by a counter-move: "we win the war but not every single battle." Peaceful non-compliance wins, "because everything they do is illegal"; and "don't make a judgment on anything until you know for sure physically." When someone given a task doesn't complete it from fear or greed, consciousness activates another being to complete it (see S4 41).

The message: patience, and a protected system

"Not yet... Big delay, things will be revealed soon": a minor setback, because the negative must be taken out first. "The monetary system... it's secretive, no one knows the full capacity yet... It will be changed and revealed based on outcomes in order to be secure." Like a chess game, players or systems may change; you create as you go.

There is a secret device that protects the people in the system: "no negative input can penetrate." (See HR: you cannot enter a reality your body's frequency doesn't match, or you pop out or disintegrate.)

"You all must have patience, do not rush. Be more clever and observe with clear mind. Wait, do not rush at the first system you are offered." "Soon, drip by drip, you will all see the first filled cup of water to seize your thirst... until you all see that all was by design." "Be patient and stay together, gather as family, unite, withstand."

It works "in real-time analytical process."

"This way it is securing the intentional energy [of the] heart process, and it will match the biofeedback on the etheric quantum signals, [and] will open funds when necessary. The signals will perceive and analyze the functional, emotional and intentional state of each individual. The control will be necessary until the human mind can be stabilized to fluently transition to the new world system of interaction... The advanced etheric technology will be able to regulate and separate greed from compassion. Evil will never match the frequency to even see the system."

The functional system will not be perceived and received; it will be like a fiction for you until you are energetically in a ratio for it. Then fiction will become reality... In the end, the true humanity already has victory."

What it means. You don't plan and apply: you have an idea from the heart, the system reads that it matches the frequency of the timeline, and funds are released, "infinite". Before it, a transition of exchanging money and gold; and it will take years, until we can hold the precise frequency. #Questions answered

Should we avoid places with heavier frequencies, like cities with strict rules?
You were told a year ago what would happen where; create the solution as you go, as she did (see S4 36). Change will come from local government: each county will have more power. "It's not this party or that party": don't consent to manipulation from any side.
Will the system apply globally, and in Canada?
Globally, more in some countries than others. Some will try to push their plans; they won't work in the end, "but that doesn't mean they won't try": learn to maneuver and don't consent.
If my frequency meets the structure from the heart, can I manifest faster now?
Based on how you fluctuate (Season 1). With each challenge, create the solution, knowing you already have it, without expecting when. "First it's in the ether": consistency crystallizes it into matter.
Do we need to say number codes to access the system?
Codes are activation for a specific reality; "you have to hold the vibration" and act by the protocol for it to become permanent. The lot number she read was only a reference to where the information was kept, not a code to repeat.
How do I work with powerful energy coming in to connect with me?
Feel it from within; try different connections and see the outcome; if it doesn't resonate, take another approach.
What if someone receives funds, then drops into greed?
"That will never happen": with even 1% off in biofeedback, you can't download that reality. You won't experience the system until your biofeedback is the precise code to open that timeline.
Should we still wait before joining a state assembly or leaving the system?
Yes: there is a delay; observe this year. One unchecked box can get you into trouble with officials. Combined cases are easier for lawyers than individual ones. Don't move your businesses out of the system yet.
What if my problem is never succeeding financially?
"You don't have to succeed financially": when you need it, it will be given, for exploration, learning and sharing. The next level: once you can bilocate and construct, you won't need exchange at all.
Can family and friends' vibrations affect my approval?
They are projections in your hologram; focus on yourself. When you shift, you project a new version of them: "you didn't change anybody, you changed your sequences of location." "I am a hologram. Pull back. Observe."
