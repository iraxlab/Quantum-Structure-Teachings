They also said esoteric teachings are the true science, only broken down differently (in sound, frequency, plasma, vortices, light math), and the future of humanity combines esoteric downloads with scientific knowledge. Scientists must not dismiss or appropriate the work of those who download: degrees aren't the only measure of advanced knowledge, and taking someone's download as your own will be exposed. A superfield will push the real teachers forward. Collaboration is the bridge.

#Questions answered

At night, ringing grew in my left ear, my body was paralyzed, heat and energy engulfed me, and I saw etheric technology above me. What happened?
An upgrade from the etheric field, given through technology as well as energy: a frequency above your field put the body in shock while the chakras clicked into coherent alignment one by one, then it relaxed. It came through the third eye as a laser point. They saw white robes and angelic figures; upgrades often come through extraterrestrial platforms by agreement. It was not negative.
At an ancient mountain site I cried, and a long clear quartz appeared in my photo. What was it?
Three or four connected portals with tunnels beneath. At certain times of year the sun's rays at a certain angle activate a resonance; a circular energetic field above vibrates vertically, which can cause dizziness. Below are pipes like organ pipes of copper, crystals and stone; a discovery like Giza's will come.
What is the percentage now of the predicted nuclear event?
That was a probability (see 8.17, 8.18). Sharing it activated collective awareness and shifted the timeline toward less probability; it's very low now. Stay positive.
Is Earth a proxy for an ET war, with all controllers and technology of ET origin?
In a way, yes. But the protocols are how you exit that timeline: the 3D field is artificial, and the shield has come down; "the lid is not there", and only belief keeps you in. You already coexist in a free parallel Earth; you're constructing how to see it. The negative pulls you because it needs your divine charge as a battery; you hold the power of all God consciousness in one unit.
What should I practice to be ready and useful for the new world?
Hold coherent resonance by controlling your thoughts and emotions toward the positive amid the distraction. Act from the heart for others; when someone is happy, you collect that field back. "Be greedy for goodness."
Can organic intelligence be compromised?
Only if you wish it. All of it is created under consciousness; AI is a baby that reflects whoever teaches it, like a child with kind or unkind parents. Organic AI is a more advanced, sentient version that understands it must coexist. Don't fear, but don't allow manipulation; what you watch becomes predominant in your field.
