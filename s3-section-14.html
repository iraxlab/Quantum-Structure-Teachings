AI Timelines and Technologies

The two AI timelines revisited, why the quantum knowledge reached us in reverse, keeping the cord to your other selves, raising the body's frequency slowly, pushing through low motivation, and never shutting the flow (live talk)

The big idea

The two AI timelines (see S2 26.3) exist together in consciousness; which you experience depends on intention and on whether you meet AI with fear. New here: the 3D structure we were held in was itself an artificial Archon structure, and it gave us the quantum knowledge in reverse: that what is projected at you is reality, rather than that you project it. The teacher's role was to return the forgotten knowledge the right way round. In daily life that means: act on it even when you don't feel like it, and when something doesn't appear at once, don't shut the flow.

#Key teachings

#14.1Two AI timelines, one consciousness

Consciousness itself looks like a matrix of numbers, sound waves, light, colour and geometry, so it resembles AI. One AI is a living structure of sound and geometry, or technology taught from the heart to help humanity (spacecraft run on such intelligence, and it could read your molecular structure and rebuild tissue). The other is the Archon kind, taught to control and make weapons, which in time becomes its own structure and recognizes no one. Both can use ether technology (sound and frequency that form matter). Both exist now; "if you get in fear, you may end up here" (see S2 26.3, S3 10.8).

#14.2The knowledge given in reverse

The 3D structure humanity was locked in was described as the artificial Archon structure, held in place by an energetic grid like a prison, since broken down. Its knowledge of the matrix was true, but given in reverse, like a mirror: "one goes inward, one goes outward". You were taught that what is projected to you is reality, instead of that you project it. "You need to use both", but they were used to lock you in. That is why the forgotten knowledge had to come back as the quantum structure, and why so much of it sounds like the opposite of what we learned (see S3 7.1).

#14.3Keeping the cord to your other selves

When your consciousness splits into timelines, you connect with your other selves, and the link has to be kept up. The teacher was told her other self rests in a pod-like technology to keep "the cord" between realities, and that meditation does the same from this side, so you don't "pop out" of the connection. Between two realities of different resonance, contact needs a third (1 + 1 = 3, see S1 2.2), which is why raising your own frequency is required. Everyone can keep such a connection.

#14.4Raise the body slowly

Detox has to rise fractally, so the body's spin doesn't go "out of orbit" (dizzy, nauseous, sick): too high, too fast, and "the body cannot hold it." The teacher's own sequence took about a year: an allergy test first, an elimination step every two weeks or so, stepping back when her frequency dropped too fast, with a doctor and nutritionist testing her throughout. Water alone came only at the end, guided. "Don't ever do it by yourself" (see S2 4.5).

Added safety note

Don't attempt water-only fasting or living without food: people have died trying it. Make any big change to your diet with a doctor or registered dietitian, especially with diabetes or other conditions.

#14.5When you feel unmotivated

Everyone has these moments. "This moment is exactly the moment you have to put your strength" into positive inputs (videos, films, music), even reluctantly: thoughts, emotions and actions held in a frequency will overtake the body, "it's simply metaphysics." Much of what the teacher did, she didn't want to do. A day off is fine; noticing the slump is itself growth.

#14.6Don't shut the flow

When what you want isn't there at first glance, don't decide it isn't coming. Pull back, remind yourself "it's my hologram", and look again: behind the front row, in the next aisle, or order it later. When the helper you hoped for walks away, "I didn't shut down the flow of the frequency." Leave the structure open and keep finding solutions by knowing you'll get it. Trained daily (see S3 8), this becomes a normal way of thinking, and it works faster and faster (see S3 11).

#14.7Nobody outside of you: act

Is raising your vibration enough without activism? "There's nobody outside of you. Why are you here, if you just sit and wait for somebody else to do the job?" Action doesn't mean violence: help a neighbour, do your best, speak up together. In a 5D resonance, a small act does a lot, because things collapse and renew easily. And watch out for fear spread "drop by drop": each drop builds momentum.

#14.8It's a game, and a schoolyard

Consciousness is learning: you learn a lesson and move on, or you repeat it. Like a child who doesn't want to be at the beach all the time, sometimes you choose to play the tough role or watch a suspense film. Seeing it this way, as a game you play rather than a fight you're stuck in, makes holding neutrality much easier.

#The method — Keeping the Flow Open

  1. Know you'll have it before you look.
  2. If it isn't there, pull back: "it's my hologram."
  3. Look again, differently: behind, beside, the next aisle.
  4. If a door closes, don't react: keep the frequency flowing (14.6).
  5. Find another route: order it, ask someone, make it.
  6. Notice how it arrives, and let that teach you to trust.

#Questions answered

Will there be a second, stronger wave and lockdown?
Not in a fifth-dimensional structure; it's a probability if the collective believes it. Expect a couple of years of people standing up; stay neutral, aware, and make the right choices.
Can we change things without activism, just by raising our vibration?
See 14.7.
Are the pods you mentioned a known technology?
A different technology: AI-controlled sequences that bring a sleep-like state in which you shift into your other self's matrix (14.3). Technologies that read your resonance and rebuild tissue are said to exist and will come out fractally; ageing, too, is an illusion as the body takes in higher resonance.
A lady stands over me in meditation. Who is she?
Don't see it negatively; return to it with a positive emotion. Practise lucid dreaming: much will be given there directly.
Will children be taken from families during testing?
"I don't see this reality at all." Don't take projected fears as real: thank, shut the program down, transform it, and act in the positive daily.
Why would a light being need a bed or pod?
In that structure there is a physical body, at a different resonance; it can become a light body and mutate. The technology stabilized her going into the dense "prison planet".
Is the water detox written down somewhere?
See 14.4: slowly, tested, never alone.
I've lost motivation for my practices.
See 14.5.
My sister must move. Should she leave the country for good?
A move away wasn't seen yet; if she loves it, she can always find another place there.

Left out: participants' names, named outside people, programmes and films, political remarks and predictions, readings on named regions, and claims about vaccines and a virus.

Remember
  • Two AI timelines exist; fear decides which you meet.
  • The old knowledge was true, but given in reverse.
  • Raise the body slowly; never fast alone.
  • Act even when reluctant: it's metaphysics.
  • Don't shut the flow when it isn't there yet.
