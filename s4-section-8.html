The big idea

The spear of destiny is "a frequency sound", a holographic timeline: the end result is 100% set in consciousness itself, positive, because consciousness can't self-destruct. What is not set is how you get there: that is decided by what you tune into and act on, fractal by fractal. Tune into the negative and you manifest it; notice, and correct it in the next moment with a positive action, and you project a new earth. But you have to construct it to see it: nobody outside you will do it, and posting fear without a solution only feeds their timeline.

#Key teachings

#8.1Tune in, and you manifest it

Her lesson of the week: she went onto social media, got upset, and "tested" a rumour she'd seen there, and got the same result. They told her: "Didn't we tell you not to go to any social media? The social media is the hologram program that the 3D matrix is running on." The moment you tune in, "since you are instantaneous manifestation like everybody else", that reality becomes a structure for you; you could only have observed it, and it wouldn't even be in your consciousness if you hadn't gone in. Post it, and you enhance it in the field for everybody (see S4 2.7, S4 4.4).

#8.2Correct it the same day

What you think at the start is what you collapse at the end (see S1 3). Corrected, she erased what she had posted from their matrix and posted something positive instead, with solutions (how to keep warm, what to do), so that the negative wouldn't manifest in her new beginning. "It's not a mistake, depending how you look at it": she corrected what she didn't want in her reality. You can do this daily, "but also every hour or every minute", shrinking it to the now. Each time you collapse something, that's one earth; when you correct it, you project into a new earth: Earth 1, Earth 2, Earth 3.

Her rule for sharing

"When you post something negative, please... have the solution. If you don't have the solution, do not put it on a platform, because you're going to put everybody in fear." Sharing fear "is not helping": everyone shares it and you infuse their timeline. Waking someone up is different, but after you've learned what's happening, ask: what solution am I taking?

#8.3The spear of destiny

The spear of destiny is a frequency resonance, a geometry and a holographic timeline, and "set in stone 100% to happen in consciousness itself". The negative side opens a negative vacuum portal into time-space, which, inside consciousness, acts "like a cancer", sucking frequency in without converting it; there must be yin and yang. So consciousness does everything possible: whatever they collapse, something appears out of infinite probabilities (a person, a situation, many people, the military) that shuts down their programme and converts it, in every fractal moment after the collapse (see S4 2.5). "The end result... our destiny is already predetermined. But what you are going to experience" depends on what you do daily.

#8.4Fear is lowered by action, not by posting

If you know something harmful is happening (in schools, say) and keep giving excuses, you consent by compliance. Go through the structure and take action without fear, because fear lowers your vibration: "you create as you go". "If you want to change, you have to physically take the action"; putting it on Facebook does nothing. Running for the hills with guns isn't the solution either: the technology used is thousands of years old. "The solution is to shift the timelines." The 3D still sets people against each other; awareness collapses into unity.

#8.5Nobody outside you

"Who is the president and who is the government? You are the one projecting the president, and the collective in the government is your particle." Waiting for someone else to change things is an illusion; so is blaming. "He did that to you, so what are you going to do about it? Just sit there and whine? Where is the action?" She asks how many supported, even with $10 or $50 in a year, those doing the work they share about: "your reality is based on your vibration and what you're projecting into it." And "education is everything": get books, the right materials, and read.

"They do not have power; the only way we give them power is by consenting and not taking action." #The method — Correcting a Slip

  1. Notice you've tuned in and dropped: anger, fear, a negative post. "Thank you very much."
  2. Pull away: another room, a film, anything until you're completely calm.
  3. Take the positive action and feeling, and go back and correct what you did: erase it, apologize, replace it.
  4. Give a solution, not only the problem.
  5. Project the new earth: the corrected moment collapses positive.
  6. Repeat daily, hourly if needed, so the positive actions outnumber the negative.

"Absolutely correct." Media runs an artificial programme with a frequency, and because you're a manifester, "that's what they use you for": you create the reality of their programme. Whatever you see is a projection; it has no say over your frequency unless you consent and go into fear.

Potency. Projected into you daily, their hologram becomes so potent in you that you lock into their timeline, like a glass shattering under sound: "your explosion is into the frequency of their reality". To convert it, tune in to the positive daily and physically act on it, so that your positive actions outnumber the times you tune into the media: whichever you act on more is what collapses (1+1=3). That mutates the particle until "you no longer will remember previous realities, because they've been shut down".

The end result is predetermined, and consciousness guides you to it through synchronicity, but your choices can make the way longer. What you don't complete in one lifetime comes back in the next; now, in ascension, you'll be bombarded with challenges, shaped by what you accumulated, until you clear every particle. Choose again not to, be lazy, or blame others, and consciousness may shut down that programme and recycle you "like a first speck of particle". But whatever you did, "there is a way out by using this protocol": take positive action now, give to humanity, and you accumulate the positive frequency that consciousness gives you as your spear of destiny (see S4 5.9).
