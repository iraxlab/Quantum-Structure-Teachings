Q & A: Density, Déjà Vu and the Eye of the Storm

Holding your ground while the old declines, how the councils see density rather than good and bad, what the rising Earth resonance is, déjà vu, how to learn to visualize, and many questions (live talk)

The big idea

We are in "the eye of the storm": the old is declining while the new rises, and things will fluctuate in between, so hold your ground and you'll always find the bridges for your safety. The councils don't see structures as positive or negative but as denser or lighter; they use our words to reach us. The planet's resonance rises because its fractal momentum tightens as consciousness rises. Déjà vu is a repetition in the structure and a memory of something that already exists. And if you can't visualize, train it through feeling: look, feel the joy, close your eyes and recall the emotion.

#Key teachings

#2.1Hold your ground in the eye of the storm

A council message said our planetary alignment "took motion for the better", but we are "in the eye of the storm". As the old guard declines and the new rises, it will fluctuate (see S2 24.2), and "when something is dying off, they fight to the bone." Hold your ground through the positive, and you'll find the bridges for your safety (see S2 22.11). In the new beginning nobody may be told what to do: people choose freely, but without deception; everything must be open and clear (see S2 7.2). And no one has the right to take your sovereignty once you're awake.

Added note

The message named one country and made claims about immigrants and a government; these are left out, as the book stays neutral on politics and doesn't generalize about groups.

#2.2Density, not positive or negative

"We never talk about structures being negative or positive; we talk about density." Positive and negative are words lowered to our understanding, so we can connect with what we experience. From their perspective every structure is a learning process, and they "don't judge even the most evil". They also learn by watching what we do daily, to find how to explain things to us.

From the booksSD speaks of "less in density realities", usually above 3D, which the body can connect with "with full memory" as its molecular vibration rises.

#2.3Why the Earth's resonance rises

The planet's resonance (the Schumann resonance) is the resonance of combined platforms of fractal movements. They connect point to point and vibrate through the structure, and because they're fractal, the resonance ripples moment to moment through every parallel reality, spiralling like a cone: the beginning of a holographic expression. As the fractal momentum grows tighter in sequence, the vibration increases. That is why the resonance is rising: consciousness is moving into higher vibrational sequences (see S2 26.8).

From the booksDP: Earth's frequency "is called Schumann resonance and is 7.83 HZ. But now because the planet Earth is moving to a new dimension of 5D structure her frequency is constantly increasing."

#2.4Déjà vu

Déjà vu is "recognizing your structure": a repetition in the structure, and at the same time recalling a memory bank of something that already exists. Is it a repeated lesson or expanded awareness? "Both", depending on your timeline and what you're vibrating at that moment.

#2.5Catch the message before the mind

If you start hearing guidance and seeing energy, the mind isn't playing with you: write the messages down, quiet down, and catch them before your human mind starts thinking. The mind is a "backup structure" processing what comes from your 3D or 5D platform; your awareness picks up frequencies above it, and combining the two connects you with higher realities (see S2 27.12).

Added note

If hearing voices is distressing, or comes with confusion or changes in sleep or mood, please talk to a doctor.

#2.6Learn to visualize through feeling

If visualizing is hard, "practise like an actress." Look at an object, a cup, for a few minutes: see how beautiful it is, talk about it, and feel how happy you are to see it. Then put it away, close your eyes, and imagine it again by recalling the frequency of the emotion you felt. It's a skill you can build, not something you're born with or without.

From the booksDP: if your vibration while trying to visualize "is not zero point steady, and relaxed", you won't tune in properly; "the memory will be distorted."

#2.7Your mission: be your higher self

Asked about missions so often, the council answered everyone: stand your ground in the new beginning and express your higher self. What can you do for humanity? What are your gifts? Check the core: do you truly want to help, or do you love the glory? "Once you get more popular, you're going to be tested exactly for that" (see S2 19.5).

#2.8Speaking about hidden harm

When people begin to speak openly about abuse and their own healing, it becomes more potent, "like a ripple effect": every person will meet a situation where they say, "wait a minute, this is not right." Healing it yourself mutates you into a new resonance, "a new human"; but it takes action, not only talk.

Support

If you're a survivor of sexual abuse, you deserve support. In the US, RAINN's hotline is 1-800-656-4673; elsewhere, a local sexual-assault service. To report a child at risk, contact the authorities.

#The method — Learning to Visualize

  1. Choose a simple object, such as a cup.
  2. Look at it for a few minutes: notice how beautiful it is, and say so.
  3. Feel the joy of seeing it: that feeling is the frequency.
  4. Put it away, go somewhere quiet, relax, and close your eyes.
  5. Recall the same emotion, and let the image come from that feeling.
  6. Practise daily, then use it for what you want to create (2.6).

#Questions answered

My father has fever, weight loss and headaches; thyroid medication only partly helps. What should be checked?
Read as an ongoing inflammation and infection the doctors are missing, with gut-lining damage: ask about testing for celiac disease, Lyme disease and lupus.
Is a particular online starseed community good to join?
If it comes from the heart and your higher self's expression, yes.
Can you explain the Schumann resonance?
See 2.3.
Since last year I hear guidance and see energy everywhere. Am I going crazy?
No: see 2.5.
My son is gay and a wonderful person. Is there a pattern of switching genders between lifetimes?
"Acceptance needs to be given to humanity." Your son came to you for a purpose: "he is your best teacher", for your forgiveness and acceptance. A soul may choose any experience for its own reasons.
Added note

Further claims in this answer, about deliberate genetic manipulation, schools and religious communities, are left out: the book doesn't generalize about groups.

Is there information about new wireless networks in my country?
They're global. Don't put it in a negative structure: hold your ground through the positive (see S2 11).
Doctors find nothing behind my chronic morning cough and phlegm. What is it?
Inflammation in the chest from something inhaled; it's still inflamed and needs care. What she did for herself when she had pneumonia was to stay three days only on distilled water, "but I cannot recommend this for anybody", and "it cannot be done by diabetic people, ever". "I'm not a medical doctor."
Added safety note

A cough lasting weeks needs follow-up with a doctor. Don't fast on water alone as treatment.

I was told a white light surrounds me. What is it?
Whoever told you is your projection: "the white light is you."
I fell in love, the connection is strong, but it keeps going back and forth. How do I get over it?
You know the answers but keep pushing. Shut the program down: calm to zero, switch, and do something positive daily (friends, music, going out), or you'll keep shifting into similar situations (see S2 22.12).
Is my relationship with my autistic son a contract I'm meant to change?
It's a lesson you can't skip: accept it, imagine your son healthy and yourself at your best, and through synchronicity new ways to help will appear. "Create it to see it."
Am I heading in the right direction with my mission?
See 2.7.
What is déjà vu? Is it the matrix locking us in a loop?
See 2.4.
Do we have one twin soul exploring the possibilities we don't take?
Not only one: there can be several, in different timelines. A shared timeline needs a contract by agreement, through your guidance (see S2 22).
I feel pulled between two places, and between a past and a present relationship.
There's doubt and guilt about the past marriage. Do you want to repeat it? "No": then be the best of yourself in the new one.
What about a new virus? Is it meant to harm many people? How can we protect our children?
"Did we not say things will escalate? We never gave you how." Ask who is projecting your reality: what you see projected is a program; take it as your truth only if you want that reality. Hold your ground, and do your best (see 2.1).
Added note

Follow your public-health authorities' guidance to protect yourself and your family.

I struggle to visualize when I meditate. Can I fix it?
Yes: see 2.6 and the method.
What happens in the structure when we speak openly about childhood abuse?
See 2.8.
How long should I listen to Bach after the break? (See S3 1.)
Don't count time: play it in the background while you do other things, and let it embed.
As I fall asleep I hear voices from my throat, male or female.
Normal: it's you, using your vocal cords as you dream.
After listening to a channelled talk I had a painful cleanse. Was it the energy?
It's the body; it has nothing to do with what you listened to.
I get bad pain under my right shoulder blade; massage helps for a while.
Read as an inflamed, pinched nerve that comes and goes with movement.
My 21-year-old son has nocturnal epilepsy and is moving out. Any guidance?
Epilepsy is a short-circuit in how the brain wires its signals, so it's individual. Rewiring the circuitry (neuroscience, epigenetics, sound and frequency) can help, and fuller technology may come. Love him and do your best.
Added note

Keep working with his neurologist about medication and safety for living independently.

Our beloved pet died. Did we do anything wrong?
No: you gave compassion and love. Sadness is a vibration she can feel; connect with happy memories instead (see S2 24.10).
Are the "parasites" seen in my blood something else?
She saw sluggish blood, with another membrane between the blood cells. Something is needed to rejuvenate the blood, creating new cells while flushing the old: "there's some kind of herbalism for that", with blood work, doctors who know how to support new blood cells, juicing and a healthy diet.
