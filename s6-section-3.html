The big idea

Sunlight entering the eyes is split and converted into a sound wave that turns your proteins into antennas and, through the water in every cell, sets off a chain reaction that activates the genome. Blocking natural light (UV filters, sprayed skies, artificial lights) sends the wrong signal. The Council of Nine's protocol: at sunrise, for up to 30 minutes, with bare feet on the soil or dewy grass, let the morning light in, well hydrated. Your words carry intention too: there is no harmless lie.

#Key teachings

#3.1Light becomes sound, carried by water

Particles mix by the trinity structure (1 + 1 = 3) and create sound and light; sound holds light. So never be dehydrated. Different spectra penetrate differently: infrared and UV go deep into cells. The morning light also builds melatonin for the night. (On wave genetics, see S5 46.)

#3.2What blocks it

UV-blocking sunglasses and lenses, sunscreens with chemicals, sprayed or clouded skies (which change the angle of the rays), and LED and fluorescent lights shift the frequency the eyes receive. She adds that signals can be sent through LED lights and smart meters, and asks you to research them. Colours and film frame rates are used to affect mood and compliance. She recommends John Ott's book Health and Light, Dr Masaru Emoto's water experiments, the documentary on earthing, and The Harmonic Reactor (p. 26).

"You have been poisoned for 100 years" (440 Hz music, sprayed skies, air, water). "Words are sound waves... be careful what is the intention with the words."

#3.3No white lies

Words reprint the true core of your heart, not the mask: saying you like someone you don't reprints an off-balance frequency that slowly sabotages your hologram. If you can't be honest yet, say "I'm sorry, can we table this for another time?"

#3.4Be careful with machines

Light and sound can carry vitamins and healing, but only at the frequency and duration your body needs at that moment. Don't use frequency, infrared or UV devices that don't first measure your state and show data; too much can harm. Be wary of big claims without proof.

Added safety note

Looking directly at the sun can permanently damage your eyes. If you try her sunrise practice, keep strictly to the first minutes after sunrise, never stare at a bright sun, and stop if you see after-images or discomfort; ask an eye doctor first if you have any eye condition. Sun exposure also needs care for the skin.

#The method — the sunrise protocol (Council of Nine)

  1. Hydrate: water must be present in every cell to carry the conversion.
  2. Go out at sunrise: from sunrise up to about 30 minutes, no sunglasses.
  3. Ground: bare feet on soil, or on morning dew in the grass around 5 a.m.
  4. Let the light in through the eyes and skin, gently (see the safety note).
  5. Keep it daily, and choose plain light bulbs and natural fabrics where you can.
  6. Watch your words: speak with honest intention.
