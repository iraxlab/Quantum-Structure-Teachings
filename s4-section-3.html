The big idea

A code is not random numbers: each number is a frequency, a sound, and sound becomes geometry; geometry becomes a matrix, and a matrix in the ether becomes a live structure. Reading her December code against the frequency table in HR, she shows how each number prepares, gathers, penetrates and powers a new hologram. But geometry is "just the field": it can't mutate into manifestation until a thought of intention is given. So the same frequency can be used for good or ill, and what you feel and intend as you listen is what it carries into you.

She asked why they give only numbers. The answer: "the numbers is simply a frequency. This frequency cannot take a mutation into manifestation until a thought of intention is given." The same geometry serves either side. 666 doesn't mean the devil: in HR, 6 is the locator, which field opens at what point; with a negative intention, sacred geometry with 666 locates a specific target (a person, a place, an area), but the positive can use it for positive penetration too. When the military code was downloaded, a positive intention was put into the hologram, "and when you give the consent", you go through the ascension timeline. Feel that you're free right now.

Music and images carry it. A song broadcast with degrading behaviour penetrates you at the gravitational level of that reality; the same music with positive words and behaviour penetrates in the positive. So when you listen to anything, your intention and thought shape the frequency. Inward and outward are the two directions (the two 9s invert), as in the latitude-altitude geometry of Season 2, moving in a Fibonacci swirl.

  1. Keep the exact order: the code works as a sequence (see the first question).
  2. Look up each number in the HR frequency table (page 48): what it prepares, gathers, penetrates or powers.
  3. Notice doubles and triples: doubled goes inward and negative, tripled goes positive; 9 is the God particle.
  4. Give your consent if you wish it to penetrate.
  5. Put a positive intention and emotion into it while you listen or read: without intention it is only geometry.
  6. Feel the outcome now, and hold the vibration without panic.

#Questions answered

Is the order in which we read the numbers important?
"Very important": it's a sequence, and to create the proper geometry it must be read exactly as given. Each fractal of it creates a certain penetration of holographic imprint.
Does our imagination influence the codes, or the codes our imagination?
Both: you're inward and outward, yin and yang, co-creating simultaneously. Your imagination is "your blueprint of the ether"; it needs a locator signal, which is the frequency of emotion, and action: "you can't just imagine anything... without lifting a finger". The geometry then forms by your intention, emotion and imagination (see S4 1.8).
Are GESARA and NESARA real? Will they truly happen?
"Real, but which reality?" If you want that reality of abundance, believe it 100%, but have no expectations of how or when: abundance may come as someone donating millions so you open your facility. In the quantum field all is possible, because you are the field and the creator.
If we live in a matrix, what is dream time?
Another resonance of reality: raise your vibration and you have another way of existence. There's no difference between dream time and this reality, except what you were taught, and that was the manipulation. You are infinite: you can bilocate, dream lucidly, travel the universe without a spacecraft. You already know it; you're remembering and creating how you got there.
Can we develop faster as a soul in the zero point?
The zero point holds the field in complete neutrality; all the geometry is prepared to penetrate your matrix, and its intention will be positive or negative by you. Faced with what you don't like, you can't reject it, but you can withhold consent: "I understand what you're doing in your hologram. I see it." The moment you give anger, you put a laser frequency into the resonance shown on the screen. So disengage, shut down that program, breathe, and be in neutrality: within ascension you can create your own positive dome of reality, whatever goes on out there (see S1 3).
Is it okay to stay on distilled water permanently?
"So far, yes", she was told; she points to Andrew Norton Webber on YouTube, who talks a lot about distilled water. The Council said distilled water is a conductor with no charge ("dead water"); in the body it goes to the highest-vibrating molecule, a chemical structure such as fluoride, attaches to it and flushes it out. She asks you to research fluoride in toothpaste, and mentions oils such as tea tree oil, a little on the teeth. "Do your own research. Don't believe everything I say."
Added safety note

Distilled water lacks minerals; drinking only distilled water long term may affect electrolytes, so ask your doctor. Tea tree oil is toxic if swallowed: if you use it on the teeth, use a tiny amount and never swallow it.

Is astrology relevant in the scheme of things?
Yes, as holographic imprint. Each reality has its own stargate, with a resonance that changes by how the planets move and connect; "you call it astrology, we call it frequency vibration and location into the field." Each stargate has its own bylaw of frequency, geometry and manifestation. The sun is a stargate, and actually cold: it becomes hot through the friction of the molecular structure ("a new info").
Are we moving into densities where manipulation can no longer be present?
Yes, as your vibration rises and connects with the heart. Now we're in the transition, with 3D and 5D fluctuating together; you mutate by action and by holding the heart until you lock into 5D permanently. Layers of hologram can be hidden in broadcasts and even children's films; so whoever is in power, create your own reality and belief system and keep going without giving up.
