The Structure of Evil vs Good, and Q & A

How a negative intention loops back as a mirror, why the positive is ten times more potent, when density becomes evil and karma, why an agreement still leaves you a choice, healing from abuse through the quantum structure, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

To consciousness, negative and positive are just densities, a yin and yang; but whatever you project comes back as a mirror, so a negative chosen again and again becomes a permanent timeline you lock yourself into. The positive is always more potent, "by the power of ten", because consciousness has to survive; a resonance that keeps looping inward can't match the rising structure, and in the end it sabotages itself. Density becomes evil when harm is inflicted on purpose, against the structure you're part of; that accumulates as karma. And whatever was done to you happened on another earth, to another version of you: you can forgive, take your power back, and overwrite it.

#Key teachings

#18.1The mirror, and the loop

Consciousness, through the one particle, expands into fractal timelines; your body projects each new one from your choices. If your thoughts are intentionally of a lower density, the next hologram is built on it: "whatever you project, that's what gets back to you", like a mirror. Repeated moment to moment, that becomes a permanent timeline you lock yourself into (see S3 17.4).

#18.2The positive is ten times more potent

Yin and yang should balance, but the positive is always more potent "by the power of ten", because consciousness must survive and preserve itself. A structure that keeps looping inward can't match the higher resonance as it rises, and can't see that timeline, however much technology it uses: consciousness "is going to give you something that will sabotage it". So even powerful attempts to read the future from a dense resonance fail, because they can't see above themselves (see S3 17.3).

#18.3When density becomes evil, and karma

Density alone isn't evil. Those who experience the negative by agreement are there to learn how to mix the two into a third, in balance. It becomes evil when harm is intended: "now you're going against your own structure, because you're also part of it". Lose yourself further, to harming for no reason at all, and it becomes insanity (see S2 27.2). A harm done unknowingly brings a lesson and is forgiven; a harm done on purpose accumulates, and that is karma. Those caught in it were offered a choice to move on; many said "this is our survival", having disconnected from the heart even among themselves. Those who do something heroic for humanity are given another timeline (see S3 12.5).

#18.4An agreement still leaves you the choice

Born into an abusive family by agreement? "Your lesson is what you are going to do with that." Will you be the same parent, or will you stop the cycle and become the best parent you can? "Even when you have agreements, you always have the choice." The teacher chose her own hard road the same way, and being first to pave it meant holding her vibration under attack.

#18.5Healing from abuse

For someone carrying PTSD from childhood abuse: through the quantum structure, "whatever happened to that girl was another version of you, on a different earth, that has nothing to do with you right now." Shut that program down and understand it happened to another version, so you can begin to heal. Forgive yourself: a child is confused and manipulated, and is never to blame. Understand that a parent may also have been deceived by someone skilled at hiding behind a respectable face. Take your power back; then overwrite the old energy with positive thoughts, actions and inputs, daily. If the past reappears, neutralize it. "If I can do it, so can you."

Support

Healing from abuse is not something you have to do alone. A trauma-informed therapist can help with PTSD and triggers. In the US, RAINN's hotline is 1-800-656-4673; in a crisis, call or text 988. To report a child at risk, contact the authorities.

#18.6Ten negatives, eleven positives

Living with negative people: "if they do ten times this negative toward you, you're going to do eleven times toward them: positive action"; the same situation a hundred times, convert it a hundred and one. Use the meditation template (see S3 8.3): build the feeling to goosebumps and tears, drop it, then act daily. But if it is constant abuse, verbal or physical, "you need to pull away from that person: it's time to move forward" (see S3 13.10).

#18.7Guides don't heal you; you do

"The guides are not here to heal you. They are here to give us the information" about who you are and how your alchemy works. "The healing is within you. You create the matrix; you even create the guides." Healing never needs to be bought from them.

#18.8Test for allergies before you detox

Detoxing well can still go wrong if you're allergic to something you're eating: allergies come in percentages, and a strong one drives inflammation in the body, with different effects in each person (skin, swelling, other symptoms). At 60 or 70 percent allergy the inflammation becomes very strong and creates mycotoxins. One friend's detox kept backfiring (she was "blowing up") until a severe allergy to kale was found. Take an allergy test and blood work before any protocol, and patch-test oils (see S3 12.1).

#The method — Eleven for Ten

  1. Check for safety first: if it's abuse, plan to leave (18.6).
  2. Recognize the hologram; thank it; bring the emotion to zero.
  3. Meditate: the peaceful home, felt to goosebumps; then drop it.
  4. Answer each negative with one more positive action than it.
  5. Keep it up, without expecting how or when.
  6. Notice when the pattern begins to turn.

#Questions answered

I received a telepathic warning about attacks through phones. Is it true?
Nothing like that was received. The technology used to spy was also used on those who spied; stay positive.
My skin broke out on a clean diet; my dermatologist prescribed strong medication.
See 18.8, and follow your doctor.
Can the guides send energy healing? Someone charges a large fee.
See 18.7.
How can I address PTSD from childhood abuse?
See 18.5.
My family's negativity is constant during lockdown. Any mantras?
See 18.6 and the method.
How did a cartoon "predict" events?
Technology was used to view probabilities on 3D timelines, but it fails above its own resonance (18.2).
Is Earth a simulation, with each of us in a pod on our own ship?
"Yes", like an avatar in a hologram; to be explained separately.
Are we heading into a mini ice age?
Not as consciousness ascends; leave it open: what do you believe?
My guides seem to be trying to reach me, but I can't get the message.
Practise: write it down at once and see if it comes true; you'll make mistakes; tune the radio until the signal is clear.
What is the "nothingness" we came from?
"You're never nothingness. You're the whole structure" (see S1 1.1).
Should my children do school online?
No closures were seen; choose what you feel. "I act in the moment, on whatever my hologram is showing me."
Do clones have souls?
A clone may hold an avatar-like soul, or an artificial, imprinted one with memories that aren't its own, used for positive or negative ends.
My 12-year-old daughter can't always tell if she's dreaming or awake.
Read as the brain's signals connecting in reverse; neuroplasticity training with a specialist, practised twice a day, and gentle explanation that waking life and dreams differ in density. "Go to a medical doctor."
Added note

A child who feels unreal or confused while awake should be assessed by her doctor promptly.
