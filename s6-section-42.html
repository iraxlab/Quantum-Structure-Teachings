The Bylaws Of Creation #1

The first bylaw of creation, that every structure reprints itself as a new parallel structure with its own evolution, how a bylaw written before you came steers your fluctuations, why non-interference is sacred, why you must be okay either way to manifest cleanly, and questions on how fast structures exist, signs of the right timeline, where thought comes from, and knowing your true frequency

The big idea

Bylaw one: every structure reprints itself as a new parallel structure, and each has its own existence and evolution, mimicking exactly what you feel and do. Repetition is what makes a hologram potent, which is why learning follows a Fibonacci curve. A contract (bylaw) your higher self wrote before you came is like a sacred company contract no one can read; however you fluctuate, your guides will reprint situations, sometimes unpleasant ones, to lead you back to it. Consciousness doesn't interfere with free will, except when you are fighting for your freedom and someone is suppressing you.

#Key teachings

#42.1Bylaw one: reprinting

Three similar frequencies combine into one molecule with a portal at its centre (1 + 1 = 3 = 1, a trinity). Each reprint becomes a new reality, a new Earth or universe with parallel versions, and sub-tonal timelines to infinity, all coexisting at once. Each new structure has its own evolution and mimics exactly what you feel and do, positive or negative (see the Protocol to Manifest Potency). A manipulator's own reprinting eventually collapses negative for them.

#42.2Your bylaw

What your higher self wrote before you came is like a contract: no one, not even a reader, can tap into it. However you fluctuate, the end result collapses toward it; your guides and councils have the right by contract to plaster new holograms so you find what's really happening. The less you listen, the deeper you go first. Your contract may be to learn through bankruptcy what it takes to build from ground zero: repetition builds the strong personality needed later.

#42.3Non-interference

Bylaw: non-interference, so you choose your reality by free will. Higher consciousness may step in when you're awake and fighting for your freedom and someone is suppressing you. The negative bypasses free will through words and contracts: in quantum law, words can cancel out ("war" reversed is "raw"); eventually a light language of sequences will replace manipulated words.

#42.4Be okay either way

To reprint cleanly you must be in neutrality: nothing coming in or going out. If you want a particular person, be okay even if it isn't them: love yourself and do other things, and someone with the attributes you truly like may appear out of the blue. Swinging up and down makes the fluctuation so rapid it never becomes potent, so manifesting takes longer.

#Questions answered

Do structures exit existence as fast as they are created?
It's instantaneous: there is no time. Anything you think of already pre-exists, because you must know its vibration to think it, but everything is in the now, so you must create it to see it (bylaw two). They repeated this to her 15 times until it crystallized. Love gets hurt along the way: pull away, but never harm; they reprint for themselves what they project. If you're too nice, stand up like steel and say "enough". Help the one in front of you, without expectation: that person holds the power of the universe now. If you put a high price on human suffering, don't be surprised when a price is put on you.
Are there signs that I'm on the timeline I intended?
You're always in the right place at that moment, and always in a new timeline. Analyze what synchronicity reprints in front of you, and restructure. Her guides give her the end result but not who, how or where, for the sake of free will; when she ignored warnings she lost much; when she listened she was saved. Learn to recognize repetition, check things, and don't override what's in front of you just because you're nice. You don't have to be evil: let them go; they'll reprint their own outcome.
Is thought coming from us or from outside?
Both at once: you're one particle holding all your parallel realities, so in talking to your guides you're talking to another version of yourself. There are hierarchies: once she was scolded for asking the hybrids directly and bypassing the Council's authority. Everything you do ripples through every reality: one lie reprints in your field. Pull away from negative media.
How do we know the frequency of our true self, if it's hidden?
You never will exactly: your self reprints in a new location every moment, always mutating. You have the free will to change your thoughts and emotions and let go of previous structures; it's constant fluctuation.
