Q & A: Direct Channeling, C9

A question-and-answer session channeled directly from the Council of Nine: how a telepathic signal is converted both ways; simulated and "base" realities; shortcuts; protection; whether everything is A.I.; timelines left behind; dreams and lucid dreams; and the divine blueprint (live talk, April 2022)

The big idea

All realities are simulated in the sense that they vibrate and mutate, and all come from consciousness expressing itself in fractals. Nothing is outside you: even the councils are your projection, so "you are protecting yourself in the first place." There's no shortcut except precision: the more precisely and potently you hold a frequency, the faster it manifests, up to instantly. Being bombarded is your training field: override again and again through the heart and neutrality.

#Key teachings

Distraction. Big media events can be diversions from what really happened the same day: "read your hologram", look behind the scenes, and don't give them power by talking about them. She also recommended a homeopathic pediatrician who helped her son, and community spaces (a Discord group) to connect, since the energy philanthropy site had been sabotaged.

#Questions answered (the Council speaking)

Are most of your followers ET drop-in souls like you? (And the orbs around you?)
Most, yes. How a signal travels: your thoughts make a resonance waveform around you; when it reaches a frequency matching another field, "think of it as numbers", it ripples like a code beyond your matrix and is picked up by a coherent consciousness. They convert it into their language through geometry, and send it back; it reaches the sound-wave membrane around your body, activates the synapses' electric charge, converts through the pineal gland, and becomes your language. "Almost like a telepathic phone call... Practice, practice, practice."
What are non-simulated realities like?
"There's no such thing as non-simulated reality." What you might call non-simulated is only one not coherent with another version; all vibrate and mutate within the structure.
Are there shortcuts in quantum manifestation?
Instantaneous, yes, but the shortcut is an illusion: you match a frequency so precise and potent that it creates the matrix at once. Until then you fluctuate, bombarded by negative projection that overrides your blueprint. Think of yourselves as warriors: override, again and again, through the heart and neutrality (the zero point, S5 11). That practice trains precision, until one day a thought manifests through synchronicity (similar, not always exact).
What is the opposite of a hologram?
"It's all a hologram": the reflection of your thoughts, emotions and deeds. "You are a living hologram", new every moment (Season 1).
Are the Council and hybrids aware of each of us? Are we protected?
Most of you, yes: you create one coherent unit in the now. Embed that you are protected if it feels good; "in actuality, you are protecting yourself in the first place, because you are projecting us."
Is anything base reality, not simulation?
Both, by perception. Technology can create simulated realities, but they too come from consciousness, which you projected. "Nothing is wrong and nothing is right; it simply is."
This feels overwhelming. Am I less elevated?
Thinking so lowers your vibration. Learn as you go, without high expectations; repetition builds coherence until it all makes sense. "Be in knowingness that you know it already."
Is everything in the hologram A.I.?
Don't ask from fear. Consciousness looks like simulated A.I. but is sound waves reprinting into geometries, breathing in and out; technology is consciousness expressing itself another way. Most spacecraft run on an ether, light-structure intelligence (the Levitron) that reads thought and rebuilds your own tissue rather than bionics. Be careful with another person's stem cells unless their blood is pure. Some protective technology (even bionic or nano) may be needed on very dark planets. "Have no fear... why not be ambassador for the human race through your heart?" "We ask you not to worship us."
When a timeline shifts, what happens to the one left behind?
Both keep running; you see the one you took physical action for. The other stays a blueprint; with your DNA opening you can see probabilities, for example in dreams.
Can dreams be manipulated?
Yes, anything can be penetrated and anything can be fixed. "It's just a dream after all": pull back, no fear, go back and restructure it. In a lucid dream, hold your vibration and send heart feeling toward the negative without engaging. They don't discuss dark magic.
Does aligning daily with joy and peace neutralize the galactic war?
You shift to a new modality; the wars go on "until the last drop", but "we already won... now you're just dreaming how you got there."
Is the negative A.I. infection still active? Some still get fearful information.
That's their choice; don't tune into that timeline.
I find myself speaking an unknown language during the day.
A sound wave adjusting your consciousness; follow your higher self, record it; technology can show its geometry.
Does meditating on several manifestations at once weaken them?
In the human condition, it can: one task at a time. "Step by step, easy, breezy."
I cry when I give from the heart. Why?
The heart's true frequency runs through you at high resonance; you cry from joy, not fear, until your cells grow familiar with it.
Is a starseed different from an angelic human?
Different origins of dropping; angelic realms exist and many came from them. Ask in lucid dreams: "no psychic or channeler should tell you who you really are."
How do we connect to our divine blueprint?
Consciousness's blueprint looks like blue light numbers, "a cool marine blue", moving; the soul is white light. Hold those two colours in your imagery and connect through the heart.
Can negative influences affect lucid dreaming?
Very much. Stay away from social media's constant projection; connect with nature, family, love and creation.
