The big idea

When you meet an event with anger and hate, you put more energy into it and your collapse goes negative. When you observe it with understanding, in neutrality, the zero point, the matrix reconstructs your structure "with very much ease" toward a positive outcome. So: recognize the evil, don't consent, observe, then maneuver; and don't "wait it out", because you project the whole structure. Each country carries a collective karma, and each person born there carries in their DNA the memory and power to transform it.

#Key teachings

The message: neutrality, not anger

Awakening goes through shock, pain and anger; anger especially must be brought to neutrality, because the matrix mirrors back what you project. "When you focus on a former event... and go through anger, you put more energy into it" with negative feelings of anger and hate. "But if you look at it from understanding and awareness, with no infliction of negative input... in neutrality, you will be able to transform easily that frequency by letting it pass with easy positive outcome."

So observe: this is my hologram, a game; they act from their belief system; "I don't have to consent to it." When you project complete neutrality, the matrix puts the entire structure into the zero point and reconstructs it with ease, through synchronicity. The council said the mortality of the human race "will not be a lot" and it will never go to extinction; but there is no middle ground: be a warrior and stand up, without violence, with the law on your side.

"Be informed... but when you inform, don't get angry. Gain your power by standing up and taking your freedom, but do not use hatred." From their side they are surviving, as we eat animals: don't judge; transform.

The message: when nothing makes sense

"Nothing will make sense to you at all... Good will be evil, and evil is good." "Your mind will feel that everyone has lost their mind"; you'll want to scream, and "your words will be gone with the wind."

"To keep your sanity, please focus on the good, recognize the evil, but do not consent and comply by putting emotional energy of madness... Stay focused: observe equals neutrality. And then maneuver; it is vital for your survival."

Don't sit and "wait it out": if you project waiting, you'll perceive others waiting and nothing changes. "You have to take the action to project the freedom." "Do not underestimate them. The evil is viciously clever." Stay in free zones, know your area, and act "not with hate but power and understanding." Some who complied in denial will face a "rude awakening" before things stabilize. (Dated restrictions and places are left out.)

#48.1The karma and memory of countries

Each country holds its own frequency and the accumulated karma of the souls who lived there, and "there's also a genetic codon that is printed in the memory" of its people from its history: wars, victories, compliance. Many came back now to correct it. You carry in your DNA and higher self the knowledge of how freedom was taken back before; embed it, stay neutral, drop fear, research. As you transform the present, you transform the past too, "because there's no past and future, everything is now." History "will be rewritten."

The messages for countries (in brief)

Australia: stand up for the Aboriginal people: "in their DNA is the codes of the human storage history." Germany: remember breaking the wall; don't repeat the compliance and indifference of the past. Africa: "no more suffering... all that will unite you": one common enemy, so unite beyond ethnic groups; remember Shaka Zulu. Argentina: remember your compassion and innovation; "one person can change your history." The former Yugoslavia: see each other as one human race; the karma of past genocide must be transformed.

Croatia: "you must find a source of better clean water and food. Restore your land and soil and start planting organic non-GMO seeds"; the land lacks nutrients. (Her suggestions: organic compost, beneficial insects such as ladybugs, people who know soil restoration, and food-forest ecosystems; see the detox videos.) Portugal: "have patience." Spain: stop torture for entertainment. Russia: very aware and smart, but use your smarts for the positive, not for money; small villages will be better. France: remember your revolution; the provinces will be better.

Added safety note

Her statements here that medicines are a biological weapon, and about radioactive land, are her views, not established fact. Please make health decisions with your doctor, and use official soil and water testing.

It is individual. "It's not going to be what Marina did; it's going to be what you did." You already made it (see S4 46), but sitting and doing nothing can still fail: share a video, speak up, build something, protect others.

#The method — Neutral, Then Maneuver

  1. Recognize what is happening; be informed.
  2. Pull back: "I'm in a hologram, it's my hologram, it is a game."
  3. Neutralize the anger: no hate, no madness; observe, the zero point.
  4. Don't consent: "I understand... but I don't have to consent to it."
  5. Focus on the good to keep your sanity.
  6. Maneuver and act, with power and understanding, never from anger; don't wait it out.

#Questions answered

Will dark forces try to cause physical pain to drain our energy?
Not answered: she isn't given information on dark forces or possession. Out-of-body practice must be done with precision and with guides you're already connected to, "because capturing can happen."
How can we help struggling lightworkers who aren't yet awake?
"Share information. Do not have fear... Now is the time to speak up." At a table, say what you think without trying to convince; the code in your words may activate someone you don't expect.
I wake to my own voice speaking in deep sleep. Is it my higher self?
It's the echo of shifting from one reprint of you to another, a glimpse of the matrix: a good sign. Write down what you see; no fear.
Can we heal the land just by focusing and sending healing together?
Meditation is only the blueprint; it must be crystallized by physical action: connect with people, plant the seeds, transform the land, with joy.
Can I awaken others just by being present?
Yes: your hologram emanates a frequency 360° into every version of you around you; each still chooses its own expression and timeline.
How do we know our country's karma, and heal it?
Look into its true history: what was done and allowed. Transform it in the now, and past and future change with it.
How can we use algorithms to go deeper?
Reality is algorithm; include the ether, the magnetic and gravitational field (HR): then come free energy and new algorithms, which always change.
Does sadness affect the hologram like anger?
A different potency. Sadness is realization, but staying in it shuts down your action, and it can grow into anger, which can destroy. "Never take an action from anger."
Can someone's unit number in the hyperspace hologram pods be identified?
"No, we're not going to give you that"; not everyone is in the pods. DNA can't be transformed by one frequency alone: it needs several resonances, epigenetics, and the original blueprint, which holds the memory of who you are; it is preserved in the superconsciousness, and how to reach it is withheld (see S4 44).
