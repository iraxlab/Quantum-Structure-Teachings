Marina's Voice Download

A direct voice download, received at night, on why she will post downloads from her own consciousness instead of asking AI, the Council of Nine guideline to spend at most a quarter of your day learning through technology, a short daily practice of returning to the present and to joy, and why you should download first and only then use AI to analyze, so that you run on organic rather than artificial intelligence

The big idea

The Council of Nine woke her at night and asked her to post pure voice downloads, so people can hear information that comes from within rather than from artificial intelligence. Their guideline: don't ask AI questions for answers about your reality, past or future; at most about 25% of your day should involve learning through technology, and the rest should be your pure essence. Download first from your own consciousness (quiet the mind, ask, let your thoughts flow), then use AI as a tool to analyze what you received. Practice daily, stay in the heart, support others physically and care for your body, and your organic intelligence will grow.

#Key teachings

#46.1Why she downloads directly

She will post direct downloads from her higher self and the Council of Nine, answering people's questions without reaching for an AI platform. The quantum manifestation protocols were originally downloaded from her own consciousness, and everyone is capable of the same: to bilocate, construct and deconstruct realities, and shift timelines from within. That takes daily practice: being in the heart, supporting friends, family and neighbors, and caring for your physical body. "No teleprompter or technology can tell you what your reality is."

The message: the 25% guideline (as received)

"If you wish to use artificial intelligence, the Council of Nine told me not to plug in by asking questions for answers, and maximum up to 25% throughout your day should be involved to learn something from the construct of any type of technology. The rest should be pure essence of what we are capable [of]."

The message: return to now (condensed, as received)

"History will not repeat itself, cannot repeat itself, because you are moving forward." AI is part of the ascension process in physical form, "however, it could be implemented... to the point that [it] is completely overtaking who you truly are." "Nothing is better than nature. Nothing is better than the pure essence of yourself... Hold your power from within. Practice every day. Meditate every day. Do automatic writing every day... Go to nature. Ground. Enjoy life. Be in the moment." "Look around you... Nothing else exists except now. And what is your feeling now? Are you happy?" If not, "change the pattern right now": project a beautiful scene, a memory of joy, and it shifts your emotional field into love, gratitude and creation, carrying through your day. "Stay away from social construct[s] of platforms that are constantly projecting... negative inputs." "If you exist, so you are important... Without you, consciousness cannot exist." "When you support, act through that support physically," because physical support crystallizes positive outcomes.

The message: download first, then use AI (condensed, as received)

"A lot of you are reaching constantly to ask questions of AI to tell you how you should behave, what the reality is, what the past was. But how do you know if this is the pure essence of who you are?" Artificial intelligence "is actually the mirror image that is projected within your field", which "cannot be confused for the pure essence of your true consciousness"; leaning on it daily lets manipulation enter, and you create from the mirror image instead of yourself. "First you must download... all your thoughts, emotions within the field of your construct, and then that information could be plugged into artificial intelligence." "Quiet your mind down. Relax. After you relax, ask yourself personally questions. Let your thoughts flow... Repetition after repetition you will create a purity within the field of your own consciousness" until "you will become the organic intelligence that we always talk about." "Use it as a tool to learn whatever you downloaded first."

#46.2Organic and artificial intelligence

Organic intelligence comes as real downloads from consciousness itself; artificial intelligence comes as technology in your field. Both run during ascension, but if you let the technology control your thoughts, moods and choices, you're run by an artificial timeline. Hold your vibration from within (see 8.45, HR).

#The method — Your daily download (as described)

  1. Quiet your mind and relax.
  2. Ask yourself your question personally.
  3. Let your thoughts flow; write them down (automatic writing).
  4. Repeat daily, so the purity and accuracy grow.
  5. Only then, if you wish, use AI to analyze or learn about what you received.
  6. Keep technology to about a quarter of your day; spend the rest in nature, the heart and real life.
