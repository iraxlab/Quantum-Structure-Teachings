The big idea

Her instructions: The laboratory of creation meditation (as led)

1. See a pulsing sphere of light; float into its centre, open your arms and relax in joy and love. 2. Little stars and crystals float around you; ask for your crystal, take it in your hands: notice its shape and colour. 3. Command it to change shape; play with it while holding love and joy. It holds the memory of consciousness and of you: your DNA and your love. 4. Close it into a diamond (two cones joined at the middle); set it before you, touch it with one finger and let it spin until it becomes pure light. 5. Let that light float into your heart, with a spark going first to your third eye: a current now runs between them. 6. Shrink the sphere until you absorb 100% of it back into yourself. 7. To create: return to your laboratory, call crystals, spin them with what you wish to create, and bring it into your heart.

Some do (the top knows, which is why it fights to hold position), some don't, through belief or ego. Don't judge: some truly believe they serve humanity. In consciousness it was all learning. "When we say to you to forgive, we never said to you not to forget."
Will this Earth be healed, or will we migrate to her 5D self?
All Earths collapse into one moment, splitting toward negative and positive. Stay in the heart and you'll experience the new Earth daily, but through an evolutionary process: you're here to pull others along. Until you help, give and share, you won't create the utopia you want.
Some of us cried during the meditation. Why?
Tears of joy: the pure crystalline frequency converted in the heart. That is exactly the resonance needed; aim for goosebumps and tears, as in the galactic meditation.
Was a "worship gene" planted so we'd look for a saviour? Will it be corrected?
You already have the gene: it's dormant, shut down by manipulation and chemicals. At the right frequency it switches on. In your laboratory, ask for your original DNA to appear and ask what is off balance: you hold the knowledge.
Will med beds alter our DNA and RNA?
To some extent, if it's the proper technology; it's coming in time. Meanwhile, cleanse and stay healthy: your body is your temple.
What happens to negative entities using humans as puppets?
Withdrawn: they don't discuss negative structures.
Can today's meditation speed up change?
Yes, if you're consistent and precise; time shrinks. Have fun: it's a game.
