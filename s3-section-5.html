Q & A: A Direct Channel with the Council of Nine

An hour of questions answered directly by the council: individual healing frequencies, crystal surgery, when an old path collapses, feeling alone, how much of a manifestation is built, not giving your power away, and more (live talk)

The big idea

For this whole session the teacher channelled the council directly, and its message ran through every answer: don't give your power away, not to a substance, a device, or even to the council: "you are it". A manifestation can be read as a percentage of how much of it you've built so far, and the rest needs more potency, without expectations of when. When an old path collapses, a new one opens, and you can still co-create the old in a new way. Seeing that it's all your projection can feel lonely, but you are alone and everything at once. And any healing by frequency must be measured for each person.

#Key teachings

#5.1The opening message

"The deaf will hear, the blind will see, the asleep will awake. You will mobilize and free yourself" through a period around 2020–2024, when "the new beginning starts". "Trust the self, not somebody else. You are it. God you are, power you are, strength you are, freedom you are. Welcome home." (A direct channel takes a specific, focused resonance; the council noted the percentage of energy left to hold the connection as the hour went on.)

#5.2Healing frequencies must be measured

Asked about the Royal Rife machine: "we are familiar with the Rife machine". It uses multiple frequencies, and some of them were changed and "are not coexisting with the DNA code", based on manipulation. "Testing is necessary": the sound should be a multiplication of sub-codes (frequency, geometry, sound waves), and "the same frequency is never the same for everybody": individualize it, never one frequency for the masses (see S2 16.1). Crystal surgery exists, but it needs AI and a team (nano scientists, quantum physicists, electrical engineers): crystals bend the sound waveform at different degrees of curvature, flipping a quantum switch in the molecules to rebuild, even a hand.

#5.3When an old path collapses

An artist whose work stopped selling just as she found a new healing path asked whether it was needed. The council's rhetorical reply: if it hadn't happened, would you have taken the new path? "Sometimes the old system needs to collapse, and in that place you have a new one." But you can co-create the old again, from a new perspective: the belief that one can't exist with the other projects situations that stop your joy. "You can combine both."

#5.4Don't give your power away

On microdosing: "Why are you giving your power to a mushroom?" If it were taken away, how would you connect? (see S2 24.9). On a water filter: believe it's good, and change the filters every three months; then the council laughed: "We actually created reality for you. You're the power. Don't give your power away, let that be a lesson." Any belief can become positive or negative depending on your perception: "learn the structure; do not give us the power to tell you. You are it."

#5.5Alone, and everything

After a light-body experience, someone felt deeply alone, realizing that no one else exists. "You did very well." You are alone, and at the same time the same as everything, because you project everything: the one molecule expands to everyone and collapses to you. In time humans will bilocate and meet beings from other timelines "like a new channel". Coming back, accept who you are right now with neutrality while you learn to switch and mobilize your structures.

#5.6How much of a manifestation is built

Asked about manifesting a particular home, the council answered with a percentage: so far she had built 25% of that hologram blueprint, with 75% still to go. Keep expressing your joy, without expectations of when or how: timing depends on how much you put in to build the fractals until it becomes matter (see S3 4.9).

#5.7Act both ways at once

After raising, holding and dropping the energy, do you act as if it's complete, or step by step? "Both." Hold the feeling that it already exists, embedded 100%, and at the same time take the actions that build it: "never stop, never stop. You need the potency to expand into matter from the blueprint" (see S2 16).

#5.8Both halves of the brain

High-vibration perception, including contact, happens when the left and right brain coexist: "almost a symphony of combining different waveforms of resonances", always fluctuating back and forth. "Only left" or "only right" is a partial understanding.

#5.9What you see in others is your reality

Asked why we're surrounded by narcissistic people: "what reality are you speaking of? While you see narcissistic people, somebody else sees beauty." No judgement, awareness: switch your understanding, and the people you see change with your timeline. Animals, too, are "another structure of you": treat them as you'd treat people, and they'll interact with you in new ways.

#5.10A program shut down, once

For someone who felt cursed by people around her ex-husband, the council asked, "Do you wish us to shut the program for you?" With her permission it gave a sequence of signals, asked her to raise her feeling to goosebumps of knowing she was already in the new hologram, and declared it complete. "That will be one-time help. Next time, study the structure." Help works through permission (see S2 8.5).

#The method — Keep Building the Blueprint

  1. Hold the blueprint of what you want, clearly.
  2. Feel that it already exists, with joy, at 100%.
  3. Take the next real step toward it, today.
  4. Assume it isn't finished: keep adding potency daily, without expecting when.
  5. Keep your power: no device, substance or person decides it for you.
  6. Repeat until it pops into matter (5.6, 5.7).

#Questions answered

Which frequency is a new virus sensitive to, for frequency machines?
See 5.2: testing is necessary.
Are certain famous magicians mastering the quantum structure, or doing tricks?
"Both, depending on your resonance": no further comment.
Do we ascend to 5D with the mind while the body stays in 3D, or with the body too?
Body and mind are one unit, mutating with your frequency; study S1 1–3. "Create it to see it."
My art stopped selling when I began a healing path. Was it needed? Should I still paint?
See 5.3.
For a year my mouth's lining has been sloughing. Why?
Read as inflammation and fungus, gum inflammation and bone loss: detox, lots of water.
Added note

See a doctor or dentist about ongoing mouth symptoms.

How do we end up in a parallel reality unconsciously, or travel consciously?
"You're always present": the illusion that you're not is what you give your structure.
My father has had a rash for years; would silver or a detox help?
Read as a fungus under the skin, fed by sugar: herbal detox, including against parasites, and a check of his breathing.
Added note

A long-standing rash and breathing problems should be assessed by a doctor.

Constant dreams affect my mood all day. How do I control it?
Log your dreams to learn your codes (see S3 3.4); when your mood drops, switch it with a physical action.
My session said to have fun, but a talk spoke against casual sex. Which is it?
Advice fits your moment. Lust between loving partners is fine; casual mating from lust alone builds a different resonance and attracts the same (see S2 22). "Make your definition clear: what exactly do you want?"
I dreamed the council tried to talk to me. Was it my subconscious?
All are projections in your hologram: "yes", if you believe it.
After an image of Saturn, the number six and 6 Hz keep appearing. What does it mean for me?
"The beginning of your learning": you opened portals and are downloading exactly what you need.
I follow the detox and live from the heart, but haven't met my galactic family. Any advice?
Contact won't come soon for you; it comes through out-of-body experiences, by agreement.
After a light-body experience I felt utterly alone for a year. Are we alone?
See 5.5.
Is the Schumann resonance reliable?
Yes, it's increasing: let it flow, and feel each cell take in the resonance (see S3 2.3).
Are cheap hydrogen-water devices good?
"This could go very wrong depending how they make it. Be cautious of where you buy and how much you're using": any frequency can be very useful or very destructive depending on how long you use it and whether your body needs it, so it has to be measured. That's why distilled water is suggested: it isn't dead water but zero point, so in the body it attaches to the stronger vibration and flushes out exactly what's needed.
Why are we surrounded by narcissistic people?
See 5.9.
My nonverbal son became very angry and hit people. How can I help?
His brain takes in sequences too fast: a specialist can measure it, so you can adjust what he reads or watches.
Added note

Work with his doctors and therapists on behaviour and safety.

Can I manifest a particular home for retreats?
See 5.6: 25% built, 75% to go.
Does an old secret order work for humanity with a council?
"We do not know this organization": you project it positive or negative.
Was a message about billions of walk-ins accurate?
Much switching, by agreement; don't attach to numbers (see S2 24.8).
My husband's projects don't earn much. What can I do for him?
"It's what you can do for yourself." See him as already successful, and focus on your own success.
My 21-year-old daughter won't communicate with me.
Let her be; live your own life and let her choose.
Does action after the meditation mean acting as if it's done, or step by step?
"Both": see 5.7.
In a lucid dream I saw people with bird and cat faces observing me.
Everything is recorded (see 5.9).
Since our second child, my husband is hostile and wants to leave. What happened?
"You cannot make somebody love you": focus on yourself and your children, and let him choose.
How do I manifest abundance now?
Embed that you are always in abundance (see S1 4).
After a heavy meal I vomited and bled and spent five days in hospital. Was my light body unable to cope?
"Medical, you must go medical": a gastroenterologist.
Added safety note

Vomiting or bleeding from the bowel needs urgent medical care.

People connected to my ex-husband have put a spell on me.
See 5.10.
(Closing) Should we stock up?
Have some food and water at home, but not from panic, which manifests through the structure.
