Bilocation of the Body

The big idea

Bilocation, said to be the future of humanity, is more than shifting moment to moment between parallel realities: it is shifting your whole being into a completely different timeline, for example from 3D Earth to 5D Earth. For that, consciousness alone isn't enough; the body has to come too. Its molecules must be raised, slowly, until they vibrate in one synchronized symphony; joined with heart-felt thoughts and emotional tone, body and heart become one unit, and that unit is the vehicle, with no spacecraft needed. The talk ties this to its detox and diet protocol, which it says must go drop by drop, never all at once, and not at all without a doctor for people with health conditions. Contact with other beings works the same way: a third reality, built on similar resonance, from the heart.

#Key teachings

#4.1The download: your body is the vehicle

The download: to achieve bilocation for physical contact, you must regulate and increase the molecular structure of your body. All the molecules have to shift their vibration in a synchronized modality, which creates a specific symphony of your singular frequency rotation. When it reaches enough potency, it is easy, just by thoughts and emotional tone, to shift the timeline, not only the parallel reality. "That is mastery."

You don't need a spacecraft or technology to bilocate, because your body's energetic form, connected with your thought and emotion, is the vehicle. You are the hologram, which expands to infinity and collapses to infinity (see S2 2.5); and since everything is frequency, you can shift and bilocate anywhere in the structure, and back and forth.

From the booksSD prints this download almost word for word under the heading "By Location", ending: "You don't need spacecraft or technology… because your body energetic form connecting with the thought and that emotion is the vehicle… So… come home. We are teaching you." DP adds that a "New Human" is emerging, with "telepathy, bilocation".

#4.2Parallel reality or whole timeline?

Every timeline has a location, X and Y, latitude and altitude, measured in degrees through 360; and each location is a hologram, a new Earth, with its own frequency, sound and timeline, and it is fractal (see S2 3.1). Moment to moment you are always shifting between parallel realities (see S1 1.3). Bilocation is a different level: shifting into a completely different timeline, with a different frequency.

So if you are in 3D and want to be on 5D Earth, you have to train your body so that the vibration of its molecules matches the resonance of the fifth-dimensional timeline, including the angle of spin of the molecules (see S2 3.3). It all fits together.

#4.3Body and heart as one unit

Once the body is clean and its frequency rises, consciousness comes into fruition, and how you behave matters: if you live in the heart structure, that is the consciousness that comes through. You have to combine the heart with the body into one unit moving as one, which is the symphony: vibration, geometry, location and degree. Combine them and you create an image of reality.

Then, if you wish to see your galactic family, your consciousness starts resonating with them; because the body is already clean and vibrating fast, the combined symphony immediately gives the code of geometry and structure to bilocate you into the platform you choose. This is how extraterrestrials shift between timelines and realities (see S1 9.2).

#4.4Why diet, in the teaching

The body is an energetic field: its molecules are frequency. The talk teaches that our molecules carry a lower, denser frequency because of everything we have taken in, and that living, uncooked plants vibrate higher. Fresh juice goes straight into the bloodstream, so drinking it overrides one frequency with another, lifting the body's frequency towards a 5D resonance. It repeats its view on distilled water drawing out chemicals (see S1 12.7). Bilocation isn't consciousness alone: "you have to bring the body."

Guidance may help along the way, in a way designed for you: an "upgrade", a contact, or something else; the talk describes its own heart structure being upgraded to hold a steady 5D frequency. It is not special, it says: do the right things and your guidance will help you one way or another (see S2 1.11).

#4.5Drop by drop, never all at once

The guidance's instruction for any cleanse: you can't flush the body all at once. If you do, you overwhelm the molecular structure, the system shuts down, and you deplete it; that is why some people look depleted after a cleanse. Open the tap drop by drop, so the body's molecules learn to accept the new frequency and the flushing stays steady, without being pulled "out of orbit".

The protocol described: keep your normal meals while adding juices, and remove one food every two weeks (for example beef first, then chicken, and so on), so the change is gradual; make a chart of everything you eat. Drink no more than two cups of green juice at a time (she saw a woman drink three cups at once and almost pass out); don't mix fruit and green juices, they ferment and you lose nutrients; learn to accept the bitterness, which flushes well. If you feel nauseous while juicing, have a shake: avocado blended with distilled water. Her own amount: 10 cups a day, six cups of green juice and four cups of fruit juice. Her elimination took one year: she cut the meat, then the bread, then the cheese, until she was only on fish, potatoes, salad, juicing and shakes; then she cut the fish, then the potatoes, then the salad, and was 15 days only juicing, after which her heart structure was upgraded. Alongside: distilled water (research Andrew Webber on distilled water) and probiotics: she now uses Visbiome ("the doctor who produced the VSL original formula moved his formula to Visbiome"; VSL can still be used, "but I cannot guarantee"). Swiss chard juice alone is so salty you'd think it had a tablespoon of salt: the vegetables carry the minerals. The talk describes going much further over a year, eventually to juice only and to near-breatharian eating, but says clearly that it does not recommend that to anyone. It also says the protocol is not for people with diabetes, kidney disease or liver disease: they need to detox with doctors.

It must also be your own choice and will: if you don't take action, the guidance backs off until you're ready.

Added health note

These are the talk's own practices, not medical advice. Long juice-only periods and anything close to "breatharianism" (living without food) are dangerous and have caused deaths. Before cutting out food groups, starting a cleanse or changing your diet significantly, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian, especially if you are pregnant, have diabetes, kidney or liver disease, an eating disorder, or take medication. The talk's claims that gluten is a "poison" for everyone, and about GMOs and alcohol, are its views; gluten is harmful for people with coeliac disease or gluten sensitivity, and a doctor can test for that.

#4.6One molecule becomes everything

Your molecule is also nano: consciousness itself is a nano structure that expands to infinity and collapses to infinity. Depending on the frequencies around it and how they are mixed, an atom mutates, like a dish that changes with each new ingredient, so from one molecule everything is created (see S1 5.9, S1 19.5). That is why a clean body matters for bilocation: if the body is full of dense matter, the structure is too weak to hold the higher frequency coming in, or the one you want to project.

#4.7You already bilocate, in small ways

Ever found yourself in the next room without remembering walking through the hallway? The talk explains that you bilocated: you don't need to pass through every in-between step (see S1 3.5, 5.5). Now imagine doing that not only within your own platform but from one timeline to another; that is what we are being taught, because it is how we will meet extraterrestrial and higher-dimensional beings, who vibrate at a completely different frequency, timeline and location.

Added note

Ordinary absent-mindedness is common. But frequent or worsening memory gaps, losing track of time, or getting lost are worth checking with a doctor; they can be a sign of a treatable or serious condition.

#4.8Meeting other beings: a third reality, by resonance

To meet beings from another timeline, you connect consciousness and body into one symphony and create a third reality between their timeline and yours (1 + 1 = 3; see S1 2.2, 17.5). From channel 3 to channel 5, you create a third: your own channel. It has to combine their structure and yours, so the resonance must be similar; benevolent beings may step in to teach you a lesson for your ascension, but you can't shift into their reality if you don't match.

If you want benevolent contact, go benevolent: from the heart. If you do negative things, you will see entities from negative structures, because everything exists in the field, even the most evil person, as a holographic imprint in your reality. Don't deny it, since it's part of consciousness; neutralize it (see S1 2.6, 3.3).

When light beings make contact, they come into your hologram and hold the vibration so that you can withstand their frequency. But for permanent contact, working with them while helping humanity, bilocation is needed, and they won't work with people in greed. It has to be the heart structure.

#4.9Habits and joy

Why keep eating or drinking what you know harms your body? The talk singles out alcohol as depleting the cells and says the idea that you need it to be happy is a belief you can change: you can shift into happiness without it. It isn't asking for perfection: change slowly; a rare glass of wine with friends is fine. As your connection with your guidance grows, it will show you what your body needs.

#4.10Money, support and the transition

Professionals have asked how to move to Energy Philanthropy (see S1 5.1). Don't jump into the fire all at once; transition, because money is part of the structure for now (see S1 4.4). Wealthy people, and even governments, will wake up and fund the right scientists to build eco-systems; with free energy, money will eventually become obsolete (see S2 1.4, 1.9). Don't reject money: give it through the heart.

And support doesn't always mean money: it means being compassionate to someone, in whatever shape and form you can, moment to moment. That is the heart structure, and it is what raises your heart's frequency above the 3D structure. Combine that heart with a cleansed body, and you have the new unit for bilocation.

#4.11When a message is slightly off

Even trusted guidance is checked: the talk describes researching a frequency it was given until it found a scientist saying the same (see S1 13, Method). It also says that some predictions about governments can be slightly off, sometimes on purpose, until what is really happening can be revealed. So hold predictions lightly and keep your focus on your own frequency.

From the booksHR describes 70 to 90 Hz as "the body. The physical human body tone duration. It must stay at that at all the times" (see the water question below).

#The method — Tune Body and Heart Together

A slow, long-term practice. Check any change of diet with your doctor first:

  1. Decide, and mean it. The change only comes when you choose it and act.
  2. Live from the heart every day. Kindness and compassion, in any form: this raises the heart's frequency.
  3. Cleanse slowly, with your doctor. One change at a time, every couple of weeks; never all at once.
  4. Notice your body. Nausea or depletion means too fast: step back, as the guidance itself advised.
  5. Join body and heart. In meditation, feel love through the whole body, as one unit.
  6. Hold the image of the timeline you want, with matching emotion, and let your resonance build.
  7. Stay in the heart, without greed, so the beings you connect with are benevolent.

#Questions answered

How long does the detox last? Is it a permanent change, and is there a move back to some cooked food? How do I know when it's complete?
The protocol is to juice as your daily base while removing one food from your normal diet every two weeks, making a chart of everything you eat. That gradual pace teaches the body's molecules to detox drop by drop without becoming ill, while the juices replace the old frequency with a higher one. Don't overdo it: no more than two cups of green juice at a time, don't mix green and fruit juices, and have a vegetable or avocado shake if you feel sick. The talk also recommends probiotics and distilled water alongside, and says dairy, gluten and GMO foods should be cut out permanently (its view). See the health note in 4.5.
What's the difference between an out-of-body experience and bilocation, and what does bilocation feel like?
An out-of-body experience is the astral body travelling (see S2 3, Questions). Bilocation is physically taking your body into another timeline, above 3D, such as 5D. It feels normal, like here, only less dense: a different timeline with a different frequency, resonance and geometry. The small version is reaching another room without remembering the hallway (see 4.7 and its note).
Do you recommend monoatomic gold as a supplement?
The talk had no personal experience with it, and pointed to the Harmonic Reactor book: it increases the purity of the molecular structure, based on frequency and teleportation. It also pointed to someone who makes a similar product: "look into Kevin Hay" and his Ormus, "the guy knows what he's talking about".
From the booksHR describes monoatomic gold mainly as a material applied to the antenna of the Harmonic Reactor device, to increase resonance for "clear voyage", and says it "opens every molecule to vibrate with clear signal." It doesn't describe it as a supplement to take.
Added note on supplements

Monoatomic gold and "Ormus" products are unregulated, and their claimed benefits are not proven. Check with a doctor or pharmacist before taking any supplement.

What's the difference between alkaline "Kangen" water and distilled water?
She had never heard of Kangen water: "distilled water has the negative charge; I will always use the distilled water." On charged or energized water in general: it puts a frequency into the water, which is fine, but the body may not be ready for it, or you may overdo it. The same frequency over and over can shut the system down; like a medicine, it has to be measured (see S2 3, Questions).
How exactly is the water-frequency protocol done, and does the waveform matter?
Use an online tone generator and distilled water, one glass at a time, in a separate room: 3 Hz for 20 minutes, then drink it; then a fresh glass at 70 Hz for 20 minutes, drink it; then a third glass at 90 Hz for 20 minutes, drink it. This is a generic practice for keeping a healthy vibration, done once a day for three days; it does not guarantee healing, and anything individual needs individual timing. The square waveform is used simply because you can hear it; the vibration reaches the water even when you can't hear it (see S1 19, Questions; S2 2, Questions).
Added health note

This is not medical treatment and is not proven to treat any condition. If you are ill, see a doctor; use any such practice only alongside medical care.

How long should you take probiotics?
The talk is clear that it is not a doctor. From experience: six months to a year for its own family's gut repair, or perhaps a month or two as a boost. She uses Visbiome (seven to nine strains, many parts per billion); it arrives in a box packed with ice because it's alive, and she keeps two boxes in her fridge. Over-the-counter probiotics may not be potent enough ("one strain is not going to do anything"). Take it with distilled water only, on an empty stomach in the morning and again right before bed, so it repairs the tissue and villi of the gut. She repeats her advice to cut out dairy, gluten and GMO foods. Ask your doctor or pharmacist what suits you.
