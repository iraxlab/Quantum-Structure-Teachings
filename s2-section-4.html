Bilocation of the Body

How body and heart, tuned together as one symphony, shift you into a completely different timeline; why the body has to be raised slowly; meeting other beings; and questions on detox, water and supplements (live talk with questions)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

Bilocation, said to be the future of humanity, is more than shifting moment to moment between parallel realities: it is shifting your whole being into a completely different timeline, for example from 3D Earth to 5D Earth. For that, consciousness alone isn't enough; the body has to come too. Its molecules must be raised, slowly, until they vibrate in one synchronized symphony; joined with heart-felt thoughts and emotional tone, body and heart become one unit, and that unit is the vehicle, with no spacecraft needed. The talk ties this to its detox and diet protocol, which it says must go drop by drop, never all at once, and not at all without a doctor for people with health conditions. Contact with other beings works the same way: a third reality, built on similar resonance, from the heart.

#Key teachings

#4.1The download: your body is the vehicle

The download: to achieve bilocation for physical contact, you must regulate and increase the molecular structure of your body. All the molecules have to shift their vibration in a synchronized modality, which creates a specific symphony of your singular frequency rotation. When it reaches enough potency, it is easy, just by thoughts and emotional tone, to shift the timeline, not only the parallel reality. "That is mastery."

You don't need a spacecraft or technology to bilocate, because your body's energetic form, connected with your thought and emotion, is the vehicle. You are the hologram, which expands to infinity and collapses to infinity (see S2 2.5); and since everything is frequency, you can shift and bilocate anywhere in the structure, and back and forth.

From the booksSD prints this download almost word for word under the heading "By Location", ending: "You don't need spacecraft or technology… because your body energetic form connecting with the thought and that emotion is the vehicle… So… come home. We are teaching you." DP adds that a "New Human" is emerging, with "telepathy, bilocation".

#4.2Parallel reality or whole timeline?

Every timeline has a location, X and Y, latitude and altitude, measured in degrees through 360; and each location is a hologram, a new Earth, with its own frequency, sound and timeline, and it is fractal (see S2 3.1). Moment to moment you are always shifting between parallel realities (see S1 1.3). Bilocation is a different level: shifting into a completely different timeline, with a different frequency.

So if you are in 3D and want to be on 5D Earth, you have to train your body so that the vibration of its molecules matches the resonance of the fifth-dimensional timeline, including the angle of spin of the molecules (see S2 3.3). It all fits together.

#4.3Body and heart as one unit

Once the body is clean and its frequency rises, consciousness comes into fruition, and how you behave matters: if you live in the heart structure, that is the consciousness that comes through. You have to combine the heart with the body into one unit moving as one, which is the symphony: vibration, geometry, location and degree. Combine them and you create an image of reality.

#4.5Drop by drop, never all at once

The guidance's instruction for any cleanse: you can't flush the body all at once. If you do, you overwhelm the molecular structure, the system shuts down, and you deplete it; that is why some people look depleted after a cleanse. Open the tap drop by drop, so the body's molecules learn to accept the new frequency and the flushing stays steady, without being pulled "out of orbit".

The protocol described: keep your normal meals while adding juices, and remove one food every two weeks (for example beef first, then chicken, and so on), so the change is gradual. Drink no more than two cups of green juice at a time; don't mix fruit and green juices; if you feel nauseous, have a vegetable or avocado shake instead. The talk describes going much further over a year, eventually to juice only and to near-breatharian eating, but says clearly that it does not recommend that to anyone. It also says the protocol is not for people with diabetes, kidney disease or liver disease: they need to detox with doctors.

It must also be your own choice and will: if you don't take action, the guidance backs off until you're ready.

Important health note

These are the talk's own practices, not medical advice. Long juice-only periods and anything close to "breatharianism" (living without food) are dangerous and have caused deaths. Before cutting out food groups, starting a cleanse or changing your diet significantly, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian, especially if you are pregnant, have diabetes, kidney or liver disease, an eating disorder, or take medication. The talk's claims that gluten is a "poison" for everyone, and about GMOs and alcohol, are its views; gluten is harmful for people with coeliac disease or gluten sensitivity, and a doctor can test for that.

#Questions answered

How long does the detox last? Is it a permanent change, and is there a move back to some cooked food? How do I know when it's complete?
The protocol is to juice as your daily base while removing one food from your normal diet every two weeks, making a chart of everything you eat. That gradual pace teaches the body's molecules to detox drop by drop without becoming ill, while the juices replace the old frequency with a higher one. Don't overdo it: no more than two cups of green juice at a time, don't mix green and fruit juices, and have a vegetable or avocado shake if you feel sick. The talk also recommends probiotics and distilled water alongside, and says dairy, gluten and GMO foods should be cut out permanently (its view). See the health note in 4.5.
What's the difference between an out-of-body experience and bilocation, and what does bilocation feel like?
An out-of-body experience is the astral body travelling (see S2 3, Questions). Bilocation is physically taking your body into another timeline, above 3D, such as 5D. It feels normal, like here, only less dense: a different timeline with a different frequency, resonance and geometry. The small version is reaching another room without remembering the hallway (see 4.7 and its note).
Do you recommend monoatomic gold as a supplement?
The talk had no personal experience with it, and pointed to the Harmonic Reactor book: it increases the purity of the molecular structure, based on frequency and teleportation. It also pointed to someone who makes a similar product ("Ormus").
From the booksHR describes monoatomic gold mainly as a material applied to the antenna of the Harmonic Reactor device, to increase resonance for "clear voyage", and says it "opens every molecule to vibrate with clear signal." It doesn't describe it as a supplement to take.
Note on supplements

Monoatomic gold and "Ormus" products are unregulated, and their claimed benefits are not proven. Check with a doctor or pharmacist before taking any supplement.

What's the difference between alkaline "Kangen" water and distilled water?
The talk didn't know that water and stays with distilled water. How exactly is the water-frequency protocol done, and does the waveform matter?
Use an online tone generator and distilled water, one glass at a time, in a separate room: 3 Hz for 20 minutes, then drink it; then a fresh glass at 70 Hz for 20 minutes, drink it; then a third glass at 90 Hz for 20 minutes, drink it. This is a generic practice for keeping a healthy vibration, done once a day for three days; it does not guarantee healing, and anything individual needs individual timing. The square waveform is used simply because you can hear it; the vibration reaches the water even when you can't hear it (see S1 19, Questions; S2 2, Questions).
Important health note

This is not medical treatment and is not proven to treat any condition. If you are ill, see a doctor; use any such practice only alongside medical care.

How long should you take probiotics?
The talk is clear that it is not a doctor. From experience: six months to a year for its own family's gut repair, or perhaps a month or two as a boost. It favours a potent, multi-strain probiotic that is shipped chilled, taken with distilled water on an empty stomach, morning and night, and repeats its advice to cut out dairy, gluten and GMO foods. Ask your doctor or pharmacist what suits you.
