The big idea

The Council of Nine said symbols are live etheric fields: geometry (such as a pentagram) combined with voice, ritual and intention, placed on the planet's electromagnetic grid, becomes a coherent field that stays in consciousness as a waveform. Anyone whose thoughts and emotions match its vibration can download it, which is why lowering people's frequency (through food, media, music) lets dark symbolism enter their field. But positive intention is ten times more potent and can quickly overtake it. When you listen to their channelings, protective symbols are laid in your field; keep them active by matching their resonance through your heart, thoughts and deeds. Knowledge once downloaded into the field, like the pyramid information, is there for anyone who resonates.

#31.1Grow food together

Where economies are strained, the Council's advice for those with family land is to start producing food now, preferably on land that already has trees or crops, and to learn to preserve food. Villages survive hard times. Without land, join friends; one person she met feeds five families from his land and livestock. Do it as a community, not alone, so neighbors help rather than take in hard times.

Only those whose vibration doesn't resonate stay under the trance.

There's no time: what happened in childhood or two minutes ago is another reality; switch your emotions, thoughts and deeds.

#Questions answered

I used pop and disco music to raise my vibration. Any advice?
Most music is tuned to 440 Hz, which doesn't match your DNA's sequence, like a blurry double image. 432 Hz is coherent with how God made us: it unlocks the DNA (there is no junk DNA), activates the chakras and can open a portal to a higher timeline. Beware catchy songs whose lyrics or imagery chant dark symbolism; choose concerts and music carefully, and favor pleasant 432 Hz music and nature.
Can we collapse into one unified DNA field and return to the omniverse?
It's already there: a thick white-gold line or cylinder of light whose sound is the current of consciousness; zoom in and you see realities within realities, every thought and symbol co-existing. Negative pockets gather by resonance and spin faster in a Fibonacci spiral until, insignificant within the light, they burst and dissipate. That rhythm is "the breath of consciousness": it breathes in, collects, and breathes out what is off-balance. Negative can never sustain itself in consciousness.
I've channeled since February but need help getting out of the way.
Keep practicing, even if it seems wrong; she'll practice in workshops. A weak laser of thought dissolves before it locks onto a reality. Above all, be calm and have no expectation of the answer: expecting an outcome tries to steer what the field holds, and blurs the download (as worry about family can for her).

As received, briefly: Poland will face outside disturbances but hold the line; Canada may see more nonsensical restrictions and needs more people to stand up, with its future tied to US-Canada relations; New Zealand may see volcanic activity, a land dispute with native people, and the discovery of deep, healing drinking water under volcanic rock.
