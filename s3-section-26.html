Be the Warrior in You

Why the path becomes clear when realities collapse, why evolution takes time, facing the darkness to transform it, bravery over fame, why constantly searching for a safer place brings no peace, the ego as a loop, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

"It's your warrior time." A warrior here isn't violent: it's someone who faces what is hard instead of hiding from it, stands their ground, and transforms it through the truth held in the heart. You can't transform darkness you refuse to look at; and you can't hurry evolution either, because two structures need time to accept each other before a new one forms. What the guides look for isn't fame or glory but brave souls who take a stand for humanity without fear. And peace won't come from running to a safer place: it comes from reading your own hologram with a clear mind.

#Key teachings

#26.1When realities collapse, the path is clear

When two probabilities collapse into the third (1 + 1 = 3), you reach zero point, "a complete click", and see clearly what your path is: every situation leads you to the action. It's still a choice, "but the reasonable choice". Those far off balance are often "pinched against the wall" with no choice but to act, because they've been awakened and have something accumulated to clear.

#26.2Why evolution takes time

"Evolution does not happen quickly." There is no time, but for two structures to merge into something new, "they need to accept each other into the platforms of the magnetic and gravitational fields. That's what is actually so-called time": the curvature needed for a new platform to collapse permanently (see S3 16.2). "Humans, why the rush?" How would you learn to construct and deconstruct if you never saw what a dense platform does and how to get out of it?

#26.3To transform darkness, you have to see it

"In order to transform the darkness into light, you have to see how it works." You can't hide from your own structure. Those who refuse to look at the truth, especially about harm done to children, create realities that aren't beneficial to them. The truth about the children will unite people of every party, globally. So face it, support those who protect children, share what is true, and act in your own life: "your children are your future."

Support

If you suspect a child is being harmed, contact the police or child-protection services. In the US, the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline is 1-800-422-4453; the National Human Trafficking Hotline is 1-888-373-7888.

#26.4Bravery, not fame

"Your fame and glory will not be the criteria for us. What all of the galactic are going to look for is brave souls taking a stand in the name of humanity, and fear has no place in that" (see S3 4.2). Most people hide from hard information; facing it, using it and taking an action is what changes things.

#26.5Hard events wake people up

Are fires, explosions and unrest good or bad? "Both." They push people to look at what they ignored. Someone who is awake can see the same events without getting upset, because they know the truth and can steer their own reality: "knowledge is power." Even the confused are pushed by confusion toward seeing what is happening: "it's all part of your awakening." And any frequency directed against people can be reversed by another frequency, "transformed to save humanity".

#26.6No peace in running from place to place

Asked which continent will be safest: within any "chaotic" place you can create a wonderful life. "If you're constantly searching for different locations, you're not allowing your consciousness to project positive outcomes no matter where you are." Fear and a confused mind jumping between options never find peace. A clear mind in neutrality reads the hologram; synchronicities then show the right path, how and when.

#26.7Other people's success is yours to feel

"There's no one outside of us." When you see someone successful, imagine the joy of being as successful as them, embed that resonance, feel it now, then act to find the tools: "you cannot imagine it if it doesn't exist already with you."

#26.8Everything you do here echoes

Are we busy in other dimensions too? Yes: every fractal reality comes from the one particle and mirrors it. "If you do something incredible here, you're also going to have that momentum in your higher self, in other fractals of reality, including the extraterrestrial platforms": your lineage is never erased. Even the council is part of you: "you are us, and you are it." Better contact with them comes simply from understanding: "you simply are".

#26.9The ego, and the warrior

The ego is a self-illusion not connected with your core: an inverted self-expression, so each reality after it loops "and will never reach your freedom" (see S3 21.3). The way out is the warrior's: when a challenge comes, "face it, stand your ground, and no matter how challenging, transform it through the knowledge of the truth that you hold in your heart, in the name of all humans and Mother Earth. This is how you gain power for truth."

#The method — Facing a Challenge Like a Warrior

  1. Turn toward it: name the challenge honestly; don't look away.
  2. Stand your ground: breathe, centre, neutral.
  3. Ask what the truth in your heart says about it.
  4. Transform: gratitude for what it shows you; shut the old program down.
  5. Take one brave action, without fear: share, protect, speak, change.
  6. Repeat every time it returns (26.9).
