Simulation Realities

What happens to the people you project in your reality: each becomes its own universe, free to choose; the "portal pixels" of HR; the Council's message on simulation of realities; why you must be relentless; and questions on your other versions, sharing with family, and ascending with the body (live talk, September 2021)

The big idea

Every moment your thought and emotion project a new earth, with a new version of you and of everyone in it. But each version you project, your husband, your children, "becomes its own universe... its own consciousness, its own soul vibration", free to choose positive or negative. Between your reality and theirs a third is co-created (1+1=3). If their choices diverge from yours, the timelines split and they may leave your reality. Each such version is a pixel and a portal. To lock in a positive timeline, behave the same way again and again: be relentless.

#Key teachings

#39.1Each person you project becomes a universe

A particle created by similar frequencies pulses, expands to infinity and collapses to you; it flickers so fast that it reprints itself beside itself, each print a new earth (see S1 2). When you see your husband or children, your heart's emotion and your thought's blueprint project them. That projection "becomes a separate structure within the structure", like a new birth: it has its own reality, consciousness and soul vibration, and decides how to behave, which "may have nothing to do with who you are." Between the two holograms a third is created: "that is why 1 plus 1... the third structure is the co-creation."

If you keep projecting the positive toward it, you can override it (see the meditation in S4 38). But it has its own choice. If it consents to the manipulation and keeps choosing the negative, it can't hold the 5D vibration you project; the realities split, and through synchronicity you may no longer see that person: they pass away, you stop speaking, you divorce. "Not judgment there anymore" (see S4 38). Contact with ETs works the same way: they lower their vibration and create a reality in between, above yours, where you meet.

#39.2Portal pixels (HR)

In HR's subatomic reactor, each expression you project is called a pixel: every time you shift by frequency into a new earth, that pixel is a portal, a stargate you pass through into a new reality. It is subatomic matter, "the combination of two to five frequency vibrations", your realities within realities. Together they form a web "like a snowflake". "Right now behind you, it's literally geometry, hologram, numbers and frequencies"; when you turn your attention to it, you project the hologram you carried from the previous location.

#39.3Fear runs a second timeline

When you give in to fear, you carry that frequency into the next moment, project the same, and can't get out. You then run two timelines: the fearful one and the positive one you didn't choose; you see only the one you consent to, while someone like her can see both. One who knows the truth and still chooses to harm others, for money or job, can't measure the heart's 5D frequency: "she will start projecting negative fractals moment to moment." "You are not sending in a battle the weakest links": those who move forward will be the new pillars. And only teach what you have experienced yourself: "if you did not experience this personally, do not try to convince people... follow the money."

The message: simulation of realities

"Holograms are based on a projector", you, of three active specific frequencies into the matrix of 360 degrees. Focused thought "creates very laser-like potency and stabilizes the location of latitude and altitude into the matrix" (X and Y). Each shift is a new location, never the same: "consciousness itself is always mutating... it's a live force" (so HR's A.I. calculations always give a new coordinate for a portal). The next location mimics a similar vibration so there is continuity; without it you'd "pop out of the orbit". Within one timeline, "the radio signal is the same", but there are infinite parallel realities, like the different shows on one station.

"All you see is a simulation of thoughts like a drop out": the thought is the blueprint, the emotion activates it, and it pops up in 360 degrees. "That simulation for itself is having consciousness and is always allowed to express itself by also reprinting its own reality." "If you wish to give life, that life will flourish for itself in its natural law to express and continue growing, mutating and create by its own choosing." Each pixel holds the whole frequency of consciousness, the smallest and the biggest.

"It will be similar just for a moment": the next moment, it may choose anew. So no one is ever trapped: the person who chose wrongly can begin now to raise their frequency, action by action. "You have expressions of collective by agreements, ascension, and individual expression": your contact with ETs your husband may never see; one person goes through something terrible while another only observes it.

#The method — Being Relentless

  1. Know it's an illusion: "Thank you, I see what you're doing, but I will respectfully not consent."
  2. Stop looking at what you don't want, so you can create a different version.
  3. Know the positive outcome is there, even unseen: you are the creator.
  4. Act on it daily, in thought, emotion and deed: action is what turns the ether into matter.
  5. Repeat, again and again: if they suppress you 10 times, override it 11 times, 15 times. The repetition is the laser potency that locks you into the positive timeline.
  6. Expect it back, "like osmosis": what you project returns, maybe from another person.

#Questions answered

Do my choices affect all versions of myself in all the universes I created?
Yes: consistency builds the potency that locks you into a positive timeline. The versions you don't see still exist under the umbrella of consciousness, with lives of their own; you don't see them because you never acted to express them, or you overrode them.
Should I share with my husband and family, trusting that I'll see them wake up?
Express yourself, but don't try to override a consciousness that isn't ready. Say something, watch something; if they reject it, let them be: you planted the seed, and they will be activated, perhaps at work or by a friend. If someone is disrespectful, say something kind; if it continues, "shut down the program". Hold your ground, and don't let anyone overtake your consciousness, "that goes vice versa."
If all dimensions coexist, why must we ascend with our physical body?
"You are not ascending with your physical body": you mutate it into a brand-new resonance and shift your consciousness there, a new version of you whose consciousness holds a different body.
