Q & A: The State of Being You Project

Why answers come with timing, a channelled message about children and contact, breaking blame loops, saying the state you want rather than what you reject, you as the creator, and many questions (live talk)

The big idea

Answers come when the timing allows, and always as the end result, never the path. Each person's ascension runs in their own hologram. A long channelled answer spoke of humanity's children: new souls, coming through new portals, who will carry the unity of the new Earth. In daily practice, the key is what you project: when someone blames you, neutralize it, or the same vibration returns through others; and when you speak, state the state of being you want ("I am free", "I am healthy") instead of naming what you reject. You are always on your path; only the belief that you're not keeps you off it. The guides are not gods: "God you are".

#Key teachings

#21.1Answers come with timing

Sometimes an answer can't be given: it isn't the timing yet (the teacher was not allowed to speak about the HR material for a year), or the person must learn a lesson first, or a situation within months will show them what to do. What is given is always the end result, never the steps in between (see S2 10.1). The planet is ascending anyway, and within it each person's ascension runs on their own platform, according to what they do, think and act on.

#21.2Through a storm: shelter within the lesson

A warning was repeated about flooding on one coastline, with the advice not to go there (a prediction). But don't be afraid: even within a lesson, you'll find what you need. The teacher was once in the middle of a historic storm and found water, food and shelter, a reality within the reality (see S2 15.6). She didn't skip the lesson; she went through it "on target": emotions, thoughts and deeds knowing she was fine, and taking the necessary practical measures. "The end of the world" means the end of the three-dimensional structure, a shift to a new Earth.

Added note

Follow official weather and emergency guidance where you live or travel; knowing you're fine and taking practical precautions go together.

#21.3A message about the children

Asked when school systems will change, the Council of Nine answered at length: it is already changing. They spoke of great sorrow for the suffering of children on Earth, and said new portals had to open because of it. The souls now coming into human bodies carry a fifth-dimensional frequency and come "in the name of humanity": as they enter, the planet's resonance rises, and outcomes change "wave after wave". Separate religions belonged to the old separation; what's coming is "one religion, which is unity consciousness". Some are called "star children".

The council also spoke about contact in three "platforms": experiences in which people were taken aboard, which they said were done with the person's own higher self to preserve humanity's DNA program; staged "abductions" designed by humans to create fear of extraterrestrials (see S2 18.5); and a third, the mastery: raising your own molecular frequency through diet, the heart and the DNA codes, guided individually, until the body can bilocate through portals. Its order: telepathic contact first, then dietary change, then a change of the heart's frequency. Whatever happens around you, stay in the positive, "to keep the potency".

Added note

These are channelled claims. Claims in the answer about vaccines altering DNA, and about hidden groups, are not supported by evidence and are left out of the teaching. Vaccines do not change your DNA.

#21.4Break the blame loop

If you were blamed for something you didn't do, and now it keeps happening with others, it's an illusion you're reprinting. "If I blame you now for what your brother did, would you take it seriously? I wasn't there." When it happens, sit and watch: "Very interesting; I understand it's a hologram", and neutralize the frequency (you needn't say it aloud). Your response calls the frequency from the previous reality, so you keep shifting into similar situations, and because probabilities are infinite, the same vibration can arrive through someone else (see S1 2, S1 3).

#21.5You are always on the path

"You are always on the right track, because you're learning through the experiences." Even when you fluctuate (the teacher kept slipping on her diet, and was told: "Look at the action you took"), the journey makes you stronger. The only thing keeping you off your path is the belief that you're not on it: change the perception, and you're on it. Activation can make the body shiver: it's like reaching a new altitude and learning to breathe there. Relax, breathe through it, and keep giving your best; healing begins in the heart, with a clean structure of giving without conditions (see S2 19).

#21.6Say the state of being, not what you reject

For anyone changing a habit (the example was an app for people recovering from addiction): don't say "I don't drink anymore". Naming what you reject keeps its frequency, and it mimics and reprints. State the state of being you want to project, in the present, as "I am": "I am healthy", "I drink juices", "I am free". Repeat it every half hour, and feel it as you say it: "repetition, here's your potency". Avoid even the word for the problem. The person must know they have the power (see S2 2.4, S2 2.8).

Further design guidance: no reds; let each person choose their own soothing colour, because a colour resonates with a particular consciousness (the teacher's is a soft grey-blue); and for voice or music, tune to 432 Hz.

Support

Addiction is a medical condition; affirmations can support recovery alongside professional treatment and support groups, not replace them.

#21.7Names carry sound, but meaning is yours

A name has a sonic vibration and can carry associations from how it has been used; fear, for example, can pass from a parent to a child as a carried frequency. But the meaning of a word is given through your own perspective. Neutralize it, accept that this is what it was, and choose to change it: study the protocol, and you can switch the program (see S2 20.3).

#21.8You are the creator

"Let's see what reality you're going to create." The guidance won't decide your reality for you: you have the knowledge and the freedom; create what you wish from the heart. Even choosing otherwise is still creation, but now everything returns quickly, like a boomerang. "They are not our gods; they are assisting": "God you are", and "even we are our own expression." They are always communicating, and anything you're doing now is a projection you made: "create, don't stop creating."

#21.9Protect your project

When invited into a promising venture: stay alert, as people's approaches can fluctuate. Don't do anything without a contract; use an independent lawyer, not one on the other side; don't give everything away at once; release information slowly. Many start with big ideas and, along the way, become greedy (see S2 19.5).

#21.10Earthquakes and the Earth's fluid

Asked about shifting plates, the guidance said fossil "fuel" isn't what we're told: it is like the blood in the Earth's veins, a fluid that lets tectonic plates slide smoothly past each other, "like oil", so there are no earthquakes. More earthquakes may come, but "they could be stopped; you have the technology" (claims).

From the booksSD gives a method to "Subside Earthquakes with Hz": measure the frequency of the landmasses and "stay steady to '0' point", so the earthquake subsides; "you can also develop a machine like that."

#21.11Entering another reality in a dream

Yes, you can experience someone else's reality. Lucid, the teacher focused, opened a portal with her thoughts, and found herself in another being's body (an extraterrestrial man), flying a craft toward her galactic family, until she was sent back: "you are not allowed to be there" (see S2 17.9). She didn't overtake him; it was a knowing that she was there with him. But remember, that "could be another version of you."

#The method — Affirm the State of Being

  1. Choose the state you want, in the present: "I am healthy", "I am free".
  2. Leave out the problem word entirely, and any "don't" or "no more".
  3. Say it as "I am", about yourself, not as an instruction to others.
  4. Feel it as you say it: the feeling is the frequency.
  5. Repeat every half hour: set a reminder.
  6. Surround it with what soothes you: your colour, gentle sound (see 21.6).

#Questions answered

Will our school systems change, so children get the education they deserve?
Yes, it's already changing: see 21.3.
For a year I've seen a sun-coloured fluid moving along my lower eyelids; once my eye pulsed for hours. What is it?
It was read as related to the retina ("almost like a twitching"), with bacteria involved.
Added safety note

Moving lights, flashes or pulsing in your vision can signal a retinal problem. See an eye doctor promptly, urgently if it's sudden or increasing.

I have palpitations and stabbing chest pains; tests were near-normal, but my blood pressure is higher. Is it energy? And I wake up often and feel exhausted.
A nutritional doctor, a detox and blood work, under a doctor's supervision. For the exhaustion: check the thyroid, including for nodules.
Added safety note

Chest pain can be serious. Seek emergency care for chest pain with breathlessness, sweating, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw; otherwise follow up with your doctor about the palpitations and blood pressure.

I lost a small, high-frequency green glass object; I feel it's in another version of my room. Where is it?
"You won't find it": for now, it's not at your vibration.
My brother blamed me for something he did, and now it keeps happening with others. How do I stop it?
See 21.4.

"You need to wait": the only answer given.

Be very cautious with offers of large "releases" of money through cryptocurrency; they are a common form of fraud.

See 21.5: you are; the belief that you're not keeps you off it. (A more specific question gets a more specific answer.)

See 21.7: a frequency passed from mother to son. Nothing major: study the protocol, and you'll switch it.

Read as pinched, with missing fluid and cartilage needing rebuilding; physical therapy eases the pain but doesn't resolve it. Look into professional help to rebuild cartilage and collagen; pain and numbness may run down the back of the leg.

Persistent back pain with numbness down the leg should be assessed by a doctor or spine specialist; ask about further imaging.
