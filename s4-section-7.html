#The method — Automatic Writing

  1. Get comfortable: she does it in bed, with pillows around her and her hands resting on pillows.
  2. Use a plain notebook from the dollar store and the cheapest, very light pencils.
  3. Start scribbling: "I don't care what comes out."
  4. Relax and meditate (the Galactic Meditation is the blueprint), then ask a question.
  5. Write down the first thing that comes, even a negative thought; if negative, "thank you very much" and pull away from it: don't reject it, transform it.
  6. Practise like a baby learning to walk: you'll fall sometimes; don't get discouraged, and have fun.
