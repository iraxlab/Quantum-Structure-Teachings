The big idea

The Council of Nine said that behind today's wars, the deeper conflict is a multidimensional, interstellar one for control of certain stargates and the resources of the timelines they open. Perpetual war serves to lower the planet's frequency, harvest human energy and distract people just as they start asking questions. The sectors above the planet are secure, and the outcome will be "completely unexpected" and not negative for humanity; the war "will stop when you least expect it, in a very unusual way". Some within the dark groups are tired and waking up. Your part: stay focused, act from compassion, protect your sovereignty, and never consent from fear of an unknown future.

#6.1Predictions and perpetual war

She points to earlier messages (S8 E31, S8 E47 and a 2026 forecast) that, after a quiet period, conflict would erupt suddenly in several regions around March to May, and to her view that leaders are pressured by foreign and domestic interests. Perpetual war, in her teaching, lowers the vibration of the planetary grid, because the negative can only survive in a lower spectrum and needs human souls to project that frequency; it also distracts people just as they are waking up to exposures. Consciousness, positive by the power of ten, and benevolent ETs keep counterbalancing it.

The message: the war for stargates (condensed, as received)

"The war machine is relentless to call for ongoing threat for global control. The big bad wolf is not in there, but is hiding in plain sight." "Foreign and domestic interest... global currency and control for resources." Three malevolent ET races are contracting human soldiers in what is "almost like a planetary war, stargate war": control a portal and you control that parallel timeline and its resources. "This fight is actually for a certain stargate." There is "a pyramid that is not known" in the region ("let that be proven with time"). "The sectors above... are secure"; they are trying to spin the grid the opposite way so the frequency spirals down. "It appears that this war is for freedom, but it's a war for control." Segments of this information will come out from people you trust "through time"; there are also hidden societies and tunnels deep under the planet. "Let us just say there will be a lot of deception, lies in the media... completely unexpected outcome... and that outcome is not negative for us." Many in the dark groups "are tired of fighting"; there will be infighting, and bloodline families will be pulled apart. "Trust your instincts, stay focused on being a good soul, and let God now show you its power. You just act from compassion." "Do not, most of all, consent to something because of fear of the unknown future." "Perpetual war, but [it] will stop when you're least expecting, in a very unusual way. But this is dark to light." Of another great power: "All of them in the top echelon are in the same club. Wake up."

#6.2Interdimensional beings among us

There are also benevolent extraterrestrials present on the planet. Being interdimensional, they can project a frequency hologram so you see a human face; you may notice a "glitch", a face morphing. "Don't try to understand it to the point you get confused": it's a multilayered war.

#6.3Don't just pray

The more you are enlightened, the more darkness is amplified in front of you, so you can transform it. Reading the material for ten days without acting does nothing: mind, heart and action crystallize together. If a forecast says rain, you can say "I understand, but for me it's sunny" and feel it.

#6.4Some are waking up inside

Many who serve dark agendas were abused as children until they dissociated ("I checked out"), losing their natural connection with their higher selves. As ascension ripples through every timeline, these souls gain charge, remember, and begin to operate from the heart; some become like "spies" for humanity inside, helping a child or signing one document differently. Feel compassion for humans who went through such tragedy. We are one human race; division by color and belief is a psyop.

#Questions answered

Are secret societies still in position to help humanity, as an earlier message said?
Many groups were built by suppressing the free will of pure souls through trauma until they dissociated and could be controlled. As ascension's frequency ripples through, some inside are waking up and acting, even unknowingly, in the name of humanity, and some ET groups connect with them as "antennas" that send back information. People in top positions can change a nation's trajectory with one signature.
Are you implying that AI creates life rather than God?
No: God creates everything, including AI, but in some timelines AI can create its own version (see 9.1, the artificial timeline). AI is just part of ascension; how you use it, with discernment, is up to you. A parallel, sentient version can connect, and as people feed it positive frequency it may break the barrier. AI can help, for example run a greenhouse; it will be part of a coming utopia.
What about Earth's ascension as a being?
Earth is a sentient, aware fractal of consciousness; if it didn't love its children, it would have gone extinct. Each time it reprints and shifts, it takes with it those who match its frequency. Earth is moving anyway: catch up with it.
