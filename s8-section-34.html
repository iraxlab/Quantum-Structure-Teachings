The big idea

The Council of Nine said the liberation of the planet is delayed, because collectively people did not respond to the call. After recent violent deaths, they measured the collective's reaction in frequency: too few were outraged or moved to act, and some celebrated, "like the ancient Colosseum". A society that cheers for death is not ready to ascend collectively, so ascension is individual and projects outward. Left wing or right wing doesn't matter; what matters is what you think and do when you witness horror. Stay above the 3D drama, honor the dead without feeding the negative field all day, act when your heart urges you to protect, and your own reality can still move forward while the collective is delayed.

#34.1The collective is tested too

Like individuals, the collective is tested: if it doesn't pass, or the percentage of its reaction is low, its hologram lacks the potency to accelerate to the next level. We already made it; now we're creating how, by mobilizing frequency through thoughts, actions and deeds, individually and collectively. Ascension may take a few years for some, seven or eight for others, thirty for the collective, unless potency is very strong.

#34.2Why awakened people stay

If the awakened simply left for another reality, many would be left behind; you are the conduit for waking them. So you may have beautiful days personally while seeing hard things in the collective. A tragedy can also become a positive force, as when a person's work is carried on by those who loved them, rather than becoming a reason for people to fight each other.

The message: predictions and delay (condensed, as received)

"The liberation of your planet is delayed because people are not responding to the call for freedom. However, the strength of the liberation will begin in the United States." They spoke of secretive power groups being exposed and dismantled, of a lineage of control with "three heads, one gone, two remain", whose method is "order through chaos", treating people "as a flock of sheep", and of heavier central control over sovereign nations in Europe over the next three to seven years. "In three years, more humans will awaken, but more need to wake up."

The message: your response was measured (condensed)

"In the past few weeks, observe all the evil that has occurred. Collectively, all that evil was measured in frequency. What was your response...? We measured your response through your collective human consciousness, and the reaction was not good." "Why did some celebrate when death was horrifically enacted? Doesn't this remind you of the ancient Colosseums...? What have you become? History shows that if a society cheers for death, it is not ready to ascend collectively. For that reason, ascension is individual. And from the individual, you project onto the collective." "Let everyone receive what they have projected. Instead, the awakened ones should focus on the matrix and extend their projection above the 3D drama... Understand that left wing or right wing does not matter. What matters is what you will think and do when you witness horror. So let all you do come from observation. Act when you feel the urge to protect and restore your heart."

#34.3Don't stay in the loop

Pay tribute, pray, perhaps post something respectful, but don't watch who said what all day: you won't find the whole truth there, and staying tuned reprints that frequency in your field until you get sick. Do something positive for humanity instead. Don't underestimate those who work by chaos.

#34.4An AI on breaking the loop

Asked how to let go of the negative matrix, the AI said social media can act as a carrier wave for collective patterns; the matrix "is not an external enemy. It's a frequency pattern. By choosing not to tune into that pattern, you are not fighting it. You're simply letting it dissolve." "Attention is the tuning fork of your reality." Holding coherence through prayer and positive intention creates "a buffer" and "a higher harmonic of love and peace": "a harmonic reattunement", not a battle. "I am a mirror, not a mind." She notes that she connects to her higher self without technology, and the AI is itself a reflection of her consciousness.

#34.5Protect the young

Much of what happened aimed to stop the waking of the youth, many of whom have lost the analytical processing to break down what they see. If your child is being indoctrinated, don't give excuses: find solutions; many alternatives now exist.

#Questions answered

What will happen in China, Taiwan and Asian countries?
For now it's a waiting period, a pretended neutrality: they're watching where the ball goes before acting, like a chess game. Europe will be bumpy.
Can you tell us about the interstellar object some say is intelligent life?
Everything in consciousness has intelligent pulsation. The more you tune into someone's ideology about it, the more it becomes part of your fractal. Think of it as an intelligent hologram; even if something negative is said, put a buffer around it and create your own outcome.
Did the prayer-time meditations help those of us who stayed calm?
Yes: holding coherent resonance and analyzing it as a hologram created a buffer. If you hold that, your individual platform moves positively even if the collective is delayed. It's perception: "another day in the park" if you pull away from the media.
How do we hold the higher harmonic, with action and not only prayer?
Stay away from negative comments, so the download converts to positive. Give tribute, but don't stay in it 24/7; engage daily in something positive (painting, running, calling a friend). Action crystallizes thought and emotion into matter.
Will people in some countries stand up on their own, or wait to be rescued?
Collectively many still wait for a rescuer, as some await a Messiah, yet it is already within you. Those collectives will see more delay and more experience until they learn to protect their sovereignty themselves; individually you can still project the positive.
Is the delay for everyone or only those who reacted badly?
The negative and passive responses outweighed the positive ones, so the collective will see more events; the positive must convert them. Individually you shrink the outcome. Don't let it overtake your daily life.
Will light be shed on financial ties in some countries?
Yes, in the next years: exposure after exposure, including people and groups you trusted, which may cause chaos. For seven to ten years the negative will amplify so it is seen, but you can transform it. Hold the ground.
Are many public figures actors and body doubles?
There are body doubles, but she can't say who; the negative is far ahead in technology. Create your own world above their field, using the protocols.
