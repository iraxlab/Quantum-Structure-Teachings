Q & A: Expectations, Power and the Source Within

Keeping going, researching for yourself, your own power, protecting your vibration, why expectations block manifestation, individual and collective ascension, sharing without imposing, a nanotechnology exchange, and many questions (live talk)

The big idea

You are more powerful than you think: weakness is an illusion, because you are creating a whole reality. The source is not outside you, so once you learn the structure you don't need anyone to connect you. What blocks manifestation is often expectation: it runs a second hologram on top of the one that's manifesting, and two codes of different resonance can't coexist. When other people's moods pull you down, train yourself back with repetition until the new state crystallizes. Your ascension is individual within the collective, and the heart holds the codes that shift timelines. Share what you know, but don't impose it: the spark wakes in its own time.

#Key teachings

#23.1Keep going, even when you fall off

The teacher admitted she hasn't kept to her protocol: she does well for a few days, then an event comes and she slips. She says this so you know it isn't easy, and that falling off is part of it: "As long as you continue and as long as you try, you may try 101 times, and I guarantee the 102nd time you'll make it" (see S2 21.5).

Added safety note

The protocol she describes is a water-only fast. Do not attempt this: prolonged fasting is dangerous and can be fatal. Any change of diet belongs under a doctor's supervision.

#23.2Do your own research, then act

"Don't even trust me: do your own research", and your higher self will recognize what is right. Don't take a narrative blindly from any single source; look widely and check for yourself. Awakening spreads by sharing information, and "you cannot switch your timeline if you only think about the goodness without taking action to create the goodness." Action in unity is more potent: it changes the collective in your hologram, not only you. "Once you awaken, nobody can control you."

Added note

This talk included political predictions and commentary about named politicians, parties and countries, and claims that spraying from aircraft carries DNA-altering nanoparticles. These are left out: the book stays neutral on politics, and the health claims are not supported by evidence.

#23.3A currency change: don't panic

There is no update on a currency reset for a while: certain systems need to collapse first, but it will be a smooth transition. Don't panic and move money around; she was never taught to do that. With crypto offers, be cautious: there may be conditions, such as funds locked for a year and withdrawals only through a particular brokerage. Don't invest large sums blindly; a little, if you wish, kept in the positive (see S2 6.6).

Added note

This is not financial advice. Be wary of any offer that promises big returns or restricts withdrawals.

#23.4You are not weak: fight for your freedom from the heart

"Be clever with the clever" (see S2 22) means: analyze the entire situation and approach it with care. Learn patience: things work when done at the right time (she once had to stay silent about what she knew for a whole year). Get advice from people you trust, explain the situation with complete honesty, and don't feel bad about fighting for your freedom: if you do it through the heart, to protect yourself and your family, "you're not killing anyone." Sometimes that means legal steps, taken firmly and with integrity. Being gentle doesn't mean being weak.

"It's an illusion that you are weak." Power is a state of being you embed (see S2 21.6): educate yourself, read, talk, take the power, then act on what you've gathered. People who use strength negatively take advantage of those who believe they're weak. "If you were not powerful, you would not even create a reality" (see S2 5.8).

#23.5How much DNA can we activate?

Realistically, in this timeline, through ascension: up to 30%, not all of us have reached it yet, and it isn't exactly 30%. That includes shifting realities and creating new structures, for example meeting a being from a higher dimension in a third reality created between your structure and theirs (see S2 4.8). It's an individual process: each person creates their own timeline and ascension.

#23.6Protecting your vibration around others' moods

Your emotions are a resonant frequency, and you only fall into another person's anger because you take that reality for real. So: pull back and see it as a hologram; give gratitude to neutralize the cold hologram you're interacting with; remove yourself from the scene; calm down completely, to zero; then switch your thoughts and actions, even if you don't want to, with uplifting videos or music. Set a reminder every half hour for a couple of days. Repetition crystallizes the new resonance: new neurons sync and build a bridge, the axon, "like a growing tree's branches", until it becomes permanent and easy (see S1 1–3, S1 7).

From the booksDP: "Repetition, repetition, repetition in order to crystallize the new structure of energetic fields that will build the new synopsis - the new bridges between the axons of the brain."

#23.7You are the source

If you try to connect with the source each morning and it doesn't seem to work, it's because you're looking outside yourself. "The truth is that you are the source." Look within, at the mechanics and alchemy of your own creation, and it switches "like a light bulb": you realize you don't need teachers like her. Our energy was used to build artificial timelines, and now it's time to wake up: once you have the knowledge, "you're it."

From the booksSD: "You are the source and you are the prime creator."

#23.8Expectations block manifestation

Why can you see new, higher versions of others but not yet of yourself? Because you have expectations. Probabilities are infinite, and while you're learning, successive holograms are so similar you hardly notice the change; once the frequency is embedded, things you think of start happening within a day. An expectation has its own holographic image: it runs a hologram on top of the hologram. The manifestation is the more potent code, but two codes of different resonance can't coexist, so the expectation blocks it. When you stop expecting, it pops up, because that is the path of least resistance.

So: run with the program, learn everything, give gratitude for everything that happens, and stay steady on vibration, without doubting yourself or asking when and how. You may need lessons first to embody what you're learning. It took the teacher two years of constant helping and giving, with no expectation, before "the bubble exploded" (see S1 5.1). Each consciousness has its own signature, and each part expands into the collective.

From the booksNT and SD: "Give without expectations. Look for the manifestation without resistance. Then all will come to place through a synchronize signal."

#23.9When awakening makes you agitated: switch the resonance

New energies arriving can feel like a warrior's force, up one day and calm the next. That fluctuation is normal while the information integrates, through the synchronicities of your research, and it will settle. To calm it, do what puts you in a good resonance: the teacher listens to easy, loving audiobooks while she does other things, or to jazz while watching a city skyline at night on a big screen, because it makes her feel good. Find your own "little pixie": jumping up and down one day, music another, something inspirational a third. "It's all about switching the resonance."

#23.10Individual ascension within the collective

Ascension is both collective and individual: a hologram within the hologram. Two people each have their own reality and share a third (see S1 2.2, 1 + 1 = 3), but each experiences the collective ascension according to how well they've learned their platform. Staying negative projects a bumpy road; staying positive, you always find the bridge, whatever is happening. As we awaken, all of it collapses into one positive collective, which is why unity and supporting each other matter. Not only the mind: the heart holds the codes of the portals that shift the timelines.

#23.11Ride the wave moment to moment

With so many challenges, should you just hold your space? Ride the wave to the best of your abilities, moment to moment, using the quantum structure: when you fluctuate, make the end result of the fluctuation positive (see S1 3.3). That takes commitment and control of your mind and emotions, which are the magnetic and gravitational field that projects the matrix you shift into next. Don't see yourself as matter, but as an energetic field and a matrix.

#23.12Share, but don't impose

To help with a terrible situation in the world, "you don't go and change there. You change yourself", by expressing the best of yourself daily to the people around you. You can offer knowledge; you can't make anyone listen, and your truth isn't theirs to adopt. Once you give the spark, the seed is in their hologram and takes its own resonance: when the time is right, something else will catch it, and they may later tell you what you told them yesterday. Many will wake when events become too outrageous to ignore; anger or despair along the way is normal. If someone attacks you out of fear or ignorance, move forward: "better lose one person who is ignorant and gain another five." What matters is how you feel and project; that brings you the people who resonate with you (see S2 18).

Support

#23.13How to handle constant downloads

"I'm just having fun." Have no fear; it's almost like talking to yourself. If you tune into something negative, neutralize it and try again. At a certain level you trust yourself so much that you know every thought has a reason, and you realize you're downloading from the matrix. Sometimes it comes as an instant image in the third eye, and you break it down fractally so others understand; sometimes you sit quietly and listen to the thoughts coming in; with complex material, write it down. What comes matches what you agreed to do. Practised, it becomes a game you play with the platforms.

#23.14Nanotechnology: let each particle choose the next

For researchers building nano-structures: work with a positive intention, because the nano mimics the plasma and gravitational field around it, and secure the structure. One nano structure mimics the universe, and with a specific frequency it starts to mutate. When building a chain, let each particle choose the next particle, the proper version of itself; it takes about three days, then the bond is very strong and you can measure the outcome by resonance. Repeats go faster because the structure has memory in the matrix, yet each rebuild is a new structure with a new frequency. To separate an environment, mimic the trees: take the air in and release it pure, like oxygen. (For protection, though, you don't need a physical dome: you can switch platforms.)

From the booksSD: "Let it stay and let it stabilize by its self for 3 days. Let its consciousness build the calling for the next unit"; and "let the Nanoparticle choose their positions. After they choose their positions then give a new signal."

#The method — Return to Your Vibration

  1. Pull back: "this is a hologram", not your reality (23.6).
  2. Give gratitude to neutralize what you're interacting with.
  3. Remove yourself from the scene, physically if you can.
  4. Calm to zero: breathe until you're completely still.
  5. Switch with something uplifting: music, a video, whatever is your resonance (23.9).
  6. Drop the expectation of when and how; give gratitude for what is happening (23.8).
  7. Repeat, with a reminder every half hour, for several days, until it crystallizes.
