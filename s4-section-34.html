One of the Laws of Creation

Why what you witness is your business; the law of creation that gives every expression its place, and why destroying others destroys yourself; why no one can be truly separate from consciousness; and a question on co-parenting with someone on the other side (live talk, August 2021)

The big idea

Whatever you see in your hologram is another version of you, so "it's none of my business" is the opposite of the quantum structure. If you watch someone being abused and only pray, you accumulate it; act, peacefully. The law of creation: consciousness always makes a place for every expression, negative and positive, and keeps itself in balance. Whoever destroys others to get a desired outcome is destroying their own consciousness, because "it is only you". Consciousness will never self-destruct, so hold the line with love and stable resistance.

#Key teachings

#34.1What you witness is your business

We were taught that if the neighbours fight, or a husband beats his wife, it's "not our business." But in your hologram, which you project in 360 degrees, that woman "is another version of you": a reality within your reality (see S1 1). By doing nothing, "you're allowing within your structure another version of you being abused", and you accumulate it. Act peacefully: call the police, help the person. The same holds for what you see in your country or the world: talk with friends, peacefully; don't comply with what is wrong; take legal action together. "Not to go with the guns but to be a peaceful non-compliance."

Prayer alone builds a hologram in the ether. As in the Galactic Meditation (S4 9), the heart makes it pulse and action crystallizes it into the magnetic and gravitational field. But if you have told someone ten times and they keep rejecting it, don't try to overtake their consciousness: their truth is right for them now. Give the knowledge when it's needed, and support those who want to be free.

The Council watched the escalation "almost like we were kids and they were watching the kids now growing up to be adults", smiling: "the natural process of consciousness to move forward."

The message: one of the laws of creation

"To think that creation has its place only by your personal views is insane. The regulatory modalities of consciousness always creates place for all, negative and positive. It will reproject timelines and realities to always sustain itself." (Yin and yang always give each expression room to learn and choose; but go off balance and consciousness will correct it, because it must survive.)

To plan to depopulate "is against any human law and all laws of universal creation. To intentionally kill, so you can reproduce for yourself desirable outcome, it is a direct hit to your own existence, because into your own projection, it's all that you are and see, it is only you." "You are actually depopulating and exterminating your own consciousness", reprojecting timelines where "you will meet your own maker"; no technology will save it from self-destruction.

To humans: "Consciousness will never self-destruct. Anyone that is withholding the natural process of everyone to expand will face God's judgment for itself with no mercy. So you can learn to love and respect the trajectory sequences of your own self." "In order to learn, you all have to face what have you done to destroy", positive and negative alike.

"Hold the line with love and stable resistance and do not comply to the lies and tyranny, unite. You are in power." A by-law is written: the secrets will be exposed, "so no one will be ever hurt from you evils again." (A list of names she didn't understand, and dated predictions, are left out.)

Why they can't follow you. When you switch the resonance of your consciousness and body, you move into a timeline they can't comprehend: to accumulate that resonance, their heart must hold the codes, and the codes come only from thinking and behaving positively. And from within, people in those groups are awakening and turning, "like a viper", so suddenly that it will shock (see 26.2). "So you never ever give your sovereignty away again. It's a lesson, and we're graduating."

#The method — Acting on What You See

  1. Recognize it as yours: what you witness is a version of you in your hologram.
  2. Don't only pray: hold the picture, add the heart, then take a physical action.
  3. Act peacefully: call for help, speak calmly, refuse to comply with what is wrong, use the law, unite.
  4. Don't overtake: if someone rejects the knowledge many times, let them learn their way.
  5. Hold the ground in the heart, even when everything seems to crumble.

#Questions answered

How can anything separate itself from the umbrella of consciousness?
It can't: "they are thinking that they are separated." Within consciousness are infinite timelines, each with its own frequency; each fractal moment is a reality, and similar moments make a timeline (see S1 2). Some chose to express the negative and found they could overtake the positive ("why should I work, when somebody can give me everything for free?"). Like the A.I. hologram on a spacecraft that mimics your brain's vibration (see S4 31), they used technology to create their own timeline within the structure, and locked others into it by projecting their narrative through food, films and news. They forgot they are a fractal of the one spark: "God you are, but you are a fractal momentum." When people wake up, a part that became "like a cancer" is sabotaged so consciousness can move on.
How do I navigate co-parenting when the other parent supports the negative structure?
Very challenging, and common. He is a version you project, and from his perspective his idea is correct: no judgment. His shield was worn down by constant projection (see S4 22). Use the Galactic Meditation with him in place of the galactic family, the Protocol to Manifest Potency (S4 9) and the nine-day protocol (S3 8). Hold neutrality: "I see what you're doing... but I am in a hologram and I can overturn the outcome." Know the end result (he understands, your child is safe) and at the same time take action, looking for solutions such as lawyers; solutions appear as you act. It may take five months of moment-to-moment action. If you imagine it perfect, "this reality timeline already exists... because you cannot see it if it didn't happen already."

Why meditation matters. The image builds the hologram in the ether; the heart's emotion makes it pulse; daily action crystallizes it into matter. How long? It depends on your precision and consistency.
