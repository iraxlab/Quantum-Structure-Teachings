How to Take the Action to Manifest (Q&A)

What "action" means after the meditation; standing between two holograms; recognizing demoralization; acting from the heart and supporting those who do the work; and questions on music, time and space, harvest, doubt, death, the laws of creation, shadow work, sleep and dream recall (live talk, March 2021)

The big idea

After the meditation (S4 9), the action is this: in every fractal moment you stand in front of two holograms at once, the negative and the positive, inside your own hologram. You must see what the negative is doing ("how would you know it's negative if you don't know where you're at?"), and then choose an action from the heart that accumulates the positive. That shuts the other programme down: not by rejecting it, but by transforming it through what you choose to do.

#Key teachings

#10.1Where to learn the action

"What do I do next? What do you mean, action?" If you're asking that, you haven't studied Season 1: "the information of how you do that, it's all in here." The order: the Galactic Meditation; Season 4, episode 9 (the metaphysics of it, S4 9); Season 2, episode 13; then Season 3, episode 8 to practise the nine days. On her channel, press the playlist's name, not only the arrow, to see all its videos.

#10.2You must see the negative to choose

Reality within reality: the negative and the positive are plastered together, 1+1=3, and what manifests depends on how you fluctuate between them. Some say "I don't want to look at the negative, it's too much"; but to find the manipulation you have to recognize it, or "you're going to consent blindly". Drop the ego, however educated you are: lawyers learn "the law of the corporation", doctors learn pharmaceutical medicine rather than medicine from nature. Pull back, observe, analyze the facts (see S4 4.2).

#10.3Recognizing demoralization

She quotes two ways people are controlled: "first, it will frighten people, and secondly, demoralize them. An educated, healthy and confident nation is harder to govern." Demoralization reverses values, "who call evil good and good evil" (Isaiah): a children's classic of thinking for yourself is cancelled while degrading lyrics win awards; children are shaped before they can recognize what's being done. "Let them choose" when they're 18; "pay very close attention to what your kids are watching." "It's easier to fool people than to convince them that they have been fooled." And: "we the people are not each other's enemies"; the enemy divides by age, race and income. "You've got to be in neutrality to find out the truth, because the negative is on both sides."

#10.4The action, from the heart

Seeing the demoralizing hologram, she chooses an action that accumulates the positive: watch positive content; share what is being cancelled. But not by simply copying someone's work: "go to their website and donate from your heart, or pay whatever, to support the person", as she did by buying from a businessman who was being attacked. "I don't just use somebody's work." When people profit from others' suffering, "that is not in the heart". This is your daily action, fractal by fractal: "I see what the negative is doing, but I have the choice to put my frequency... in the positive." You are shutting their programme down, not rejecting it but transforming it. And find the truth by the facts: "if you see it, that is the truth."

Consent and mandates. When you're presented with a requirement, read what's really happening first (she points again to stopworldcontrol.com and the doctors there); in her understanding of the bylaws, "you cannot mandate something if you already have a cure for it". She also names Dr. Burzynski as a suppressed cancer researcher, and Tesla.

#The method — The Action After the Meditation

  1. Stand in front of the two holograms: see clearly what is negative and what is positive, from your own perception.
  2. Drop the ego and look at the facts; peel the layers until you understand.
  3. Don't reject it: "I understand, I see, thank you."
  4. Choose an action from the heart that accumulates the positive (positive content, sharing, a solution).
  5. Support the people doing the work; never just take their work.
  6. Repeat each fractal moment: each positive choice shuts the other programme down.

#Questions answered

Can we use music to bring up joy and love during the protocol?
Yes, but first quietness: while you are learning the material, let it penetrate with your brainwaves focused on it. Once it has crystallized, add music in the background as another layer of hologram. For complex material, "we prefer... a quiet place, so you focus".
Is space the same as time, seen from a different perspective?
Yes, but they act differently in physics: manifestation has a different spin, location, latitude and altitude. In actuality there's no space, all is in the now; tuning into fractal moments gives the continuum, the illusion of time. To teleport or bilocate, you give your consciousness the resonance of the place's image and you're there (see S3 33.8).
How often should we do the New Earth Galactic Meditation?
Any time you wish. The more you learn, the more perception and neutrality come, and manifestation quickens, "like practising a muscle". Depending on location, perception and agreements, it may still take time: that process is part of your experience.
Are we doing well? Is there a difference between harvest and ascension?
"If you were not doing well, you wouldn't be here." Harvest can be positive, or the way your energetic force was harvested. Even the negative is doing well from its perception: "it simply is". But "no one in consciousness has the right to suppress you": that is what they are against. Ascension is now; all of it is ascension, including what you perceive as negative.
How can we overcome doubt and fear and change a belief?
By a protocol of choosing actions, and choosing comes from perception, which comes from awareness. Become aware by looking at the facts and peeling the layers, moment to moment. If something doesn't make sense, educate yourself more about it: new knowledge mutates your original image into a new one, and switching realities becomes easy.
The matrix is glitching a lot. Is that me, or general?
Both. Consciousness never stops moving, and your perception changes moment to moment. Be curious: curiosity leads to something new, even if it discovers something negative, which may be there to awaken you. "The truth will set you free, when you do not block it with the ego."
When we die, do all versions of us die?
Dying is a perception. If you create a reality in which your body decays and dies, it's true for you. But you can create realities above your structure: contact, or technologies (med beds, hyperspace holograms) that mutate the body so you could live another thousand years (see S4 6).
Are the laws of creation the same on each timeline? Is the law of attraction the only law?
Both, by perception. There is math not yet given to people here (vortex math, light math) for teleportation, mutation and manifestation; it was suppressed. In other worlds the protocols are understood and applied differently; other laws may not exist for you in 3D only because you haven't yet accumulated that knowledge. Curiosity opens the different math and bylaws.
Must we clear all our shadow self and past traumas to ascend?
Yes: those are plastered holographic imprints to be transformed, not rejected, with the protocol; you need a clear sound in consciousness and structure to hold the vibration. "Have no fear, little one, you'll be helped" (see S4 5).
Is the eye a portal?
The eye is a holographic imprint that turns light patterns into electrical charge in the brain, making a blueprint image in your third eye. "The true portal is your heart": positive action accumulates its portals and positive timelines (see S4 7.4).
When we manifest in the now, does it affect past, present and future?
Yes. Changing an entire timeline, so that someone says "remember, we were in the library" and you have no memory of it, needs a lot of potency of the same frequency and action; a next-day fractal shift needs less.
Can we manifest more than one thing at a time?
Yes, simultaneously, once you understand it; it becomes part of you, "almost like shape-shifting" of body and realities. In time, by thought, you'll shift from a 5D platform to the spacecraft for a discussion with your galactic family.
How does sleep affect our timeline?
In sleep you visit other realms and receive information. You're not leaving your body: you create another version of yourself, above the 3D matrix, while holding the vibration of your sleeping body. Making that a physical experience needs very specific instructions, diet, and 100% truth that it's possible; you may not retain the information in the lower 3D vibration, and the body needs "an absolute diet".
What's the best way to start remembering dreams?
Lie down (in the morning, or now), trust yourself and ask: what did I dream last night? Relaxed, let the imagery come into your third eye; catch even a speck of an image, focus on it, feel it, let it build, keep asking and tuning in. You'll train your consciousness to remember more. The records ("akashic") differ in each reality: crystallized resonances that look like a computer, with colours and sounds, unique to your consciousness.
Energies seem to be peaking and dipping for everyone. Is it the fluctuation between realities?
Very much so. Stay in the positive: if you felt unwell for a day, don't attach it to a negative idea, "perhaps somebody did something to me". Even if someone meant harm, shut that programme and don't let it sink into your now; fear lowers you. "I understand, I see, thank you", and transform.
