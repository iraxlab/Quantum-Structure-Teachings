The big idea

Consciousness is accelerating, so both sides speed up, but alongside the chaos, wonderful things are happening simultaneously. Because all parallel realities exist at once, we have already made it: in another timeline we came back from the future to create a new, positive version, avoiding the old mistakes, like a déjà vu. She was told consciousness is raising cell and DNA frequency by up to 30%, starting with the first wave of volunteers, and that this will bring a new ability: a moment plays out, then repeats a few seconds later, and because you already know the outcome you can choose a different response. Learning to use it needs a controlled mind and precise, heartfelt emotion.

#Key teachings

#14.1Two realities at once

While one parallel reality is in commotion (disruption to infrastructure, distractions in the news), positive change happens simultaneously. Focus on what you can do locally: help your farmers and community, don't consent to manipulation, keep some food at home. If you find yourself in a hard situation, you're there to experience it and transform it: dark to light.

#14.2The eclipse accelerates; others try to harvest it

The eclipse ionizes and accelerates particles, raising the frequency of the collective, your cells and your DNA (see 7.13). Like wind, the same energy can be redirected, and some try with large technologies to lower the vibration and open portals. Consciousness won't allow it, though their attempts may still cause ripple damage.

#14.3We came back to fix it

Everything exists at once, so we already made it, and many of us also exist on extraterrestrial platforms. After an earlier timeline failed, a version came back to collect DNA and recreate the reality; now we create it again from the future, without repeating the mistakes, like a déjà vu. Think of the Avatar film or the hyperspace-hologram pods. You already won, but you must go through the physical experience of how you did it. (See S1 episodes 2–3 on mobilizing structures.)

#14.4Her experience

Within two days a video seemed to repeat a sentence, and in a session she "heard" a client tell her something, repeated it back, and learned the client never said it: she had foreseen it a few seconds ahead.

The message: déjà vu and 30% acceleration (condensed)

"Combination into the field of mobilization happens in the zero point", and from that black-hole portal you shift. "Control of the mind is absolutely necessary with the emotional laser frequency input in order to shift in the now": be precise in emotion, from the heart. "The first wave of volunteers will have up to 30% cell acceleration in frequency", helped by the eclipse; nobody will be left behind, but they get it sooner, to help others. Many will have "repeat memories": a situation, positive or negative, repeats "like a déjà vu, and if you learn that, you can predict and change the outcome". "That acceleration is a natural process of reactivation to your true God-given abilities. However, you must match the proper frequency." First little flashes, then more elaborate ones: a few seconds ahead, later perhaps a week. "Let your mind be free... you all must learn to control it."

#14.5How to use it

When you experience something and it repeats, you already know the result, so choose a different behavior and change the outcome. Dreams already foresee the hologram symbolically; this is real-time repetition. Over time you may see the whole fractal sequence (who said what, who went where), not only the end. The hybrids once told her they couldn't see her near future because her choices moved too fast.

#14.6The vessel is the spaceship

Some try to foresee and stop time through technology ("looking glass"); your pure DNA can do it without technology. Use technology to help, but preserve your body, DNA and consciousness, and train your abilities consistently through the heart.

#Questions answered

Is the 30% acceleration caused by ETs or other forces?
It comes from consciousness itself, a natural process; ETs help along the way.
What does it mean that a portal was created while two people hugged? Was it in their hearts?
Yes: portals open from the heart, and emotions can open or block them. A good-hearted hug opens a small one; strong positive emotion in meditation (goosebumps) opens more. Lovemaking at its peak creates a very potent field, which is why some use it in dark rituals. Male and female frequencies combine to procreate in consciousness and then in matter. Those who choose a same-sex relationship chose it to experience something.
How should we react to ego that the eclipse will supposedly present to us?
The eclipse has nothing to do with ego; it restores frequency and moves the ionic atmosphere positively. Ego is a self-chosen disillusion. If you let it in, you keep repeating your own vibration, reject what's there and fight the shift, so the eclipse can't help you.
Some say to stay indoors during the eclipse to avoid misfortune. Should we?
The eclipse isn't negative; others try to harvest its energy and scare you, and fear makes your emotions a dense frequency that strengthens the negative. If you believe it's negative, you may experience that, because consciousness mirrors you. For some it will be just another sunny day in the park. If something happens, it isn't the eclipse.
What if I see vivid scenarios far into the future, positive and negative, that don't happen like a déjà vu?
That's wonderful; déjà vu is the short span. Some see further, like great seers, and this ability will expand.
How do we work with the eclipse energy so it flows freely?
Pray and use the energy for the positive: for the planet, children's freedom, food and land. Strong fear may bring negative experiences; otherwise it passes like an ordinary day. They harvest it negatively; you counter by harvesting it positively. It's a chess game.
Can the potency protocol help us see the most accurate probability?
Not yet. As consciousness accelerates, the probabilities flicker too quickly between both sides to predict exactly. The end result is known, but how it happens varies, partly because people activated for a role sometimes fail under pressure, and consciousness must activate someone else. Overcome, forgive, forget and move forward.
