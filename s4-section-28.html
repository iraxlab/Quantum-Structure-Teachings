Using it on people without their consent goes against the law of consciousness itself, which will preserve itself. To avoid being a casualty, know who is doing what and why, so that with a clear mind you don't consent.

#28.4Prepare, and go back to nature

She described a chain reaction she knew from living under communism: fewer people working, shortages, rising prices, inflation. She asked people to prepare "not from fear" but from understanding (the prediction parts are left out here). Probability means for some there will be shortages and for others everything is provided: "that's quantum manifestation."

Her instructions: preparing
  • At least a month of food, and medicine for colds; learn herbalism, teas and oils: "go back to nature. Nature has it all."
  • Look up super weeds; get organic, non-GMO seeds and sprout them at home (lentils, beans, alfalfa): sprouts hold the most nutrients in the first days. Indoor hydroponic gardens also work.
  • Get distillers; connect with local farmers.
  • Learn permaculture: plants grown together as an ecosystem (cabbage beside potato, banana beside fig) so no pesticides are needed; a desert turned into a food forest on about one and a half acres. She showed a permaculture company's website and recommended the book The Self-Sufficient Backyard; combine it with Jacque Fresco's technology, and with 3D-printed homes ("three-bedroom home $10,000 to $20,000"). "Technology is not bad."

She also said fluoride in water and toothpaste weakened the immune system "drip by drip".

Added note

Fluoride at the levels used in public water and toothpaste is considered safe by health authorities; if you are concerned, ask your dentist or doctor.

#Questions answered

How do we implement the codes? At certain times?
Any time. You can sleep with them under the pillow; even a year from now they will penetrate. "The only way it will penetrate is if you give the consent for it and you truly believe that this is possible. Otherwise it's just numbers."
In a lucid dream I received a message, but forgot it on waking. Can I recall it?
Yes. The Council taught her these steps:
  1. Relax and go back to the moment of the dream; repeat the scene in your mind ("I was in a house").
  2. Add the heart frequency: joy and happiness; increase the imagery until the vibration is high.
  3. Ask questions: what was that, what did it mean? You are opening the resonance the message was held in.
  4. Watch and write down what you see next, in your third eye or telepathically; it is part of the truth.
  5. Repeat and adjust, like tuning a radio, until you download it fully; or wake up in a lucid dream and recall it there. You will be guided by your higher self.
What will happen to our pets during ascension?
Pets are already in the heart. As you shift realities moment by moment, you reprint another version of yourself and of everything you see, including another version of your dog (see S1 2).
Why is my old self hanging on? I'm tired and alone.
Because you allow it. Think now that you are the new version: what does it look like, think, feel? What can you do right now to make yourself happy? "Don't keep recalling the same frequency."
I feel blocked when I try to channel. How do I remove blockages?
"There is no blockages. You're the one that allowing this blockage to be there." Call yourself, right now, the version that is receiving everything: "nothing is wish to be, everything is right now", and override it with consistent action and belief.
