The big idea

Two polarities are merging very fast, and where they meet you may hit hard moments; your knowledge has to hold the positive right through them. The negative is denser, so it's easier to fall into, especially when you've been trained that way; train the other way by choosing positive action again and again until the positive outweighs it and a new third structure forms. And when what you see and what you feel within disagree, trust the feeling: "it's the combination of your pure resonance, and it will lead you to the truth."

#Key teachings

#28.1Where two polarities meet

The global frequency is collapsing, moment to moment, into one reality, while the planet splits into a negative and a positive version. As the two merge (1 + 1 = 3), there are key points where you meet something unpleasant; your knowledge must hold the positive through it. You can't reject the negative, but when you face it you transform it by holding your ground. Those with negative intent act from their own perspective too; the positive is the more potent (see S3 18.2). Beware: a negative structure can deceive, "talk positive", to take over, which is why discernment matters.

#28.2Why the negative is easier to fall into

Living with negative people, you keep slipping into their energy? "Negative energy has more density; it's heavy. When you've been trained to think this way, it's easy to go toward the denser energy, because the gravitational pull becomes easier by your training." Retrain by expressing the positive by action, thought and deed, even while the negative is working, until the positive potency grows bigger and transforms the two into a new structure with a positive outcome (see S3 18.6).

#28.3Nature's frequency

"You are never separated from nature. You are the nature." A forest as a unit has a resonance slightly higher than yours, very fluid and pure, that flows through you "almost like reverse osmosis". What you call stillness is "so much symphony". So go to a forest: breathe the trees, talk to them, feel yourself part of it, and it neutralizes your resonance. Breath and wind are "one of the same": one inward, one outward.

#28.4Parallel reality or timeline?

Three similar resonances combine into one molecule that keeps splitting, flickering moment to moment, each new imprint next to the last. Each such imprint is a parallel reality; "similar vibration reprinting of the same on and on becomes a timeline" (see S1 1.3).

#28.5From one to five, without the steps

Everything is now: project your emotion and thought, and hold it in neutrality, joyful but without expectation of the outcome, "so nothing stops the input and output". To be, say, an actor, feel now that you are one, and act. Each action builds a step (one, two, three, four) until the fifth is complete manifestation. "But when you really know how it works, instead of going one, two, three, four, five, you can go from one to five." Why the delay meanwhile? For the same reason a law student doesn't argue in court the day after reading the book: you're learning how much of each density to combine.

#28.6Trust the feeling within

Honesty about what you see keeps your senses clear, and you'll often feel something contrary to what you see: "always trust the feeling from within", because it's your pure resonance and will show you if someone is manipulating. Questions like "when will we know the truth about space?" already contain the block: "you already know all"; the belief that you don't is what blocks it. Answers come through synchronicity: a friend, a video, a call, a nervous feeling that something's off.

#28.7Upgrades without knowing

Can people ascend or be upgraded without knowing about ascension? "Very much so; it's happening to all of you." As consciousness moves to the next level, its resonance grows stronger, rippling and pulsing through the whole structure, and everything connected to it changes, including you. Glittery particles on the skin are part of this: "you become more aware of who you are within the structure".

#28.8Music that is yours

432 Hz is more in tune with the body; music off balance with the body, especially with negative words, becomes potent in your field. The sign that a sound is right for you: it makes you breathe deeply and sends goosebumps through you in a wave, completely relaxing you (see S3 17.7).

#The method — Retraining Away from the Pull of the Negative

  1. Notice the pull: you're sliding into someone else's negative energy.
  2. Name it: "this is just denser, so it's easier; it isn't truer."
  3. Don't fight it or let it be: choose one positive thought, word or action instead.
  4. Repeat every time, even while the negative continues (28.2).
  5. Go to nature regularly to reset your resonance (28.3).
  6. Notice when the positive starts to feel like the easier way.
