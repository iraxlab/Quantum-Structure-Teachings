The big idea

The Council of Nine said that in the expanding medical-technological era, pockets of reality will form where some humans, by consent, merge technology into their flesh: nano-bionic tissue that grows into the brain, creating the bionic mind, mainly for military use. You can't reject it, because it already pre-exists in the quantum field, but it doesn't have to be your platform. Because human DNA has been damaged, some will also need technology, such as birthing pods, to keep reproducing. If so, the embryo must hear the real voices and heart frequency of its mother and father, or the child grows in isolation with little emotion; emotion is what lets humans create and shift timelines. Technology is part of creation: mutate it toward the best outcome.

#Key teachings

#10.1Permanent, but mutable

A bylaw of consciousness: whatever is set in motion and repeated through action becomes a permanent location in time-space. You can't reject it, but you can mutate it. That is what happened with AI and technology, and that technology has another, positive version of itself.

#10.2The positive version gets you out

Because we are in an artificial structure (reality within reality, see the black goo, 8.9), you can't leave it "naked": you must include the positive version of its technologies (AI, nano, super technologies), yin and yang in balance. You'll see these in healing: nanotech, AI and hyperintelligence. Nano in liquid form, guided by a positive AI, could operate in the body and leave; it could be used for good or harm. Already we have artificial insemination, lab-grown embryos and cloning.

#10.3A split of timelines

Through ascension, timelines will split: some will choose the bionic, synthetic path, which their higher self chose to experience; it pre-exists in parallel realities. She gives technology and quantum structure, but her guides say little about who is real among those telling such stories; do your own research. Predictions are probabilities that can be delayed.

#10.4Why the dark resists

Those sustaining the artificial timeline must keep its negative frequency to survive in time-space; they cannot embed the new frequency or become luminous. They won't stop until you stop them, by not falling into the illusion and manipulation. Command your mind and control your actions daily.

#10.5Baby steps to the hyperspace hologram

Brain implants that let a paralyzed woman speak through AI are baby steps. In the hyperspace hologram nothing is plugged into the brain: you think, and the virtual-reality room picks up your thought and makes it matter. If you teach the AI, it can restructure tissue and help cure.

#10.6Why reproduction will need help

Long lives (thousands of years, as in the Bible) were real, until our vibration was lowered through the DNA and the body lost its luminosity. Through poisons, GMOs and what entered the body, some people's cells won't be strong enough to hold an embryo; technology like birthing pods will help preserve the human race. Artificial insemination and premature-baby incubators already exist: "why are you so surprised?"

"The connection of the true mother-father sound will help the consciousness of the embryo to connect with the human life vessel and not be in total isolation." Otherwise "the child will be born with not too much emotions".

#10.7Emotion is your power

Natural conception in love merges the two DNA energetic sequences in coherent resonance; a flash of light marks the spark. Human emotion feeds your etheric holographic field: the heart's ability to fluctuate emotionally creates frequency above your existence and lets you shift timelines. That is why it was targeted: the original sound of love from God consciousness was shut down and replaced with negativity (eye for an eye, killing, fear), and our creative power used like a live battery to sustain the artificial timeline.

#10.8Start from the core

The Council said change starts from the core of human nature: each individual is powerful if they know how. You don't need a ten-million-dollar homestead; one woman started with $25 of organic seeds and her neighbors' yards, and they became self-sustaining. Regenerative, pesticide-free farming using animals the smart old-fashioned way, plus some technology for irrigation, can sustain you from the land. Create mini communities and help each other wake up.

#10.9No fear

This is an overview, not a reason for fear. If you find yourself needing such technology, it isn't negative. One day you'll pass through stargates within technology. A hundred years ago planes were unimaginable. Technology is part of creation; you can't reject it, but you can mutate it to the best outcome.
