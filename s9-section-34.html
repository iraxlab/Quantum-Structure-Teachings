The big idea

The Council of Nine said: don't be overtaken by the illusion of what's happening around you; "all that is happening is for good. Let consciousness naturally select its most coherent fields... That is the natural order of things." It is order through chaos, the same method the evil uses, but to weed out the weakest links: the ones who move on are those not knocked down by the first blow, who get up and say "I'm not going to allow you to do that, thank you very much", and build. "The wars will stop when you stop playing and paying attention to it." Go back to the land (foraging, composting, homesteading, grounding), using AI as a tool, and approach it not with fear but "like a treasure hunt": the world you're looking for will appear.

#Key teachings

#34.1The black pearl and the right questions

Her guides told her to wear a black pearl for its "constructive elements". A promised supplier never replied; then they said "call Amelia", a silversmith friend who, unexpectedly, had natural Tahitian pearls and made a custom ring. Asking AI only generically gave little; asking quantum and geometric questions revealed more: pearls are aragonite, piezoelectric and phononic crystals, and black pearls contain melanin, a semiconductor between the crystal platelets, so they take in the environment's frequency, restructure it and send it back more potently than white pearls. Your guides won't do everything: at a certain level you must do the work, and help comes when things don't work out.

Added note

Added note: the effects she describes for pearls are her teaching, not established science; the crystal and melanin facts are simplified.

#34.2How we were made easy to control

Every particle has free will to choose its position, and that was hijacked: people were pulled from their land, ancestral homes and families, so everything came through corporations for profit ("it's not personal, it's business" was another manipulation). In her view, framing work outside the home only as liberation also served taxation, and welfare without teaching self-sufficiency builds dependence. Technology should be a tool, not part of your body; the natural order preserves the DNA of a biological, not artificial, being.

Added note

Added note: these are her views on contested social questions.

#34.3Why a homestead isn't enough

She heard a former soldier explain that if the grid failed for months, water pumps and sewage would stop, sickness and looting would follow, and gangs would overrun isolated homesteads; even a can of tuna in a backpack could make you a target. Her guides' answer: foraging. Wild plants are everywhere, especially near suburbs: dandelion, purslane and more. Constant mowing destroys the land's biomes. A forager she follows eats only wild food and medicine; start with the one plant closest to your door. Learn canning and preserving too.

Added safety note

Added safety note: identify wild plants with an expert before eating them (some look-alikes are poisonous), check for allergies, and avoid land that may be sprayed or polluted. Fasting, juicing or water-only detoxes need medical supervision, as she says.

"Do not connect and do not be overtaken by the illusion of everything that is happening around you. Do not worry. All that is happening is for good. Let consciousness naturally select its most coherent fields, frequency of people. That is the natural order of things. No need to panic and get caught into the global illusion." "These people have chosen from their perception of the free will to be here." "The wars will stop when you stop playing and paying attention to it." "You know the truth; then focus on something positive." Doing physical work with the land "becomes more coherent, because you're... activating your chakras, your DNA." "Once you start doing this physically... you are going to separate from the illusion of the teleprompter that everything is a war" and start creating the new 5D platforms, where you hear that things are getting better. "This is the natural selection through consciousness... to pick up the coherent resonance of the people... order through chaos", as the evil does, "but to actually weed out the weakest links": "the strongest ones... that are going to push through the barrier... not knocked down by the first moment that somebody says something." "Who are you to judge what is right or wrong?" "It is simply a selection... and it is up to you through the free will what you are going to experience."

#34.4What it means

You can be compassionate and help, but you can't help people who don't want to be helped; some chose to be examples (see the etheric legions). Get up and say "I'm not going to allow you to do that, thank you very much", then build your own community. The journey is sometimes lonely, so your frequency can become "a strong, stable tree" whose roots protect others. Even the evil thinks it's doing right for its survival. Watching a video or two won't do it: the information must be crystallized through consistent mind, in-the-moment awareness and action; then synchronicities come, as with the pearl.

The message: the 90 Hz threshold (condensed, as received)

"The 70 hertz to 89 hertz [of] our spectrum of reality vibration [is a] very weak field" for the mind. "When you reach 90 hertz", by constant positive physical action ("by the power of 10 actions"), studying the quantum structure or whatever is coherent for you, "the mind can pick up more power of how [it] collects the field, and how fast with accuracy [it] can analyze the spectrum of the front imagery... the self-projective mirror hologram wave." "All of you have it, and it works." Then you'll easily maneuver your positions as the driver, not the passenger, converting negative to positive.

Added note

Added note: the hertz figures are received teaching, not a measured physiological scale.

#34.5The sovereign body knows first

When you're sovereign in your body, you feel the frequency shift: you know something is off before your mind can explain why, which food is right, which land is healthy, which people are coherent. "The body is the instrument." That independence threatens the system. Use AI as a tool for the land (sun, slope, companion planting: marigolds against pests, the three sisters, hügel beds, leaf mulch instead of bought mulch).

#34.6A treasure hunt, not fear

She now composts leaves and lets them mulch her slope to restore the soil, practising before she ever buys land. Her guides: "Think of it not... for fear, but... through exploration, like a treasure hunt"; you'll discover where you want to be and people you never knew existed, "because you are going to create them", and "that world that you are looking for is going to appear". You'll still be informed when needed. When a fuel scare made a hundred cars crowd a station, she didn't worry, took a left, and a path opened within minutes. Look for unrestricted land if you want to build naturally.

#The method — Back to the land, without fear (as given)

  1. Stop feeding the global illusion: notice what you see and hear around you.
  2. Learn one wild plant, starting with the one closest to your door (identified safely).
  3. Do something physical with the soil: compost, mulch, plant.
  4. Use AI as a tool: ask the right, specific questions about your land.
  5. When knocked down, get up: "I'm not going to allow you to do that, thank you."
  6. Treat the journey as a treasure hunt; let people and places appear.
