The big idea

The 3D and the 5D play at the same time, one inside the other, so you will keep seeing what the negative structure does while you move through your own reality; see it so it doesn't overtake you. Consciousness never lets itself self-destruct: if one person doesn't complete what they came to do, another probability, another person or a whole group takes it over. The Council is giving a 5% increase of frequency into the collective, fractally, drop by drop, but the timeline only becomes potent through physical action: "just the meditation is not going to work".

#Key teachings

#26.1A hologram within the hologram

The 5D can be here, and within the 5D the 3D still plays: "those two platforms are playing at the same time." That is why she asks you to know what is going on around you: to maneuver yourself, a hologram within the hologram, you need to see the 3D and negative structure so that it doesn't overtake you, without going into fear (see S1 2, S4 4). She sees it as a chess game: one side plays a move, the other answers, back and forth; even after winning a battle, stay alert, because there will be a counter-move.

Practical advice she gave. Keep supplies at home for a month ahead, "always be a month ahead", in case restrictions return, and keep using home delivery; prepare without fear. She also gave political commentary and expectations for 2021–2024; they are left out here (these talks are years old).

#26.2Why a mission is never lost

Many came here, "from different spacecraft", to help in this holographic expression, but free will remains. If you decide not to do your part, "it's got to be somebody else": another probability of you, another person, or even a group or a country takes it over, because consciousness "will never allow to self-destruct. It has to exist." "If you don't do your job, somebody else will take over. If you're not in the heart, somebody else will take over." She adds that the negative side's own future timeline, finding it could no longer reproduce, comes back to restructure and sabotage itself, so that we can move forward: all are part of one consciousness, with only the illusion of separation. "We already made it, but we are dreaming how we made it."

This builds on the fluctuation of realities (see S1 2, S1 3): each time you switch your emotion and action to the positive, you fluctuate in the positive; the timeline collapses where you stayed most often.

The message: constructive new timelines

"The sectors of all your realities are not moving fast enough." A coordinated frequency from the heart essence is combined into the collective, so the resonance of the fifth-dimensional structure becomes more potent, magnetic and gravitational, pulsating faster, until it is stronger than the 3D.

They are giving a 5% increase to all of the planet, fractally, over the next three to five months, to help redirect your body's consciousness to the resonance needed for your DNA downloads and upgrades, so the shift to your new parallel timelines is smooth. About seven months from now, new upgrades follow. After that, "you will see all humans awaken."

It's given drip by drip, like drinking a glass of water slowly rather than at once, so the resonance can embed before more is given (see the Detox and Juicing Guide on going slowly).

#26.3The potency comes from action

The frequency becomes stronger "by more action... override it, take over; they do something, you get up again and do it." She says we have been too passive: being sorry for the children is not the same as doing something for them. Once you act for humanity, even for your neighbour, your vibration rises in a specific pulsation, it emanates into the quantum structure, and the next moment you meet the person or situation you were looking for: "you have to create it, to see it." There is nobody outside of you; even she is projected in your reality to bring the information (see S1 1).

#The method — Holding the Upgrade

  1. See the 3D as it is, without fear: it's the hologram within the hologram.
  2. Stay in the heart: that is how the collective frequency enters without barriers of conditioning (see the questions below).
  3. Take one physical action for humanity, however small, and repeat it whenever you are knocked back.
  4. Go slowly in body and diet, so the resonance can embed ("drip by drip").
  5. Prepare practically (a month of supplies), then hold the light for others.

#Questions answered

Did humanity cause this through the law of attraction?
No, it was predetermined in the structures of the timelines. But free will was given to everyone who came to help, not everyone completed, and that created fluctuations, new situations and new decisions, "like the chess game": a new opponent, a new strategy. It's played on many timelines at once, planetary, individual and multidimensional, with the help of your guides.
How can we raise awareness through the internet?
Any way you wish, but cleverly: in a battlefield you make bunkers, come out and go back; "you don't go in front of all the bullets". Use the same platforms created to suppress you to expose the truth, slowly but surely.
How do we best receive the 5% frequency?
Be your true self and act from the heart: the frequency then penetrates consciousness "without any barriers of conditioning", because it must be similar in resonance to flow through you.
Aren't there still many variations of reality in 5D?
Yes, running simultaneously, a different resonance and reality in every moment of the now, more or less similar depending on how you construct your moments and on your belief system. "We can just point the direction."
How do I keep belief and trust while the gap widens between the 3D money reality and the new one?
"Control of your mind is absolute necessity." Choose in each moment to believe you have everything you need now, even if you don't see it. Once your belief converts, your vibration overwrites the old belief system, even when you see the opposite, and the manifestation becomes more potent, "where the end result... will be in your riches" (see S1 1).
Will there be food and water shortages?
Sooner than you think, but fractal: easy for some, hard for others, depending on their belief system. It isn't the end: be prepared ahead, so it's easier to maneuver. If you find yourself in chaos, have no fear, hold the light for others, and play the hide-and-seek game: you know you have it, so look for it, "because you are it" (see S1 1.7).
I'm spreading the quantum structure in Indonesia. Any suggestions?
Stay in the heart and trust what you do; it will expand and bring people who listen. "Make sure that you use the proper codes of that knowledge even if you give your own little twist to it": the protocol in the quantum structure is very precise.
Can't we ignore all this and just live in the 5D Earth?
You live in a matter-density reality: to create it you must take physical action to see it. If not, why did you come? It's your free will, but "now we need all our warriors." "Get up and do something about it."
How can we change human laws? Is there a shortcut?
"Yes, leave. That's your shortcut." The human expression is a beautiful original creation, a combination of races and DNA from other galaxies, manipulated over generations; you can choose to be human or another expression. But if you chose to be here now, you came to hold the light and do something about it.
How can I use my original music more consciously for healing?
Separate the layers of your music and listen to each tone by itself, then to two tones together: which give you the feeling of goose bumps? Those are the precise frequencies your consciousness resonates with; combine them, tone by tone, into your own symphony. It's a slow process, "baby steps".
