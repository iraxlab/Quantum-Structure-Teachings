The Orbs and The Matrix

The Council of Nine's explanation of the orbs seen around her while channeling; why contact shows only as much as your body can hold; why you won't see a complete collapse if you hold the heart; the Council's comments on experimental medicines and choosing partners; finding a loving partner; and questions on orbs, contact and moving into 5D (live talk, April 2022)

The big idea

The orbs are real contact, "truly entities", or even a spacecraft or place, shown only as much as we can hold. An orb is a stillness that reprints from within; it accelerates its frequency, changes its location in the field, and pops out of our timeline. While she channels, she holds her field and theirs at once, a bubble co-created within consciousness, which is why channeling exhausts her. You don't see the full spectrum because your body can't yet hold their vibration (see S5 4). The same holds for the collapse of systems: hold the heart, act, and you'll see a transition, not a collapse.

#Key teachings

The message: the orbs

"The movement shows the trajectory of the expansion [in] latitude [and] altitude in your matrix. And when [it] expands, [it] reprints more, also escalates the frequency... then pops out of your orbit frequency, orbital hologram, to above-structure timeline resonance." "The orbs are truly entities... or even a spaceship or place, that reprints from within, expands from within. But actually it's a stillness that reprints within itself... and then the movement is changing the location of the reprint in orbital time in the quantum field." "Orbital time means the expansion reprint in zero-point location of the latitude [and] altitude."

#13.1Two structures at once

Consciousness contracts and expands like a breathing machine, reprinting from within (see S5 11). Downloading from a higher resonance, she holds both the 3D and the higher structure at once; held from the heart, she sees the negative without experiencing it. Connected with the Council or a spacecraft, she co-creates a bubble, a reality within the reality: "you could be in the spacecraft... and you're thinking that you're on the planet." Viewers see the orb because they project her in their field and co-create that resonance. When an orb's frequency rises too high to hold, it accelerates and pops out. Practice trains the body to hold more.

The message: no complete collapse

"You are not going to see complete collapse in humanity's system and monetary system, if your reality is [a] smooth transition." Holding the heart, something may still bring you to your knees (losing a home, having to sell and move): don't wait for someone to save you; maneuver, act, reconstruct (see S4 36). The negative structure spins without accelerating with consciousness, and self-destructs. "You will hear something is changing, and some people have a very difficult time, but you will continue thriving." Prepare without fear. Ask daily: "What can I do to help humanity?"

The message: experimental medicines and partners (her report)

She was told the experimental injections varied by place and batch, "like a Russian roulette", to create order through chaos while keeping enough workers alive; those who exited join the etheric legions (see S4 38), "no judgment." The Council advised being "very, very careful and very selective" with intimate partners, and she said to treat it as you would a sexually transmitted infection. Those cross-contaminated in other ways may restore their DNA and flush it out, if their body is strong enough. "I'm not a doctor... do not send me emails" asking what to do.

Added safety note

This is her report, not medical fact. Authorized vaccines cannot be passed from person to person by touch, food or intimacy, and vaccinated people should not be avoided or treated as infectious. Please make health decisions with your doctor.

#13.2Finding a loving partner

There may be a moment of being alone, as the shift sorts who is where. Hold the positive: know and believe you have a great, healthy, loving partner, "feel right now that you're loved... that you're beautiful", and act on it with joy (a new haircut, clothes from a thrift store). You'll meet people who share this knowledge. "Do not rush"; the fluctuation will go up and down. The balancing is of frequencies, not judgments of people.

#Questions answered

Are orbs always positive?
Not always: it depends on who you connect with and the field you hold. Positive consciousness can activate you even in a negative reality. Negative entities take over those easy to control, through greed, money and fame; if one speaks to you, hold the heart. She isn't given information on dark forces (some who work with them know the laws; she recalled energizing water in a circle facing the four directions, with the heart).
Are flashes, sparkles and orbs different entities or contacts?
They can be. Each person creates their own contact (lucid dreams, bilocation, out-of-body, a light being, a spacecraft). Don't rely on a psychic to tell you: "anybody that is very greedy can lie to you."
What do the colours mean?
Friction in the field, portals, the dimension they come from, or their thoughts. Hers looked light white-blue.
Orbs appear around my children on camera. Would tuning in to them help?
Tune into that field and see what reflects back; practise; it can collapse into contact, like a phone call. No promises of how.
Can we move into 5D automatically, without intending to?
Yes: once your positive collapses become permanent, you stay in the higher field while seeing the negative, without dropping back.
Aren't orbs optical illusions?
Sometimes. But what you believe is what you project and collapse: "don't take somebody else's reflection... to be your truth."
Is our first connection always with our higher self, then others?
Consciousness is your higher self in expansion; who else you connect with depends on agreements and on what you can hold.
