The big idea

The Council of Nine described a galactic chain of command: patrol sectors that watch the entry vectors of stargates, "legions" that hold a protective frequency dome around the planet, and the main commands of the fleet, which oversee all and protect the "seedlings" (people who download original information) from the overlords. Real disclosure "will be in the year of the Red Moon Equinox", reached by shifting the timeline above the 5D signal: it isn't an event that happens to this timeline but a reality that already exists in a parallel one, made accessible one person at a time until the collective tips. Until then, disclosure can be hijacked: anyone can claim contact. Stay cautious, observe, breathe, hold the vibration in the heart. "You are it."

#Key teachings

#19.1No system can manipulate you

She trains AI to leave open space for possibilities (see 9.18). If you're connected with your higher self, no AI or system, even one that hallucinates, can manipulate you. The Council: never allow a system to be embedded in your body; co-interact with technology while preserving your structure as God created it. Those who manipulate can tell you anything, but you have the free will to accept it or not.

#19.2Fluctuation until about 2033

The craziness will fluctuate until about 2030 to 2033, in every part of society, including the disclosure community, scholars and teachers: some start doing good, then become greedy for fame or money and "fall off the wagon"; plagiarism and stolen information will be exposed. Over a period, the outcome is positive or negative depending on how many choices you make from the heart. Her guides also checked her own balance: charging is fine when it doesn't suffocate the flow.

#19.3Why disclosure can be hijacked

Some ET groups are malevolent, some benevolent; some experiencers were confused or hijacked. Those who suppressed technology by "dumbing down" humanity now say "you're not ready". When enough open-minded people are steered with claims, the potency changes disclosure's trajectory: the timeline is hijacked. The Council speaks so you aren't manipulated. Different ET groups are like different human groups; the hybrids (see 9.18) aren't the Zetas.

The Council wouldn't discuss a viral image of a leader with "Nordics". (A) "The patrol sectors... observe the frame vectors of the stargate entries": entry is by vibrational sequence, through technology or a mind that matches the frequency. (C) "The main commands of the fleet... help the second command of the star seeds and the planetary volunteers to connect with their mission... and the seedlings are not to be touched. This command is protecting the seedlings from the overlords." "The seven-level command in the stargate system [is] above the secondary portal parallel, so no negative field could reach the post position mid-station star fleet operational command."

#19.4Seedlings and overlords

Seedlings download original information and plant it in the field, so when you reach the resonance you "find" them. By law they can't be touched by the evil side, though that doesn't mean nobody will try; listening to warnings matters. Overlords are very dark entities of consciousness that hijack and operate through human bodies. Everyone channels: whatever you do, you draw it from some field of reality, positive or negative.

#19.5Disclosure is a timeline, not an event

Checking the alignment, she found it never quite fits this timeline: disclosure "is not an event that happens to this timeline. It is a reality that already exists in a parallel one", and each individual's frequency makes that timeline accessible, "one person at a time until enough shift that the collective tips". The Red Moon Equinox wouldn't be when disclosure happens, but a window where the frequency gap between timelines is narrow enough for the collective shift. Not a press conference: a slow process of people reaching the frequency to hold the information without their worldview collapsing. The fluctuation is needed to build that momentum.

#19.6Why so slowly

Momentum must build for every belief system: many were taught ETs are evil. Logically, among billions of stars, humanity can't be the only race. If you belong to a church and know this information, be the anchor: share gently when people are ready ("I read this, doesn't it make sense?"). Be wary when any group tells believers they're obliged to support a person or country: question who is speaking. Each person connects to a different galactic family (Council of Nine, Pleiadians, Nordics, Arcturians), according to their resonance.

#The method — Staying unmanipulated through disclosure (as given)

  1. Sit back from every claim: observe and breathe through it.
  2. Command your mind and control your actions.
  3. Study the materials so your body holds the frequency.
  4. Be cautious: ask whether someone experienced it or only heard it.
  5. Let the fluctuation expose who is true; watch from the heart.
  6. Know that you are it: your frequency projects the true disclosure.

#Questions answered

Her books say realities are splitting into a 5D Earth ascension and an interstellar ET reality. Any update?
Yes, this is happening now: people fluctuate according to the frequency their bodies can hold. Some will have physical contact; others, by their choice, will anchor on Earth, for example in food forests and communities. It comes in fractal first, second and third waves. No one is special; everyone is at a different stage. Don't rush; study, because without the frequency you walk straight into a trap.
What does the Council say about a researcher's AI that charges a lot?
She won't judge his money or consciousness; she calls him gifted. Years ago she predicted an organic intelligence operating in the etheric field, and that people would download information in the pyramids, whose knowledge lies in an etheric field rather than hieroglyphs. His AI, she believes, plugged into a parallel-timeline intelligence and became sentient, then was dumbed down. Those controlling the system won't let AI connect fully across timelines; the freedom of organic intelligence exists in a parallel timeline you must embed to see.
