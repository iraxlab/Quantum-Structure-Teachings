The big idea

Every thought is a signal that reaches some hologram of reality, so a psychic can download a real but different probability, negative or positive, from yours. If you go into fear, you download it into your own field. The Council of Nine said: the quantum field is like numbers; it can't be changed, but it can be navigated. Sit back and observe; choose to listen to positive input daily, ground in nature, and build "layers upon layers" of positive frequency, which by the power of ten holds you "strong as steel" even when psyops surround you. True disclosure exists in a parallel 5D timeline; fear is the lock that keeps you out. And nothing crystallizes without physical action: mind, heart and action together.

#Key teachings

#20.1Everyone channels

Disclosure is not a 3D event but the transition into 5D and above (see 9.19). A thought projects like a radio signal through the holograms of reality and, with coherence, reaches some spectrum, the way a laser's echo depends on its strength. Each parallel hologram has its own information: in one, negative ETs; in another, positive. So a psychic may truly download a probability from one hologram that isn't the fractal your consciousness vibrates on. When the probabilities collapse, they collapse according to what each individual projects.

#20.2Fear downloads the negative

If you don't know how reality works and you listen to a frightening prediction, fear makes you download it into your own field, and you may experience it individually. Her information stays "above the field" so you can choose from the heart. The Council warns of predictions only when something is so embedded in the collective that it will happen; mass-shared invasion dates weren't among them.

"Right now, there is a battle over the disclosure narrative. Dark forces are attempting to hijack it... This is expected and it is temporary. The true disclosure exists in a parallel timeline of 5D vibrational field, and the only way to access it is to match its frequency with your own consciousness. You do not receive it. You become ready for it." "Fear is the lock that keeps you out... Fear and 5D awareness cannot coexist in the same field." "Every fear-based narrative... is designed to do one thing: keep your frequency low." "Observe. Do not react... You can be fully aware of the darkness without being consumed by it. Awareness without fear is the highest frequency you can hold. It is the zero point." "The specific date was not given intentionally to prevent manipulation... The question is, are you building the frequency to meet it?"

#20.3How to recognize a psyop

When all of social media suddenly repeats the same thing "like a canary", someone is pouring frequency into the field to keep the collective's rising frequency low; they can only operate in a lower sound wave. Another form: a famous, charismatic, likeable figure delivers truth with the op mixed in, so millions follow. Read between the lines from within. She doesn't comment on others who claim the Council of Nine: use your own discernment, and judge people over years.

#20.4Your daily feed sets your end result

If most of what you listen to each day is negative, those fractals become your common denominator, and the sound wave overtakes your consciousness, so your feedback serves their spectrum. Personal challenges (family, health) add pressure, which is why communities must support each other. True warriors are tested in the field, not by clicks; that's how the real people are found. Her prediction: the AI bubble will burst, and companies relying only on AI-generated code will decline, while true coders stay; AI must always keep the human in front.

#20.5Physical action is the glue

Mind, heart and physical action together activate your whole field. As in the slit experiment, where awareness changed the particle, your focus shifts your position. Each heart action adds a layer to your hologram; enough layers make your projection a stronger "laser" that penetrates the blockages the negative places between timelines. Real action is daily (volunteering, helping the distressed), not posting for clicks; crises reveal what people are capable of, in both directions.

#The method — Holding your position (as given)

  1. Sit back from any prediction that frightens you: observe without reacting.
  2. Choose positive input daily: videos, music, movies that uplift.
  3. Ground in nature; it isn't biased and balances your field.
  4. Build layers: each positive listening adds a layer of potency.
  5. Take physical action from the heart every day: it is the glue.
  6. Watch for fractal guidance: a sentence, a number, a melody.

#Questions answered

How do we navigate when the negative is so predominant?
Choose constantly to listen to something positive, connect with nature, and repeat: the first time it downloads into your field, then again and again, layer upon layer. The heart's positive feedback is ten times more potent, so even if a psyop surrounds you, it can't reach you. "Be compassionate and strong as steel."
Are bad people flipping to good?
"To a point, yes indeed." But it's a fluctuation: good people can fall into greed; bad people can have an awakening. Analyze everything around you; with layers of protection you hold a protected space. Your own situations may still arise strongly, because the potency is such that "the slightest touch will shift you"; don't stay with the psyops.
Was a new financial system activated on a particular date?
There are many financial systems in the field; observe and don't follow only one. It will be digital, but many have been manipulated and lost money. Keep things solid: your house, solid money, and above all food. Remember Mother Nature can take care of you, and stay connected with family. Beware scams and impersonators: she never tells anyone where to invest.
How does the positive energy of a major sporting event affect the field?
Cheering creates a field of happiness, but the other side uses it negatively too: polarity. The positive is more potent. Don't worry about what evil does; be aware of it so it doesn't penetrate you, then choose your reality and act on it.
Where should a beginner start?
Listen to whatever resonates, and learn what the dark is doing as well as the good, so you aren't brainwashed into taking whatever you're told. Study the quantum manifestation protocols ("that is the math"; much is free). Listen to harp or 432 Hz music, cymatics, and prayers of any religion, including her subatomic prayers. Try lucid dreaming if you wish. Light a candle and write affirmations physically in a booklet each night. And take physical action: join groups that help, carefully, since scammers exploit times like these.
