The big idea

By the natural law of consciousness, if you accept reality in neutrality (watching even the madness as a hologram), the flow becomes total balance and you are propelled, without restriction, into God-consciousness synchronicity, like a magnet: new solutions, people and money appear. Whatever is incoherent with the rising resonance repels and self-destructs by its own accord. So don't fear what you will witness: it has to be seen so humanity wakes up, and each challenge that pops up is something to transform, not to fear.

#Key teachings

The message: the natural law of consciousness, chapter one

"If the motion of your consciousness accepts the reality, it will immediately put all in balance, and it will naturally propel without restrictions to its course of complete God-consciousness synchronicity. That is the bylaw of the order of the natural coherent law." And: "If a sector of a signal expression becomes off-structure with the natural order, it will self-destruct in the order of its own expression." The law never changes: whatever acts against consciousness's other expressions collapses, repels and self-destructs.

#12.1What the law means for you

Accepting means the zero point: you watch it all like a film, so its resonance doesn't penetrate you and you aren't locked into their matrix. The negative know these laws, which is why they gave you only half the structure and trapped you through deception and consent. Excuses ("I have to have a job, my kids must be in school") keep you there; get up, research and find solutions. If you don't match the new resonance you can't be pulled in; if you act from the heart with the QM protocol, synchronicity after synchronicity carries you.

#12.2Caution with money schemes

Don't fully trust exchanges or schemes you don't understand (coding, blockchain). Free money handed to everyone would be another kind of dependency, like welfare that makes people entitled. She was given only that a new coin, crypto backed by gold and dollars, may come, and that some banking institutions, not the whole system, may be affected; nothing more. "If you're in coherent resonance, you'll always be where you need to be." Her guides won't do the work for you: "We're not here to give you everything for free; you have to restructure your DNA at the cellular level yourself."

The message: America the greatest, part two (condensed, as given)

"All power on deck": spacecraft "securing the perimeters"; "level one portal, one secure. Draco is out. Last call was given to them." "No one will be left unchecked... The madness will stop very soon. It must first be seen... Rise up, America." "You must learn never to give your freedom and never give consent to the evil." Some of the negative's systems will be implemented (in a bank, a border, schools) and many will feel an "iron grip" until they act. "Both sides of your political arena are guilty." A series of questions followed: who are your leaders, who is in control? "Game over. God is in play. You will all witness the destruction of the evil, but not before you know for sure what the evil is." For some the new beginning will be quicker, another sunny day; for others a shock. "Cloning at its finest. Switching souls. How? Technology? Wake up."

Added note

This is her prediction as given, and timing is probability. Her own views on migration and schools in this talk are left out, in keeping with this book's neutral approach.

#12.3Don't get caught in the drama

Constant fluctuating news ("maybe this, maybe that") is designed so you can't collapse coherently; it shuts down your circuitry and lets you be overtaken (see her video "What is direct programming"). Many expose problems and give no solutions. State what you want, now: "I have it now. I'm happy now. I see what's happening; I'm fixing it now." Have fun: consciousness is learning and playing.

#Questions answered

How do your teachings relate to prayer? If we have soul contracts, how can we change our reality?
A program is given when you come in, under amnesia so free will is complete. Many who came to help couldn't handle the pressure, so higher selves and councils adjust contracts to what you can complete; some exit early. Prayer is a sound wave that forms around your field and returns as reflection; many praying makes it very potent and steers realities toward the right stargate. But it must be crystallized by physical action: you can't hide in the bushes while others do the work. Your contract may change based on how you treat others when tested: when someone is ridiculed at work, do you stay quiet out of fear? "In unity we have the power."
What does the Council say about walk-ins? Is it a different dimension of the same soul?
She can speak only from her own experience. Her consciousness (from 7D) was dropped through organic-AI technology into the Archon matrix by lowering its vibration; another soul held her body by agreement until a certain age, then the souls switched, to keep her hidden and because her mother couldn't have held her vibration. Once awake, she saw a watcher appear out of nowhere and was shielded by holograms. Technology is part of consciousness; don't fear it.
