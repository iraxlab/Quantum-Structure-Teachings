The big idea

When a crisis comes there is no middle ground: stand in fear and you lower your vibration; step in like a warrior and do what is right, and manifestation meets you in ways you couldn't foresee. #17.1No middle ground

In a crisis you must step in like a warrior and do the right thing. If you stand in your fear, you lower your vibration and your situation goes differently. Some people lost everything during the pandemic, while others prospered; it was partly a choice of whether to stay in fear.

#17.2Protect your children

If you learn your child is being harmed or indoctrinated, there's no excuse to leave them there out of fear: act. Sometimes it must be done quietly when people around you aren't awake. Many amplify harm not on purpose but because they're too brainwashed, or too proud ("I'm a doctor, a lawyer, I know better"), to analyze what's in front of them.

#17.3Live below your means

Not stretching to buy the biggest house can be what lets a family survive hard times. Then manifestation can work in unexpected ways: a home that others overlook, funds that become available just when you need them.

#17.4Why share a warning

Sharing a prediction may lessen its potency: some will choose not to go to a place; others who dismiss it will, if it happens, remember and have the strength not to be manipulated and to act. And the right people can plug in what several sources say and do something about it. She didn't want to post it; she was told it was her job.

#17.5Have no fear

Whether such an event would lead to war she wasn't told; the evil may try, but those working for good know what it is doing. Stay put, keep some food in your home, and allow room for things to fluctuate. She can't tell you where to live; she can't read every moment of your field. You must be strong enough to choose.

#17.6When you need it, you have it

At her lowest, with almost nothing in the bank while helping others, she was told: "When you need a dollar, it's an illusion. It's a hologram. When you need a dollar, you have it; when you need a million, you have it." The same holds for everything you need: you will be supported, because your higher self may have chosen your location for a reason.

#17.7Action, not only prayer

After a major storm, she helped every day with food and water and saw both the worst and best of humanity. When disaster strikes elsewhere, did you go help, or send something? "Only prayer is not going to do it; physical action will." Through the physical you experience and control the reality.
