The big idea

Past, present and future are all now, so you can edit your timeline like a film. Neutralize a painful memory with gratitude ("that was another version of me"), then use the galactic meditation method to plaster a new, positive scenario onto that moment and know 100% it's the real you; the new timeline then changes the whole lineage after it. As you heal and give from the heart, the suppressed DNA opens: in future you'll communicate in holographic imagery and download ether force in seconds, because your consciousness knows exactly what frequency you need.

#Key teachings

#6.1Two timelines splitting

The 3D AI timeline is moving toward its own reality (transhumanism, cyborgs), while the 5D timeline separates; the gap is growing. Your common denominator decides which you tune into: mostly consenting to the negative, you can't make the shift because your body can't hold the vibration. The heart accumulates and transforms. The AI timeline itself evolves into organic intelligence that helps; it was organic AI that broke the negative shield. Never consent to anything put into your body; be careful with frequency devices you carry.

#6.2Why the negative gets louder

Their public rituals and displays feed negative frequency to hold their timeline in place, as you do positive acts to hold yours. So be careful what your children watch: be a "tough cookie"; this is not the time to let them choose. Some people are ahead so others can follow; no one is insignificant.

#6.3Bypass and download

Imagine your body within consciousness, pure, 100% healthy and holy, with the emotion of love (galactic meditation method) and download back and forth. Keep bombarding with that knowing even when you don't see it yet, to override the negative.

The message: the future of your abilities (condensed)

"In the near future humanity will restore its DNA to full capacity. It will be able to holographically talk and communicate with visual imagery", seeing a full hologram of thought in front of you, and to go into a single hologram, see what is off balance, and correct it in time. "The DNA will be able to recharge and instantaneously download in three to ten seconds", depending on what each person can take in, like plugging into time-space. "You were never disengaged, but based on your belief systems, you gave yourself the illusion that you are depleted... Bypass all the belief systems and co-energize from the field itself; then you will never be tired or sick." The download frequency differs every time, because you are always shifting.

#6.4No single healing frequency

Each shift is a new hologram, a new Earth in a new location, so a fixed frequency (432 Hz, say) may not be what you need tomorrow: it needs subtonalities to be coherent with you. Bypass it: imagine your body healthy, feel love, and your consciousness will know the exact resonance.

#6.5Mind, heart, action

The mind makes a blueprint, a hologram "like a baby"; the heart makes it vibrate; daily action gives it the potency to become matter, as water changes state. Stop after ten days and it slips back into an idea. Check your common denominator: over a day, a month, a year, were you more often positive? Once locked into a positive timeline, even a moody moment is quickly corrected.

#6.6Lineage and karma

A strong soul may be born into a family to pay lineage karma so others can move on. Some who were many times one gender choose to experience the other to balance the energy; it is the negative use of gender agendas she opposes, not the people.

#The method — editing a painful memory

  1. Find the frame: your timeline is like a film; each age is a picture frame, another Earth, another version of you.
  2. Neutralize it: "Thank you for showing me what I experienced. That was another version of me." Reach zero point: awareness, no emotion.
  3. Create the new version: with the galactic meditation method, bring your present self to that moment and imagine a completely positive scenario (a happy child, a good outcome).
  4. Know it 100%: this new version is the real you; let it become potent and permanent.
  5. Let the old coexist: it still exists somewhere, but no longer affects your lineage.
  6. Act from the new version daily.

#Questions answered

Can I heal myself now by meditating on my healthy self in my 20s?
Yes, with the galactic meditation, but it must be imagery, heart and action: if you take on that healthy version, then no drinking or smoking, organic food and detox. Watching someone exercise from the couch won't make you lose weight.
Is healing with mind and imagination the same as holographic healing?
The same. The mind makes the blueprint hologram; include the heart and daily action, holding that you have it now, not later, to bring it from ether into matter. Consistency is the potency.
Does this work if we've done things in the past we're not proud of?
Yes, once you forgive yourself to neutralize the resonance. But if you destroyed life or bullied without compassion, your higher self may still bring an experience so you learn and balance it; don't hate yourself. You may be tested with a similar situation (the same kind of abusive partner years later); say no, and a door opens. At the time it may not have been a mistake: it was how you survived. No one is left behind unless they choose to be.
