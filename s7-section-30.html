The big idea

She was told an event (its nature not given) will shock humanity into a total stand of disbelief and realization, and from it a 3% collective quantum leap in frequency within about three to five months, activating chakras, DNA and awareness. Then people will rise to rebuild, restore and reset ("the triple R") schools, police, courts, post offices, banks and local offices. Hard things are allowed because we learn by experiencing them, like a child who learns to work rather than being spoiled. Your reaction is the lesson: act from power and analysis, not panic. Don't feed degradation with attention or reposts; that strengthens its frequency. Then "miracle after miracle": an epic comeback of the good.

#Key teachings

#30.1Don't feed the spectacle

She objects to spectacles she sees as mocking the sacred and to algorithms that inflate some views and bury others (her own reach is restricted). Their trick is to reverse good and bad. Homeschooling is a growing movement with many new resources; if you homeschool, make sure children socialize and learn hard work, humility and to follow appropriate authority.

Added note

Added note: her opinions on a sporting ceremony, entertainers, political events and parties are kept general or left out.

#30.2Why hard things are allowed

Certain timelines allow some events because a person who is given everything becomes spoiled, waiting for mommy or daddy. By experiencing, we gain the percentage needed to lift humanity collectively. As in any battle, some get hurt; what matters is your reaction: not passivity, not panic, but standing up smartly from knowledge.

#30.3Wars and stargates

She was told many wars were really about stargates, bombing a region to tilt a stargate's frequency, and that some ruling families aren't seeking humanity's extinction, so nothing will be allowed to go that far.

But know that you must move on and start rebuilding your systems of social, schools, churches, police, the judicial system, the post office, banks... municipal offices." And then God is in play, and you will all see a miracle after a miracle": "an epic comeback of the good". "This will put your faith in God again"; a sense of patriotism and gratitude will return. Reposting and repeating "can you believe this?" puts more frequency in their field. "Do not give attention to complete idiocy and human sickness of degradation. It's that simple." On civil war: "Why are you always asking the wrong fear questions? Ask how humanity is winning."
