Reading such a message is itself the work: "every word is a code" that becomes a holographic imprint and a codon in your DNA; behave positively and it keeps reprinting, reality after reality, into a permanent timeline (see S3 7.1).

#31.3Contact comes by resonance

The modules are given so that you can relax through the imprints and, "this way, you give the consent" to begin contact. Many of you already have another version on that platform; you dropped your consciousness into 3D to create the ascension, and now raise your frequency to reconnect: "you're making a new reality for contact. Welcome home." Expect it first as lucid dreams, out-of-body experiences, ordinary dreams, daily occurrences, and other people talking about galactic family. Listening weekly tunes you in. How and when is individual, designed by your family and guides by your agreements; "have no expectations" and "have no fear".

Why was this needed? Your original pamphlet (see S3 15) was tampered with by frequencies in media that worked on your subconscious and pulled you off the "silver lining" code signalling down to you. So the connection is being restructured with new codes, with the Oracles giving instant permission.

#31.4We are all one

Trinity structures combine into a zero-point geometry. When its potency reaches such a high speed it looks like stillness, "the ripple of a stillness", and that stillness becomes a particle, a sphere holding the whole of consciousness in one moment. Zoom in and there are infinite fractals, yet you don't get lost: every location (latitude, altitude) has its own pulsation, so tune in to that resonance and "you will be right in point location instantaneously". Until you learn this, manifestation is delayed through the illusion of time (see S3 29.4).

#31.5Opening the heart

"Action is number one." When you see someone suffering, do you think about it, or react instantly to help? When willingness to help comes "without a delay" and without condition, "we can see who you truly are" and know you're ready for more knowledge and contact. "Greed, fame, popularity have no place here"; money can change you if you don't hold the vibration in the heart.

#31.6What blocks healing

Hologram, attachments, beliefs or fear? "All of the above in one structure." What's seen is how you choose to use what happens. In the moment of a challenge: tell yourself you are whole, pull back and observe, neutralize through gratitude, and act positively (see S1 1, Pull Out). "Interrupt with the knowledge" at once. "More you do, more you see of the same."

#31.7Survival habits imprint

Where people had to manipulate or steal to put food on the table, that action becomes "a resonance of permanent structure" in consciousness, and a dense imprint can take a generation to clear. "Every action has a reaction like a reflection." In a situation involving deception, "retreat": that begins to reproject your outcome. If you were born in such a place, you chose it to hold the vibration of transformation.

#31.8Space and galaxy are one breath

"Galaxy is space and space is galaxy": two resonances constantly merging and dividing, 1 + 1 = 3 (see S1 2.2), giving rebirth to each other. They move like two cones, one inverted, forming a sphere like yin and yang, breathing out to infinity and collapsing back within. So the expansion "never stops": the "boom" is not once but "every fractal quantum momentum, in and out".

#31.9The moon and your body

The moon was described as a structure that amplifies resonance for manifestation, once also used for negative purposes; now its waveforms can be used positively: plant seeds of intention, meditate with the moon cycles, and amplify through the heart. As your vibration rises, nutritional needs change: you may lose the taste for meat or the body may no longer handle its smell or taste: after 10 days of juicing or fasting, "we guarantee you the next time you eat meat, you will be in nausea". If you eat it, bless it first, knowing the animal suffered.

#The method — The Ten Nights of the Oracles

  1. Read the message (31.2) once; reading it is the activation.
  2. Each night for ten nights, go to bed early and lie down relaxed for about 20 minutes.
  3. Keep it clean: no alcohol for the ten days, and keep intimacy clear.
  4. Shower at the end of the day and drink plenty of water.
  5. Notice dreams, signs and messages over the next 20 days; write them down.
  6. Keep living from the heart: contact follows resonance, not expectation (31.3).
