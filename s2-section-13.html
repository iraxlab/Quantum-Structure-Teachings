The big idea

The teachings are getting complex, so this talk walks back through the quantum bylaws in order and shows they are one process: see your location, calm it to zero point with gratitude, transform the emotion with action, keep fluctuating into the positive until 1 + 1 = 3 crystallizes a permanent timeline. The hardest part, even for the teacher, is calming the emotion to zero and overriding the negative; skip it, and leftovers return until cleared. Don't reject what you feel: acknowledge, thank, then switch. Contact works the same way and comes with tests. And practise: check your location every 30 minutes, and feel the joy, without expectations.

#Key teachings

#13.1Why review, and what is hardest

This review will happen about once a year, because the structure gets complex. The biggest struggle for everyone, the teacher included, is to switch a negative frequency and override it with a positive one. What helps most: calming yourself down, then positive thinking and positive action, talking and engaging. As frequencies rise, thoughts become reality faster (sometimes within hours), and with practice the switch becomes the norm.

A warning from experience: watching a lot of violent drama, even "for learning", led to recurring nightmares of the same scenes. Everything you think and hear becomes a sonic whirlpool that ripples through parallel after parallel (see S2 2.4). Watch difficult material once, for education if needed, not over and over; and if something disturbs you, overwrite it with something positive, more than once (see S2 2.12).

#13.3What the review adds

The one particle. A hertz is one waveform per second. Vibrations similar in hertz gather into a trinity, and its centre becomes the zero point, the "black hole". Lower that particle's vibration, and it becomes thought, then matter. Each new hologram carries some vibration from the last, which is what gives you continuity; similar holograms in a row make a permanent timeline.

Zero point. If you don't fully calm yourself, particles of the old situation remain, and you'll meet them again through synchronicity until you clear to zero (see S2 3.6). It may be the thing to practise.

Fluctuating. Review your day honestly, moment by moment: how did I act with my partner, my friend? Peel the layers to see the truth (see S1 2.9). When someone says "that was your ego", listen: it gives you the true frequency, which you can then switch. And feeling sad isn't a sign that something is wrong with you: your consciousness is working perfectly, telling you you're off-balance with the heart. Take it as positive information.

Don't stop halfway. You don't know how long an override will take; for one person minutes, for another a week of constant action and thought, because you're learning along the way (sometimes someone has to come and tell you what you couldn't see). Stop in the middle, and you may never reach the positive outcome.

Stop, transform, let go. Every moment is a brand-new holographic imprint with nothing to do with the previous one; let go of yesterday's argument, or even of two minutes ago. We are learning the process so we can become masters of instantaneous manifestation. Whatever your weakest link (reacting too much, or not acting at all), work on it honestly.

#13.4Acknowledge; don't repress or reject

If you're crying and choose to smile, aren't you repressing it? No: you transform it. Acknowledge it, thank it ("thank you, negative emotion, I recognize you; you show me my location"), subside to zero, then shift with a new action. Rejecting is different ("I can't believe this!"): rejection is another frequency, and "boom, it comes back with a vengeance." The same with rain: "Thank you, rain, for watering the flowers", and then switch the hologram to sunshine. The same with money: not rejected, but received through the heart (see S1 4.5).

#13.5Contact comes with tests

Contact happens in a third reality you create between your timeline and theirs (see S2 4.8), through synchronicity, belief and your agreements with your guidance. How it happens is individual. And you will be tested: with your permission and your guidance, they may imprint a simulated holographic reality in your consciousness to see how well you've learned: what will you do, in the name of humanity, from the heart? Pass, and the next step and more knowledge come. The teacher was tested this way, every time, and chose to help without asking anything in return.

Contact also fluctuates: the more you trust, learn, read and imagine positive contact, the more experiences come, until they're constant. Don't fill yourself with frightening films about it. When she was introduced to the Council of Nine, she heard them telepathically checking the "particles" in her heart for anything unresolved; correcting quickly, rather than being perfect, is what counts.

#13.6Honesty clears the channel

Honesty gives the true vibration of your heart. A "white lie" imprints a probability in your consciousness that blocks full manifestation, until you clear it (see S1 3.2). This matters more now because consciousness's frequency is rising: what you could get away with before now shows quickly, even in the body (after her frequency upgrade, some foods make her ill). Detox only with a doctor's supervision.

#13.7You heal yourself

Other people don't hold energy for you; you imprint them as a permission slip. You already know the vibration: grab it and use it yourself. Even the teacher is a projection in your hologram. When you lower your emotional vibration, you give a permission slip to anything similar to come into your hologram, simply because it's the same resonance; so shift it (see S1 1.2).

#The method — The 30-Minute Location Check

Given as a challenge for the day, with results to be shared at the next talk:

  1. Every 30 minutes, check your location: what am I feeling and thinking right now?
  2. If it's negative, acknowledge and thank it, and calm it to zero point.
  3. Give gratitude for everything around you: clothes, food, family, friends, the sky, the air.
  4. Imagine what you want and raise the joy until you feel goosebumps, even tears of joy.
  5. Take a positive action: smile, a good film, a kind word.
  6. Have no expectations about how, when or from whom. A steady frequency has to reflect back: "it's a quantum bylaw."

#Questions answered

I left a toxic job a year ago and have meditated for a better one, but nothing has come. Will I find one? What can I do better?
Not while you hold expectations and doubt: doubt lowers your vibration and stops manifestation in the middle. Know you have the job now, and feel it. Meanwhile, don't sit and wait: take any job, even a humble one, with gratitude ("thank you for letting me pay my bills while I transition"), and keep working toward what you want; the two go together. Do you have a home, clothes, food? Then remove the negative emotion. Smile all day, check yourself every half hour, feel the goosebumps of happiness. You create the hologram that gives you the job; nobody gives it to you unless you projected it first.
I feel I'm resisting my ascension, not connecting to higher consciousness.
That belief is the only block. You're connecting right now: indirectly, through this talk, which is also in your hologram. Know you're connected, and the clear one-to-one channel follows. (A personal note: you're being given many opportunities, especially professional ones, but you're "picky" in choosing among them.)
I don't want to kill any creature, but what about mosquitoes?
It's part of learning; don't feel bad if it isn't intentional. Prevent them: certain plants, and oils such as mint and lavender around you, keep them away.
Who is "Morduk", mentioned at the start of HR?
A negative entity or structure of the three-dimensional platform, no longer there, because the timelines were switching to 5D. HR and NT are written in a 5D sequence, in code, that activates your DNA and RNA even if you don't understand them; that's why only the original material should be used (see S2 7.1).
From the booksDP: "Morduk is not there anymore but the throne is. So proclaim what is rightfully yours."
Should we take money out of education and pension funds soon?
A smooth transition is intended. A new cryptocurrency "code" is coming that will not allow manipulation; who and when isn't known. Don't act from fear; observe.
Added note

This is not financial advice. Talk to a licensed financial adviser before moving retirement or pension savings.

I've been ill for a year and a half, after a woman attacked me in a dream. Others are holding energy for me, but I still feel interference.
Others don't heal you; you heal yourself (see 13.7). For your health, go with doctors' supervision: it's a learning lesson; accept it and move forward. Then shift your vibration, practising every 30 minutes.
In the astral, I was shown portals for healing and regeneration. How can I create them?
Train lucid dreaming, so you can control it. There are mechanical portals and ether portals; your body-consciousness can create ether portals with clear focus and potent emotion (if it doesn't open, focus more clearly and try again).
What do you think of another channel on Pleiadian contact?
Not known here, but the guidance confirms it's okay. The teacher keeps to her own downloads so the material stays pure.
Is there anything the council would like to tell me?
A personal note about a family estrangement: it will resolve itself, without your getting involved.
As a teenager I left my body and had moments of knowing everything; now I have no spiritual experiences. Why?
You always do: "you are a spirit", so how could you not be having a spiritual experience?
Any advice for all my recent changes and challenges?
Go back to the quantum manifestation and study it; practise every 30 minutes to grab the vibration of joy. That knowledge gets you out of the challenges, with positive outcomes.
I feel my energy has been manipulated with technology for years, and I feel lost.
"We know who you are." Manipulating energy without permission is the reverse of consciousness. Mobilize your realities and act from the heart, and a permission slip will be given to get you out of it: "we promise you a free pass." And "you're never lost."
Support

If you feel watched, targeted or controlled, please talk to a doctor or someone you trust. These experiences can be very distressing, and support helps.

I woke up with three long, sharp lines on my body and no memory of the night. What happened?
Whether you remember a dream depends on its sequence and your lessons: some experiences vibrate too high for the three-dimensional brain to hold, but your higher self holds the information. Meditate to extract it.
Added note

Unexplained marks or injuries on your body deserve a doctor's look.

If I'm crying and switch to a smile, aren't I repressing the emotion?
No: see 13.4.
I'm a personal trainer. How can I join energy philanthropy?
While you keep paying your bills, offer something freely: for example, one hour every Sunday of free online workouts, live so people can interact, with a way to donate if anyone wishes. Thank every gift, even a dollar. Do it for the joy: that joy of giving is the frequency that creates full abundance. Leave how and when open.
