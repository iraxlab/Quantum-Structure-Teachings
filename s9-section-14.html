The big idea

A message received "from God" says the wheel is turning: "in the time of the Blue Moon, the Red Dragon will vanish", hidden power structures will be exposed, and "the dark overlords have six years left". The Council of Nine described the steps: until about 2029, infrastructure for digital control and surveillance is planted; in the following years it is implemented; around 2033, every force for humanity's liberation reaches the final stage of the battle. Evil can take a position by free will, but never win, because positive is ten times stronger. Don't consent and don't panic: if you find yourself in a tough situation, pull back, know you already have the solution, and keep looking until synchronicity brings it. Be the anchor: strong as steel and compassionate.

#14.1Evil can take a position, never win

Every particle born of consciousness mimics the original and receives free will, so a "child" of God can choose darkness and even forget it came from God. When God moves to the next level, the ripple gives all a chance to repent. Some choose to stay evil, but they remain under the umbrella of consciousness, and positive by the power of ten always activates someone or something to complete what another couldn't (see S7 E22).

The message: the next years (condensed, as received)

"For the next three years, basically until 2029, the evil structure... [is] setting up all their infrastructures to... take another level of entrapment": digitalization, banking, data systems. In the next three years they will implement it; by "2033... God, angels and God's powers and all the parallel realities and everybody that is involved in the liberation of the human race will [reach] the final stage of the battle", bombarding the evil "until they will vanish". Some regions and cities will face more challenge (15-minute cities, social credit). What you experience depends on what you amplify: "Let your body be sovereign, the way God created you." Technology is like a knife (it can harm or cut a birthday cake); until the evil is defeated, any technology can be used for harm.

Added note

Added note: these are predictions as received, not certainties. Her examples (a data-company contract for farmland, a law on vehicle shut-off technology) are her interpretation of real reports, which have other explanations; check the facts yourself.

#14.2If you're in a tough situation

"Say thank you; it's my own hologram within the collective... However, I can amplify my own version." Pull back, restructure your hologram, play the hide-and-seek game (see 9.10), consistently and relentlessly. Ask: "What can I do to help others?" Synchronicity will bring what you need. You came to help move humanity forward: be the anchor, "the boat that whoever is drowning can come to". Every day, do something in the name of humanity, without attaching to fame.

#14.4Reading the symbols

With help from AI, she explains the images: a Blue Moon (a second full moon in a month, about every 2.7 years) is in folklore a threshold of change; the Red Dragon of Revelation 12 symbolizes the adversary; "the wheel" means cyclical time across traditions; the Red Dragon "vanishes" rather than being defeated, because the frequency that sustained it is no longer generated; "apocalypse" originally means unveiling. "I am that I am" is the divine name from Exodus. Because all are fractals of God, God knows every thought, good or evil.

Added note

Added note: this is a received message and one interpretation of religious symbols; traditions read them in many ways. Its language about the fate of evil describes, in her teaching, a frequency that no longer matches, not a call to harm anyone.

#Questions answered

What does the Council say about "dark ninth-circle codes" that keep humanity imprisoned?
She has never heard of them and received nothing on it.
Who are the entities inside the moon?
She can't prove it: she hears they relate to the Nephilim, a mixture of cultures, and that the moon is an artificial surveillance body with bases on its far side connected to a "dark fleet". Others who speak about these programs may know more.
Can sleeping under a metal roof interfere with dreaming?
Heavy concrete blocks some signals, and metal, in her teaching, blocks the back-and-forth exchange between your body and consciousness (prana), which is why she advises against living in shipping containers; she cites old experiments in shielded boxes where people grew tired.
Will God spare those who were split personalities working for evil?
It isn't punishment but frequency: God gave each fractal the free will to become anything; some vibrate so negatively that, if they won't repent, they no longer match God's new frequency and "disseminate" back to source as a spark. The religious language helps people accept the quantum structure. Respect every person, whatever religion they were born into; all is one unit. Wake up and convert physically; you always had free will.
