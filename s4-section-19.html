Nanobots

What her Nano Technology book says nano material is built from; nanobots for construction or destruction; how nano in the body can be neutralized and flushed; the molecular machines already in your cells; a message about not consenting to bidding wars; and practical steps for a home (live talk, May 2021; the recording begins partway through)

The big idea

Nanotechnology is "the particles of the human evolution", building blocks of the cosmic energetic field that can be assembled into tiny robots for any function. Like a computer, they can heal or harm: the intention decides (see S4 15.2). Have no fear of the technology; hold the positive, and you create realities where it serves you. The same law applies to money: when systems manipulate people into fighting each other, don't consent by joining the race; combine with your family, from the heart, and build from there.

#Key teachings

#19.1What nano material is built from

Her book Nano Technology (download it from her website, Downloads → Books) was given by the Pleiadians "because they want us to know what is possible in technology". Nano particles are created as blocks that combine into robotics, "constructed elements that are built of 7 hertz to 8 hertz mega and anti-mega particles". Made from the positive structure, one nanoparticle and another must be allowed to accept each other through their energetic field; this usually takes about three days, after which the bond in the nano chain is very strong.

From the booksFrom her books (NT): "Constructive elements are build of 7 Hz to 8 Hz mega and anti-mega particles... This energetic mega force is the building blocks of all the cosmic energetic field and is constructing the structure of the nano anti-matrix of reality." When a particle starts to split, "let it stay and let it stabilize by itself for 3 days" (see S3 36.3). To build with nano, "start with the Frequency and Sound structures".

#19.2Construct or destruct

Nanobots could build buildings and materials, "you can do anything with that"; or destroy, such as breaking down walls by frequency, evaporating them through sound. Chromosomes can be neutralized at a zero point that redirects the nanoparticles. And if nano material has entered your body, it can be neutralized through resonance and flushed out through fluids, with certain frequencies, "easily done". Anyone working in a facility with nano-coated fabrics should be protected. The Council said human consciousness will be helped globally by galactic resonances that neutralize and restore the body to its original codon. (Her disclaimer: "a fictional story... my belief system.")

Machines already in your cells. She showed a scientist's video of the molecular machines at work in every cell: kinesin, which "walks around" carrying things; the ribosome, "the factory floor", which reads RNA and builds new machines; machines that rotate, copy DNA, and carry garbage out of the cell. Nature already runs on nanobots; with positive intention, super technology could help the body heal, as with protecting the gut's villi (S4 15.2).

#19.3The message: don't join the race

"You are going to see the worst and the best of humanity." Watching home prices, she started channelling: builders "withholding homes and making bidding wars between people" while so many suffer is "absolute disgrace".

The message

"In the end, the only companies that will survive for the new beginning will be the companies that are changing their ways that are beneficial for humanity. To start this new beginning, you as a human race have to choose not to consent to the manipulation by not including yourself in the so-called rat race. Start being compassionate with each other... Through your fear and outbidding each other with money, you're literally playing in the hands of the same system that you are running from."

"Stay with your families; include all that you can do from the heart, by combining all your income into the household, and construct your new realities from there. The constructive motion of that action in your collective heart structure, the family, will reconstruct the negative realities to positive, and you will be able to survive."

"Not a judgment; a fact. The fact will lead you to the truth, and the truth will set you free." Making money is fine, "but you have to do it in a humane way"; extortion in a crisis is not capitalism. And think it through: lower taxes on a new home mean nothing beside a mortgage three times the price. "Education is fundamental, so we can see this manipulation."

#The method — Building a Home Outside the Race

  1. Combine with family: move in with parents or family, pool incomes, help each other, pay off homes rather than pay billions to those with no heart.
  2. Use family land if you can: ask for half an acre; start with an RV, live there, gather the money, everyone works, then build. Learn on YouTube (she cites a single mother who built a five-bedroom home herself).
  3. Research low-cost methods: Aircrete homes (not only domes; any shape), 3D-printed homes, people living in vans.
  4. Before you build, ask the municipal office whether it's allowed, and about water and electricity and their costs.
  5. Before you buy land: check for liens and back taxes, test the soil for toxicity (what was there before?), look into unclaimed properties; if you use an attorney, make sure you are the buyer, not the attorney.
  6. If you build with a builder now, lock in the price of the lot and house: prices may be raised before you finish. Watch for lifelong builder fees in some communities.

"If you look for them, you are going to create situations to give you the solutions" (see S4 9, S4 10).

What she saw: "a year to a year and a half of battle" with this craziness, "like vultures" profiting from desperation. Be cautious; if you do buy now, lock in the price (see the method).
