The big idea

Latitude and altitude (X and Y) is your location in the quantum structure. Every time the one particle flickers, it imprints a new hologram with its own frequency, and that frequency is where you are. A situation simply shows you your location; you then pull out, go to zero point, switch from the heart and hold it steady until the fluctuation settles in the positive (1 + 1 = 3). Whatever you see around you is a reality within the reality: you don't have to make it yours, and you don't reject it; you transform it by running your own frequency inside it. Acting out of fear to protect yourself infuses the very thing you fear: project what you want to see it, rather than waiting to see it first.

#Key teachings

#15.1Reality within the reality

The quantum structure includes the whole field, so you may see platforms of realities anywhere: a war, a new mandate, a technology everyone fears. That doesn't have to be your truth: you create a hologram within the hologram, resonating on your own vibration and timeline. You can't reject the quantum, but you can transform it: be aware of what's around you, and keep projecting your own frequency over it, again and again. That repetition is how "mutation" happens: whatever you focus your energy into is what's projected back.

While one system collapses and a new one comes in, you must project the new to see it, and that depends on your belief system. When you drop into fear, your consciousness tells you you're off balance with the heart: can you fix it?

#15.2Acting from fear creates what you fear

Reading more and more, getting emotional and rushing to "protect myself here and there" can backfire: an action taken to protect yourself from something infuses its probability. Why protect against something that isn't there? Nobody is outside of you (see S2 9.6); the action projects a reality in which you will need protecting. So leave it open, and know you are well, whatever is happening in the field.

Added note

This is about acting from fear. Ordinary prudence, health care and reasonable preparation remain wise, and the teachings themselves advise them (see S2 8, S2 14.3).

#15.3Project it to see it

Knowing the mechanics answers the "why" behind the advice: why forgive, why pull out, why not believe what you see. In the quantum, "you have to project it to construct it to see it, not see it to construct it." Taking what you see as the only reality is like watching yourself in a mirror and insisting the reflection is the real you.

#15.4Latitude and altitude, step by step

Picture two axes, X and Y, crossing at the zero point, with four areas: minus-minus, minus-plus, plus-minus and plus-plus. This is your whole hologram, a matrix of numbers, and numbers are frequencies. It is also one molecule, expanding to infinity and collapsing back (see S1 1.1).

As the particle flickers back and forth, it imprints itself: each flicker creates a part, a fractal hologram within the hologram, a new Earth, to infinity, even within each dot. That is the ascension process. Each imprint has its own frequency, and that frequency gives a signal: your latitude and altitude, your location. Your emotions, thoughts and deeds create the magnetic and gravitational field of the pulsing molecule, so each choice shifts you into a brand-new hologram, at a new location.

Flicker between negative and positive without settling, and you fluctuate across the quadrants (see S1 3). Shift steadily to the positive, and you collapse everything into 1 + 1 = 3 in the positive. So this one bylaw connects collapsing realities, fluctuating realities, the zero point and 1 + 1 = 3.

From the booksSD: "RESTORATION – Happens when you need a jump from one hologram… to another when you change the sound equilibrium Hz to a slight angle into the matrix ( X, Y ) Latitude and altitude… Every degree of angular expression has different sound sonic cymatic whirlpool reality holograms" (see S2 3.3).

#15.5Steady, with a blueprint, and sovereign

It has to be a steady vibration. With a clear blueprint and constant input from the heart (neutralizing and switching whatever you see), the particle of what you imagine becomes so potent that it pulses and pops into matter. Without that training, you'll get outcomes you didn't want, because you never pointed your emotion steadily at what you want. No expectations about who or how. Practising every half hour trains exactly this (see S2 10).

It's hardest with people: someone is mean, and you're asked to smile and imagine you get along. But don't give away your sovereignty: imagine the positive, yet pull out and observe so no one takes it; "that doesn't mean you go back into the lion's mouth" (see S2 7.4). Peel the layers.

#15.6Watching the movie: two examples

Rain. Before an outdoor gathering it poured all day. Instead of rejecting the rain, the teacher thanked it, knew it was sunny, felt the sun and the joy, and kept holding that vibration; within an hour of the party the sun came out. Rain isn't negative; it's another hologram. Holding one vibration steadily replaces one holographic imprint with another.

A storm. With the power out and everyone in fear, she neutralized it with gratitude ("storm, thank you"), played a game (imagining life in an earlier century, by candlelight, with family), and found everything she needed: fuel, a quiet shop, food. Inside the same storm, she ran her own hologram with a completely different outcome. Even there, gratitude: some people a short distance away were suffering far more.

#15.7The zero point, clarified

The zero point (see S1 2.1) is not "being thankful I'm sad and choosing love" in one step. First, realize you're in a situation and pull back: "I'm a hologram." Let the emotion subside to nothing: aware, observing like a film, with no opinion and no emotional frequency at all. Then give gratitude to neutralize whatever came in. Only then imagine what you want and feel it, so it starts to vibrate. Think of a computer: you shut one program down and install another. The two holograms run on different frequencies (as an illustration: 1 Hz and 2 Hz); the zero point is the neutral gap in between. Never reject: rejection is itself a frequency (see S2 13.4).

#15.8The blueprint is a house plan

Imagination is the blueprint, like the plan of a house: picture your new Earth, what it looks like, what you're doing. That Earth already exists with you; to make it matter, infuse the blueprint with emotion from the heart until it vibrates and comes alive, and take action toward it. You are building your new house, your new Earth, and shifting into it.

#15.9Intuition and desire

Intuition is frequency: your higher self showing you that something is going on that needs your attention. Listen to it, explore it, run with it. And to choose what to do: the most exciting option is the highest frequency from the heart, so it makes the molecule vibrate fastest and has the most probability of expanding into matter. What you only half-want has less potency.

#15.10Guidance, tasks and integrity

A health condition doesn't stop you connecting with guidance. People are given tasks; if they go badly off track (greed, conflict), guidance may pull back, but "there's always another level" as long as you stay on the platform. When collaborations broke down around the teacher, she gave gratitude and moved on, without blaming, keeping her own integrity. Not everyone can stay on top of that wave, especially around money; it's challenging.

#15.11Support each other

To build a society from the heart, we must support each other through the transition: "you cannot just take", or there's no exchange, only overtaking. Communities like this one can connect and help each other locally: clothes, painting a house, a little money for someone in need. Energy philanthropy can work for anyone (see S1 5). And "one person can change the entire world": whose world? Yours, because it's your perception.

#The method — Find Your Location, and Move It

  1. Read the situation as a location: "This shows me where I am." No judgment.
  2. Pull out: "I'm a hologram; I'm watching a film."
  3. Subside to zero: no emotion, only awareness.
  4. Give gratitude to neutralize what came in ("thank you, rain").
  5. Switch: imagine the blueprint you want and feel it from the heart; take an action toward it.
  6. Hold it steady, every time the old picture returns, until the fluctuation settles in the positive.
  7. Keep your sovereignty: positive, but observant; never back into the lion's mouth.

#Questions answered

I'm aware of so many distortions around me that I can't find myself, and fear clouds my decisions.
It's confusion, not loss of self. Know what you want, and imagine it. Whatever happens around you, neutralize it with gratitude and tell yourself "I'm watching the movie", then switch to what you want. If I give you tea and you'd like coffee, you don't get angry; you just ask for coffee. Play the game. For decisions: if you're connected with fear rather than knowing, you'll produce more of the same; know that, whatever comes, you're fine, and you'll find the solution (see 15.6).
My cat has a broken, abscessed tooth; the specialist costs twice my monthly income. How can I help her?
She needs a doctor's intervention. Take action: call or visit the expensive specialist in person and ask about payments or a lower price; the teacher once did this for her son and was charged half. The price, too, is part of the hologram you can transform. Know she is healed, and act; if not this vet, a better one. A temporary fix isn't a permanent one.
Added note

A dental abscess in a pet is painful and can be serious; get veterinary care promptly. Ask about payment plans, and look for animal-welfare charities or veterinary schools that offer lower-cost treatment.

Is the zero point accepting the situation, being thankful I'm sad and choosing love?
Close, but first comes stillness: see 15.7. (A health note was also given: possible calcium and magnesium depletion from the diet; look into it. No galactic information was given.)
Added note

Check with a doctor before adding supplements; a simple blood test can show what you actually need.

My whole life has shifted in the last two or three years. Any advice?
You'll keep shifting, according to how you hold your frequency. Stay positive and neutralize everything; systems are collapsing. Stay away from negative social media input: be aware, but without anger, "like swimming between situations." Positive frequency connects you with benevolent beings; challenges still come, because you're here to learn both sides and become a master of creation. Do your research, and stay in the heart.
Can you have dreams within dreams while lucid dreaming, as in the film? What's the benefit?
Yes. Every reality is like a dream state (see S2 12.9). A dream is another parallel reality running at the same time. When you become lucid and open a portal, you create yet another reality within it: three structures running at once, with awareness. It can take you to very different levels, each with your own guidance when the time is right. Don't rush; you'll be tested on how you treat people: "what stays in the end is how much you did in the name of humanity."
A huge snake fell on me in a dream. In meditation I befriended it and I'm no longer afraid, but it hasn't shrunk. What does it mean?
It doesn't matter how big it is: it's positive (sometimes you need a lion to protect you, not a cub). Yours was the transformation of fear. "You don't run from situations; you face them and transform them."
I help animals, especially horses, with energy; visible lights now come to help. Am I doing it right?
Your consciousness will know. Write down the first thing that comes, before old beliefs override it. Each animal is individual; let it be guided, and trust the process. Unlearning a lifetime of beliefs takes time, and the past wasn't "wrong": the deeper you go into the dark, the faster you reach the light (see S2 10.8). As you keep this frequency, more like-minded people come into your reality.
I've inherited a house and orchard and want to create a retreat centre for awakening. Any guidance?
Start small, from the heart, and build the vibration; stay out of the trap of the monetary system. If people respect you, support always comes, one way or another (see 15.11). (A personal skin note was also given: very thin skin layers and inflexible capillaries; avoid too much sun, and above all, love and accept yourself.)
Added note

Facial redness such as rosacea is common and treatable; a dermatologist can help.

I'm trained in a healing practice but can't get started on my mission. What should I do?
It's confusion. Take the first step: act, trusting that you will make something of it. Productivity comes from action.
Must the elimination diet, juicing and distilled water be done together? Can children do it?
This is how the teacher was taught and did it, shared freely, not prescribed; she isn't a doctor. The elimination diet matters because the body should flush out steadily, slowly, step by step, "without opening the dam", or you deplete nutrients and get ill. For children: not three days of distilled water (at most a day, on and off), because their immune systems fluctuate; juices, shakes and an elimination diet only with a doctor's supervision.
Added safety note

Don't put children on fasts or restrictive cleanses. Any diet change for a child, or for an adult with a medical condition, should be supervised by a doctor.

I cleansed too fast and now have ongoing bowel and leg problems. Is this an illusion I should just relax into?
No: it isn't an illusion. Get to the bottom of it with doctors (a gastroenterologist if your gut is affected); rebuilding the gut is its own process. Even when guidance spoke to the teacher, she always acted together with medical supervision. Open your heart, yes, but take the action.
How does intuition work, and how do I know what I really want?
See 15.9.
Does a condition such as rheumatoid arthritis block connection with guidance?
No: see 15.10.
