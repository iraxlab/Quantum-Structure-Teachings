The Emergency Broadcast System

The big idea

As the frequency rises, the negative side pushes harder for its own survival, so hold the line daily: thoughts, actions and deeds in the now, without expectations. A channeled message says one collective signal will reach everyone, but each person receives it according to their own coherent resonance, like a radio, and it begins restoring the original DNA according to each soul's agreements. Asked about an emergency broadcast system, the hybrids answered: not yet, have faith, but the biggest broadcast system is awakened humans. You don't have to consent to a negative frequency; you stand up, share, and use the legal means that already exist locally.

#Key teachings

#18.1Why it feels harder now

The more the frequency increases and we fight for freedom, the more the other side fights for its own. What was right is presented as wrong (the upside-down world), to lower the vibrational sequence of your consciousness. But a reality that cannot hold the new vibration coming from God consciousness will self-dissolve, "out of nowhere". Your job: keep consciousness, mind, soul and body in the highest resonance, using everything learned in quantum manifestation. Expect more pressure and "hold the line": the resonances are separating.

#18.2How she channels

She is always in open channeling: sometimes direct trance, sometimes she pulls out to explain complex information in plain words, sometimes it is simply knowing. A single live question can open entirely new information.

The message: the collective signal (condensed)

Each of you will receive "a specific pulsation within its own field". When the collective reaches coherent resonance, "even when one signal is given, all of you will receive whatever you need... accordingly to your vibration like a radio signal". "This is why it was so necessary for you to reach that level of consciousness individually" first. The signal acts "like a switch" that starts "recording your original DNA". "Whoever needed to exit the reality will exit by agreement"; those confused through fear "will receive the sequences eventually". How fast you recover depends on your genetic component and on how strong the synthetic sequences in your body were. It will be helped by a web of antennas linking to "mother antennas" in a zigzag geometric pattern, "held from above" and below: the receiver and the sender. "When that will happen, we will not say."

Added note

Added note: the message refers to synthetic substances taken into the body. This is her channeled view, not medical information; make health decisions with a qualified doctor.

The message: you do not have to consent (condensed)

"Anyone negative or positive can choose to do whatever they want in the field. It's a free will... However, you do not have to consent to the sequence of that negative frequency." In consciousness there is always a balance; when one side dominates too long, consciousness reprints new frequencies to override it. "Have no fear, have no hatred." What you project comes back to you: you hold, project and receive the light, a Trinity structure.

The message: the hybrids on the emergency broadcast system (condensed)

"Not yet. Will be soon, but it's a bit tricky. Have faith." No prediction was given. Evil "is very clever, manipulative and has a lot of minions", souls overtaken by a dense oversoul ("walking dead"). "Biggest broadcast system is the humans... You will be so clear on vibration... you will all shift the reality instantaneously at once." Evil cannot rise to a higher frequency, so it needs you like a "breathing living pure source battery", which is why it aims to turn humans into synthetic beings connected to an AI "motherboard". "In energy [it] is simply replacing one frequency with more potent one." "Wake up, stand up, reflect with action... share, discuss"; go to your local leaders, who "work for you, not the other way around", and use the legal statutes.

Added note

Added note: she says she is not a lawyer. Check any legal step with a qualified professional. Claims about specific groups, documentaries and public figures are left out here.

#18.3Those caught in fear

Some people who were overwhelmed (for example a single parent working long hours with no time to research) may, at soul level, have taken that role so others could hold the vibration and move forward. As old systems go down, new ones rise.

#18.4Share freely

Share information with love, even if you aren't the popular one in the family: the first warriors go against the grain. The Council of Nine's point: don't escape one prison only to build another by locking up information people need. If you charge, charge reasonably and leave room for donations; work from the heart with many people and you will always make a living.

Added note

Added note: this teaching echoes the "permission slip" idea; the related HR point is that action, not prayer alone, completes manifestation.

#Questions answered

How will the new model of consciousness, ground zero, help all forms in consciousness?
Ground zero is a fully balanced coherent resonance of everything your collective consciousness gathered, in one moment: the portal of total coherence, but also stillness, a zero-point void that floats like a vacuum. Shifting from portal to portal goes through the heart structure. In a portal you are actually spinning so fast it feels like moving in a stream; that spinning creates the void that holds your position, and in the stillness frequencies convert.
How do we overcome cycles of fear that mask spirituality as something negative?
Fear is only a vibration, and it isn't always negative: at times it protects you and asks you to be cautious and pull back, while at other times you need to push. Too much joy can also make you careless. It becomes a problem when low fear dominates, fed by constant low-resonance input, overriding your analytical process; with your consent you are then overtaken. Don't reject it; balance it.
How do we become pillars of the new society and find our purpose?
You are the broadcasting system, connected to God consciousness and downloading in the now, without consenting and without fear. No one is in power over you; you consented to a frequency so long it repeated. Stand up physically: mobilize neighbors and local schools, and learn your local bylaws and legal rights. "Instead [of] sitting back and praying... take the physical action."
Can you say more about May 5th and rising consciousness?
Some dates carry precise photonic resonance, different for each planetary system. On Earth, such a date locks in a permanent timeline from what you collected through your behavior, thoughts, actions and deeds, positive or negative. Rituals use geometry and planetary alignment to amplify intention; this also happens naturally for beings connected to God consciousness. Knowing the mechanics, you can use it positively.
