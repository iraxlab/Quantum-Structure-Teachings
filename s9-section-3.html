The big idea

The Council of Nine said all religions are expressions that ultimately come from source: consciousness chose to experience itself through different belief systems, and each person chooses by free will what to experience. Through history, texts were reprinted, distorted and sometimes infiltrated, and radically different belief systems were used to divide one human race so it could be controlled. An oversoul can send the same information through different figures, Ra for one person, Jesus for another, the hybrids for her, so each population receives what it can accept. Christ consciousness is not one religion but a frequency: acting from the heart with compassion and inner strength. The truth is found within; the true saviour is you.

#3.1One race, divided to be controlled

In her teaching, when consciousness was trapped in an artificial platform (see S8 E36, 9.1), one way to control the "flock" was to give different regions belief systems so opposite that, brought together, they would clash. If you study history (ancient tablets, the Book of Enoch, scholars who connect the dots), you find one race and one consciousness split by prejudice and dogma. Someone of another faith is another version of you; the quantum structure should unite, not separate.

#3.2Texts changed through history

Many original texts were never shown to the public, and reprinting through centuries allowed infiltration. She noticed similar stories repeated in different regions under different names, and decodes advanced technology in old texts. When facts are shown and someone rejects them out of fear, that fear is how control works. "I'm here to present, not to convince."

#3.3A scholar's comparison

A scholar points out that "Amen" was the name of an Egyptian sun god; that in Revelation Jesus calls himself "the Amen"; that he is called "the bridegroom"; and that Psalm 19 describes the sun coming out of a tabernacle "as a bridegroom coming out of his chamber", together with the child Jesus being taken to Egypt. He suggests a continuation of ancient sun worship.

She was first contacted by the hybrids, then by "Ra consciousness" (a spectrum of an oversoul), which introduced her to the Council of Nine. Someone like her could never speak in a church; someone else can bring it to believers. To wake up fully, you go through all of them.

Yes: every word is a sound; words in combination become mantras that form fractals in the field and embed in your subconscious, which in time leads you consciously to change your trajectory. You must first know a reality is possible to project it, which is also why familiarity with AI and technology prepares humanity to become interstellar.
Are the Hindu gods ETs?
Most religious systems are linked with off-planet worlds, though not exactly the same; originally the separation of religions came from off-planet intelligence, for the control of certain groups.
