Warp Speed Method

The big idea

Every fractal moment of every life collapses into one precise pulsation, so precise that God consciousness recognizes it anywhere at once. As consciousness rises to its next level it opens a portal for itself, a whirlpool, and pulls each soul of matching resonance through, warp speeding it to the place in the "future now" where it belongs. So the collective's 30-year transformation can be lived by you as 3 years. You don't do the warp speed yourself: you create the conditions, purity of heart, positive accumulation, a clean body, and consciousness does it.

#Key teachings

#32.1One pulsation per soul

Each person is a particle with its own reality and purpose for the collective. Every free choice, moment by moment, fluctuates and collapses until a whole lifetime ("George", from birth to death) has one measurable resonance with sub-tonalities, and thousands of lifetimes collect into one pulsation. Bylaw: even on a pre-written path, each moment is free will. When you become fully self-aware, opposite lives join, and you feel at once the pain of the one you hurt and the pain of the one who hurt you, because each is your reflection. That is why lessons return: what you did comes back to you in another life.

#32.2Light curves

Light has curvature: each flicker changes position in longitude and latitude, a Fibonacci spiral. Negative consciousness measures everything in the lowest sequences and fights itself without knowing: it's like amnesia. The whole universe, Pleiadians included, is moving to the next level, not only Earth.

#32.3The warp speed

When consciousness reaches enough resonance it hits zero point and switches. Souls who chose by free will to hold the negative stay in a parallel reality that eventually spins off and self-destructs. Those of matching resonance are pulled "like a magnet" and warp sped into a timeline above 3D, each to a different reality according to their pulsation and agreements (one to 5D, another to 7D). Even a good person who slipped in fear may still be pulled through, if the overall pulsation is positive. Failing an agreement isn't failing: guides never give up.

Added safety note

Added safety note: she linked physical shifting to water fasting and detox, with her own disclaimer: "do not try this" unless truly connected and guided. Prolonged fasting can be dangerous; never do it without medical supervision.

#32.4The picture-frame method

A life is like film frames, each a hologram with its own frequency and coordinates. Recall a frame's frequency (the feeling, the geometry) and you can shift your consciousness there, as when you recall your bedroom rather than walk every step: you erase the steps, shrinking time. Use the galactic meditation method, but with the new Earth in place of your galactic family: feel you are already there, 100%, and when things collapse you'll find a new bank, new friends, new positions.

The message: warp speed (condensed)

Each fractal hologram must be "in very specific frequency to be able to accept the restructuring of God light in order to maintain its own orbital position." Where recognized, it "will be given the sound of light", which speeds its time leap. "Each particle can hold individual world of galaxies." Consciousness "opens portal... creates the invert vertical cymatic sound that ultimately speeds up time evolution and shifts that particle reality of so-called future now" to its exact position. With the proper resonance you are warp sped 30 years ahead, though it will seem like three and feel normal: "the air is clear... the water is clean. People are nice." "Soon for some of you" this will be the case.

#32.5Not the technology

Asked whether this relates to the technology of that name, the Council said no: this is the pure consciousness and natural body. Artificially speeding an unready or negative consciousness causes amnesia, psychopathic behaviour or dissolution; those relying on technology, cloning or transhumanism to reach higher realms "will be truly sadly disappointed" and stay only in the lower realms.

#Questions answered

What if I'm about to go through the 5D portal, feel fear, and get pulled back to 3D?
It won't happen: when you're ready you won't even notice, it will feel like another day. In 5D there are still emotions and challenges, but no fear, because you understand how things work. You'll be tested individually: "your fame, glory and money is not going to be the criteria", only the heart's accumulation. She was once tested by helping a sick young woman no one else would help; treating others as yourself is the quantum structure.
Won't everything collapse? How will we convert our bodies?
Pull back and use the methods: America already made it. Don't worry about what's put in food; do your best daily. In individual ascension you'll find the organic farm, the right school, because you create it by looking for it. Don't let anyone's fear project their reality into you: compassion with steel.
