Section 13

Q & A: Staying Young, Sharing Freely and Collective Permission

Raising frequency and youth, building new technology from the ground up, how contact happens, why the shift came, and natural versus artificial timelines (live Q&A)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

Raising the frequency of your consciousness, and your body with it, is linked to staying young, but it has to be done gently and safely. New technology will come from the people up: share designs, pool small amounts, help each other build, rather than waiting for sponsors. The old systems are collapsing because, collectively, the positive became more potent, and that joy gave permission for benevolent help, which never comes without it. Some use devices to build artificial timelines; master the natural way, through the heart and the body as a vessel, and no one can manipulate you.

#Key teachings

#13.1Raising your frequency, and staying young

Question: how can aging be reversed, if everything comes from consciousness?

The answer links youth to activating a "young enzyme". As you raise the frequency of your consciousness, with lighter food and, in time, a vegetarian diet, you draw frequency from prana, the energy of the quantum field, and your molecules vibrate faster (see 9.7). Any change of diet should be made slowly, with professional guidance.

The teacher's own experience of a long honey-and-water fast is described with a clear warning: don't do it; it was done only with special preparation. The distilled-water claims from 12.7 are repeated here. The talk also mentions urine therapy and a long water-only "cure" raised by a participant; see the health note.

A lesson worth keeping: guidance was checked with research before it was trusted. Don't follow anything blindly, even from a trusted source; find out why.

From the booksA message titled "The Recovery of Cell Degeneration To Stay Young" says that with the right frequency sequence applied to the DNA, "your body cells will not degenerate again and it will stay young" (SD, NT).
Important health note

Prolonged fasting, honey-and-water or water-only regimes, and drinking urine are not safe or proven ways to reverse aging or detox, and can cause serious harm. Don't try them. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before any big change to what you eat or drink, especially if you have a health condition or take medication.

#13.2New technology comes from the people up

Question: a study group is setting up a science non-profit and plans to share a low-cost energy design. Any advice?

The new platform will come from the people up, not from governments, with support following later. When you have a working technology, share it: build a few units, tell others how, and pool small contributions so everyone can produce their own and help each other. Waiting for a sponsor or trying to profit keeps a great invention stuck, because not everyone finds a sponsor.

This is the heart structure in practice (see 5.1): sharing and giving, and if you have money, supporting someone who doesn't. Otherwise you pour your energy in and people only take. You can receive from other platforms too; learn to manifest, so what you need comes to you the proper way (see 4.4). The real change comes once something visibly works.

From the books"The right scientists will appear assorted by us energetically to perfect the technology," bringing "FREE ENERGY to everyone's home"; "Do not be dazzled by money and popularity" (DP).

#13.3How contact happens

Question: in what ways have people had contact?

In different ways: telepathically; a light being appearing in front of someone with instructions to do something for humanity; ongoing messages about structures, for instance for building a device to be given freely; or being taken out of the body, like astral projection, and shown what to do. Many people are being contacted quietly, and it isn't in the mainstream news yet. Some information is held back for a time on guidance's advice.

All of it points the same way: toward a unity structure, where the competition for money, glory and fame dissipates. So stay in the heart.

#13.4Why the shift came: collective permission

The collapse of old systems is described as "an avalanche" (see 12.1). Why now? A reality has to be potent to shift (see 2.2, 3.3). Collectively, the positive became more potent than the negative. Even people who know nothing of these teachings contribute, simply through the heart.

That joy and wish for change gave permission for higher consciousness and benevolent races to step in and help the ascension, because humanity was manipulated and needed a boost. Benevolent help never takes over without permission. Real guidance never hands out very personal information about the people around you; at most a short caution, such as "stay back". Anything that overrides your consciousness is using negative structures, not the heart.

From the booksHigher consciousness "collectively ask[ed] your permission to increase the sequence" while rebuilding the grid toward the 5-dimensional one; "Well it is all new now. Enjoy your freedom" (DP).

#13.5Natural shifting versus artificial timelines

Technology is neither good nor bad; it depends on how it's used. As part of ascension we go through technology, and it's exciting. At the same time, teach your body to be the vessel of that energy field. Then you can shift realities by thought, without a device.

Some who can't ascend naturally, because their frequency doesn't match those realities, use devices to build artificial timelines and holograms they can control. Learn the quantum manifestation platform instead: you'll be able to tune your energy field, leave one timeline and create another, and no one will be able to suppress or manipulate you. If you are manipulated without your permission, or act while blinded, you'll be helped and given the chance to change. And don't judge anyone who does harm; they may have been manipulated too (see 12.6).

From the books"The physical body of yours is a vessel of energetic force. To reach certain realms of existence, you have to match the frequency components and then shift to that reality" (SD).

#13.6Remembering is co-creating

Question: do I have a contract to remember contact with my star family?

Tune into the structures; you already know how. Remembering and co-creating happen at the same time, because it is one structure (see 5.4, 11.7). The answer isn't simply handed over, because finding it yourself is how you learn. (The recording ends during this answer.)

#The method — Become the Vessel

  1. Stay in the heart. Project joy and the wish for change; that is your share of the collective.
  2. Raise your frequency gently. Lighter living, changed slowly and with professional advice. No extreme fasts.
  3. Check guidance with research. Don't follow anything blindly; find out why.
  4. Share what you have: knowledge, designs, time, a little money. Build together instead of waiting for sponsors.
  5. Don't judge. Neutralize, and master your own shifting so no one can manipulate you.
  6. Want to remember something? Tune in and co-create it; finding it is the lesson.
