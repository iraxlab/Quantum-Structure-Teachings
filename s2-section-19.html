Self-Worth and Giving, Through the Quantum Structure

Why "charge to value yourself" is an illusion, how free giving returns as abundance, free will and the tasks you must complete, a guide to focused manifestation, releasing grief, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

"If you don't charge, you don't value yourself" is called a three-dimensional illusion. In the quantum structure, whoever you give to is another version of yourself: setting a price puts a condition on your own reflection, and conditions come back. Leaving it open, so people support you freely from the heart, builds heart frequency that returns like a boomerang. So "you value yourself the most when you give freely." This is a transition, not a leap: a main provider can charge and pay the bills, then give a day or two. Free will works "to a point": tasks you came to complete will be completed, with guidance nudging you back on track. And grief and old pain can be released, because what happened belongs to another timeline.

#Key teachings

#19.1The illusion: "charge to value yourself"

When you think a thought, your molecule imprints it in the quantum structure and you project another version of yourself (another person) to interact with; between you, a third structure forms (1 + 1 = 3; see S1 2.2). If, into that thought, you put a dollar sign ("I value myself; to get this information, you must pay"), then that condition is imprinted on your own reflection. You are putting a condition on yourself, through another version of you, for information that was given to you freely. "Tell me if this is in the heart."

Charging isn't wrong: it's the three-dimensional structure, and you can make billions that way. But we're moving into the heart structure, where you leave it completely open: let your reflection come to the heart and decide freely whether to support you. Then the exchange, and the third structure it creates, is entirely in the heart, with no condition on manifestation.

#19.2Giving freely returns like a boomerang

Support given from someone's free heart carries a completely different frequency back to you. Do it moment to moment, with joy, and you accumulate heart frequency; the next hologram you shift into is in the heart structure, a new timeline no longer in the conditioned one. "Our job is to accumulate by giving": the more you accumulate, the more potent it becomes, until it pops into full abundance (see S1 4, S2 12.7).

The lesson from the teacher's own week: an urgent call came from a mother with a sick child; busy as she was, she helped fully, asking nothing, in joy. Within half an hour, an unexpected donation arrived that more than covered that time. "You cannot make money on people's suffering." And to be supported, project support: she regularly gives small amounts to others whose work she values.

From the booksDP: "Does it not hurt you when you are in need of help and yet someone is plastering a false price to your suffering?… Be love, be joy, be giving" (see S2 7.7).

#19.3Asking without permission overtakes free will

"Asking for money without a permission is overtaking somebody's consciousness": you don't give them the free will to support you. It comes from a self-illusion of looping, needing something because you don't know how to manifest freely. The council's challenge: "Would you help that human being if you couldn't put a value on it?" If someone has nothing to give and you have the knowledge, how is withholding it valuing them, or yourself? And you're never "depleted" by giving: keep it in neutrality, without rejecting either the person or the money. "When you give from the heart, you preserve your soul" and keep your consciousness in balance.

#19.4In practice: a transition

No one is asked to drop everything as the teacher did (she had constant guidance through it, and it was very hard). If you're the main provider, charge, pay your bills, and then set aside one or two days to help people, just because, so you begin accumulating the frequency of giving. Without the action, you won't see it, because you never projected it.

Use the monetary structure as a step toward what you love: take work that pays while you transition. And don't see professional help as negative: a good lawyer, for instance, knows the system and can guide you; take it as positive, and the reality shifts (see S2 14.3).

#19.5Why this keeps coming up

Because the councils see how often people, including light workers, separate from the heart as they become popular: prices rise, VIP tiers appear, and fame and greed take over what they originally came to do. Some even find they can no longer download information (see S2 12.7). "You value yourself the most when you give freely", and the repetition is for anyone who needs it.

#19.6Free will, and the tasks you must complete

"Free will is a factor, to a point." Moment to moment, you choose your thoughts and emotions, and your fractal imprints change accordingly. But if there is a bylaw, something you came to complete, it is inevitable. If you go off track, your guidance places synchronicities or imprinted situations into your hologram (you meet someone, someone says something, something "smacks you in the side") until you return to the right location. "It's always going to get completed… they won't give up on you" (see S2 10.1).

#19.7Ultimate focus: the instructions for complete manifestation

Instructions the teacher was given for a full manifestation: for a period, consume nothing except what relates to what you want (no social media, no other books or films). Want a garden? Watch only gardening, non-stop. Detox and eat cleanly at the same time, to raise the molecular structure. Check yourself every half hour. This creates a steady vibration that keeps building the next hologram at the same frequency. It's very challenging, because we were taught the opposite: it takes "absolute control of the mind".

How long? Unknown: it depends on desire, emotion and the person (the teacher's own giving took two years before it became "like an avalanche"). But the more you practise and stay in the heart, the quicker it gets, and as you see results, fear falls away because "you literally know". A film about a man who meditated his way into another time shows the same principle.

#19.8Mistakes are part of it

Clearing toxic thinking doesn't always feel heartful, and that can frighten you. "Don't worry if you make errors; it's normal, part of the process. As long as you keep trying, you're always helped." The teacher herself slips with her diet and goes back to it. The challenge is controlling your emotion to stay steady.

#19.9Grief belongs to another timeline

When grief or depression won't lift, it's because you keep reprinting the emotional vibration of a past hologram. The loss happened in a different timeline of Earth, to another version of you; you hold it in memory to stay connected to it. Understanding that it's not happening to you now is itself the release: "You don't need to think of that anymore; you're free" (see S1 14.5).

Support

Long-lasting grief or depression is real and treatable. Please talk to a doctor or counsellor, or a grief support group. If you ever have thoughts of ending your life, call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number.

#19.10Help without carrying the burden

Every interaction is with another version of yourself, so you may help someone in hardship (in prison, say), but don't carry their vibration: "you cannot carry the burden of illusion." Be there for assistance, without depleting yourself. If you keep repeating yourself to someone who doesn't want to hear, you're trying to overtake their belief system. Tell your truth; the right people come, according to your vibration.

#19.11Stay neutral; don't target individuals

Thoughts come all the time; what matters is how you meet them. The teacher once named a specific public figure publicly and, the next day, felt a strong telepathic attack. She stayed in neutrality, observing without fear or judgment (even that consciousness chose its path by free will), apologized, and promised not to name individuals. "You stay in your structure; we stay in ours." At that level, keep the balance, and express love: negative structure cannot sustain itself in a loving vibration.

#19.12Animals, insects and the vibration you carry

Insects (and bees) levitate on magnetic fields and read your frequency: they retaliate when you project fear, and won't attack someone resonating above the ego. Any animal recognizes the frequency of the heart. Eating meat accumulates pain frequency ("don't say you love animals when you eat them"), so transition, step by step (see S2 11.6). Bees keep the resonance around the flowers; their loss reflects a vibration change they couldn't hold, "but the new Earth has plenty of them." And you don't need to spin your body to raise its frequency: the heart, action and diet do that.

#The method — Begin Giving Freely

  1. Keep paying your bills as you do now; nothing is abandoned.
  2. Choose a day or two each week (or a few hours) to help someone with what you know, for free.
  3. Give in joy, expecting nothing; no price, no hint of obligation.
  4. Leave it open: if anyone wishes to support you, let it be their free choice, and thank every gift.
  5. Support others whose work you value, when you can.
  6. Stay neutral about money: don't reject it, and don't attach to it.
  7. Keep going, moment to moment; the heart frequency accumulates and returns.

Families of autistic children deserve support too. A paediatrician or neurologist can assess for seizures, and autism family organizations offer practical help and community.

"We don't have to die" to shift timelines; the body was designed to mutate and shift physically (a claim; see S2 14.9).

"What do you think you were made from?" It's part of creation and evolution, all part of God consciousness, as long as it isn't used for destruction. What matters in the end is how it's used.
