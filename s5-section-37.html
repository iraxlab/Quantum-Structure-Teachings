The big idea

The weekly codes point you to a location, like arranging to meet in front of a supermarket; what you create there is your free will. Contact with positive extraterrestrials requires that your trinity (mind, heart and daily action) hold a frequency able to sustain their reality: today only about 30% of the collective does. Doubt and fear are "weakness" that can't vibrate with them; blaming the messenger when experiences stop is projecting your mirror image. Know 100% that they exist, build potency, practise lucid dreaming, and contact grows back and forth, longer each time, until you can stay: "that is the ascension".

#Key teachings

#37.1Dropped in through a hyperspace hologram

Organic intelligence can place a being in a state and send sequences holding a hologram and a matrix; knowing the exact coordinates of a pulsation, it projects that hologram into the being's consciousness, which creates a reality inside it. That is how she says she was dropped in (not med beds): realities within realities running at once. She teaches the quantum structure because it can be proven by mathematics and metaphysics, while personal ET lineage cannot yet be shown physically; any channeller may be accessing a different timeline.

#37.2A location, not your reality

The codes are coordinates in star quadrants: "Hey Joe, let's meet in front of the supermarket." Reading them seven times builds that sound-wave location in your synapses; what you create there depends on how you think, feel and act. To shift timelines you must raise frequency through repeated positive collapses, through fluctuation, clearing karma, until the positive is more potent and permanent. When someone's visions stopped after the codes and she blamed Marina, the hybrids explained: "How can you possibly think it's me when you are projecting even me in the first place?"

The message: 30% and weakness

"In every hologram of the collective human now is less than 30% connection": only about 30% hold "the proper frequency for some connection with us. The rest still have to awaken more and learn how it works... and to have 100% understanding and acceptance of our existence. Only then it will be possible to access our reality hologram." "For now, [it] is only certain individuals, and even they have challenges popping in and out... because they cannot sustain 100% balance in the mind and the body and the consciousness behavior." "What we do not like and want to be a part of is your weakness. You must step into your power... Show your power to us." "Weakness, fear... will never be a part of our reality, because the vibration is too weak and low." "Have some faith and keep pushing even if [it] does not make sense."

#37.3Why contact stops, and how to hold it

If your frequency is too low, they can't appear, and if they did you might shake, lose your voice, feel ill (as she did as a child near a boy who saw ETs; she was told she needed around 78–87%). Reasons vary: diet and body (hers), behaviour (being unkind), fear at first sight. To hold their reality you must create them: know 100% in your heart they exist (leave how and when alone), build potency by tuning in constantly, and practise lucid dreaming, automatic writing or telepathy, whichever comes easiest. She was twice put out of a craft ("you're not allowed to be here") before she could hold it.

The message: contact back and forth

You connect with them, return to Earth; connect again, stay longer; return; and again, "until you are able to hold the vibrational sequence longer and longer... and then you're ready to let go of the planet Earth permanently and stay with them. And that is the ascension." "We don't have to die in order to have experience": death is the decay of cells imposed on us; we were meant to evolve and shift. Your family continues to project their own version of you: one never sees you again, another sees you daily.

Her instructions: the code

Begin at Zion, the zero point: neutrality, "nothing comes in, nothing comes out". Then read seven times in a row:

"13714421 Levitron. Axis, hologram, quadrant 3783.3783.33008473, and connect with 733-843-444, looping, and everyone that is ready to have dream reality with us. Then collect the data to see how much percentage is in the collective."

It also gathers data on how ready the collective is; no one can tell you when contact will come.

#Questions answered

What does "create it to see it" mean? Just being positive?
No: it is the whole quantum structure. Everything comes from the one particle, you, expanding into the collective: thought becomes a blueprint around you, emotion activates it, action materializes it, and it appears like a screen in front of you. "If you want to put lipstick on, will you put it on the mirror image or on you?" Those who feed you images through the screen know you are a master creator and use it; tune into the positive, and use the codes to switch the program. Goodness is not weakness: say "no more" to people who use you; give your knowledge to those who deserve it.
If we project everything, why do we need contracts?
The full law of creation will be taught later. Free will is the law of creation; entities learned to manipulate it with lies (predictive programming in shows), so karmas were created, and contracts keep balance: what to complete, learn and experience, like choosing a virtual-reality game to learn a skill. "An eye for an eye" loops you in the same hologram; face the dark, understand it and mutate it, and you create no karma. Consciousness says this will never be allowed to go out of balance again.
Can people who don't believe hinder our experience?
They are just dropping into your hologram. Override it: even when you see they don't, keep affirming "I know humanity gets it now", over and over, until that belief is more potent and reprints permanently. Use the galactic meditation's method; the old image is strong, so it takes practice.
