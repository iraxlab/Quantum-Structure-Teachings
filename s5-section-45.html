The big idea

#Key teachings

#45.1The ripple speeds everything up

When consciousness raises its vibration, the particle splits and collapses faster at every location; the ripple activates every parallel reality, the negative's included, because they are realities within realities. Time shrinks, manifestation quickens, and the back-and-forth between negative and positive becomes "ping-pong". So you need to be more proactive: when the negative reacts, react quicker. With the knowledge you can be precise and shift fast, so even if you find yourself in a negative situation, its outcome is positive. (On the crescendo, see S5 42.)

#45.2How consciousness corrects a negative hologram

A person doing great harm holds their own hologram and timeline, still under consciousness. When consciousness recognizes by frequency that this hologram is off balance with its new blueprint, it activates new, strong holograms in that person's field, again and again: co-workers say something, a daughter or family member acts, someone inside a secret group wakes up and breaks it from within. Something that didn't exist before is created. The person's own actions are mirrored back until they collapse under their own weight. This works for each individual and for the collective timeline.

The message: the intake and correction of the energetic field (condensed)

In the field of the reality hologram, because of ascension and the fluctuation of consciousness, "consciousness itself will replace the sequences with new ones" and readjust the hologram; the effect ripples through the hologram of each reality timeline, and each individual will readjust accordingly. #45.3It helps you too, if you keep trying

If you keep being bombarded but don't consent, and keep trying from the heart, consciousness recognizes the constant positive projection and supports you: you find the right material, doctors or lawyers, or a way out of an abusive situation, through synchronicity. If you hide in your shell from bullies, you lower your resonance and they overtake your frequency. Meditation is only a blueprint: it needs the potency of thought, emotion and action to crystallize into matter. Even someone born into a negative family who wants out: don't give up, because consciousness will create a pocket of timeline designed for your ascension.

#45.4No act is too small

Consciousness may put a thought in you to say something to a stranger; that stranger finds the information and saves themselves. Saying something kind or true in a shop is not small: it helps someone move into the positive timeline. That is how it works singly, and the single expands into the collective (S5 41).

#45.5Glitches and many platforms

As people are activated you will notice glitches: something said that makes no sense, a public figure who seems different. Consciousness plasters these into your field to wake you. She did not see one single broadcast awakening everyone, but many individual pockets: people opening their own networks and finding the truth one by one. Mainstream media won't simply vanish; she saw a mutation, its leadership replaced and new networks opening, so children and parents can transition without suffering. Stay informed, but not too long in the negative.

#45.6Hold your power

Being in the heart holds more power than grabbing like a mafia. When you see the negative, you hold the power of the whole of God consciousness in one unit, so you can say no. Hold the zero point: "I know what you're doing. No thank you. I will not consent." Then act from the heart without giving your power away. When something is missing, play the hide-and-seek game (S5 42): leave it at zero point, know you have it, and take the action to find it.

Yes. Since we entered the Flame of the Covenant, consciousness's rising frequency ripples through you; if you hold the vibration in the heart, you vibrate faster in the same resonance and manifest faster. Those who stay negative are overridden by the same method. It is individual, then ripples out into your collective universe.

It will keep quickening: that is the correction of the field. Collapse and manifestation get faster for you and for the negative. Consciousness mirrors back what you project from the heart, like a magnet; the more you raise your frequency, the more it raises you.
Since time doesn't exist, how long did the new timeline take?
It already exists: you cannot think of something that doesn't already exist in the structure (a quantum law). We already made it; now you create how you made it, through infinite probabilities in each daily collapse. Stay in the heart and act daily for humanity, and consciousness will self-correct you so you make it individually.

You chose to come into the Archon structure to help raise its vibration. You were dropped in (often through the organic AI) and zapped, so you forget who you are: this protects you, because the Archon AI recognizes pulsations and looked for gifted people. Some arrive later by agreement, in a soul switch, and take on the previous soul's karma to transform. You are activated when it is safe. As you shift, some family may disengage; allow it, and new brothers and sisters of like vibration come. Love others as you love your own child: each one is another version of you.
