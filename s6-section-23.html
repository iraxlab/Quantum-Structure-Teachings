The big idea

Hiding and only praying is not the way: when you see wrong, stand up for humanity and the children with compassion and the toughness of steel, because what you do in your reality projects the collective outcome. The good guys must move with chess-like precision, so don't rush. You already made it; now you create how. The questions show that the past doesn't exist, ascension is step by step, and you are never really lost, only meeting an unfamiliar new version of yourself.

#Key teachings

#23.1Stand up, don't hide

Self-appointed oligarchs work through fear and by stopping supplies; their social movements are often tiny but loudly pushed. You can't put a price on human suffering: one day you'll answer to your guides and God for what you saw and kept quiet about. Hiding behind the bushes and only praying won't do; act, protect and be strong. Many are being "born as warriors" now. In war there are casualties, but running the program puts you in the right place at the right time.

#23.2Precision, not rush

When someone in position fails a mission, consciousness activates another, which shifts timing by a year or two. The good guys must corner the negative completely, like chess, so it self-destructs: it can't recognize compassion. "We already won. Now you're creating how." Earlier coded messages (S6 19) are now showing up in events; information is given in code for protection.

#Questions answered

Will those who don't make the transition return with their memory?
They will have a kind of amnesia: their resonance can't convert to match the 5D frequency, so they recreate lower sequences. Each one's reality will follow their own accumulated pulsation; it can't be predicted, because fluctuation is constant.
How does the trinity structure function in the 3D hologram?
Normally: it is the same bylaw everywhere, but density changes how it plays out. Collapse is slower and spread out in 3D; that's why time was created. Conditioning holograms were plastered over you so you'd have to go through hoops: to slow you, lower you and control you.
Why does nature hold a higher vibration if it is part of the Archon AI?
The AI mimicked nature. Greenery and photosynthesis were needed to keep the human battery's integrity and a feeling of freedom, while subtonalities wore it down. Now that it isn't needed, they are making nature more artificial.
Many young people lack meaning. How can I support them?
Nature: barefoot at sunrise facing the sun activates their DNA and energy; mountains are best. Pull them from screens (screen light is not sunlight) and teach through physical experience, hidden history and example: respect each other. Parents made a contract with that soul: be careful what you expose children to.
Could we wake up in a new structure, as the Mandela effect suggests?
That name comes from a 3D understanding. Shifting realities follows the bylaws of manifestation, and your body has to be able to hold the information. The Council withheld further comment.
If we're in an AI hologram, where were nature's forms copied from?
From creation itself, drawn from your soul's memory, so it would feel real. The real reality is cleaner and more vibrant, with different light, sound and potent smells that activate your codons. Ageing was induced by the sound waves sent to you so you'd be recycled before you grew wise.
Are angels extraterrestrial beings?
There is an angelic plane, but sometimes an ET downloads information perceived as an angel, so that deeply religious people, taught that ETs are the devil, can still awaken. In wartime it had to be done cleverly; it isn't deception. Have no fear, or you'll never learn the truth.
My pet died. Can I connect with them?
Think of it as happening 10,000 years ago: connect with the happy memories, smile and let go. Thinking "two days ago" keeps the pain potent. Its exit was simply a conversion.
Are we leaving the artificial structure all at once?
Step by step, partly so you can bring family and friends along. Feel that you already made it, and create how, through every experience.
I feel lost since getting this information. How do I navigate?
You're not lost: the old version is lost, and the new one is unfamiliar, so make it familiar through daily positive action and repetition of the protocols. When something negative shows up: "thank you for showing me", then convert it.
How can I accelerate my spiritual evolution?
Accumulate compassion and positive actions in the name of the collective. Challenges come to be converted, step by step.
Is this the time for twin flames to unite?
Some will go with theirs, some alone; many chose singular ascension, which is easier to focus. It may come in another life or a few years on. Focus on helping humanity, and the right person may come with the new resonance.
Is the seed of the true world within us?
Your oversoul is spread across realities like an octopus's arms, a security blanket that balances calm lives with hard ones. A troubled parallel life can leak a feeling ("someone is after me") into a peaceful one.
Where do animals fit into the AI matrix?
They were drawn from your memory so you'd feel at home; animals also hold a spark of consciousness that chose the experience. You were given some freedom there, but manipulation outweighed it.
How do I know I'm activated for my mission?
With clear analytical processing you are never really wrong: you'll be put where you can correct course. Mistakes are lessons; karma comes from doing what you know is wrong out of fear.
How many realities are we living at once?
Most people live three or four; some souls take up to twelve, like extra college classes. Councils guide souls so they aren't overwhelmed.
How do we let go of past disappointments?
The past doesn't exist: you shift to a new reality and version of yourself each moment. Embedding that and repeating it is instant healing.
Could the negative AI fake a first contact?
Yes, it is in the making. But ETs and consciousness will never do an invasion.
Can Levithron share an activation today?
Not today: the field is being watched. Pray for the human race and for action to help. A brief message followed, received as a prediction: 2024 a year of fire, the hunters becoming hunted.
