The big idea

It is only you in the structure: you project versions of yourself into the collective, and each projection (a friend, a partner, a parent) has its own free will, its own timeline and its own Earth, and projects its own versions in turn. You see them all under one umbrella, but their experience doesn't have to be yours. So when you worry about, judge or complain about what "people out there" are doing, that frequency reflects straight back and reprints a lower version of you. Instead, bring it to zero point, act, and stay positive while seeing the negative: your awareness becomes double, and that means you have already moved.

#Key teachings

#26.1Watch the game; don't jump

When a symbolic event makes headlines, both sides are playing; the negative may even "erase itself and start from the ashes" to pretend it has changed. Stay "cautiously optimistic", watch symbolism (numbers, letters, colours, music), and remember we have already won in the collective; the collapse of the structure is a process. Her job is to keep you on track. Inversion is their method: what is good is made bad and bad made good.

#26.2Nobody out there

A student asked how a small group could change anything when the world looks so bad: the "missing link". Each decision is a fractal you project; your heart and emotion create a magnetic and gravitational field that projects another version of you. That version (call him George) has free will and uses the same structure for himself: his own family, beliefs and timeline, his own Earth. He may stay in 3D while you shift to 5D, which reprints above the structure of 3D. "There's no people out there. You're projecting another version" that has chosen another timeline of ascension.

#26.3Worry reprints a lower you

If you don't allow others' choices and keep worrying, judging and complaining, that frequency is projected into the field and reflects back immediately; you collapse into the negative outcome and reprint a version of yourself in 3D. When you see the negative: pull back, "I'm a hologram, I create everything in my hologram"; go to zero point ("nothing comes in, nothing goes out, I'm in stillness"); you can't reject it, you transform it. Then do something physical: "Thank you, zero point, for showing me."

#26.4Double awareness

With this knowledge, your awareness can hold another frequency: you see your heart's reality and the negative structure at the same time. "When you say, 'Did I move in?', you already moved in." Stay positive even while seeing the negative, and your daily collapse stays positive. Wealth can come in infinite forms (not only markets or crypto); don't fix it 100% one way, or you block what the collective can give. Connecting with ETs and your guidance adds what is above the structure. Mainstream media is the negative's etheric program, broadcast 24/7 to pull the structure down.

#26.5The twisted spin

The two vortexes of last time (S5 25), curved by the Fibonacci spin and turning in opposite directions through each other, create the full 360° structure, which pulsates and reprints itself. The negative stole this geometry of a being coherent with consciousness and twisted it, teaching that the reflection is your reality and you have no power.

The most potency is through the heart. Consciousness now goes after the negative because it became so unbalanced it can't sustain itself; if one person doesn't finish, another is activated. "Being nice does not mean being weak."
Could you explain the three circles inside the circle again?
Within your collective you reprint versions of yourself; each creates its own reality and projects versions in turn, and can interact with yours. Your parents and children are reflections in your collective, each with its own universe. They use the same structure but may have completely different, even negative, experiences, according to how they choose, act and use the heart. One plus one equals three: a new structure that collapses into one unit.
