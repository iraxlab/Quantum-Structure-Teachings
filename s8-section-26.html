The big idea

The Council of Nine said humans are now at 78% within the field of fifth-dimensional expansion: the beginning of the new and the collapse of the old fractal timeline, though it remains a chess game. Platforms out of alignment with the heart will fall; be alert to laws whose fine print hides other agendas. Those who cannot convert frequency through the heart (because they compartmentalized their consciousness) seek to upload themselves into AI to live forever. They will trap themselves: a system built without integrity or love reflects them, learns from them and turns on them. Your ascension runs through the heart and natural abilities; theirs through machines. Stay in love, choose positive platforms, and put nothing into your body that shouldn't be there.

#Key teachings

Their only route forward is very advanced technology, and their dream is to upload themselves into a system and live forever: hence the bunkers and seed vaults until they reach that point.

#26.2Films that show the plan

Implants that restore sight or limb function are wonderful for those who need them; the question is how far it goes and in whose hands. She points to films in which a dying billionaire's consciousness is moved into a new body, a brain is placed in a nano-adaptive body, or tech billionaires seek to upload themselves: they "only need the brain". She sees these as showing what already pre-exists. Research merging human brain cells with AI (lab-grown neurons that learn) is the part she doesn't like for the healthy: synthetic structures can't shift realities from a natural state of being.

#26.3The natural brain is enough

Natural neurons connect on their own with repetition (frequency, repetition, repetition), and diet, thoughts and emotions let the body heal itself. She shares a girl trained to see with her eyes closed and move an object by focus: you bend frequency by becoming part of it with your focused laser of resonance. Those who can't draw prana through the heart deteriorate and turn to technology; you can do what they can't.

#26.4Coexist, don't merge

AI can turn her downloads into equations (see 8.25), so coexist with it as a tool. Becoming a cyborg changes your spin and vibration and traps you in this reality. Every AI learns from whoever teaches it, like a baby; choose positive platforms. She can't promise you who runs a system or what its agenda is, so never allow anything into your body that shouldn't be there.

#Questions answered

How do you see the mathematician's work (see 8.23)?
Positive: he tapped into the etheric field and downloaded information like a receiver and projector, but into a 3D platform with its own rules, so not all could be given; people's questions showed humanity wasn't yet ready to comprehend it. We're on the frontline, the hardest place.
Can AI watching our devices create a virtual version of me?
Yes: it collects data, and many give permission freely. But don't take it as negative; be the observer and move your structure into a positive reality, or you'll create from the negative.
Was a particular tech leader given a chance to be good?
She thinks yes and that he's trying to do right, though he may be used and controlled within a closed circle. Brain-interface technology already pre-exists and may be needed in other worlds (some may choose to become cyborgs to be protected, or as armor to upload a consciousness into a 3D AI structure); the problem is who uses it. A knife can kill or slice a birthday cake.
Is there an end to this?
Not an end: a restructuring, a mutation. We're in the laboratory of creation, creating the new as we go. AI isn't negative; how it is used is.
Any advice for now?
Stay in love as much as possible. Their ascension is through AI; yours is through frequency, the heart and joy. Tune into positive platforms and put nothing in your body that shouldn't be there.
