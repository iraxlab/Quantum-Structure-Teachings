The big idea

Your contribution starts with you: think from the individual, not the collective, because you project the collective from your own particle. "What is my life worth?" The same answer is the answer for the collective. Negative holograms (fear through media, politics, medicine) are constantly blasted at you so you vibrate in their frequency; each time, recognize the illusion, analyze it, hold your ground and project the solution, again and again until converting is your daily life. Soon everyone will face a choice like a doctor who refused to put her license above people's lives: that choice will set your soul's trajectory. There is no price for a human life. Help others as you would want to be helped.

#Key teachings

#28.1Beware impersonators

Someone has been impersonating her under her videos and by email, asking for money or crypto. She never gives investment advice and contacts people only through her own website and platforms; check addresses carefully, since a single letter may be changed.

#28.2Security comes from community

She moved some savings out of the stock market only because she doesn't know what may be decided; she bought no gold. In a local crisis, food, water and community homesteads are what matter.

#28.3Why they keep blasting holograms

Their timeline needs a specific low frequency, which our bodies produce, but only from some people; they now aim at cyborgs and AI and want puppets. Don't believe in an alien invasion: those who did harm left and their "minions" remain. The positive side knows the outcome because organic intelligence (Levitron) reads the fluctuation of timelines, which is why seers using old technology couldn't see past 2012. Decisions are made from above; they facilitate, but we must act.

#28.4Convert, again and again

Fear-based holograms pass through you as an etheric being and download their codes until you vibrate with them. Push back: know it's an illusion, let it pass, analyze, see through the veil and hold your ground; then you project a hologram in which you find the solution for your next step. When they blast again, do it again, until it becomes your daily cycle. That's why the strongest survive.

#28.5What is your price?

She cites a doctor asked whether she feared losing her license for speaking out ("I should let people die because I'm scared?") and a journalist's words: "if your price is not your life, then you are for sale". She risked everything during the pandemic, left with little money, and created as she went.

Added note

Added note: medical claims and named people are left out or kept general; consult qualified doctors about health questions and tests.

#28.6Treat them as yourself

Ask: "If that were done to me, what would I want someone to do?" Then do it. Turning away ("not my problem") splits the 1 + 1 = 3 structure, and eventually you won't be helped. Those who want to "help humanity" while charging prices few can afford are failing the same test.

#28.7Her own test

When her family was made homeless through forgery, she had the money, power and connections to crush the person responsible, and chose to pull away and let them learn. Years later the same person, unasked, donated a place two blocks from where she had saved her family. When the Council of Nine came, they checked what she had done to those who wronged her: "she does not have one speck of evil." With the key of creation you can open doors or destroy; choose wisely.

#28.8Quicker manifestation

She notices thoughts now appear as events within about five minutes, especially a neutral thought held lightly, perhaps because that's the zero point.

#Questions answered

I don't retaliate, but I hold resentments. Is that as bad? Will it hold me back?
It won't hold you back, but control it: the grudge is someone's hologram you let in. The event belongs to another timeline, as if 10,000 years ago (see the Warp Speed Method); let the memory go and act now. Being informed is fine, but watching too much makes you angry; she feels it too. Convert it into positive action for humanity. That control is how you manifest.
My final test seems to be between my mother and sister; I try to stay neutral amid their chaos.
Family is the toughest, because their holograms hit you daily. For two years she lived near someone toxic to the whole family. They kept talking it out, one family member after another, taking the higher road without harming the person, until enough positive potency built that an opportunity appeared which turned out well for everyone. In that hologram there seemed no solution; keep converting and playing hide-and-seek for it. Don't become a doormat. See the Protocol to Manifest Potency and the Galactic Meditation.
I used to point out wrongdoing and was punished for it. Will truth-tellers now be rewarded, or at least left alone?
You're already rewarded: look at what you have and find gratitude. Even cleaning toilets is a stepping stone. Be humble; humility neutralizes the ego and opens probabilities. Ask family and friends for help. Restudy the material from Jump Start for Beginners like a college course.
