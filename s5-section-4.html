The big idea

To contact your galactic family or pass through portals, your body's cells must vibrate above the 3D structure: more conductive, more silica, more crystalline. Meditation alone won't do it; the body changes by physical action, slowly, so you don't "pop out" of the timeline. Detox and live green juice raise the cells' frequency; water then conducts the new frequency to every cell, and the body "becomes lighter and shines like a star." A crystalline body forms etheric geometry that shifts with each thought: that's how your consciousness opens stargates. It needs consistency, and the balance of love and strength.

#Key teachings

#4.1Why the body must change

The hybrids are the timeline that went into technology, lost reproduction, and joined with the Pleiadians to help wake humanity now (see S5 1). Because every reality is under consciousness, you are already on the spacecraft in parallel (see S4 46). To connect with it, telepathically or physically, "the body that you have in the 3D has to raise the vibration": its cells must become more conductive in a higher resonance, through frequency and sound. First know it is possible and 100% true; then work on the body. She said she achieved everything they asked except physical contact, because she broke her diet: the molecular structure must hold the frequency.

Try different diets if you wish, but "to become completely transformed requires a steady signature of behavior [and] actions", equal to the shifts; only then "you let go of the previous attachments." "Control your body and mind and structure in positive actions at all times through your heart."

"It is you projecting even us now. That means that you are ready, but what is needed is action from the heart and consistency. Show what you are made of." "The balance is between love and strength... like yin and yang": "beautiful in the heart, very loving and creative, but at the same time... incredible power to withhold the changes. To be strong as steel."

Her instructions: detox, water and juice

Do the detox protocol (see the Detox Guide and her detox videos) only with doctor supervision: blood work to check you aren't allergic to anything, eliminating what you are, and monitoring "every month" how your blood works. Find an open-minded doctor, for example through private membership associations.

Distilled water: she was told it "can deplete the nutrients", which is why it needs supervision. She first did about a year of the protocol with green juicing only, which raised her cells' frequency; then distilled water, to gain conductivity, carried the new frequency.

Juice, not powder: powder has less frequency; it "does not hold the vital frequency vibration that is a live structure." "The best way to do it is the most work. You have to really do juicing."

Probiotic: the original VSL#3 formula moved to the company Visbiome; she used it for her son, "with distilled water in the morning on an empty stomach." Her detox videos cover nearly a year of elimination diet, every two weeks; a raw diet for diabetics; and a frequency-healing-through-sound video ("the first hour"), "but do not do it for a long time, because the body fluctuates in vibration."

Added safety note

Distilled water, long detoxes and restrictive diets can cause deficiencies and are risky with diabetes and other conditions. As she insists, do them only with a doctor and regular blood work.

#4.2Changes from the ground up

The Council had said the country would be taken back "from the ground up": working people acting for freedom. Prepare food, not from fear, "in every battle you have to be prepared." Most people don't know the law, because the Constitution stopped being taught; she shared one parent's story of using a public official's surety bond to challenge school rules. "Everything is in your hands": she took her children out of school (unschooling), built their own programme from books, including manners, and asked parents for tough love: "to make a change, you have to be the change."

Added note

The surety-bond method came from a video she shared, not from her guidance; check any legal step with a qualified lawyer.

#Questions answered

Is taking action what physics calls collapsing the wave function?
She learned it from the Pleiadians and the Council, not from physics: each reality is one unit; moment to moment, by thought, emotion and deed, you fluctuate and collapse positive or negative. The end result depends on how many times a day you choose the heart; sometimes you must decide in seconds. Each person acting in the heart adds to the collective's potency. Live "a Marina day": do what you feel like, moment to moment, with joy (see S4 29).
If the negative destroys itself, how will we grow without negative influences?
There will always be balance, but as challenges to overcome and learn from, not horror like this. Even the ETs didn't expect it to get so bad, and it was a lesson for them.
Do we go somewhere else through the stargate, or does this Earth become 5D?
"Quantum manifestation 101": every reprint is a whole new Earth, one unit; "nothing else exists except this particular structure." Consciousness already made it in 5D: embed its frequency with thought, heart and action, and you reprint your own ascension. "You're not changing anyone; you're changing your structure from within."
Do negative forces exist in other universes?
Yes, for balance, but the positive always tips slightly more, so consciousness never self-destructs (see S5 1).
In a dense area, can I rise above it without relocating?
You may; and if your contract requires you there, accept it, without fear, until the lesson is learned. But if the collective around you is brainwashed, she would move: it gets harder the longer you wait, as the guards hold more potency and the collective changes around you.
