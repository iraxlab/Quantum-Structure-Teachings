#The method — converting a phobia or overwhelming emotion

  1. Understand it: a phobia is often a resonance carried from a traumatic experience in another lifetime or timeline; it reconstructs itself in your new life.
  2. Recognize it as it comes: pull back and stop. "Thank you for showing me this resonance. I understand it."
  3. Do something completely different, at once: watch a funny film, go outside, walk, exercise, punch a bag. The new sound wave must be more potent than the old one.
  4. Repeat every time: the same or similar action each time, until it mutates and is overwritten.
  5. Notice your decisions change: once the resonance subsides, you decide differently, and your timeline changes.

This is mutation, not rejection. It does not apply to substance use or verbal or physical abuse: then stop the process physically, at once.
