The big idea

Different teachers can present different truths because each is the truth of a parallel reality running at the same time; when you collapse them, you create your own result. So don't point fingers: stick with the quantum structure and let synchronicity reveal any manipulation. A soul can switch with another, or a higher self can activate a lower version of itself, to preserve humanity. To recall your higher self you must gather all your experiences into one coherent resonance through the heart, nearly 100% synchronized; only then does a portal open. You can download only from the levels your frequency matches. Negative entities get in only when you keep tuning into negative signals. A positive guide never teaches you to lie or manipulate.

#Key teachings

#9.1Two truths, two parallel realities

When people hear different stories from different sources it creates confusion, which is dangerous for ascension. Two people's truths can be the truths of two parallel realities running simultaneously under consciousness; when you collapse them, you create a new result from your own perception. So be the driver, not the passenger: stick with the quantum structure.

#9.2Never comment on others' material

She never publicly speaks about someone else's information when she isn't an expert in it. If someone is manipulating, consciousness will push the truth in front of you through synchronicity (a video, a comparison) because a lie accumulates until consciousness rejects it in a visible event. Popularity and fame aren't the criteria for ascension; integrity, love and the heart structure are.

#9.3Soul switches and activated higher selves

To preserve itself during ascension, consciousness clusters holographic imagery to mutate what's off balance. A person (even a politician) exists in multiple realities; by permission, their higher self may activate and suddenly act very differently for humanity. Or another soul may come in and switch, as happened with her. There are organic ways and technological ones (think of the film Avatar). In the laboratory of creation (see 6.48) everything is possible, including a positive hyperspace hologram, like a virtual-reality game.

#9.4The etheric legions

Don't worry if you're a good person who doesn't know the quantum structure well. If your end vibration can't move to the next level, after passing you join the etheric grid, like the loved ones mediums speak with, and assist everyone moving through the 5D structure while continuing your own ascension. Only the most negative, which can't be restructured, returns to source.

#9.5You download only what you match

Your pulsation ends up in a particular location in consciousness. Like a phone call, you may reach 7th-dimensional information but not 11th, and couldn't comprehend it anyway. The heart measures what you converted, and that frequency opens portals and stargates (the way you could shift or bilocate).

The message: switching souls and the collective portals (condensed)

"To recall your soul of higher dimension, your lower aspects... must have been on the level of the proper resonance." The heart holds the keys. "The soul itself must have accumulated all the experiences and combine them in one collective self-coherent resonance", combining all subtonalities into one sound wave (see HR). "To restore the new realities, the soul has to be very potent in its own vibration" (see the Protocol to Manifest Potency). At almost 100% synchronized coherence, "the individual soul can construct and open new timeline interdimensional portal": one timeline completes and you are permanently coherent, no longer fluctuating. Each person is a note in the symphony of the 5th dimension. Higher souls can lower their sequence to enter matter; from below, we gather it through challenges. If you build negatively with artificial structures, "consciousness will use another structure with technology to stop" them, "because you cannot destroy something negative that's so potent... just by praying".

#9.6How negative entities get in

Only by constantly doing negative things or tuning into negative platforms (see "What Is Predictive Programming"). Repetition shuts down your analytical process; then you summon them by tuning into their radio signal, and others can enter. Stay positive and everything that tries to manipulate you comes into the open: dark to light.

#9.7Positive or negative guide?

Judge by synchronicities and outcomes after you follow the advice. A negative entity will teach you to manipulate, lie and deceive; a positive one never will. "Positive will teach you to be strong, compassionate, and trustworthy", and will put you back on track, sometimes through tough lessons.

#9.8Allow other information in

Thinking only you know the truth is ego. No one holds the whole truth; it is everyone together. Listen, analyze and grow wiser from others' views and mistakes. You are free: like the cow that stays by the chair after its chain is removed, the chain is off, so walk away and create.

#Questions answered

If we switch places with our higher self, will we know?
Most likely not; it's very subtle. You may wake up feeling like a new person, or dream you're a warrior and feel you know what to do. Your simultaneous realities (an ET, a Roman soldier) hold strengths you've forgotten, and at ascension you download them into the now. It can be your own soul or a switch, by agreement.
If we create a different life, what happens to our old interactions?
All your lives run simultaneously, like TV channels; the remote is your consciousness, your thoughts and feelings. With higher abilities you can tune into and re-experience a timeline. Since everything already exists, act as if you've already made it: the next moment shifts to that vibration. When you raise your knowledge and field, your higher self and helpers on that timeline can carry you, without overtaking your evolution.
