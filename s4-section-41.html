Activation of Human Contracts / C9

Consciousness as a living being that heals itself; the councils as its "supreme court"; the Council of Nine's message on how human contracts are activated, tested and changed; how contact really happens; and questions on peers' ridicule and the oversoul (live talk, October 2021; it ended with the geometric meditation of S4 38)

The big idea

Your soul is "energy of accumulative vibration"; your oversoul carries the signature of everything you have lived. The councils read that signature to place you where you can best serve, and write your contract: where you're born, your task, when help comes, when the program ends. But it is quantum: the contract is also being made right now. Your actions, thoughts and deeds are watched; do well and more is revealed and given; pull back in fear and your reality shifts toward exit. Contracts can change, but only through many layers. "Never think that you are weak."

#Key teachings

The outage. Asked about a simultaneous shutdown of three major social platforms, the Council said it was done "to implement back doors globally", so all systems "can be seen from a third party, so you have no privacy"; "not to worry, it will be restored", and the positive side "collected all the data... We have everything." It is a chess game, back and forth; "the battle has begun, stay strong." (She added that crypto can't be controlled, because of the blockchain; the system "with no name" will come when it's time, see S4 40.)

#41.1Consciousness heals itself

Consciousness is "a living being", self-knowing, with its own resonance, always wanting to expand. Within it run infinite realities, each a universe. When a negative structure grows "like a cancer cell", consciousness recognizes it and, to preserve its balance, creates more positive structures to attack and mutate it; if one doesn't work, it creates another, immediately. "You can never be able to overtake consciousness itself because you are part of it." That is what you do when you hold the vibration. Within consciousness, the councils are like red cells, a supreme court that protects and decides; extraterrestrial realities are overseen by them, and work within contracts too.

The message: how we activate the human contracts

"Look at the soul as energy of accumulative vibration." Your oversoul has gathered all your lifetimes and experiences into one resonance, like a symphony, and splits into many timelines. The councils look at that signature to see where your oversoul can split and fulfil a mission, and decide where you are born, what you do, and when help comes; it's in your contract, "which is sacred". Then "the soul is recognized to what it may possibly do to increase the frequency in a specific location and packet of the consciousness."

It is decided "not only in the future, but... right now": your actions, thoughts and deeds are watched and analyzed. "If you do so well... they're going to look at your actions, performance, behavior, strength and weaknesses", and more activation and tasks follow. You will be tested: a situation is presented, often shocking, to see what you would really do from your heart. If you are self-centred and don't act when you see another suffer, they back off, and someone else, or another version, is created to complete it.

Contact is decided by the frequency you accumulate, and comes in the form you can hold: for one person extraterrestrials, for another "an angel". Contracts can change, but it must pass through many layers (your consent, the extraterrestrials, parallel realities, the councils), so it is very difficult. If fear makes you not complete, you're given the choice to exit: that probability closes and your reality shifts to a different timeline. If you do well, you are "placed into consciousness packets", stabilized in the positive.

"No one is saying that when you are a very strong soul you will have it easy." All who came now are very advanced souls who forgot who they are. Recognize the negative, then hold the vibration with more potency than it has, to mutate dark to light. "Never ever think that you are weak... Stay strong, be the warrior that you are."

#41.2What she learned about contact

Trust in her guides was built when their advice proved right and ignoring it brought trouble. They often won't explain someone else's behaviour, so as not to overtake that person's consciousness; the truth shows through synchronicity later. When she begged them to change things for her ill son, they said "accept it, it was a lesson", yet insisted she bring her mother, who pushed for the hospital in time. Her first meeting with the Council came in a lucid dream: asked to do a task, she consented and found herself on a craft with a group who acted by "quantum telepathy", keeping their emotion perfectly steady so a hostile being couldn't sense them. The lesson: many contacts happen in a lucid state you can hold; taken physically, "you're going to lose it", unless technology holds your vibration. Many people have contacts and don't remember.

#Questions answered

How can we overcome the ridicule of peers when we speak of the future we know is here?
"That's an illusion": there are no others, only versions of you. Project the knowledge and let it be; the seed is planted. As you shift, you project versions of them who begin to grasp what you said, in a different timeline (see S1).
How does our higher self or oversoul relate to the other versions of us we meet each day?
It fluctuates. When you open to channeling, information is downloaded from other versions of your oversoul, if you are ready and if it may be given to the planet now; not everything you seek will be given.

Then the geometric meditation (see S4 38), now set to a video with her voice: hold the image of a free Earth, increase the feeling seven times, open the stargate. "Have a Marina day" (see S4 29).

Left out: greetings, products she recommended; political commentary.
