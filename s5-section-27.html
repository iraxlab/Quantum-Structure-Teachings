#Questions answered

Why is this universal law being given now?
It was the right time, because she reached the level of understanding and resonance to download it (see 27.2).
Can one universal law override another, e.g. free will?
Yes, through councils (details to come). From her experience with bylaws: you write a script before birth (parents, a spouse); within it you have free will. If you skip a meeting meant to be, synchronicity brings it back; refuse it and it may carry into a next life. After a life you sit with your councils "like in front of a court", review it, and a bylaw can be changed ("she really did learn... let's change the bylaw"). Completion is a zero point: you graduate with honours and are given a new task. Councils oversee ETs too; your higher self, above 3D, takes part.
Are these the same as the 12 universal laws in other books?
They will go much deeper, into the quantum structure under each sentence. She hasn't read other books on purpose, to keep her downloads original.
How do sound and colour relate to our reality? Can we project through them?
Yes. You project everything into the collective and collapse back; each shift changes location, colour, sound and charge, merging, like brain activity seen as flickering colours. In each moment you are the sound and the colours; each moment is a universe.
How should we use the energy of crop circles?
Just see them: your consciousness recognizes the geometry, which has location, numbers and sound, and activates. Images carry sound and subliminal messages too; potency depends on repetition. That is how predictive programming works: a repeated word becomes an image, a sound wave, a magnetic potency, and then matter in the collective. Be aware and you can shut that program down and create your own.
Is clearing our traumas important for shifting?
Very: transform the dense frequency so you resonate with the new one. Things don't change for some because they don't know how to manifest, or they ignored the signs and complied; correcting later takes more time and effort, and for some it may not be reversible (see S4 38).
