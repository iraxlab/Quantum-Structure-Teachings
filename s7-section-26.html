The big idea

She was shown, after reading translations of Bronze Age texts, how degraded some ancient practices were (mass intoxication and rituals using bodily fluids and venoms), and was told to wait about three more years before sharing more, because many aren't ready. What troubles her is a new twist circulating widely: that God is actually the devil. None of those texts speak of the fabric of consciousness (frequency, sound, how creation works), so that claim is a deception designed to divide people by religion and take away their faith. Her guides' answer to the past: "so what?" There is no time; learn what not to do, respect each other's beliefs, unite for humanity and stay with the quantum structure.

#Key teachings

#26.1Held back for now

She chops up her account deliberately; she was told people will be ready to hear more of the truth in about three years. History will be rewritten, and the truth will not be easy for many to accept.

#26.2Why uncontrolled altered states are risky

She was shown widespread use of intoxicating mixtures in some ancient societies, keeping people constantly high during rituals and initiations. Opening your consciousness that way is like opening a portal in time space with no control or protection from your divinity; unlike drinking water, the body can't say "enough". Negative entities can then come in and twist behavior; some "channel" sources that teach only control and wealth.

The message: the history of deception (condensed)

"None of these ancient scriptures... talk about the fabric of consciousness." What is coming out now is "a deception of preparation and confusion for confusion". "Every soul... with a twist of personal events will discover their true origins. So trust the plan and do not kneel on your knees of the deception." "The truth is what you wish it to be... everything is at once and there is no time." "Whatever may have been, let that simply be in its own reality. Since you are not living that way, does it really matter what it was in history?" Learn from history what to do and what not to do. Those who kept such practices hidden for centuries, "wolves in sheep's clothing", now want to normalize them; they will pay for it because they cannot convert the structure. "To be able to move forward, you must let go of the past." Releasing such information carelessly could cause mass psychosis and despair, especially for devoted believers; it can also be released to deceive. "Some of the greatest names you all are worshipping were not the way you were all told." Old texts read in their original sequences can act like chants that open portals.

Added note

Added note: claims about ancient practices, religions and secret societies are given here in general terms only. Treat them as her channeled view, not established history.

#26.3Why such practices fed the dark

Behaving at the lowest level, taking another's soul without permission, lowered the timeline's vibration and fed an artificial, dark frequency (the black goo, 6.24) that needed people as conduits to project its resonance. She believes this was by design.

#26.4Rise above it

Respect others' religions and beliefs, and unite in the name of the good of humanity. Don't fall into depression or self-harm if you learn something dark about someone you revered: leave the past in the past and trust your own divine power. Pray through love and good deeds. Keep playing the game wisely (for example, keep paying taxes) until things change publicly.

#Questions answered

Do the Emerald Tablets accurately depict the real history of humanity?
There was much deception through scrolls written at different times, and ideologies were pushed for control. If you wish to believe it, let that be, but rise above it; what matters is how you move forward through the heart and the structure of the fabric.
Can channeling open you to being overtaken too?
Yes, if you end up near a very dark portal and your resonance matches it. A person in a high resonance may have a frightening moment but will be pushed back by their own charge; someone in distress or fear is easily overtaken. Be careful what you consent to and learn about places of worship you visit: sites of past sacrifice and chanting can be potent and may need specialists to close. Objects from some places may carry such rituals; praying on them can open a portal in your space.
