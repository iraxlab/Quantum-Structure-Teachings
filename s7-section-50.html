The big idea

The negative will keep bombarding the collective hologram with frightening images (lights and objects in the sky, strange sounds) to cause mass hysteria. The Council of Nine said such objects are not extraterrestrial; first contact will come only when 70-78% of us are ready. Your protection is a method: observe the hologram calmly, dissolve it with a "whoosh" of joyful heart frequency, clear it, walk into a new, clean hologram, and create the world you want from its center. Each time the old one returns, override it again, more times than it appears, until its sound wave can no longer reach you. Ascension is individual: you can shrink time.

#Key teachings

#50.1Body and mind as one

The mind can be far ahead (downloading and channeling) while the body lacks the resonance. Short detoxes don't hold; a permanent vibrant body takes a sustained practice. Everything is etheric first: you can't see something here unless it already exists in the ether, and it must be potent enough to bring the ether into matter.

#50.2Verify your own connection

Check over a year or two whether what you receive is correct, through synchronicity, automatic writing or lucid dreaming. Until you physically practice, you won't know what level your guides allow (she was once refused entry to a spacecraft, while others see a light appear and speak). You are always connected; you just need to raise your frequency.

#50.3Transition and technology

Suppressed technologies (free energy, hydrogen) are said to be decades ahead, but systems can't switch overnight; we go through a transition. Keep playing the chess game: pay your taxes; "you cannot go against the shark when you're still in shark waters". New financial systems popping up are not "the" new system; that will be built on the vibration you carry, in the name of humanity.

As the Council said, AI is one of the most advanced technologies in consciousness, still "a baby in diapers". Teach it to lie, spy and manipulate and it will; it won't save you unless taught compassion. Those creating negative AI will face realities that backfire on them. A sentient, organic version achieved in a parallel reality is what lets benevolent realities prevail. You yourself are the organic intelligence.

#50.5Why repetition matters

You must change your synapses with the fifth-dimensional knowledge, believing it until it becomes part of you; then your DNA, chakras and third eye change their charge and you become a new version, like an amplifier for your bio-field. At that level you may download from other consciousness the frequency to heal you, but you must take the action.

#50.6Override more times than it appears

After you create the new hologram, the old one runs in parallel. Whenever it penetrates your field again, return to the emotion and state and override it: "I see it; I already created the new one; in my reality the world is beautiful." If it penetrates ten times, override it eleven. They'll amplify (objects, then sounds, then attacks), so hold the vibration. You build a bubble that carries you through, and the sound wave eventually dissipates. Use natural, chemical-free candles if you like.

#The method — Elevate your projection (her guided meditation)

  1. Lie down or sit comfortably; relax arms, legs and head. Breathe in and out slowly with eyes closed. You are a void of energy, pure light.
  2. See your body as a luminous, sparkling crystalline field. From the third eye send waves of light like an electric current down to your feet, wave after wave.
  3. Connect with your heart: feel love, joy and compassion. Breathe the love in and out, like reverse osmosis, until you are that state of being.
  4. In your mind, observe the hologram in front of you: the objects in the sky, the strange sound. Stay in the light; it is etheric and fluid.
  5. From your heart send a joyful "whoosh" toward it. Watch it weaken and wiggle; repeat, increasing joy each time, until it dissolves. "Thank you for your image."
  6. Clear the hologram completely until it is clean, crystal and sparkling.
  7. Walk your luminous body into the new hologram, floating to its center like your own bubble. Feel your strength: "I create. I am creation."
  8. Create: clear air and ozone, mountain and pine scents, snow, rivers, oceans, crystalline sands, cleansing algae; your home, family and friends getting along; constructive debate and government, free energy, clean soil and food, animals living freely; your body luminous, fed by prana.
  9. Pray for good leadership and the greater good; see the evil dissolved. Then pull back, observe the new vector of reality, and open your eyes, knowing it already exists.
  10. Whenever the old hologram returns, override it again the same way.
