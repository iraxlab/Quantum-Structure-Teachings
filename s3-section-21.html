Freedom, Sovereignty and Spiritual Guidance

The big idea

Freedom "is a word that you must not take without caution": it is taken with ease, but through the heart and with power, standing on your own two feet. No one has the right to destroy a consciousness; a negative structure can only be given conditions within which it destroys itself. The ego is a false pretence plastered over your true self, and everything built from it runs in reverse. Honesty is the core of the heart structure. And no portal, stargate or level of contact opens unless your consciousness matches it, which is why the negative can never enter where the positive is going.

#Key teachings

#21.1Freedom through the heart

The freedom being taken back is freedom "to your nation, to your people, to your mind and to your soul". Take it with ease, through the heart, with power, and with gratitude for what is given to you freely: "all our knowledge has been given to you for free, without a condition". Let that free giving be an example you carry into daily life, since daily reality is built by your belief and perception, moment to moment.

#21.2No one may destroy a consciousness

Asked about the death penalty: "you have no right to destroy any consciousness; however, you can put conditions by your choosing, and it will be self-destruction." A structure that keeps choosing the negative (the inward pull) builds its own reality, which in the end destroys itself (see S3 18.2). There is no justification for killing another being.

#21.3The ego runs in reverse

Ego is "a form of reality not connected with your heart structure": a fractal of illusion plastered into your beliefs, a false pretence that inverts within the structure. It can't expand into the new realities, so you shut yourself down; every reality after that is "not truly who you are", and "you wonder why everything you wish to do turns out exactly the opposite: you used it in the opposite direction." The remedy is truth: "stay truth, speak the truth, be your truth", and feel by honesty, because "honesty is the core of your heart structure". Truth and feeling together create the outward and inward in balance.

#21.4Why channels receive only part

"Every psychic channeler is given certain information to a certain level, but never the entire picture." Some fell into greed, money and power, and a channel could be endangered by what they gather, so information is given with caution, only what's needed for the moment. "It's done not to manipulate; it's done to protect" (see S3 17.3).

#21.5A stargate matches your consciousness

There are many stargates, in many universes, each with its own signals, pulsations and colours, designed for a purpose (to enter, or to stop someone). Some are matter; some are of the ether, and only a consciousness matching their resonance through the heart can pass. "You will never go through a specific portal if your consciousness does not match it" (see S3 9.4). Most can now pass into realities of fifth density; those with galactic agreements are guided individually, through lucid dreaming, physical contact or guidance such as diet. "That is why we never worry that the negative structure can ever enter these platforms."

#21.6Others in your timeline, and repeats

Is everyone a reflection, or a real soul? Both: the soul splits into many timelines, and the version of a person in your hologram is also creating its own parallel timelines. Each time you shift you can imprint a new version of them; until the frequency you share is fully transformed, "until all of it subsides to zero in neutrality, not even one number", that person or situation may keep reappearing (see S3 19.3).

#21.7Twin flames and the balance of relationships

Twin flames are parallel versions of your own consciousness running at once; of several, one may act as a balance, for neutrality. Intimacy brings another person's resonances into your parallel realities, so life balances itself: many partners early and solitude later, or a marriage that becomes a friendship. In the ascension, many will fluctuate between being with someone and being alone, by what each most needs.

#21.8Giving love heals you

Someone sending love and healing to others found their own back pain healed. "That is correct: you are the projection in your field", and whatever you project reflects back to you as similar sequences: you healed yourself.

#The method — Finding Your Way When You Feel Lost

  1. See "lost" as a perception: the feeling of being lost gives you more of the same.
  2. Write a list of things you would love to do.
  3. Act on them in no particular order, whichever feels right at the moment.
  4. Shift back and forth between them; the movement crystallizes new realities.
  5. Affirm the state: "I am healthy, I am strong, I am free", not "I don't have…" (see S2 21.6).
  6. Notice the day you turn around and find you're happy in what you do.

Support

How can I help others while I often feel lost myself?
Be yourself, act from the heart, learn the structure; express from the heart daily even with one person, because "that one person is your entire universe". More of the same will come.
I was part of a community focused on fighting dark forces. Did something stay with me?
No comment on the group; if it feels wrong, your higher self is showing you another path: "you are now on the right path". Study the structure and create.
I'm overwhelmed by stress.
Switch through action from negative to positive: do something that makes you happy, until what you see no longer bothers you.
Will the council help my panic attacks in small spaces?
"What panic attacks?" Meditate knowing you're healthy and free; say "I am healthy, I am strong, I am free", not "I don't have panic attacks".
Added note

If panic attacks continue, a doctor or therapist can help alongside this practice.
