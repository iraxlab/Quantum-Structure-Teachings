#Questions answered

Can a soul transfer into these clones? What lifespan? Will they connect to source?
Yes, by technology like the Hyperspace Hologram (compare the film Avatar). The body and soul must learn to adapt over some years. Lifespans may reach 150–200 years, later perhaps 1,000, as cells hold their vibration from the ether instead of self-destroying; lungs will have extra crystallized structures to carry more oxygen. Children given oxygen at birth often process faster: what looks like poor focus is speed, so keep them busy with knowledge and physical activity; slower children can do brain exercises.
Will this knowledge reach cell memory? Will a diet accelerate it?
Yes: plant omega-3, vegetables, fruit, fibre; plant-based protein and calcium are suggested over animal protein ("not that you cannot eat it"); non-GMO, organic, local food from soil rich in life. Weeds with long roots are superfoods. Eat produce within about two hours of picking if you can; she suggested storing farm produce unwashed with a little of its soil on bleach-free paper in the fridge. Hold an organic seed under your tongue for a minute before sprouting it, so it carries your DNA's sequences (as in S6 19).
Why is everything going artificial? Are med beds real?
Med beds are real. Bylaw: what is created by thought, emotion and action becomes part of the quantum structure and can't be rejected, but you set boundaries (AI doing certain work, not replacing all human jobs). Don't consent to robotics in your body unless absolutely necessary; organic intelligence can help rebuild your cells instead. Fear shifts you toward a negative experience of technology; you choose.
Are there physical changes as DNA is activated?
Taller, highly intelligent, leaner and more defined, though varying with the parents' DNA.

Held back for now: the technology exists and will help save human DNA.
