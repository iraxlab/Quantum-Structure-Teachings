The big idea

This is the final move of the chess game: the negative escalates and exposes itself because it must out-potency your fight for freedom, but the end result is already won. Prayer alone isn't enough; united physical action builds the potency. An ET fleet, by collective permission, will send oxygenated particles into the atmosphere to combust toxic waste and renew the ozone in about three months, with a protective layer for the lungs. Have no fear: stand your ground like steel, with compassion even for the negative.

#Key teachings

#7.1When you find yourself in the middle of it

If you're caught in a crisis, you are there for a reason: to help (your knowledge holds a higher vibration than the panicking collective), to witness, or because your family chose the lesson; you'll be given the chance to maneuver and find water and shelter. Pull back: "I am a hologram, I am energy. Let me analyse before I react." Then maneuver.

#7.2Why they escalate

Their consciousness doesn't recognize compassion; it survives only in the AI structure, so it captures your energy by projecting negative frequency. As the planet rises, their displays must grow more extreme to overtake your hologram. So action and unity are needed: alone, you can't out-potency them; together it grows stronger.

The message: oxygenated particles (condensed)

An ET fleet will send frequency (like wave genetics) into the atmosphere carrying "oxygenated particles". "Sectors of oxygenated particles will combust the oxides of the toxic ammonia acidic waste" (from recent explosions and pollution), all around, 360 degrees, "to clear the damage that was done." "Have no fear... In three months it will be all new ozone layer," faster in some regions than others. The new ozone will have "a combination of particle layers that will clear the lung layer of the tissue of anyone that took the toxic air." Help is given with the permission of the ETs and the higher order, and our collective higher selves. "The negative is desperate. In three years you will all see the fruits of your work. Stand your ground." No one on the level of ascension will suffer; there will be a bit more battle, but it "will never take a momentum"; the final battle is God's play: "checkmate."

Added note

This is her prediction as given; she says to allow about six months either way, because the negative may add more and the trajectory shifts.

#7.3Compassion

Many who do the most harm were abused as children and split, like hypnotized assassins; they don't recognize what they do. Find a way to subside your anger: forgiveness is number one, or you project hatred and drop. Have compassion for those who exit (the etheric legions): some chose to sacrifice themselves so others wake up.

#7.4Every soul has a place

Some people seem oblivious, living happily in "pink glasses": they chose to hold a steady vibration of joy and love for the collective, which is needed amid all the fluctuation. No soul is out of place.

#7.5Nutrition and the doctors

She notes a surgeon told her doctors study only about two months of nutrition, though nutrition is number one for health. Some students are recruited into secret projects with advanced science (AI, nano, free energy) that the public doesn't see.

#Questions answered

How can we tell whether a technology or platform is for good?
You won't know until it's experienced; ask questions and research, and when you're in a high resonance, your hologram will bring the answer (a friend's story, an article). A frequency device must measure your state every time, because each day is a new hologram; a cancer frequency must match your own cancer. Don't blast fixed frequencies into your body; give it rest between sessions, like muscles after a workout. Even structured water or 432 Hz music: not non-stop. Get blood work and medical supervision for anything you do.
When you say there is no one outside of me, do you mean I'm the only person who exists?
Your particle vibrates so fast it flickers into a new version of itself in a new location: Earth one, Earth two. In each, you reprint everyone around you. Each person you reprint is a soul with its own reality; you tune into the collective hologram of ascension and meet in the now, because there is no time. Between your structure and mine, a third is made: 1 + 1 = 3.
What is the best protocol to connect with ETs and live among them?
They vibrate much higher, so you must accumulate some of their frequency: the QM protocol, compassion, meditation, action for humanity; the heart converts it most. Clear karma: whatever is off balance shows up so you can thank it and convert it. When your resonance matches, contact may come (lucid, out of body, or physical) as a third timeline between yours and theirs. You won't know when; it happens when you can hold it, as astronauts train for a year. Too high a resonance too soon would "burst" you; sometimes they use technology to seal you.
