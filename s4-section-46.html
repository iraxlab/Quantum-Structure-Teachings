The big idea

Past and future exist now. The negative timeline went into the future, saw it could not continue, and is coming back to sabotage its own versions; the positive timeline has already made it. So the data of everything is being collected and locked into a safe spectrum, and you are back "in the now to create a new reality outcome": reconstructing the timeline. "You already made it. You're just dreaming how you got there"; but to see that timeline you must act upon it and hold the vibration. Behave as if you're already on the new earth.

#46.1Every technology has its antidote

Nanobots designed from someone's DNA could be targeted at a person, a family or a group, or used to heal (see S4 44). Films now show both: one says that once nano is in the body "you're finished"; another shows nanobots removed. The Council of Nine said they can be removed "through specific frequency and sound": "you never ever make a technology without knowing how you can protect yourself from it." Where DNA has been reconstructed negatively, technology and the original blueprint are needed to restore and upgrade it. The original blueprint is held in the universal mind, and how to reach it is withheld "because that is preservation" (see S4 44).

Protect your DNA data. Think twice before sending your DNA to a company for an ancestry test: it is how genomes are gathered. Know what is being attempted, so you can maneuver and not consent innocently.

Added safety note

The claims that nanobots are being put into people through the air or through medicines are her views, not established fact. Please make health decisions with your doctor.

The spectrum runs from ultraviolet-violet to orange; mixed, it ends in burgundy, which "will hold the in-between collective data [of] geometric code patterns": all the data, good and bad, gathered and secured in a resonance no one can reach without the frequency.

"These patterns are recalling the new codes and sustaining them", keeping them in place by latitude and altitude (X and Y), "to be precisely ready for integration": locked in. The data is kept because you learn from the past: to change the future in a new timeline you need to know what must never be allowed again.

"To restore all the realities into your collective, you must sustain the belief system that you already exist in a new earth. And [the] new start in the now begins as you are in the new earth." Old systems go down, new come up; in the chaos, be "precisely steady and focused". This "will help the location synergy in your holographic imagery to become permanent in the sequences."

"Coordinative multi-universes will reattach the signal and you will create open new possibilities for creation. It's called reconstructive timelines": with the collective data in your DNA consciousness, the super mind and the oversoul combine to rebuild, restore, or build something brand new. You still have the choice to comply and move to another timeline.

The message of the hybrids: you already made it

Two timelines run at once: the negative one, past and future, which didn't make it, and ours moving to the positive. From the one that made it, you drop into this holographic imprint, the old prison-planet structure, and hold the vibration from the spacecraft to your version here, downloading what is needed to transform it. "Since you already made it, there's nothing to be afraid of."

"You're just dreaming how you got there." But to shift toward that timeline, "acting upon it, and hold the vibration"; everything else is "like a scorpion", eating itself. "It is like a script, and now you are replacing the old program with a new timeline." Your agreements are the program you downloaded; the Council or your star family can change the code if the scenario switches.

"Whatever we do from here to here will become a live structure in geometry patterns." It is like "a virtual reality... a game", there so the human mind can grasp that it is already predetermined. "You are all back in time in the now to create a new reality outcome. It is that simple." "The sequencing brings [the] body to the proper frequency, and you will be able to reconstruct higher frequency vibration wave", holding the timeline "without popping out of the orbit."

Peace, not fear. When you connect this with the technology and the quantum structure, the fear dissipates. Watch more of the positive; if you challenge a teaching, ask before you attack.

Her instructions: fasting and exercise (from the Council of Nine)

If you do the detox protocol with the elimination diet, always have doctor supervision and blood work, "slowly but surely", to make sure you're not allergic and don't deplete: kinesiology, homeopathic nutrition, or a medical doctor who is open-minded.

On a juicing or water-fasting diet: "water fasting is not recommended for people that are diabetic"; it has to be "100%" supervised. During water fasting or juicing, "do not do so much of exercise, hardly any exercise": rest, because the body changes very rapidly and needs the energy for the conversion. You may feel sleepy and tired at first; after a couple of days, or seven or eight, energy comes in.

Once the body stabilizes, you reach the level you want and you're no longer tired, do another blood work; then exercise. With the fat gone, the muscles restructure quickly into a lean, healthy body. (See the detox videos and the Detox Guide.)

Added safety note

Fasting can be dangerous, especially with diabetes, other conditions or medicines, pregnancy, or a history of eating disorders. As she says, do it only with a doctor and blood work.

#Questions answered

What about relatives who don't believe in 5D?
They are another version of you, projected in your own matrix (see S1 1). Tell your truth, live by example, but don't pressure them: let that consciousness choose, as the Council shows and never forces. Stay neutral, not upset; never judge. Keep projecting the positive moment to moment and they may change, but that is a probability.
Do we have many purposes depending on the timeline?
Yes, many; you focus on the one you have the agreement for. The version you see is the one you put physical action and belief into; the other choice splits into its own timeline.
Those who comply and end up on a timeline that doesn't make it: what happens to them?
It depends on the person and their agreements; she is not shown it. That timeline reaches super technology and can't advance, learns it is part of consciousness, and comes back to sabotage its own versions toward the positive.
If we already made it, why be concerned about now?
Because each now is a new parallel reality, its own universe; every shift creates a timeline, and similar actions make it permanent. Without awareness it goes randomly, and someone who knows how can program you, as was done.
Does everyone learning here have a role, and why didn't I find this sooner?
When the time is right, the teacher comes. Once activated, a sentence from someone else may confirm what you were told; trust is built when guidance proves right.
What about the money system?
Still a system "nobody is going to know", so it can't be hijacked; don't believe one person holds everyone's money. Learn syntax grammar and quantum law: from birth you act under a fictitious corporate name, and the real you is sovereign. The old system will collapse piece by piece. Be clever with crypto: "don't... put all your money in one pot", observe, don't follow billionaires; collect and save, step by step, like chess. (Dates left out.)
