Her instructions: What to do (as she listed)

Plant a garden; restore soil with worms, ladybirds, crickets, compost and super weeds. Keep bees; eat organic honey. Find organic heirloom (ancient) seeds; hold a seed under your tongue for a minute before planting. Move toward a clean plant-based diet, with medical guidance. Build community (she mentioned Ubuntu-style community projects) and forest gardens. Spend time in nature: parks, trees, birdsong, barefoot morning dew. Rosemary hedges keep mosquitoes away. Look every day for local organic farmers: you will create them in your reality.
