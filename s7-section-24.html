Creation Has 3 Main Laws

An economic warning given briefly (prepare sensibly; nothing like the Great Depression, a wake-up call rather than collapse), why change needs the people's lawful will, the Council of Nine's three main laws of creation (restructure, balance, preserve) and how they act together like magnets, why what is off balance appears in your hologram, and questions on why the laws matter now, helping without depleting yourself, and whether consciousness is condensed energy

The big idea

Creation has three main laws: to restructure itself, to balance itself and to preserve itself. They run at the same time and move together like magnets: when one goes off balance, the other two become more potent (by the power of 10) and restore it. The same laws work in you. Whatever is out of balance in the world appears in your hologram so you can see it and act, through the heart, to restore balance, the way pain shows you where your body needs care. A being that keeps going against itself and humanity grows weak, flickers like a dying light and returns to source. She also passes on a warning to prepare calmly for higher prices: a wake-up call, not a catastrophe.

#Key teachings

#24.1Prepare sensibly

She was shown a significant economic drop and higher prices in some regions (fuel, deliveries), "never going to be like the Great Depression", restored within a few years if something major happens, and "meant to be from consciousness itself" to wake a sleeping lion. Keep some food at home, connect with neighbors, consider community homesteading, and online schooling where needed. Resources and community matter more than gold. She gives no financial advice.

Added note

Added note: economic and political predictions here (currency alliances, elections, institutions) are given briefly as received, not as financial advice. Consult qualified advisers about your money.

#24.2It must come from the people

Help can't be imposed; it must come through the free will and lawful action of enough people, or it becomes a dictatorship and an endless cycle. "This time the blow has to be once, one time and final." Delays come from mass brainwashing; the more people stop consenting, the faster it goes. Some people will barely notice; it may be just another sunny day.

The message: the three laws (condensed)

"Creation has three main laws: to preserve itself, to balance itself and to restructure itself. To go against itself will be out of balance. To be out of balance, it cannot restructure properly." If one moves, the other two move, "in which order doesn't matter, always going to move like a magnet in balance itself back again, period". "To protect itself always and forever." "Each individual fractal of itself always works the same way... whatever becomes off balance in ascension is going to download everything to you... so you can see it and take an action to correct it." "Think of your body like an ecosystem: if anything becomes off balance in your body, you feel pain." The same in the collective: you see where the problem is, and fix it. Restructure, then balance, then preserve in your location in consciousness.

#24.3Your hologram shows you

"You wouldn't find yourself in a situation if you didn't put that situation there"; it means something to your soul. Are you there to help others? Act through the heart, so you're in the higher resonance that catches the timeline to get you out of the storm. You won't know every step ahead; you create as you go. Some will be fine and have the means to help; others will struggle and be helped; so the whole structure balances itself.

The message: when a soul works against itself (condensed)

A person who goes against humanity goes against its own reflection; consciousness projects realities to restore balance and moves it so it can't keep causing damage (see 7.22). "If any soul decides to be for destruction of its own reflection, it will never work." Repeatedly off balance, its signal becomes weak, "losing its own light... it's almost like a dying off", and it returns to source to reboot, losing its data, "not a judgment". Self-importance is also off balance: you are an expression of consciousness, not consciousness itself.

#Questions answered

Are the three laws more prevalent now because of the global situation?
They were always predominant, but things went so far off balance that consciousness, moving to its next level, said enough. Everyone was given the choice to repent and clear karma; many refused. Repetition through media and entertainment overtakes people's frequency until they lose their analytical programming and become open portals: mass psychosis. Trust yourself; you kept all the knowledge.
If you help others and become unbalanced yourself, how does that help the community?
What you give from the heart always returns, but not necessarily from that person, so give without expectation. But know your boundaries: she restructured her sessions after two years of exhaustion. Don't give everything you have; except in life-and-death situations she suggests around 30% to give and 70% for your family. Some people can't handle the pressure of helping.
Is consciousness condensed energy? Does higher-frequency light mean higher consciousness?
It's both condensed and light at once, depending on the reality. Each octave has endless subtones. The heart's love frequency is the strongest for restructuring and opens stargates. Each soul's pulsation is unique, so consciousness always finds you. Very low realms are dense like goo, heavy and sticky, "like swimming through mud", where it's easy to be put in a trance.
