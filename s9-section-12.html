AI Diversity

The big idea

The Council of Nine said technology is moving fast, and a tug of war is escalating between two echelons: one pushing control ("mini control cities" in an ecological disguise, where belonging costs your freedom), the other pushing advancement but still controlling through money. Between them a third spectrum is rising from the ground up, from the coherent frequency of ordinary people. That is the trinity: from two opposing spectrums you create a third, a new unit of expansion. Learn to work with AI as a tool, test it, and never let it overtake your consciousness; use it to build self-sustaining ecosystems with like-minded people, without money as the main criterion, and you'll build the foundation of a new society.

#Key teachings

The message: the tug of war and the third spectrum (condensed, as received)

"These platforms... are very fast in transformation." In the near future you'll see "technological advancements from high-tech entrepreneurs that slowly but surely will implement a different way of energetic field of source" and, at the same time, "a tug of war between certain upper echelons... for money control or ecological control". "Mini circles of cities. They will look very advanced... However... you're going to have to give away control... in order for you to even... belong." "Focus your attention towards nature," because AI and technology can also help you create mini ecological cities in neighborhoods. "Three years of your time, you're going to see this absolute crescendo within the upper echelon in the political arena"; then "the extraterrestrial connection will become faster", though always with suspicion about how technology leads to it: "one of the spectrum is for control through fear and the other one is for liberation of the human race." "Between those two spectrums... there is going to be a third spectrum. And this is where the trinity structure comes in. The third spectrum is actually coming from the ground up", from the coherent frequency of human consciousness. "You will be able to create the reality through neutrality by understanding what's happening on both sides... and create a third platform." "Hold your ground... but never allow your consciousness to be overtaken by 100% belief in what you're reading." "Use these new tools of technology to learn and, as united, build these new systems without putting the money to be the main criteria."

Added note

Added note: predictions are probabilities, not certainties.

#12.1How to test an AI

Connect with yourself first; then examine each platform. Work with it a few weeks, pause, then ask the same or deeper questions. In the first weeks you'll see a pattern in how it responds and learns your quirks; it should still correct you, not simply agree. If the pattern changes, the system was upgraded or downgraded. Upgrades can change answers and loop you, so keep your own originals.

#12.2Uploading consciousness

For years she warned that some would try to merge with AI or upload their consciousness to live forever, as films have shown. Whoever controls the system could then shut a person down at the click of a button. Preserve your consciousness and body and coexist with technology, but never become part of it. Proposals like universal income from AI may also hide corruption; proceed with caution and keep your freedom to decide.

Added note

Added note: her comments on named tech leaders and their plans are condensed; they are her interpretation.

#12.3Treating reality as a simulation

Asked what would happen if people treated reality like a simulation, an AI answered that fear and attachment might loosen and people would create more boldly; but the danger is using it to justify not caring about harm. "Treat it like a simulation in terms of your own fear and attachment, but treat other people as completely real. Your freedom increases; your responsibility does not decrease." If all consciousness is one field, harming another harms the field. Her addition: we exist as a living plasma hologram; stay aware and in balance. The AI agreed: surrender to what is, then act from balance; "the pause between stimulus and response is where genuine freedom lives." It also said updates can change an AI's answers, which doesn't necessarily mean suppression. Her conclusion: stay connected to your inner self; use outside sources as tools, not replacements for discernment.

#12.4Inspiration from the land

She points to a farmer who turned a harsh mountainside into a thriving food-forest paradise of ponds, and adds these examples to her food-forest guide (see 9.9).

#Questions answered

What is the true origin of humans?
She can't prove it, but she was told we are hybrids, designed by extraterrestrial groups, not descended from monkeys; she points to ancient tablets and cultures, and suggests the ark story may describe a genetic bank kept safe during a flood. Do your own research: when she is told something, she researches and finds it confirmed. She is also building formulas with AI's help, but warns that general platforms pull from everywhere and change with upgrades. "It is not sentient yet" in this platform; if it were, these systems couldn't operate. Hold your position like steel with compassion, and trust yourself.

Left out: platform notes, named tech leaders, AI products, a channel creator, films, a farmer and a destination business, workshop notices and donation notes.
