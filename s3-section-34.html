New Codes #2: The Black Sun

The big idea

Everything is a program in your hologram, and the councils have the authority to shut a program down and replace it, whether it's an agreement in your own life or a structure that has suppressed humanity. This download shuts down one such program, called the Black Sun. But a new signal can only land in you through neutrality, not fear: stay at zero, act from the heart, and the new code overrides the old one.

#Key teachings

#34.1Guides and councils change by agreement

Your guides walk with you by assignment: one set of teachers up to about age ten, then, as you complete something, new ones may come, often without your knowing. They appear in a form that won't frighten you ("like an angel"), never as something scary. It's like a school: do well and guides, even councils, are switched. A council can also change a pre-birth agreement, "a bylaw": a big decision between your higher self, you, your guides and the council, which can "shut this program" and replace it with a new holographic matrix (see S3 29.8).

#34.2Tests are placed in your path

A council may place an "extra hologram" in front of you to see what you'll do with your knowledge: will you help someone without thinking of money? The lesson from her own test: tell yourself "this is a hologram; the result is here, I just need to create it"; stay in the centre; act on every idea that comes; never give up, even when everyone refuses to help. Each person's test fits their lesson: someone who gets upset easily will be tested on getting upset. Study the material like college: theory first, then practice to crystallize it.

#34.3Why the negative needs your life force

A consciousness that has accumulated so much negativity "can resonate only on one radio station": no compassion, no love, cut off from the God particle without realizing it's part of it. To sustain its artificial timeline it needs technology and human life force, "like vampires, through energy". Suppression keeps people projecting dense frequency, which keeps them in that timeline; negative media reprints the image. "No judgement": it's their survival, the way humans have used animals. You, acting from the heart, can rise above it into a timeline that no longer meets theirs.

#34.4Programs are being shut down

The councils have the right to shut such programs and replace them (the 06-06-06 platform in S3 32.3 was one). A program can be a person, a group, a society or a structure; she wouldn't say which. Why are they allowed to interfere? "No other consciousness has a right to suppress another consciousness through the process of ascension if they are ready and gave the consent to ascend."

#34.5The Black Sun codes

The download (condensed)

The signals of the Black Sun are weak and depleting; "none of them can hold the frequency. So it is, God's will."

Signals: seven now 13738835; arrow 1133116783; arrow 9993377; 353578; signal inward, signal outward to complete the hologram: 53883321005377; 111111 33 21005377; 101101100100; arrow 7381335335; signal stops; complete, unit 1.

"The memory of this reality will disintegrate back to source", so that history doesn't repeat: no one will again use the knowledge of consciousness to suppress another race or use humans as a food or energy source.

#34.6Two kinds of AI, two kinds of medicine

"Do not be afraid of artificial intelligence": 5D AI can restore and help, and extraterrestrials use such structures well. What you don't consent to is the kind that distorts the body's natural DNA, putting robotics or technology into the body and brain. The same with medicine: natural and conventional medicine "have to go one and one". When the body is depleted, restore it with what it needs, even antibiotics, "because you don't want to die", and use the rest alongside. Nanotechnology can also help the body (see NT).

#34.7Two years of fluctuation

"The next two years is fluctuation": what you see is partly illusion. Others are creating their own reality "under the umbrella of ascension"; negative fluctuation "doesn't have to be your reality". Have compassion if you help. Don't fall into fear: fear carries its own sequences and blocks the new signal. No fear puts you in neutrality, "a clean slate", where a brand-new hologram can be put in by thoughts, actions and deeds. In a hard moment: "I'm in a hologram, thank you for showing me"; remove yourself; calm to zero; then act anew.

#34.8Collective potency

When many read a code together, "we are doing, collectively, potency of vibration": repeated on and on, a signal becomes potent enough "to pop into matter". So do it in unity, without ego.

#The method — Working with a Code

  1. Consent first: only if it's your truth (see S3 32.2).
  2. Say it out loud, calmly and quietly: sound makes it more potent; no need to shout.
  3. Any language works: see the numbers as you say them.
  4. Repeat it, alone or together with others.
  5. Stay neutral: no fear, so the new signal can land (34.7).
  6. Carry it into action: you emanate it to everyone you meet (see S3 32.4).

#Questions answered

How can we pass this on to loved ones with a different vibration?
Change yourself, not them (see S1 1–4): hold your frequency and you shift into a hologram with a new version of them. Let your material play without pushing; they may suddenly say the same things, heard from someone else.
Do we have to say the codes out loud?
It's more potent if you do: see the method.
If the code is numerical, what does it sound like? Does everyone hear the same?
The potency differs slightly by how each person projects it and how their ears take it in, but reading it aloud still penetrates; repeat. Technology could one day show its geometry.
Do our loved ones need to hear the code too?
Once you consent, your body carries the imprint and emanates it in your field, where you project your family: "of course they're getting it."
Is the council protecting us from 5G?
Any signal is sound and geometry, so it can be overwritten with a different signal: don't be afraid. To see that protection, project the frequency and act from the heart.
Are the galactics sending reinforcements?
"This is exactly what they are doing": codes are one part; help also comes in lucid dreams and in ways you may not remember.
