Season 1 · Section 17

E.T Ascension thru Quantum Field

Why ascension doesn't require death, the new grids, meeting other beings in a third reality, and why each person creates their own ascension timeline (pre-recorded talk)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

Ascension doesn't have to pass through death: the body is a vessel for the energy force we are made of. The old negative grids are down and new ones are in place, so no one can manipulate your field; you only have to match the new frequency by living for others, sharing and without greed. Whatever you hold steadily crystallizes into your timeline, and contact with other beings happens in a third reality you create together. Above all, each person creates their own ascension timeline: what you see depends on your vibration, so trust your heart, keep projecting the positive, and don't feed what you dislike by fighting it.

#Key teachings

#17.1Death is an illusion

This talk, said to come from the 11-dimensional beings, to whom the Council of Nine introduced the teacher, is about ascension through the timelines. It opens with a striking point: ascension doesn't have to be a death process. Death is described as an illusion, because the body is a pure vessel for harvesting the energy force we are made of (see 13.5, 6.8).

It then recaps the structure: light and sound create trinity plasma and a magnetic and gravitational field; consciousness creates through that plasma; you project the holographic structure and manifest everything you see; and you and everyone else are made of the same one particle, flickering through different timelines so that you can have an experience (see 1.1, 4.1).

#17.2Holding an emotion crystallizes a timeline

Your thoughts create holographic expressions, and you see a timeline that matches the vibration you send from your emotions, thoughts and deeds together. You don't only shift from one parallel reality to the next; if you keep the same level of emotion, for example happiness, by watching uplifting films, staying positive and doing positive things for others, you crystallize a whole timeline from that frequency (see 1.3, 2.7).

#17.3The old grids are down

A message given the previous year is recalled: the old, negative grids around the planet were taken down and new grids put in place. The meaning: we are no longer trapped. You are free to manifest and to ascend, and nobody can manipulate the frequency and energy field around you.

But to live in that freedom you have to match it: vibrate in a fifth-dimensional structure. In practice that means doing what you do for humanity and for others, without greed, and sharing with each other (see 4.3, 5.1).

From the books"Know that all the negative grids are down. The new fifth dimensional Earth grids are working in full capacity. To create and give and share as one" (SD). Negative souls "can not be in the new grids," which "absolutely contradict" negative energy (DP; see also 13.4).

#17.4What you imagine already exists, and this is a dream state

You can't think of something that doesn't already exist in the field (see 1.4). So if you hold an image of something in your mind, you already exist in that reality somewhere. To make it permanently visible to you, to become matter for you, you crystallize it: hold the vibration of your deeds, thoughts and emotions, moment to moment, because thoughts and emotions are frequencies that create your hologram.

What you take for solid matter is a hologram. This reality is described as another dream state, like lucid dreaming (see 11.9). The illustration: under hypnosis, if your consciousness is told the wall isn't there, you could walk through it. Matter is held in place by belief (see 6.2).

#17.5Meeting other beings: a third reality

How can we see beings who live at a much higher frequency? It works by the 1 + 1 = 3 formula (see 2.2). Our reality is one, theirs is another. For contact, they lower their frequency and we raise ours, and together we create a third reality, a new parallel timeline between the two. That is also why there is no time: you each live in completely different timeline structures, yet you meet in one location (see 2.8, 10.3).

#17.6Each person creates their own ascension timeline

Every individual is creating their own timeline of ascension, and in a sense we have already made it, because you exist at the same time in many parallel realities, perhaps also as an Arcturian, an Andromedan or whatever your agreements are (see 6.9). So people see different things: depending on their vibration and how well they did their lessons, some will see the ascension of Earth, and others will see intergalactic connection and contact. Each person is crystallizing their own hologram of Earth's ascension.

Collectively we are doing this together, yet individually we experience very different timelines. That is why a war can be happening in one country while you watch a talk about timelines in another: the people there are on a different timeline by agreement, perhaps to learn, or to show the rest of us that something is out of balance and needs work (see Section 1, the question on lifting humanity).

And why don't others understand? Within your hologram, other people are expressions you project in order to have your experience; your ascension timeline has nothing to do with theirs. So if someone says "it isn't time" or "only a certain number will ascend," that is the timeline they project from their point of view (see Section 16).

From the books"You are dreaming the ascension but you already made it" (DP; see 2.8).

#17.7Trust your heart, project the positive, don't fight

So it doesn't matter what someone else says, "or what I say to you," or what any source says. What matters is what you truly feel from within, from your heart, because the heart is connected with consciousness, and the pure essence of consciousness is love. Consciousness always leans slightly to the positive in order to exist; if it went only negative, it would disappear (see 2.8).

Whatever you see happening, keep the positive frequency. It is a projection, and it won't become your reality as long as you don't agree with it and don't act on it. Talk about the people and things you like, but don't go and fight what you dislike: fighting infuses that timeline and makes it part of your reality. Project the positive, act positively, and spend time with positive people. The exception is something right in front of you that you genuinely need to deal with (see 7.5, 14.4).

The recording ends here, mid-sentence, as the talk was about to explain what happens once consciousness reaches the third reality.

#The method — Hold Your Ascension Timeline

A daily practice for living on the timeline you choose:

  1. Choose the emotion you want your timeline built on, such as joy, kindness or gratitude.
  2. Feed it daily: uplifting films and music, positive people, kind acts for others.
  3. Live for more than yourself: share, give, no greed. That is what matches the new grids.
  4. When something heavy appears, don't agree with it and don't act on it. It's a projection; keep the positive frequency.
  5. Don't fight what you dislike. Deal only with what's truly in front of you, from the heart.
  6. Check with your heart, not with what others predict. Your ascension timeline is your own.
