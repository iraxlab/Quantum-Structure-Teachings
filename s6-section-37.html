The big idea

Consciousness holds a timeline in perfect balance at the zero point between positive and negative, a playground in which all who are making it learn at their own pace. Right now about 70% of those making it convert slowly (their transformation looks like 30 years) and about 30% convert quickly (it looks like 3). Because the 70% still tilt the collective, even the fast converters will keep seeing fractions of the negative around them while their own lives flow. Every awakening shifts the percentages, so your daily job is to keep choosing the positive and pull others into balance.

#Key teachings

#37.1The zero-point balance

1 + 1 = 3 = 1: two realities collapse into a third, one unit with a positive or negative charge, and that charge sets your location in time space. To shift into another fractal you must start from the absolute zero point, in 100% balance: you can't shift at 99%. In that balance you see both the negative and the positive at once, because you came to pull others: if the awake simply left, who would bring up those still asleep but above the negative?

#37.270% and 30%

How quickly you convert from one reality to the next sets how fast you shrink time; compassion, love and creation raise it. She was told that of those making it, about 70% still hold some negative belief or don't yet know how to convert (about 30 years of transformation), and about 30% convert quickly (about 3 years). That tilt is why the 30% still see the negative in the collective. It applies fractally: to a planet, a country, a city, a family (why one child thrives and another struggles). As people awaken the 30% grows, and the 30 years shrinks to 15 or 10.

The message: hexagonal influence (condensed)

In the fractal momentums, 1 + 1 = 3 = 1, every particle ends with a positive or negative charge. "To restore the new sequences of the new resonance... the interaction must be in absolute balance of a zero point": absolutely void of frequency input and output, absolute stillness. "The human mind at this particular time of evolution has a very challenging time" restructuring with new sequences, so 70% struggle with the 3-years-for-30. For the 30%, "the three years of configuration... will be smooth and transitional... all available somehow to them, when is needed." The most important thing is to stay focused on positive actions and thoughts and not engage with negative platforms: "Matter and energy are the same thing. Apply enough energy and the matter changes... You are the alchemist." "The reality is all you wish to be, there's no limits." The 70% "will see what negative is doing, and in some cases will truly experience [it]" until they transform.

#37.3Every moment is a new you

Consciousness is saying, "here goes, babies": a school in which all are the best, to see who graduates quickest. The outcome is predetermined (you make it); you create now how. The past no longer exists: transform its lessons into a completely different behaviour and action. That is the hardest transformation, changing how your body habitually grabs frequency to match the hexagonal structure.

Is that why we say we already made it, because God holds the timeline for us to ascend?
Exactly. Consciousness holds this reality so that even those who didn't know, and even those on the negative side who were threatened into acting and then wake up and serve humanity, get a chance in a sub-tonal parallel reality to complete their lessons. Don't judge: you don't know what goes on behind the scenes. Those wholly committed to the negative are another matter. If more converted well, 30 years would become 5 or 10; unity puts a pulsation in the field, prayer alone doesn't manifest physically.
How do I release childhood trauma hidden from my consciousness (dissociation)?
When it's that deep in the subconscious you need help: very deep hypnosis that reaches beyond the conscious mind to the higher self and guides (she named the Dolores Cannon method). Once healing begins, the protocols become easier to apply.
When we ask for help from angels, family or guides, how do they reach us?
Differently for each person: through another person who speaks without memory of it, direct channelling, a white being seen briefly, telepathy, lucid dreams, automatic writing. It comes subtly, when least expected, to see whether you're ready, because fear lowers your vibration. Bylaw: no positive being can overtake your consciousness without full permission; the negative bypasses this by getting consent through its platforms.

Left out: disaster commentary and remarks about named people.
