The big idea

Above the extraterrestrials sit councils, and above them a priesthood connected with mother consciousness. After a great debate, the priesthood granted the fleets' request: humanity, which "has suffered big amnesia" through manipulation, will be helped. A restoration of DNA and RNA is to be given through frequency, with each person's original codes downloaded over up to ten months. The exception is those who, shown everything, still chose blindly out of ego, not those who acted in fear. Meanwhile, expect months of fluctuation; ETs are securing what could cause harm by replacing holograms in the collective. Don't take fear from scary stories: they may be told to lower your vibration.

#Key teachings

#32.1The orders above

Within the matrix there are tiers of existence. Extraterrestrials oversee humans and planets; above them, councils gather from many galaxies to oversee both; above them is a consciousness connected with mother consciousness: the priesthood, the highest of orders. In every reality, higher orders can download information, and you can connect with your other versions to download knowledge. Many patriots sacrifice in silence inside systems, some even passing data to the ETs.

The message: the DNA restoration (brief)

"We are [the] 7th fleet of Ortinaki. We collect all the data from the human DNA... help to the humanity in the DNA, RNA will be given and restoration will begin." Not for those who saw everything and still chose blindly out of "ignorance and ego", but for those who acted in fear. "It was a big debate... but the priesthood has made the decision to our humble request to help... Humanity has suffered big amnesia from the manipulation, and [a] gift was given." The method is "based on... coordinative cellular... vector signals... quantized entanglement to restructure and restore the sequences" so synthetic compounds are "neutralized"; "in seven months cleansing restoration"; reconnecting "the original geometrical spinning of each individual"; "the download restoration on [the] cellular level will take up to 10 months... Welcome to 5D Earth, everyone." "The mother consciousness restoration is in order."

This is her reported message, not medical fact. Please make any health decisions with a doctor.

The message: five months of battle (brief)

About five months of battle for the human race: "incredible fluctuation in political [and] monetary systems"; markets up and down; crimes "literally in your face" because the negative no longer cares; "checkmate in the end is for humanity." Things become clearly better from March to May next year, when "you're going to see the fruits of your doing."

#32.2Securing the reactors

Shown reactors in a conflict zone with a craft above, she was told: "secure, secure, secure." They replace the hologram the negative tried to place in the collective with another (codes given: "773 platform with hologram... 331 rebuild all sectors... into the trapezoid section"), secure rod one and rod two, and set an "advanced technological atomic self-perpetual sector": any leaking energy goes into a field "like a black hole" so dense that it spins out of orbit, dissolves into tiny particles and returns to source. Replacing holograms is done only through councils and collective agreements. As with all atomic and nano technology, the perimeter of the hologram must be secured.

Her instructions: stories and markets
  • If you hear something frightening (a reactor leak, say), don't take fear from it: it may be announced even if it was stopped, to create panic and lower your vibration.
  • Markets: expect up and down, especially around November; "stay low"; don't put all your money in; know what you're doing. "As long as you know the game, money will be made." She doesn't understand markets and gives no further advice.
  • Keep doing what you need to be doing, in the heart, helping each other.

#Questions answered (channelled)

Where are we in the battle?
"33%... in the holographic field. More to go."
Will there be more active guidance on contributing to the new consciousness?
When seven layers of technological advancement are observed in the collective, the downloads of the new DNA come; each person reconnects with their higher self and the galactic family matching their resonance, and meets them through synchronicity.
How can we return to the avatar pods? How do we exit the program?
(To one person) "Avatar pods, three layers, 113." Interact with this knowledge daily and help humanity; at 73% in your consciousness your reality shifts and you can reconnect through lucid dreaming, not necessarily a pod: "within three months."
I'm spiritual and my boyfriend isn't. Will he ascend with me?
"You're misunderstanding the structure." It is his own spiritual path, not a lower one. Let go of being "more spiritual" and your reality will recreate a version of him with which you can continue together.
What connects astral projection and sleep paralysis? Should we fear it?
Different platforms. In projection, 30% of you stays in 3D to hold your location, 70% leaves through a stargate along a precise trajectory, joined by a cord of light; enter and exit badly and you can meet negative entities. Raise your frequency; dense food makes it hard. Lucid dreaming is safer: it opens a space between realities within your own matrix that you can control.
I've tried astral travel and can't. Any codes?
Start with lucid dreaming; astral projection can be dangerous if you can't hold the vibration. "Do not run before learning how to walk."
If we can't connect with our higher self or galactic family, is it frequency or a contract?
Both, or a resonance not yet spinning enough to hold the signal. Earth was "a prison planet" whose frequencies were built to overwrite your codes; "it almost looks like we are talking into a void that never had a receiver."
Should we erase as many memories as possible for these downloads?
No. Understand them and let them go; replace them with new understanding and new action. "You cannot reject your own existence; you simply create new ones."
Will the upgrade make family and friends exit, die or fade from our realities?
All of the above (see S4 38). Each chooses to exit or stay; with a contract, you can reprint another version of them.
What happens in enlightenment? Is it full alignment with the original blueprint?
Alignment can be fractal, in one moment; full alignment is consciousness itself, one unit with the collective. If you choose alignment in a moment, "so it is."
Do we all have an etheric body?
"You are etheric at all time"; it only takes different densities.
