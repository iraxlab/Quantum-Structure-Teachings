The big idea

Predictions come in pieces, sometimes as codes, sometimes held back on purpose, and can be slightly off; so hold them lightly and don't fear them. What is said to be coming, hidden things surfacing, withheld technology appearing and new structures rising from the people up, will unfold step by step so that no one panics, over a 30-year conversion, with interstellar travel at around 50 years; but your own experience can come much sooner, depending on your vibration. Positive and negative run side by side, so take the positive. Civilizations reset because consciousness learns through both sides. And the direction is clear: bow to no one. You are the creator; respect every belief, live through your heart. On health, the answers here vary widely, and some are unsafe; the notes in this section say which.

#Key teachings

#5.1How predictions are given, and how to hold them

Predictions come "out of the blue": the guidance decides what to say and when, and sometimes keeps part of it back, so as not to show everything to those working against humanity. So a prediction may be half the picture: the talk admits one earlier prediction was interpreted wrongly because only part of it was given (see S2 4.11). When asked what a coded line means, the answer is often "the information will come when it's the right time."

The talk also says it follows certain online sources that decode political messages, because they seemed to match what it was receiving. This book leaves those sources out and takes no political side; treat any prediction about governments as a prediction, not a fact.

#5.2Why so much is happening now

Globally, not only in one country, there is a lot of diversion: when something is being done in secret, attention is pulled elsewhere. The talk says groups that were asked to stop working against humanity chose to continue, and that this is why events are escalating. But everyone is part of consciousness, including the negative side: their own higher selves and guidance will sabotage their platforms so that ascension happens, because ascension is part of their consciousness too (see S2 1.3, 1.6).

You can't change anything until you wake up from within and see the manipulation for yourself. Do your own research, calmly; things come to light step by step, and each person sees them on their own platform.

#5.3The predictions read in the talk

Read as given, without interpretation, except where the talk offered one:

• The blind will start seeing. • The dead will rise up. • The sick will be reborn. • The bloodshed will stop. • The monkey will not hide (not explained; possibly a code). • The willow tree will burn (the talk senses an exposure of "black magic"). • The humanity of the Hindus, India, will rise up. • The historical society will be exposed: so much of history was covered up that it is hard to know the truth, and at the right time each person will have an upgrade, an awakening and a knowing of what the truth is and where they come from (the talk links this to humanity having been genetically engineered from different extraterrestrial races, which can be done positively or negatively). • Medicine will be replaced, as withheld technologies come out gradually. • New technology will come. • The secrets will stop. • New structures will rise from the ground up, from the people. • The military will recognise the preservation of humanity. • The fifth-dimensional AI will be launched, and the system will be saved (see S1 8.1). • The extraterrestrials will arrive. • A new interstellar alliance and a new fleet will form; new humanity will rise; interstellar travel will start. • The systems of all negative beings will fail through 20 platforms, twenty timelines of reality. • "Lesson done."

The talk explains the last line: we are running a holographic program, a living intelligence, built on lessons; humanity is completing it and moving forward. How you experience each of these depends on your own structure: hold your vibration high, through the heart, to see them on a 5D platform.

From the booksDP prints this list. Its line reads "The meaningless medicine will be replaced" (the talk read it as "meaningful"). In DP the list is preceded by other predictions, including political and regional ones, which the talk didn't read and this book leaves out.

The talk also expresses its own warm view of Americans as a "sleeping lion" whose awakening will shake the planet, and says arrests are happening that national media don't report. These are the talk's views and claims.

#5.4The time frame: 30 years, then 50

The guidance gives 30 years for the conversion to come to fruition, the new platforms set up, and about 50 years for the interstellar alliance and travel (see S2 1.4). But don't put a frame on your own experience: it can come much sooner. In a sense we have already made it (see S1 2.8); yet you can't just skip to the end, because the journey is the lesson, and you came to help and interact with others along the way.

The talk repeats its claims about future regeneration technology, measuring a person's molecular vibration so that tissue can be restored (see S2 1.7, 2.13). Meanwhile, keep your body in a healthy vibration (see S2 4.5 and its note).

#5.5Positive and negative run side by side

Asked about almost anything new, such as a change in law, a new product or a new technology, the answer is the same: both sides run at once. Pay attention to the source and the details, but take it from the positive point of view, so that you shift into the timeline where it turns out positive. That is the teaching on fluctuating realities (see S1 3.3).

#5.6Why civilizations reset

Because of learning: consciousness expands by experiencing both sides, like someone who wants the beach after the mountains, or an action film after many gentle ones. To create, you need to know what each input does, negative and positive, so you can balance them like an alchemist of creation. Life is like a holographic game we are completing: "lesson done, completion of program." Which door you open is up to you.

#5.7Bow to no one

The direction for the coming years is to be yourself, live through your heart, and bow to no one: not a guru, not an intermediary. That is why the quantum structure is being taught, so you realise that you create everything. The talk says religion was used to divide and rule; "an eye for an eye" keeps you looping in the same reality, because going after someone reprints the same structure (see S1 14.4).

At the same time, respect everyone's belief system, even that of the most harmful people: you have to neutralize everything to switch timelines, and you can't do that while judging. You can say "I don't like what's happening" without judging, because even the worst person is another version of you in the hologram (see S2 1.6). Each person will meet what they need to wake up, at their own pace; some need ten wake-up calls, others one. You can call it God, the quantum structure, or the living intelligence: it is everywhere.

The talk also makes sharp remarks about particular religious practices; this book leaves those out, in keeping with its own teaching to respect every belief.

And keep yourself grounded: asked whether it is a "starseed", the answer avoided titles ("I don't want a crown"). No one, teacher included, needs a crown.

#5.8You are powerful; you don't need anyone to tell you

Asked what blocks someone's vibration, the answer was: all of it, but mainly allowing a belief system to be part of you. Put the teachings into your belief system 100%, without judging them first; do your best from the heart; give and share daily. That creates the frequency you shift into next. "Don't give me the power to tell you that you are powerful, because you are powerful. You don't need me." Sometimes you project someone into your reality to remind you; that's fine, but the power is yours.

#The method — Hold the Future Lightly

For predictions, news, and anything new or unsettling:

  1. Pull out and watch. Notice diversions; don't take sides or panic.
  2. Hold predictions lightly. They come in pieces and may be off; don't build fear on them.
  3. Check before you trust, especially anything about health or money.
  4. Take the positive view of whatever is new, so you shift into its positive timeline.
  5. Respect every belief, bow to no one, judge no one.
  6. Do your part daily: live from the heart, give, share, and look after your body with your doctor.

#Questions answered

Why is cannabis legalisation spreading so fast right now?
It is part of the change that is happening, but both sides are running at once. Pay attention to where it comes from and what it is, then take it from the positive, so you shift into a positive outcome (see 5.5).
It feels like this time is a great opening, and that we could enter the new Earth, Aria, right now if our vibration aligns. Do you agree?
Thirty years is the frame for the whole conversion, but that doesn't mean you have to wait; your experience can come much sooner. Keep your body in a healthy vibration and do the work to tune into that platform. Just remember you can't skip to the end: the journey is the lesson, and you are here to help others too (see 5.4).
How long does it take for the pineal gland to decalcify once you drink distilled water, and what happens when the third eye opens? Any tips?
The answer given was about three months. The third eye opens according to your vibration and the knowledge you tune into, and it doesn't work while you keep choosing to do negative things. Tips: the detox, distilled water, and a lot of knowledge and heart-based learning, all at the same time. Everyone is being activated anyway, which is why people suddenly feel drawn to certain teachers. These are the talk's claims.
Can you explain procreation through the structure?
There is no such thing as procreation; it is always creation: co-creating, instantly and simultaneously. Think quantum (see S1 2.2).
A friend has had MS from the waist down for about 15 years. Will he be healed, and what would help?
The answer pointed to a product called "MMS".
Added warning: do not take MMS

"MMS" ("Miracle Mineral Solution") is chlorine dioxide, a bleach. Health authorities warn that drinking it can cause serious harm, including severe vomiting, diarrhoea and life-threatening dehydration, and that it does not treat MS or any other disease. Please don't take it. For MS, work with a neurologist; there are effective treatments.

Can I add baking soda to distilled water to make it more alkaline?
Yes, according to the answer, using aluminium-free baking soda, but no more than 30 days of drinking it, then a break; gargling is fine. See the baking soda note in S1 15.4: don't drink it regularly, especially with heart, kidney or blood-pressure problems, and ask your doctor first.
Where is my spine misaligned, causing me to stay in a stressed state?
A personal reading was given: a problem in the middle of the back, with the pain coming from higher up than where it is felt, and advice to change diet. It added that people with metal in the body (implants and the like) should not do the detox. A reading is not a diagnosis; for back pain, see a doctor or physiotherapist.
What about MSM (a sulphur supplement)?
Be careful: it has to be measured individually, and too much can interfere with the body's chemistry. Check with your doctor or pharmacist.
My mother lost her sense of smell years ago after a brain tumour. During a Reiki session she felt as though she was smelling again. What can she do?
Have her recall the memory of a smell, an orange, for example: picture it, feel it, and tune into its vibration, because smell is a memory bank she is calling on. And don't let worries about her memory become a frustration; that is only a belief system (see S1 8.4).
What is blocking my vibration: my beliefs, or a health issue?
All of the above, but mainly allowing a belief system to block you. Keep tuning into the material daily, stay in the heart, give and share; and take care of your body (see 5.8).
I've been working on my thyroid and struggle to lose weight. Is it back to normal?
Not yet: it fluctuates. Always work under a doctor's supervision, with regular tests, while you follow your own practices; that way you can see the results. The talk recommends asking a doctor about more sensitive testing, and repeats its view on cutting out dairy and gluten. Don't stop thyroid medication without your doctor.
Is turpentine good for cleansing?
The answer seemed to describe a tea made from the pine tree, organic, as good for bronchitis and the airways.
Added warning: turpentine is poisonous

Turpentine oil is poisonous if swallowed and can cause serious harm. Never drink it or use it as a "cleanse". If someone has swallowed it, contact a poison centre or emergency services immediately. A pine-needle herbal tea is a different thing entirely; check any herbal remedy with a pharmacist.

If we drink only distilled water, won't we become mineral-deficient?
"Again, Andrew Norman Webber, distilled water: you have videos and videos on that." Distilled water has a negative charge and takes only the negative chemicals out of the body, not the regular nutrients, as the extraterrestrials told her; her homeopathic nutritionist disagreed, so she researched it and found Webber. Her own experience: in hospital with pneumonia, coughing blood, her guidance asked her to take only distilled water for three days; she took only the two antibiotic pills given in hospital, and nothing came back. "I never recommend that for people that are diabetic or with kidney failure."
Added safety note

Pneumonia needs medical treatment: finish antibiotics exactly as prescribed. Water-only days are dangerous with diabetes, kidney disease and many other conditions; ask your doctor first.

Highly evolved civilizations seem to have existed before. Why do civilizations reset?
Because of learning: consciousness expands by experiencing both sides, so that, like an alchemist, it knows what each input does (see 5.6).
With so much manipulation in religion and spirituality, what is the healthy way forward? Being ourselves, living through our hearts, bowing to no one?
Yes: they don't want us to bow to anyone. You create everything. Respect every belief, judge no one, and live through your heart (see 5.7).
Would short fasts of three to five days help alongside the detox? And what about hydrogen or alkaline water?
Start step by step so your body gets used to it before a fuller programme; a short fast can be a gentle introduction, for example three days detox only with distilled water (some people did ten days), then go back, and later the complete detox. For hydrogen or alkaline water: "alkaline water is good; again, it has to be measured", because a frequency does something to your structure. The water protocol of 3 Hz, 70 Hz and 90 Hz, 20 minutes each, is generic for everybody: a healthy body vibrates between 70 and 90 Hz, and 3 Hz cleans everything around, prepping you, like before an operation; anything individual must be measured precisely (see S2 4, Questions). Check any fast with your doctor first (see S2 4.5 and its note).
I'm writing a spiritual book and want to sell it to support myself. Is that all right?
Yes, it's fine. Take it step by step: once you have enough, start giving and distributing from the heart, to tune into the platforms of abundance. Don't throw yourself in all at once without the belief that you can; that can block you (see S1 4.4, S2 4.10).
Is wheatgrass all right if I'm avoiding gluten? I want the nutrients from wheat.
Wheatgrass is fine. You can also get nutrients from sea vegetables and raw vegetables; find a nutritionist who can tailor a diet for you, and keep juicing (see S2 4.5).
A friend's kidney transplant isn't working well, and her thyroid was removed. What should she do?
She will have to go through an experience, but shouldn't take it negatively. Meditate, but above all take action: keep working with her medical doctors, and look for other opinions and solutions alongside. Never stop transplant or thyroid medication without the transplant team.
Are souls I've been close to in other lives here on Earth now, in my city?
Yes: three of them, from three previous connections.
