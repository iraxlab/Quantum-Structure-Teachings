New Predictions/ 2024

How a prediction becomes near-certain, why no one should be judged without knowing what's behind the scenes, a long received set of predictions for 2024 (given only in outline here), why we are already in the "war", caution about money and systems, and questions on other countries, colleges, and the redistribution of wealth

The big idea

A prediction is given only when the collapse of parallel realities is about 90% or more toward one outcome; the Council gives the end result, never the who, how or when, because fluctuation is constant and opponents mustn't be told. The end result: humanity wins, and you will each experience the passage according to how you respond. Don't wait for anyone, any system or any money to save you: keep a few months of supplies from discernment, not fear, act daily as a decent human being for humanity, and create your wealth now.

#Key teachings

#50.1When a prediction is given

In the quantum structure, a prediction becomes total when one outcome between parallel realities is far more predominant than the other. Below that, they give probabilities, and these can change within weeks. Sometimes the positive side allows crimes to continue to map an entire network. Don't judge individuals: many were born into or threatened by such systems. But now every soul must choose humanity or darkness, and darkness collapses because consciousness by law never self-destroys.

The message: 2024 predictions (outline only)

She received detailed predictions naming countries, alliances and political leaders: exposure and judgment of corrupt figures, a "300 days" battle for humanity, coordinated attempts in the USA over three to seven months that would be stopped "like... frozen in time", wars declared that would quickly subside, a financial fluctuation of about 3% to 30% held from collapse by an AI system (three months of recovery if it happened, at about 3% probability), banks transitioning rather than all collapsing, colleges to be rebuilt, free press to flourish slowly, new technology released over about seven years. "God will not allow [a] third world war... you're already in it... your body, your soul, your flesh, your blood" were attacked. "The game is already over. You just see it how it happened... 2024 is crucial for your individual survival." "No one can escape persecution... The human race survives. New beginning for all of you, have no fear."

Added note

As with every prediction in this book, these are given only in outline, as received, without political endorsement; the named leaders, countries and claims are left out. She repeated that she is not a financial adviser and that it's a "fictional story" for financial purposes: don't act on it financially.

#50.2Practical guidance

Keep about three months of survival supplies (food, some money), from discernment, not fear. You'll face events as in 2020: no one is exempt, but experiences differ with your response, so pull back and analyze. Be wary of anyone selling frequencies or holograms for hundreds of dollars, or investments on the promise of a new system: "the system is not installed yet... even the people that think they know, they don't know." Look into new schools and vocational routes. Unite as one human race: forgive past wrongs and choose humanity, not division.

(Place details left out.)

Many colleges were infiltrated to disconnect students from right and wrong. Be very careful where she studies; look at how students respond in interviews online and make your own judgment; consider the new alternative colleges.
What about the redistribution of wealth: what form, what timeline?
"Not the time yet": the system isn't installed, perhaps three years at most for some distribution, with vetting. Don't wait for money: you'll keep waiting, because that's what you project. Act daily as a decent human being; create your wealth now. Don't sell your home to chase gold or build bunkers in fear; in the right frequency, what you need comes when you need it.
