The big idea

A reading of your field is only a probability: it changes the moment your choices change. Consciousness watches its creations like an experiment and rebalances what goes off balance, which is why positive is ten times stronger and evil is now self-reversing. To change a reality that has crystallized into matter, your new signal must be more potent and consistent than the old one, which is why it's hard. In the Q&A the Council said there is always free will, though the bylaws of creation never change; that neutrality as compliance feeds the negative, so you shift between stillness, force and softness as each moment needs; that family contracts can complete and new soul family can come; and that deeply buried patterns may need a capable professional's help.

#Key teachings

#42.1Readings are probabilities

She asks for questions that serve humanity, not personal fortune-telling: any reading shows only a probability in the field, which changes instantly with your decisions. The Council gives a prophecy only when the combined fluctuation has become very potent; positive or negative is your perception of the outcome.

#42.2Consciousness rebalances

Like a scientist restarting a failed experiment, consciousness observes its many self-expressions; when the collective field goes off balance, it rebalances, which is why positive has ten times the potency (see S7 E22). The suffering is real, but it's now self-reversing: people are being activated, and you always have free will in how you react to the mirror image. Division by religion or color is a perception someone programmed; you conquer consciousness by dividing it.

#42.3The new signal must be stronger

What became matter from the etheric (like water's states) must be mutated back: think a new reality, make your thoughts, emotions and actions potent and consistent, and let it crystallize as a new version. Your new signal has to be more potent than the old one, and any expectation still attached to the old belief keeps projecting similar outcomes. That's why it's challenging. Abilities aren't junk DNA; they're dormant and must be reactivated, partly by knowing the true prehistory (see 8.40).

#42.4Each country's trajectory

Each country has its own pulsation (culture, beliefs, emotional sequences), so some will leap forward faster while others clear karma. Her view of the USA: many came fleeing regimes with a heart conversion toward freedom, which amplified a common denominator of freedom in its field (the heart as converter, not pump).

Added note

Added note: her commentary on politics, named countries and groups is condensed; no group of people should be targeted.

#42.5Build community, with research

As the society splits into two trajectories (see 8.41), you'll always find your path to freedom. Before homesteading, research land deeds and back taxes, water, power, and what the municipal office allows. Communities can form where you are: neighbors with yards, rooftop greenhouses, homes clustered around shared space (she cites a pocket neighborhood of homes around preserved trees, made possible by zoning reform), as a country once survived crisis by planting every open space. Water comes first. Check who is building a community and whether you'd be restricted (no chickens, no goats): that can be a control mechanism. Local offices and mayors matter, so get involved.

#42.6Share with solutions

Engage enough to point things out to neighbors and friends; you've projected the frequency, and someday it lights up for them. If people attack, pull back, hold your ground, stay compassionate, and offer help; she found that after two years of being kind, colleagues began asking. Doom without a solution amplifies the negative; the race for clicks and who-knows-more is the archon loop. Be informed, then give the solution. In-person interaction exchanges a different frequency than screens.

#Questions answered

What is the alchemy of emotions, and how do we harmonize it?
See the message above: the alchemy is the conversion of the spectrum. Stabilize your field at zero point with understanding, and your emotion becomes a stable sound frequency that can shift reality precisely.
Is it true that free will exists only on Earth?
"We never said that there is no free will in the universe." Everywhere, beings follow the bylaws of creation (metaphysical and quantum laws of conversion), which are omnipotent and omnipresent and can't be bypassed. But you always have free will in how you express within them: "Without the free will, consciousness cannot exist," since it needs it to create something new.
Are the Earth grids rising through activation of sacred sites?
Activations are happening now. The ancient sites held the planet's timeline like a sonar plasma field; an artificial grid placed above them canceled their frequency, shut down the DNA's memory of prehistory and galactic family, and shifted Earth onto another timeline (a "soul grab" and reset). The new knowledge lets you, by free will, restructure your thoughts and actions toward the new Earth that already pre-exists: a rebirth.
If we've been off balance, should we strive to stay neutral?
No. Neutrality as compliance amplifies the negative. Analyze each moment: sometimes observe from zero; sometimes be forceful without giving up; sometimes be soft (love, silence, pull back, then interject with wisdom), "because sometimes the slightest touch makes the best conversions"; sometimes say "no more" and take physical action. Pulling back from the negative comes after you've learned the protocols. From the stillness of awareness you can shift, like a chameleon, into whichever version each moment needs.
How do we clear the mind so the chakras can function properly?
If something shocks you, remove yourself and "get into your shell" until the emotion subsides into a stillness of understanding; then act. Analyze each situation through the teachings, and study, study, study: tuning into higher-dimensional information changes the sequences in your consciousness.
What is the lesson around family dynamics and karmic contracts? How do we know one is complete?
Observe, and use the protocols daily, knowing everything is perfect even if the reflection shows the opposite; it may convert, faster or slower depending on each person. If nothing changes no matter what you do, permanently pull out: that completes the contract. New people will come and act like family, possibly your soul family from another timeline; go by how you feel, not by titles. Don't become an accomplice to wrongdoing because of blood. You can make a new contract consciously: ask a dear friend, "Do you want to have another lifetime with me?" Letting go of family patterns is the hardest conversion, because it's your most predominant program.
How do we manage buried subconscious patterns that we see only through their effects?
Deep pockets of the subconscious hold permanent imprints and can bring deep depression, so heavy that even the protocols can't be held. Then seek capable people who can help you reach the subconscious (like a doctor or psychologist), so healing from within can start. Lucid dreaming or out-of-body states can reach it but can be hard to return from.
