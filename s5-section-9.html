#The method — Automatic Writing

  1. Shower first: the water clears the energetic field of the day and makes the skin a conductor.
  2. Relax completely, and relax your hand. Use the cheapest, lightest pencil you can find, so the hand moves freely.
  3. Ask a question in your mind, with a positive feeling: mind and heart combine into one sound wave that is picked up by higher dimensions.
  4. Write the first thought immediately: it comes faster than thinking.
  5. If it doesn't sound right: "thank you very much", and try again, and again: you're learning to dial resonances.
  6. Each night, write your future: who you are, your family, your world. Ask for synchronicities to show the next step, without expecting how.
