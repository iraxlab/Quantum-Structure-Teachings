The big idea

Many people ask: "if something negative shows up in my reality, did I do something wrong?" No. Manifestation has a curvature: an expansion that takes a certain momentum to build, because your potency isn't yet strong enough to be instant. What shows up off balance is part of that learning curve: it shows you where to switch. Noticing it is your growth. Shut that program down, switch, and build again in the positive; the more fluently you do it, the faster it collapses. The council then gave the whole protocol in one sequence.

#Key teachings

#16.1Every reality has its own blueprint

Each moment you shift by frequency into a new hologram blueprint, with its own location (see S2 15.4), and print a new version of yourself and of everyone in it. Between two realities the third forms (1 + 1 = 3, see S1 2.2), built by fluctuation. You carry vibrational signals from the previous reality, so you print a similar resonance unless you change it.

#16.2The curvature of time

Though there is no time, manifestation has a curvature, an expansion that needs momentum: you repeat the same emotion to give it potency until the third reality collapses into matter. Too much fluctuation and the outcome may not be what you wanted; lean into doubt and despair and it collapses toward the negative. That curve is "your learning of how you will collapse through time-space".

#16.3It's never a mistake

"When something happens in your reality, don't think that you did something wrong: it's actually the curvature, you learning how to combine your alchemy into the projection of your choosing." Asking about it shows you're aware: without awareness you wouldn't notice you were off balance. Then shut the program down, switch, and collapse again in the positive; that switch can move you into a different timeline altogether. Learning control matters, because a reality popping up that you can't hold would leave you not knowing where you are.

#16.4The protocol, in one sequence

Asked whether shifting to the positive happens through the zero point, the council gave the full sequence. Recognize you're in a holographic matrix: that alone eases the anger. Subside the emotion through action to absolute zero of understanding. Give gratitude to the platform (you can't reject it). Switch to the positive, and in meditation increase the joy of your blueprint to its peak, "almost to tears", vibrating through the whole body. Hold it, then drop it. Know you have it: "if you see it in your hologram, it exists in the structure." Then act daily as if you already have it, without expecting who, how or where, and let the collective do its part (see S1 1–3, S2 16).

#16.5Memory holds you in place

If you're always shifting timelines, why keep memories? So you stay anchored in a reality and can continue the next structure: "if you pop out of the reality, you will not hold the memory, for example, who your husband was, and we don't want that."

#16.6Families, land and nature

A move back to family land for farming was encouraged. Nature and family structure will be part of the healing: living as a unit, with parents, grandparents and children together, gives more potency to come through the changes. This doesn't mean going back to the old ages: new technologies will be there too. As you act, you shift into a cleaner earth, step by step.

#16.7Serving and teaching

Service happens in every moment, in any form. Believing you're never doing enough is an illusion that loops you. "Would you plant flowers for your neighbour? Try that. If it's not what you like, find your passion." To teach well: speak from the centre of your heart, with compassion, no judgement, and give your knowledge without conditions. You broadcast that resonance into everyone, and it can expand beyond what you imagine.

#16.8Growth is given by how you do

There is an order: do well with your tasks here, and your councils and guides give new opportunities to expand. Decisions are made collectively, combining all your parallel selves, not one unit alone. Humans are often eager to rush ahead, "like a little puppy jumping for the bone". On human significance: you are fundamental to the whole, but hold it in neutrality, without ego, since every expression has its own importance.

#The method — The Protocol in One Sequence

  1. Recognize you're in a hologram; let that ease the emotion.
  2. Bring it down to zero through an action (step away, breathe, music).
  3. Give gratitude to what you see: you can't reject your own structure.
  4. Raise the joy of your blueprint to its peak, almost to tears; hold it.
  5. Drop it, and know it exists because you saw it.
  6. Act daily as if you have it, with no expectation of how (16.4).
