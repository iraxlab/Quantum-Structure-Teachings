Added safety note

She stresses that she is not a doctor and this is her own experience. Resting and hydrating through a mild illness is sensible, but never stop prescribed treatment or avoid medical care for a serious, persistent or worsening illness, injury or pain. See a doctor about chronic pain, autoimmune conditions and diet changes, especially for children.
