Season 1 · Section 7

Stop. Transform. Move On.

A three-step protocol for any upsetting moment, holding steady through big changes, and a group water-intention practice (live Q&A)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

A simple protocol for every situation: Stop, Transform, Complete. Stop dead the moment an emotion takes over. Transform it by switching to a heart-felt positive emotion and feeling it. Then treat it as done: no more talking about it, no going back to it. Whatever you keep replaying, you carry into the next reality; drop it 100% and you shift into a new one. It takes practice and devotion, so treat life as a game you are playing well.

#Key teachings

#7.1The protocol: Stop, Transform, Complete

This protocol was given as a new, "universal platform" for manifesting, prompted by getting too upset in a family situation. When you keep reacting to a disturbing moment, you carry its frequency into every next reality you shift into (see 2.7). The formula:

1. Stop. Stop immediately, completely: a dead stop, like zero point (see 2.1). Pure awareness, no reaction. Stop fighting, stop complaining, stop repeating it.

2. Transform. Switch your emotion to the positive, through the heart, and imagine everything is already fine. Like walking from one room into another where everything is beautiful. You have shifted to a different timeline; the fight happened in another reality, so there is no need to keep feeling it.

3. Complete. Leave it alone. It's done, it's already here. No more talking about it, no more going back into the negativity.

Do it at 100%. Even 1% doubt carries some of the old reality's frequency into the new one (see 1.5). Done fully, the other person often just stops too: "You're right, let's stop." Mastered, the same platform becomes the way to bilocate to a completely different reality (see 3.5, 5.5).

#7.2Why letting go is hard, and how to train it

Everyone struggles to let go, wanting the other person to understand, replaying it for hours. That comes from how we were raised to think. The fix is the same for everyone: practice. Every time an emotion runs away, the first word is "Stop." Then: transform it, and let go of everything else.

Emotional people actually have powerful frequencies. Thinking a feeling isn't enough; you have to feel it, because the emotion is the frequency (see 2.6). Train it like an actor on cue. With mastery you only think it and it appears, because you have risen to less dense realities where manifesting is easier. When you keep hitting obstacles, you're still carrying a piece of an old reality. Manifestation is in the now; what happened two seconds ago doesn't matter. (Review Sections 1 and 2 for the full mechanics.)

#7.3When you keep seeing what you don't want

Worrying events in the world are part of the collective ascension process. What you see are different screens within the hologram of your own Earth, and you are shifting through them (see 1.4).

Don't add fearful thoughts such as "maybe my frequency is too low": each one becomes part of your reality. Neutralize with gratitude: "Thank you, I see what's happening" (see 2.6). Then transform: know and feel that everything is changing for the better and is beautiful now. Accept the whole transformation without conditions or expectations, because expectations carry the old vibration forward.

The more you worry, look at it and wonder about it, the more you project it. Turn to the screen you prefer, like the sun or something you love, and keep tuning to it with your emotion until a new timeline crystallizes, drop by drop (see 6.5).

#7.4Know it 100% — and play it as a game

Don't wonder whether you're "in ascension"; that doubt makes you wobble. Knowing is 100%; "I don't know" is 50% or 90%. You can't yet believe you could fly, because years of conditioning haven't let it sink in; it comes step by step. Rewatch the teachings, even five times each, and practise.

When a hard situation comes, don't think "I'm in trouble again". Think: "I'm transforming something. Let's see how well I do." Life is a game in a hologram, and you are the main character. If one window closes, look for another. Playing it with the best of your abilities, from the heart, shifts you into less dense realities, where negative structures can't exist because they don't match.

#7.5Big changes coming: hold steady

More upheaval is expected, with old structures of manipulation crumbling and coming into the open worldwide. Hold your positive vibration, and don't hate the people involved; many are manipulated too (see 2.8). Hold it together, and many people doing this shifts what everyone sees.

Anything not for humanity's benefit will be stopped at every turn. There are infinite probabilities, so plots surface and fail in ways no one can predict, because you cannot go against creation: it preserves itself, and acting against it sends your own actions back to you (see 1.2).

The same applies personally. A teaching example: an outcome was foretold; every effort to change it failed, and once it was let go, it unfolded exactly as foretold, just not in the way anyone expected. If something is meant to be, let it be.

So enjoy your transformation. Even in sadness, say "it's part of the process" and transform it. Stand for what you want to be part of, take physical action that makes you happy, support the people you love, and don't worry about how the money or things will come (see Section 4). Do it from the heart.

From the books"We will actively stop you in every corner," the books say of those manipulating humanity, and "the ones that are disturbing the levels for ascension will be personally extracted from the reality" (DP).

#7.6A group water-intention practice

Asked how to help in a disaster area, the talk describes a group meditation it was taught:

Place a large pot of water at the exact centre of the building (measure to find it). Four people sit at the four directions, north, south, east and west; a circle also works, though the four directions are preferred for geometric reasons. One person reads aloud, slowly, a written description of the reality the group wants; everyone repeats it and concentrates on it, in meditation, for about 10 minutes. In the version taught, the water was first left to stand for about 20 minutes, and afterwards the participants drank only that water for two days.

The teaching is that thought and vibration can crystallize water by creating a plasma, magnetic and gravitational field around it.

From the books"Water has memory and can reconstruct the system of a new platform of structures" (SD, DP).
Important safety note

Treat this as an intention practice, not a way to make water safe. It does not purify or disinfect water. In a disaster area, or anywhere the water may be unsafe, use bottled water or properly boiled, filtered or treated water, and follow local health guidance. Use only clean drinking water for this practice.

#The method — Stop, Transform, Complete

Use it for an argument, a fear, bad news or a replay you can't shake:

  1. Stop. Say it to yourself: "Stop." A dead stop. Zero point. Don't speak, don't react.
  2. Neutralize. "Thank you, I see what's happening." No fearful extra thoughts.
  3. Transform. Switch to a heart-felt positive emotion and feel it, like walking into a beautiful room.
  4. Know it 100%. "This is done. I've shifted." No doubt and no expectations.
  5. Complete. Leave it alone. No more talking about it or replaying it.
  6. Act. Do something physical that makes you happy; support someone you love.
  7. Practise like a game. Every hard moment is a chance to see how well you transform.
