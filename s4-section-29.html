To Awake the Hz to Feel / Protocol

Why imagery needs emotion; the protocol for those who find it hard to feel, by being spontaneous through the day; where it sits in her study path; and questions on calling a galactic aspect, easing into a fast, honesty in conflict, timelines, being monitored and undoing a crystallized timeline (live talk, July 2021)

The big idea

Manifestation needs two things working together: the imagery of the mind, which places the hologram, and the emotion of the heart, a frequency that activates it like a laser. The stronger and longer the emotion is held, the more potent the vibration, until it "pops into matter reality outcome". Some people can't call up the feeling at will. For them she gives a fifth protocol: be spontaneous through the day, asking "what do I feel like doing right now?" and doing it from the heart. That trains the heart to feel, and keeps you living in the now.

#Key teachings

#29.1Imagery and emotion in synchronicity

Emotions are frequencies; frequencies are codes; codes become the geometry of the ether platform of your hologram. When you project a picture without emotion, it has little potency. The emotion is the laser: stronger or weaker, held longer or shorter, it makes the hologram vibrate, grow and crystallize. If you can't tune your emotion, your hologram resonates with something else, "and you're curious why you keep looping of the same similar situations." Love transforms everything because its resonance overrides every program with persistence and precision (see HR on healing from the heart by the power of 10, and S1 1).

#29.2Where this protocol fits

Her study path, in order: Season 1, episodes 1–5 for the basics (and keep going through the rest); the Galactic Meditation and the Protocol to Manifest Potency (S4 9), whose "action" means taking a physical action to make yourself feel positive; the nine-day protocol (S3 8) to shift negative to positive with consistency; and, for those who can't switch into joy at will, this fifth protocol. "Watch it 10 minutes at a time... do not rush."

#The method — To Awake the Frequency to Feel

  1. On waking, notice your emotion. If you feel moody, your higher self is telling you something is off: "Thank you so much for letting me know how I feel"; then shift it toward the positive.
  2. Ask: "What do I feel like doing right now?" Not tomorrow, not later: now. Being spontaneous is the zero point, living in the moment.
  3. Get up and do it at once (the park, for example), with music: something physical, so nothing pulls you out of the focus.
  4. Put happiness into it as you go. Your whole hologram shifts from moody to happy. Take as much of it as you can: "be greedy to accumulate this stuff."
  5. When you feel complete, ask again: "What do I feel right now?" The dollar store, a visit to your mother-in-law, whatever comes. Fill it with joy again.
  6. Chain it through the day. Each now is a new earth; held from the heart, the chain becomes a timeline, and you will recognize the emotion of love so easily that holding it becomes natural.
  7. On work days, find the spontaneous moments: "Thank you, work, for giving me the ability to pay the bills"; listen to a positive podcast or music on the way; drop the ego and talk things out.

The heart is what controls the resonance: "it is the heart that knows the codes and opens the frequencies for the timelines and the portals. That is why I keep saying the heart, the heart, the heart."

#29.3Warriors of the transformation

Anyone who claims that from one day to the next everything will be beautiful in the fifth dimension is wrong: "this is transformation", building new societies from the ground up. "You would not have even been here if your guides did not know that you have that power... When you want to win the war, you get the best of your soldiers on the ground." Be proud to be here, and protect humanity through the heart. (She also pointed to Article 6 of the Universal Declaration on Bioethics and Human Rights, on consent; her other news reports are left out.)

#Questions answered

I feel a being, an aspect of myself, around me. Is she my future self, or am I her avatar?
The name given was Intrea (she also spelled it A-N-T-R-E-A), not the one you heard. Call the name: "Hello, Intrea, I'm here." A name has a sound and frequency, "almost like a phone call to a specific location in a field of reality", which converts into numbers and geometry. Hold a high vibration to connect.
I've been vegan for eight years. Do I still need to ease into water fasting with honey?
Absolutely, and follow the protocol strictly (see S4 23). As you fast, the molecules spin faster and faster; you don't want them to "pop out of orbit", which depletes your nutrients. Have a medical doctor or nutritionist check your blood work: for some, nutrients drop quickly. Juicing is the same for everyone, but the detox may take a year of elimination diet, always with a doctor.
Her instructions: easing down, two weeks at a time

Fish with potatoes and salad, and keep juicing → two weeks without fish: salad, potato, juicing and shake → remove the potatoes, depending how you feel: salad, juicing and shake → only juicing and shake. "I do not ever recommend that being done without a doctor supervision" (see the Detox and Juicing Guide).

Added safety note

Fasting can be dangerous, especially with diabetes, medication, pregnancy, low weight or a history of eating disorders. Only fast under your doctor's supervision.

Can I raise my frequency during a confrontation?
Speak your truth with power, but with honesty, from the heart. Whatever you project now, the next moment will be the same. A lie won't resonate with what truly happened in your field; your heart memory stores the truth, so "if you keep lying, you're going to have tough situations happening."
Why can some people shift dimensions without practices like astral travel or lucid dreaming?
It was meant for them; they have a different task. Some fix realities in other timelines; some travel quietly, downloading information and opening portals. "It's not because they are better... this was by design, by agreement." She had to learn everything step by step so she could teach it.
How do we know when the shift has happened, and whether we're in 3D or 5D?
Both timelines exist now. Know that you already made it, visualize the world as beautiful, and take the action: "you have to create it to see it." "See it to believe it" was the manipulation (see Direct Programming, S4 22, on how the shield was shut down).
How can I tell which timeline I'm on?
Each moment's emotions, thoughts and deeds shift you into a parallel reality; held with consistency in the heart, they become a permanent timeline, each with its own frequency like a radio signal and many realities in it like a symphony (see S1 2, S1 3). As you accumulate knowledge and understanding, you build fractals of a higher resonance: another timeline above the 3D and 5D, where extraterrestrial experiences come. These coexist, which is why a psychic can see what's coming. "Don't skip... all the information is there."
I dream of being monitored. By whom? Are my guides helping me?
Everyone has been monitored, and the negative side was monitored too, by the good side. Some of us are in med beds, projected into the 3D hologram to download information and raise the collective. You are helped by your galactic family, angels and councils, which oversee extraterrestrial platforms "almost like a supreme court". Changing the agreements you signed to come here is decided by you, your councils and your families; your higher self lives in a parallel timeline, perhaps seven-dimensional and up. The shield around the 3D blocked information, but consciousness "dropped" in from extraterrestrial structures was a loophole no negative structure could tap.

Why the darkness? To transform darkness you must know it. Consciousness needs yin and yang in balance; when the negative went out of balance, consciousness said "no more", and plasters the 3D with hologram after hologram of situations so the positive can over-mutate it. You accumulate that through the heart.

Can we undo a negative crystallized timeline with intent and prayer?
Yes. As your potency accumulates, manifestation "shrinks": you put a laser focus from the heart on what wiggles and switch it quickly. But have no expectations of the outcome: expectation plasters a hologram on top of you, and nothing else consciousness offers can get in. "Consistency is the key, and of course the heart."
