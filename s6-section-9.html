Reclaiming Your Sovereignty

The quantum structure of sovereignty: how a thought becomes a hologram, how fractal moments become a permanent timeline, why rejection feeds the negative, and how to override a recurring negative pattern (an abusive relationship, for example) with a more potent resonance from neutrality; with questions on shared realities and whether a fractal timeline ever stops (talk)

The big idea

Every fractal moment you live is positive or negative depending on your thoughts, emotions and actions; similar moments repeated become a permanent timeline. To reclaim your sovereignty from a pattern that has become permanent, you must replace its sound wave with a more potent one: observe it in neutrality, without rejecting or judging (rejection is itself a sound wave that feeds the negative), then act positively and consistently, more times than the negative was given. Once you recognize the pattern, you shut it down instantly and shrink time.

#Key teachings

#9.1When the test comes

She tells of a teacher who left a goldfish to die on a desk and forbade anyone to move: only one student saved it. "Everything is perfect on paper; we are all warriors on paper." When a situation tests you, fear, public persona and ego show up; your true self shows. If you don't step in when something is wrong, you give your sovereignty away, and sovereignty is a holographic timeline.

#9.2From thought to hologram

A thought projects a frequency into your matrix: a hologram in the ether, not yet crystallized. Adding emotion makes it pulsate differently, and emotion plus thought (the magnetic and gravitational field) gives it a positive or negative input. Repeated over and over, it manifests as a positive or negative outcome (S4 E15 on potency and collapse).

#9.3From fractal moment to permanent timeline

Each moment is a picture frame, a new Earth where you reprint everyone. A chain of fractal moments of the same or similar frequency becomes a permanent timeline; it is "constructive" when it has potency. If your common denominator stays positive, the whole timeline's outcome is positive.

#9.4Neutrality, not rejection

You cannot judge or reject the negative: it is part of the yin and yang of consciousness. "I can't believe this!" projects a negative sound wave that inflates the very hologram you dislike. Instead: "I know what you're doing. I understand it's your survival. Let me pull back and create my own reality" (community, land, like-minded people). Awareness is the zero point: stillness, like a black hole, and through it the portal to the next reality. But don't be too nice either: if someone tries to overtake you, stand in neutrality and say "stop right there"; protect yourself.

The message: reclaiming your sovereignty (condensed)

"Relocating in your singular structure in your fractal time frame with consistency of the same pulse sound wave will project and reclaim the previous field." If a constructive projection has become a stable, permanent structure, positive or negative (for example, allowing an abusive spouse to overtake you while you shine at work), "it will be very challenging to restore"; your new sound wave must be more potent. "Matter is only an illusion... a frequency holographic sound wave. It is a live conscious field; it moves and responds to every input of your thoughts... it shifts and mutates at every moment." "To reclaim your sovereignty, you must restructure the recurring frequency code with a much more potent resonance." "You think that to defeat the evil you have to destroy it, but in actuality it is simply overtaking one frequency with another." Do not reject or judge: both have a frequency sound. Observe, analyse, "stay calm and aware... total knowingness," then add new action to replace the old.

#9.5Good and evil are perception

From their side, what they do is survival, as we accept harming animals (she recommends the documentary Earthlings, with parental guidance). You replace a frequency permanently only when you understand the whole concept.

#9.6Protect the children

Children are seeds: if their analytical program is overtaken now (by explicit material, indoctrination), it's very hard later. "You are the advocate." Connect with like-minded families. Your original DNA will be priceless; the hybridization programme preserved pure DNA, and a "sixth race" is coming. People will start seeing ETs, smoothly and without fear; any scary staged event is manipulation.

#The method — overriding a recurring negative pattern

  1. Recognize it: your heart tells you something is off balance: you're suffering.
  2. Observe in neutrality: like watching a film, calm, numb to the drama: "I see it; I get it." No hatred, no judgment.
  3. Put your foot down: "I know what you're doing. I will no longer allow this." It's power, not spite.
  4. Act positively and legally: family, a lawyer, protection; take what is yours with compassion, not revenge.
  5. Out-potency it: if it was given 100 times, override it 120 times, moment after moment, for as long as it takes (sometimes a year).
  6. Shut it down early next time: when you recognize the same frequency in a new person, decline at once. You've shrunk time.

#Questions answered

To manifest a shared reality, must everyone share the same picture of it?
No: you project even the collective. Keep projecting positively and looking for like-minded people (calls, websites, conversations); rejections just mean that resonance isn't yours. When you're potent enough, a new hologram appears where you find them. You change your hologram, not the people.
Does the fractal timeline continue if we stay neutral? Does it ever stop?
You collapse and shift into a new permanent timeline; how fast depends on how precise your actions are. The previous timeline still exists in consciousness; you just no longer see it, because you're above it. Those above (ETs, guides) can see each reality; that is the Akashic record, so ask which version you are reading. You don't see higher realities as matter because their resonance is too different; as you learn to hold it, contact becomes possible.
