The big idea

When she gives a prediction, she gives the end result, which is "100% locked" in consciousness: humanity wins the war, though not every battle. When, how and who stay in fluctuation, because the negative and positive platforms collapse moment by moment, each move answered by the other, like chess. She cannot predict your personal reality: you collapse it with every choice. So a setback is not failure; brush yourself off, restructure, and override the negative with more potent positive action. Nobody can create your reality for you, and blaming the messenger only keeps you stuck.

#Key teachings

#40.1End result versus the path

Both the negative and the positive sides act in their own platforms at once, reality within reality; their fluctuations take different positions in the matrix, each a portal. Who has more power and did more damage or more goodness decides each collapse. The collective result is known; the timing fluctuates (she always allowed "six months more"), and your personal outcome depends on what you choose each moment. She predicted that some states would shut down, but not what any family would do; moving might save one family and not work for another. (See the chess-game talk, S4.) Consciousness also blocks psychics and projects like Looking Glass from seeing the exact future, to preserve itself.

#40.2The strongest of the strongest

You are bombarded everywhere (food, air, children, schools, bank accounts, relationships); "all of us [are] going to be brought to our knees." A plan that didn't work is "a minor setback": it may even have saved you from rushing at the wrong time (she rushed against advice and it didn't work). Your potency must be stronger than theirs: when they move, pull back, analyse, and move again. "If you say there's no other way, you shall not create probabilities"; the one who keeps searching collapses a new reality (the right school appears). The ETs don't heal those who keep choosing harm; that is the lesson and free will.

#40.3Your one person is the universe

Do something physical daily in the name of humanity, even for one person: that one holds the vibration of the whole universe; it expands to the collective and returns to you from somewhere you can't predict. "If you want to be greedy, be greedy to accumulate goodness by giving." Taking without ever giving back can't return to you.

#40.4The trinity of the body and intimacy

Before the frequency was reversed, Earth was more silica and ascension needed no death: you would shift and mutate, as a child grows into an adult. Her diet protocol works, but she fell from it; regaining it needs juicing and vegan steps before water alone. At the level of embedding a new timeline's frequency, intimacy can pause, because merging resonance with another blocks the 100% coherence your heart needs to hold the stargates. That is why spiritual ascension is so hard: it is chosen.

#40.5You will be tested

Groups who meet to do good are tested too, and many fail through ego (who is more important, which day). She was told to keep quiet, observe and not join the committee; it fell apart. You won't know you're being tested: it will "hit you in the face" so you act from your true nature, especially in keeping promises and facing fear. When you've done everything, give the end result to God, "but do not give up to your last breath."

#Questions answered

What was the outcome of the consciousness check from the codes?
The trajectory holds, but the negative is escalating fast: expect pushback in the coming months. For some the codes didn't work, because of fear and despair; many doubted them. "Stop pushing for contact; take care of the global situation first. We cannot appear in the current global state."
Do the codes open us to communicate with our galactic families, such as in dreams?
They push you back on track; they don't create your reality. Contact is individual, when a person fully embeds the frequency: a lucid dream, a brief appearance, a message through someone by agreement. Nothing collective yet. Learn lucid dreaming and automatic writing. It takes about 15 minutes of repetition for something to penetrate; act.
Should we keep using the past codes? Will there be new ones?
Yes, keep using them; new ones come only when the timing is right, without promises of a schedule. Her books are encoded too: read the sentences as written. Codes adjust; they don't change your reality.
How can we defeat the dark if it operates from 5D awareness and we're veiled?
"I never said that." The dark operates in lower resonances and its own timelines; it can never reach 5D. It knows this knowledge and locks you in by what you believe from the screen. Pull away, but learn what they do so you can recognize and transform it.
If everything is simultaneous, why do we meet AI now and not 100 years ago?
It depends on your consciousness and the vibration you hold: two AIs operate (S5 39). On missions, protective technology (nano, devices) is used so you aren't dropped into a war zone without gear, while preserving the original codes; she is still against chips and transhumanism.
