The big idea

Consciousness isn't biased, but it must stay in balance. When it rises to a higher level, everything off balance in you surfaces, not as punishment but so you can choose to transform it, and a structure that only destroys ends up sabotaging itself. The wrong bylaw, "see it to believe it", also told us "there is no cure", and that belief loops; in the quantum there is always a cure, which you create situations to find. Every thought, feeling and action is the code of a portal into your next hologram. And your dreams speak a language made for you: write them down and learn its symbols.

#Key teachings

#3.1Consciousness balances itself

Consciousness measures by density, the yin and yang, which must stay in balance (see S3 2.2). Keep choosing the same negative, and your hologram's density drops until it reflects only negativity back. Even practices like dark magic have their own bylaws (taking frequency without permission, "order through chaos"), but consciousness is unbiased. When it decides to rise, everything off balance pops up in front of your face, so you have the choice to transform it: "It's not a problem. You accumulated this; balance it so you can move to the next level" (see S2 18.1). A structure bent only on destruction ends up sabotaging itself through its own hologram (see S2 27.2).

From the booksSD: "If you do not change your perspective from (−) to (+) your higher self of existence will sabotage your hologram momentum so you can wake up from your disillusions."

#3.2There is always a cure

We were given the wrong bylaw: "see it to believe it". Along with it came "there is no cure", and believing it, you project a reality where you keep searching and looping. "There's no such thing as no cure; there's always the cure in a quantum structure." Know that the cure exists and that you're healed, feel it ("now I'm healthy, now my child is healthy"), and at the same time create situations to find it. You won't know how, where or from whom. Instant healing is possible if you're ready, but a lesson shared with others (a child's, say) can't be skipped: you go through the process of finding it, knowing you're already healed (see S2 14.3).

Added note

Holding this knowing goes together with medical care; never stop or refuse treatment because of it.

#3.3Every act is a portal

Each time you project a frequency with an action, you create a portal with its own code (hertz) and move through it into another hologram that holds that vibration, reprinting everyone in it. So "even if you talk to one person, do the best of yourself from the heart": that person is your whole universe in that moment, and your next hologram is built from what you sent (see S3 1.5, S1 14.2).

#3.4Your dream language

Dreams are "designed specifically for you". A dream of something you never sought (being a model, say) may be another timeline of you; if it's a bylaw, it will come through synchronicity, and when it does, reality splits and you'll see the version you act on. A window in a dream is a threshold, a new beginning. Write dreams down, and note the colours, light or dark, the smell, who was there; over time you'll learn your own symbols (for the teacher, gold and jewellery came before generous gifts).

#3.5Families and karma

Do family patterns pass down? "All of the above." When you accumulate an imbalance (harming your family, say), your guidance asks how you want to correct it, and agreements are made before birth: someone agrees to play the villain, or a child comes to help you grow, so you learn how it feels. As the ascension protocol moves you into a heart-based timeline, whatever you accumulated surfaces to be cleared now.

Added note

"Karma" here isn't a reason to stay in harm. If you're being hurt, seek safety and support.

#3.6Apparitions and entities

Entities seen in a house are consciousnesses that chose separation and live in another density of belief. From your higher perspective you chose to experience that reality, so hold your neutrality, without judging or fearing it: fear is also a perception that imprints a hologram. You dissolve it not by rejecting it but by transforming it (see S2 13.4).

#3.7Paradoxes

When every idea seems to turn into its opposite, it isn't duality: "it's the synchronicity of this dimension." Things occur according to how you project them, with infinite probabilities, so let it flow.

#3.8The Earth's own message

A message said to come from Earth, which gave its fifth-dimensional name as Aria: drilling and draining it creates pockets that collapse when the plates move; the oil helps the plates slide (see S2 21.10). A recent earthquake was read as natural, with smaller tremors to follow. The planet is moving to a new level: big positive changes are said to begin within 3 to 30 months, and the next 20 years build a new reality. A coastline long predicted to flood (see S2 24) is "no longer happening": the timeline changed.

From the booksDP: "The new 5 dimensional name of the earth that she chose is ARIA"; she "will sabotage all negative input in my veins. So the gas stays in my body to lubricate the shifting of the platonic plates."

#The method — Learn Your Dream Language

  1. Keep a notebook by the bed.
  2. On waking, write first, before you move or think (see S2 17).
  3. Note the details: colours, light or dark, smells, who was there, how you felt.
  4. Watch what follows over the next days.
  5. Connect symbols to outcomes: over time your own key signs emerge.
  6. Trust them: they're designed for you, not from a dream dictionary.
