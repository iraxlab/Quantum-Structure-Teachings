The big idea

Each moment is one unit: challenged, you choose positive or negative, and that choice collapses with the previous one into a third unit (1 + 1 = 3, which is still one). A day's units collapse into the day's result, days into the week, weeks into the year. Because you can choose differently at any moment, every outcome is a probability, for you and for the whole planet's collective. That's why predictions shift by months. Yet humanity making it is 100%, because consciousness never self-destructs. Who, how and where depends on each person's daily choices.

#Key teachings

#6.1How probabilities are collected

Recall S1: three similar vibrations make a molecule that expands and collapses; repeating the same emotion, thought and deed builds a timeline. Now: through day one, each time you are challenged and choose to raise your frequency from the heart ("I'm a hologram... thank you so much", then act), that moment collapses positive. If at 3 p.m. you stay in the negative, you loop. At the end of the day all the units collapse into one: positive or negative, depending on which you collected more of. Day two may end negative, day three positive; the week collapses into whichever is more potent. "That could be for the day... a year... one second." Then a new challenge comes, and if you drop, you lower into another timeline with its own sound and geometry: "your prediction could be completely different."

#6.2Why predictions are probabilities

The Council and the ETs measure, in real time, the potency of the whole planet's collective, billions of individual fluctuations. So a prediction comes with a window "six months before and six months after." They avoid naming places and dates, because if a probability shifts, the information would lose credibility, and people abandon the whole protocol over one missed detail. "The prediction of us making it is 100%... But who, how and where is going to be a probability." Mainstream media lowers the collective's frequency on purpose, even with staged events (role players are hired), so the collective drops to their resonance. "You have to be accountable for your manifestation."

#6.3Potency, and the heart

Repeating the same positive frequency makes a sound so strong "it can break through anything." Transformation is in the now: past and future collapse into it, and in the now both the negative timeline (which didn't make it) and the positive exist (see S5 1). The heart's frequency holds the codes of the portals; each timeline has its own geometry. The negative side can't pass through the heart's portals, so it uses technology to change your DNA: with the chakras and immune system shut down, you can't process the higher frequencies. Choosing a deception for money is a conscious decision, and it accumulates. HR was never edited because its vibration had to stay in that resonance.

Her claims that medicines or products change DNA or shut down chakras are her views, not established fact. Please make health decisions with your doctor.

#6.4The five-year window

There is a fractal window of transformation, for example five years. If someone keeps collapsing negative through it, converting becomes twice as hard and needs far more positive action to overtake it. When a person truly wakes, the ETs and higher consciousness help, "so-called miracles". The dark side too will be given a parallel timeline to clear its karma (see DP). She is kept away from details about the negative and off-planet matters she can't prove.

Guides and negative thoughts. Practise automatic writing and lucid dreaming. Telepathy is like a phone call: sometimes you reach a negative entity. "Thank you so much, I understand, but I'm going to think positive": raise your emotion, hold the positive frequency, which the negative can't hold; if the thought comes 10 times, override it 20 times. Guidance proves itself: ignore it and you meet the negative situation. Watch what children absorb: films can carry sacrificial themes in plain sight. "I don't have a choice" drops your frequency and builds the block; imagine the solution and construct it.

#Questions answered

Can we raise potency by asking our galactic neighbours, and contact them?
Fluctuate into the positive by your choices and actions until it's potent; believe 100% you're already connected, because in another fractal you already are. Practise lucid dreaming and automatic writing; live a Marina day; the heart opens the portal. No expectations of when.
If I can redirect my reality, why be patient?
It's a learning process: shift all at once and you'd pop out, "then why did you come here?" You're here to build the potency for those still trapped, in the prison matrix of 3D.
If our chakras were shut down as children, how do we reactivate them?
Consciousness sends ripples of activation through every timeline; everyone gets a positive experience that shows something doesn't make sense, again and again. Then you must choose to act. "Nobody's left behind. They decided to let themselves behind"; some chose to exit and help from the ether (see S4 38).
Should I stay quiet about what I want to manifest?
Not necessarily: a shared intention with others makes it more potent. Telling someone negative toward you may not be wise, but you can still manifest it.
