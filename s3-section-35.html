The big idea

Around the planet's hologram are portals, gates between realities, and they had been manipulated to hide layers of timelines. A new wave of light and sound is cleansing fear and pain, and a three-layer download was given to help. But nothing you see, from your room to the news, has power over you without your consent: it's a holographic imprint. Trust the process, hold the heart, and you keep ascending.

#Key teachings

#35.1Portals around the planet

Around the holographic imprint of the earth are many portals or gates, "like zones", for moving from one reality or location to another, out into the galactic structure. Passing from one portal to another must be allowed: some open by frequency, some by resonance. Super technology had been used to manipulate them and to hide "layers of layers of parallel realities of timelines". This is why the quantum structure was taught first, so that this doesn't sound foreign (see S3 21.5).

From the booksFrom her books (DP): "in every system in the galaxy there is a Stargate"; your energy moves easily "through the new timeline reality star gates" when you match their frequency.

#35.2A baptism of light and sound

"You have been given the choice to see", and from there to be reborn. The message called it a baptism, "not in water, but in the new frequency resonance wave of light and sound. That new wave will cleanse you of all your fear, all your pain, so you can start your new beginning." "Have faith not in us, but in your power: God you are." Everyone is being given an individual waking-up; consenting to manipulation after seeing it is a choice of timeline. Stay neutral; humanity wins in the end.

#35.3The three-layer download

Given first for one person whose connection was "slightly blocked from the left side of the DNA" (the advice: more detox, more trust in your channelling, relax in nature), then to everyone watching, as a gift after the fear of recent days.

The download (condensed)

Unblocking: seven layers of the DNA, a signal of three layers, 331; cleaning frequency three, cleaning structure 20.1113, three structures.

Layer one: 1173 3314, reversed, combines with the DNA and RNA in one structure. Layer two: 77331, structure one, converts to 11378999, God particle inverting. Layer three: signal 11111, repetition 1111, repetition 1111, stop.

"It sustains only if you stay in your heart structure." Given as a very small sequence, it won't build "as an avalanche" until you consent; the more you act from the heart, the more it lets you slide through situations "with absolute ease".

#35.4A picture has no power

How do you stand your ground without reacting? Everything you see, even the room around you, is a holographic imprint: ether resonance combining into a magnetic and gravitational field dense enough to be matter. "You are simply floating in the air of the matrix, creating by thoughts." "To give a power to simply a picture, isn't it kind of funny?" What you see on television is someone producing a frequency along a narrative; it becomes your reality only when you consent and keep tuning in. "You are like babies that need to learn to walk, and there's nothing wrong with that."

#35.5Catching signals like a radio

Images, music or a voice on waking, a knock at the door when you're alone: other realities co-exist with yours, slightly above your structure, and you're picking up their signal "almost like a radio". Of the many, one is more potent, usually the closest to your own resonance. Meditate and trust the process; in time you'll hear whole messages. Stay in the heart, or you won't be allowed to download from heart timelines. That's also why different people receive different outcomes: each gets a fractal of a reality.

#35.6Healing old fractures

The child who was hurt lived on a different earth, a different version of you (see S3 33.10). If you keep feeling that memory, you pull its resonance into now, and it builds, even into depression, "for something that happened to another version of you". Understand that and healing is "instantaneous": "you're already healed". When the emotion returns, redirect your attention by taking action on something positive; repeat until your consciousness stops picking up that old frequency.

#35.7You're already ascending

"It never stops. Do you stop thinking or feeling? It's the same." Don't be too eager: the fluctuation is used by collapsing everything to zero point, and that happens in stillness, not while worrying about the next moment, which lowers your vibration. "Trust yourself that you are already there, even if you don't see it"; acting builds the potency that shifts you into the 5D reality. Give your sovereignty to no one.

#The method — Standing Your Ground Without Reacting

  1. Notice the picture: a scene, a screen, a memory.
  2. Remember it's an imprint: it has no power until you consent (35.4).
  3. Withhold consent: "this is not my reality."
  4. Go still: collapse the emotion to zero (35.7).
  5. Redirect your attention by doing something positive (35.6).
  6. Trust the process; act again tomorrow.
