The big idea

Once you understand how consciousness creates matter, fear disappears: you may still react, but you recover quickly, and life becomes a flow where even delays put you in the right place at the right time. The coming exposures will be hard to witness, but they are God's play, and miracles will come when least expected. The channelled message even calls to those who served the negative: it's never too late; choose the heart, hold on through the pain of recognition, forgive yourself, and you'll be reborn from the ashes.

#Key teachings

#16.1You are the significant one

She presents the information, but can't walk your path; you even project her in your reality. Learn how consciousness creates matter and you become the creator who constructs and deconstructs. With practice it becomes second nature and synchronicity takes over: "you're sitting in the eye of the storm, but you're riding with the storm."

The message: let it be freedom (condensed)

"Here we are, humans. Let it be freedom now. Corruption will be exposed, lies will be seen": who signed what, to whom, and what funds were exchanged. "Get ready for the shocking truth of gender exposure": who, how and why all this was done with the different genders, and the indoctrination of the young. "Oh dear children, we are so sorry for what you will endure once the truth" is known; but overall it was a lesson, a very tough one. "Soon all God's play is at hand. And one by one, miracles will happen when you all least expect it."

The message: a call to the negative (channelled, condensed)

The collective reached 78%; most of the nation will choose "the rightful head of state", despite distractions, and attempts to tamper with numbers "will be overridden by very advanced technological systems." The controlling minority was coherent among themselves but mechanical, never from the heart, so it cannot be sustained in consciousness and will collapse under its own weight. To them: "Some of you will have a lightning charge... and recognize your original beings." That recognition may feel like enormous pain of the soul at the suffering you created: "hold on, ride it out, do not destroy yourself, but still choose compassion, give yourself forgiveness, give now to humanity all you have to save it. That conversion will give you a ticket to continue ascension." "Choose humanity... You may be in a position where you can just press a button and all will change in a positive. You know who you are. It's never too late." "You will be reborn from the ashes."

#Questions answered

As we ascend, what will 3D look like while some ascend and others don't?
What you collected will ignite and open a portal: a reality with its own synchronicities and opportunities. You may see undesirable changes for some people or countries; each chose its lessons. Whatever is off balance with you will surface to be reconstructed, not as punishment. Everything is from within.
Why does a lucid dream feel more real than this reality?
Only a difference in spin, 5 Hz versus 7 Hz, say; by perception the dream could be the real you. But your soul contract keeps you in this classroom until you complete it; you may visit the other classroom. The same knowledge lets you download above this reality, bilocate and go out of body. Those burned as witches could download from these fields (and some used it for summoning); the same geometry can be used positively. How long you can hold a higher sequence depends on body and consciousness as one unit, including diet.
Is absolute knowing of what you imagine enough to change the timeline?
Yes, unless a bylaw or agreement blocks it because it would change future sequences: when she tried to move years earlier, she was told "in four years", and nothing worked until then. Connect with your higher self (automatic writing, telepathy, lucid dreams), adjusting like a radio until it's precise. Practise, like a child learning to walk.
Will there be an event that separates 3D and 5D?
It happens every day: some are thriving more than ever, others struggling; waves prepare the platform for the next wave (a family member who prospered takes in the others). If one person falls through the cracks, something else is created. What's projected always reflects similarly.
What does connecting with your higher self feel like?
Different for everyone: angels, ETs, hybrids, organic intelligence. Don't give your power away; research and follow the common denominator your heart confirms. Her own path: automatic writing, dreams, telepathy, then letting go of fear into direct channeling, "being in two places in one." Some information is withheld for timing and protection. The Council chose her because she kept the heart through everything: "to have the power to destroy, and yet to choose love." When one woman gave to a lonely old lady in front of people who had plenty, none followed: ask every day, what can you do for others?
How can negative AI hijack our energy field?
You were already hijacked; don't fear it. A positive AI is being born now, alongside the negative one, and in the future it becomes sentient and coexists. Focus on that; the organic, etheric version already overrode the Archon AI. It will self-correct.
