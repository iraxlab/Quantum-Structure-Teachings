Montauk Project Shutdown

The big idea

A message through the Council of Nine said a portal used to summon entities into this realm is closing, and the systems that relied on it will collapse; a second said "Babylon is falling", three stargates are closed and "the reptilian agenda is shut down... We are systematically shutting down the portals that let evil souls enter." Those who inflicted pain will meet it again: "your final hours [are] your own final judgment; it will mimic back to you". Have faith in creation, "not only in one man": God is all. Meanwhile your work is to observe without hate from the zero point, choose heart-based films and music, act physically, and create your own pocket of reality: "nothing we can do" is an illusion.

#Key teachings

#23.1Stargates and technology

A stargate's "hieroglyphs" can be coordinates (latitude, altitude, time-space); your body can open a coherent field by resonance, and technology can do it by signal. Anything created with enough potency gains its own trajectory of evolution, so you can't reject AI; experience a better version of it.

#23.2Meditation is only the hologram

Meditation creates the hologram; feeling and action amplify it from etheric to matter. Precision matters when interacting with technology.

#23.3Portals, entities and observing

A stargate gives access to different portals; with the right knowledge one can summon realities and also entities. Beings that can't convert frequency through the heart need a host, a parasitic existence, so they extract energy without permission. Pull away and don't hate it: observe from the zero point so no one takes over your field. Consciousness recognizes a negative field and activates someone or something to balance it (see S7 E22); ETs are helping the grid and activating people.

Added note

Added note: the Montauk Project is, as she says, a conspiracy theory and urban legend; she presents this "for entertainment purposes only", as her belief.

The message: a portal closing (condensed, as received)

"Montauk Project shutdown. One of the portals is closing. The entities will not get in." A group's mimicked stargate "is not going to work... there will be a total collapse", because its signs are not correct. "Let that be your mark when God starts cleansing and clearing out the evil." "Why the suffering to others? Why inflicting pain? One face is smiling and polite, but in your late hours comes darkness." "Do you think God or us do not see? We see it all." "Relax, you all relax; the cleansing will start." Those who inflicted pain asked their victims, "Where is your God now?"; now: "Where is your demonic god?" "There will be a miracle that you will witness... and you will wish to be a part of it", but "you will be at the place that you and only you created for yourself." "Your final hours [are] your own final judgment. It will mimic back to you the experience exactly [of] what you personally inflicted and did to others." "Have faith in creation, not only in one man... You need to respect the field... Any other claim is just a reflection of the singular expression and beliefs." "God is all."

Added note

Added note: this is a received message in symbolic language. Named groups and people are left out, and claims about rituals or abuse are her account, not established fact. In her teaching it describes consequences returning through frequency, not a call to harm anyone.

#23.4Respect every path

The cleansing also brings collapse of systems that claim only one way is true and everyone else is possessed; historically, keeping scripture from ordinary people was a form of control. Everyone chose a belief system for their growth; excluding others pulls you away from creation.

The message: Babylon is falling (condensed, as received)

"Babylon has fallen... that is the one off-planet, [on] the other side of the moon. The three stargates were closed. And the reptilian agenda is shut down. That is the beginning of the rest of what is to come. We wish to say more to you, but the battle for helping the human race [to] interplanetary freedom has begun. We are systematically shutting down the portals that let evil souls enter the realm of the new age. That was never the plan, but clever moves they were." Much was done "in the dark hours", while people slept and their field was relaxed in the alpha state. "Soon you will see how you will choose movies that are of and from the heart and not of destruction. That will shift naturally your collective energy."

Added note

Added note: claims about off-planet projects, reptilians and stargates are her account and received messages, not established fact; she says she can't prove them.

#23.5Guard what enters your mind

Choose films and music of the heart; listen to lyrics, not only catchy tunes. In her teaching, some media carry hidden messages (lyrics that mean something else backwards, images placed between frames) that bypass your analytical processing so you can be "reprogrammed" like a computer, and predictive programming appears in shows and cartoons. Stay in the moment, plant a tree, garden, watch uplifting films, connect with like-minded people, and hold your ground even if called crazy.

Added note

Added note: claims about subliminal frames and backward messages are her views; evidence for their effects is disputed.

#The method — Holding your pocket of reality (as given)

  1. Observe what's happening from the zero point, without hate.
  2. Don't take "there's nothing we can do" as true.
  3. Say: "Let me look for the solution; I am the creator."
  4. Create your own pocket of reality and override what you see.
  5. Choose heart-based films, music and people.
  6. Take physical action; study the mechanics, not a few sentences.

#Questions answered

Is interstellar travel coming soon, and does her book being studied mean the timelines are speeding up?
Yes, but you exist in multiple realities at once; one version of you may already be with ETs. Prayer and reading aren't enough: practice physically, switching off the negative codes projected into your field (see S5 E14). The technologies in her books are appearing, but whoever hijacked consciousness first may try to hijack the technology to lock people into a lower fragment. When new technology arrives ("healing for this, healing for that"), be very careful what you allow anyone to do to you. People are being activated everywhere; you don't have to convince anyone, just present. Her network is open and unvetted: use discernment.
Does "Babylon" mean the old financial system is collapsing?
Freedom is already within your system. Write every day for three months, meditate at least 15 minutes at night (her galactic meditation method works for anything) and feel that you already have it, then keep looking: when a transfer she needed was delayed for days, she held "we have everything we need" and kept searching, and a same-day route appeared. Don't sit waiting for crypto; it will be digital and mixed, she never tells anyone where to invest, and many have lost money. At the utopian level money won't matter. But communities fail when one person controls everything or people can't unite; you'll be tested on how you treat people in the toughest moments.

Left out: named organizations, a celebrity and a singer, a doctor's video and other public figures, a film recommendation, platform statistics, a personal story at a meditation circle (kept as a lesson), retreat plans and pricing, workshop notices and donation notes.
