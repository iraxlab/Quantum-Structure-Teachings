The big idea

Consciousness lets every particle choose its position in time and space: that is free will. But when free will constructs negative input all the time, it becomes a whirlpool, a black hole of "vampiric" energy that only takes and never gives back, "like a big cancer". Consciousness must correct it: it activates new realities, new people and new blueprints that bombard that structure until it shuts down, while the gap between negative and positive widens daily. So the plots won't take momentum. Your soul is consciousness expressing itself: an oversoul that reprints itself up to twelve times, and in the new evolution the 12 strands double to 24.

#28.1Be clever in a war zone

The cartel has infiltrated every chain of command "like an octopus", and one wrong signature can bring trouble. Be careful whom you trust with your papers and money; study a great deal; question who local officials are connected to. Counties operate as corporations. There is nothing wrong with wealth as long as you act from your heart; but waiting creates more waiting (S5 25). Propaganda erases history so each generation can be told anything; that is why many turn to private schooling and unschooling. Expect pushes for war and fear to keep you in the matrix. "The pull for power is not strong. It is crumbling from within. So many are awakened and do not wish to cooperate. Did you all forget that consciousness reacts as one power?... frequency activation... will ripple... through every single particle." To the negative: "Stop at once and do not think that you will be allowed to continue the charade. The only one that you will hurt is yourself. Your hunger for power, selfishness... self-righteousness, without the power of love, will stop... because the true conversion of everything starts from within the heart... It is dark, muddy, and full of rust... Do not think you will start war; it will not take a momentum. It will stop in its tracks, we promise you... It is not allowed. It never was."

The message: the bylaw of free will

"In consciousness there are laws... one of them [is] to allow every expression of [a] particle to choose... its position in a matrix of time and space. So this free will... creates its own reality. However, when this free will construct[s] negative input in time space at all time... it becomes like a black matter, and is [a] whirlpool hole that is continuously sucking energy without giving back... a never-ending energy pool that becomes stronger and stronger of its own energetic force... like a big cancer... and consciousness has to correct it."

#28.2Yin-yang or black hole

A sound wave spirals; seen from above it forms the yin-yang of two opposite spins, balanced, because what you give returns like a mirror image (S5 25, S5 26). Collapse negative all the time and it becomes one inward whirlpool, a black hole that can't comprehend anything because the heart structure is gone; the heart is what converts collapses and opens portals. It becomes a matrix within the matrix. Consciousness answers by activating multiple holographic imprints in other locations (including people in that reality, woken by ETs) that bombard it through the heart structure to overwrite it. In her dream, that darkness was so thick it choked the voice: it feeds on evil and "feels good" doing it, because it knows nothing else.

#28.3Even the negative can restart

Those in the negative who wake up ("what have I done?") and start acting in the name of humanity are given a timeline to begin again from zero, back to source, and restructure. Many inside have woken and now fight from within. Don't fear; maneuver physically around what they try, and stay in the knowing that you are the one particle and the rest is illusion. Share honest lawyers and doctors who truly practise for humanity; that road is the toughest, and it is how leaders are separated from followers. "Every little act from the heart... expresses within the collective."

#Questions answered

Will the universal laws explain the nature, origin and essence of the soul?
More will come, but she downloaded this live. Consciousness began as sound: the OM, "one tonality" of infinite sub-tones, "like a million sounds at the same time". From it came the trinity structure, then the one particle that expands and collapses. Each time it reprints into another reality, the new particle becomes aware of itself ("who am I?"), creates realities, and interacts with itself. The first in the human was the thought; its magnetic and gravitational field became matter at its location; in denser realities it took other forms. The soul is a thought that reflects itself in individual fractal moments until it becomes a specific pulsation in a timeline. The oversoul reprints versions of itself, up to twelve times of similar vibration (a Pleiadian, a human, an Arcturian): "that's what they call 12 DNA." New: in the next evolution "it's going to double itself... 24-strand DNA." A third, the coherent resonance, opens portals through the heart. "Consciousness itself is a soul."

You don't receive love because you don't allow it. Don't let ego refuse help given from the heart; it's not weakness, it's another version of you choosing to give. Reject it and you keep looping in similar situations. A repeated pattern with partners shows who is not for you; but hating all men after one bad one shuts the program when consciousness offers something good. Express love too, without letting anyone step on you: be soft and giving, and "a heart of steel" when you must stand your ground. That is yin-yang balance.
