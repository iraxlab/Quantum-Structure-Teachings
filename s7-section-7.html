New York-Boom Boom / Candle In The Dark

Why consciousness sends its warriors where something goes off balance, why a technology is like a knife, a repeat of the "candle in the dark" prediction plus a new unrequested message about New York given in outline, how to act if something happens (it's a hologram: help), why what you watch and listen to matters, and questions on raising your frequency and the purpose of nanomaterials

The big idea

Consciousness never lets itself self-destruct: like the body sending cells to an inflammation, it activates its warriors where things go off balance. You'll be placed, one way or another, where you can help. Technologies are like a knife that can cut an apple or wound, so the task is coexistence with limits. The prediction from 7.5 is repeated and a second, unrequested message speaks of a shock in New York (or perhaps another city) that would wake people up; it is a probability that has grown, not a certainty. If anything happens, don't panic: treat it as a hologram, play the game, look for the solution and help with water, food and shelter. Be careful what you watch and listen to, because sound and imagery can program you.

#Key teachings

#7.1Consciousness sends its warriors

When any part of consciousness goes against itself, like a cancer cell, consciousness creates situations, activates its warriors and projects holograms to stop it, the way the body sends special cells to an inflammation. Everyone will help at some point, whether it's a neighbor or a dog in the street; some are already prepared to catch others when they fall.

#7.2A technology is like a knife

The same knife cuts a beautiful apple to share or destroys. What's created in consciousness becomes part of the structure; don't reject it, learn to coexist and regulate it, while preserving the body. Those who can't restructure their own heart and DNA must turn to technology to survive.

#7.3How a prediction forms

There is no fixed prediction, only probability: each side acts and layers its resonance back and forth until one becomes predominant, collapses through zero point, and produces the outcome. A message repeated a second time means that probability is taking a stronger momentum. She still holds faith that it can shift.

The message: "New York boom boom" (condensed, as received)

Banks: "Humanity will resolve the differences with the banks... humans will collaborate globally"; leaders will be replaced rather than systems collapsing, for a smooth transition. "New Yorkers will rise up; money will come from the top penthouses." "Not one deception will fly by... All will be exposed... You will be the news." "Build a wall of strength." "It was meant to deceive you... but no one will suffer from the deception, you will all see through it... The only way you will stop it when you face it." Then words about a shock at midnight, debris in canals, silence, disbelief and suspicion of the "streaming TV of lies", and plotting in underground corridors. She hopes it will be stopped, and says it may refer to another city.

Added note

Added note: this is a received prediction of a possible attack, kept in outline. It is not a certainty or a warning from any authority. Rely on official emergency information, and don't let fear take hold.

#7.4If something happens: it's a hologram

Treat it as a virtual-reality game. Pull yourself together and ask, "What can I do now in the name of humanity?" Once, close to a disaster, she kept telling herself "I cannot be afraid, it's just a hologram", then spent every day helping with food and shelter. Prepare sensibly. Even from far away you can help: send money, help a neighbor.

#7.5Face it to transform it

A negative hologram that keeps attacking can't be hidden from; it exists in the field. If you hide behind the bushes, the hologram feeds your field with negativity through people, music, shows and rituals. Face it and transform it.

#7.6Watch what you take in

Some music carries subtonalities that shift your brain's alpha frequency; spectacles can act as rituals; single hidden frames can deliver subliminal messages your subconscious picks up. Hypnosis shows how powerful suggestion is. As with any object used in a spell (see 6.34), don't expose yourself or your children to what summons such energies.

Added note: claims about migration, specific events, public figures, entertainers and court cases are left out here. Her personal stories of escaping trafficking as a young woman are summarized only as a warning to be careful.

#Questions answered

How do we reach a higher frequency and use it to help others in the Great Awakening?
Study the quantum structure from Jump Start for Beginners and Season 1. In a crisis, the way to raise your frequency is to help through the heart however you can. Don't panic: see it as your hologram, which means the solution is in it, so play the game and look; sometimes someone else in your hologram finds it. Fear lowers the vibration; control it, and you create a reality within the reality: water, food, shelter, the right people, being the beacon.
At five I had recurring dreams of seeing nano-structures as if under a microscope. What was that about?
She can say little. Nanotechnology is a tiny box that can carry functions; it can heal or operate inside the body and can be pulled out again with frequency through super-technology ("if anybody tells you that they cannot be pulled out, this is not true"). Like an aspirin overdose, anything can be used for good or harm. Many brilliant scientists were used without knowing. See her NT book.
