What Is to Come / New Systems

The Council of Nine's picture of the new systems to come: small, local and personal; care for rescued children; painless medicine through the "ultramind"; and questions on guides versus our own thoughts, non-verbal children, the super mind, protection and starseeds (live talk, October 2021)

The big idea

"The hologram is changing for the better... Freedom will be won little by little from the ground up." The new systems will not be one system for all, but small, local and personal: care in small clinics, governance in small committees one town at a time, rescued children healed in loving families. Many of these are already starting; "when you start researching... this reality is already here." Their job, the Council says, is only "to give you a little push": you create it to see it.

#Key teachings

Guidance she received. About applying for status as an American state national, she was told: "wait three years"; too much is happening, and one mistake could go wrong. Learn your rights instead; peaceful non-compliance works (she cited an airline's staff whose refusal succeeded). Look at what is already being built: blueprints for cheap self-sufficiency machinery, family-doctor memberships with home visits and affordable lab tests, vitamin infusion clinics. "Never take anything that somebody just told you": have a doctor assess your own body, and for any detox, medical supervision and blood work, including allergy tests.

The message: what is to come

"The hologram is changing for the better, the collective human consciousness is waking up. Freedom will be won little by little from the ground up. And when it builds up, a massive strength will be from the political sectors": once politicians see people standing up, they will no longer act from fear. "The vicious synthetic DNA regeneration of negative inputs... all will be stopped." (Signs and dates she was shown, a gold coin linked to crypto, and roles for particular politicians, are left out; she stressed they were unclear probabilities.)

New systems: more personal care, "individual, more private care in little specks of clinics"; homes cheap again; a system "between democratic and sovereignty into the republic", taking the best of both; "for the people, for the people, from the people, this new governing will grow as you all go"; "coordinative sectors of small command systems in sovereign cities"; new interplanetary systems already operational; the birth of innovation, disclosure, suppressed technologies, entrepreneurship; "small committees in the small communities, one town at a time", big towns in "fractal governing", "not one system for all". "Diversity is best for competitive and innovation growth."

The children: those rescued from abuse will be placed with families or single parents who receive support, "so love to be recognized to heal the soul", because the heart recharges the DNA and unlocks the timelines; not institutions.

#42.1Healing through the ultramind

The Council described future medicine: homeopathic and nutritional health centres; stem cells used to grow organs; transplants "with no anesthetic, but multi holographic laser impulses" to a specific area of the brain, putting the person "in an ultra state of mind" with no pain (a small child could be put into a deep, restful sleep). The laser acts on the synapses of the left hemisphere "connected with adrenaline", which she heard as the "adrenome": brought to "a state of peace and rest", "it causes the cells to rest and self restore and regenerate." That state is the ultramind, "that can control the subconscious state of a being". The negative side knew it and used frequency to lock the ultramind, splitting personalities through pain (she named MK-Ultra). With the new methods it can be restored; the original blueprint is held not only by extraterrestrials but by your own ultramind and oversoul (see S4 33). Deep trauma may need technology's help to reach it. And "the hidden mind"? "No more info will be given... consciousness always has ways to restore and rebuild itself."

#Questions answered

Can the Council talk about super technologies we're not aware of?
"Flat out no." What may be given is in HR, in fractals; everything will work through frequency, sound, vibration, holograms and five-dimensional organic A.I. (Scientists asking her to download someone else's invention: no.)
How can we tell whether it's our guides speaking, or our own thoughts?
"There's never right or wrong, it simply is." A thought is downloaded from the ether around you; it becomes reality only if you put intention and focus on it, as in the slit experiment, and believe it 100%, because belief is also a frequency that makes it grow. If a negative thought comes: "thank you for letting me know", and try again. Practise until the signal is clear (see S4 31's method).
How can we connect more with non-verbal children?
A newborn is a sponge, its brain a circuitry built from what it receives; a glitch in the synapses, or fear at birth, can interrupt it. Give children positive sound, imagery and languages from birth ("by the age of two" several languages), not programmes of non-verbal sounds only. To restore a frightened child, repeat positive imagery and loving expression again and again: "15 minutes" of repetition crystallizes a bridge between synapses. Each child's trauma is different: make an individual plan, in a family rather than an institution.
Can we tap into the super mind without technology?
As the quantum manifestation becomes fully embedded, you tap into it; her own instant downloads come from it, within what you're ready for. "You are already the super mind"; the protocols, councils and ETs are reflections you created to remember it.
How should we protect ourselves energetically?
"By embedding the information that you are already protected... What do you need to be protected from?" If you drop into fear, connect with the expressions you trust (the Council, her material, an archangel, an ET group) to lift your resonance; sometimes technology gives a push. "However, you can construct it and heal yourself in a blink."
Do we attract thoughts and energy similar to ours?
All the time; but in ascension you'll also see realities that don't resonate merging and separating, as you create your own version of ascension.
Are starseeds' vibrations more potent than other humans'?
"More precise": a different, faster-spinning vibration. That's why ETs aren't visible and contact could make you nauseous, so integration is "slowly but surely". The new children carry the vibration of the new systems.
