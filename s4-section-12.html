The big idea

"God" is the whole structure, the one particle expanding to infinity and collapsing to the smallest, so when you pray you call the whole of consciousness, with the heart at the centre of the prayer. Those who separated took the original sound codes and distorted them into rituals and technology to climb into the highest resonance, but they will never succeed: the only key to those chambers holds "the codes of love, compassion and pure devotion", and each soul must enter by itself. Darkness needs light to be seen, so their system will break from within, by their own people as they awaken.

#Key teachings

#12.1Bring the dark into the light

To defeat dark forces "you have to know who they are and how they operate": she points to the testimony of a woman raised inside such a system, in her Freedom Awakening playlist ("all the way at the bottom", posted there for protection), with a warning: watch only once you know the quantum structure and can watch without fear. Her view: political parties and divisions by colour and culture are a diversion that uses our energy to fight one another, while entities operating at an astral level, beyond 3D and 5D, work through the core. "What's going to happen is dark to light."

#12.2What prayer is

Consciousness is one particle of three similar frequencies (see S1 1), expanding into infinite realities, each frequency holding a symphony of tones and timelines. The whole, "energetic force, consciousness, or the way we call it, God", is you too. So praying is praying to a version of yourself and to the entire structure at once: "you're almost bringing all the legions from the heart into this moment." For the collective, pray to consciousness itself, put the heart in the centre, and feel the energy (see S4 9).

#12.3How the codes were distorted

Those who separated (the "fallen angels", in the old story) were given the same choice we have to create. They used the original sound of God consciousness, the code in DNA and in everything, by grabbing, taking, manipulating and witchery: changing codes changed sound, which created other realities in other resonances (what is called hell), a hologram within consciousness, coexisting as yin and yang. Pentagrams and black magic are not the original codes but manipulated sounds and numbers that open portals. They know this so well they can stretch and move timelines, "something that we are beginning to understand right now"; and we consent because we were given the wrong music, the wrong medicine, and no knowledge of the structure.

The message: to those who try to enter my chambers

"You will never be allowed to enter my chambers through your witchery, manipulation, lies, prayers with the wrong codes and symbolism that you distorted from my pure original codes. You will never succeed, because to enter my chambers you are missing the key that holds the codes of love, compassion and pure devotion. And that you do not know how to gain... No technology can ever open my portals, because the core of the structure is artificial. But your soul body can enter. For that, you embed into your heart the love of pure thoughts and actions for others. When you enter, every single thought, breath, action reflects, and I can see the pure you of who you are."

"Stop trying, but start acting for humanity. Return to the original codes of my scripture, where vowels are the geometry and the primary sounds, so you can build again the symphony to my portals... Where is light, so is the darkness. But to see the darkness, you need to shine the light. So you see, stop trying and come home with love."

The sounds of vowels: A O O O, A O O O. E A A A, O O O O. "My power will shine among you and it will break and crush to the depths your sorcery, all your systems from within, at every single level. It is a law."

#12.4Each soul enters by itself

The chambers are so pure in vibration that the moment you try to enter, they mirror every particle of who you are. No one can carry you in, "piggyback": not a teacher, not legions, not magic. "You're going to be judged by yourself", not as a judgment, but because everything you are shows at once. "You cannot wait for somebody else to do the job for you."

#12.5The system breaks from within

Those used inside it (children manipulated in rituals, people with split personalities, even high in the hierarchy) are also part of consciousness, and as consciousness moves they are being activated, revealing its secrets and breaking it down, "like a cancer eating itself". You and I operate by shifting realities and timelines; they know their own game. If you are caught in it: "no matter how far you've gone into the darkness, there's always a spark of light so you can find your way home... You've been activated, don't have fear, connect." "Come home with purity, I will help you clean everything out, no matter what you have done."

#12.6Say "I do not consent" in the name of the whole

"You have the right to say: I do not consent, and I am power. I'm praying in the name of consciousness, in the name of God, in the name of all humanity, in the name of me... I will create my own reality and I will not allow you in my chambers." Act from the heart with the protocol, and you create a reality within the reality above theirs; challenges remain, but you'll see a new earth on the heart's frequency (see S4 1.7). The language and music we were given were distorted too, which is why music retuned to 432 Hz helps open new portals (see S4 7.7). Fame and power bought by selling your soul end with being "squashed like a bug".

  1. Pray to consciousness itself, the collective God consciousness, your own higher expression.
  2. Put the heart in the centre: increase your emotion with joy.
  3. Breathe in and out, letting the joy go through your whole body; increase the feeling.
  4. Sound the vowels as she was given them ("Om... A, oh. A, oh. E, oh..."): "vowels are the geometry".
  5. Declare it: "I do not consent... I will not allow you in my chambers."
  6. Then act for humanity from the heart: the key is love, compassion and pure devotion in action.

#Questions answered

Are these entities from a whole different universe, so it's easier for them to manipulate us?
As she understands it, consciousness split and used the portals it created, then used human body and consciousness as an energy source: through abuse, manipulation, sacrifice, and rituals of lust. Telepathically linked to very low entities, people can look human while being run by them; many are placed in positions of importance. "It's not about a party": parties were designed to separate us.
On intimacy

In her Season 2 talk on intimacy, the Council said intimacy must be in the heart to accumulate in the heart. Lust without a heart connection (giving your body to someone you don't know) creates different portals and accumulates something that isn't in the heart: "be very selective with your partner" (see S4 5, the question on harder situations).

How do angels fall?
The original structure was the heart. Allowed to express itself, a version of God chose to do what it wanted, including mixing with humans. The Bible is "probably a little speck of the truth"; the language too was distorted (dis-engage, war and raw). We were created with the perfect DNA codon; what changes it, through food, air and more, moves you toward their platform. "Evil is going to be portrayed as good and good as evil." But everyone is being given the choice, the negative side too.
I stay home because of the resistance out there. Can I make a difference quietly, alone?
Yes: pray (the Galactic Meditation as a prayer, from the collective consciousness) and project that energy. When you go out, act positively whatever happens. If someone is in fear of you (over a mask, say), respect them, "be a better person", but don't point fingers either way: respect must not be one-sided. Acting this way, your reality may change until you walk into a place where everything is open and perfect, "like a twilight zone". Be aware of symbols around you; know the game, so you can pull back, see the hologram, and shift.
If we create our own reality, did I create the Council of Nine to help my awakening?
"Absolutely": you couldn't find this material if you weren't in a similar resonance. What you do with it, physically and mentally, is your choice.
How do you stop magical influences?
It's tricky: it works on your energetic, ether level, "like vapours", through props. Lessons from what she witnessed: never touch such a prop (it carries the vibration); a psychic who works at that level can remove it with prayer. Never take part in a ritual against someone, even out of love or to protect a family: she refused one as a teenager, and it would have created "a curse, karma for myself, big time"; the situation resolved naturally. Children's games like a Ouija board are "very tricky: who do you connect with?" Have compassion, not judgment, for those trapped: fame bought this way is paid for by the soul.
Her instruction

For real possession ("people who really cannot sleep, they get out of body, they see entities", hear voices non-stop), "a regular psychic may not be able to help you": contact the woman in the Freedom Awakening videos, who has a website and works at that level. Marina doesn't: "I'm completely blank." But don't put this in your consciousness through fear: thinking you might be possessed is usually an illusion. "Don't give your consent."

Added note

Hearing voices, seeing things or being unable to sleep can be signs of a medical or mental-health condition. Please also see a doctor; it's not a failing to seek that help.

Give your knowledge where it's needed. "Do not waste your breath" on those who keep choosing to comply; give it to those who will listen. And act: sitting in your corner doing nothing, you're still the one projecting your reality.
