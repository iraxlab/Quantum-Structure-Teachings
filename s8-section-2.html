The big idea

The Council of Nine said the truth is not harsh; it is only the truth. Many complied, made excuses for money, house and job, said "not my problem", and turned away while others suffered; now the same hologram is reaching them. Those who suffer are also showing the rest what evil does, so "you are sheep no more". Both sides are fighting for resources and people are pawns in a chess game, but the end is in God's plan. Know what the negative does so you don't consent and can maneuver; hold a positive vibration so you find solutions; and take your freedom back while the window is open. Pray for the whole human race, not one religion.

#Key teachings

Added note

Added note: she presents videos and claims that recent wildfires were deliberate, linked to land grabs and planning agendas; those are her views and others' allegations, not established fact, and are condensed here. Named officials, celebrities and commentators are left out.

#2.1Why warnings matter

She avoids negativity so as not to lower anyone's field, but warnings come because of the reality within the reality. Right now it is a fight for the human soul: harvesting or preservation. Off-planet entities ride the human spirit for energy, and to harvest it they must lower your vibration. The protocols give you the alchemy to construct, deconstruct and switch frequency out of the negative matrix; learn them in order, like math.

#2.2Know it, so you don't consent

When something sounds too horrific, the mind shuts down and calls it conspiracy. But if you don't know their agenda, or deny it to protect your house and money, you'll eventually fall into one of their traps: they are in food, schools, the economy, politics, even neighbors. There is no middle ground; you must pick a side.

#2.3The lonely path

Taking a different path (pulling children out of school, exemptions, the diet) can make you ridiculed and isolated, even by family. Build thick skin; everyone on the path of ascension meets this. The scientists who move humanity forward are those connected with their higher self; you need the same or higher resonance to download from a parallel reality. People like her are in the war zone too: listen to your guidance; a misstep can affect your family.

The message: control of the opposition (condensed, as received)

"Who creates the structure of control? How is it that only one sector is telling you what to do? Do you listen even when you see it's wrong... like a puppet? Excuses: you're making excuses for money, house, job, food, to be compliant. You think that is your freedom when you comply?" "How long will it take you to stand up? Is it obvious that for some of you to wake up, all must be lost?" "You were compliant, not compassionate for others, and some say, not my problem. So is it not your problem now?" "Your compliance was part of that evil... Know that you have chosen to see it now with wide-open eyes. But at the same time your personal suffering is helping others to wake up. So thank you. So you are sheep no more."

#2.4Why victims have a role

The Council asked: why does the truth sound harsh? You collectively denied it, and now receive what you projected. Those who find themselves in hard situations chose it from the higher self, sacrificing themselves so others see what evil does and decide: consent, or take freedom back. "The truth cannot be harsh. It is the only one... Only from the truth can you maneuver and build the new."

The message: the chess game (condensed)

"Tens of cities, disasters, separations, all by design to control." "Take a stand now; fight for your freedom now... you have a window of opportunity to take your freedom back." "It will be a shift, a movement from one system to another: collapse, then digitalization. Protect your digital assets", including from an EMP. "For some it will be another day in the park, and for some apocalyptic: all by design of your own vibrational sequence and choosing." A big plan by both sides: "not one of them is playing the final cards yet... back and forth for resources, but know that in the end it is in God's plan... big surprises, unexpected events, and people are the pawns." "Watch March."

#2.5Don't get caught in the drama

If you become too emotional you can't maneuver and may do something inappropriate that pulls you into the negative reality. Pull back, know the game, and in a crisis be a pillar: help those around you. In that moment you'll see the best and worst of humanity, and everyone is tested.

Digital assets are volatile and scams are common; consult a qualified financial advisor and never invest more than you can afford to lose.
