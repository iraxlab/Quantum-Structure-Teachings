Fragmented Particles

A night download from the Council of Nine: how God consciousness analyzes every collapse, finds your "common denominator", and either pushes you toward the positive or holds you in a pocket between realities to recover; why everyone makes it; and questions on negative entities, the age of love, dreams and difficult people (live talk)

The big idea

Not every shift completes: you fluctuate, flop and try again. Consciousness, to preserve itself, runs a "comprehensive analytical process" over every moment you collapse, the fragmented particles, and finds the common denominator of your polarity. If it is positive and you keep trying, it reprints a situation in front of you (a person, a challenge, even a hospital stay) that pushes you like a magnet toward the positive. If it is deeply negative, or a soul is badly traumatized, it places it in a pocket between realities, a "remission", to reprint and recover until it is ready to return. "Every single one of us will make it. There is nobody that is going to be left behind."

#Key teachings

#18.1Fragmented particles

Moment to moment you shift, creating a new fractal reality each time; each collapse becomes a particle, a fragment. Each new reality reprints the frequency you had, with a different amplitude. But you don't always manage to pull back and switch from negative to positive (see the galactic meditation and its quantum structure, S4): you get off track, flop and keep trying, "as long as you don't give up". Consciousness has "pockets" that can create fragmented particles to restructure what you collapsed.

The message: reconstructive evolutionary restoration

"In one sector of [the] position of the location that the body consciousness is taking is a reconstructive evolutionary restoration. [A] comprehensive analytical process is happening into the velocity of the molecules through the shifting of the position." "Historically, consciousness of God is not always allowing any off-balanced sequences to penetrate [a] location that is in space shift": it corrects "slightly different vibrational sound frequency into a precise 100% position", locked and synchronized. "That is why you are all able to move forward now": even if someone is trying to stop your ascension and you fluctuate in fear, "God consciousness in the collective will always restore and reposition the missing [link] of your frequency."

The message: the void of its own reflection

Anyone who tries to dismantle your body, brain and molecular coherence is placed "in the sectors between the space realities in an infinite void of a vacuum, floating in servitude of its own reflection", which "may learn its lessons even to eternity until this negative soul is ready and is in balance" to be shifted back into space-time. "All that was created in the secondary beginnings, when God consciousness learned about itself."

#18.2The common denominator

The analytical process combines all your coexisting timelines and finds whether your common denominator is positive or negative. To download from or bilocate to a timeline you need a 100% precise location; when you are conflicted and can't complete, consciousness gives you a push by creating something you move toward "like a magnet", sometimes "a big kick in the butt". The same works for those caught in negative situations who put their heart in and act in the name of humanity: you must "put everything on the line". Being "brought to your knees" (a lost home or job, a divorce, false friends revealed) is not to bring you down; it shows the dark to the light so you can choose.

#18.3Remission: the second structure

Consciousness has "a second, like a reflection of itself": a subtonality platform where everything that needs remission and restructuring sits, while the coherent front structure moves fast. It is "almost like hospitalized", like a coma: the soul creates its own timeline, reprinting what it did until it learns (some need one hit to see, some a lifetime), then is pushed back. It is conversion, not destruction; this is what "back to source" means. It is also for good-hearted souls who went through great trauma, to recover. This is why her guides sometimes said a departed person was "in remission". Your goal: stay positive daily so your common denominator never puts you there. "You're the creator."

#18.4Observe, don't fear

When something reprints in front of you, analyze honestly: did you put ego in, do something silly? Then act through the heart. Don't judge public figures: some were placed through fear and must play the game; "in a war zone you don't give all your cards." The global economy won't crash into a dark age; both sides are fighting, and consciousness with humanity. Stay off mainstream media and be careful with alternative channels that are "everything in fear": repeated fear pulls the collective's common denominator down. Nano and AI can serve positive or negative; she promotes only the positive. (See S5 16.)

Her instructions: passports

If you wish to travel internationally, check that the countries are open, and if you don't have a passport, apply now: applications can back up if many people want to leave at once.

#Questions answered

If we project our reality, how do negative entities appear in it?
You are projecting them: in your fractal moment you chose to learn by experience, and you can't know light without references. Consciousness may project a negative experience you chose, or to push you to learn; then, through your analytical process, you choose to shift and act positively. "Nobody [is] outside of you."
Are negative frequencies in remission transforming into new positive consciousness? Will we enter the age of love?
"There is no years to come." Embed love now, project it into the collective, and when love is your common denominator you are pushed to experience it; a friend running a different program lives a different timeline at the same time.
Can we achieve real change in dreams or visions if we choose consciously?
Yes, but "who is we?" Believe 100% you are experiencing it, feel it, and act as if it is now; you build enough potency for the positive collapse to expand into the collective from what you first embedded.
Will truth prevail more? People are making life very difficult for me.
Difficulty is perception: consciousness is showing you what is off balance. Pull back to zero point, analyze, and ask what you can do; you are given the opportunity to push yourself forward. Have your moment, then get up quickly and act. If someone is constantly negative, why choose to stay? Don't attach to the illusion of the hologram; mutate it by taking action (see S4 Examples of Manifestations).
