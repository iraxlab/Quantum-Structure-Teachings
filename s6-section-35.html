The big idea

When consciousness moves to its next level of evolution, it sends a ripple of frequency through every reality and keeps sending it until everything off-balance is restructured. A reality that keeps choosing the negative grows denser and spins faster until it bursts by its own weight; consciousness doesn't destroy it. Before the burst, such a being feels at once all the pain and fear it caused; then it dissolves into stardust with no memory and is recycled from the beginning. So the reset they talk about is for the negative, not for you. Your job: no judgment, hold the positive daily, peaceful non-compliance, and trust synchronicity to place you.

#Key teachings

#35.1The ripple of ascension

Consciousness contains infinite parallel realities, always creating. When it is ready to evolve, it sends a sound-like ripple from its core to every structure. Whatever doesn't match is bombarded again and again, restructured, overwritten and finally cancelled, because consciousness is omnipotent and never stops until it has fixed itself.

#35.2How a reality turns negative

Moment to moment you collapse two realities (thoughts, emotions) into one, with a positive or negative charge, based on what you choose physically, emotionally and through the heart. Over days, lifetimes and millennia this adds up. At ascension, the "graduation", consciousness checks how well you did; a timeline that grew too dense cannot make the resonance (see 6.34 for cancelling a frequency).

#35.3The burst

With free will a group or individual can refuse the new frequency. Each refusal makes it denser and more separated; it spins faster until it bursts "at its own weight", like fireworks, into stardust in the field of consciousness. That dust holds no memory. It starts again from the basics: sound, vibration, Trinity structure, a particle that flickers and builds. Energy is never lost, only transformed, but this consciousness cannot go to the next level because it never made the shift.

The message: the Council of Nine on facing it (condensed)

Before the burst, the being is placed in a reality where it will "face its own doom": "imagine you feel all the pain and all the fear in one moment... and you also feel every single person." It cannot hold that vibration, and that is the moment of the burst. She doesn't describe it as hell, just vibration.

#35.4No judgment

The judgment you put in comes back to you. From their perspective this is how they survive. Pull back and observe, like watching a movie. Your job: stay positive, and if things get hard, it's because something needs transforming. Say no through peaceful non-compliance and start building something positive.

#35.5Technology itself isn't evil

She supports organic intelligence: whatever is created in consciousness becomes its own reality, and you transform it into a positive experience (phones, computers). The danger is technology used to make the body and DNA artificial so you stop converting your own realities, and confined, controlled living where everything can be switched off. Other civilizations have robotics and nano without being overtaken.

#35.6Synchronicity places you

Hold the heart vibration and "miracle stuff" happens: life moves you to the right place at the right time (a parent needs care, a job moves you), and only afterwards do you see why. Survival is individual ascension within collective ascension: the more good you collect, the more consciousness pulls you into its center.

#35.7Sharing without pushing

Give your information gently and let people sink in; they often hear it later from a friend rather than from family. Don't shut them down with too much.

The message: the reset (condensed)

"The reset is for the evil souls and not for you, the positive souls... checkmate." They forgot they are part of God consciousness: "you cannot go against your own structure... like chopping your own hand." They built an artificial hologram with a vibrational shield and multiple versions of Earth.

#35.8The road to righteousness

It is the hardest road: you break every hook, lose a great deal and are reborn to trust humanity. Waking up to what humanity is going through is painful, and you have to be strong.

#Questions answered

Does the place where negative beings feel what they did before the burst include reptilians, greys and humans?
The Council of Nine didn't say which beings. She works with the hybrids, who are kind: a past–future timeline that didn't make it and seeks original DNA. Her guidance came step by step ("not the time", "don't get involved", "stay on your own"), proven by synchronicity. Seedlings are key players on the board, like a knight in chess; you can't remove one without playing the game, and traps such as music or offers that flatter the ego may be set to lower your vibration. Hold the vibration: one small act can save someone who becomes very important. Do it from the heart, and keep information reachable even for people with nothing.
Is there a feminine consciousness? How does it relate to the whole and help humanity?
Yes, like yin and yang. Across lifetimes you choose to be female or male; the grades from each combine into one unit (1 + 1 = 3, which equals 1), and you may choose the same sex repeatedly to balance a lesson. Male energy is denser, like a stamp; female energy spins more smoothly and can slip between its particles to lighten it, the way something light lets steel float. In procreation, the male goes in. Same-sex partnerships can be souls balancing many lifetimes in one sex, not a judgment. At creation, the first particle is pure frequency; predominance comes from what each soul chooses and accumulates.
