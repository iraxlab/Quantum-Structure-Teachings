The big idea

History holds a culture's codes: the examples of how it overcame its mistakes. Erase it and each generation repeats them. Each country, like each person, accumulates what it chose and must complete its cycle; some will go through more cleansing. Change comes from the ground up: one person, consistently and with compassion, can start a movement. In the channeled part, creation begins as a precise zapping sound that splits into self-expressions; a reality that becomes stable and permanent becomes matter, the first spark of which is a crystal with memory, and its best spin, 47 degrees, creates the zero point. To move into the new history, a cycle must be completed 100% precisely, then crystallized through physical action.

#Key teachings

#6.1Why history is erased

Within history are the original codes of a culture, including how it learned from challenges and destruction. Without those examples a new generation repeats the mistakes or is led "like a lamb" to the slaughter. That is why the Council of Nine said history must be rewritten.

#6.2Technology needs boundaries

We connect to consciousness through the original body and its codons; going synthetic disengages you from the original blueprint. A quantum bylaw: whatever you build with lots of intention over time takes a permanent position and begins to reproduce itself, fed by many souls; it becomes a lineage reality (one future branch becomes organic intelligence, another the Archon AI). So coexist with technology, with boundaries; never let it overtake the body. Be cautiously optimistic about new platforms; some are pacifiers that distract.

#6.3Countries complete their cycles

A country, collectively and individually, accumulates whatever it chose: crimes against humanity, conquest, or care. Each must complete its cycle. Some advanced countries will go through cleansing, as a new generation switches the trajectory; others will need more cleansing to move forward. She was told her birth country will move forward.

#6.4From the ground up

She was moved by grassroots movements: a young man who gathered local farmers and opened stores selling directly from producers, another who helps elderly people in poverty. Start with one video, one act: if even one person listens and you're consistent and compassionate, you build momentum. Change will come from the people, not governments, so support your community and the silent heroes. "If you're greedy, be greedy to do goodness."

#6.5Tested 100%

Everyone is being tested, when least expecting, for what they do from the heart for humanity. If you're in a hard situation: pull back, tell yourself you are a hologram, breathe, step away if needed, then go back and help. When your true vibration is found, guides add holographic expressions that speed your evolution: 30 years in 3 to 5 (see 7.4). Prayer and visualization help, but crystallization is physical action.

The message: history in the making: the spark of creation (condensed)

"Each particle is created of a zapping sound that is very specific and resonant", splitting into multiple self-expressions, each a reality within the reality, each with free will to evolve. In the laboratory of creation they split "like little eggs of itself" into little universes, zapping each other magnetically; when they become similar enough in vibration they take a permanent position with a new subtlety of sound. Spinning so fast it seems still, the position stabilizes into the zero point, and matter begins. "This is the first spark of matter: it becomes a crystal." The crystal is conductive and records memory at every angle; "the best rotation within the spinning when it's creating is 47 degrees", which creates a geometric pattern and, at the center, the black hole, the zero point, holding the memory of everything created.

The message: completing the cycle (condensed)

Pleiadians and other races have their own evolutions; combining with them for the sixth race requires each position to be very pure in vibration: "you cannot download DVD 99%". Countries, platforms and people must complete their cycles 100% precisely to pass the portals and stargates. "Clear out all the negative pre-accumulated fractal expressions... in the name of love, compassion, and be strong as a steel." A strong word or a soft word, as the situation needs, are both methods; be precise and potent (see the Protocol to Manifest Potency). "It doesn't have to be your reality. Choose your reality by physically expressing."

#Questions answered

How do we avoid the artificial when nano dust and artificial light are everywhere?
That fear is an illusion: if you act to cleanse your body, what you need will be provided, including new sound and magnetic-resonance technologies that restore your cells' spin. Any harmful resonance can be cancelled by another. Fear reprints the very reality you fear. Detox, eat clean food, support local farmers and pure soil, refuse chemicals on your own land, and if you want to act collectively, form a group with neighbors and friends.
Can our family's not-so-good history be corrected by our actions? Are we responsible for what earlier generations did?
You carry the molecules and the vibrational memory of your lineage: its karma. Through ascension, challenges arise in your life or your children's, giving the free will to stop the cycle, usually by forgiving, which is hardest around money, property and ego. Souls born now are stronger and usually complete it. If you lower your ego, forgive and move forward, things change; if you argue and cling, the cycle continues. Drop expectations of those you helped: you did your best from the heart, so let them choose their own path. "If you love somebody, let them go."
