The big idea

The Council of Nine said greed will be exposed and crash "with a loud noise". Everyone is tested by money: you may begin with good intentions, but the money frequency conflicts with the heart. When you put a price on what you give, you place a hologram between you and the other person that slows the return; when you give from the heart and let them choose their support freely, the frequency returns unblocked and, built with daily consistency, becomes a steady flow. If you're the main provider and afraid, charge with reason, by what an ordinary person can afford, and practice free giving at least once a week. The true quantum financial system is etheric: an infinite flow of giving and receiving.

#Key teachings

#3.1Everyone tested by money

As DP predicted, every person (business owners, the wealthy, everyday workers, especially this community) will face a test connected with the monetary system. Study S1 E4 (money structure), S1 E5 (energy philanthropy) and S5 E41 (solitary distribution).

#3.2A price is a hologram

When you give from free will, your projection goes into the field and comes back with nothing stopping it. When you say "it will cost you $500 to talk to me", that amount becomes a hologram between you and the person: the frequency becomes denser, and manifestation is delayed, because what returns must pass through it again. You keep running on the money timeline instead of the heart timeline, where nothing blocks and it grows more potent.

#3.3The heart timeline

Energy philanthropy means offering your goods and services and letting people choose freely how to support you, knowing you still have bills. At first you'll still run on the money structure; moment to moment, train the heart structure until it lifts you into a parallel timeline of abundance. Constant taking from someone who gives, knowing they must survive, creates a reality where others take from you (not someone in a hard time for a few months). See S5 E41.

#3.4If you're the main provider

If you're alone or the family's main provider and still afraid, charge, but with reason, what an ordinary person can afford, so you don't empty someone's account. And at least once or twice a week give fully by energy philanthropy, so the heart frequency integrates and becomes familiar, until it is more potent than the conditioned one. Give the free will: suggestions are fine, conditions are not.

#3.5Modify as you go

Lower your overhead to make it possible: work from home or a friend's room instead of an expensive facility. Adjust session length to avoid exhaustion. New ideas come moment to moment; you co-create a new timeline between the two structures. Those working this way have been saved when disputes broke out around them.

#3.6Being shown the truth

Running on this frequency, situations arise that reveal who is manipulating you: a suspicion, a call, someone at an event saying the same thing. Pull back in time. Ignoring her guides' warnings cost her and her family dearly. Don't lose your trust, heart or love for humanity: learn the lesson, stay compassionate, and become "strong as steel". Guides rarely give direct predictions, because you may need the lesson.

#3.7Breaking through fear

When sessions brought no support one day she panicked, then pulled back, meditated ("I have everything I need now") and help came. You must push through the fear like through a placenta: a birthing process. "Business is not personal" is a lie: manipulation collects in your field.

#3.8No home quantum computer

A true quantum computer needs a vacuum-like space with no vibration and costs millions; anyone selling a home "quantum" device is not telling the truth. The true quantum system is etheric; on the way you'll pass through gold, crypto and other systems. Be careful with anyone holding "gold" for you on a notarized promise; ask real coders. She will never tell anyone what to invest in.

#The method — Bringing energy philanthropy into a business (as given)

  1. If you're afraid or the main provider, set prices with reason: what the average person can afford.
  2. Give fully by energy philanthropy at least once or twice a week.
  3. Lower your overhead (home, a friend's room) so the free will can work.
  4. Offer suggestions, not conditions; let people choose their support.
  5. Modify as you learn (timing, length, format); retreat a bit when needed, then expand.
  6. Repeat daily for consistency; push through the fear when support is slow.

#Questions answered

With products rather than services, a greedy person could donate far below value and resell. What then?
With goods, calculate and charge what it costs you (materials, travel, venue), then add energy philanthropy for your time and effort, perhaps with a suggestion. Books and retreats have a price; extras are by donation. Find the common denominator: charge for months of work, but not $10,000 for something that cost $100 and two hours.
Do we tell clients about our energy-philanthropy structure?
Yes, if you wish; explain what it is, as a teacher would. Just don't make merchandise using "energy philanthropy" in the Harmonic Reactor sense, which she has protected. It's a learning process for everyone.

Left out: greetings and platform notices, her personal stories about hosts, a house, a retreat venue and a lawyer (kept as lessons), named people, and thanks.
