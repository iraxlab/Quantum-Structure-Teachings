Bulgaria Will Survive / Good News

A Council of Nine message for Bulgaria that she says applies globally: old warrior souls reincarnated now and "activated like a switch"; why regions must balance each other; and questions on the past in the now, soul agreements, the role of older souls, and whether 5D is real (talk)

The big idea

Consciousness keeps the planet's portals in balance: when the negative acts in one region, something positive pops up in another. Souls who once fought for freedom (for Bulgaria, the warriors of Khan Asparuh's time) have reincarnated now and will be switched on, in every walk of life, even people who did wrong, so the work won't wait a whole generation. Everything past is also in the now, so you can tap into who you were and act from that knowledge. Start small: one act of kindness a day builds the potency for something big.

#Key teachings

#2.1Regions balance each other

To hold the planet in the 5D structure, certain global portals in Mother Earth must be stabilized; no single region can stay all negative or all positive. So you'll see the negative act in one place while something positive appears in another: consciousness always creates pockets of reality to stabilize the field, and it will never let itself self-destruct.

The message: Bulgaria will survive (condensed)

"Bulgaria will survive and push through. Khan Asparuh's spirits, souls, are in motion now and activated": many souls from 681 are present now and will stop the negative. Victory for Bulgaria: the euro will not be allowed; the lev will remain and later be restored to its original glory. The land is rich in minerals and gold, with pyramids and tunnels leading to dimensional portals. The Black Sea will soon have an algae that cleans pollution; the air will receive a new self-restoring wave frequency; harmful towers will be replaced with a frequency that is not harmful. "God's hand is in play here." Her guides added: globally, in 30 years humanity will rise again, "healthy and holy"; the globalists "will not be able to exist in three years from now"; in May 2023 their operation from the top will be stopped, though traces of their minions may remain.

Added note

This is her prediction as given; she says the end result is fixed but how and when are probability (she adds that something may pass and then be stopped). Her historical and political commentary in this talk (on migration, banking, religions, groups and communism) is left out, in keeping with this book's neutral approach.

#2.2Souls switched on

You may have been born into the Archon hologram, by a soul switch or as the original soul not yet activated. When the time is right, an experience or your guides activate your DNA and consciousness. The warrior souls will "switch" not only in understanding but in acting: a corrupt politician, a doctor, a lawyer, a worker or a child may wake one morning and say "no more." Even the young who seem lost will have profound experiences. Children are the new starseeds, and a hybridization programme is coming.

#2.3Know your history

Words become sound waves and realities, so rituals done without understanding can feed low vibrations. She urges learning history combined with science and metaphysics, to see through deception.

#Questions answered

If we create from the present moment, why look at what happened years ago? Is the law of attraction the most powerful law?
Everything past is also in the now. Knowing your history keeps you from being manipulated now and lets you tap into the version of you who lived it, embed that knowledge and act from it. A soul that lived through communism sees manipulation sooner. All realities coexist; you only need to download, embed and act from the version you want.
Why do some connect with their guides while others fall back into old habits? How do we know our soul agreement?
You learn your agreement when you reach the level of connection yourself. Practise automatic writing and lucid dreaming consistently until what you receive comes true. Guides may guide you ("don't call that person; let them call you") without telling another's private story.
I'll be 60 next year. What role do older souls have?
Older souls were necessary: they carry knowledge from other times. Combine it with what you have and help daily: assist someone in a shop, fix an older neighbour's garden, bring food once a week, call your parents and grandparents. One person holds the vibration of the whole universe; small deeds build the potency for something big you can't yet imagine. "You've got to create it to see it."
I feel in 5D sometimes. Am I making it up?
Reality is constructed by perception: if you believe it's real, it is. When you see something negative, thank it (it's already in the field, so you can't reject it) and transform it with the QM protocol.
