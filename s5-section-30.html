#The method — the one-minute focus

Given for mental clarity; it is the beginning of creating a blueprint.

  1. Place an object about 30 cm (half a metre) in front of you: a melon, a pencil, or a picture of what you wish.
  2. Hold your attention on it only: what is it, how does it look, smell, what colours, what could you do with it?
  3. Add the heart: feel joy, the taste, how it feels to have it.
  4. Hold for 30 seconds to one minute without any other thought; most will drift within 20 seconds at first. If your mind jumps (to your child, say), you stopped the waveform building that matrix; begin again.
  5. Practise until your focus is "like a laser point"; the longer it spins, the more potent. Combine with the galactic meditation (S4).

Too much information confuses; pick what truly resonates and perfect it.

#Questions answered

What is the connection between the heart and the soul?
One and the same, in different expressions. The heart's sound wave ripples through the soul and oversoul; the soul collects that resonance into a unique pulsation recognizable instantly in God consciousness. It keeps growing, through dark and light; going through the mud, like a soldier, makes you a leader. "Your compassion here... will ripple through every single structure... nothing is hidden."
Is this process part of clearing ancestral trauma?
Yes. Everything is now; each moment is a new version of you. If a strong emotion leaks from other realities, take care of yourself each time (a tea, herbs, yoga, sometimes medication in the beginning) until, like a ripple, it subsides; "medicine and the natural consciousness... go hand in hand", but not as permanent crutches. Then act physically, rebuild your thinking and mutate it, with consistency, until it pops into place.
Added safety note

For trauma or heavy emotional distress, please work with a qualified doctor or therapist, especially before starting or stopping any medication.

If white lies are wrong, isn't "creating what we want to see" lying to ourselves?
No. Creating your reality is not deception. A lie is when you knowingly say something to manipulate or hurt someone; that deception ripples and returns. If you can't say the truth now, say politely, "Can we talk about this another time?"
Is there a technique to move the energy forward, alone or in groups?
Groups are very potent, like more wind. Alone, feel that you are meditating with millions at the same time: in one unit you hold the power of the whole collective.
How powerful is the subconscious, and how do we get past it?
The subconscious is always aware and direct (as in her channeling); the conscious mind restructures what it gathers and gets distracted. Learn to trust the subconscious; practise.
How do we work with the 8/8 Lion's Gate portal?
Alignments are potent energy you can harvest, positive or negative: use it to meditate on what you wish, knowing you hold the power. Nobody will be handed 5D on a given day; you must learn.
What happens to those who don't have this knowledge?
Go back to S1 and watch "Etheric Legions". Each person goes into the timelines they chose; you can't know their agreements. Let go of expectations; you may meet them in another timeline.
How do we manage the energy surges we feel in our bodies?
First get medical checks (blood work, vitamins such as D3, diet); don't assume "it's the energy". If all is fine, it may be the shift. When detoxing, rest ("almost like you're in a coma"); after a week or two, gently add stretching and yoga. "Do not do... anything without doctor supervision."
How can parents be more confident supporting children who may have chosen differently?
Some will go through that trauma. Watch "Etheric Legions" and learn to let go of the anger and pain; it may be the toughest transformation.
