Seven-Level Pamphlets Hologram

The big idea

Because the dense side was fighting so hard, guidance said a seven-level "pamphlet" (layer) of hologram from a higher reality was placed within the 5D structure to strengthen its resonance. A geometry was shown to lock the new earth into your consciousness: seeing it is itself an imprint. But a layer only holds if you hold it, through the heart, with action, never through violence. And the same mechanism explains addiction: a negative action repeated until it becomes a permanent timeline, which is converted the same way, by repeated positive action, with help.

#Key teachings

#17.1A layer added to hold the 5D

We live in a living-structure hologram, fluctuating between 3D and 5D. With so much destruction in the matrix, "they had to implement a seven-layer pamphlet of hologram reality within this 5D": an extra holographic imprint from another reality, adding resonance so the 5D's potency holds. The evil "is powerful, but it's defeatable". Our part: keep the 5D frequency going from the heart, by action, "but the action is not through violence".

#17.2Geometry is an imprint

The geometry given for the new signal (a central image of the Harmonic Reactor) has a triangle and an opposite triangle that rotate in opposite directions and create a zero point in the middle, like a cone, from which frequency spreads (see S2 24.3). Seeing such a geometry imprints its structure in your consciousness, "locking in" the new earth, which is why it was shown. The same mechanism works the other way: hidden sub-tones or messages in media imprint without your knowing, so choose what you watch.

#17.3You receive only what your resonance allows

Why do channels and psychics give different futures? Each downloads from the timeline their consciousness and body resonate with: "you will never be given information above your structure" without a similar resonance and permission. Otherwise those who wanted to control the future could simply read it. So don't put 100% trust even in what the teacher says: trust the quantum structure, and watch whom you listen to, because constant fear keeps you on that timeline (see S3 4.2).

#17.4How addiction becomes a timeline

Repeat the same negative action, moment to moment, and you reprint yourself again and again on that frequency, until it becomes potent enough to form a permanent timeline with permanent outcomes: you loop through similar parallel realities and feel you can't get out. To convert it: take physical action with help (doctors, counselling, deep work with a qualified practitioner) and keep repeating positive actions, each time thanking the experience rather than rejecting it: "thank you; now I know how I was addicted." Release must be slow and supervised, because the body's molecules have been vibrating on that frequency (see S3 14.4). For ordinary detox, use the slow elimination of her detox videos, one thing every two weeks; for an addiction it has to be under a doctor's supervision, depending on the addiction, because of the toxicity accumulated. She speaks from experience: she smoked and drank wine ("I was an alcoholic"), and had to stop both and detox.

Support

For alcohol or drug use, in the US the SAMHSA National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357 (free, 24/7). Withdrawal from some substances can be dangerous: please do it with medical help.

#17.5Peel every layer

"Be brutally honest about what you're really doing." Change starts from the bottom layer: one hanging layer is "a whole worm running like a program" that keeps everything else off balance. It takes bravery to face your mistakes, and no one is judged for it.

#17.6Mistake or evil?

Evil isn't the same as error. If you hurt someone through not knowing, you can correct it. But harm done viciously and on purpose comes from the illusion of being separate from the whole structure, which is how one consciousness came to treat humans as animals. Even so, those beings also have positive versions, and their higher selves are trying to help them. Don't hate: "I don't consent; I'm a free soul, and so are you."

#17.7Your resonance in sound

A single frequency (such as 7.9 Hz) is fine for a while, but change happens through many layers of frequency, "like a symphony", constantly moving. The sign that something is resonating with you: goosebumps, a wave of feeling. It can be a piece of music, spoken words, or an inspiring film (for her it was one particular Tibetan music; it may not work for everyone). Find yours and use it daily; you wouldn't take the same pill for every illness (see S3 7.7).

#The method — Converting a Habit That Has Become a Timeline

  1. Name it honestly: peel the layers (17.5).
  2. Thank the experience: "now I know how this works in me."
  3. Get physical help: a doctor, counsellor or support group.
  4. Choose one positive action to put where the old one was.
  5. Repeat it, again and again, especially when it's hard (17.4).
  6. Go slowly, supervised: the body releases over time.

#Questions answered

I'm eight months pregnant. Is there a message about my baby, and the souls arriving now?
Children entering now carry a resonance above 3D, to continue the mission. A gentle, sensitive soul: play music, especially Bach. (A suggestion about breathing extra oxygen during birth was given.)
Added note

Please follow your midwife's or obstetrician's guidance on oxygen and everything else during birth.

Will there be three days of darkness?
Not seen: it's a layer of reality someone is printing. See 17.3.
Are pictures I see in my head future events or warnings?
Both: probabilities; not everything seen becomes real, because there's so much fluctuation.
How can I free myself from addiction?
See 17.4 and the method.
For five years I've woken several times a night feeling I'm dying.
"There's nothing wrong with you": you're so sensitive that you notice yourself jumping from one hologram to another. Say thank you and go on with your day.
Added note

Frequent night terrors are worth discussing with a doctor or sleep specialist too.

Something is missing in my life. What can I do differently?
Study the structure 15 minutes at a time and do the nine-day protocol (see S3 8): switch physically and emotionally, then act, so you choose your frequency rather than drifting.
Will people with heavy burdens, such as caring for a violent adult child, still struggle in 5D?
Changes are coming individually; sound technologies to restore the nervous system were described, but it's a complex, individual process. He needs supervision.
My adult son has constant headaches and says his only choice is to end his life.
Read as spasms causing pain and pressure; it's complex, and the teacher was sorry not to be able to do more.
Support

If someone talks about ending their life, take it seriously and get help now: in the US call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or 911 in an emergency. Stay with him, and involve his doctors.

Everything comes from one unit, so they carry our signals and understand us; look at it as a whole structure learning, not good guys and bad guys (17.6).
