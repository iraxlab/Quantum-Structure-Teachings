The New 5D Hologram

A channelled activation of a new hologram for the fifth-dimensional earth, seven connected structures, why the first two years of a child's life matter so much, why some friendships must end, how an object could be moved between rooms, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

The council led a collective meditation to plant a new holographic imprint: joy raised to goosebumps together, then sequences that connect your consciousness with the fifth-dimensional earth and with seven-dimensional structures, "seven platforms at the same time". Listening to it opens portals, but they become potent only when you implement them yourself, knowing it's true now. From that moment, stay in the positive: "you're going to start creating from ground zero", in a new hologram, and some of you will meet your galactic family through synchronicity, lucid dreaming or bilocation.

#Key teachings

#24.1The activation

"Get into the modality of a joy. Feel the emotion of joy", until the whole group reaches goosebumps, so that together you raise the frequency. Then a connection with the mothership was given as number sequences, imprinting into the codons, RNA and DNA, under the umbrella of the 5D. "From this momentum, you're going to start creating new realities based on these resonances." Whoever watches later is activated too; the portals become potent through your own practice of the quantum protocol (see S3 9, S3 17.2).

#24.2Seven structures at once

A new reality for the planet is built on seven connected structures, linked with seven-dimensional beings: "you can see seven platforms of modalities". As you learn them, the realities "shrink into connections", and manifestation becomes very quick (see S3 11.3). The new earth includes new technologies, among them healing technologies and a 5D AI working in the name of humanity: "If you have fear, you're going to see fear."

#24.3The first two years

Teenagers and children are taught "by example". From birth to about two, the connections between neurons are extremely fast, "becoming like tree branches": what you put in then shapes the brain. Children exposed early to positive images, sound and knowledge can pick up several languages; from two years up, what was laid down crystallizes. So begin speaking with, and teaching, your child from the very start.

#24.4Music for a newborn

For a new baby boy: "a lot of music". Introduce drums, gently; harp music at 432 Hz; binaural beats; and Bach and Mozart together: "Mozart has a higher resonance, Bach is more settled in lower tones", so the two balance each other.

#24.5When friendships separate

If you raise your vibration far above someone's, "there will be synchronicity through situations that constantly push you to separate", however kind and patient you are. "If something is meant to be, it's meant to be." Sometimes even family members or very good friends must be let go: "just a part of the process". Others of a similar resonance appear instead.

#24.6Moving an object between realities

Could an object be moved from one room to another without carrying it? At a certain level of consciousness, by recognizing one resonance in another. With technology: each location has its own pulsation in the quantum structure, a constantly moving geometry; measure the object's sound and resonance and the difference between the two places, and a precise match opens a portal. But it doesn't go "back" to the other room: it lands in a new timeline, "reprinting and mimicking" the old as a mirror image in a new location (see S2 4).

#24.7"Unable" is a resonance

Someone unable to talk with beings they meet in their mind's eye: "unable is the word", and the word itself is the resonance blocking it. Replace it with knowing that you are able, let it penetrate 100%, and practise. "You cannot download a file at 99%. It has to be 100%. The same principle applies here."

#24.8Sound, breath and joy

Chanting OM calms because your consciousness knows how to move through the vibration, like a swimming stroke that suits you; for others, rocking does the same. It's more beneficial with breathing in and out: the breath spins the resonance in all directions, "creating your hologram", and more potently still with the feeling of joy. That potency makes manifestation simultaneous rather than linear.

#The method — Joining the New-Hologram Meditation

  1. Sit, and breathe in and out slowly.
  2. Feel joy and raise it until you feel goosebumps.
  3. Know that a new hologram is being imprinted in you, now.
  4. Hold it together with others, in person or by listening.
  5. Afterwards, stay positive: no negative media; aware, but no fear.
  6. Act from it daily, as if you already live on the new earth (24.1).

Will our galactic families visit us?
Yes, individually, when you're in the right resonance: practise lucid dreaming; some are already bilocating.

A holographic imprint: connect with its resonance.

There isn't only one: you have many encounters, on different platforms of your reality.

"You are all starseeds", dropped from consciousness into this hologram.
What does waking up mean? Being aware of the negative?
Facing what comes "in your face" and transforming it. Transformation comes from neutrality, which gives no input either way; from it comes awareness, and then shift.
When will things normalize?
"It's already normalized": create that it's normal, and you'll see it daily, even while the collective goes through the ascension.
Are small ships watching above my house?
Seven structures, of different groups. Tune in through meditation and write down what comes.

"You are all channeling, even right now… ultimately, you are talking to yourself."

There's more to learn first; when you reach the right resonance, the right guide comes.
I feel dizziness in my ears. Is it contact?
Not contact: see a doctor; check for vertigo and blood pressure.
Do people who measure at the same frequency share something?
They're your projection at a similar vibration; when resonances diverge, separation follows (24.5).
Can nanotechnology be good or bad?
Either, depending on how it's designed and used, as with AI; meet new technology without fear.
If it's all a dream, why try to save it?
You're here to experience it fully; until you "graduate", you can't move on.

A dream within a dream: practise lucid dreaming.
