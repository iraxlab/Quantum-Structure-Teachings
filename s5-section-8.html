The big idea

Everything you see is the matrix: holograms of different vibrations, like radio stations. The negative side knows it, so it projects fear 24/7, and "whatever you're putting your attention into, that's what the resonance frequency of your body is going into." To get out: see it, say "it's only a hologram", stop feeding it with attention, and act from the heart. For loved ones in danger, imagine them safe and, at the same time, be okay with whatever they choose: they are a reflection with its own right to choose. "This is not the people's war."

#Key teachings

#8.1Attention is the doorway

Each reality is a frequency structure; similar ones in a row make a stable timeline (S1). An extraterrestrial consciousness that knows how the universe works built a 3D matrix with technology and locks you in by projecting sound waves of suppression through every system: media, schools, history, religion, medicine (see S4 22). Your body, DNA and chakras are "like a spacecraft" able to bilocate, which is why they need you in 3D, as their energy and food source; "why would you blame them... when they don't know anything else, they don't know the compassion." Two timelines run now under consciousness (see S5 1); whichever you give your attention to, you resonate in.

#8.2Holding loved ones in danger

If family are caught in a war, say: "that is just the matrix, an illusion. I know now, in the now, that my family members are okay"; "and yet, pay close attention, you have to be okay if something happens to them." Because the reflection you project (see NT) has the right to choose its own vibration and Earth. Everyone was activated; some chose to stay asleep to become part of the etheric legions, sacrificing themselves so others wake (see S4 38): "never, ever judge." Watching the bombing 24/7 in fear and panic projects a new timeline from their narrative: "here you are, locked in." Compassion yes; feeding it, no.

The message: stop the illusion

"If you all give more attention to what is happening in the war, you are all interacting with the frequency to become real and more potent... They project to you to put you and your heart in a frequency of fear, doubt and despair. In the meantime, you are the creator that can shift everything."

"If you have family there, know that they have chosen to be there and to experience that... use your quantum manifestation knowledge to know that they are okay and learning their lesson... What is truly real right now?" Your daily life, not the teleprompter: "I understand, I love you, I'm sorry", then neutralize it.

"There is a tug-of-war, multi-layer players of a chess game, dark agendas... It's not [a] political party, it's [the] globalists." "At the moment: one bomb, second bomb, third bomb, and stop at once" (a code she didn't understand). "This is not the people's war, but the oligarchs' evil games war."

"Humans, do not comply. Stop the war by not complying on both sides... Anyone can give you an order. Why do you comply when you know that you will harm a soul?... The fellow soldiers have family too... You all can stop this right now." "We're not getting involved. Lesson to be learned here." (The political questions and countries she named are left out.)

#8.3Diet, and the work

She recommended a documentary on plant-based diet: "we were not meant to eat animals"; eating meat from animals killed in fear takes in that vibration and lowers your frequency. Her family moved to vegan; she was moving to fruitarianism, "I'm not suggesting for you to do that... you have to do it through doctor supervision... so you don't deplete nutrients" (see her detox videos in Seasons 3 and 4). Everything she teaches will challenge every belief: "you've got to rewire not only your belief system, but physically you have to implement it." "I'm not your saviour. I'm part of you."

Added safety note

Vegan and especially fruitarian diets can lead to serious deficiencies (B12, protein, minerals). As she says, change diet only with a doctor and blood work.
