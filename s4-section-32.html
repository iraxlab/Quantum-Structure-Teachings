Gifts to Humanity

The Council's good news that the positive blueprint is locked in; why fighting locks you into the other timeline; surrender as the zero point; how to stay "at rest but in control"; and questions on the tests each of us will face, feeling locked in, and having no conscious contact (live talk, August 2021)

The big idea

"Everything is completed." Collectively, the positive blueprint of reality, the pamphlet, is already locked in and stabilized, and the stargates are open and secure. The negative side will throw everything it can, hoping you will vibrate on fear or anger. But the structure reflects back whatever you project: fight with their frequency and you lock yourself into their timeline. Instead, understand without judging, don't comply, and act differently: neutrality, the zero point, from which you create a reality within the reality.

#Key teachings

The message: gifts to humanity

"Very big gifts are coming to humanity." "Everything is completed, LOL": they see the other platforms of reality, while we see only ours. "The program that you are running collectively is working in full capacity." The blueprints of the oversouls in the holograms are "completely stabilized"; the positive pamphlet is "100% in place". "Humans, congratulations." Give or take 30 months of back and forth, "like a ping pong", the outcome through the fluctuation will be positive. "The stargates for all are wide open and secure."

"Do not fight, do not engage in civil war, do not fall for the false agenda" that would make people fight each other; protest, if you do, peacefully. "Be in rest, but in control": rest, but don't let the rest consume you until you're oblivious; rest plus control equals alert. "You plan, you wait, you maneuver with patience and let them think that they have won." Many will be put to the test in the final battle, to see who the true leaders of humanity are. "Do not get caught up in the illusion, stand your ground and take your freedom."

Her instructions: food

Buy food for three months now and save it until it is absolutely necessary; meanwhile keep buying your food month by month. From about November (three months on), start buying food online. If you plan to move somewhere, do it now rather than wait.

#32.1Why fighting locks you in

Your thoughts, emotions and deeds make your hologram resonate; your timeline has its collective frequency, at a location of latitude, altitude, X and Y (see S2 15). Those who want to keep you in their timeline create situations in a location, diversions, fights, disasters, so that you vibrate on that frequency. Whatever you project, the structure shifts you into a new earth of the same frequency (see S1 2). So if you fight with the same frequency of fight they gave you, "you think you're winning, but you're actually reflecting whatever the negative side gave you", and you can lock yourself into their timeline and not know how to get out. To leave it you need enough positive potency, held all around you, "360 degrees", until the timeline splits off to the new vibration.

#32.2Surrender is the zero point

She showed a film scene in which a girl, made to fight her own double in a simulation, refuses to fight and surrenders, and the program ends. "When you surrender, that is the zero point and neutrality within your quantum timeline structure." You don't reject (rejection holds the same frequency), you don't comply: you understand, and you mutate. "Thank you very much for showing me what you want me to do... it doesn't have to be my platform. I don't judge you. I understand you." Then you do something completely different, and you lock into your own reality within the reality, where you find food, water, friends and family, or move by synchronicity.

If someone told you to jump from a third-floor window, you wouldn't comply; why comply when you see lunacy? "If they're projecting and you're consenting, whose fault is that? It's not theirs, it's their survival. It's yours." The transformation happens within your structure. Remember the balance: the positive is "the power of 10" compared with the negative, because consciousness keeps yin and yang slightly in the positive to survive (see HR).

#The method — Neutralize, Don't Fight

  1. See it: "I see what you're doing." It's a hologram, a chess game; stay alert.
  2. Don't judge, don't hate: understand that it's their survival.
  3. Don't comply, and don't fight: "Thank you, but I don't have to." Fighting projects their frequency back.
  4. Do something different: change work, go to another location, call friends and family in other places, go online. No fear about money or how.
  5. Hold the heart, 360 degrees, with consistency, until your reality within the reality forms.
  6. Let synchronicity bring the solutions: the hide-and-seek game (see S1 1.7).

For parents. "This is a life and death situation... it's still your child." Show your children what is happening, several times if needed; if they still choose otherwise, let go and respect their choice.

#Questions answered

Can you give examples of the tests we will each face?
Everyone who came at this time of ascension is a strong soul, and new leaders must be chosen: people whose heart and consciousness resonate enough to meet extraterrestrials, "could be everyday Joe". Holograms will be plastered into your reality to see what you do. Most tests involve people and money: what you do when someone steals from you or is mean to you. Forgive 100% and move on, or be vicious and spiteful? "Viciousness and spitefulness, I guarantee you, downhill with that one": what you project reflects back and accumulates. The test comes "exactly at the moment you least expect", so your immediate reaction shows. (She described a test she passed by helping a drugged young woman when no one else would, and a man who boasted of tripling prices after a disaster: "this is the time for you to give everything in the name of humanity.")
How do I know if I'm locked into the negative structure?
You will be bombarded with situations to make you feel locked in, but "you are never locked in"; you always have the choice to create a reality within the reality. Say thank you, don't comply, and look for solutions, knowing you have them before you see them. Take action "relentlessly, non-stop". The deeper you allowed yourself to go, the more potency you need (see S1). Use the Galactic Meditation with your own subject and its action steps (S4 9). "Do not go and fight; project the positive by taking actions peacefully."
Why are some of us awake, but with no conscious contact with our galactic families?
You have connections. Learn lucid dreaming (the books she learned from are listed on her website): it's the shortcut. But being lucid doesn't mean you'll be shown what you ask; she was twice told "You're not allowed to be here" at a spacecraft. "If it's not your time, it's not your time, have patience and learn." "If you were not awakened, you would never even project me in your hologram... So if I know it, so do you."
