The big idea

But with so much background noise, the brain's synapses can be too weak to restructure what they receive in real time, and then anyone can steer you. Like Newton's third law, but from the heart it is by the power of ten. Your job: be aware of the deception, don't let fear take your free will, and live in the moment, where no system can predict you.

#30.4Newton's law for consciousness

Suppression creates resistance, which creates awareness, which creates more suppression and more awareness, back and forth, until the system tips. Newton's third law (1687): for every action, an equal and opposite reaction; push a wall and it pushes back. Everything is held by magnetic and gravitational fields, like magnets moving one another. But from the heart, the reaction is by the power of ten, so it always tips toward the positive: consciousness can't self-destruct. "Same law, different scales": one version for the scientist, one for the soul. Hold the line; the ball bounces back.

#30.5Get up quickly

No matter how strong you are, you may fall off the wagon, especially with family. Get up quickly and do something to realign: call a friend, dress up and dance. She once pulled a downcast friend out by clearing the living room, putting on techno music and dancing in high heels, drinking cranberry juice. There are no strangers, only other versions of you: treat people as you'd treat your own child. The more negativity accumulated, the more action it takes, "like spices".

#The method — Staying grounded (as given)

  1. Physically remove yourself from the situation: another room, outside.
  2. Do something until the emotion subsides: music, a friend, a sport.
  3. Once it reaches zero, put new information in: a positive video or meditation.
  4. Use a 15-minute meditation (subatomic or galactic method).
  5. Repeat every time you're hit, until you can say: "I see you. Thank you."
  6. Live in the moment: no system can predict your trajectory there.
