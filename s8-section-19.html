The big idea

The Council of Nine said each microbe in your biome is a tiny spark of light with a strong electromagnetic field, pulsing like a heartbeat, pulling energy into a vortex and reprinting itself again and again. Together these billions of interdependent microbes hold your body in position from moment to moment, produce its heat, protect the intestinal wall and carry your ancestors' immunity. There is no simple good or bad bacteria: there is balance, or overgrowth. Toxins, depleted soil and some medicines have wiped out ancient microbes; fruit and vegetables alone can no longer restore them. They suggested measuring each microbe's frequency and, with AI in sterile labs, helping lost microbes re-emerge, then restoring each person's biome individually.

#19.1One ecosystem

There is no such thing as simply good or bad bacteria: the biome is an ecosystem in which each microbe depends on the others. A so-called bad bacterium may be needed in a small percentage to keep balance and teach the body to fight; it becomes a problem when it overgrows. Microbes, the longest-living organisms on Earth, respond to vibration; each has a specific frequency that can be measured, and sound can act on matter (she points to ultrasound that breaks up targeted tissue).

The message: the spark of the biome (condensed, as received)

"Each molecule has a life force that is connected with a higher frequency matrix. It looks like a very small spark of light... with a very strong electromagnetic field... a glow that casts pulsation like a heartbeat." The spark "casts tremendous power of a vortex... pulling like a vacuum" all the energy around it, "spins it around... and flushes it out into the ether" as a Fibonacci spiral. Each microbe has its own toroidal field and its own position in time-space, "filled with its own pulsation and its own vibrational sound wave". Each reprints itself as "the infinite vortex of self-reprinting"; "this is the secret of how your body is able to hold its own position... when you shift to a new hologram." But only "if your environment... is not toxic". "This is also why your body is able to combust and produce heat at all times."

#19.2pH and balance

Each microbe exists in a certain percentage, and together they set the body's pH. An acidic body throws the ecosystem out of balance and lets any microbe overgrow. Microbes live on the intestinal wall and feed from it, like a protective layer; when they overgrow, they can damage it.

#19.3The gut as a second brain

As she understands it (from a book on neuroplasticity, not as a doctor): microbes break food down; through the villi, products such as amino acids enter the blood and reach the brain, where each acts like a key in a specific neuron's keyhole, unlocking a function. An imbalanced biome produces toxins that disrupt these signals, which may affect memory and behavior.

The message: ancient, intelligent microbes (condensed)

"These live microorganism biomes are truly from an ancient time, from the beginning of everything that is created. They are very intelligent... they also have the consciousness to rebuild itself and survive", in support of each other's functions. "The biome holds the records of your predecessors... If your ancestor had smallpox and survived naturally, the DNA and the biome collected from that era will be reprinted into your family." "The biomes are passed from generation to generation through a natural birth." "You may have genetic predisposition, but... the biome is the one that has the knowledge and the bacteria to fight diseases."

#19.4Why it's depleted

Poisons, toxic food and synthetic medicines strip the biome. Wipe out the protective microbes and others overgrow, the body is chronically inflamed, and food injures the intestinal wall. The Council said fruits and vegetables alone can't restore it, "since your land, soil, air and water has been systematically poisoned", and many ancient microbes no longer exist in our environment. She adds what she learned: ask your doctor about probiotics alongside antibiotics; and some say a C-section or cutting the cord early means a baby receives less of the mother's biome.

The message: restoring the lost biome (condensed)

"You can create in a lab a natural system... mimicking the human body. Then you can add some microbes, do testing and keep the data... some of these extinct biomes will reappear and re-emerge." "In the field of consciousness frequency exists... Each biome has its vibration, and you can reproduce it via sound frequency." Measure the missing microbe's frequency from a sample, give that signal in a sterile environment, and "within three months, even three days, you're going to see that bacteria re-emerging". Collect samples from tribes and ancestral families who kept biomes the wider population lost; use AI and live electron microscopy to guide analysis; perhaps nano to place them in the colon. "No human body is the same... similar but not the same": each person must be tested individually, by region and environment. Doctors who do this testing shouldn't charge extreme amounts: the depleted biome is "a pandemic".

#19.5Eat by your region

Eat what your region produces, including local bee pollen, since your biome adapts to your environment. Children who play in clean, unpoisoned soil and breathe fresh air build stronger biomes.

#19.6The skin's biome

Your skin is your largest organ, home to billions of microbes that protect you. For herself, she was told to wash the whole body about every fourth day with natural products and clean water (a scrub of sea salt or aluminum-free baking soda, then a natural oil or shea butter), avoiding chemical soaps that sterilize the skin and shift its pH. "If you wouldn't eat it, don't put it on your skin." Grounding by the sea and morning sunlight also support the biome.

#Questions answered

What does it mean that the gut is a second brain?
Gut microbes break food into compounds that, through the villi and blood, reach the brain and act like keys that unlock specific neurons' functions; so an imbalanced gut affects the brain. She points to doctors' explanations for detail.
Which formula of a particular probiotic do you use?
She isn't a doctor and won't recommend one; she shares only what was prescribed for her family member at the time and urges you to research and consult your doctor.
