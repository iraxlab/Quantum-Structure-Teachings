The big idea

The Council of Nine said that after every lesson is learned and every utopia built, it all eventually becomes "completely boring"; the only thing that fulfils the soul is love and the eagerness to help, create, nourish, give and teach. That is the ultimate power, and the frequency that moves you to the next level. The coming period is a birthing pain: everything hidden will be shown, and "God's prediction" speaks of shock, silence and then victory after victory. What appears in front of you must pass through you to be mutated: help, don't consent, share without arguing. The priesthood meditation, imagining yourself as water becoming light, raises you to the Christ (crystalline) state.

#46.1Why the future came back

In a parallel future timeline humanity went so far into non-organic technology (chips, bionics, a changed genome) that it could no longer reproduce. Using time-space portals they connected with benevolent versions of themselves and made agreements to open a portal to a timeline where the original human DNA still existed: the hybridization program, by soul agreement, to help them and help us evolve. Positive ETs found Earth's "black goo" by its signal and saw our timeline had been hijacked, keeping our DNA from converting to the next level.

#46.2The only thing that satisfies

Asked what happens after all the lessons, they said the only thing that satisfied them was to help other beings and worlds create, since many Earths in parallel realities were trapped and didn't know how. The pure frequency of love is what constructs it all. She notes the Council of Nine's messages sometimes merge with God consciousness: tapping the omnipresent Christ consciousness directly.

#46.3Dissolve and rebuild

To restore what was abused, something must dissolve and rebuild, like the phoenix from the ashes; the negative uses this too, but you can convert it to the positive. It is a birthing pain: everything dark will come in front of you; "every rock will be turned over". Last year was revelation, this year judgment, and 2025 the battlefield where negative is converted to positive.

#46.4It must pass through you

Everything is etheric; when something appears in front of you, the collective already projected and experienced it. To transform it, it must pass through you so you can mutate one hologram into another. If you see it: "Thank you so much; what can I do to help?" If you find yourself in it, you came to help humanity: go through the heart, with your power, without consenting to lunacy. Prepare sensibly (a couple of months of food, she suggests), though you may not experience it.

#46.5Share without arguing

If you don't yet know your gift, share information. Don't judge, don't argue: present it and let it go. The frequency is downloaded; when they later see it for themselves, the spark activates. Be "greedy for goodness": the more good you do, the more luminous you become, creating a new reality above the negative.

#The method — The priesthood meditation (as given)

  1. Count: "1 2 3 totally be; 4 5 6 goodness to be; 7 8 9 all is in me."
  2. Present water. Imagine the water; you are the water, "the cup of youth".
  3. Let it run through your body, vessels and cells, reaching the nucleus of every cell: the life force. Activate it now.
  4. See a constant, systematic current of light go through you and recharge all your being; you get brighter until your body is luminescent, too bright to look at.
  5. It ignites and spirals into millions of infinite particles of light, and that light is all of God. You are all-knowing, in the presence of Christ consciousness (Christ = crystalline bright light vibration): a stable, fluid state of being.
  6. Close: "1, 2, 3, so it is. 4, 5, 6, God is in you and it's new. 7, 8, 9, let it be. I am light."
  7. Repeat for up to nine days; you may continue the method every nine days.
Added safety note

Added safety note: the priesthood suggested water-only fasting for nine days alongside it, "if it is given by your doctor"; otherwise drink plenty of water and use the meditation. She describes her own fast with raw honey in distilled water. Prolonged water fasting is risky and must only be done with medical supervision; many people (including those who are pregnant, diabetic, underweight, on medication or with a history of eating disorders) should not fast.
