#The method — The nine-day feeling training (from her video "Council of Nine: Nine Days of Manifestation")

  1. Days 1–3: set your phone to buzz every three hours. Each time, ask: what am I feeling right now, negative or positive?
  2. If it's negative (or you're watching something negative), pull away and say: "I'm a hologram, thank you for showing me my vibration is low." Then immediately do something positive (a positive video, film or action) until you feel good.
  3. Days 4–6: the phone buzzes every two hours; repeat the same check and correction.
  4. Days 7–9: the phone buzzes every hour; repeat, training yourself toward neutrality so nothing negative comes in or out.
  5. Train like an actress: learn to really feel it. Also learn the first Galactic Meditation (the alchemy of mobilizing into manifestation), then replace the galactic family with whatever you wish.
  6. Never expect who, where or how. Consciousness mobilizes the most predominant coherent resonance, like ten magnets all shifting when one moves, so your positive feelings must predominate through the day.
