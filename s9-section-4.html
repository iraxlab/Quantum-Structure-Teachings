The big idea

When you recognize an evil system, don't consent by using it: physically look for, or create, new stores, schools, platforms and communities, and things will appear "out of thin air". Posting on social media is not enough; it pacifies you into thinking you've acted while you scream into the wind. The hive of the mind holds the field of all possibilities: choose your position in it from love, compassion, wisdom and a warrior mind that protects all living things.

#Key teachings

#4.1Drops, not a flood

Exposure comes "fractally", in drops, so people can retain it, get out of shock, research and transform it, rather than be overwhelmed into amnesia or turned against each other. Flooding people with misinformation is the evil's method: you no longer know whom to believe.

What you see is a hologram your own higher self projects to wake you up; to convert it you must act from the heart and physically. The Council: social media is an artificial construct; "tell me, what exactly have you done?" Posting keeps it a holographic image and makes people fight; meanwhile you haven't talked to your neighbor or parents, or helped anyone. You can say everything "but they will allow you to scream, scream and scream until your voice is gone": a pacifier.

#4.3Protect the children

Take a stand for children, even if family disapproves. If you distrust the school system, look for solutions: she pulled her children out after they agreed to take responsibility for their studies, and taught them what schools leave out. Home-based childcare allowed in some districts, independent schools and alternative academies exist. "There is no excuse"; when you start looking, solutions appear.

#4.4Integrity in new systems

Many new platforms, especially AI-built ones, copy others' work and pretend it's theirs. Those who steal intellectual property will collapse: what they project comes back, and they no longer match the heart frequency. Expect companies to rise and fall, and clinics and health options to fluctuate, until you find a system that works for you. Demand integrity, including in AI.

Many teachers and doctors are open when approached with research and respect.
