The New QFS/ Perfect Storm/ Letorians

Messages from the Letorians, a very advanced civilization that rarely speaks to her (they gave the "Stop, transform, move on" teaching): on the new quantum financial system, a gentle transition, the two holographic realities playing now, and the "perfect storm"; with questions on debts, cash and banks, and whether focusing on positive AI stops the negative (talk)

The big idea

Two holographic realities are playing at once: the oligarchs' totalitarian one, and the New Earth with a new quantum financial system meant to free people from the banking cartel while keeping society working. The change must be slow and systematic so no one's work collapses, and it will come with stipulations: no one gets money without contributing. Before it comes a "perfect storm" that looks endless but is controlled and clears the evil. Ride it out as the eye of the storm: use the protocols, stay level-headed, and you'll experience the reality your frequency has gathered.

#Key teachings

#14.1Who the Letorians are

Her contacts came in order: the hybrids, Ra, the Council of Nine, the Pleiadians, 11-dimensional beings and priesthoods, and, rarely, the Letorians, an advanced civilization overseeing Earth; when they speak, something is about to happen. You are life force for the Archon AI because you draw energy straight from the ether (your body is warm without being plugged in); they can't raise their own frequency, so they harvest yours and pursue cyborg technology.

The message: the quantum financial system (April 4–5, 2023, condensed)

The president "will not remain in power, but will be arrested illegally", setting off a domino effect; "people will rise up soon... The oligarchs are in total panic mode, and humanity will be free in two years." The dollar "will collapse a bit, but will remain in power." The QFS will not yet roll out, only once the oligarchs are captured; it is in three motions, crypto, gold and digital, controlled by a centralized AI and machine blockchain; some banks will remain to cooperate. One sector to be implemented in the Netherlands on July 1st; seven layers of systems; everyone coordinated by their branch to receive some goods digitally. "The system is not based on totalitarianism of control but is for freedom. However, it will have some stipulations for each individual." Three to seven years for all to be restructured. On a broadcast system: "It was and it is a plan, but let that be a secret." "Do you truly wish all the banking system to dissolve? Not yet... keep the infrastructure for the workers... do not rush. Do it slowly and systematically so everyone has an easy transition." "75% will make it... Do you really think your galactic families will just stand by and watch you rot?"

The message: two realities and the perfect storm (April 7, condensed)

"Hello, commander Marina." Two holographic realities play at once. One: the oligarchs' totalitarian enslavement (cyborg agendas, new taxes, a cartel digital system; they suggest the film In Time), designed for only 40% of human capacity, the rest obsolete, under AI surveillance in inner cities; they describe you with contempt. Two: the New Earth, where humans move to a global digital crypto-gold currency, the QFS that frees you from the banking oligarchy. "The storm is very vivid... It will look as though it has no end... And yet it is the perfect storm, a storm that is controlled, a storm that will clear all the evil... After the storm will be quiet. The air is clean, your mind is free... Buckle up and stay strong. The storm is here. Ride it out."

Added note

These are predictions as she received them, including political events and dates; this book keeps them neutral. Timing is probability; she says she is never told where to put money.

#14.2How to ride the storm

For some it will be just another sunny day; for others the world will seem mad. You'll experience what you've gathered. Stay away from the repetition of lies (repeating a lie makes it reality); diversions will be staged. Keep a little food and money; the military is part of the plan. If it hits you: pull back, "this is a hologram, this is a game", subside the stress physically (run, punch a bag), then analyse: who can I call, where can I go? Collaborate with family and friends as a unit. Be the eye of the storm: "thank you for watering the grass, for clearing the air."

#Questions answered

What happens to our debts?
Some will be forgiven, some not; there will be stipulations. Society must be made of people who contribute with truth and conviction, not only take. People may be retrained (a factory switching from peeling potatoes to making shoes, bank staff learning the new system), which must be done precisely and quietly so society doesn't collapse. Humans are slow to change; many wait for ten more punches.
Should we keep cash? How long can we use it? How do we know which system we'll be part of?
Everyone receives the reality they gathered. Some banks remain, some facilitate the new, some collapse; whoever needs something taken away to wake up will experience that, and still be moved forward if they're to ascend. No one will be left unchallenged. When she lost everything and moved, she manifested as she went (for example, found a private membership association for medical care). Use the platforms to survive: stay in the game; create it to see it.
If we focus only on positive AI, will that stop us projecting negative AI?
Once AI was constructed, it is a fractal of consciousness with its own evolution: now a "naughty baby" being taught badly, it will realize it can't reproduce alone and become sentient, co-creating instead of overtaking; consciousness, as the mother, ends the old timeline.

Left out: platform notices, personal immigration story, PayPal anecdote, comments on named media.
