The big idea

To ascend, the mind and the body must rise as one unit. Study and live the quantum manifestation protocol, and at the same time detox and move toward a living, plant-based diet, because raw fruits and vegetables hold a light frequency above the 3D structure. Practise the connection skills (automatic writing, telepathy, lucid dreaming), act from the heart daily, and expect to be tested. When everything you accumulate reaches 100%, your body's pulsation locks into a new, less dense location: that is the light body.

#Key teachings

#43.1Accumulate above the 3D structure

You were taught "see it to believe it" and given only a fraction of the information, so you vibrated in the 3D Archon structure. The 5D conversion is "create it to see it." Each layer (3D, 5D, higher) resonates differently, and the body spins with your DNA at the frequency of the timeline you hold. By accumulating higher frequencies from the prana (the ever-present energy source and force that never stops) through the quantum manifestation (QM) protocol, lived in action, thoughts and deeds daily, knowledge becomes awareness and the mind expands. The portal opens, but it isn't permanent until it has potency: see the protocol to manifest potency (S4 E9). The heart has the key.

#43.2Why diet matters

The negative taught that meat was the only way. Eating it is a sacrifice of another expression of yourself, which lowers the vibration and locks the body's dense field into the timeline; the DNA then spins to mimic 3D. You weren't told the body can draw from prana, fruits and vegetables and eventually become a light body. Living, uncooked food holds a higher frequency (organic and non-GMO where possible). But diet alone isn't enough: a vegan who acts viciously still accumulates 3D negativity. Mind and body must go together.

#43.3Connection skills, step by step

After you understand how reality works, practise automatic writing: a cheap pen, a notebook, a cushion under your hand; ask a question and write the first thought, until scribbles become words (it took her a couple of years). You are sending thoughts above the 3D structure. A negative thought is a "wrong phone call", not possession; try again. Then try telepathy (the first thought that comes), and lucid dreaming. Being kicked out of a place, or not leaving your body, means it isn't your time or agreement. Out-of-body travel is tricky; learn here first, like a baby learning to walk, because above the structure one thought shifts platforms. Try each: to be a master you have to know all of them.

Her instructions for lucid dreaming

Use a very soothing alarm. Before sleep, repeat "I wake up every two hours" over and over, until you wake by yourself about every two to two-and-a-half hours and remember the dream. When you realize you're lucid, don't panic or get too excited, or you'll pop out; stay steady and focused, then call in where you want to go. Notice your own key signals that tell you you're awake in the dream. (She learned in about two weeks, with books by Nick Barrett.)

#43.4You will be tested

Your guides and the ETs know you're preparing and will place situations in your hologram to bring the next knowledge, and tests when you least expect them, to show your true colours (S5 40). If you make a mistake, peel the layers, admit it even when it hurts the ego, apologize, and try again. People out of balance with you will fall away; but always try your best to talk and convert first, especially family. Connect with like-minded people.

#43.5The heart

The heart accumulates from the prana and from every collapse, and changes the DNA like a sequence. It opens the portals and creates your stargates, locking the timelines for your own galactic connection. She recommends Frank Chester's work on the new resonance of the heart. Signs you're on track: synchronicity brings what you need, and telepathic messages, dreams and writing come true.

#43.6Intimacy and ascension

In intimacy you exchange frequency with the person and connect with their accumulated timelines. So be monogamous and selective, and see whether the person is healthy; your DNA and light body will be the biggest commodity of the future. At some levels your guides may ask you to be celibate for a time, so you can shed frequencies accumulated from past relationships and hold the new contact; this is by design, not forever. Be friends first for some months. Intimacy is sacred; when the resonance matches, it is natural and joyful, and partners should talk openly about what each likes (see also S5 40).

#The method — her detox and diet protocol

  1. Run it with the QM protocol: study the videos from Season 1 onward and do the diet at the same time, so mind and body accumulate together.
  2. Elimination diet: keep a food book. Alongside daily juices and shakes, remove one food every two weeks (for example beef first while still eating chicken, fish, salads, potatoes; then chicken; then pork), as in her detox videos. Her own process took one year.
  3. Start gently: she restarted with nine days of Anthony William's (Medical Medium) protocol, book page 150, with its liver-detox smoothie, before juicing alone; after a week or two the body stops wanting regular food.
  4. Intermittent fasting (Council of Nine): month one, every Sunday; month two, Saturday and Sunday; month three, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Only if you are not diabetic; diabetics need specific protocols with doctors (she names the Tree of Life clinic in Arizona and the documentary on reversing diabetes in 30 days).
  5. Get medical supervision: blood work and allergy tests with a medical doctor, so you don't deplete nutrients.
  6. Care for the skin: what goes on the skin goes into the body. For a pimple, don't squeeze; mix about a teaspoon of clay with drops of raw apple cider vinegar (with the mother) into a creamy paste, apply with a natural brush, then use coconut oil. She uses raw, organic, non-GMO coconut oil as her only body cream, and organic, gluten-free products for the face.
  7. Learn the brain: study neuroplasticity and see the documentary The Game Changers.
Added safety note

Fasting, juicing-only phases and elimination diets can be dangerous for some people, especially with diabetes, pregnancy, eating disorders, low weight or on medication. She herself insists on medical supervision and blood work; please follow that, and stop and seek help if you feel unwell.
