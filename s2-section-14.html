Season 2 · Section 14

Q & A: Lessons, Tests and Where You Come From

Why you're told only part of your origins, why lessons can't be skipped, tests placed by guidance, a new message, brutal honesty, fear in readings, and many questions (live talk)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

Since you are the one particle expanding to infinity, you "come from" infinite places at once; a reading tells you only what you need now, according to your agreements. More is given as you complete your tasks. Lessons can't be skipped: fighting your own hologram makes it loop; accepting it and going through it, with proper help, is what brings the knowledge and the resolution. Guidance may test you with situations placed into your hologram, to see what you do from the heart. When the same people and problems keep returning, look at yourself with brutal honesty and override it. And remember that a reading reflects your field in that moment: fear and doubt tip it.

#Key teachings

#14.1Where do I come from?

You are part of the one particle that expands to infinity and collapses back; each little part becomes a hologram and a reality for itself, with realities within realities (see S1 1.1, S2 3.1). So when someone asks "who is my galactic family?", what can be read is the frequency you're running in this moment: you may hear "Pleiadian", yet you could just as well be Lyran, hybrid or anything else, because you are infinite. You're given exactly what you need in this timeline, according to your ascension agreements with your guidance; nothing more until you're ready.

So don't question when; do the work as well as you can, and follow what you're passionate about. That projects the frequency that eventually leads your guidance to say: "That person has gained enough knowledge; let's go." Earth is a classroom: the better you do here, the better the platforms you're given to ascend.

#14.2More is given as tasks are completed

Guidance (Pleiadian, Arcturian, councils) can give extra tasks to someone who keeps completing theirs from the heart, even if they did negative things when younger and then fixed them. The teacher's own path went from hybrid beings, to Ra, who introduced the Council of Nine; she heard them check the frequency of her heart, including how she treated people who were mean to her. There may also be long periods of silence (she held the downloaded HR material for many months), because timing protects people.

Trust is built through action: each time she acted on guidance, it proved true. And guidance doesn't always show you the end result; it helps you through the process, because you can't skip the lesson. In her family's health crisis, guidance insisted for months that a relative come to visit; it was that relative who noticed the child was seriously ill, which led to hospital care in time. A teacher has to master a lesson to teach it.

#14.3You can't skip a lesson: accept it

Asked how to manifest away a growth in the neck: "intervention". If you need a doctor, you go to the doctor: that's part of the experience. You can't just shift the reality away, because through the process you learn (about nutrition, herbs, the body).
