Q & A: Lessons, Tests and Where You Come From

Why you're told only part of your origins, why lessons can't be skipped, tests placed by guidance, a new message, brutal honesty, fear in readings, and many questions (live talk)

The big idea

Since you are the one particle expanding to infinity, you "come from" infinite places at once; a reading tells you only what you need now, according to your agreements. More is given as you complete your tasks. Lessons can't be skipped: fighting your own hologram makes it loop; accepting it and going through it, with proper help, is what brings the knowledge and the resolution. Guidance may test you with situations placed into your hologram, to see what you do from the heart. When the same people and problems keep returning, look at yourself with brutal honesty and override it. And remember that a reading reflects your field in that moment: fear and doubt tip it.

#Key teachings

#14.1Where do I come from?

You are part of the one particle that expands to infinity and collapses back; each little part becomes a hologram and a reality for itself, with realities within realities (see S1 1.1, S2 3.1). So when someone asks "who is my galactic family?", what can be read is the frequency you're running in this moment: you may hear "Pleiadian", yet you could just as well be Lyran, hybrid or anything else, because you are infinite. You're given exactly what you need in this timeline, according to your ascension agreements with your guidance; nothing more until you're ready.

So don't question when; do the work as well as you can, and follow what you're passionate about. That projects the frequency that eventually leads your guidance to say: "That person has gained enough knowledge; let's go." Earth is a classroom: the better you do here, the better the platforms you're given to ascend.

#14.2More is given as tasks are completed

Guidance (Pleiadian, Arcturian, councils) can give extra tasks to someone who keeps completing theirs from the heart, even if they did negative things when younger and then fixed them. The teacher's own path went from hybrid beings, to Ra, who introduced the Council of Nine; she heard them check the frequency of her heart, including how she treated people who were mean to her. There may also be long periods of silence (she held the downloaded HR material for many months), because timing protects people.

Trust is built through action: each time she acted on guidance, it proved true. And guidance doesn't always show you the end result; it helps you through the process, because you can't skip the lesson. In her family's health crisis, guidance insisted for months that a relative come to visit; it was that relative who noticed the child was seriously ill, which led to hospital care in time. A teacher has to master a lesson to teach it.

#14.3You can't skip a lesson: accept it

Asked how to manifest away a growth in the neck: "intervention". If you need a doctor, you go to the doctor: that's part of the experience. You can't just shift the reality away, because through the process you learn (about nutrition, herbs, the body). When the teacher tried for months to "project away" her child's illness, nothing worked and she grew angry; the message she kept hearing was "accept". "You are fighting with your own holographic imprint." If you reject a lesson, more of it comes: you loop. Go through it with the best of your abilities, find the right doctors and solutions; the journey is what gets you there.

Added safety note

Any lump, growth or ongoing symptom needs proper medical care. The talk's "readings" of illnesses in this session are not diagnoses. Its suggestions of MMS for a growth and for Lyme disease are dangerous: MMS is a bleach (chlorine dioxide) and should never be swallowed. Lyme disease is treated with antibiotics prescribed by a doctor.

#14.4Tests placed in your hologram

Guidance can test you by placing a holographic imprint into your hologram: a thought put in as a code (like a number sequence) immediately becomes a hologram, and suddenly you're in a situation designed by your guidance. In that moment you won't remember the teachings or the teacher; you'll simply act from the heart, and that is what is watched. Depending on what you do, you're given more, or you stay at the same level to learn more (see S2 13.5). Some people notice such an inserted situation only afterwards, when they reflect on it.

#14.5A new message

Given about a month earlier, as end results: court systems cleaned of corrupt judges; a new cryptocurrency code that won't allow manipulation; new technology back in people's hands; those who ran corrupt systems staying in the timelines they match, because they simply can't pass through portals of a higher resonance ("it's not a judgment"); and a new unity: "one nation under God you are", no longer separated by belief systems, respecting each other "regardless of skin colour", with control of the human soul only by the human itself. How you experience it depends on the hologram you run on (see S2 10.3).

The grids around the planet were removed, so the soul is free to ascend through platforms, connect with guidance or shift into a 5D Earth; before, a code imprinted in consciousness kept it looping in 3D. In 2020 a "correction on all levels": souls being born will no longer come from negative platforms (see S2 10.5). And the 3D code itself "has a virus": its spin no longer matches consciousness, so it collapses ("delete, delete, delete") back to source, to zero, to be recycled. The word "genocide" in the message meant this collapse of a code, not harm to anyone. "Freedom to all. Love to all."

#14.6When the same people keep coming back

If people from your past drift away, or the same kinds of people and situations keep returning, you are reprinting the same vibration until you complete it (see S1 1.5, S1 3). There is a program running in your hologram; to shut it down, deal with the differences, without judgment. Be brutally honest: write down exactly what happened and how you acted. Was it from the heart, or from ego, jealousy or anger? Those three keep recalling the frequency, and it comes back as a mirror image, in a different but similar situation.

Then override it: "If that person comes in 100 times, you're going to do it 101 times: what can I do to make the situation better?" This is not about staying with abuse: if someone is hurting you, the 101 times mean finding a way away from that person, through the heart.

#14.7Fear tips the reading

A reading reflects your energetic field at that moment. Someone asking out of fear and doubt ("should I cancel my trip?") tips the probability toward the outcome they fear, and the reading shows it. You can't be made unafraid by someone else: you have to shift your own emotion. And if there is a lesson, you'll meet it whichever you choose, so how you go through it matters most (in the same situation, one person laughs and another shouts back, and they go into different realities). Learn to read your own dreams, and to trust yourself.

Added note

For real travel decisions, check official travel advice for your destination, and take ordinary safety precautions.

#14.8Dreams are realities: act toward them

Every reality is true for itself: a dream, a lucid dream and this waking moment run at the same time (see S2 12.9). Whether you take a dream as true is up to you; if you do, take action in this reality toward the dream image: it already exists, and action crystallizes it into matter.

For lucid dreaming: when you become aware, stay calm, because excitement changes the vibration and pops you out. Then, in control, open a portal with your thoughts (your consciousness knows how), go through, and call in what you want, or call your guidance to be shown. A lucid dream is another vibrational timeline with the same modality as here. Study, detox and practise, and your guidance will decide when you're ready.

#14.9The body, and "immortality"

"We are all immortal; you just need to learn to switch and shift the vibrations." Your consciousness can already connect with other beings, but to hold their vibration physically, the body's plasma and gravitational field must rise, which is why detox matters. Over time, humanity's bodies will become lighter, more "silica", and we'll move through higher dimensions until it's a normal state. It is gradual, through diet, experience and learning. The teacher's own honey-and-water fast is not a recommendation: "please do not do what I'm doing."

Added safety note

Living without food, or on honey and water alone, is dangerous and can be fatal. Don't attempt extreme fasts; any detox should be done slowly and with a doctor.

#14.10Orbs, and perception

Multidimensional orbs will appear more often: at the edge of your vision, briefly in front of you, or in photos. Whether you perceive one as an orb or a reflection is your choice, not someone else's belief. "If it's in the structure, it's meant for you to see it."

#The method — The Brutally Honest Review

For people or situations that keep coming back:

  1. Pull out and see it as a program running in your hologram.
  2. Write it down, exactly as it happened, and how you acted.
  3. Ask honestly: was I acting from the heart, or from ego, jealousy or anger? No self-judgment.
  4. Neutralize it: acknowledge it, thank it for showing your location, calm to zero (see S2 13.4).
  5. Override it, every time it returns (101 times if it comes 100): "What can I do to make this better?"
  6. If it's abuse, the better way is distance: leave, through the heart, and protect yourself.
  7. Afterwards, practise every half hour for the rest of the day, and write down what you notice.
