#Questions answered

I never see Earth in dreams or visions, only other beings and places. How do I focus on Aria?
The sphere meditation above is the Council's answer: it is "a blueprint to you all, how that begins". Use the zero point (latitude, altitude and the void) and the feeling of the new Earth to shift.
How can we be sure the light beings we see in meditation aren't negative entities or Archons tricking us?
You are in control: every thought is a probability, and the emotion and belief of your heart decide which you create. If you find yourself in fear, neutralize it with gratitude (it's another version projected into your field), that's your zero point; then switch to a positive feeling, and the new sphere you create is positive. To manifest, it needs the power of 10 of your action and belief.
Is the concept of a twin flame real?
From your perception, yes; a contract can continue with a being across lifetimes. But you exist in infinite realities: you may be Pleiadian, Arcturian, a mantis or a reptilian. "Do not lock yourself only in one understanding"; if you wish to create a twin flame, so be it, "but it can get kind of boring".
How do we know when the shift will happen?
"The shift already happened." Embed the belief that you're already there, even when you don't see it; until then, you'll keep waiting.
In a dream mirror I see another person, not my face. Why?
Another version of yourself, a different being from the same fractal soul in another lifetime. Embrace it and ask more questions. The Aria codes are already shifting with new sequences: "we gave you the ingredients... become a master chef of your realities."
Objects have vanished around me, one reappearing a year later exactly where it was. Why?
Each moment you project yourself into a new Earth with another version of you; in that shift the object's frequency wasn't projected, so you "teleported" to a reality with a different sequence. Her way: "Oh, thank you for letting me know I shifted." Play hide and seek: feel from the heart, now, that it's already here, imagine it, give thanks so you don't shut the programme, and act by looking for it; be okay even if you don't find it (zero point). Practise like a surgeon, and one day you'll think of something and it's in your hand.
When we create it to see it, aren't we focusing on the future and leaving the now?
No: there is no future and no past. Both realities exist simultaneously, reality within reality, the particle appearing next to itself to infinity. You create in the now a sphere-blueprint, add the heart again and again until it's potent, and when you shift, the new reality is as you wished.
Is all there is a positive-negative matrix? Can we escape it?
"You escape the way you want to escape." Watch the negative narrative daily and your hologram vibrates on it; shift your emotion and watch positive information instead. "It's your choice."
In dreams I often feel left out of a class or event. Am I on track?
Switch the belief now: if you weren't on track, you'd never have found this material. Doubt makes you fluctuate (see S1 3); fix your emotion that you're already there.
If others recreate us when we go to the ship, why not when someone dies?
Many versions: some choose to experience the cells deteriorating and die, and may be trapped; others shift through ascension, with technologies such as med beds (see S4 6.6), or by raising the body's frequency. Even bylaws agreed with the councils can change with what you achieve: "ask for permission". They watch what each of us is willing to put on the line; those who do much here may be given more authority "in another era with them".
Will the new Earth have only the positive?
No: it won't be "all butterflies and flowers". It will be work: creating new societies and systems, teaching the new generation; the children coming hold 5D structures and won't allow manipulation. Its pillars will be those who did much in the name of humanity, daily, even speaking to one person with the best of their ability. Don't consent: there are online schools, or a few families sharing a teacher. Speak your truth; if someone has rejected it 10 times or attacks you, back off. "Share, help, give and love. And that's the new beginning."
