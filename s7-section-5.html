New Predictions 2024/2025

An unrequested Council of Nine prediction for 2024 and 2025 given in outline ("all cards on deck", the snake that whispers lies, the candle in the dark for New York), how to respond to deception (wait, hide your intention, strike once), and questions on a teenager's predictive dreams and what year it really is

The big idea

Much of this talk is commentary and a received prediction, given here in outline and as received. Its teaching: the negative side will try everything, but because mass awakening has raised the frequency, "if they're baiting you, you're not biting". Expect back-and-forth and a trajectory of learning rather than an instant end. When deception comes like a snake whispering lies, don't panic: acknowledge it, pull back, analyze, hide your intention and act once, clearly and decisively. Peaceful non-compliance and physical action, not hiding, are what allow help to come. Dreams that foretell events are increasing because consciousness is raising its frequency.

#Key teachings

#5.1Why the bait no longer works

Those who manipulate are geniusly clever and will try everything, but humanity is now so awake that much of it no longer bites. There may still be casualties among those who keep the "pacifier" in, by their choice. Those on the negative side who risk everything for humanity are promised a parallel timeline to continue ascension.

The message: 2024–2025 (condensed, as received)

March 2024: an "uprise of evil and all in place", attempts that will subside. May 2024: "control over the evil", though not a complete stop: "don't expect this to finish right away". About three months of struggle at certain US borders. "New Yorkers will hold candles in the dark soon. That will awaken them"; a lot of smarts in New York but big egos, "soon all of that will be challenged"; "use your money to help." November–December 2024: "all cards on deck"; "horoscopes of people will be challenged"; evil like a snake, "venom and whispers of hissing of evil lies... no mercy", to remain in power. Then silence and exhausted ease; 2025 brings big challenges and, from March 2025, "the fruits of your victory... much positive surprises... victory will be sweet like a peach rum." A new monetary system is "in place but on standby... not yet".

Added note

Added note: these are received predictions, kept brief and neutral; names, countries and claims about specific groups, events and religions are left out. Treat them as probabilities (see 7.3), not facts.

The message: how to catch a venomous snake (condensed)

"You wait and hide your intention, and you have one shot to strike a murderous blow right between the eyes and it's over." In practice: don't panic at the plot; acknowledge what is being done, pull back, analyze, and when you take a stand, make it so clear and quick that it cannot regain power. This is the chess game: give time so every player is exposed.

Added note

Added note: the "strike" is a metaphor for a clear, decisive, lawful step taken at the right moment, not for violence. She repeatedly asks for peaceful non-compliance.

#5.2Take action, don't hide

Help can only come with the consent and action of the people: stand up, don't consent to manipulation, act peacefully and legally. "It will not happen if you just sit and pray and hide behind the couch." Be vigilant and cautious; she was told the guides can neutralize hidden technologies with a little frequency, so don't let fear take over.

#5.3Compassion is what they lack

Those raised in such systems live by strict power and fear and don't know compassion; without it they cannot convert through the heart, raise their frequency and shift timelines, so they self-destruct when things get hard. Underneath religions and nations, "it's only human and we should love each other".

Added safety note

Fasting, distilled water and changes to sleep are her suggestions, not medical advice. Don't put a teenager on a fasting regime; anyone fasting should do so only with a doctor's supervision, as she herself says. Persistent nightmares and distress deserve a check with a doctor or counselor.

#Questions answered

My 18-year-old daughter has nightmares every night that sound like predictions. Is she getting downloads, and how can I help?
Very likely. This is increasing for many people because consciousness is raising its frequency, so downloads and the fluctuations between realities come through more clearly. She suggests detoxing, intermittent fasting (eating in a short window) and a regular early bedtime for about ten days to reach a good alpha-state sleep; dreams and thoughts become clearer. Introduce a young person to what is happening gently and by age: history and governments for younger ones, more for older teens, never frightening details. Young people are from the new vibration and are often very open.
If time doesn't exist, why does the Council speak of next years? What year is it really?
It is the year of the reality we collectively agree on. (The recording ends partway through this answer.)
