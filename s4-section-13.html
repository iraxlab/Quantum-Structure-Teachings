The big idea

You are the zero point at the centre of your matrix, projecting 360 degrees, and everything you see is a symphony of resonances, holograms within holograms. Zooming into one is also expanding into it, at the same time; what you perceive changes with how you tune in. So when a place, a job or a situation keeps bombarding you, it is showing you it no longer matches your frequency: pull back, give thanks, take action without conditions, leaving it open whether it changes or you go, and synchronicities will build the bridge to the next reality.

#Key teachings

#13.1You are the zero point

"Train your consciousness like you're playing a virtual reality game": pull away from the illusion, observe, and ask "can I switch that?" Around you, 360 degrees, everything (your mother, a creek, a house) is a frequency with a symphony inside it. She was shown that when you zoom into a hologram another opens around you, and at the same time it expands, because you find yourself inside it: expansion and collapse at once. The signals coming into the now change with the resonance of your consciousness, swirl together so fast they pop into matter; a castle in the distance is both zoomed into and expanded out. "You are the zero point that is controlling everything that is around" (see S1 1).

Perception changes reality. She showed an optical illusion of a train that seems to go one way, and, as you change your perception, comes toward you instead: "show it to your kids... how perception changes your reality."

#13.2Should I move?

The most frequent question in her sessions. If your workplace keeps bringing pressure while a colleague's experience of the same place is normal, that resonance no longer matches your frequency within the ascension protocol, so you mutate it with the quantum structure: pull back, know you're the zero point and it's a hologram, give thanks (you can't reject it), and override it, 1+1=3, with positive action, including looking for another job or place.

Leave it open

"Do not give a condition that your workplace is going to change." "Hmm, it doesn't matter, I'm going to do my best, but if I don't stay in that place, I know that something is there for me." Take the action to look elsewhere and be okay either way: you may find a better job, or the boss may be fired; both can happen. When everywhere you go (even the shops) the same thing doesn't resonate, that's a stronger sign to relocate. Then synchronicities create "a bridge": money, a call about a job, family with a house.

A negative event may itself be the synchronicity telling you it's not a good idea to stay: "Oh, thank you", not "I can't believe this happened". Under peer pressure at work, ask "what is your life worth?" Following wrongdoing for the money makes you part of the problem and builds the same frequency; trust, giving and sharing create the synchronicities instead. Knowing the deeper "rabbit hole" (the Freedom Awakening playlist) lets you spot at once what doesn't balance with you, and decide.

#The method — When a Place No Longer Matches

  1. Notice the pattern: the same pressure or imbalance, again and again.
  2. Pull back: "I'm the zero point; it's a hologram." Give thanks; don't reject it.
  3. Drop the condition that this place or person must change.
  4. Take action: do your best there, and look for the other job, place or area.
  5. Be okay either way: stay or go, leave it open.
  6. Follow the synchronicities: they build the bridge, moment to moment.

#Questions answered

How do we use last week's codes with the New Earth meditation? Codes first?
Say the codes at any time, even a year later: there's no time, and your ascension is in the now. Saying them on Earth Day adds potency because everyone says them together (see S4 11).
When you speak of layers, do you mean sound, geometry, light?
All at once: your sound is your hologram, your geometry is your hologram, a symphony of all of it (see S4 3).
When we channel our galactic family, are we using the ninth chakra?
All the chakras, interconnected like a chain of sound; every one must be aligned. That's why keeping your original DNA codon unmutated matters, to download from the source that you are (see the chakra question in S4 2).
What is the significance of 3, 6 and 9?
All numbers are significant; none exists without the others. 3-6-9 are exit points, but must combine in coherent resonance and in position (latitude, altitude) in your matrix, because position carries the sound that opens portals. And none works without the void, the stillness of the zero point: that's why technologies must be secured in a vacuum, with no frequency in or out. In manifestation, the void is neutrality: choose what you wish, but leave the structure open, without condition, so synchronicity can combine what's needed.
Why can some people who know nothing about quantum teleport or astral travel, while it's so hard for me?
Different agreements. Nothing came easily to her: she practised from childhood so she could teach from scratch; the journey builds the power of your knowledge, and instant manifestation would bore you. The thought "I can't manifest" itself blocks you. Say you're in power, drop expectations, practise, write down what you need. It took her two years. "We are an expression of your consciousness... you are even projecting us."
Should we keep repeating last year's codes, or only use the new ones?
If you feel to repeat, do; your higher self knows how much resonance you need. But not excessively: excess is another way of not trusting yourself. Repeat the quantum knowledge until you know it instantly.
What message is most urgent now?
"Stay in your hearts." Humanity won the war; you may lose a few battles: so what? Don't fix your belief on a timing. In the months ahead you'll see the worst and the best of your race, "no middle ground". When the negative squeezes you, even five times a day, it shows you your power: transform it and collect the bridge of synchronicity. "You are the beacons of light to show the path to others. That is why you are all here."
If we create our realities, why do our friends and family stay stuck?
Their projection is also an illusion. Let them express as they wish, without conditions; tell your truth, but don't change them. Change your vibration; you shift to another earth and project another version of them. Some stay, some go; leave it open (see S4 5).
Is hypnosis therapy a good modality to learn?
Yes, from the heart: it helps some reach their deeper self, though for others activation comes through an experience, a lucid dream, a friend's sentence. If something negative shows up, don't fear: sometimes evil must be seen to understand its view, held in neutrality without hatred. Even in 5D there are challenges, but as debates and courts, not killing. Today's "elites" are the dark ages' nobles with better technology: "in technology, you're still looked at as the peasant"; you lost your power by consenting. "Education gives you more perceptions."
Can we heal our body in lucid dreams, asking our higher self for help?
Yes, with permission. You create a healthy body as another hologram in a 5D structure; if consciousness holds that awareness in the DNA, the new reality overrides the old, and you survive what the 3D body couldn't.
I hear a continuous sound in my middle brain. Is it a signal?
Don't worry so much: signals from different sources. "Stop your WiFi, see what happens."
How do I know whether I've connected with my galactic family?
Every thought you put toward them is a part of you creating them now. Create that reality: connect telepathically, ask questions, write the answers, until you find the frequency of contact, with no expectations (see S4 7, automatic writing).
Should humanity change to a 13-moon, 28-day calendar?
Why put a condition on it? Many universes are harmonious without it. Stay neutral, imagine consciousness in synchrony of love, devotion and creation, and new systems will be born from that.
How do vowels relate to the seven tones and the seven colours?
Why only seven? Colours and scales are symphonies of layers, changing with location and reality. The 440 tuning was designed to throw you off your original frequency: research when it changed, and what Mozart and Bach tuned to. Tune instruments to the new frequencies and new music will open new portals. Sing with only vowels, any sound that comes; record it, and you'll find it isn't 440: it's aligned with your heart (see S4 12).

Yes, as she did. When you become lucid (her trigger was seeing pitch black, once a floor), focus on a location, and with the thought, open it "with a laser" from your consciousness, drawing a perfect circle of colour (her first wasn't perfect; focus harder). When the circle closes, a portal opens; walk in. You can also raise your body's resonance and bilocate from there or from here. Open portals through the heart, and have no fear, or your frequency, and so your reality, will change. "Only your belief system is putting you there."

"There's no past." It closed with the signals 001133445566778899.
The exercise: numbers into geometry

Play with numbers in pairs of the same (11, 22, 33... 99). Draw a clock face (12 at the top, 3, 6, 9). Take pairs, for example 11, 33 and 99, or 88, 55 and 66, and mark each pair's position on the clock; join them with lines, knowing the degrees, and you'll see a geometry. Combine three, or six, in different colours; then think of it in 3D: cut it out and fold it like origami to see your geometry. This is how you create structures by numbers. She also points to Marko Rodin on YouTube for geometry (vortex math).
