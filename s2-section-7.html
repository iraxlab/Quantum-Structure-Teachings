Deception, Lies and Taking Your Sovereignty Back

What a lie does in the quantum structure, why deception surfaces by itself when you live from the heart, how to respond without attacking, and money as a transition (live talk with questions)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

There is no small lie: whatever its size, the intention is the frequency you project. When you live from the heart and someone deceives you, your frequencies don't match, and the third reality between you makes the deception surface through synchronicity. For the deceiver, a premeditated lie splits the structure into duality: no longer resonating with the heart that consciousness is moving into, they fluctuate, loop and eventually collapse into a negative singularity, unless they choose to change, which anyone can at any moment. Your part: forgive, don't attack (going after them is going after yourself), but take your sovereignty back by protecting what is yours, then move on. And with money: earn what you need, and give freely beyond it, letting others give by their free will.

#Key teachings

#7.1Why this download came

The download came right after the teaching's own free material was taken, locked behind a paywall elsewhere and presented as "Energy Philanthropy". The message received was: take your sovereignty back. Energy Philanthropy never charges: support comes only from people's hearts, by their free choice (see S1 5.1). Asking for anything, help or money, as a condition overtakes someone's free will (see S1 4.2).

It also explains why original material matters: the downloads are given word for word as a sequence of geometry and structure, and copies can leave out what matters most (see S2 2.1). Look for teachings at their original source.

#7.2There is no small lie

A white lie, a small lie and a big lie are all lies: what counts is the intention, because that is the frequency you project into the structure around you (see S1 3.2, S2 2.7). A genuine mistake is different, and is forgiven. Deception that is premeditated, planned while claiming to act "in the name of humanity", is what this teaching is about.

#7.3Deception surfaces by itself

If you run on the heart structure, that is one frequency; someone who is manipulating vibrates at a completely different one (as an illustration only: 2 Hz versus 1 Hz). Between your reality and theirs a third reality is created (1 + 1 = 3; see S1 2.2), and it becomes the centre, the black hole. Because they aren't on your platform, the mismatch has to show: through infinite probabilities, synchronicity brings it in front of you. One person mentions something, another says something else, and you end up exactly where you see what is happening, though you'd never have gone looking.

So if you keep living from the heart, anything out of balance with it will pop up in your reality, and more so as things escalate. When it does, you have something to balance.

#7.4Respond: forgive, then take your sovereignty back

Balance it the quantum way: neutralize it by forgiving and pull back, but also take action to protect your borders so it can't continue. Ask respectfully for it to stop; protect your name, your work, your business, legally if needed; make a clear public statement if that's what it takes. What you don't do is go after the person viciously: the other person is a projection of you, so going after them is going after yourself, and it re-activates that frequency in you (see S1 14.4).

Then stop, transform and move on (see S1 7.1): even if they refuse, let it be. Whoever receives the material that way still receives it, "because that person is the entire structure." Learn the lesson and secure your borders so it can't happen a second time.

#7.5The download: a singularity built on a lie

From the heart, everything becomes singular: one unit, one structure. You stop fluctuating so much between realities, and the looping becomes zero point, stillness (see S1 2.1). You are the vessel, building moment upon moment; you are the quantum, because you are the whole structure.

"But contradiction is happening when you try to build momentum of giving through the false pretense of not giving conditions to your momentum, and yet you choose to ask for singularity that is created on a lie." In plain words: claiming to give freely while secretly deceiving tries to build oneness on a lie. That creates duality: the structure splits into two different frequencies.

From the booksDP prints this download in full, adding: "You are trying to create singularity on a lie… In this moment you are creating duality of false frequency realities… The fluctuating realities cannot merge with each other 1+1=3."

#7.6What happens to the one who deceives

Consciousness itself is in the heart structure, without conditions, and it is sending waves of heart frequency through the whole universe as it ascends to 5D and above. A deliberate deception no longer resonates with that. So the person starts to split; they can't co-create in the fifth-dimensional structure; negative experiences follow moment after moment, and synchronicity reveals what they're doing.

They fluctuate up and down, and the 1 + 1 = 3 of their realities collapses under the weight of the frequency they keep projecting, down to zero point, but in the negative: the negative side of the X and Y of the quantum structure, not the heart side (see S2 3.1). The download describes "silhouettes of false timelines created through diversions of lies" and "horizontal holograms switching so quickly but not collapsing": realities flickering too fast to hold the heart's frequency, so the person loops in an illusion and can't see a positive outcome while they continue.

But this is not a sentence: "You have the power to change that any moment. You are the creator."

From the books"You are in illusionary world that eventually will disengage with fifth dimensional structure and collapses in negative platforms of its own waves of deception. Remember this!!! You have the power to change that any moment. YOU ARE THE CREATOR" (DP).

#7.7"Does it not hurt you…?"

The download ends with a question to all of us: why still deceive each other through a monetary structure that is only the trajectory of an old, false evolution? "Does it not hurt you when you are in need of help and yet someone is plastering a false price to your suffering? Do you not see that all is love, all is joy, and is sharing with no conditions of false projections that create duality that separates you from the heart? The love of self and preservation is fundamental. And yet you choose deception. You must wake up… Be love, be joy, be giving."

#7.8Money as a transition, not a condition

Money is part of how the 3D money reality and the 5D heart reality collapse into one unit: we can't remove money all at once without collapsing everything, so we transition (see S1 4.4, S2 1.9). The key is free will: when you give or donate, it has to be your choice from the heart, with no one putting a condition on you. When someone gives that way, they send a fifth-dimensional frequency; that is the energy field to resonate on.

This is not a judgment on anyone who earns a living. If you are the main provider, keep your job and support your family; meanwhile, start bringing in the heart: help someone, each day, just because. As you do, the money reality and the heart reality fluctuate and then collapse into one unit, money and heart coexisting. And be aware that even spiritual teachers can slip into greed under pressure; spiritual titles prove nothing.

#The method — Take Your Sovereignty Back

When you discover you've been deceived or used:

  1. Notice how it surfaced. Synchronicity showed you; thank it for showing you.
  2. Pull out to zero point. Don't react from anger.
  3. Forgive and neutralize. Wishing them harm is projecting it into your own hologram.
  4. Protect your borders. Ask respectfully to stop; secure what's yours; take legal or public steps if needed.
  5. Don't go after them. Going after them is going after yourself.
  6. Let go and move on. Stop engaging; don't keep pulling their frequency into your hologram.
  7. Stay in the heart, and check yourself: am I attaching any hidden condition to my own giving?

#Questions answered

Someone has been using money to manipulate me, and lying about it; it took me years to see it. What happens to him if he keeps going down this path?
It isn't your job to think about whether he collapses; stay in the heart and don't wish anything negative on him. However clever deception looks by the old 3D rules, he will keep re-projecting the same frequency, looping, and won't move into the fifth-dimensional structure; eventually the outcome turns negative, because deception doesn't hold value (see 7.6). Your part: set your boundaries, take legal action if you need to so it can't happen again, and ask respectfully for what is yours; but don't go after him. Severing ties is right: as long as you engage with his hologram, you keep pulling his frequency into yours. Let go, move forward, and get your sovereignty back in whatever way fits your situation. (The talk offered to speak with the participant privately afterwards.)
I make products, so I must buy materials and spend time making them. And as self-employed people we have to earn a living. How does donation work then?
If you're the main provider, charge what you need for your services and pay your bills. Once you have enough, start helping freely: make a commitment, for example to help a few people each week or at weekends, and let people choose whether and what to give. Do it intentionally and keep doing it; your frequency will start to resonate with the heart, and through synchronicity a path opens where the two realities, earning and giving, collapse into abundance, perhaps over years. Don't jump in all at once without that foundation. Put boundaries on people who keep taking and never contribute at all, and on how much you can give without exhausting yourself. The idea that you must charge to be respected is a 3D illusion: giving from the heart brings a different kind of exchange, without pressure (see S1 4.4, S2 4.10).
Is the time it takes for someone to collapse, or for good to return, proportional to how long you hold the heart vibration?
Yes: it mirrors. Whatever you project from the heart comes back as the same vibrational sequence. What confuses people is the infinite: you don't know where it will come back from, or who will give it, or when. So leave it open, enjoy helping, and trust that it can't fail to return (see S1 11.3).
Most of my dreams are frightening: people trying to hurt or kill me. Do nightmares affect us, and is it because I did bad things in a past life?
It relates to agreements. If you can't change what's happening in them, work with someone very good at deep hypnosis to connect with your higher self; lucid dreaming or out-of-body work may also help, and the talk offered to connect the person with a group working on this. The message: "Do not put fear, dear one. Help is on its way; that's why you are here." (see S1 11.9, S2 2.12).
Added note

Frequent or distressing nightmares are common and treatable. If they affect your sleep or daily life, talk to a doctor or a licensed therapist; therapies such as imagery rehearsal work well, especially after stressful or traumatic experiences.
