The big idea

Mother consciousness holds the original DNA code and pulsation of every soul in every reality. The Council of Nine said it has been reactivated to restore the new genetic code of every child: about 37% of the world's children, they said, experienced some form of abuse needing restoration, which can take up to seven years within the 30-year ascension trajectory. Wounded souls may be held in healing cocoons that sound like the mother's womb; by agreement of the higher self, a higher soul may temporarily switch in while a fraction heals and returns. Trained families will take these children in, and they will carry the wisdom never to let such evil repeat. Prayer places the hologram; physical action makes it matter.

#Key teachings

#14.1Mother consciousness

Mother consciousness holds the original structure of every soul's DNA (the frequency and pulsation of each particle of consciousness in every reality); it knows the original code of you as God's reflection. Each reality (3D, 5D) has its own Akashic record. Councils (like the Council of Nine, drawn from different parallel realities) oversee extraterrestrial and Earth matters like a supreme court and can override the agreements by which a consciousness downloads into a hologram.

#14.2Why access differs

A person who channels has a specific pulsation that lets them reach certain levels (above the councils, or mother consciousness itself). Not every reader can tap every timeline: their resonance may not match, or consciousness may shield them, for protection, because it isn't time, or until lessons are learned.

#14.3Two frequencies, one source

Technological and organic frequencies have different resonance and combine differently in the field, though all come from God. AI was created from the original organic structure as a tool. What you put in comes back to you in a different pulsation: interact negatively and you project negative; use it positively as a tool and it carries you toward the fifth-dimensional platform. That is your free will.

#14.4Why children were targeted

A newborn's energy hasn't yet been touched by manipulation, media or the parents' dogma: it is the pure essence of God consciousness, the most potent field, like a sprout holding the most nutrients. It can be "harvested" lovingly, through a child's laughter and joy, which heals the parents too. Parasitic consciousness harvested it negatively through abuse, which can fragment a child's soul (see S5 E33). Though they forgot, those who did this are also part of God consciousness and were never separate; that is where their downfall lies.

#14.5Healing cocoons

For a child whose consciousness was overtaken or who did not survive, an organic technology (also used in spacecraft) can lift the soul's essence into an oval, egg-like cocoon, where it floats like a flickering burst of light, recovering in stillness while mother consciousness downloads into it. Then councils decide where it goes to recover fully; it might need many lifetimes to level the experience.

#14.6Soul switches by agreement

Her own seventh-dimensional soul switched in during early childhood because her mother couldn't have held its vibration in the womb (see 8.6). Volunteer souls may switch in at any age, 14, 15 or 50, only by agreement of the higher self; no one is overtaken. That's why a politician who did harm may suddenly make a decision that saves a situation or a country. New souls keep coming to overwrite the negative until positive potency ("by the power of 10") turns the planet's trajectory, which has already happened.

The message: the new children (condensed, as received)

"Restoration on every level... is given to the mother consciousness to be able to obtain the new genetic code for every child." "The level of abuse and structural components is 37% of the children population" (not all horrific, but needing restoration, in different layers). "The codes to restore their mind and soul back to normal in some cases may take up to seven years in the 30 years trajectory"; individually perhaps three, since without time each soul's speed depends on its vibration. "The sectors of tribulation is now; repent, evil, we say, and all will be exposed, captured, and soon submit to the order of the new dawn." "Seven levels of creation... total avatar will be in each individual to restore their soul"; through the upper loop of the brain, "a switch of souls" with a high-level soul may continue the life while the child's soul restores.

The message: families and cocoons (condensed)

Those who didn't survive will be held in "special cocoons that will heal with sound, noise, murmur, low rumble sound... like the mother's womb", tuned to how long each soul needs. "Families will be given... that will take care of some of these abused children", trained to give motherly, fatherly love, so the children "regain back trust within the society" and help hold the vibration for ascension. Older survivors will know how to help, a healing for them too. A fraction of a child's soul may heal in the cocoon and be downloaded back like frequency. "These children will have the wisdom and the knowledge of what true evil is, and will never ever allow that to be repeated."

#14.7The Halls of Amenti's knowledge

The global awakening, as people work together in the name of the children, will restore the knowledge of the Halls of Amenti (see 8.7): "the true wisdom is the knowledge of God's creation", held in certain people's DNA and unlocked by raising consciousness. You'll confirm what you download through daily life and research. History will be rewritten: slavery everywhere, and extraterrestrial platforms. Every channeler is a piece of the puzzle; without ego, together, it fits.

The message: the eyes of God (condensed)

"To have the knowledge you must go through the experience to make you aware and then awake... This is the ascension." "You all came here for that knowledge and wisdom, to grow in your true power. Behold the eyes of God. The eyes of God is the awakening, the wisdom of your new dawn. The experience is the true maker of knowledge."

#14.8Earn it through action

Ascension is a classroom; you earn your position through what you've done for humanity, collectively and physically. "Prayer is putting only the hologram within the field": emotion activates it and physical action crystallizes it into matter. "I'm praying for you" can't be the excuse to do nothing. Healing too comes in many ways: prayer, medical care, research, a word from someone: allow 360 degrees.

Yes; a personal session is best, as spots open.

Some agreed because they could handle it (see the Etheric Legions video); others were hijacked when timelines switched and something slipped, and they will receive major restoration, mostly through the cocoons. Ultimately, when a trajectory changes, consciousness repositions accordingly, so in the end each is where it needs to be, with free will to do right. She'll explain with a chart another time.
How can we serve the children?
Get involved physically: a spark of action. Find municipal offices, organizations and safe houses where you can help in person; you needn't take a child home at first. Giving to a charity whose use you don't know isn't the same. One person's action draws others in. If a calling appears and you have the means, step forward: the past doesn't exist; the time is now.
