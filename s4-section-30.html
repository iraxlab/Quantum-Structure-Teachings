The big idea

A cyborg body is not organic tissue: its molecules spin differently, its brain takes commands from computer code, and it can never reach the natural order of ascension. The organic body can. Your chakras turn frequency into the functions of your organs, "almost like a spacecraft", and your cells can take resonance from the ether. When heart and mind give the command and the chakras are in pure alignment, the cells spin higher until they match a timeline, and you shift. The heart is the gatekeeper: it knows the codes of the stargates and timelines. That is why you keep your body pure and accumulate frequency in the heart.

#30.1Cyborg vs human

The Council said the cyborg is "a two-way street": it could help humans with tasks, but it will have many glitches. It is not organic tissue: "it has a different molecular spinning and is not even made from the same molecular matter." The brain's neurons take commands from signals given through computer codes, "and because of that, you will be not human ever." The negative side may sell it as the end of dying; in fact "you can be replaced like a doll... you will be dispensable," and the body can never reach ascension.

The organic body can mutate its cells through the chakras. Each chakra has a specific function and analytical structure; it restructures frequency into functions and passes them to each organ, "like a spacecraft" (see S4 18.1). The cells have an organic frequency that can always take resonance from the ether, from parallel timelines, so you can learn to bilocate and to mutate.

#30.2The heart is the gatekeeper

Your thoughts, emotions and actions accumulate as frequency in the heart. As the heart becomes potent, it activates each chakra, each a geometry with its own vibration, in a chain reaction. When the chakras are in pure resonance and alignment and mind and heart give the command, all the cells spin into higher resonance until they stabilize; when the frequency matches a specific timeline, "you simply shift." At that point the code in the heart unlocks a stargate and a portal: you can download information from the proper stargates and timelines, bilocate, mutate. "It is the gatekeeper, your heart."

#Questions answered

How do we best integrate the light codes coming in now?
Hold the vibration in the heart and stay neutral. If you panic, pull back: "this is a hologram", watch it like a movie; the analytical process activates and you can mutate it (see 27, method). Practise: the nine-day protocol (S3 8), the Galactic Meditation and the Protocol to Manifest Potency (S4 9). Deal with family situations up front, ego out.
If I have negative thoughts I can't control in the Galactic Meditation, will they manifest?
"Very much so." Train yourself with the nine-day protocol, and have a "Marina day": the spontaneous protocol of S4 29. The physical is stronger than the thought at that moment, so get up, put on music, go into nature; when you've had enough, ask "what do I want to do right now?" "It took me two years of non-stop work" to build potency.
I'm experiencing time shifts: some nice surprises, some not. Why?
They'll continue. Some surprises come from your resonance, some from your expectations. Lower the expectations, stop attaching to one outcome, and the answers will flow exactly when you need them.
I feel I have hidden talents to heal. How do I unlock them?
Know they are already unlocked, and practise in the moment; if something goes off balance, continue until you catch the proper frequency, and it becomes fluent.
I saw a big spaceship in front of my window three years ago. How do I connect again?
Recall the memory and its vibration: you tune into its codes and make "a telepathic body phone call". With the permission of your guides and the contact, more will come. "This is not the first time you've seen them."
What about abductions where people remember long periods on other planets, then are regressed?
"We truly do not wish to tap into this information... it will put you in fear." There were abductions and memory changes ("look into the black fleet"). Memory can be moved into avatar bodies grown in water cubicles, in a solution you can breathe, using A.I. The technology isn't negative in itself; overtaking consciousness for an agenda is against the laws of consciousness, and "it stops now." They will intervene more visibly once humanity rises; the next two years are humanity's growing up "from a teenager to an adult".
If I build a new timeline focused only on me, does my interaction with others matter?
Greatly. Each moment you project another version of them from your resonance; it becomes part of your collective. Wishing to help others in your structure is vital: it builds the potency for your new earth.
I'm working toward contact with my guides. Is it just a matter of waiting?
"It's never a matter of time of waiting, it simply is." Know you already have the contact. What would you feel if you had it now? Hold that emotion in your daily life, with no expectations of where.
How can I understand the new language and codes in my dreams?
With practice. Learn sacred geometry and HR's meanings of the numbers; tuning into it brings more by synchronicity. "You already know it all."

The energies have intensified and seem to clear old energies. Will they keep increasing?
Yes, it's ascension. Before everything comes into balance, what is out of balance must be mutated; "you are in a tipping point... it's going to get a bit rough."

You are already getting out of the matrix: discovering this knowledge activates you. "You already made it. You need to create it to see it."

You always dream; you don't remember. Stress makes it harder: relax, meditate, practise lucid dreaming. Some manage it in two weeks.

Actions. The meditation is the blueprint; the heart is the laser that makes it pulse; daily action crystallizes it into matter: "the Trinity structure".

You raised your frequency, so whatever is out of balance comes in front of you. "You're doing great... correct it, pull back, mutate all that through the heart."
I saw energy swirling and shifting around me. Is that the quantum structure?
Yes: you glimpsed part of the structure. Your senses are your chakras; as they align, your perception expands beyond what you were locked into.
