The big idea

She was shown Earth as a living, breathing, etheric stargate: two fields spin in opposite directions, inward and outward, forming cones, a torus and a zero point, so that when the planet reaches a certain frequency it shifts to another dimensional reality, carrying with it the humans who resonate like it (like a magnet). That geometry keeps Earth on its trajectory as it reprints itself; an artificial parallel Earth built by technology can't calculate consciousness's ever-changing equation and will fall off orbit. The message: one day the hollow earth will be revealed, with its civilizations and strict respect for the Earth, but its beings saw us as primitive because we see the truth and still don't help each other. Stay on the heart's course and act.

#Key teachings

#17.1Earth as a stargate

Given as numbers and geometry: one field goes outward, one inward; spinning in opposite directions creates cones, a strong vibration and a zero point, a torus. At a certain frequency the planet shifts its whole structure to a parallel reality. You go with it only if you resonate like the field; the wave carries you. There are portals within portals into the hollow earth, used by various ETs.

#17.2How the orbit holds

As the Earth reprints itself moment to moment, a petal-like counter-rotating geometry holds its orbital trajectory so it doesn't wobble off course. It shifts slightly tilted, with etheric hieroglyphs (think of the Stargate series), tied to a mathematical equation that keeps changing with consciousness. Each time Earth flickers to a higher version, a lower version remains for those not matching it.

#17.3Why an artificial Earth fails

Large technologies tried to create a parallel Earth and partly succeeded, but that timeline can't calculate the real position as all of consciousness moves; like a spacecraft re-entering at the wrong angle, it will go off orbit and collapse, with cataclysms in that reality. That is not the reality the planet is moving into. Don't ask her to confirm disaster videos: you are the master of your own reality, and propaganda wants you on their trajectory.

#17.4Not allowed interstellar, yet

Humans weren't allowed interstellar because of how we treat each other. As you act in the name of humanity your frequency rises and matches the planet, which supports you step by step, shifting higher like a symphony. Prefer solutions to endless alarming news; fear lowers your vibration.

#17.5Glass walls between realities

She shares Bashar's words: "glass walls" are forming between reality vibrations. You may still see a reality you don't prefer, but it increasingly can't reach you; living your preferred reality makes you an example others can choose.

#17.6Doors you can't see

Some people with abilities pass through what looks like rock; an entrance can be in front of you looking like a tree, because it exists at a different frequency.

#Questions answered

How do the avatar energies come through our vessels into the Earth's core? Is that how energy is grounded?
Human and Earth exist as parallel realities. When your mind and heart become coherent and similar in sequence to the planet's, the two resonances become one (1 + 1 = 3), a portal extension in time space; you coexist in that timeline. Ascension is the same: rise as the planet rises, using the heart daily. No one doing it through the heart is left behind; guidance will nudge you, sometimes through separation or sadness that sheds the negative.
Are we creating the hollow earth collectively, since reality is a matrix?
It is singular projecting the collective: one unit of consciousness projects the collective, and the collective can project you, but it comes from one unit.
Can our bodies be portals that pass information to others without our knowing? Someone saw geometric shapes flowing from my chest.
Yes. Your body is a vessel that can shift and bilocate; you are a living, breathing stargate.
Are the Halls of Amenti in the Emerald Tablets connected with beings under the Earth?
Yes, and many more; there is a crystallized city. A planet that shifts through stargates needs crystalline silica in its matter. Earth was once a silica planet with tall crystalline trees and giants, when telepathy and bilocation were natural, until it was taken over and civilizations were repeatedly wiped and restarted for control. History will be rewritten, and some of it will be seen individually.
With bilocation, is there another version of you already living there? Can you change your past?
Bilocation is more physical and crystallized; out-of-body travel is the soul leaving through portals. Both require a certain expansion of resonance. Out-of-body travel meets many entities, some not in your service; she suggests waiting and practicing lucid dreaming instead, which is safer. Even then some locations aren't open to you.
How can we connect with inner-earth beings?
Based on agreements.
You mentioned the Elohim: do they live in the hollow earth?
Don't think of it as a person: it was given as a mathematical code, a sound wave, at "the edge" of the Earth's equation.
