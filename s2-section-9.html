Q & A: A Pleiadian Channel on Governments and the New Society

A first channelling with a Pleiadian consciousness: how old structures give way to new ones, why nobody is outside of you, parallel channels, healing with frequency, and many questions (live talk)

Big ideaTeachingsMethodQuestionsRememberReminder page
The big idea

A new consciousness, a Pleiadian from a spacecraft that is part of the councils, steps in to speak about governmental structures. Its message: the old structures, built for control, will stop working, and new ones will come up at the same time, so this is a smooth transition, not chaos. Your job is to hold your frequency and your dignity, without ego or judgment, moment to moment. Nobody outside you can affect your manifestation unless you engage with them, because everyone you see is a hologram in your reality. Healing and creating work best together: both sides raising the emotion at once create a third reality that expands; then you hold it in daily action until it becomes a permanent timeline.

#Key teachings

#9.1A new channel, and a geometry to tune in

This talk was channelled from a Pleiadian consciousness (named in the talk as Hashna), speaking from a spacecraft that is part of the councils; the Council of Nine (see S2 8) stepped back for this topic. The channel was done with eyes closed, so the new connection could flow without distraction.

A geometry was shown behind the speaker: two pyramids pointing in opposite directions. Moving in opposite directions, they create resonance, and the infinity sign given with them leads to the zero point (see S1 2.1), connecting with the Pleiadian structure "like a radio signal" to a spacecraft. Anyone watching tunes in and receives increased frequency at the molecular level.

#9.2Why the language is unusual

The channelled words are deliberately above ordinary talk: they are a sequence of vibrational platforms meant to merge with your new consciousness, so you can understand information at a different vibration. Less and less will be given this way, until you resonate on your own and can connect telepathically, "without seeking the help of somebody else". Contact with your galactic families will come individually, moment to moment; don't expect it right away. If you are here, in this physical moment, you have galactic family on this platform.

As always: it doesn't have to be your truth if it doesn't resonate with you (see S1 2.5).

#9.3Old structures stop working; new ones come up

The message, as given: society was built in layers, and from the late 1960s structures were slowly put in place for control (through governments, politicians and bylaws) while people were told they were free. What is happening now is the collapse of the negative inputs, helped by people's own expanding consciousness, by the ascension process and (in the talk's words) by "white hats" within the military.

This is not a collapse of society. A new law may be passed, but the program behind it simply won't work; a new system comes up while the old one dissipates, or people stop paying attention, "because the power is from within." It was prepared ahead of time, with the help of benevolent beings, so that nothing gets out of control: a smooth transition. Still be practical: a couple of weeks of food and some cash at home (see S2 6, S2 8).
