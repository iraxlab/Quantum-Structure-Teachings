Q & A: A Pleiadian Channel on Governments and the New Society

A first channelling with a Pleiadian consciousness: how old structures give way to new ones, why nobody is outside of you, parallel channels, healing with frequency, and many questions (live talk)

The big idea

A new consciousness, a Pleiadian from a spacecraft that is part of the councils, steps in to speak about governmental structures. Its message: the old structures, built for control, will stop working, and new ones will come up at the same time, so this is a smooth transition, not chaos. Your job is to hold your frequency and your dignity, without ego or judgment, moment to moment. Nobody outside you can affect your manifestation unless you engage with them, because everyone you see is a hologram in your reality. Healing and creating work best together: both sides raising the emotion at once create a third reality that expands; then you hold it in daily action until it becomes a permanent timeline.

#Key teachings

#9.1A new channel, and a geometry to tune in

This talk was channelled from a Pleiadian consciousness (named in the talk as Hashna), speaking from a spacecraft that is part of the councils; the Council of Nine (see S2 8) stepped back for this topic. The channel was done with eyes closed, so the new connection could flow without distraction.

A geometry was shown behind the speaker: two pyramids pointing in opposite directions. Moving in opposite directions, they create resonance, and the infinity sign given with them leads to the zero point (see S1 2.1), connecting with the Pleiadian structure "like a radio signal" to a spacecraft. Anyone watching tunes in and receives increased frequency at the molecular level.

#9.2Why the language is unusual

The channelled words are deliberately above ordinary talk: they are a sequence of vibrational platforms meant to merge with your new consciousness, so you can understand information at a different vibration. Less and less will be given this way, until you resonate on your own and can connect telepathically, "without seeking the help of somebody else". Contact with your galactic families will come individually, moment to moment; don't expect it right away. If you are here, in this physical moment, you have galactic family on this platform.

As always: it doesn't have to be your truth if it doesn't resonate with you (see S1 2.5).

#9.3Old structures stop working; new ones come up

The message, as given: society was built in layers, and from the late 1960s structures were slowly put in place for control (through governments, politicians and bylaws) while people were told they were free. What is happening now is the collapse of the negative inputs, helped by people's own expanding consciousness, by the ascension process and (in the talk's words) by "white hats" within the military.

This is not a collapse of society. A new law may be passed, but the program behind it simply won't work; a new system comes up while the old one dissipates, or people stop paying attention, "because the power is from within." It was prepared ahead of time, with the help of benevolent beings, so that nothing gets out of control: a smooth transition. Still be practical: a couple of weeks of food and some cash at home (see S2 6, S2 8).

You may see separation in many structures, among politicians, actors, even neighbours and best friends. The channel chose not to say who did what: the point is that you create the new governments moment to moment, by the timeline you tune into.

From the booksDP has a related download from the Pleiadians: some races used a "glitch" to manipulate humanity, and the councils objected "because no one has the right to overtake and manipulate the natural sovereignty" of a people. DP also advises: "give to all from your heart. Do not watch TV because full disclosure is happening now in fifth dimensional heart structure."
Added note

The talk also gave a prediction about the 2020 US presidential election and comments on particular leaders, the US Congress and the courts. In keeping with this book's neutral approach to politics, these are not reproduced. The teaching kept here: what you see on the surface may be a diversion, because "you don't give your enemy your steps", and the work goes on behind it.

#9.4Your part: hold your frequency and your dignity

"Your goal is to maintain the frequency of understanding, to hold the dignity in your consciousness." Don't put ego or judgment on what you see: it is an illusion, a holographic imprint, and judging it keeps you in it. Hold your vibration so you can shut the old programs down and move forward. Don't let yourself think anything negative: this is manifestation as you go, so stay on the same channel, moment to moment, every day (see S2 8.1). And everything is a game: your reality is a projection you shift through, moment to moment.

#9.5The ascension protocol

Consciousness runs on its own vibrational sequence for each timeline; this is called the ascension protocol. Those who are ready are given the proper sequence to construct and reconstruct their realities. It is activated in the body (through the pineal gland), which is why the detox mattered, though not everyone is doing it (see S1 15.4). Your thoughts, emotions and deeds then tune you into one of the many probable timelines of ascension.

#9.6Nobody is outside of you

Can someone else's consciousness affect your manifestation? No. You are the one particle that expands; as it flickers forward, you project another version of you, and the other person is a holographic imprint in your reality. It only becomes true for you if you engage in that frequency by believing it, going into fear and reacting.

So: give gratitude, go to zero point and shut it down ("thank you very much"), pull back, and switch your emotions, thoughts and deeds into the positive to overwrite it (see S1 2.1, S1 2.5). "Nobody is outside of you; you are the creator."

#9.7Parallel channels

You exist in infinite parallel realities and timelines at once. Think of them as different movie channels: in one you may die, while in another you're doing something else. All of them exist, and each is independent. But where two realities are similar in vibration, they are connected, because particles work like a ripple effect: something from one may be felt in another, in a dream or even in the body (see S2 4.2).

The same applies to the beings you connect with: you are running simultaneously in many realities, and a Pleiadian you speak with could be another version of your own consciousness. A higher being "is another version of you"; a channelled being is a spark of the same consciousness.

From the booksSD describes the same ripple at the origin of creation: sound vibrated through the ether, and "that vibration as sequence fractal created a ripple throughout the structure of consciousness existence", then multiplied and "rippled fractal and started to interact with each other."

#9.8Healing with frequency and sound

Every person's molecules vibrate differently. The chromosomes switch your frequency, flipping from the zero point; because they are connected with the DNA, the switch ripples through your whole field and changes how your molecules spin. That is how resonance is transferred to your entire consciousness. The talk added that much was done from the negative side to stop chromosomes, DNA and RNA from switching, which is why so much physical and spiritual work was needed.

So you can construct and deconstruct molecular structure with frequency and sound. Once the frequency of each part is measured for each individual, an organ could be rebuilt without touching the rest, because the body is connected through geometry. Two rules: the new resonance can't be the same structure, it has to override the old; and your consciousness must accept the new frequency, or the system goes out of orbit and needs regeneration.

From the booksHR: chromosomes "are the ones that regulate the frequencies when is out of alignment"; they adjust "the missing frequencies", and as the frequency evolves, "the chromosomes then will adjust accordingly to their vibration."

#9.9Why healing is step by step

Why heal some things by thought and others with technology? Because we came here to experience, and the transition is slow so we learn each platform; when you master one, you move to the next. It will be a combination of both. Instantaneous healing and manifestation need you to be totally in the heart, with no condition in your belief system, 100% believing, and fully free of old beliefs; that is hard for humans for now. Meanwhile, knowing it's possible is already training you.

Energy is also given step by step so the molecules can speed up without going "out of orbit". On a treadmill, you don't start at 100 miles an hour. Each step is a jump through a portal, a shift of timeline. When a peak of energy comes, not everyone takes it well: for those deep in the negative, it brings things to the surface and is a big challenge.

Added note

Healing by thought or frequency is a teaching and a claim, not a substitute for medical care. For any illness, see a doctor and use proven treatment.

#9.10Opinion, fact and truth

There is no fixed relationship between opinion and fact: anything is true for you if you believe it at that moment. The next moment you can switch belief, and you shift into a brand-new reality (see S1 2.7). To create a permanent outcome, you need a steady vibration of the same belief, and you have to take action with it.

#9.11Why the collective needs structures

We chose to live as a collective, and a collective needs bylaws everyone follows. When things change, the positive side of protective structures helps: "If somebody comes into your home and attacks you, would you just sit there… or protect your borders?" (see S2 7.4). Having chosen planet Earth, you can't reject the structures that exist now; you incorporate them. We were taught that what we see is the only truth; in reality, what we believe and think shifts us into the reality we choose. Even hard experiences let us know the difference between negative and positive.

#9.12Money: a transition through the heart

Monetary systems weren't only on Earth: a controlling consciousness created such systems across worlds and parallel realities over millennia. We will be free of money, but slowly: free-energy technology comes out, people and scientists are sponsored, until we build self-sustaining platforms where no exchange is needed. Until then we change it through energy philanthropy: support given freely from the heart, with no condition (see S1 5.1, S2 7.8). The only way to see abundance is to be the abundance. Don't reject money given from someone's heart: that would be rejecting your own structure (see S1 4.5). Engage with society and enjoy it, and transition through it slowly.

#The method — Healing or creating together

Given for a distant-healing practice shared by two people; it works for anything you create with others:

  1. Share the blueprint. Talk about what you're creating together.
  2. Raise the emotion as you speak, until you reach the summit: goosebumps, the body starting to vibrate.
  3. Do it simultaneously. Both or all of you, at the same time, in joy, love and gratitude.
  4. Give from the heart without conditions: no demands, and nothing taken without permission.
  5. Let the third reality form (1 + 1 = 3; see S1 2.2): your realities create one particle, which expands like glass shattered by sound.
  6. Hold it afterwards, moment to moment, in your thoughts, emotions and deeds, so it becomes a permanent timeline.

Not a collapse of society: the old structures stop working and new ones replace them at the same time, prepared ahead of time so nothing gets out of control (see 9.3).
Can someone else's consciousness affect my manifestation?
No, unless you engage with it (see 9.6).
When a person dies, do they die in all parallel realities?
No. Each reality is like its own channel; similar ones are linked by a ripple effect (see 9.7).
Why can we regrow some things by thought but need technology for others?
We came to experience every platform step by step; it will be a combination (see 9.9).
Is there a special activation, with photonic emissions, around March 21?
The energies were already activated; that date is their peak. Energy comes step by step, and each peak is a jump of timelines; for some it will be challenging (see 9.9). (The source the question quoted is left out.)
How do different frequencies in the body work together in harmony?
Each person is different; the chromosomes switch the frequency and it ripples through everything (see 9.8).
Is one particular election result the only way for things to change politically?
No: one leader is part of a structure moving to the next level, and much of what is visible is a diversion while the important work happens elsewhere. (Political details are left out; see the note under 9.3.)
When will we see these changes?
They are ongoing and never stop. Help adjusts constantly, more here, less there, because so much manipulation was done. "Don't you know you went through the threshold?"
What is the best way to work the energy field for the outcome I want?
Already taught: switch your emotions, thoughts and deeds, and the reality changes (see S1 1.6).
Any guidance for our distant-healing practice?
See the method above. Raise the emotion to goosebumps; do it together, at once, without conditions.
Why are women often more tuned in to emotion, and why do some people feel drawn to the same sex?
It depends on the timelines you're tuned into: if most of your realities were lived as a woman, they come together as one unit now; yet that's an illusion, since a woman can also be a warrior. People may choose to experience more of one energy to balance before ascension, based on their agreements. So there should never be judgment on gender or on who someone loves; there is nothing wrong with them.

Are we on the ships as our higher selves while also in the body?
Yes: you run simultaneously in many realities. Connecting creates a third reality between you and them, telepathically, or physically when your molecules vibrate in their sequence (see 9.7, S2 4.8).
