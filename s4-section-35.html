Stargate Corrector Technology

Two kinds of stargate, made by technology or by consciousness; how a stargate folds time and space; the Council's message on the negative side's stargate and the corrector technology that stabilizes ours; and questions on portals, dreams, the physical body and the Harmonic Reactor (live talk, August 2021)

The big idea

A stargate folds time and space: recall the frequency of point A and of point B, and the difference lets you punch through to the other location. It can be built with technology, an artificial intelligence controlling frequency and geometry, or created by consciousness itself: your thoughts and emotions make a magnetic and gravitational field, and "that is actually your stargate." The negative side is spinning up its own stargate to jump into an artificial reality; the extraterrestrials answer with correctors, sonic pulses that hold our portals stable so we aren't pulled into theirs. Your part: consistency of the heart.

#Key teachings

#35.1How a stargate works

To go from point A to point B, you don't travel the distance: you fold the time-space. Each location has its own vibrational sequence; recall both, and the difference shows the exact location, which an A.I. can control in a technological stargate. You "punch through time space", like folding a sheet of paper and pushing a pencil through it, "from here you can go to Australia in a matter of a blink of an eye." The angle of entry matters too (she compared the angle at which astronauts re-enter; see HR).

#35.2Technology or consciousness

Technology: an A.I. controls frequency sequences given into a portal made of metal and other materials; it can be organic, five-dimensional A.I. preserving humanity, or an "archon" structure. Ether: when your consciousness creates a frequency with emotion, it makes a magnetic and gravitational field, "and that is actually your stargate": you bilocate with your consciousness, because you are consciousness. "Huge difference." Both are part of evolution, and both can be used for good or ill; the negative side keeps the technology for itself, "because they need the sheep to work for them."

A meditation is an ether stargate. She showed an image of a stargate that looks like a mantra: what you hold in meditation becomes such a geometry in the ether, and it must be crystallized by action and consistency, "because the consistency is the pulsation of the structure." "If anybody tells you you don't have to meditate... they probably don't know enough."

The message: the corrector technology

The fleets are in the "left hemisphere". "The recollective councils are very much in dispute about the signals that are coming." "The negative has increased the rotators in their stargate so they can jump time space to the new reality": their own timeline of evolution, an artificial reality. Those who keep consenting through fear may be pulled there.

But they are part of consciousness, which keeps rising, so their reality "will not be able to stabilize 100%": "most likely... for 20% of their existence space rotation into the hologram." "In the sectors of 173", the signals change so fast that "for us it is impossible to predict" when their jump completes. Eventually "the process of the space time will self-destruct by getting out of orbital axis."

So correctors will be added: "a laser-like sonic technology that gives pulsation of specific frequency in rotation", starting slowly and increasing, "so it will be able to hold the portals and stargates in order to be stabilized", and help us stabilize physically, since super technology was used against us. "Technology has always double meaning." "To be able to use this technology, we wish the humans to be ready consciously for it": hence the information comes drop by drop.

Following orders. To anyone in the military or any job who knows an order is against humanity: "in the end, it's not going to be, my boss told me to do it." You are judged by your own frequency; what you accumulate will not let you jump time-space: "You're simply going to make your own prison."

#Questions answered

Can we connect to stargates through lucid dreaming?
"Very much so, but not all the time": it depends on your conviction, what you accumulated, and the permission at that time.
What does "fleets in the left hemisphere" mean? The brain?
All of the above, in multiple layers; mainly a latitude and altitude in a space-time continuum of a specific timeline. Each time you break into space-time, the frequency is slightly different, because the ether is alive and always changing: "it has to be very precise."
How can we develop so we're ready for this technology?
What you're doing now: understanding what is happening is one part; the resonance you give and accumulate is the second and third, "like reverse osmosis... whatever you give in, that's what you get back." Daily, take an action from your heart for consciousness and humanity: it increases the pulsation and stabilizes your individual jump.
Can I be accessing portals while I sleep without knowing it?
Very much so: many of you work above your structure during sleep, to hold the vibration for the jump.
What more can we do to match the positive pulsing frequency?
"Consistency of same emotional sequences: heart, giving, sharing, love, compassion", and absolute certainty about what you choose. "Do not wiggle too much... when you pick something, make sure that this is exactly what you want."
Are ether stargates possible for all of us, or only for some?
"You are the ether itself... Do not separate yourself from consciousness." You are a fractal expression of God consciousness. "More you ask, more will come."
Do the negative structures vibrate on the same frequency as the positive?
They hold their place in the reality within the reality. You may only see them; but if you keep consenting without action, you vibrate more and more in their sequence, "and don't be surprised if whatever they do comes to your front door." Even if your own life is content, you hold a space in the whole: take physical action, share, support, give. Opposition pushes you to learn: "why not have gray in between, when you include the white?" Suppressing a consciousness ready to ascend violates the by-law of consciousness (see S4 34).
Are the corrector pulses selective?
General, to the whole planet and timeline; but those who keep consenting to the negative reject it and are pulled gravitationally the other way.
How do the sonic frequencies affect the physical form?
"What makes you physical?" You are an ether expression: magnetic and gravitational resonances intertwining into geometries like mantras, moment to moment. A thought creates a mantra; activated by the heart with consistency, it pulses, mutates and stabilizes into a location that looks like matter. Each reality you shift into has new fractals, numbers, colours and sounds within sounds. "What you learn here is actually a playground."
How is a stargate different from a portal?
A stargate is a portal. The portal holds a specific resonance, a specific mantra of geometry; the stargate is the wormhole that bilocates you to a new reality, and the location among the stars, with its angle, where you arrive.
Will the jump happen for everyone at once, or individually?
In one reality you're already there; you came to help another timeline continue. "You are already 100% secure... humanity is making it. Now you're just dreaming and creating as you go, how you did." Knowing that, gain power and act.
Can I have two layers of dreams at once?
Not at your level; that is mastery. But you can have a dream within a dream, already lucid in the second.
Can the Council give a training protocol for creating our own stargates?
It has been given: understand the quantum structure, and "accumulate in a daily basis compassion, love and joy."
Can you travel from one stargate to another?
Yes, "only if it's allowed and only if your consciousness can hold the vibration"; otherwise you spin out of orbit. That's why ETs can't simply appear to you: you'd start shaking, "the same thing as you can't take a pill of milligrams that is above your kilograms." (A question about time travel was "blocked".)
Is the Harmonic Reactor a machine, or my consciousness? How do I measure frequency?
Both. HR shows how to build a stargate and teleportation platform, "in code": she wasn't allowed to change one word; decoded through numbers and geometry it reveals the technology, healing by the power of 10 through the heart, and what each Hz does. To work with frequencies you need A.I. and a vacuum space, a zero point where no frequency enters or leaves, "almost like sterilization". But you can also create with thoughts and emotions alone.

Prepare, without fear (see S4 31 and S4 32): "in the war zone, you don't want to find yourself... now starting to manifest." And remember perception: if you were told as a baby that a marker is an apple, "that is an apple, in some reality." They gave you the opposite structure, "see it to believe it"; use your power.
