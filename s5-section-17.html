The big idea

A healthy cell divides through coherent vortexes spinning in opposite directions. A strong, "pulsative, stagnant, repetitive" frequency, which she calls the G force, shakes that division out of balance, penetrates the nucleus and, with consistency, mutates the DNA away from its coherence with God consciousness; she was told this is one way, through frequency alone, that disease arises. Reduce exposure, keep devices off the body, choose 432 Hz music, and spend time in nature, whose sounds belong to the original blueprint. Thinking alone won't fix it: a technology-made problem also needs action and technology.

#Key teachings

In a negative reality your body takes on its frequency. If you collapse daily into the positive, you also receive the positive platforms while still coexisting with both: you open portals to information above the structure and become "like a little bubble" that moves through easily (see S5 13).

The message: the G force and the cell

The cell divides through vortexes that "rotate in opposite directions" and form a coherent vortex in the middle that splits, and splits again. A strong G force in a certain resonance "destabilize[s] the natural process of the division": it shakes, "like a double image", non-stop. It "breaks down the natural over-layers of the cell and then penetrates the nucleus"; because cells hold water, all cells synchronize to it. The division becomes "discoherent", changing the original DNA code "in alignment [with the] God frequency collective", and you co-create lower realities. "And voila, this is how [it] creates cancer cells", from frequency alone, before diet, air and water are counted. The G force arrives as a "pulsative, stagnant, repetitive sound wave frequency", on-off like a fractal; consistency transforms, the way one steady tone shatters a glass, and the way a lie repeated on TV becomes "truth". "The G force is active, but not for long."

#Questions answered

Can 432 Hz, ocean or birdsong recordings substitute for nature?
Yes, it is possible, but nature is more potent: you include the whole planet's structure, and that hologram is stronger than sound in your own room.
What were the "three days" on your board?
Three days of water fasting, together with time in nature, starts to shut down the negative vortexes and restructure the cells into a positive spin, starting the healing process; it doesn't heal you in three days. How long depends on how much you accumulated and how long you used devices. Then use the frequency-and-sound water protocol (S5 16), no more than three days, once a day.
Can't we transmute these frequencies with thought and intention?
Not by meditation alone: in this lower timeline you were bombarded with technology, so you need the full trinity (imagery, heart and action with consistency), and sometimes technology. That mutates your DNA to a new coherent resonance while you still stand in the negative platform.
What action can we take now?
Stay in the heart and use the trinity. Start distilled water if you wish (not if diabetic), find an open-minded doctor, and go into nature to download its original sound. Diet matters too: at a higher level even healthy food can stop being coherent with your new resonance; she named herself an example of not yet taking that action.
Is the type of light important?
Yes: light changes with your location through the vortexes, pulsating and layering like ocean colours. Every moment your body has a slightly different resonance and colour.
Does being with pets count as nature?
Partly. You exchange frequency through the heart because you love them, and that changes your pulsation. It can't replace nature; combine both.
Distilled versus spring or reverse-osmosis water? Can I carbonate it?
She played a video saying pure water (near zero parts per million on a TDS meter; reverse osmosis is close) has room to pick up deposits, unlike mineral or spring water, and recommending about a gallon (4 litres) a day. The Council told her the distilled molecule has no charge, so as a conductor it picks up the most potent foreign frequency in the body, usually a chemical, and flushes it out. "I'm not a scientist... look into it." (The carbonation question was not answered.)
Does "three days in water" mean water fasting and nature?
Yes. For detox: distilled water for three days, with a tablespoon of honey every couple of hours, but only after proper preparation: she took a year of elimination diet (removing one thing every two weeks) and juicing, using the hybrids' protocol, before juicing alone. See her detox videos.
Added safety note

Water fasting is risky for many people, especially with diabetes (her guides warned that blood sugar drops very quickly), pregnancy, eating disorders, heart or kidney conditions, or medications. Drinking large volumes of water quickly can be dangerous. Please fast only under a doctor's supervision.
