Pamphlet of Your New Holograms

The original blueprint your higher self holds; how, with permission, it can replace damaged DNA and RNA; the 90-degree entry and the three-to-five-day process; the chromosome's zero point in HR; and questions on physical feelings during ascension and visualizing the DNA (live talk, August 2021)

The big idea

Everyone who came into the 3D was bombarded through a shield of negative frequency, and many were damaged by it. But your higher self holds your original holographic pamphlet: the true code of your DNA, RNA and codons, preserved in the fifth dimension and above. With permission from your councils and higher self, which depends on how you behave and whether you keep consenting, that pamphlet can be downloaded through the heart to override and replace what was damaged. The download enters at 90 degrees and takes three to five days, and you may feel tired.

#Key teachings

She repeated the previous week's message for those who missed it (see S4 32): the timelines are secured, "almost like a birth"; don't fight, stay at rest but in control. And she reminded people of their power: if many walk out, pull their children out or file lawsuits together, "the core system will crumble... You don't know your power."

#33.1The pamphlet held by your higher self

The 4D is the merging line between the 3D and 5D timelines; within each, realities fluctuate and collapse positive or negative (1+1=3, see S1 3). If you keep vibrating negative, a shield of frequency blocks you from downloading from the higher platforms: "you never implemented these platforms within your structure." Yet above you, at the fifth dimension and up, a higher-self pamphlet holds "the true version of your consciousness... the vibrational sequence of your true DNA RNA codon", preserved so that you never dissipate. If you choose the positive, you begin receiving from it, and your outcomes are positive "no matter what they do". If something alters your DNA and you project a different resonance, the pamphlet can't be downloaded.

The message: the pamphlets of your new holograms

Everyone who came to 3D Earth entered a hologram with many glitches, inside the shield of a prison planet, systematically bombarded until they vibrated on the negative frequency and locked into it. "Everyone has an original holographic pamphlet code that is connected to your higher self."

To be reconstructed, permission must be given by the councils, based on your agreements and on what you accumulated: were you manipulated? Having seen it, do you still consent? If you woke up and stopped ("I see what's happening, I'm going to move here, I'm going to go there"), the 5D pamphlet code "which is holding the original code of your consciousness" can be downloaded to override the previous one, mutate the DNA and RNA, and change your codons. What they look at is how you behave and whether you hold the higher resonance in the heart, "because the heart is opening the portals."

"Correction of the counters of each connection into the field of 90 degrees": the shift into the new location (latitude, altitude, X and Y) enters a portal at 90 degrees; the new location has different codes and numbers, which reprogram the negative and replace the old hologram. The process takes three to five days; "you're going to feel tired and exhausted."

If the councils and your higher self decide not to override your 3D platform, because the behaviour keeps matching the negative, they will not interfere: that would be overtaking a consciousness. "You must hold intentionally in your heart and mind the new understanding" to reach the resonance of the new 5D pamphlet.

From the booksHR p. 9 and p. 60 speak of rotating the grid to a 90-degree position and joining particles "by 90 degrees' rotation vertical grid", when "spontaneous reaction will occur". The three days match SD p. 13 (nano recovery of cell degeneration): once the new Hz is given, "let it stay and let it stabilize by its self for 3 days."

#33.2When family members can't follow

This is where much family misery begins: one person wakes up, and others are oblivious or deny everything. Their shield was overridden (see S4 22). The Council called it the natural process of consciousness: those who stay behind create their own timeline, and those who resonate move forward. Love them and allow them their experience. Many on the negative side wanted out, "they're simply too deep", so don't judge. In the 4D, positive and negative fluctuate "like in a battlefield" until you lock into a constant 5D outcome.

#33.3What she learned in hospital

She described being admitted with influenza ("this is my experience... I'm not a doctor"). Her blood pressure fell very low, yet she walked and ate; the Council told her not to worry, that her higher self and heart were holding the vibration and replacing her hologram with a version from the higher pamphlet, and that her heart kept her circulation low so the virus would not spread quickly. "This is possible with all of us." If fear made you do something you didn't want to do, learn your power, take action, and don't consent a second time (see S4 18).

Her instructions: in hospital
  • Ask for a saline drip (salt and water) constantly, so you don't dehydrate: water is a conductor that carries the charge of frequency to every cell.
  • If you're given antibiotics, ask for probiotics, and eat something, to protect your gut flora.
  • Always see the doctor and check; then add what you have learned.
Added safety note

Very low blood pressure and influenza can be life-threatening; follow your medical team's treatment. Ask them before adding anything.

#33.4The chromosome's zero point (HR)

In HR (p. 33) she showed the platforms of realities and monatomic gold, which makes downloads clear as you go through portals; and (p. 66) how, with the proper technology, a genome can be replaced with a new one, "you can create life", or misused. As she was shown: where the strands twist and join in the middle of the chromosome, like an X, is a zero point that connects the codons into a specific frequency. At the moment of the twist a new code can switch in, and the whole body begins to vibrate with the new sequence; it takes a couple of days. Positive or negative depends on how it is used.

From the booksHR p. 29: monatomic gold "can take the mind to open all that it is, and the connection to everything is very clear... The monoatomic gold also opens every molecule to vibrate with clear signal."

Her own choice. For a student under pressure to take something to stay in college, she said what she would do ("I'm not suggesting you do it"): take a break for a couple of years, wait it out with family, and finish the degree later. She had pulled her own children out.

#Questions answered

Are there physical feelings during ascension?
Very much so. Downloading your pamphlet can leave you tired and exhausted. "But don't confuse that with sickness, that's why you always check with your medical doctor." She never self-diagnosed: she went to the doctor, then downloaded what to do.
Her instruction for women

"Do not detox if you're in your period, with only water": the flushing is so strong it may lower your vibration and make you nauseous and sick. Wait until after your cycle, then start the water detox (see S4 23).

Must we visualize the DNA and RNA structure to control outcomes?
"Oh yes, that will help a lot": you project the pamphlet of that reality. Include the heart and increase the feeling (the Galactic Meditation, S4 9); if you can't, use the "Marina day" (S4 29). Sometimes you must do things you don't want to, because the negative must be overridden.

Left out: website instructions, greetings; disaster news and the videos she recommended.

Remember
  • Your higher self holds your original pamphlet.
  • Permission depends on your behaviour and consent.
  • Enter at 90 degrees; 3-5 days; you may be tired.
  • Love family who can't follow; don't judge.
  • See the doctor first; never self-diagnose.
