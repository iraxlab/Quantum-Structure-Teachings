Quantum Manifestation for Our Children

A talk dedicated to the children: why awareness must become physical action, how one person's knowing shifts both their own world and the collective, why there is "no time" to wait, "backdrop" people, pain as block or gateway, influencing someone without overriding them, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

Posting "save the children" doesn't save them; facing it, transforming it and taking physical action does, by supporting the right people and places, after checking who really runs them. These are "our children", not someone else's: every child is part of your structure. The same principle runs through every answer: you don't wait for a reality, you feel it now, act now, and project it now; and while you can't escape what is happening around you, you can always create your own positive hologram within it.

#Key teachings

#27.1Awareness must become action

Being awake already places you on a timeline where, fractally, the children are safe; but "when you take the action, you're crystallizing the reality to become an actual matter structure". So act from the heart: give time, money or skills, and research first: "make sure who is controlling" an organization before you give to it. "They are our children": every child, as a soul, is part of your structure.

Support

In the US, report suspected trafficking to the National Human Trafficking Hotline, 1-888-373-7888, and child abuse to Childhelp, 1-800-422-4453, or the police. Check any charity's registration and accounts before donating.

#27.2One person's knowing, and the collective

How can everyone agree on world peace? You are the one particle, the main character, projecting a movie of mirror images. Embed that humanity is free and act on it now: you shift into your own earth where the people you imprint share that resonance, "and you'll see most of the people around you thinking like you". If one person decides 100% on a new reality for the planet, it changes their fractal; the planet as a whole collapses by its common denominator. "You cannot escape what's really happening in a resonance, but you can transform it" into a positive outcome for you, and the more you do, the more you connect with the planet's own positive timeline (see S3 15.2).

#27.3There is no time: it's now

With job and family, how do we find time for so much information? "There is no time." Stay in the moment and read the one article that genuinely excites you now: that moment turns into fractals and collapses positively. With spare time: "What would you wish to do now? Are you happy now?" Learn, express, help, give, by your skills. "You never wait; you simply are."

#27.4"Backdrop" people still carry soul

Are some people mere backdrops, without souls? Every choice exists in both versions (you went out, you stayed in) until your action collapses one into matter, which is how the council can read probabilities. Anyone you project, even as a background, "is a reflection of the one particle": it always has memory, vibration and some soul connection, though more or less of it. In the coming 5D you'll feel a person's energy directly, so "they can no longer hide behind word manipulation and beauty" (see S3 11).

#27.5Pain: a block, or a gateway

Can constant pain block spiritual awakening, or open it? "Both", depending on the person and their agreements. The council added that many souls agreed beforehand to very hard roles in order to help humanity, as someone might give their life to save others.

Added note

This teaching is about the soul's perspective. It never makes harm acceptable, and a child is never to blame for what is done to them.

#27.6Suggest, persist, never override

With a friend who keeps drinking and drains you: "You can always suggest. You cannot overtake someone's consciousness by trying to make them see your way, or you become like them." You may be persistent, "but that does not mean we make sure you do exactly as we say: we still give you a choice."

#27.7Food in transition

Vegetarianism isn't required at first: "it's a transition", and those who are ready will be activated and know when. Meanwhile, if you eat meat, pray for the animal given for your survival. Honey is "good, we approve honey": nourishing, with antibiotic properties, taken humanely from the bees.

Added safety note

The talk mentions moving toward water or "breatharian" diets. Living without food is dangerous and has caused deaths; make any change to your diet with a doctor.

#27.8Giving, and a fair price

On spiritual teachers who charge a lot: many begin with good intentions and money can become a source of harm. If you're the main provider you can charge, but so that people are comfortable: "don't wipe out their accounts". Charging more because you became popular "is connected exactly with the ego" (see S3 21).

#The method — From Awareness to Action

  1. Choose one cause that moves you, such as protecting children.
  2. Research who runs the organizations working on it (27.1).
  3. Give what you have: time, skills, money, a voice.
  4. Act in your own life: what your children watch, who they're with.
  5. Feel it as done: "our children are safe", now.
  6. Repeat the action regularly; it crystallizes the reality.
