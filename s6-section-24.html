#Questions answered

What are the guidelines for galactic intervention? Do they step in to protect Gaia and the children?
The Archon structure was so dense it looked like black goo: an endless black hole of perpetual motion, "vampiric", looping souls in their own friction and feeding on God consciousness. First missions vanished; Mars was an artificial structure linked to it. Every attempt left residue stuck to them "like glue". The breakthrough came when consciousness raised its own vibration: they rode that ripple to penetrate layer after layer. Help still needs a person or nation to have a matching resonance to accept the new frequency. That is why ascension is individual.
Do galactics follow events on Earth and learn from them?
Yes. Some, including some seventh-dimensional beings, chose not to enter, because the goo is dangerous, like nursing a highly contagious disease; don't judge them. Those who came, such as the Pleiadians, had suffered in wars themselves.
Does every human have a galactic family? Are they also us?
Yes: most souls stuck in the goo had higher-dimensional versions. Those versions connected through the silver cord to their trapped selves and downloaded information out, very slowly so as not to alert it. That was a secret the dark matter didn't know.
Can galactics die?
Some dissolve into the field and move on; some pass through portals and become another version, similar in vibration. To hold your galactic family's vibration you must raise your frequency and mutate your body (detox, diet): like training to become an astronaut.
Is Earth run by the Galactic Federation for souls' benefit?
At times, once it could be penetrated: soul switches and walk-ins ran the program. Be happy: your collective vibration is now very light. Focus on family, love and creation. A huge scare (of invasion) is planned but will come and go; about 70% of the collective is aware, and it will look like a circus.
How can we know our galactic family?
Through the heart: the more you act for others and the collective, the more you build the resonance; then come synchronicities, lucid dreams, a spacecraft experience. They may not look as you expect. Ego and greed have spoiled past contacts; they watch what you do from love and how you react to evil. They are always there, waiting for you to wake up and act.
How many galactics came to help?
34: spacecraft, interdimensional beings, reptoids, snake-type, angelic beings and the oversouls of those trained in the Mars wars. Beyond 5D you'll meet races of such high vibration that they bilocate in a blink. Every moment you shift to a new Earth, even travelling from America to Brazil.
How do we know how close we are to physical contact?
Stay in your highest expression daily; at your specific resonance, contact happens in a blink. Be patient, stay together and help each other.
How do we use 173173173 in daily life?
It is already in your consciousness; repeat it a few times if you consent. It carried the three secrets of how the goo was penetrated. Study, act positively, unite, and have no expectations of when. The planned invasion scare won't take full motion: "sit back and watch the show."
