The big idea

Humanity already made it; now you create how. To hold higher realities your cells must be restructured: first by detoxing with fresh fruit and vegetable juices (their structured water, H3O2, regenerates the cells), and only then by distilled water, which removes negative charge and conducts new frequencies like an electrical current. Nature mirrors God consciousness and recognizes your true DNA. AI can't predict you, because the human heart is unpredictable. And for America: restoration in waves over three to ten years.

#Key teachings

#10.1Two kinds of water

She points to a herbalist's presentation on water: plain water is H2O; the water inside fruits and vegetables (and mother's milk) is H3O2, a structure "like a key", gel-like. When she switched her DNA to water (S6 1), she saw the code become a gooey substance; now that makes sense. Her thyroid (Hashimoto's) improved on her nine-month elimination and juicing protocol, which she chose instead of medication, with a doctor monitoring; this is her experience, not advice.

The message: distilled water (Council of Nine, condensed)

"H2O distilled: positive and negative equals zero", complete neutrality. "When you consume it, it takes all the negative charge frequency out and allows the positive, the natural state of the body, to stay," making the healing frequency more potent. "The body has to be trained; do not give it to everyone." First, convert the body through fruit and vegetable juicing and elimination until the cells are clean enough to hold the higher resonance; then it can hold distilled water, and frequencies (as in wave genetics) can be downloaded into the cells. Water is the conductor that activates the cells like an electrical current.

Added safety note

Distilled water and juice-only phases are not suitable for everyone. Please do this only with medical supervision and blood work, and stop if you feel unwell.

#10.2Nature recognizes your DNA

"Use your trees, fruits, nuts, the original structure, to recognize your true DNA; Mother Nature is in total coherence with the body," because it mirrors God consciousness. Because we were poisoned, technology can sometimes help until you're ready, but they will teach you to do it yourself: she begged to be helped and was kicked out of a spacecraft in a lucid dream, because her own DNA had to hold the 7D vibration first. They are not saviours; they show the path.

#10.3Why the AI can't predict you

The AI predicts through machine learning under fixed conditions. Consciousness is organic, and human fluctuation is unpredictable; without compassion it can't model how you'll react. Each time you hold a different vibration, it scrambles its sequences.

The 30% of souls who shift will be with their higher selves, replaced by new original DNA, "in three to ten years," in three waves of three years each, with the tenth year for restoration and completion. Speaking as God consciousness: "Why should I, in God's will, let you rot in sorrow? It will never be allowed, so have no fear, my children... I, and only I, in one particle of force, will expand your collective by switching your new world." Of the secret societies, 75% destroyed; the 25% who tried and risked all to correct their wrongs may pass through: "use your powers to help, use your wealth to restore, and use your banks to give and not only take, so none of it will collapse." It ended in words she didn't recognize ("Aamino itonaro...").

This is her prediction as given; timing is probability. A smooth transition (digital, gold-backed and real money together, banks involved) is her earlier teaching (S5 25).

"Don't trust me": study and do it yourself, as she had to; you can't be on crutches. Abundance requires letting money flow (energy philanthropy).

#Questions answered

Changing my DNA sounds like a big lifestyle challenge, but my diet is changing by itself. Will we move into it naturally?
Yes: as consciousness raises its frequency you're activated by its reflection, so you'll naturally want a shake instead of bacon. Or you'll be jolted ("look what I did to myself") into acting. Mind and heart together activate the DNA. Even if you fail five times, the sixth won't fail; potency builds. The only failure is giving up.
Is structured water what you mean by this molecule arrangement?
Yes: fruits and vegetables give natural structured water your body knows how to take. Don't add structured water to everything; let your body tell you what it wants, as it tells you about people. Get medical testing (allergies can cause inflammation); use medication if needed, with herbalism and natural remedies alongside.
How do I create my new reality without interfering with others' free will?
Just live your truth in your shared space, without trying to convince: watch what you watch in front of them. You project it into the collective; in their reality they now hold the code, and later someone else, elsewhere, says the same and it clicks. Your underlying intention is the heart, not deception, so it isn't overtaking. You create a new timeline with a new version of them.
How do we protect ourselves when astral travelling or connecting with the Council of Nine or Galactic Federation?
She was never taught protection; she held an inner knowing and, in negative encounters, neutrally observed them as holograms. Be careful with out-of-body travel: many realities are out there; learn from experienced people (she mentions the book The Multidimensional Traveler). Trust a source through years of experience: is it kind, does it tell you to give and love, or to put something in your body or do esoteric magic? Your own DNA will know the truth through synchronicity.
