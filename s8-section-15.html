The big idea

The Council of Nine said the world is passing through a transition into a digital system: some systems will crash and be used to bring it in, and for some that will feel like the end of wealth, for others the beginning of great wealth. Those seeking global control will reach only about 30% (certain countries or cities); about 70% of humanity will experience digitalization positively on the ascension trajectory. Eventually money itself will be outgrown. Your part: stay calm in the eye of the storm, be smart and wary of crooks, and when something blocks your freedom, look for the solution: it will appear, because in the five-dimensional matrix it is always available.

#Key teachings

She teaches the quantum structure, not investing. #15.2Real coders will see

Those who will navigate digitalization well are people who truly know how to code, not those who copy and paste code they don't understand, which crashes when one piece changes. As DP says, the new financial system will span many platforms and many programming languages; gifted people will create new languages and secure systems, and one system will rise to stabilize against manipulation.

#15.3A transition by design

Some markets will collapse during the transition from one system to another. It was necessary because the old system was spiraling down by design, draining people. No single leader is the savior; each is part of the process. Allow it to settle. The negative will try to grab and steal from the system while others use it for freedom: a chess game over about five years.

#15.6When something blocks you

If a transfer or account is blocked, don't panic: calm down, know it's your reality, and look for another way; a new service or person tends to appear. Losing something may be the lesson that lets you rebuild from the ashes. Those who lost jobs may have been moved out of a controlling system on purpose.

#15.7"It's a hologram"

When chaos appears, pull back and say: it's a hologram. Do I have food? Water? Clothes? A home? Then I have everything I need, and each step I create something positive. Everyone will face similar scenarios; individually you choose what to implement.

#The method — When the system blocks you (as she does it)

  1. Pull back and remind yourself: it's a hologram.
  2. Check what you have: food, water, clothes, a home. You have what you need.
  3. Stay calm; don't panic or blame.
  4. Physically look for another way, knowing a solution exists in the five-dimensional matrix.
  5. Accept the help or new system that appears, and use your common sense.
  6. If you lost something, treat it as a new beginning and rebuild step by step.
