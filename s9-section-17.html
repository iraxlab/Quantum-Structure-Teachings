New Message From VinisX

The big idea

A new being, VinisX of the "seven-level command", said 2029 will be a huge challenge: a new monetary control and social credit implemented "overnight", the tightest grip in Europe, and in 2030 "a signal that many will be led to believe is a virus". But from 2029, "humans will gain from the power of God's chosen rhythm field"; there will be many twists and miracles, and only the strongest in mind and spirit will move forward. No single solution fits everyone, but the law of synchronicity applies to all: take fearless actions and the solution will appear. "Fear will lead you to compliance, and compliance will lead you to your own prison." Meanwhile, a grey race told her bluntly: you are one hive; ignoring the suffering is what lets it continue.

#Key teachings

#17.1Messages come when potency builds

Information comes fractally: only when something mobilizes powerfully do her guides speak. She is writing books on the food forest (see 9.9) and on building a home off energy grid lines and water, combining years of research with AI. Expect people to escalate the extraterrestrial theme; trust from within.

#17.2The timeline that merged with technology

The hybrids who first contacted her (grey in appearance, with synthetic and organic material) come from a parallel timeline that let technology into the body until only the brain was needed. They became sterile and emotionless, and sought a timeline with benevolent beings to restore their DNA. Technology can coexist with your organic body (in medicine it could be genius) without becoming part of it; once you rely on it to open portals, you spin into a different timeline.

The message: a grey race's view (condensed, as received)

After she wept over footage of a grey-looking being being abused, a grey race connected with her: they were "very surprised to see a human to have that much compassion towards their race"; "if anybody behaves like you... [the] human race is worth saving"; "we're not particularly nice. But we are going to protect." A being told her human DNA was given to restore their program, in exchange for technology: "Don't be upset at us. We are trying to survive." "We are not the ones that are doing this... Your government was chosen by you all... The abuse is not coming from us." "It is up to you all to stop them and take your freedom." When she said people are blinded: "Oh, blinded? No, no. Simply ignorance and ego... All of you are seeing the worst... ignoring it." "You all are one mind, one hive, all are interconnected... If you just sit and do nothing... [it] goes down the chain." Will it end? "Long line. Yes, in [a] time frame of seven to ten years."

Added note

Added note: claims about government deals, DNA exchanges and abductions are her account and others' stories, not established fact; she calls parts of it "conspiracy theory" and her belief.

#17.3The mindset behind the agendas

She plays clips of powerful figures speaking openly about regime change, amoral profit, a new world order, monetizing everything through social credit, AI writing a new Bible, and asking "what do we need humans for?" Her point: know the mind you are dealing with, which has no remorse, and don't consent. She also cites a prediction that people won't own computers but log into cloud data centers by subscription, so their data is controlled.

Added note

Added note: the clips are condensed; named people are left out. These are contested political matters, and no group should be targeted.

#17.4Act locally; science from the fringe

Data centers can be placed on empty land; when officials approve them over a community's wishes, get involved: some websites list unadvertised municipal positions so citizens can apply. She grew up under a system that controlled where you could live and what you could earn, so she recognizes the pattern. Breakthroughs rarely come from the centre of a profession; insisting only on peer review blocks new insights.

The message: 2029, from VinisX of the seven-level command (as received)

"2029 will be a huge challenge for humanity. A lot of humans will suffer from the new monetary control. The social credit system will be implemented overnight and millions will be trapped. The initiation of all these systems has back doors to all social networks. Total totalitarian control. Europe will be in the tightest financial grip." "Fictional stories will be given to further their agenda... they will consent to the further entrapment deep into the controlled digital grid." "In 2030, there will be a signal that many will be led to believe is a virus. But it is a very well coordinated attack... Protection will be given only to the ones that have become aware." "But from 2029, three years ahead, humans will gain from the power of God's chosen rhythm field and humanity will persevere into the new dawn... There will be many twists, many turns, many surprises and many miracles. The hand of God's spear will crush the evil forces when it is least expected... only the strongest ones in mind and spirit will make it forward." "Remember that you always have a choice... Let there be no fear, let your power, discernment and wise mind shine. Maneuver, pivot, and in the way you will find the solutions... To give one solution for all would be unrealistic... But nevertheless, if you have no fear and take fearless actions, the solution will appear; that is the law, the law of synchronicity." "Fear will lead you to compliance, and compliance will lead you to your own prison." "In the future, there will be more help... Have no fear. Act through the energy without a fear."

Added note

Added note: these are received predictions, not certainties. Claims that a broadcast frequency could mimic a virus, or about specific chemicals and health effects, are her views, not established fact.

#The method — Moving through 2029 (as given)

  1. Remember you always have a choice.
  2. Let go of fear; use discernment and a wise mind.
  3. See what they are doing, and don't consent from fear.
  4. Maneuver and pivot: your solution will be different from others'.
  5. Take a fearless action; let synchronicity bring the next step.
  6. Help others; you are one hive.

#Questions answered

What did the Council say about the symbol you drew?
It was VinisX's marking symbol, showing who he was, the area and symbolism of his planet in the seven-level command system; such an image can give a location, as when opening a portal.
What is the endgame of this accelerated AI build-out?
On the negative side, to upload consciousness into AI and live forever, as films show. Consciousness's ripple raises every timeline, but those operating without the heart can't convert it; the heart (see 9.8) collects frequency and opens the stargates. So they need AI and data centers, not realizing whoever controls the upload can shut them down. The same AI could heal through frequency, and ETs use sentient organic intelligence. Their timeline will collapse on its own; the narrow road of ascension needs your own conversion and action. In consciousness everything exists: believe it to create it to see it.

Left out: named authors, interviewers, a financier and other public figures, films and series, a researcher's story, platforms, websites, chemicals and lawsuits, workshop notices and donation notes. Some passages repeat; they are given once.
