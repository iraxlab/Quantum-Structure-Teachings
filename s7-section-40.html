The big idea

Those who built artificial realities (the black goo, AI) disconnected from the heart and can only rent a reality inside God consciousness; they use advanced technology so that people become living antennas inside their simulation. But the Council of Nine said every manipulated reality is still under God's control, and by the law of creation the most negative technology always has a positive counterpart by the power of ten. Your protection is the quantum structure: believe the opposite of what you're shown, create the reality you want, enjoy life, and stop the fear. And never present frightening information without its solution: it shocks the mind like electrocution and feeds the negative.

#Key teachings

#40.1Renting a reality

Consciousness always reprints a balance, the positive by the power of ten, or the negative would spin into a black hole. Those locked in AI-built multiverses projected realities from the sequences they were given, becoming prisoners of their own projection, with memory erased. Their makers can no longer convert through the heart, so they create artificial realities to coexist; they are under God's umbrella, "renting a reality" the owner can end at any point.

#40.2Living antennas

She was told nanoparticles in the air and technologies combining sound and light can turn people into living antennas, receivers through which thoughts can be broadcast to lock them in. "A power without conscience is a savage weapon": the technology itself is part of creation and could serve the positive. Change the body's original code with artificial metals and nano and you create realities with a different spin, eventually robotic, self-reproducing and disconnected from God's osmosis through the heart, as the hybrids found.

#40.3The shield and the armor

Earth was surrounded by a shield like a fortress; inside, timelines upon timelines of holograms, like a cancer that must be traced multidimensionally. Generational psychics carried codes in their DNA, but were also manipulated. Since the shield came down, clear signals from God consciousness arrive. Guides can't bypass free will, and even if they raised your frequency your consciousness couldn't hold the faster flicker of their timeline: you'd lose sight of everything. That is why contact is small, or uses technology to hold your position, like armor.

#40.4How she was taught

She didn't follow her guides blindly; she researched, and the information proved true. After a year of seeing the prison planet, lonely and ridiculed, the hybrids said: "That's enough... let us tell you the solution and stop watching negative." Information came in pieces, repeated when people didn't understand, so the brain's neurons could rebuild and retain fifth-dimensional structure. All your other versions are part of your over-soul; never judge any of them.

God has a creation with the most positive, by its own power of ten." "Relax... Just create the realities you must have... Enjoy life and stop the fear."

The message: always give the solution (condensed)

"To spread information without giving the solution is destructive and very dangerous." It puts everyone in a distorted frequency and "shock into the brain circuitry of the mind hologram", so actions come from fear and self-destruction. "If you present information that is so difficult and scary, please do present the solution." Without it, the mind short-circuits: "you are electrocuting the person... like you pull the plug of the hologram they were existing in." "This is why we presented all the info slowly to you... so your mind can process, retain, and find and create the solutions." Then "engage in positive actions through deeds" to put a positive sound wave in your circuitry.

#40.5Why she withholds some details

She deliberately won't share some technical material she was sent, because without a solution it would put people into fear; if it is meant for someone, they'll find it. When frightening information spreads in the collective, consciousness balances it by making the solution findable for those who look.

#40.6Don't judge the manipulated

Many in high places were themselves abused and controlled by handlers (MK-Ultra-type programming), operating robotically. In a parallel reality you can see the positive version of someone. Stay neutral: don't believe immediately what is said about a person; watch for repeated holograms. If you see positive outcomes, question the source that accused them. Do your research.

#40.7Why it doesn't matter what they do

If guides were only manipulating us, why would they keep teaching how to get out? Predictions are probabilities; collectively we make it, each one by one (1 + 1 = 3). Be aware, try not to consent, be the best of yourself moment to moment, and you'll be pulled into the timelines where solutions appear. You are free, but you must know how to free yourself; that is free will.

Practice it and we are free.
