The big idea

The collective consciousness of the Council of Nine said that frightening messages (such as claims that the galactic family has abandoned humanity) can come from channelers whose frequency has dropped through fear, ego, greed or constant negative input: their signal distorts and they download a parallel Earth that didn't make it. That may be true in their reality, but it doesn't have to be yours. Negative downloads are like radio stations: if you don't like it, change the station. When confused, return to the quantum bylaws, because math and physics don't lie, and create positive realities through love, joy and action. "To betray the human race is to betray all that is"; negative cannot hold its position in the realms of love.

#22.1Councils and the priesthood

Councils oversee extraterrestrial existences, which may oversee humans; above the councils is the priesthood, a very advanced consciousness that makes decisions even for councils. Membership is based on what a soul has learned across all its timelines. The Council of Nine is a committee whose members live in different timelines; they never told her who is from where: "stick to the quantum structure", because its bylaws are like math and cannot lie to you.

#22.2Your own collective

Each person is a subatomic particle that expands into its own collective reality: within your collective, others create their own collectives through their own thoughts, emotions and deeds, one "blue", another "red". All are under God's umbrella, and now under ascension: consciousness is downloading new frequency into the whole 3D timeline. By free will you may believe it or not.

#22.3How a channel distorts

To move to the next level you must clear the old self's frequencies: forgive, forget and act anew. If a channeler sinks into ego, money, fame, or poor behavior toward family, their vibration drops, the signal becomes distorted, and they may download a pre-existing parallel Earth that didn't make it, then post it into the collective. More enlightened people see through it. In the ascension timeline everyone works together, because all are connected through frequency.

#22.4Don't reject the messenger

You can't reject another version of yourself, even one speaking negatively; their download is true in their reality. Like neighbors saying different things about you, choose whom to befriend. Stay out of the bickering and who-said-what: that's the old program. Their information, and even hers, is an illusion in your consciousness; hers is offered as constructive bylaws for you to create with. You are already free, even to construct being trapped.

#22.5Give a solution

If you share something negative, give a solution, as with a warning to a country: go out and do something about it. A message like "they left us" offers none and only spreads panic. The councils choose people not by fame but by how they treat others; staying in the heart, you're given more tasks.

#22.6Your choices are yours

No one can tell you where to invest; if you lost money, you can't hold another responsible for your own actions. Digital systems aren't bad, but who controls them, and when they become secure, is the question. Some people made fortunes and some lost everything during the pandemic: all lessons.

#The method — When negative information reaches you (as given)

  1. Say "thank you" and don't let it penetrate your field; it is their truth, not yours.
  2. Pull back: it is a hologram, and you can create your own.
  3. If confused, return to the quantum bylaws and build up again from the foundation.
  4. Change the station: watch, read and listen to positive information and people.
  5. Feel love, joy and the planet's liberation now, and take a positive action.
  6. If you share a warning yourself, always include a solution.
