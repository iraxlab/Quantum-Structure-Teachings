Her warning first

"Water fasting is dangerous for people; you cannot just go into it, your body has got to be trained." To reach this level: at least one year of the elimination detox, a connection with your guides (lucid dreaming, automatic writing, telepathy), and a doctor's supervision with blood work, "no matter what". Not for diabetics: "the sugar is fluctuating so much and you can get really sick. It's dangerous." "Depleting out of nutrients... could be fatal." She did hers under medical supervision.

Added safety note

Prolonged fasting can cause dangerous drops in blood sugar, salts and blood pressure, heart-rhythm problems and refeeding problems. Never do it without your doctor; don't do it if pregnant, underweight, diabetic, on medication, or with a history of eating disorders.

#Questions answered

I finished a 10-day detox. Can I start this straight away?
No: the body isn't ready. Go little by little, back and forth, with a doctor's supervision.
Can it be herbal-tea fasting, made with distilled water?
Not in a true fast: herbs have their own properties and sequence. The purest form is only water. Once you've reached that level you may indulge now and then (tea with a little lemon), but not before.
What should we do after the 12 days?
Stop, if you haven't done the detox protocol: it may not be healthy for you; it's individual and must be supervised. Follow the one-year elimination diet exactly.
Her juicing rules
  • Never mix fruits and vegetables: they ferment and you lose the nutrients.
  • After a green vegetable juice, wait one hour before fruit juice (she knows 45 minutes is said; she asks one hour); after fruit, wait one hour before green juice.
  • Still hungry half an hour after a green juice? Have avocado blended with distilled water, then wait an hour for the fruit.
  • Juice, not shakes: juicing has more potency. Start with no more than one cup; the bitterness flushes you, so stay home.
  • The elimination diet: while juicing, eliminate chicken in weeks 1–2, beef in weeks 3–4, and so on, with a doctor and blood work (see the Detox and Juicing Guide).
Should we start on the new moon?
You don't have to, but the new moon is more potent if you wish. Start any day, now. It won't feel like joy at first, "exactly when you start juicing with the bitterness": do it through understanding.
Do we do the 12 days continuously, or with breaks?
Start with the three-month protocol: month 1 only distilled water every Sunday; month 2 Saturday and Sunday; month 3 Friday to Sunday (see S4 14.3). You need to hold three days of water steadily before the next level.
What if we have no access to a doctor?
Without supervision she would not recommend it: "it's a mutation... you become like a new species". You may do one day, or three, and see how you feel; train for three months, then try three days on weekends; this may take months. Her own detox took a year. A nutritionist, herbalist or homeopathic doctor with an open mind can monitor you, with blood work; many doctors are afraid to speak about such results.
Can I juice non-organic vegetables?
Not recommended: pesticides change the body; produce must be non-GMO (see the documentary Seeds of Death). Weeds are superfoods, full of protein: look them up. Make a garden, go local, start a community garden, or grow at home with a small hydroponic kitchen garden. "I can't find it" is a code you project: say "I know I have it", and look; a new store or a nearby organic farm will appear (see S4 9).
Should we keep taking medications and supplements during this?
She's not a doctor: it must be done with your doctor, slowly, "the way you transition from steroids". She points to Dr. Janko (drjanko.com), a medical and homeopathic doctor in New Jersey, for weaning off with homeopathic remedies. "Never try to do it by yourself."
