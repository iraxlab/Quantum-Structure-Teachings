The big idea

The higher consciousness will now give matrix codes when needed: sequences of numbers that become a sonic geometry and a new holographic imprint in you, as protection from the frequencies you're bombarded with. #Key teachings

#32.1A push, not a rescue

"They're not our saviours." Because the times are so challenging, globally and personally, they'll give "enough push" for some protection, but "they want us to do the work": stay positive, go through the heart, learn the quantum structure (Season 1). Almost every human now has about three higher-consciousness helpers around them. To see that help you still have to tune in: understand the concept, then construct the matrix by action, thought and deed, consistently.

The request that came with it: for the next ten days, stay away from media that keeps talking about negative things, "who said what, how they did it": you already know what was planned for humanity, so you don't need to stay there. Positive things that make you feel good are fine.

#32.2A code needs your consent

A download is a hologram, "a reality structure that you can shut down just by rejecting" it. Once you consent, it becomes a spinning sound resonance that grows in potency and shows up in daily life, given "in fractal momentums". It works whenever you hear it, "a week, a month, even a year from now". It's given in such a small fractal push, matched to each person's vibration, that it never causes major pain. "Nobody is going to put in something on you that you are not ready" for.

Added note

She says: if you feel strong vibration, aching or pain in your body, go to a doctor; don't assume it's the download. And don't expect something major: some people dream, some don't. Go on with your life.

#32.4What numbers do

Every word and every number has its own sound, and a combination of numbers creates "a whirlpool sonic vibrational frequency" in the matrix, "a brand new matrix for you": the moment someone says 133, a geometry forms. So when you hear codes, simply read or listen and consent if you trust it. It works like a screen projecting a reality into you, but in ether, directly into the DNA, so it can override what you're bombarded with. Holding the code, you emanate it into every matrix around you, even at home: "you are an antenna".

#32.5Activated is not finished

Being activated "doesn't mean you sit back and do nothing". To sustain the matrix's potency so it crystallizes into matter, act from the heart. Your matrix is ether: lower its frequency and it becomes matter, like water's states (see S1). "Somebody can give you the book with all the codes, but if you don't read it and practise, it will not manifest for you."

#32.6Override a slip at once

If you watch something negative by mistake, recognize it and "immediately watch something positive": you override the lower hologram with a new one, "a mutation of the alchemy of your creation". And if a protocol (such as the ten nights, S3 31) is interrupted, just restart: "once you give the consent, it takes a life of its own". Slowly but surely, under the radar.

#32.7If one approach doesn't work, change it

"Timelines are hard to tap into" is itself a belief that becomes reality: switch it to "I'm strong, I have the information" and act. If one piece of information doesn't resonate, look for another. If one doctor isn't helping and you're getting worse, keep imagining and feeling yourself healthy and also take the action of seeing a different doctor. "Action is crystallizing the matrix, and belief is the sound-wave potency." Then override the negative every time you see it, daily: "I never said it's easy."

#32.8Create it to see it

You were programmed to "see it to believe it". You were given half the picture, the mirror image: the reflection is you, but not that you are consciousness itself. Believing it, anyone can project a narrative into you, and you consent and loop as a prisoner. The councils teach the opposite: "you have to create it through the frequency to see it." No blame and no hatred: "that's how they survive. You know the structure now. Transform it." Study Season 1 from Section 1, 15 minutes at a time (see S3 22.6).

#32.9End results are written; players change

When timelines change, do events change? "Yes and no." An end result can be "a law, a code" already written: humanity wins; consciousness moves up. But who plays which part can change; even someone chosen can fall into greed or fame. Someone with a mission is pushed back on track whenever they stray. Lesson: replacing a hurt with the good feeling of being admired is ego. So the timeline changes fractally, "but the end result will always get there". Hold the positive; another timeline, of those who consent blindly, runs at the same time.

#32.10What light warriors can do

"All of the above", and now especially: share positive things. Post, share and talk about what is positive, "positive and positive and positive", with your neighbours and online: that frequency is needed. And if you get upset: "thank you, I'm a hologram", pull back, remove yourself, and do something positive.

#The method — Receiving a Code

  1. Relax and close your eyes while the code is read.
  2. Decide freely: say "accept", or say "stop". It's your choice.
  3. Breathe in and out for a minute.
  4. Go on with your life, with no expectations; see a doctor for any pain.
  5. Act on it: thoughts, actions and deeds from the heart, daily (32.5).
  6. Override at once any slip into the negative with something positive (32.6).
