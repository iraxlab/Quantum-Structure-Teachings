The big idea

Consciousness, moving to its next level, activates more and more people to expose what was hidden: first one, then a second, a third, until it becomes an avalanche, even as each is discredited. Because of what everyone has been doing, the back-and-forth of the final battle is shortened from about two years to about one. Be patient, don't rush, and take action in your local community. Don't wait for people to wake up: hold that they already are, and be the liberator.

#Key teachings

#10.1An avalanche of exposure

This year many who witnessed abuse will come forward, willing to lose everything for the children. The first response is to discredit them as unwell; even well-known people may hedge to keep their own platform. Research widely, don't rely on one video, and don't point fingers: sometimes people say things to survive. Consciousness activates a second, third and fourth person, until it's undeniable: dark to light.

#10.2Act locally, legally

Some groups provide the papers and steps to hold local offices and school boards to account under the law of the land. Follow such instructions in your own area, with trusted legal advice. "In order your reality to change, you have to take a physical action, so you can create it to see it."

#10.3About one year, not two

Because of all the fluctuation and action, she was told the crescendo of the final battle will take about a year rather than up to two (see 6.32, Warp Speed Method, on shrinking timelines). How you experience the positive end result is up to you.

"Historical achievements were achieved. Negative souls have been told on every level: you can't continue. "In the crystal structural component on every negative soul, the pyramidical structure crumbled by not giving the proper sequences of the holographic signals." As your body's frequency rises it becomes more crystalline, like silica, and conducts the new wave; theirs can't convert the new signals, so their molecular spin shuts down and nothing they try works. "Now every challenging thing will go into manifestation of victory."

"Seven galactic ships on standby"; you will see more strange things in the skies, "but in the end you will know who is who". The good ones never invade; anything presented as an invasion may be a hologram or manipulation to start a perpetual war. "Get your popcorn ready." Every wall they build is crumbling. "Be patient... do not rush into anything... everything has very strategic position and timing. Take action and see what you personally do for your local community."

After being suppressed for so long, people don't know how to use freedom, even how to support those who serve humanity. We have to learn it step by step, with positive encouragement, and transition smoothly so workers aren't left behind. Suppressed technologies and true history will return gradually.

The message: closing message (condensed)

"This is the playground, this is the school... almost like a graduation." Achieve high honors and more opportunities open with your galactic families. "What have I done today in the name of humanity?" One positive act a day for one person calls in the vibration of the whole of consciousness. "Your fame, glory, and money is not going to be the criteria": what you collected through actions, thoughts and deeds is measured. "Ride the wave of that storm... allow the storm... and the storm will carry you exactly where you need to be."
