How To Literally Do Anything

What you send out — thought, emotion and deed together — is what you shift into. Change the frequency, hold it moment to moment, and let go of how it will come.
THE PULL OUT METHOD
  1. NOTICEHurt, fear or doubt = off-balance with love.
  2. PULL OUT"I am the structure." Don't react the same way.
  3. CALM DOWNCompletely, before anything else.
  4. BE GRATEFULFor what is already here. Don't reject it.
  5. LIFT & PROJECTFeeling, words, tone and action — all positive.
  6. LET GO OF THE HOWNo script. No expectation of outcome.
  7. ACT & HOLDMoment to moment, even if nothing changes.

If you slip, go back to step 2.

Remember
  • You are the structure. Nobody is outside of you.
  • Change the frequency — then hold it.
  • Know it 100%. A 99% download won't play.
  • Act. Be grateful. Accept any outcome.
  • "You have to know it in order to see it."
