Detox Protocol: Raising the Body's Hz

Why body and consciousness must vibrate as one unit, the medical checks first, her juicing rules, shakes, the step-by-step elimination diet, the gut, the heart as the key to contact, learning your guides, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

Your consciousness can rise with knowledge, but your body has to rise with it: the two must vibrate as one unit in synchronicity. Her detox protocol, taught to her over a year, raises the body's frequency "slowly but surely", one food at a time, under medical supervision. The deeper goal is the heart: an upgraded heart frequency holds the codes that open the portals for contact.

Her own words first

She gives this as her own experience, not a recommendation: "this is for entertainment purposes only; I'm not suggesting what you should do." She insists on a doctor and a nutritionist throughout, an allergy test and blood work before you start, and regular tests during it. Anyone who is diabetic "has to be very careful with this type of detox diet": the sugar level fluctuates so much that it needs a doctor's supervision.

Added safety note (not from the talk)

If you have diabetes, kidney or heart disease, an eating disorder, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take medication, don't start without your doctor: juicing can swing blood sugar dangerously. Juice-only, water-only and "breatharian" regimes can kill; never attempt them. Don't stop prescribed medication.

Step-by-step guide

The practical kitchen guide to this protocol (weaning off, the gut, ingredients, how to juice, the fruit rule) is the separate Step-by-Step Detox and Juicing Guide: its own PDF and web page, and the last part of the Complete Book.

#Key teachings

#38.1Body and consciousness as one unit

Each reality (3D, 4D, 5D, an extraterrestrial timeline) has its own body resonance, like different radio stations. As you detox, the body's molecules vibrate faster; as you learn the quantum structure (Season 1), your perception gathers the frequency of a higher reality. Combine the two and you imprint yourself into a new, higher reality moment by moment. "The heart structure, the consciousness and your molecular body have to be one unit." Then, even amid 3D challenges, you'll find the food, the water, what you need.

#38.2Her juicing rules

Use the juices exactly as in her detox videos. Never mix fruit and vegetables: leave an hour between a green juice and a fruit juice: because of their different molecular consistency they ferment and create mycotoxins in your body. It usually takes about 40 minutes to start changing into the gut flora, so after one hour you can take a fruit juice. Start with no more than one cup, finished within about ten minutes, on a day off near a bathroom: it's potent and may make you nauseous. Get used to the bitterness; in her experience it's what makes the body flush. Juice rather than whole produce, because breaking down fibre uses up energy, and fresh rather than powder, which has "lower potency" (powder only when travelling). The full protocol is about eight to ten cups a day. For the first two weeks, copy the green juices exactly as in her video, juicing throughout the day. She also made a frequency sound healing video of 20 minutes for each protocol.

#38.3Shakes

A shake is more diluted and less nutrient-dense than a juice, but filling: distilled water with avocado, with spinach, or with both; a fruit shake is distilled water with banana. If you're still hungry after a juice, have a green shake rather than going back to eating.

#38.4The elimination diet, slowly but surely

Write down everything you eat. Keep eating normally while juicing, and remove one food every two weeks: beef first, then chicken, then pork, then bread and gluten, and so on; dairy, eggs and refined sugar also went (she kept only stevia and raw honey). Vegetarians: about four weeks per item, including cheese. Weight drops slowly, and that is the point: "drop by drop", so the spinning molecules don't move "out of orbit", losing nutrients and making you sick (see S3 30.2). Test with your doctor every month or six weeks. If you feel nauseous or weak, go back a step: her own body "couldn't handle" a step, so she was told to return to fish, potatoes and salad for a few weeks before moving on. By the fourth or fifth month she was down to potatoes, fish, salad, the juicing (eight to ten cups) and the shakes. The whole process took her the better part of a year: "I detox for one year", and more knowledge was given to her, leading to the Harmonic Reactor and the Quantum Manifestation videos.

  1. Doctor, nutritionist, allergy test and blood work.
  2. List everything you eat.
  3. Juice daily as in her videos, plus shakes, while eating normally.
  4. Every two weeks remove one food (four weeks for vegetarians).
  5. Test every four to six weeks; adjust nutrients.
  6. Feeling unwell? Go back a step, and see your doctor.

Diabetes: she points to a clinic in Arizona called Tree of Life, whose medical doctors, she says, help diabetics recover through a raw diet and juicing, and to a video called "Reverse Diabetic in 30 Days". In her words: "I'm not a doctor, I don't claim this to be true, but I want you to do the research"; "I'm not recommending diabetics without a doctor's supervision to follow anything I say here." Go first to the medical clinic.

Added safety note (not from the talk)

Diabetes medication and insulin doses can become dangerous when diet changes quickly: make any change only with the doctor who manages your diabetes. People with type 1 diabetes always need insulin.

Instead of alcohol: she stopped drinking and smoking. In place of wine she suggests: cold-press grape juice and let it ferment for two days; by the third day it is fermented ("like a wine") and, in her words, healthy for the body, used as a juice.

Added safety note (not from the talk)

Fermented grape juice contains alcohol and can grow unwanted moulds or bacteria: avoid it if you're pregnant, don't drink alcohol, take medication that doesn't mix with alcohol, or are driving.

#38.5Look after the gut

Restore your gut flora with a good probiotic, taken with distilled water. She explains that VSL changed its formula and the doctor took the original formula to Visbiome, the company she now uses; if you're on antibiotics, take it about two and a half hours after the antibiotic, since antibiotics kill good bacteria too. Some stomach pain can come with flushing, but regular pain means stop juicing: with a nutritionist, switch to a soft, cooked, low-fibre diet with no spice, citrus or raw food: everything cooked, very soft, almost puree. She recommends studying the book Fiber Menace: when the stomach or colon is inflamed, less fibre but more nutrition is fundamental. Get nutrients from natural sources (vitamin D from mushrooms, for example); if you take supplements, choose natural, reputable ones, and keep monitoring.

#38.6The heart is the key

After her long detox and a short period on juices, she describes feeling her heart lifted out and returned, and hearing "your heart is being upgraded" to hold the 5D heart. The teaching: the heart holds the codes that open portals. Each portal, or stargate, holds a resonance, a code for a galactic location; you can't enter it unless your heart and consciousness match it, "like a key", and even technology can't force it (see S3 35.1). As your vibration rises, the heart unlocks code by code, monitored by your guides. Physical contact comes only after you already have telepathic or lucid contact, proven by synchronicity, and then they guide you individually, perhaps over years.

Her experience, in order: she was trained to go 15 days only on the juicing, "the exact protocol that you have in these videos". Later, after her frequency was raised, she was sick for two hours and cleansed; for about a week she lived on herbal teas, lemon and honey, then water with a tablespoon of raw organic, non-GMO honey in distilled water every half an hour ("honey is also food").

Her warning

She calls this "very advanced": "you do not in any circumstances practise this", "you may completely die". On the honey-and-water stage: "never, ever follow that protocol, because that is very, very dangerous." "There's no way you become a breatharian in ten days."

Added safety note (not from the talk)

If you vomit repeatedly or can't keep fluids down, get medical help. Never live on juice, honey-water or water alone: people have died.

#38.7Learn your guides

Practising contact is like learning to fly or make a phone call to fluctuating stations: at first not everything you hear comes true, so don't be discouraged (see S3 36.7). In time you'll learn your guides' personalities, how they act and speak with you, and the lessons they set, which differ with the level you're connected to (see S3 32.9). No fear: guides don't take this path with someone who is afraid.

#The method — Raising the Body with the Mind

  1. Get checked first: doctor, nutritionist, allergy test, blood work (38.4).
  2. Study the knowledge (Season 1) alongside: body and mind rise together (38.1).
  3. Follow the juicing rules: fruit and vegetables an hour apart; start small (38.2).
  4. Remove one food at a time, slowly but surely, with regular tests (38.4).
  5. Protect your gut; stop and adjust if pain is regular (38.5).
  6. Act from the heart: that, not fasting, is the key (38.6).

#Questions answered

Should dairy, eggs and refined sugar be removed too? Is the goal veganism?
She removed dairy, cheese, gluten and sugar, keeping stevia and raw honey. Work out your own path with your doctor.
How long does the protocol take?
Her elimination took six to eight months or more: see 38.4.
Can we eat the fruit and vegetables instead of juicing?
For this protocol, juice: see 38.2.
Should we take a multivitamin?
She didn't; follow your doctor. If you do, choose natural, non-GMO, reputable supplements.
We're vegetarian or vegan. What do we remove first?
List what you eat and remove one item about every four weeks while juicing, with probiotics and your doctor's advice on supplements.
Do the council feel emotions as we do?

That shows them how to advise you, and your bylaws. "We do feel your pain, we do feel your joy." Their consciousness is spread into millions of pieces, helping humans; "you are our ancestors".
Have reincarnation cycles changed for this planet?
Two cycles run at once, one toward extinction and one toward 5D ascension; when the timeline collapses you'll see the outcome.
