The big idea

The Council of Nine said God's angels and freedom fighters are already here. Over the next five to ten years, attempts to hold power through manipulation will fail: "even if it succeeds in one sector, at the same time [it] will fail in ten others". New systems of exchange (decentralized, untraceable, attached to blockchain and AI) will keep the integrity of each transaction so the evil cannot grab control, though it may seem so at first; hidden agendas will be exposed through AI records "in the blink of an eye". As digital control rolls out, do not consent from fear: look for the loophole, because one will always appear in your reality. It's a game of hide and seek: if a door closes, look for the next.

The lesson she draws: many crusades and wars, fought in the name of religion or nation, were bloodshed that should never have happened, and the same patterns of conquest (control resources, then starve and control people) have been renamed and repeated.

She shares accounts she sees as proof of a long-planned agenda: an insider's recollection that the ultimate goal was to put everyone on chips that could be switched off; a former banker's warnings about Agenda 2030 and 15-minute cities; and a country that closed many millions of bank accounts that weren't linked to a biometric digital ID. #37.3There is always a loophole

Don't consent to the lunacy out of fear. As during the pandemic, people always found food and money another way. New decentralized systems you don't know yet, built on blockchain and AI, will run alongside; even if you must use a digital ID, something will happen so you pass through to the freedom you need. It's a game of hide and seek: if a door closes, look for another. If you consent without looking for other solutions, you trap yourself through your own consciousness.

#37.4Watch the narrative

When new voices appear in youth movements, ask who sponsors them, what they talk about, and what they never mention (many genocides and enslavements are rarely discussed). Some sell their souls for money or fear. Use the protocols: meditation, daily affirmations, and study (a concept takes about 15 minutes of repetition to become permanent in the brain).
