#The method — composing your butterfly symphony

  1. Learn the method of the Galactic Meditation (the green meadow) first, then use it without the galactic family.
  2. In meditation, imagine an etheric, living butterfly: let it rotate and mutate, twisting into a torus. Feel passion and joy; let any dialogue come.
  3. When you feel it has downloaded, start humming or singing your own melody, whatever feels right to you, low or high, all octaves if you wish.
  4. Combine intention (the butterfly as an energetic, electric field in your mind) with the heart's feeling for a stronger magnetic pull.
  5. Compose it yourself; don't copy another artist's sounds. Goosebumps can show you've found your notes. Record it and replay it if you like.
  6. Send it with love to humanity, the planet and consciousness; you can share it simply ("I created my butterfly symphony") without explaining.
