How realities are compiled: why a fluid, coherent shift lets you carry the memory of one hologram into the next and manifest almost instantly, how "middle-man" holograms plastered between realities slow you down, the seven layers and the number nine, and questions on knowing whether you're compiling, the seventh layer, fluctuation, and new opportunities (talk with channeling)

The big idea

When your thoughts, feelings and actions from the heart stay similar and coherent, each shift into the next hologram is fluid, like water, and you carry the full memory of the previous reality into the new one; manifestation then shrinks almost to no time. When your resonances are far apart, you create extra holograms in between, hoops to jump through, and that is how we were trapped: illusory "middle man" realities plastered over us. Mutate each of them, without rejecting, until they become one sequence and a zero-point portal.

#Key teachings

#17.1Act together

She recommends researching certain whistle-blowers and support independent platforms. In 3D we need the potency of collective, physical action to convert reality: we move forward only when we're in power together.

#17.2Fluid shifts keep the memory

Each thought becomes a hologram: dots that expand to the collective and collapse to singularity. Shifting to the next hologram must be coherent or it dissipates. When frequencies are close, the movement is fluid like water, and you retain the information of the previous reality as a permanent memory in your field; then 70%, 90% or more of what you want arrives with little time. Potency comes from consistency of similar heart actions.

#17.3The middle-man holograms

If your resonances differ (3 Hz versus 5 Hz), a third hologram forms between them with its own, often unpleasant, reality to go through (the bank, the office, the director), and another, and another: this prolongs manifestation. These illusory realities were plastered on top of our consciousness to lower our vibration and lock us in density. Don't reject them: transform each one by repeated projection, until they all become one sequence and a zero-point portal through which you go directly. Don't reject the banks either: they may become something new.

The message: compiled memories (condensed)

In fluid motion all moves at once, like a chain reaction; "the magma is very light and the seven layers are complete... so precise that the mobilization is quantum." In the coming years most of you will collapse and reflect into the next hologram with such precision that "all the livelihood will become fluent, coherent and easy... Your wishes will be as command." "The memories are holographic singular structures of resonance in fluid, coherent motion", transforming from one hologram to another "almost like water", gentle waves.

#17.4How others read you

ETs read your frequency as one pulsation holding all you've collected; within it, each reality opens like a page (your life as Marina, as a Pleiadian, as an American Indian): the Akashic records. There are infinite Akashic records (a person, the universe, Egypt), so a reading may come from a different reality. Bylaw: you can't imagine anything that doesn't already exist in the quantum field, yet you are creating it in the now ("they had to repeat it fifteen times to me").

#Questions answered

How do we know if we're compiling, or rejecting and overriding?
Your mind doesn't know, but your consciousness does: whatever is in front of you was compiled. The goal is to override the middle realities so you go directly to what you want, without the hoops.
Have the seven layers always been present as a bylaw?
(Seen as she answered.) Six layers of frequency collapse, and the seventh is the mobilization and stabilization that makes it potent: the fifth-dimensional platform of mobilization.
If we stop fluctuating and stabilize, will reality flow fluidly?
You never stop fluctuating: consciousness always moves, in 360 degrees, like a torus. But once permanent, each shift carries and mimics the same vibration, creating a permanent positive timeline.
Are the seven layers the seven planes of consciousness?
It depends how you look at it. The seventh completes; the ninth is infinite, the whole God particle: that's why calculations (vortex maths, Fibonacci) keep returning to nine. In 3D the infinity sign is a flower-like shape; the flat figure eight is half, the mirror image.
Does each new collapse bring better opportunities? Is that the universe's intention?
Yes, but free will chooses, and fear drops you into another timeline. That's why media bombard you with incoherent sound and images. The elites stayed united with iron rules while families were split; so unite, as family and community; then no one can lower your vibration.
