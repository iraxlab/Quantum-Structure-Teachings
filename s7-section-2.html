The big idea

We are in a schoolyard: every person, and especially everyone in the truth and channeling communities, is being watched and tested for how they treat people, because Earth's graduates will be the pillars of the next, intergalactic stage. Ego, jealousy, withholding information and over-charging fail the test. The Council of Nine described prehistoric creation as consciousness playing a game of mixing its own creations, like a laboratory: some species couldn't self-sustain and became violent, and consciousness preserves those able to hold a coherent thought through the heart. Negative structures can't do that, which is why they turn to cyborgs and eugenics. Seeing this is not enough: you must act, self-check, and transform 100%.

#Key teachings

#2.1The testing ground

As the battle for humanity begins, pressure rises: monetary changes, squeezing, families and friends turned against each other. Consciousness is testing every individual like a schoolyard to find the new pillars, because the leaders of the next stage must be people from Earth whom others know and trust. The community of truth-tellers is watched most closely.

#2.2What fails the test

Many fall through money: charging so much that people "cannot breathe" (a fair, voluntary support such as a small monthly subscription is fine; a fixed high price removes free will). Others fail through ego: withholding or erasing someone else's information so they look more important. Negative players also hide in communities and churches, sometimes recognizable only by their symbolism.

The message: prehistoric times (condensed)

"In prehistoric times, the singular expression of existence was predetermined. But with time... consciousness decided to play a game of mixing different specks of its own creation." New molecular structures and species formed; over millennia "a new form of species was created" and allowed to stabilize in a controlled environment like a planet. "When that form of creation was ready to make a conscious coherent thought, the consciousness restructured more advanced species civilization to show the way." But, as with every experiment, not all of it could self-sustain: "Like every child is different."

#2.3Why some creation went wrong

In the laboratory of creation some combinations, like a friendship that doesn't work, couldn't self-sustain and became violent. When a species is aware, self-sustaining and open through the heart, consciousness preserves it. To move to the next level you must hold a coherent resonance permanently, constructing, deconstructing and reprojecting it (the quantum manifestation protocols). Consciousness cannot carry you if, by your own free will, you aren't ready. Negative structures only overtake, even their own children; unable to restructure themselves, they turn to cyborgs, eugenics and AI.

#2.4Humans as a battery

We were genetically helped by ET races, and Earth was later taken over, dumbed down and pushed onto an artificial timeline. Because humans are live batteries connected to the ether, negative consciousness kept you in fear (media, schooling, repetition like a mantra) to produce the low vibration it needs to survive. Seeing it isn't enough: you have to analyze it and act. "The action is crystallizing the magnetic gravitational field to become matter", like dieting and exercising rather than wishing.

#2.5Pull back and don't be upset

Many who do the worst harm were programmed from birth into split personalities and can be switched by a word. Your core, connected to God, recognizes a very low frequency and rejects it, sometimes as nausea. Stay in positive input: inspiring films and true stories. Avoid harsh lighting where you can (she was told some modern lights lower your vibration).

#2.6Self-check, 100%

Nobody is perfect. When you catch jealousy or ego, peel the layers to the core and correct it: apologize, fix it. Transformation must be 100%, not 99%. If you behave out of fear of losing people or money, you won't match the resonance; when you need the money, it will come. Lack of integrity (being late, withholding, constantly taking without supporting) makes you a follower, not a leader, at ascension.

#Questions answered

What is the Council of Nine's view on psychedelics for healing or contacting other realms?
Not every psychedelic suits every body: potency differs, you can damage something, leave the body, see levels you're not ready for, and be overtaken in an environment you can't control. Some people have positive experiences, but no one can guarantee the outcome, and you can become dependent instead of learning to connect yourself. To hold a permanent position, your chakras and DNA must convert through the heart and keep a constant coherent resonance on their own. The same applies to machines sold to "fix" your frequency: use support briefly if badly out of balance, but learn to be the driver.
Does my reality reflect the frequency I'm on now?
You always control your reality; doubting it projects the frequency of not controlling it. Correct it by believing now that you are in control and in the proper frequency. There are no mistakes: at each moment you chose what seemed right.
What is the difference between power of influence and free will? Can influence have negative repercussions?
Yes, if you keep insisting on overtaking someone's consciousness with your view. Allow people (often children) to experience and learn, even uncomfortably; stay near to protect. Don't force a spouse or child to wake up: watch your material in the room with them and say nothing. The frequency you project will later be matched by a neighbor or coworker, and they will come home amazed by what you've said for years. Stick with your family; some will find their "galactic family" among friends.
Is there a practice for feeling the presence of angels or extraterrestrials?
Think of them consciously: it's a radio signal into the ether. A positive, coherent thought and emotion ripples through the realities and is picked up, because we are genetically connected. With enough potency you may have an out-of-body experience, a lucid dream or hear thoughts. Don't get caught in fear of what is happening; fear traps you in a low vibration. If you're chosen to help others, you may find yourself in a crazy situation: you gave permission, so lead.
