Q & A: New Year Predictions and Your Mission

A set of predictions said to come from the extraterrestrial fleets; why predictions give the end result but not the path; taking change without fear; and questions on twin flames, purpose, mission and preparing for change (live Q&A)

The big idea

A new set of predictions describes the negative side being stopped, exposed and transformed, country by country, with "no more delays". The teaching underneath is the important part. Predictions give the end result, not the path: the in-between is kept hidden so that no one can sabotage it, and if one person doesn't do the job, another will. Every prediction is a platform you can take as true or not; whatever you accept, you project into your hologram. So meet change with understanding, not fear: love those who have to transform, stand up peacefully, prepare calmly, and do the work. Your mission is to hold the vibration and do what you love in the name of humanity.

#Key teachings

#6.1The predictions, as read

The talk reads a set of predictions given in mid-December, said to come from the extraterrestrial fleets (starships gathered together), and passed on after a delay the guidance asked for. As read, in brief:

• No starship will be allowed to enter or leave the planet (meant, the talk says, for an off-planet fleet of the negative side). • That negative fleet is destroyed; battles were fought off-planet, on the planet and in inner Earth. • The "deep state" negative structure was momentarily disabled. • The negative side's commander is dead and all are captured. • There will be no plea deal: all back to the beginning, no bargain. • It is now a "do-not-enter zone". • There will be a complete (financial) reset. • No more delays.

• Hoped-for new republics in several European countries; one country "defeated" and another "freed" of the negative side; an end to money laundering in one region and to a war between powerful families in another; all exposed. • The negative side in one region is "wiped out… It will be very quiet there. Trust us." • From 2020, a new frequency platform that negative souls can no longer enter. • A political storm and an awakening among generals in one country; new leadership in another. • In the United States, "the lion will roar": liberation for all the states, funding to free themselves from the central bank, and freedom from the families who controlled them; those who didn't help held accountable. • The control reached through 20 layers of parallel Earths: "Dig deeper, deeper, and all are gone in 20 platforms of holograms."

These are predictions as received, reported without judging whether or how they came true; several referred to 2019–2020. The talk adds political commentary, references to outside channels and claims about named groups; this book leaves those out and takes no political side. It also leaves out a passage on immigration that generalises about whole groups of people, which doesn't belong in a book about the heart structure.

From the booksDP prints this list, dated 12/16/18, closing: "Phuuu… so much work we did, but all worth it. Control is now here. The Extraterrestrials Fleets."

#6.2The end result, not the path

When a participant reported that one prediction already seemed to be coming true, the answer drew the lesson: "Did I know it would be the women? Did I know it would be the temple? No." Predictions give the statement of the end result. The in-between, who does what and how, is deliberately left out, because "somebody can go and sabotage it." And there are infinite probabilities of how it happens (see S2 1.6, 5.1).

So if one person given a task doesn't do it, someone else will come in and finish the job: consciousness is ascending, a program is running, and "nothing can stop the snowball now. It's like an avalanche."

#6.3A platform you can take as true, or not

"I'm not convincing you that this is true. I'm presenting to you a platform of holographic imprint reality, that you can take it for true or not. This is quantum structure." Whatever you accept, you plaster into your consciousness and project (see S1 16.2). Trust is based on your own belief system.

The same goes for the news. What national media put up front is described as a program, a projection of a hologram that someone wants you to tune into. Tune in constantly and you project that platform into your own realities, keep seeing the negative, and miss the change as one structure collapses and another appears (see S2 2.6). Hold your vibration high, from the heart, giving and sharing, whatever anyone else does, and you will see the platforms you are projecting: "one structure collapses, a new one is suddenly there, and you didn't know how. But you projected it. You created it."

#6.4Love those who have to transform

The talk says those working against humanity were asked to stop and chose to keep fighting. But "this is not a judgment": they are part of consciousness too, and fighting is the only way they know how to survive after so much negativity, completely separated from the source. "Let's not put a judgment; just love them, because it's their time to transform." (see S2 1.6, S1 14.3).

From 2020, a new frequency: as the planet's frequency rises, a soul that doesn't resonate with it can no longer enter that holographic reality, like a game you can't access without its code. The code is your frequency.

#6.5Revealed step by step; stand up peacefully

Things are revealed gradually, so people aren't shocked into breakdown and so that change, including new technology, is a smooth transition rather than chaos (see S2 1.4). And when you find out you were lied to, don't destroy: burning or wrecking ordinary businesses only hurts your fellow people. Make your stand through action against the negative structures, peacefully. Peaceful protest is powerful, because people who are awake can't be stopped.

#6.6Money in the meantime

The talk expects a reset and more digital money. Its advice: don't depend on only one structure. It repeats its caution about cryptocurrency (see S1 11.2), saying some groups control a "back door" (the talk's claim), and suggests backing up with something tangible, such as gold. Until the proper systems are in place, "play the game" to get through: pay what you have to, and hold your vibration high. This is the talk's view, not financial advice.

#The method — Meet Change Without Fear

For predictions, upheaval and uncertainty:

  1. Treat each prediction as a platform. Ask: do I want to project this? Take it, or leave it.
  2. Don't marinate in the news. It is someone's program; notice it, thank it, switch channel.
  3. Expect the end, not the path. Don't try to work out who and how; it stays open.
  4. Love those who must change. No judgment: it's their time to transform.
  5. If you stand up, stand up peacefully. Build; never destroy what belongs to others.
  6. Prepare calmly, from understanding, not fear.
  7. Hold the vibration, and do what you love in the name of humanity.

#Questions answered

What do the numbers say about twin flames?
Twin flames are based on contracts. You can deeply love someone with whom you have an agreement across several realities, learn your lesson together, and then love someone else in another reality. So don't believe there is only one flame, one person for you to infinity; you will have different experiences on different platforms.
What did I decide to accomplish on Earth before I was born?
No one can tell you; you have to know. You came here to learn. Ask what excites you most, and bring the stamina, action and perseverance to do it. If you came to complete something and gave it up because it got hard, you'll meet a similar challenge again, in another life, until you complete it, because consciousness is learning through you (see S1 5.7, S2 1.8).
Can we have more guidance on the zero-point columns of light anchored in the Earth, the "rainbow bridges" and the fifth-dimensional corridors?
They are already constructed. To experience them, you have to be on their vibration; like a TV, the channel is already there and you tune into it. Use them by staying on the vibration and knowing everything happens when it needs to, with no expectations, since there are infinite probabilities of how. Watch the teachings and meditate in whatever way resonates. When you anchor in, you step into what is already there, and you create it at the same time; that is the quantum (see S1 1.4, S2 3, Questions).
I had a download that we can eat anything if we make peace with the food, so it won't lower our vibration. Is that true?
You can always make peace with food by giving it gratitude; that's part of the transition, so don't judge what others eat. Plants carry a higher frequency than the body's molecules, which helps restore the body (see S2 4.4). As your consciousness rises, you may simply stop wanting certain foods, and your guidance will tell you. Until then, choose from your heart, for example by asking how you feel about animals' suffering (see S1 9.7).
What is the best way to discover my purpose and mission?
Learn the quantum structure: there is no shortcut. You have to restructure your consciousness, even your brain's connections, into the new vibrations by tuning into the material until that reality crystallizes. Study the manifestation series and apply it; the changes may not be immediate, but they come (see S1 10.4).
Is there any insight on my mission, and how I can be of service to humanity?
Your mission is to hold the vibration through the ascension. Every time you join something heart-based in the name of humanity, it resonates in you, and since you move through parallel realities, you emanate it into the structure around you, raising the collective's potency. Open your heart, find what you love, and do it for humanity. Follow the money: some who say "for humanity" charge fortunes. If you're supported, let it be from people's hearts. Hold the vibration of giving and sharing, without fear, and support will come in a way you create: be the abundance (see S1 4.3, 5.1).
Could you say more about the prediction that India will rise up? And how can we educate people, when education is being run down?
What was shown was a nation realising how it has been suppressed, through poverty and through division, including religion used to divide (with respect for every religion, see S2 5.7); people's consciousness is already activated, so they won't keep taking the blows. A participant noted a recent large protest by women for equal access to a temple, which the talk saw as the prediction beginning. On education: it is already under way, people are awakening, and each person will experience it as much as they believe. Stand up for yourself, peacefully; your action creates your reality. And people need to learn the quantum structure, the alchemy of creation, so they don't loop in despair; that's why this information is given free. The answer added a prediction about particular religious sites; this book leaves it out, because it concerns a contested dispute between communities.
Is there a time frame for the revaluation of currencies?
The talk said attempts were made to collapse the system three times, to blame the head of state, and were stopped; another reset was mentioned within three months, but it wasn't known whether it would happen, since it depends on who controls what. The talk's claims; not financial advice.
Shouldn't we prepare, with food and water at home for a few months, in case the reset disrupts things?
Not three months: the guidance said poverty to the point of suffering will not be allowed, and spoke of a backup plan and safe zones. If you prepare, do it from understanding of the transition, not from fear, because fear projects a negative outcome (see S1 8.12). The host and participants agreed a small reserve, a few weeks to a month, is sensible, along with a basic kit to help others. The talk also suggested having a water distiller or a generator, raw honey as sustaining food, and, "in extreme cases", drinking urine.
Added safety note

Sensible emergency preparation is calm, not fearful: official guidance generally suggests keeping water, non-perishable food, medicines and a first-aid kit for several days to a couple of weeks, depending on where you live; check your local emergency-management advice. Don't drink urine: survival guidance warns against it, as it can worsen dehydration.

The host added that this is why the material matters: when change comes, you have the tools to stay calm and abundant, and there is less fear when you know the structure.
