Q & A: Tests, Codes and the Signs of Your Path

Why readings show probabilities, how everyone is being tested and what happens to those out of tune, how your purpose keeps reappearing, signs of a timeline jump, contact through others, and many questions (live talk)

The big idea

A reading shows what you're vibrating now, a probability; guidance shows you the way, not the whole story, because knowing too much would throw you off balance. Everyone is being tested now, channelers and scientists included, and fame is not the criterion: those who drift from the heart lose the codes to draw information from the 5D platform, and their work fades. Your true purpose keeps reappearing until you take it up. Strange gaps in memory can be signs you've jumped to a new hologram. And to build a big reality, feel its resonance in every small act: talk to one person as if to millions.

#Key teachings

#4.1Guidance, not the whole story

A reading shows what you're vibrating now; do something different tomorrow and the timeline changes. Only something crystallized 100% may be shown, and even then not always why. If you were told "you'll marry in two years", you might marry the first person you met, though you were meant to date several first, and you'd be off balance. So guidance helps you be your best through the process rather than telling you what will happen; information that would cause panic is given in a way that keeps the transition easy (see S2 22.1).

#4.2Everyone is being tested

Everyone is being tested now, "including channelers, including scientists", because humanity is moving toward an interstellar structure and contact will come to specific people. Fame and glory are not the criterion: "the way you give the information, the way you treat people, that's what we're going to look for." Those who stop resonating with the new heart structure lose the codes to extract information from the 5D platform: what they receive becomes inexact, and through synchronicity their work subsides. Those who hold the vibration grab the proper codes (see S2 25.2).

#4.3Giving trains the frequency of abundance

Keep charging what you need as the main provider, and once a week or so give something freely: "you need to start familiarizing yourself with the frequency of giving without expectation." Abundance is a reality you must resonate with by acting on it, or you never build it. "You cannot reject the money structure, but you have to transform it." An obligation blocks your resonance, so the teacher keeps giving open, and gives regularly to others' work herself (see S2 19.4).

#4.4Who is speaking, and protection

At a certain level you stop asking who is speaking: "it's always a knowingness." Expressions differ (the council is gentle and fluid; others are blunt and to the point), but ultimately it's you projecting versions of yourself to interact with. You're never alone, and guidance can even plaster a protective hologram on top of yours when you hold a high resonance. There's never fear.

#4.5Signs you've jumped

When something you remember using is said never to have existed (a network, an account, a detail), you may be "extracting information from a previous hologram": you shifted, and you remember the old one. Likewise, when exactly what you needed appears, you reprinted it in a new hologram with another version of yourself. Notice these; leave the how open.

#4.6Your purpose keeps reappearing

Purpose can be pre-ordained, yet you choose whether to do it well. If it's yours, "it's going to keep coming in front of your face through different situations." Ignore it and you may sink "deeper and deeper in mud". Transformation doesn't mean doing the same thing again: if it didn't work, try a different approach, the hide-and-seek game (see S1 1.7).

#4.7Shadow work is done in action

You can't skip your shadow work by simply feeling love: you created matter, so you must take action through it. Situations will come for everyone to express themselves from the heart, large or small (a kind word to your mother; still loving a relative who disrespects you). "What counts is the vibrational sequence of the intention." Be honest: peel the layers of ego, apologize, and the program shuts down (see S2 14).

#4.8When a frequency is too high to hold

Beings above the fifth dimension can't simply appear to you: you must hold their vibration. So they may speak through another person, with that person's permission. Tuning in to a frequency far above yours can make you shake, "like ten cups of coffee", or feel nauseous. Joy keeps the conversation open; fear closes it.

#4.9Feel the big reality in every small act

If you want to speak to many people, don't wait for the crowd: feel, even when talking to one person or posting one video, the emotion of speaking to millions. That resonance builds the reality. "It's not about how many people; it's about the resonance you project", and you can be glorious, but through the heart.

#The method — Feel the Reality You Want, Now

  1. Name the reality you want: speaking widely, a thriving business, a healed family.
  2. Find its feeling: how would you feel if it were already so?
  3. Bring that feeling into small acts: one conversation, one post, one task.
  4. Check your motive: from the heart, not for fame or money alone.
  5. Act daily from that resonance, whatever the numbers look like.
  6. Leave the how open: notice what appears, and keep going.

#Questions answered

How precise are channelled messages? Can channels be manipulated without knowing?
See 4.1 and 4.2.
At 19 I have PCOS. Should I take medication or change my diet?
She can't advise on medication. She shared her own detox and elimination approach (allergy test first), and her view on dairy: from a medical book she read, the body has to reprocess it and it can take nutrients out of the bones ("I cannot back this up"); yogurt can irritate the gut flora too. For a probiotic she uses Visbiome ("VSL turned into Visbiome: the doctor took the formula"), which helped her son recover: "I can guarantee for the Visbiome right now because we're using that." To flush out the body, drink distilled water for a whole hour, because its neutrality reaches the most potency (she suggests watching Andrew Norman Webber on distilled water). She removed one food every two weeks while juicing and doing shakes, fruits and vegetables kept one hour apart. She said that if you ever come off a medicine, do it slowly, with your doctor: stopping suddenly can shut the system down.
Added note

Decide about PCOS treatment with your doctor; never stop prescribed medication on your own.
