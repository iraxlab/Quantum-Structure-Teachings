The big idea

The one particle moves because countless tiny universes, each with its own pulse, push and pull against one another like magnets; that flow curls into circles and then spirals, which is why your DNA mimics the cosmos. A change of reality can be instant, or can take a long curve of learning, because every fractal of a lesson has to be completed. To speed it up: repeat what you're building in focused sessions (about 15 minutes builds a bridge between neurons), keep your daily inputs positive, and treat most daily "problems" as distractions from your higher focus.

#Key teachings

#22.1Why hidden truths are surfacing now

The truth is revealed step by step, and every consciousness, even on the negative side, is being activated to wake up, because the whole structure is ascending as one unit. Those who refuse to look at the facts aren't changed; they simply stay in that resonance and project its negative outcomes. Because consciousness must balance and survive, whatever the negative structure does, "we're going to go forward". The difficulty is that so much will surface at once: without the quantum structure, a person can be overwhelmed. "Your head cannot be in the sand." What unites people in the end will be the truth about the children: "there will be a human race."

#22.2What sets the one particle moving

In the magma of the quantum field are countless little portals, each "a universe for itself", each a sequence of resonance and geometry with its own pulse. Differences in their magnetic and gravitational fields build space between them, "almost like floating, resisting each other like magnets". As they move against one another, "like the sea", the flow becomes a circle, then a spiral: "that's why your DNA structure is mimicking the cosmos." Consciousness is a sphere; the spin goes both ways, up and down through 360 degrees, and as the pulsing fields combine and speed up they create new structures and multiply into new universes.

#22.3Dreams are schooling

Where do we go when we dream? Into whichever reality your frequency, agreements and resonance connect with. Returning to your galactic family happens only by condition, "through the council approvals". Think of it as school: each planetary system is a grade; when you complete the grades and combine those realities into your higher self, you're given instructions to return to your galactic family's timeline, "which is going to be happening soon to most of you", once you receive guidance directly from your higher self with no source outside you.

#22.4Tuning in to the council

You already know their frequency, because you're tuning in to it as you listen. So afterwards, meditate on the feeling you have right now while listening; held in your system, it begins to decode and combine your structure with theirs. Then ask a question and listen for the answer "without interference of your belief system". Contact itself follows your individual agreements. In meditation, a strong vortex-like vibration means you're holding two parallel realities at once, as the teacher does when she channels while awake: a third reality between two (1 + 1 = 3).

#22.5How long does a new reality take?

It can be instantaneous, or take a long "curvature of learning": if a lesson must be completed, its signature is "almost like a bracket", and every fractal of it must be finished, like each part of a nanoparticle, before it acts as one unit (see S3 16.2). Shutting the program down through gratitude at zero point and acting on the new signal shrinks the time (see S3 11.3), until you think of something and it simply appears through synchronicity. The 11th-dimensional teaching "stop, transform, move on" applies here.

#22.6Repetition builds the bridge

Keep every daily input (TV, social media, friends, a walk) at a high frequency, and transform anything negative through understanding. "Fifteen minutes per time of repetition non-stop creates signals between one neuron and another, to crystallize as a bridge." A bigger creation is the same process at a larger scale: repetition daily, perhaps for a year. Most daily concerns are "merely distractions from keeping your higher focus": notice them, transform them, act, and move on.

#22.7Recovering lucid dreams

For someone who lost childhood lucid dreaming and astral travel: before sleep, meditate deeply for 15–20 minutes, preparing the emotion of entering a lucid dream, and lie face up rather than on your side (see S3 8). When you become lucid, "hold the excitement in neutrality so you don't pop out of the reality"; neutrality keeps you in resonance with that dream reality.

#The method — Tuning In to Guidance

  1. While listening to a channelled talk you trust, notice the exact feeling it gives you.
  2. Later, sit quietly and recall that feeling until you resonate with it again.
  3. Hold it for 15 minutes or so: let it settle in.
  4. Ask one question, simply.
  5. Listen for the answer without letting your existing beliefs interfere.
  6. Write it down, and repeat on other days (22.4).
