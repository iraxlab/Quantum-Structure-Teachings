Channeling the Council of Nine: The Current Situation

A direct channel at the start of a global crisis: transforming rather than rejecting, reading your hologram, two years of fluctuation, customized medicine, fractal detox, your unique spark, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

At the start of a global crisis, the council's guidance was practical: what is happening exists, so don't deny or reject it; go through it with the best of your abilities (sensible everyday precautions) from understanding, not fear, and hold your centre so you keep finding what you need. Read your own hologram: the clues are there because you projected them. Expect about two years of fluctuation, in which even the council reads the timelines fast and they don't always come as expected. And the future of medicine is customized, modern and natural together, never rejecting either.

#Key teachings

#6.1Shift your density to harmony

"We don't understand this as negative: we see density" (see S3 2.2). Your daily choice is to shift your density to a more harmonious resonance. When you know someone is projecting a negative input into your consciousness, pull away from it and use the quantum structure to shift. Hold that steadily, and "no matter what happens around you, you will find your food, your money, anything you need".

#6.2It exists: transform it, don't reject it

"The virus, by the way, exists": don't disengage from reality by pretending otherwise. You can't reject what is part of the structure; you transform it. Go through it with the best of your abilities and take "the normal things" (such as washing your hands), and buy what you need, but from understanding, not fear. "If you continue with the fear, do not be surprised that more of it will come."

Added note

In any health emergency, follow your public-health authorities' guidance.

#6.3Read your hologram

"You know the knowledge, but you need to learn to study your hologram and follow the clues": what appears around you is there because you projected it, and your higher self is helping you through. Messages come through synchronicity, as the timeline shifts (see S3 3.4).

#6.4Two years of fluctuation, and honesty about it

A period of about two years was described as one of "absolute fluctuation", with systems changing: one area worse while another gets better. If one person doesn't complete their part, another takes over. The council was candid: "sometimes we ourselves have to read the timelines very quickly, and they do not come exactly the way we expect." The host drew the lesson: have compassion for yourself for not always knowing the right thing to do. The end result: "humanity is free" (see S2 6.2).

#6.5Don't hand over your consciousness

Asked to remove someone's fears before a big project: "No, we will not do this for you. You need to meditate to get yourself into a frequency of knowing. This way you give us control over your consciousness. Don't you see what the lesson is?" (see S3 5.4). Serving humanity is also now: "what do you do right now? Take that expression of joy and implement it daily."

#6.6Your unique spark

Energy "is simply a structure" that changes with how it's combined, like nano. So study the quantum structure, and anything else that resonates, combine it all, and create your own understanding from your perspective. "To have somebody tell you exactly how you should think, how you should feel, it's wrong": your unique spark is needed in consciousness.

#6.7The norm is your higher self

To open a portal into a higher-density realm, combine the same frequency of feeling, emotion and action daily: they form a whirlpool that opens a structure in your hologram, and, fractally, you shift permanently. We were trained to accept poverty or certain situations as "normal": "nothing is normal about that. The norm is to be in your higher self of expression." Detox matters too, as the body's spin changes (see S2 25.5). For sensitive people exhausted by crowds: think the opposite, that you love people and are comfortable among them, and act on it daily.

#6.8Customized medicine is the future

Every body has its own genetic code, and the gut flora can switch chemical reactions differently in each, so the same medicine acts differently. "If you combine homeopathic medicine with modern medicine, you will see better results": always with medical supervision, customized. "Do not reject modern medicine: that will be your future", with more technologies revealed within about five years.

#6.9Detox fractally

A liver-only or kidney-only detox is risky: "it has to be the entire system, or you may shut down your system." Slowly, surely, systematically: "do not overpower one system with another; do not flush too many times; it has to be done with precision", and be careful who helps you (see S2 4.5).

#6.10Home as a place to transform

With everyone at home, the teacher's family put up string lights, brought out games, origami and knitting: "transform your understanding in this moment, to gather together, to be a family again". Turning it into something fun transforms the hologram. The host added: focus on whatever lifts you (uplifting music, a song) rather than what you can't control.

#The method — Meeting a Crisis Without Fear

  1. Acknowledge what is real: don't pretend it isn't happening (6.2).
  2. Take sensible precautions, following official guidance, from understanding, not fear.
  3. Pull away from inputs that feed fear; neutralize what you've seen (6.1).
  4. Hold your centre: know you'll find what you need.
  5. Read your hologram: notice the clues and synchronicities (6.3).
  6. Do something that lifts you with the people around you (6.10).
