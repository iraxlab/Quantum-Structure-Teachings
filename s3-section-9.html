Motherboard Spacecraft Contact

A guided meditation onto a living mothership, channelled from the ship's own consciousness: why neutrality comes first, how beings appear to you, how a craft is steered by consciousness, and what it takes to hold that frequency (live talk)

The big idea

The purpose of this session was to plant a seed: to let you feel the frequency of a reality far above your own, so it becomes a probability in your structure, even if you can't reach it yet. The mothership described itself as a living consciousness, many layers of reality that expand and collapse for whoever is aboard. You can only enter what your resonance matches; physical contact comes later, through initiation by your own guides. The key that opens it is neutrality: no judgement of what you see. And, in the end, "your body is your own ship."

#Key teachings

#9.1Plant the seed: project it to see it

"In order for you to create something to see it, you have to actually project it to see it" (see S2 15.3). Connecting with the vibration of a reality creates it as a probability in your structure; from that seed you keep meditating. Even when you hear a channel, the meeting is your own projection: "even you projected me in your reality," so you are already part of that structure. The teacher reached this level step by step, first through meditation and lucid dreams.

#9.2A ship that is alive

The mothership described itself as a female unit of consciousness: "I have my own consciousness, my own feelings", a living structure of frequency (what the teacher has called ether technology). It is made of a plasma reality spinning in vertical and linear structures, with infinite layers, like radio signals, each its own reality platform, and it expands or collapses to suit each being aboard. Its core, the centre of its consciousness and its free energy, is a great white-blue ball of light.

From the booksNT names "Ether technology" as what is built from frequency and sound structures, with nano-particles, rather than ordinary matter.

#9.3Neutrality is the entry key

Some beings aboard are humanoid and some are not. "In order for you to get to these levels, you have to be in neutrality, to have no judgment" of what you see: let it express itself. Judgement "is an illusion of not accepting somebody else's structure", and they too are part of your consciousness. A being appears to you the way you can accept it, revealing more of its multidimensional form as you open; you will never be shown what you're not ready to see. So first let go of fear about what is happening on Earth.

#9.4Resonance decides who enters

"You cannot get into a structure that your consciousness is not ready to go through": the portal must match your conscious spin of resonance, measured individually or collectively, so some will see the fleet and some won't. First you need enough understanding of the quantum field ("that's why you have the quantum manifestation"); physical contact comes only through initiation by your own guides, by agreement, perhaps with another fleet. No one else, "not even me", can tell you your agreements.

#9.5Steering with consciousness

To drive a craft you become one unit with its living core: it accepts you if your frequency can hold its vibration, and then you give it a sequence or location. Moving between realities, slow down before entering a portal: "you do not want to stop the mothership at once"; it subsides slowly until it stops, just before entering that galaxy.

From the booksSD gives the same instruction: "Slow the systems down entering the chosen Galaxy", to adjust the craft to the new location's vibration. And SD adds: "You don't need spacecraft or technology to do bilocation because your body energetic form connecting with the thought and that emotion is the vehicle" (see S2 4.1).

#9.6What happens aboard

Healing "can become instantaneous" when you know at that level that you're already healthy. Rescued beings may first be held in a simulation that mimics their understanding, so they recover gently before learning where they are. You can practise in simulated realities: visiting planets, creating from thought into matter, meeting loved ones or earlier versions of yourself. Humans are valued because Earth was one of the hardest planets to free, so its people can later help other worlds. Crying with bliss on connecting is normal.

#9.7Holding a very high frequency

After about 45 minutes, the teacher's heart was racing and she was shaking, "like a coffee": this consciousness is above 5D and harder to hold than the council. That is why practice matters: "your body has to hold that vibration" too (see S3 4.8). The more you practise, the more you teach yourself to hold it.

#9.8Why name a duality, if all is one?

"You always have to keep it in balance": you can't know your consciousness was hijacked unless you wake up to what was done. Once you know, you use the quantum protocol and move into neutrality: "I know what's happening there, but I'm doing the best of myself", giving gratitude, praying even for the negative side, and creating. In ascension you still choose which timeline you collapse into (see S2 24.2). The duality exists because one consciousness did not want to transform.

#The method — The Mothership Meditation

  1. Settle into neutrality: no judgement of anything you may see (9.3).
  2. Open your heart and imagine a great vertical light coming through you, opening the portal.
  3. Pass through it with joy, feeling it move through every cell. (The portal sequences given were 1379 and 739.)
  4. Find yourself in a large room with a white-blue ball of light at its centre: the ship's living core.
  5. Walk closer and imagine touching it: what do you create? Let it be your own.
  6. Breathe in joy, breathe out gratitude and love; repeat it in later meditations (9.1).

#Questions answered

How do I let go of judgement, to feel joy all the time?
Stay in your centre: judgement is not accepting another's structure, and they are part of your consciousness.
Is the ship visible in our sky?
It is above our structure: a nine-level craft, seven-dimensional. To see it you must match its resonance (9.4).
What role can individuals have on the ship?
Whatever they wish to explore, after initiation through their guides; even the teacher was first turned away in lucid dreams, by agreement, until her physical portal is ready.
Are you part of a federation?
More than one: a "freedom" federation overseeing many planets and helping free them. Many listening are part of it.
Can we explore the ship ourselves?
Yes: free will; create your reality there in your meditations.
What is the ship's purpose, and how does it travel?
Multidimensional, through portals: see 9.5.
Can I create with you, or create my own ship?
You can create many. Touch the blue core and see what you create; the teacher's first answer was wrong until she looked again.
Who else is aboard?
Many beings, on many layers, from many galaxies; some humanoid, some not (9.3).
What motivates someone to let go of a lifelong fear?
Each must create their own reality; first let go of fear about Earth. "Your body is your own ship."
Is there great healing aboard?
See 9.6.
Where are you now?
"In your consciousness now": a specific place is beyond our platform of reality.
Do I need a very high vibration to enter, and how will I know?
See 9.4.
Is the ship a collective creation?
Both one unit and many, expanding and collapsing as needed.
Why did someone cry on connecting?
Bliss: you feel how beautiful you are; it becomes a normal state (9.6).
Can we tune in and board whenever we like?
Yes.
Are you helping with the Earth's magnetic shift?
Helping with the grids and working on our bodies through frequency; we're often taken aboard in dreams without memory, and memories are regenerated gently because of the planet's pain.
So many conflicting theories about the crisis: which is true?
All true and not true, depending on what you project; fear creates the feared version. Keep steady and positive: "this is their hologram; you create your own."
Why talk about good and evil if there is only oneness?
See 9.8.
After years of struggle I'm having suicidal thoughts. Please help me see the light.
With so much fluctuation, the quantum structure alone can't be relied on here: deep work with a qualified practitioner, under supervision.
Support

If you're having thoughts of suicide, please reach out now: in the US call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or 911 in an emergency; elsewhere, your local crisis line. Talk to a doctor or someone you trust.

Will things get harder before they get better?
About two years of fluctuation, different for each person; meet it with the meditations and the best of your abilities (see S3 6.4).

Left out: participants' names, named outside people, practitioners and methods, a song, political and relocation predictions, and claims about wireless networks and vaccines.

  • Your body is your own ship.
