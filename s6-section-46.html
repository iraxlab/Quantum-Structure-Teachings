The big idea

Creation happens in a perfect sphere of light-energy, a laboratory so dense yet light that the slightest thought shifts every particle into a new position: a new Earth each time. The shift twists inward and outward through a cone into a tunnel and a torus, a portal, and must be smooth, like a ripple in water, because the whole structure behaves like a water molecule: a pure (distilled-like) conductor carrying your sound wave in every direction. The negative poisoned that water and tuned you off balance (440 Hz) to create a near-identical twin of the true structure. Purify from within and reprint consistently to create the original sphere.

#Key teachings

#46.1The laboratory of creation

Light is an electromagnetic wave that changes into an electromechanical wave called sound (see S6 3). When everything collapses it becomes a sphere, with the new creation at its centre. The movement twists like a cone into the zero point and out in the opposite direction; seen from above, it's a tunnel you pass through: a portal, teleportation from one Earth to the next. Inward and outward pulsation makes a torus vortex. The velocity and slope of the shift must be smooth, like ripples on water, so the new reality can take its position. Each sphere's signal is almost identical to the last, which keeps the shift easy (see the geometry in HR).

#Questions answered

If everything is energy, is water energy? What powers the shift: the zero point?
Water is energy and a conductor, created from the conditions of the perfect sphere. The sphere is like a droplet of pure, distilled-like water; when you give thoughts, emotions and action, it mutates its chemistry, and the whole structure's chemistry changes and reprints accordingly.
Is source constantly sending molecules of itself into the sphere?
Yes and no: depending on your vibration and actions, your droplet of creation may be stagnant or less pure.
Is primary water, from deep inside the Earth, the right, unpoisoned water?
Yes: water from deep toward the core, with the right minerals (sulphides, magnesium, silica, salt), purified as it flows over rocks. Best is spring or waterfall water at first sunlight; water from near where you live suits your body best. The Earth's core holds a self-feeding, atomic-like etheric energy that recharges you through bare feet (earthing).
