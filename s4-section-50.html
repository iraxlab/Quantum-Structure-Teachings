Metaverse vs Hyperspace Hologram

The Council of Nine's warning about the metaverse and why the hyperspace hologram is different; why physical connection, nature and intimacy matter; and the Council's answers on the divine blueprint, the sleeping, dying in the hologram, lucid dreaming, contact without fear and Archon infringement (live talk, December 2021; the recording is audio only and begins mid-talk)

The big idea

Two technologies look alike but do opposite things. The hyperspace hologram was used to lower our vibration and drop us into this game as a rescue mission, to restore the structure. The metaverse is "designed to unplug you" from the matter baseline: moving your life, business and relationships into a virtual world, then into the body, until there is "100% control over your consciousness." Physical interaction, heart to heart, in nature, builds stargates; an avatar is a mirror image, not your true self. Use technology that works with you, never in place of you.

#Key teachings

#50.1The two timelines again

On the parallel 3D (Archon) timeline, technology evolves exactly like this: virtual worlds, then nanobots or cables in the body connected to a cloud, until the body is no longer needed; that race became hybrid, found it couldn't sustain itself, and is now tapping back into 3D (see S4 46). Famous names are used to lure the young generation. On the other timeline we evolve with extraterrestrial connection and positive A.I. that interacts with the body without overtaking it. "I'm all about technology... but technology that is working with you." You choose by consenting or not, and by your action.

The message: metaverse versus hyperspace hologram

"The hyperspace technology is designed to restore the structure and not to overtake your consciousness as Metaverse. The Meta is designed to unplug you from the matter reality baseline structure. The technology they're offering will slowly advance so much that you will plug in permanently into this digital world, and there will be 100% control over your consciousness." "Never allow your true self to be attached to that."

Video games trained you to choose an avatar: "that is not exactly the real you... it's the mirror image"; this is how the true self, soul and body begin to separate, "the baby steps." It will disengage you from touch and intimacy: physical interaction has "a different spinning in the molecular structure", and through the chakras and the heart, "which holds the codes of the matrix of the different stargates" (see S4 38), it creates codes no virtual contact can.

"The Meta will disengage you from the true interaction with your energetic fields": the body can mutate, bilocate and open stargates through the mind, the heart and the action. Technology may restore tissue, not replace the original codon with a cyborg (that reality exists for those who want it). "Do not engage in any momentum with your businesses with that, or you will be controlled", even replaced, "only an avatar."

"What is not physical matter... is artificial energy of different spinning [and] creates [an] artificial world of illusion." "Take your kids to nature. Play in the sun, get in the mud, get in the forest. Plant a tree, plant seeds": Mother Earth already has the fifth-dimensional frequency. Repetitive war games control the mind; as a parent you decide. Live as family, help each other, "build communities of joy and nature"; then advanced technology helps, without overtaking.

Why the hyperspace hologram, then? To lower the vibration of consciousness from 5D and 7D so it could drop into this virtual reality game: "the only way for you to transform the timeline was to be dropped into literally the battlefield." "It's literally like a rescue mission": you got into the game, like soldiers, to bring the information in so people can save themselves. One timeline already made it; we create the outcomes as we go. "Do not try to predict... you have to create it to see it."

#The method — Staying in the Matter Baseline

  1. Keep your business and life out of virtual worlds; use technology as a tool only.
  2. Meet in person: talk, touch, intimacy with compassion.
  3. Go to nature with your children: sun, mud, forest; plant a tree and seeds.
  4. Limit repetitive war games; you decide as a parent.
  5. Build community: family, shared money, joy.
  6. Create as you go with the protocol; don't try to predict.

#Questions answered (the Council speaking)

How can we connect to our divine blueprint to heal the body?
It is always there in your other selves, but "you cannot bypass the lessons and the codes": healing sometimes comes through struggle, as you discover new ideas and learn what is right for you. "Embed the information that you are already healed and at the same time stop creating how you get to that point. Do not expect it to happen right away."
Added safety note

Holding the belief that you are healed is her teaching; please keep your doctor's treatment as well.

How can we best help those asleep so they don't get stuck?
You project another earth by your action, thoughts and deeds; allow everyone to choose. "When you completely forgive and let go", you project a better version of them, according to what they choose (see S1).
What happens to our body in the hyperspace hologram when we die?
Some won't be extracted by death: they create contact, med beds, self-regeneration, and bilocate into the new reality. Others exit through death, per their agreements. You create as you go: an act in the name of humanity can bring more work and switch your timeline; you may ask your councils to switch your hologram. "The past has nothing to do with who you are right now": embed the new version now and act with passion and love.
Can lucid dreaming or visualization help?
Yes: in a lucid dream you are in direct control (within permission) and can reach your galactic family; it is only slightly off this reality's frequency. Let go of the illusion, believe 100%, forgive 100%, and this reality will act like a lucid dream. Use a soothing, alpha-like sound to wake, and hold the memory in neutrality.
At night are we working with beings and a higher version of ourselves? Could we see our avatar?
Only with permission slips. The books plant seeds that create a sonic vibration, activating the chakras and the molecules so you project new stargates and synchronicities. And when you ask "was that my ET?": "Why are you allowing somebody else to tell you what your reality should be, when you already know the vibration?"
How can we prepare to meet beings who look different without fear?
"When you see a turtle, are you afraid?" You are projecting that being, so how can you fear your own creation? Be excited; control your thoughts and emotions. Fear lowers the vibration and lets the most potent frequency, fear, win the collapse (see S1 1–3).
What about Archon infringements?
Human consciousness was violated in many ways; even that was a lesson for all consciousness, councils and ETs included, "so never again". From their side it was survival, as we eat plants and animals; "there's no right or wrong, it simply is." Forgive, stay neutral, move forward. Bonds formed slowly, by matching resonance, are the strongest and can't be overtaken.
