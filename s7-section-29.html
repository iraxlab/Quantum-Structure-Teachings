The big idea

Much of this talk is political; it is given here only in outline, as received. The teaching: Earth has an energetic grid of ley lines and stargate pillars, and the planet must hold a certain tilt to pass through the shift into 5D. Humans, with original DNA moving toward 12 strands, are the conductors who hold that grid; when one lights up, the whole circuit lights. That is why our DNA is targeted, and why raising your frequency through the heart, action, diet and water helps the whole planet. Shocks may come, like shaking a jar of sleeping ants, to wake people. Don't panic over money: something will always appear. You can help without a public platform: never comply with evil, and do your best moment to moment.

#29.2Humans hold the grid

The planet has grids and stargate pillars connected with the inner Earth. Our original DNA (12-strand, once living thousands of years without needing to eat) makes us conductors that hold the grid's energy. Diet, water, the quantum structure, thought and action activate your chakras and DNA to draw energy from prana. Attempts to take over key grid points would pull the planet onto another trajectory; ETs and our rising frequency keep them positive. In 2012 there was a shift: "humanity made it" to a certain vibration, with more to do.

#29.3Why the free-will bylaw matters

What happened here broke the bylaw that each particle chooses its own trajectory; its ripple affects other planetary realities, which is why many ETs are involved. Telling people what they're doing and having them comply out of fear lets manipulators claim consent.

#29.4Who cares about the money?

Asked again about the currency, they said: "Stop. Who cares? It's not for long. Let the world see what they are doing." Attack brings counter-attack; nobody will allow dark ages. You may not even notice while others panic. If you can't do something, an alternative appears. "You all have everything."

#29.5The jar of ants

To them, humans are bugs, a nuisance. "How do you wake up little bugs? You shake them": like shaking a jar of ants, a shake-up makes people look around and ask "what's going on here?"

#29.6Put it all in

Freedom takes everything, not 90%: she left with her family, lost insurance and created as she went. The strongest will be the new pillars and future interstellar leaders. With practice you can tune in directly through your third eye, sometimes bypassing intermediaries.

#Questions answered

Can we activate the 12 strands only through the Council of Nine's diet, our actions and raising our vibration?
Yes: through the heart and service to humanity first, then the diet. Her year-long elimination diet, juicing, shakes and then water raised her frequency, cleared her skin and removed a wart, which returned after she broke the diet. Intermittent fasting alone isn't enough, and smoking, drinking and bad food block it. Do your best; use whatever platform helps ("play the game"), even if you dislike its owner. Don't alter your DNA; express yourself however you like without destroying it.
How do we help humanity without creating a public platform?
Not everyone belongs at the front. Some are placed in difficult workplaces by agreement, quietly gathering information for their guides. The key: never agree to do anything evil, because complying creates karma and lowers the grid. Settle disputes through the heart rather than suing each other, which also creates karma. Stay together as a family. Do your best for humanity moment to moment: you hold the whole power of consciousness in the now.
