The big idea

Light does not move in a straight line. As it moves, its sub-sound reprints it; it begins to spin, faster and faster, collapses to the zero point, "clicks" and spins the opposite way, and shifts slightly to a new location, because consciousness itself is always moving. Each collapse adds curvature: that is the torus and the infinity sign. The zero point, the void, "holds the power of the entire quantum field"; collected with this knowledge, it is free energy. And you are that structure: "you're actually the zero point." The negative side used only half of the knowledge, so it eats itself "like a scorpion".

#Questions answered

How do we use light curvature in daily life: for meditation, or understanding?
For understanding how shifting works; it is not what science teaches. Scientists told her light doesn't curve; she presents what she was shown and sees.
Is the torus field connected to light curvature?
"The torus field is the light curvature"; the infinity sign is the torus curvature.
How does this translate into practical life?
You are the structure: sound waves collapsing into one unit, the zero point, creating everything around you. Knowing it, you are no longer a puppet of someone's narrative; you become "a mechanic of your structure". See S4 36 for examples.
Does latitude and altitude mean the field is 2D?
No, 3D: a circle with up, down and sides, X and Y with positive and negative. Each move creates a location; whether the third structure is positive or negative depends on what you collapse (see S1 1–3).
How does another person's positive prayer affect my vibration?
That person is a version of you projecting into the 360° field (a torus); it reflects back, and you can take that frequency in and collapse it into a new earth. If both keep praying, it grows more potent, reprint after reprint. If it's negative: "thank you, I can create something different."
Do collapsing vortexes mirror each other?
Yes: a slightly different position, very similar resonance, and between two people more potency.
Does a spiral ever slow down or get pulled by another magnetic force?
Only if you wish, or a program is running: how it collapses depends on the intention in its sub-tonality, "like a symphony".
My children jump timelines and see other beings. How can I help them?
Their heart frequency knows how and where to jump; no need for fear or to intervene. Embrace it and encourage questions: ask the beings what reality this is, which lineage, positive or negative. Without fear it stabilizes into knowing.
Does the zero point have a specific frequency?
Yes and no: she was shown pi, 3-point-something, that stops and shifts (.32333...) frame by frame; a fractal with a whole number of location.
Can you feel the spinning in meditation?
Yes: tapping a higher resonance can make you slightly dizzy until you balance. First make sure there's no medical or nutritional cause; then keep practising.
Should we use the merkaba's finger and breath techniques?
If you wish. Breathe in to take in energy, "the fuel"; breathe out to project it. Without fear your own consciousness will know what geometry to create: "the power is within you."
