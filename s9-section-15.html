The big idea

The Council of Nine said the collective has built enough potency for a permanent outcome, but whether you ride it into a higher timeline is your individual choice. Within three years globalist control will be fully established, and by the end of 2029 they will push with fear for your consent. Know their game: it's a chess game, so play it. Watch, anticipate their moves, and make yours calmly, creating as you go; solutions will appear as you move. "God will never allow an awakened consciousness to continue suffering": a negative field can never permanently penetrate your field unless you let fear in. Witnessing is not absorbing. Command your mind and control your actions.

#Key teachings

#15.1Land grabs and local positions

She points to data centers placed in communities rather than uninhabited land, and to land being taken by eminent domain, as part of a land grab. In her view these projects won't roll out as planned without new technology (free energy already exists). Get involved locally: open positions in municipal offices are often not advertised; some websites now list them so ordinary people can apply.

Added note

Added note: her comments on particular places, companies and officials are her interpretation of reports; check facts locally.

#15.2Programmed personalities

Some people placed in positions may have been abused into split personalities that switch on a word, without memory, so don't be shocked at what they do. Exposure needs a "victim" for a community to act.

#15.3The body can adapt

Because you carry God's vibration, your body, like an etheric biological machine, can mutate at the cellular level to withhold or transform negative inputs (sound, news, situations). She recalls a woman who said experiments let her body survive in a contaminated zone. "Have no fear, hold the vibration."

Added note

Added note: she presents this "for entertainment purposes only" and as her belief; it is not medical or scientific advice. Never expose yourself to toxins or radiation.

#15.4Look-alikes and confusion

Things that don't make sense may hint at events behind the scenes; she recommends a 1993 film about a leader's look-alike and wonders whether people will notice someone they knew no longer recognizes them. "Trust your own ability."

Added note

Added note: claims about doubles or cloned leaders are speculation she says she cannot prove.

#15.5Those who held the line

Those on the front line who held their vibration when it was hardest, called crazy and unsupported, converted to a higher resonance; now they project from a timeline where they see what happens but stay safe. Consciousness will never allow mass destruction of the human race this time, though many will have challenging times in the battlefield.

#15.6What it means

The collective wave is crystallizing; your participation is your choice. Like chess, think several moves ahead, from clarity not emotion. Don't wait for a perfect plan: solutions appear only while you move. Seeing difficult things isn't merging with them: "The chess player sees every piece... but does not become the opponent's pieces." Suffering ends not because someone rescues you, but because you stop generating its frequency. "The chess game is not happening to you; it is happening for you." The game is multidimensional, across timelines and infiltrated religions; division by belief is how chaos controls.

#15.7Why reacting traps you

When hit with a shock, most people react instantly, so it penetrates the field and they keep mimicking it. Getting out may take months or years of practice: think the opposite of what you see, feel as if what you want is happening now, move to a different place or do something physical to subside anger, and give your best to the person in front of you as if they were your whole universe, without expectation. Meditation puts you in a state; action crystallizes.

#15.8Forgiveness over revenge

When a relative stole her family's homes and money, she had the power and connections to destroy that person, but chose not to: "This is blood money; this is not yours." She took care of her family and taught them over years to forgive. Years later, after she forgave, the person's partner donated an apartment to her near the old home. Such choices, she says, are what her guides watched: that her field held no speck of revenge. Balance matters too: charge with reason, always give the original material freely, and support flows back.

#The method — Playing the chess game (as given)

  1. Recognize their move without fear: "I see what you're doing."
  2. Stay collected: command your mind, control your actions.
  3. Anticipate their next moves; don't react to the last one.
  4. Make your move calmly: a new idea, a new solution, a new connection.
  5. Create as you go; don't wait for the perfect plan.
  6. Witness without absorbing: don't let fear root in your field.
