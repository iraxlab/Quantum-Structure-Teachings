#Questions answered

What will happen with certain coins?
Some will become obsolete. Be flexible and cautious: "be willing whatever you invest to lose"; if your whole savings are in one place, pull back, restructure and diversify. Digital ID controls will be tried in some regions and will subside with global awareness. Fluctuation, but no drastic collapse.
How do we avoid becoming obsolete? Should we trust AI for our websites?
To a point: humans will still control the AI systems in collaboration, though fewer. Keep learning and adapt like a chameleon; you'll operate and communicate with organic intelligence.
How do I know I'm ready to experience another platform, like the astral world? Is there a limit?
There's no limit for consciousness, but in the 3D confinement there are rules to the game. You limit yourself by lacking the knowledge of how to convert structures: "the sky has no limit."
Will there be a complete global financial reset?
Not the major collapse you imagine; a stabilizing system works underneath. Individuals may still experience personal collapse, but those who can construct and deconstruct will restructure. In your highest expression, synchronicities may move you somewhere safe months ahead. A harder hit is a wake-up call to analyze and act. Unite with family: if one falls, the rest carry; don't put all your goods in one basket.
When will the system be fully implemented? Will it be used with ETs?
There are transactions with ETs, but not as you think. The 3D Archon and 5D systems run simultaneously; you experience the one matching your vibratory rate. Know you have everything you need and act daily.
