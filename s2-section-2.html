Sound Continuum Vibration, Part 1: How Words Build Your Hologram

How sound, words and repetition create vortexes, tunnels and matter; why intention is the frequency; what to take in; and how to stay clear in a negative room (live talk with questions; part 2, Restoration, follows)

The big idea

Everything is built from light and sound, and so is your own reality. A word is a sonic vibration: joined with the meaning you give it (your thought) and your action, it creates a vortex in the quantum field that stretches like an elastic into a whirlpool tunnel, shifting you from one platform of reality to the next. Because that elastic moves back and forth endlessly, it repeats, and repetition is what we experience as solid matter. So your words are the sonic materialization of your hologram. What decides the effect is intention: it is the frequency. Choose what you say, hear and watch; build what you want brick by brick with the same joy; and when others project negativity, pull out, thank them, and keep projecting the positive.

#Key teachings

#2.1Deeper material, step by step

The new material is more complex, so it comes in two parts: this one, on how everything connects through the sound, vibration and frequency the quantum structure is made of (the trinity of frequency and sound, see S1 1.1, 1.2), and the next one, on restoration. Information is released according to how well people are taking it in consciously, level by level (see S2 1.1).

Downloads come in a specific sequence and are passed on without changing a word, even when the English sounds unusual, because the sequence itself carries the structure. It isn't learning something new so much as remembering what is already known in the field.

From the booksSD's opening notes say the downloads should not be changed: do not "substitute words and sentences because they are in code." SD also says "every word has sound geometry."

#2.2Your words build platforms of reality

When you speak, a sonic vibration comes out of your mouth and creates a structure, a whirlpool, in the reality around you. Combined with your thoughts and actions, it creates the hologram and the portals into your next timeline. So when you say something negative, you need to know what you are building around you.

That is why constant negative films, music with harmful words, and negative talk tune you into low-density timelines, where negative entities can exist. Keeping people in that vibration through media kept them trapped in an illusion of reality (see S2 1.2). The same applies to prayer: words repeated over and over build a whirlpool that crystallizes, so be careful what you repeat; if it is fearful or negative, you may loop in a dense reality without knowing why negativity keeps arriving.

The talk illustrates the prayer point with a claim about one particular religion, taken from an outside video, while saying it isn't against any faith. This book leaves out that example: it is an outside claim about a faith, not a teaching of the structure. The teaching applies to any words you repeat.

#2.3The download: Sound Continuum Vibration

The download names the process in one sequence: sound, continuum, vibration, sonic, equilibrium, cymatics. The words are given in that order on purpose, because each one builds a platform on the one before; drawn in sacred geometry, the sequence would show its own geometric structure. What the terms mean here:

• Sound: a waveform, measured in hertz, how many times it vibrates back and forth (see S1 12.5).

• Continuum: repetition, something that keeps coming and coming within the structure.

• Vibration: the sound's repetition becomes a vibration resonating at some frequency in the field.

• Sonic: like a light of sound, something you can actually hear.

• Cymatics: as downloaded, "symphonic, liquidating sequences that are repeating in a fractal structure momentum, implemented in a whirlpool of realities, creating the portals in sympathetic structures, most commonly known as platforms." It is how your expression resonates in the structure.

From the booksSD and DP print the download: "SOUND CONTINUUM VIBRATION is sonic equilibrium cymatics whirlpool into the time-space continuum of the matrix holographic expression memory bank. Your words are the sonic materialization of your holographic expressions continuum." The books spell the last term "cymatics"; in the talk it sometimes sounds like "semantic".

#2.4Words are the sonic materialization of your hologram

Every time you speak, you are plastering a vibrational sequence in geometry into your hologram, at that exact moment, based on exactly what you said. The download calls this the holographic expression memory bank. Why "continuum"? Because each hologram holds infinite holograms, probabilities and timelines within it, each its own Earth. Every moment is infinite reality, and your job is to hold your vibration.

#2.5Vortexes, tunnels and the illusion of matter

Your word, joined with your thought and action, clusters geometry into the quantum field and creates vortexes, with the zero point in the middle (see S1 2.1). A vortex can be horizontal or vertical, at any degree up to 360, and can be measured. It expands like an elastic into a whirlpool, a wormhole, a holographic tunnel that shifts you from one platform to another.

Because it is continuous and infinite, the elastic moves back and forth, and so everything in the structure repeats, moment to moment. This is why the structure looks the same wherever you cut into it (a fractal). That endless repetition plastered into the matrix creates the illusion of reality, the thing we know as matter: it looks and feels solid, but it is really sound continuum, vibration, an elastic frequency whirlpool.

The rhythm is like breathing: one movement expands to the infinite, the other contracts to the smallest of the small, with a tiny still pause, the zero point, in between (see S1 5.8). Every platform you exist on goes through the same repetition.

From the books"Create vortexes and expands like an elastic into the whirlpool structural wormhole holographic tunnel that is plastering infinite continuum into the matrix and creates the illusion of reality over and over again and that is matter structure" (SD, DP).

#2.6How a screen can overtake your hologram

When you watch a film or TV, you are still hearing words, but someone else is projecting them onto you, plastering vortexes of holograms on top of yours. If you believe that the mirror image on the screen is true, your platform switches immediately: where you were resonating in the positive, you now resonate in the negative, because a different holographic image has replaced yours in your consciousness (see S2 1.2). The same goes for advertising, which shapes your hologram visually.

#2.7Intention is the frequency

What makes the vortex is intention. A lie is a lie whether "big", "small" or "white": its potency may differ slightly, but the intention plasters that frequency into your hologram all the same (see S1 3.2). And a word on its own does nothing until you give it a meaning; once you mean it and speak it, it becomes geometry, plasma and gravitational field, the trinity of thought, emotion and action (see S1 1.2).

Thought or spoken word? They spin slightly differently but come from the same structure, so the effect mimics each other; the spoken word is more potent. Combining both, with repetition, is what people who know this structure do.

#2.8Repetition builds it brick by brick

Think of building a dome. The thought is the foundation; then each action and each sound adds a brick. Repeat, repeat, repeat, and brick by brick the hologram grows, until it has enough potency to expand and crystallize: it becomes a reality in your hologram. You are mastering how to create your own world.

Hold the same joyful vibration while you build, so every brick has a similar frequency. If you drop into negativity or doubt, you shut down what you were building (see S1 2.3). Even if something negative happens around you, keep building: that image is only an illusion.

#2.9Potency: how you say it matters

The same words carry different weight depending on how they are said. A firm "Don't do that" is taken far more seriously than a soft one: the sound vibration has more potency, like a stronger dose of medicine. And the same word, said playfully or said with contempt, lands completely differently, because the intention and resonance you send into someone's platform are different. You can feel the difference, and so can they.

#2.10Numbers, letters and sacred geometry

Letters and numbers matter when you know sacred geometry. Combined with sound and with a location in the quantum structure (latitude and altitude, see S1 5.3), number sequences create Fibonacci whirlpools that open certain structures; this is how worlds and holograms based on different belief systems are created. Used wrongly, the same knowledge can create very dense, negative matter. A negative thought has a different spin and makes a different whirlpool, leading to dense timelines. So combine your thoughts and projection with the geometry in the positive, to align with the higher structures of love.

To hum a tone to find your own frequency or jump timelines, it has to carry intention and belief to complete the trinity; and a very potent intention can overtake, so be careful what intention you put in.

From the booksDP says the Pleiades are "interjecting" new Fibonacci sequences now.

#2.11Light language: check it before trusting it

Every word and sound has a different spin in the quantum structure, and the spin decides the timeline it creates. With an unfamiliar "light language", you don't know what you are saying or what it creates, and there is a lot of manipulation. So don't take any light language on trust: it can be measured scientifically, its frequency and tone recorded with equipment, to show what it creates in the field. Some ancient languages are said to have been suppressed precisely because they created positive structures. Don't judge people who speak one; they act from their own belief system (see S1 16.1), but wait for evidence before relying on it (see S1 3.9).

#2.12Choose what you hear and watch

Music, films and their sound effects all carry frequencies. Music that relaxes you and gives you goosebumps is resonating with you in the positive (see S1 8.13); meditation and harp music are suggested. The talk also claims that some music and films carry hidden sounds or images designed to distort your frequency and plant patterns in the subconscious; these are the talk's claims, and the teaching is simply to choose consciously what you take in.

Action or horror films aren't forbidden; just don't make them a habit, and skip the scenes you don't need. Watching heavy films, especially when you're low, can bring nightmares: the talk explains these as holographic expressions you created on another platform, a sub-reality you also exist in, because the film was plastered into your subconscious.

The talk adds that most films show extraterrestrials as frightening so that people would reject the benevolent beings who could help bring down the old grids (the talk's view; see S1 17.3). Don't mock people for what they believe; research for yourself, and choose unity and humanity over picking sides.

#2.13You don't have to die to ascend

Asked where death fits in, if we are holograms and vibration, the answer is that death as the only way out was a manipulation. Consciousness is always experiencing, so some may choose to experience death; but the natural way was to ascend vertically from one timeline to the next: merging with a higher consciousness to create a third reality and shifting into it, even onto a spacecraft and another planet, without dying (see S1 17.1, 17.5).

What happened instead: people were taught that the mirror image is real and told what their reality should be. Believing it, they looped in timelines locked into 3D, and without the knowledge of how to create above 3D, believed the only way out was to grow sick and die, and so created that. The grid around the planet recycled souls back in (see S2 1.3). The talk also repeats its claims about poisons and vaccines (see S2 1.12 and its note).

To connect with higher beings you need a similar resonance with them, and you need to believe it is possible: if you don't believe in them, the whole thing is blocked (see S1 18.5). From 5D, the heart structure, you can create a shared third reality with them. Eventually humanity goes interstellar, while others stay to help the planet; you may even move back and forth. The talk recommends channelled books on Arcturian ascension and on living with the planet, which make most sense once you know the quantum structure.

It also repeats its claim that regeneration technologies already exist and are being held back, so that in future people may regenerate their own tissue and look 30 rather than 80 (see S2 1.7). These are the talk's claims.

#The method — Speak Your Hologram

For building what you want with words, and keeping your field clear:

  1. Know what you mean. Words do nothing until you give them meaning; set a positive intention first.
  2. Speak it. Say it out loud, with feeling and a clear, firm voice: spoken is more potent than thought.
  3. Match it with action. Thought, word and deed together make the vortex.
  4. Repeat, brick by brick, keeping the same joy each time, until it crystallizes.
  5. Guard your words. No small lies, no habitual negative talk, no fearful repetition, even in prayer.
  6. Choose your input. Goosebump music and uplifting films; negative content only occasionally, if at all.
  7. In a negative room: pull out, thank them inside, hold your space like a shield, and keep projecting the positive.

#Questions answered

So our thoughts, our words and the words we choose form a geometry and a vortex that keeps projecting into our hologram, like a ripple. Does our breath affect the vortex?
What carries the frequency is the intention: the intention is the frequency. It doesn't matter whether a lie is big or small; the intention creates the vortex, with slightly different potency. So learn to stop saying negative things, and be careful what you watch, because films project words and holograms on top of yours (see 2.6, 2.7).
Do our thoughts and intentions have the same effect as spoken words?
They spin slightly differently, but they come from the same structure, so yes: one mimics the other. The spoken word is more potent; people who know this combine both, with repetition (see 2.7).
Can we use the tone of a letter or number to find our own frequency signature, and does humming it help us jump timelines?
Only with intention and belief to complete the trinity, and be careful what intention you put in, since a potent one can overtake. Letters and numbers matter when you know sacred geometry: combined with sound and location, they create Fibonacci whirlpools that can build worlds, positive or very negative. Keep your thoughts and the geometry in the positive to align with the higher structures of love (see 2.10).
Is there such a thing as a lower-frequency light language, and can combining light language with sacred geometry give the strongest effect?
Be careful with light languages. You don't know what you are saying or what it creates, and there is a lot of manipulation. It can be measured scientifically, by recording its frequency and tone, and that is the way to know what a language does. Some people can feel or even see whether sounds are positive or negative. Until there is evidence, don't rely on it, and don't judge the people who speak it (see 2.11).
In meetings, colleagues often use words like "hate", "not possible" or "too complex". How do I keep from getting drawn into that vibration?
Pull out to zero point and observe without giving emotion to it, to preserve your own hologram within the collective one. Think: "I'm watching a hologram. Thank you for existing in my hologram." Don't think "these people are so stupid"; that is engaging and projecting the same frequency. Neutralize it with gratitude, hold your space like a shield, hold joy, and keep projecting positive thoughts and actions, over and over. You don't have to say anything or agree with them. When your positive input becomes potent enough, the next moment shifts, sometimes as simply as someone saying "That's enough, let's get a coffee" (see S1 2.6, 16.3).
Many words sound the same but mean different things: medal, metal, mettle. Which effect do they have?
The word itself makes no difference until you put a meaning into it. Once you mean it and project its sound, it becomes geometry and matter in your hologram, the trinity of thought, emotion and action (see 2.7).
Does repetition make it stronger each time?
Yes. Think of building a dome: the thought is the foundation, and each action, word and sound adds a brick. When there is enough potency, it expands and crystallizes as your reality. Keep the same joy while you build; doubt shuts it down (see 2.8).
Can certain notes, such as C sharp, said to be the Earth's tone, help raise our vibration?
No single note is "the" tone: every tone has sub-tones, to infinity, and what you'd measure depends on the platform, the location and the vibration in that moment. It is much more complex than one note (see S1 19.4).
Is there more on treating distilled water with frequencies?
It is in an earlier talk on frequency and sound: 3 Hz, 70 Hz and 90 Hz, about 20 minutes each, as a general protocol (see S1 19, Questions). Individual timing differs from person to person. The talk is clear that this doesn't mean anyone will be healed: at most it may help align the body so it can heal itself.
Added health note

Frequency and water protocols are not medical treatment. For any illness, see a doctor; use practices like this only alongside medical care, never instead of it.

Can the music or sound effects in a film, even a heart-warming one, lower its vibration and affect our field?
The talk believes so, and claims that some music and films carry hidden sounds or images that distort your frequency (the talk's claim). The practical answer: notice how music makes you feel. Relaxing music that gives you goosebumps is resonating with you in the positive; choose that (see 2.12).
If I listen to something like Swan Lake before and after a horror film, will it balance me out?
The answer was simply to keep heavy films occasional, skip the scenes you don't need, and pay attention to the words and intention in what you watch, because heavy viewing can create sub-realities you then meet in nightmares (see 2.12).
Is speaking softly better than speaking loudly? And are solfeggio frequencies safe and good?
It is about potency: a firm, clear voice carries more weight than a soft one, like a stronger dose, and the intention behind the tone changes how it lands (see 2.9). For solfeggio frequencies, it depends how they are used: every number does something in the structure, and how numbers combine is fundamental (see S1 19.5).
If we are holograms and vibration, where does death come in, and why do we disappear from a hologram?
Death as the only way out was a manipulation. The natural way was to ascend from timeline to timeline, merging with higher consciousness, without dying. Belief in what we were shown locked people into 3D, so they created illness and death as their way out (see 2.13).
I'm reading a book on lucid dreaming. Do you really feel you are somewhere else?
Yes, you think just as you do now. Practical tips from experience: train yourself to wake by yourself, about every two to two and a half hours, rather than with an alarm, which can stress you and make you forget the dream. When you realise you're dreaming, stay calm, or you'll wake up. From there you can open a portal with a thought. You may not be permitted to go everywhere you wish, depending on your agreements; experience it anyway, because you only know by experiencing (see S1 8.14; S2 1, Questions).
