The big idea

"What now?" Her answer: stay in the vibration. Anything you see in front of you, however frightening, is just a hologram, a matrix of a sequence of reality, and both the negative and the positive versions run at once. So pull back, say "it's a hologram", observe, imagine the positive scenario, and feel it now with the emotion of the heart, fractal after fractal, until it crystallizes as your timeline. Manifestation of anything works the same way: feel now that you already have it, give from the heart, and act the same way moment to moment until the potency pops it into your reality.

#Key teachings

To control a large population, "everything has to be easy enough", with just enough information that people don't ask too many questions. Having grown up under communism in Bulgaria, she shows how history is selected (genocides and empires left out, leaders taught as heroes), and how a system can change its disguise while the same people keep control. Her view of equal-pay "big government": a doctor who studies for years earns little more than someone who doesn't lift a finger, you can't leave, and everything is controlled. She asks you to learn this history for yourself: "it's time to pick a side."

#4.2Psychological warfare

The former KGB officer she played says only about 15% of their effort went to espionage; 85% went to ideological subversion, changing every person's perception of reality "to such an extent that despite the abundance of information, no one is able to come to sensible conclusions". Its first stage, demoralization, takes 15 to 20 years, one generation of students; a demoralized person can't assess true information even when shown proof. This is why even a very intelligent person can be completely brainwashed by a narrative projected 24/7 (see S4 2.7).

Through images and schooling. She points to music videos and stage performances that, in her reading, act out rituals ("this is how it's done... what our children are watching"), to a television series whose imagery mirrors a controlled, depopulated world, and to school testing that trains children to follow steps rather than think critically. She took her own children out of that testing. With 24/7 exposure, these images and their resonance penetrate your consciousness (see S4 3.6).

#4.3The quantum bylaw: it's just a hologram

"Anything you see in front of you, it's actually a matrix of a sequence of reality." You couldn't see it if it didn't exist in the matrix, but both realities co-exist, and to see the other one you must create it. So when something negative happens (an arrest, troops, a bombing, a changed rule), don't go into fear, which lowers the vibration: that is what they try to do. Pull back and tell yourself it's a hologram; observe; then imagine the positive scenario as a blueprint in front of you and project frequency "through the emotion of the heart". Feel now that your country is free, even while the commotion plays out, and repeat each next moment: one timeline is replaced with another, and to become permanent it needs "fractal moment after moment of the same positive feeling" (see S1 3, S4 2.2). "The toughest part is to pull back, to neutralize to zero point."

The images you watch carry a specific resonance, a set of numbers, that penetrates your consciousness. Pull away and watch something positive, and you shift to a new hologram "with completely different numbers"; hold it, and you shut down the program put on top of your consciousness, get out of their timeline, and no longer lock yourself in their narrative and geometry. Their consciousness can only survive by artificial timelines built through technology (see S4 2.4); your body, through the heart, can mutate into high resonance.

#4.5How manifestation really builds

Her simplest example, as she was taught. When she was told to stop working and practise energy philanthropy, she reached a point of almost nothing and was ready to crumble. She heard "stop": "It's an illusion. You are in a hologram." In the hologram, it doesn't matter whether you need one dollar or a million; if you stay in the heart, what's needed is presented through synchronicities, because you project the frequency that creates them. "Do you have food? Clothes? A home? Well, what else do you need?" Keep helping, keep giving, without questioning: that resonance builds, moment to moment, until it pops in as an avalanche. For her it took two years of constant giving from the heart, trusting 100% (see S1 5).

Feel it now. "You have to feel right now that you have the money... that you are famous, whatever you want to imagine in that blueprint." Then act the same way, with the same feeling, each next moment. If you want to speak to millions, speak to one person with the same heart you would give millions: that is the code you project into the next moment. "It may take some time"; the learning curve is how you grow in potency. And thinking "if I had billions I'd be a philanthropist" is an illusion: money has numbers and an imprint in the hologram, but giving from the heart has its own ether structure. Think from the ether: when you need it, it comes, sometimes as the gift itself (an apartment, a facility) without money at all.

#The method — It's Just a Hologram

  1. Pull back from what you see, the moment fear starts.
  2. Say it: "It's just a hologram."
  3. Observe only, until you reach neutrality, zero point.
  4. Imagine the positive scenario as a blueprint in front of you (your country free, your body healthy).
  5. Feel it now through the emotion of the heart, while the other reality still plays out.
  6. Repeat each next moment, with the same feeling and actions, and stay off the mainstream media, until it crystallizes.
