The big idea

Season 1 gave 1 + 1 = 3; this download adds −1 + 1 = zero point. Frequencies of similar vibration combine into a swirling waveform: one swirl goes inward like a cone (−1), the other goes outward (+1), and between them is the zero point. Seen from above, the two turn in opposite directions and merge into the geometry at the centre of the flower of life. This happens inside every molecule, and every dot it touches is a new universe, so realities overlap and 3D and 5D exist at the same time. The inward and outward swirls are also the two sides of a portal, the torus; and the small delay between one hologram and the next is what we experience as time.

#Key teachings

#1.1Why this download matters

Every pixel of this reality is its own hologram, its own Earth, and you shift moment to moment into a new one according to the vibration you sent two seconds ago (see S1 1.3, S1 7). Knowing the geometry behind that explains the "now": why everything is happening at once. It came in the early morning, "very technical"; information arrives according to how well the group is learning.

#1.2−1 + 1 = zero point

Similar frequencies combine into the trinity, the magnetic and gravitational field (see S1 2.2). They don't simply add up: they form a waveform that swirls. One part goes inward, narrowing like a cone: that is negative one. The other goes outward: positive one. Between the two lies the zero point. Viewed from the side it looks like two cones; viewed from the top, two triangles turning in opposite directions that connect and merge: the geometry at the centre of the flower of life. "This is what's happening inside the molecule", and inside it is all possible geometry, expanding on 360 degrees, with every point a location (latitude and altitude, see S2 15.4).

#1.3Every dot a universe; realities overlap

As the waveform comes out, it touches different dots of the matrix, and each dot repeats the same in-and-out structure: "a brand-new pixel of a universe, with infinite universes inside". Because one swirl is always going out while another, overlapping, is coming in, you get reality within the reality: everyone has their own reality, overlapping with others', and 3D and 5D exist at the same time (see S2 20.1). Repetition happens because the waveform gives force like a fractal momentum, reprinting the same geometry in a similar vibration: you fluctuate between versions until, by action, you make one permanent (see S1 3.3).

#1.4The switch at the zero point

Before reaching the zero point, the swirl is at its most potent: "spinning, spinning, spinning", until it reaches complete stillness. That stillness is a switch: another universe, a shift of timeline. The negative and positive inputs together create the zero point; with only one, it's off balance. It can never be perfectly balanced, so consciousness corrects itself by adding more of one or the other. The push to a new structure of similar vibration goes on "to infinity", fractally (compare S2 24.3).

#1.5Portals, stargates and the torus

The inward swirl creates one portal and the outward swirl the other: you go in through one and come out of the other. This is the geometry of stargates, and it is the torus. Why the signs? +1 gives a curvature outward, growing bigger as it exits; −1 curves the opposite way, growing smaller as it goes in. Technology can do something similar, but it must be measured, because it works through matter. (Asked about the merkaba: "it was blank"; no information was given.)

From the booksSD and NT describe "2 toruses in the light structure of the magnetic and gravitational field" that "are connecting with portals" as "a unit of 1 single operation".

#1.6Time is a delay between holograms

Moving from one hologram to the next, the fractal momentum has a slight delay. Repeated and looped, that delay creates the illusion of time. Knowing the structure, even without understanding it fully, plants a seed in your consciousness: "you're remembering what you already know", and it makes your manifestation more potent.

From the booksHR: "There is no time. Time is an illusion of the contradictory brain given as a module computer to decode the frequency waveform of time."

#1.7Anger: you're powerful, not broken

Someone "riding waves of anger" takes in energy very strongly and all at once: "that's not a sickness"; you need to learn to control it. The moment you get angry, remove yourself from the situation. Release the pressure safely (music, a video, a punching bag in another room) for as long as it takes, even an hour, until you're no longer upset. Don't act until you're calm, because an action taken in anger is built on that resonance and loops. Then thank the situation (you projected it, so you can't reject it) and switch (see S2 18.6).

#The method — Training Anger Down to Zero

  1. Notice the anger rising: that's the signal.
  2. Leave the situation: another room, a walk outside.
  3. Release the pressure safely: music, a calming video, physical exercise.
  4. Wait until you're calm, however long it takes. Don't act before.
  5. Thank the situation: it's your hologram, and it lets you practise.
  6. Switch to a positive emotion; set a half-hour reminder to check yourself.
