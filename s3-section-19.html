#The method — Recalling a Dream

  1. Lie as if to sleep: the body relaxed, almost asleep, so the energy moves easily.
  2. Breathe deeply, in and out, until the blood flow is steady.
  3. Think of the dream: search for any image.
  4. When an image comes, look at it closely: colours, who is there, what is said.
  5. Feel its emotion: the feeling brings back more images.
  6. Keep going: you are recalling and creating it at the same time.
