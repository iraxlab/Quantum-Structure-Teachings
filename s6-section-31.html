The big idea

America is attacked from every side because the generosity of its people's hearts produces a potent positive vibration, the switch point for the new beginning. The received message: correction will come slowly but surely, through negotiation and a tipping point when the people, waking like a sleeping lion, act lawfully; there will be fluctuation but no financial collapse. Chaos seen from afar is order when you zoom in: everything finds its place by its own magnetic and gravitational pull. Don't let anger overcome your soul; act through the law and the heart.

#Key teachings

#31.1Why America

Suppression shapes a heart structure: under long oppression people learn to survive by cunning and even small lies, which accumulate. America, built for freedom and comfort, accumulated a generous, giving heart frequency, and as an immigrant nation collected a specific frequency that holds the ground. That is why it is poisoned and bombarded most: to lower it into the Archon parallel reality. Some information is blocked from her: where dark forces are involved there is a neutral playing field and rules (see her video on probabilities and predictions).

The message: America 2023/2024: tipping point (condensed)

Negotiation and compromise between negative and positive, "to compromise does not mean is without conditions"; some compromised people will stay in power under control to carry the positive agenda. "Correction in government will be slowly but surely... It is the tipping point... America is like a sleeping lion. Once is awake... the roar will be rippling like a wildfire." Some public events will be "like a theatrical show for the sake of the individuals that are still in denial." "Slowly but surely. We do not wish chaos." "You will see the good and the bad... Do not allow your anger to overcome your soul. Act... through the law of the land... through the heart and through honesty. So never use your power to hurt another being." "There will not be a financial collapse", only local glitches.

Added note

The message included predictions about percentages of arrests, Congress and tribunals. As with every prediction in this book, these are given only in outline, as received; political specifics and named people are left out.

#31.2Order through chaos, for the positive

The negative uses order through chaos; the same principle brings positive outcomes. In nature all moves at once and looks chaotic from afar, but zoom in layer by layer and everything has its own place and order, healing by its own magnetic and gravitational pulsation; whatever can't match the resonance collapses. "All that needs to happen already happened", yet you have the power to change its course in the now: every thought you have already pre-exists, and you create it now.

#31.3Patience is love

Exposure goes slowly because so many good-hearted people are deeply preconditioned; patience and compassion are how you show love for them. Like investigators who let a theft continue to map the whole network, the positive side lets some things run to capture the chain of command. If you meet injustice, seek the proper channels and others who know the law: in numbers you win. Practically: keep about two months of food at home, and don't make fear-driven money moves; create as you go.

#Questions answered

Can the Council address the geoengineering we breathe?
It won't stop yet. She was told the particles also block certain systems or signals that could attack through the air. A frequency technology to clear the air is coming. Do the best you can for yourself and family; have faith.
Shouldn't we run to the mountains?
Running in fear keeps you in their reality: which mountain would save you there? Switch the timeline instead, converting without fear. If you land in a negative situation, perhaps you're there to help others out: the knowledgeable are placed to carry the rest.
Do Earth's frequency shifts affect our manifestation?
Yes: Earth is part of God consciousness. When it rises it creates another Earth; whatever matches is pulled forward, whatever doesn't repels into another reality: the split. The 5D Earth already exists; match it now. Treat others as you wish to be treated, since they are your reflection, and hold compassion like steel.
If everything already exists, should we practise the frequency of freedom?
Yes: feel the emotion of already being free, even while seeing otherwise; it must be more potent than what you see, moment to moment, until it tips. There will still be challenges: you're here to raise others. Shut down the ego and analyze; let family choose their reality, even when it hurts.
