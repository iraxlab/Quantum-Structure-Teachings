The big idea

The hardest skill is shifting your perception from negative to positive while chaos is going on around you. Stop panic first, because panic drops you straight into a denser signal. Then, whatever you're looking for, hold both probabilities (finding it and not finding it) and neutralize them at zero point by already having a replacement in mind. That keeps your vibration on the positive, and something you couldn't have planned often appears. You won't always be told what to do: once you know the structure, you read your hologram and play.

#Key teachings

#7.1Why the downloaded books read oddly

The predictions are given as an end result, already crystallized; how it happens stays in fluctuation, so it isn't spelled out (see S2 6.2). The wording isn't ordinary grammar on purpose: each word carries a sonic, vibrational whirlpool that forms geometry in the quantum field, and in the given sequence the words make a portal that energizes your DNA and consciousness. But a spark isn't enough: "we have to put the work", studying it (and whatever else resonates) to keep up with that vibration.

From the booksHR and NT: these first two books were downloaded with the instruction not to change any words; DP and SD continue the same encoded style.

#7.2First, stop the panic

Panic "immediately is going to lower your frequency to a lower density of vibrational signal", and since you shift moment to moment, your next platform is then built on that density. So the first move when chaos appears is to recognize "I'm in a hologram", breathe, and let yourself calm down. Even with all the knowledge, the teacher still feels the jolt; what changes is that she controls it at once.

#7.3Hold both probabilities, and have a replacement

When you go looking for something (guided or not), you split two probabilities: you find it, or you don't. Collapse them to zero point in advance by deciding what you'll do if it isn't there (another source, a way to make it yourself, "something else I may find") and giving gratitude for it. "Find the solution, so you can collapse this to reality, so you can hold the vibration on the positive." Then keep looking as in the hide-and-seek game (see S1 1.7): knowing you have it, even before you see it. The result often comes from a direction you couldn't have known.

#7.4The next level: you won't always be told

Guidance teaches the structure through examples, then steps back: "you don't have to have somebody constantly telling you; once you know the knowledge, study your hologram" (see S3 6.3). Acting on a clue, such as buying supplies, is done from understanding of what's coming, not from fear of it: the same action, a different frequency. The more you play this way, the more you resonate in the heart structure, and the faster and more synchronous it becomes: "more you do, more it comes."

#7.5Free, and becoming free

Asked whether we are free or still gaining freedom: "It's both. In order to become free, you have to embed the knowledge and the knowingness that you already are free." Feel now that you're free, act free, and that creates it. Where you put your attention is what you project, so endlessly studying theories of deception crystallizes that reality instead.

#7.6Others change as you do

When you raise your vibration, "you restructure them as you're choosing": each time you express your new self you shift into a new hologram and give the people around you another version of themselves, as a mirror. The same holds for loved ones far away: hold the knowledge, and they too can express positive outcomes (see S2 1.2: you are the projector).

#7.7Your body is the barometer for sound

Any music can be beautiful, but your consciousness is a barometer: the music that sends a wave of joy through you, "ecstasy of joy", is the one resonating at your highest. What lifts one person may not lift another, because each fractal is unique (see S3 6.6). Follow your body's resonance.

#7.8Protection becomes part of you

Is protection needed? Once you understand how the field works, you create permission slips (a meditation, a thought) that build a field around you; embedded, that becomes your everyday resonance. You may still see things happening, but your hologram runs at the highest resonance, "and you will always find the bridge for saving yourself."

#7.9Why the dense side doesn't give up

Lower-density beings can tap into a point of your resonance. The teacher learned to neutralize them too, without hatred: from their view it's survival, and "you're actually asking another consciousness, part of consciousness itself, to extinct themselves. No one will allow that." So it looks like good versus evil, but the highest resonance wins, because consciousness must keep existing (see S3 2.2).

#7.10The collective mission

Collectively the mission is ascension. Think of the planet as a living being moving into a fifth-dimensional structure: it needs its beings to hold a similar signal to push it forward, and when you hold your higher self, the merge happens faster. "There is no time": how fast you see outcomes depends on your knowledge and perception, "because you're already there; you just have to create it to see it."

#The method — Playing the Field Through Chaos

  1. Recognize: "I'm in a hologram."
  2. Breathe until the panic settles (7.2).
  3. Raise the feeling: think of the heart's 5D platform and feel something positive.
  4. Name your replacement: if it isn't there, what will you do instead? Give thanks for it (7.3).
  5. Look as if you already have it, and follow small nudges.
  6. Read what appears and act from understanding, not fear (7.4).

Both: see 7.5.

Yes: see 7.6.

"You need medical attention": a mass of tissue that is reproducing itself. "Medical intervention, please."

There are lessons still to come in your timeline; hold your vibration: "you are the only savior for yourself."
Added note

Changes in vision should be checked by an eye doctor.

Express from your higher self at all times: that crystallizes your field and shows you the next step daily, which you can choose to take or not.
