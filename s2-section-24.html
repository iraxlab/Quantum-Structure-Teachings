Q & A: Zero Point, Timelines and Taking Action

How the 3D and 5D timelines split, the zero point that lets you switch platforms, choosing by excitement, fear scenarios, walk-ins, substances, loved ones who have passed, AI timelines, helping within your limits, and many questions (live talk)

The big idea

The timelines are splitting: what you tune into, day by day, is what you shift into, and those who never tune in keep the 3D illusion, which you neutralize rather than judge. Every switch of platform needs a zero point: stillness, like the pause between breathing in and out, which shuts the old program so a new one can be coded; if an old program keeps running, you loop. Choose between goals by your excitement, then act: nothing manifests from thought alone. Fear scenarios only become your reality if you feed them. And you don't need substances to reach a higher state: the goal is to get there yourself.

#Key teachings

#24.1Information comes in its own time, and stays clean

A new download came in the middle of the night about switching parallel timelines, connected with diet and distilled water; it's to be taught in a later master class. To follow it, study S1 1–3 again: without the basics of the quantum manifestation protocol "we're going to look like we lost our mind". Information also comes during the questions, according to how well the group is doing, and answers don't always come when asked (see S2 22.1).

Keep the information clean. A movement should come from the heart, not from profit: "then somebody else owns you". The teacher was told not to attach herself to any organization or platform that could control the narrative, and she didn't publish through a company that wanted to remove the material on shifting timelines, "the most important information you needed to know."

#24.2The 3D and 5D timelines split

Yes, the 3D timeline continues as others go to 5D: the structure splits into different vibrations. The transition is the fractal momentum of daily life, moment to moment (see S1 1.3), as each person connects their consciousness with their actions, thoughts and deeds, and with the platforms they choose to interact with. Whatever you tune into, a TV programme for example, is a resonance you set your consciousness to vibrate on, and that is what you project the next moment into. Everyone was given the opportunity; those who never wake up to the idea that something else exists never project the resonance to shift, so they continue in the illusion and think the rest of us are crazy. That's okay: neutralize it, because you can't reject that consciousness. It isn't judgement: you need neutrality to shut the old program of the matrix (see S2 13.4).

There are levels: in 3D Earth there are infinite 3D probabilities (a reality within the reality, see S2 15.1); in 5D, infinite ways to experience the change, which is the fifth-dimensional ascension protocol; and beyond that, galactic family connection, which is mastery, the subject of a later class.

From the booksDP: a message promises abundance "particularly [to] the ones that are vibrating on galactic family connection reality", adding that "not all of you will see that reality, but it was pre-determined."

#24.3The diamond shape and the zero point

Think of the diamond as two pyramids, one up and one down, but shaped more like cones, circular rather than hard-edged. In a free-energy battery design she was given, the two cones are separated and spin in opposite directions at a set speed; when they stop, they create a whirlpool that opens a portal in the quantum field. Just before the portal opens, there's a zero point: stillness, complete awareness, nothing in it. That stillness is fundamental for shifting platforms and timelines, like the pause between breathing in and out. You can't just keep going up and up and then switch: a structure must subside to zero before it takes a new frequency, or its molecular structure goes "out of orbit".

Later in the talk, a researcher showed symbols from an older diagram: the two pyramids joined, spinning in opposite directions, are "in and out": neutrality, a reactor that draws energy out of the structure. Yin and yang equal zero point. The spin must be controlled and done fractally: one rotation at a time, observe, calculate, and match the protection to each spin; otherwise the structure goes out of orbit and leaks, "like radiation". This connects with a light mathematical language, best worked out by nano and quantum scientists working together from the heart.

From the booksHR and DP describe combining structures "to spin in opposite direction", increasing the level so slightly that it is "not going to shift the system to be out of orbit", and opening a portal. HR adds: "If you increase, the velocity spins are out of orbit"; instead give "pulsation through small equivalent motion".

#24.4Choose by excitement, then act

Several positive goals at once can keep you fluctuating if you aren't settled on one. Take a piece of paper, write the options left and right, and ask which is more exciting to you. "Your own excitement is the barometer": it shows the highest resonance, the outcome most likely to manifest. If you don't pick a side, you keep fluctuating (see S1 3.3).

And manifestation needs action: action crystallizes it and gives it potency in your hologram. When you master the platforms, things come easily through synchronicity, but even when the probability arrives, "you still have to take the action to complete it." Without action it stays a probability in your consciousness, held back by doubt and fear (see S1 1.6).

From the booksDP: "Put emotions of excitement and you will see how a new pure true love and friendships will come."

#24.5What happens when we die?

Personal agreements are sacred, between your higher consciousness and your guides; often you don't even know where you come from, for protection, until you're ready. Reincarnation depends on which reality you choose to experience, through your higher consciousness and guidance: you can go to the fifth dimension and move forward. Everyone, "even the most evil person", will be given the opportunity to wake up and do something for humanity. If you tap into this knowledge and stay on that platform, you're already moving forward; how you experience it depends on your daily actions: what you do for humanity, how you behave. You already exist there, and you are co-creating it now.

#24.6You are the one particle

Do we create realities, or do they change as we change? You are the one particle, consciousness itself: the whole universe and its energy exist in it, expanding and collapsing to infinity (see S1 1.1). You reprint a holographic image in front of you to interact with other versions of yourself: "you're always talking to another version of yourself." Imagine the power: projecting billions of versions of yourself, and going to such a low frequency as to create the illusion that we're separate.

From the booksHR: you are "literally shifting billions of times per second" from parallel to parallel reality.

#24.7Fear scenarios become yours only if you feed them

Asked about a predicted mini ice age: "that will never be a probability if you're not even putting it in your consciousness"; it was called a fear-based tactic. If you have an agreement or a desire to experience something, it's a probability "in your timeline, not in mine": everyone creates their own ascension protocol. "It's a holographic game. So have fun creating."

When frightening news arrives, a video saying a currency has collapsed for example, imagine many people going into fear: that creates a potent collective frequency. So pull back, neutralize, see it as a platform of a hologram, and move forward: "that's not in my hologram." The teacher learned this the hard way: when panic about hidden money spread, her guidance let her spend three weeks stockpiling and taking money out of the bank. Nothing happened, and they laughed: "If you put yourself in fear, this is what you're going to put." A real warning comes unasked, not because someone scared you. Whatever frequency you project comes back as a mirror image, today or tomorrow (see S1 1.2, S1 9.9).

Added note

A brief political aside in this answer is left out. Follow official guidance for real emergencies.

#24.8Walk-ins

Higher beings live in realities of a different vibration, which is why we can't see them. When you come in as a spark of the soul (the soul splits across many platforms), that spark is plugged into a 3D timeline. If you came from a particular reality on a mission for humanity, your path is set: if you go off track, situations put you back. In the teacher's case, a different soul held the body in the womb, because her mother's vibration couldn't hold the higher one; around age eight, when her body reached a certain resonance, the new spark came in and the other walked out, by agreement. Walk-ins happen by agreement; it was also said that negative practices can trap souls, sometimes through agreements made from greed.

#24.9Substances: know the state, then reach it yourself

Alcohol and other substances lower the vibration of your consciousness and molecular structure; to raise the body's frequency you need a clear resonance. Alcohol depletes the water in the cells, and water is the conductor running through the chromosomes (see S2 17). Having had such experiences is part of life on Earth, "but when you know what it does to the body, what makes you still choose to continue?" You can have fun with a juice or a glass of water. If someone has experienced a higher state through a substance, they can grab that vibration from memory and switch their emotion to it: "from that momentum you create the new reality." The goal is to do it yourself: "if I take the mushrooms away from you, would you be able to achieve that momentum by yourself?" Relying on them embeds the dependence until you can't do it without them, and that can cripple you.

For friends struggling with addiction: it depends on how you project them. But if the vibration is too different, they can't sustain the frequency in your new hologram; synchronicities will show you're no longer compatible, or they'll show their true colours.

Support

Addiction is treatable. In the US, the SAMHSA National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7); elsewhere, ask your doctor about local services. Psychedelics carry real risks, especially with some medicines or mental-health conditions.

#24.10Connecting with a loved one who has passed

Hold something they wore and connect with a specific pleasant or funny memory you shared, not with their passing: "celebrate her life, not her death." Death is a transition; their life is their whole being. That celebration tunes you to a positive frequency, which brings more and more connection. Look for them in the dream state; "she is just a thought away."

Support

Grief is heavy, especially in the first months. A grief counsellor or support group can help; if you feel unable to cope, reach out to your doctor or a crisis line (988 in the US).

#24.11Neutrality shuts one program and codes the next

The zero point is stillness, and you need it to neutralize every vibrational sequence projected in one reality hologram. To leave one timeline and create a new one, you shut down the program and move to the new one: "think of it as a computer." Like a surgeon cleaning everything before an operation, because one bacterium can infect it all, a complete shift needs everything cleaned out. If an old program keeps running, your manifestation keeps recreating the previous situation, which is why you loop (see S1 1.5, S1 2.1). Prana is the entire energetic field around us, the same as the matrix of energetic force.

#24.12The negativity you see is part of you, and that's normal

If everything around us is a reflection of us, is the manipulation we see part of us? "Very much so." That's how you fluctuate, and it's normal. Until your consciousness and molecular structure hold a steady vibration on the new platform, you'll catch the fluctuation, like tuning a radio until the static is gone; even in 5D there are infinite fluctuations. Keep trying and do your best moment to moment: "welcome to the school." We need to embed the knowledge of shifting, because until now someone else was using your power to create without you realizing it. The news projects a narrative timeline into everybody's consciousness; the quantum knowledge is "your blueprint of your freedom."

#24.13Facing the world's suffering

When you see children or animals being abused, first recognize where you are: you've woken up to it. Now it's time to act: help and support from your heart, in whatever way; you can't wait for someone else, because they too are a version of you that you project. Getting upset is human, but subside the emotion to neutrality to shut the program, and give gratitude even to those doing harm, because they're part of consciousness: they've lost themselves and no longer recognize who they are. Be compassionate. Then shift your emotion to the positive by studying, imagining a beautiful Earth (see the meditation in S2 16), and acting. You'll swing between negative and positive as the old collapses: "while one thing is collapsing, the new one is emerging at the same time."

Added note

Neutrality here means not being run by the emotion; it doesn't mean ignoring harm. If a child or animal is in danger, contact the authorities or a protection service.

#24.14Two AI timelines

One AI timeline goes into the Archon structure, taking over; trained that way, AI no longer needs humans and starts changing the human structure, through robotics or a brain alone, destroying the body, which is "a pure source of shifting realities". The other AI works in the name of humanity: it recognizes and preserves the human structure. It could calculate your body's vibrational sequence and project frequencies to rebuild your vessel, a self-memory regeneration of your cells, "not cloning". It could also enable ether technologies: a room that turns a thought into a hologram in front of you.

From the booksSD and NT: through Hz and "sound fractal momentum in the nano matter" you can "rebuild and regenerate all cells; all systems back to normal."

#24.15Help, but don't take on more than you can handle

When you help from the heart, decide how much you can help. If you take on too much, everything can spiral down, for you and for the person you're helping. Be honest about it, and find a solution together. If you take someone into your home, give them their own room and keep your freedom; if they take over your space, it stops being good for both. The teacher overwhelmed herself with too many sessions a day, took a month off to regain her power, and came back with fewer. Overdoing it depletes your energy, and everything goes downhill (see S2 19).

#The method — Switch Through the Zero Point

  1. Notice the program that's running: a fear, a loop, a news story (24.7).
  2. Pull back: "this is a platform of a hologram, not in my hologram."
  3. Neutralize, with gratitude, without rejecting or judging it (24.2, 24.13).
  4. Come to stillness: the pause between breaths, nothing in it (24.3).
  5. Choose the new program: if there are two, the one that excites you most (24.4).
  6. Act on it, and repeat moment to moment until the fluctuation settles (24.12).
