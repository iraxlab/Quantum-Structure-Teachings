#1.7Hard times show who you are

Under pressure people show their best and their ugliest. If you find anger, jealousy or a push to do harm, acknowledge it: that is your awakening moment of recognizing the frequency. #Questions answered

I was raised in the US from a young age and know little of my birth country. Must I go back?
It depends on potency. If you lived there long enough, or carry it strongly through ancestral DNA, something will happen to complete the cycle (meeting someone, a visit). If nothing is happening now, don't worry; your cycle may need completing in another way.
If we live by our agreements, how does ancestral healing benefit us?
Each person accumulates structures through ancestry and location; children merge the parents' DNA with greater potency, reprinting you. A child who moves far from the ancestral location will most likely need to go back to complete a cycle. But if your agreements place it elsewhere, a spouse, child or in-laws can hold that vibration around you so you don't have to. Usually something small is still completed at the birthplace: a month, two weeks, selling a property.
What if my homeland is at war and I don't want to go back?
If you need to go, you'll find repetition: every other avenue is blocked and the only working solution is to go. Weigh your situation (children, finances, work) honestly. Most of the signs (about 90%) should point to going; don't go out of fear. Fear plastered over your true frequency merges into a mishmash, and you project a timeline that loops with no solution. Pull back, look clearly, then act. If you are pushed so hard that moving is the only way, move, perhaps to another town or country. Sometimes you are where you are to help others: every pack needs a leader.
What about reported tall beings in Miami: is this Project Blue Beam?
Whether it is true for you is your creation; she chooses not to worry. The Council of Nine warned to be very cautious of any claim of invasion: positive ETs will never attack like that, and such stories feed perpetual war. Most of us are hybrids. She doesn't pass on unprovable off-planet information; she sticks to the quantum structure so you can research and grow.
