The big idea

The Council of Nine said that now, with the commotion, everything will be exposed, and the confusion it creates is by design: you can't know who is who until you see the manipulation, and when you get confused, so does the evil. In the end humanity is free; the conflict "will stop when it is least expected". Whenever a negative plan is set in motion (a conflict to drive up prices, a crisis to push an agenda), consciousness activates people and situations around it to override it, ten times stronger. Your part is not to panic: neutralize to zero point by noticing what you already have, then play hide and seek for the solution, and you'll be supported in ways you couldn't predict.

#10.1Consciousness activates around every plan

When the negative activates one area to, say, drive oil prices up, consciousness activates people in offices and situations around it to stop it, location by location, until the end result serves humanity (see S7 E22, S5 E14). You'll see it if you keep your vibration high, coherent with what consciousness is doing. Negative rituals can lower a person's vibration until a dense entity rides on their consciousness, overtaking their thinking.

#10.2Crisis, then the solution they offer

In her view, crises are created and then the "solution" is offered in exchange for your freedom: she cites reports of fuel-rationing laws revived during an oil shock, proposals for fewer private cars, and calls to choose between fuel and food. Pretty words like "clean air" can hide another purpose, while free-energy technology stays suppressed and electricity still comes largely from coal. A celebrated dismissal may actually remove someone before they must testify. Don't cheer or panic at the surface; see the whole picture.

Her guides have long given her May 5 for a key date, which she cannot prove. Different Bible editions differ (one mentions fasting, another doesn't).

#10.4Hide and seek at the gas station

Years ago, amid panic at a gas station, she stayed calm by asking: Do I have a home? Food? A little fuel in the other car? "What's the big deal?" That brought her to zero point, so the collective panic couldn't overtake her field. Then she took an action (try this station, and if not, go home) and a gap opened through hundreds of cars; she was in and out in ten minutes. Say to the projection: "Stop. I see you. I get it." Keep a little in reserve, not for the end of the world, but for calm. As in a past crisis when a nation planted every open space and rooftop, start growing food with neighbors.

#10.5Unpredictable in the now

An artificial structure predicts you by reading your emotional sequence; if you stop and shift into something new in the moment, it can't catch you. Practice the meditations until you can switch "like an actor" on waking: command your mind, control your emotions and actions. Mastery needs physical practice, not meditation alone.

#The method — Hide and seek in a crisis (as she did it)

  1. Notice the collective panic and say: "Stop. I see you. I get it."
  2. Bring yourself to zero: list what you already have (home, food, a little reserve).
  3. Feel that you have everything you need, even before you see it.
  4. Take one calm action toward a solution, with a fallback.
  5. Let the solution appear; you'll be supported in ways you can't predict.
  6. Afterwards, build a little calm reserve and grow food with neighbors.

#Questions answered

Could time travelers have reintroduced material and books for this timeline?
Great inventors like Tesla had abilities and connected with higher selves or ETs, downloading from above the 3D structure where knowledge was suppressed. She downloaded the quantum structure; AI now helps her turn it into mathematical equations. Beware the narrative that ETs are demons; if "flying saucers" start shooting, it won't be ETs: if benevolent ETs wanted to harm us, they could have long ago. Negative ETs also exist, but consciousness no longer allows them to overtake those awakening.
Is the Council's May 5 date based on a 13-month or lunar calendar? Is that why we're going to the moon?
The moon mission involves more than is said. Consciousness moves in a spiral, and when someone interjects to change a trajectory (as was done to Jesus), the reality is distorted, so dates calculated linearly may not match. Calculating them would need AI to include positions of moon and stars and vibrational pulsation across parallel realities. She can't prove May 5. Consciousness is collapsing everything back into coherence with the original spectrum, which is why not panicking matters so much.
