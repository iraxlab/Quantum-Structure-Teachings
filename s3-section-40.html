The Stock Market: A New Download

A final test of the human race, words that carry numbers, what to do when it hits like a brick, creating what didn't exist, returning to family and land, reliving outcomes, exercise while detoxing, prayer as one hand, abundance without fear, and questions (live talk with channelling)

Note on this talk

Despite the title, most of this talk is about holding your ground. Its forecasts about the stock market, crypto, courts and elections are left out: this book gives no financial or legal advice, and no prediction here should guide your money or a legal matter. Talk to a licensed adviser or lawyer.

The big idea

"In the months ahead you will all see the worst and the greatest of the human race. That is the final test." Timelines separate by the resonance you hold in your heart. So when something hits you like a brick, pull back, name it a hologram, give gratitude and mutate it, again and again; by acting without consenting, you create solutions that didn't exist before. Lean on family and the land, and drop fear, which itself attracts more fear.

#Key teachings

#40.1The final test

Positive and negative coexist under the umbrella of ascension; as they separate, "this one goes, this one stays, depending on your choosing", with no judgement. They will do something; you hold the vibration again, "on and on". The negative "is weakening, but fights cleverly": treat it as a good opponent, "not with hatred, because it's their survival, the way you try to survive" (see S3 34.3). Stay neutral, but "learn their deception and have no consent". "In two years you are free" is the linear view; "we are already free" when you hold the vibration.

#40.2Words carry numbers

Every word has a number and a geometry (see S3 39.6), and combinations can cancel each other out: a word you think means one thing may encode the opposite. That's why she says "off disengage": you're taking the disc out of the hologram, rather than just "dis-engaging". She calls this "quantum grammar": complex, "you have to be a lawyer, or very good with literature, to understand it". It's also why the councils give codes in numbers, which mean the same in every language.

#40.3When it hits like a brick

"Whatever you see is going to hit you like a brick." Pull it back, look at it as a hologram, call it a hologram, and off-disengage from it; remove yourself; give gratitude ("thank you"); then start mutating that hologram with something new, on and on. This is Season 1, practised in S3 8 (the hourly phone protocol) to train your synapses to switch resonance. To make a new hologram permanent, act moment to moment with consistency: "you cannot be fluctuating".

#40.4Create what didn't exist

"You have to create it to see it." When you refuse to consent and start looking for solutions, you create the reality that brings the answer "through a person, through a situation, something that did not exist before". If a situation demands you go against yourself, remember: your body, your sovereignty, your DNA and your soul matter more than a particular job; hold that, and you'll create the way to a new one.

#40.5Return to family and the land

If you're stripped of the illusions (artificial friendships, a job that asks you to harm others out of fear), go back to family: a family is "a specific unit of resonance", and helping each other opens heart chakras you never opened with neighbours or co-workers. After this life you answer, before the councils and yourself, "what have you done to inflict pain on others?" And the good you do here "leaks into" the realities you create next. Being nice isn't enough: they need to know you're a warrior who won't consent (see S3 26). Return to the land: fresh air, trees, organic food, planting. The earth is a living being; her fifth-dimensional name, given to her, is Arya, "new beginning".

#40.6Reliving with a different outcome

Can we relive our life with different outcomes? "Very clever. Yes", connected with your councils. At every choice, reality separates into probabilities: the one you act on becomes physical; the others still exist in the ether. You'll learn to be multidimensional, as she hears several thoughts at once, and to download from your other realities to bridge into new ones (see S3 30.10).

#40.7Exercise while you detox

Exercise "is always necessary": stretching opens the body's junctions so blood and nutrients flow (see S3 30.11). But while juicing, keep it gentle (stretching, yoga, ballet, walks, barefoot on the ground) and avoid heavy exercise like running, so you don't lose energy too fast. Increase it once your body holds the new frequency. It's "all about balance, drop by drop".

Added note

She says further stages (fruit only, water only, living from the ether) are "very tough", only ever under guides you're already in daily contact with: "do not go and listen to one person", "we do not wish to give you harm". Never attempt them: people have died. See S3 38.

#40.8Prayer is one hand

Who do we pray to? Think of a hand: each finger is its own pointer, yet all are one hand. You came from the one particle as a reflection of yourself, to experience. Being taught that you're separate from the hand, praying to someone "out there", gives your power away. The councils are "also part of this hand": they offer conditions for better realities by your consent, "not to control you". "When you do well, so do we."

#40.9Abundance without fear

Why doesn't abundance flow? "All of the above", above all fear. "Fear holds a resonance" that reflects back like osmosis and brings more fear through others: in fear you may not find the job even when you want it. So feel now that you have the job and the abundance, believe it 100%, hold it through every challenge, and act from the heart. Money is only part of it; what matters is how it comes and how you use it. Charge what you need, never so much that people suffocate (see S1 4.2, 4.4), and a couple of times a week just give. "If you need $1 million, you have it; if you need $1, you have it": it comes through synchronicity.

#The method — When It Hits Like a Brick

  1. Pull back the moment it hits: "this is a hologram."
  2. Off-disengage: remove yourself from the situation (40.2).
  3. Give gratitude: "thank you for showing me."
  4. Don't consent; look for solutions instead (40.4).
  5. Mutate it with new, positive thought and action, again and again (40.3).
  6. Lean on family and the land; help each other (40.5).

#Questions answered

Will Marina's current fractal stay on after ascension?
Yes: her consciousness will split to see simultaneous timelines of her galactic family and earth. "That is a first contact."
If everything is holographic, is it all AI? What lies outside it?
"Why do you have such a fear of AI?" Ether-technology AI is part of creation; many spacecraft are living light structures made by it. The negative kind is the 3D one, used against humans; the same technology could rebuild tissue and restore DNA. Both timelines exist; choose (see S3 34.6).
Can we relive our life with different outcomes?
Yes: see 40.6.
What is the council's view on exercise?
See 40.7.
Are we creating the Council of Nine in our reality?
Yes: "you project us in your consciousness". So why not hear them in your own thoughts? Write it down and practise.
Do animals, such as dogs, have a veil?
"Animals are directly connected": a dog loves purely, without condition, like a child's heart; it mimics deception only if taught. The new children come through 5D portals and will reject deception.
How am I doing at holding a higher vibration?
A code was given: 3313. "You're doing well"; some cleansing, then a move forward in a couple of months.
Who are we praying to?
See 40.8.
Why does abundance not flow freely for many of us?
See 40.9.
How should we prepare for a coming cosmic event?
"Let it flow." It's a rise in the galactic sun's resonance: like riding a bicycle at a hundred miles an hour, you choose where to steer, into the ditch or the meadow. "Shift wisely."
Does contact need the council's permission? What tests must we pass?
You're all being prepared, individually, guided by your own families and guides; permissions come from councils. Start with telepathic "phone calls" to where you want to connect, and ask. "We are your teachers, but not your saviours."
