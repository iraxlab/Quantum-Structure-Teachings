Awareness, Fear and Building a New Channel

Two councils' messages on a feared technology, the trinity of awareness, desire and action, why traces of the old reality still appear, honesty and self-check, and questions (live talk)

The big idea

Many people were in fear about a new wireless technology, so the messages about it were revisited. Their core is a teaching about perception: fear projects the very reality you fear, while a changed perception creates a sonic whirlpool that projects a new hologram where the old thing slowly collapses. The recipe is a trinity: first awareness (know what is really happening), then the desire to change, then action to bring it into matter. Traces of the old reality will still leak through for a while, because we chose a gradual, collective ascension; thank them for showing you where you are, and keep holding the heart. Be honest with yourself, act to help without conditions, and remember: nobody is outside you.

#Key teachings

#11.1Why fear doesn't help

This talk came because so many people were afraid of the new 5G network. The first message (11/18) sounded almost surprised: why panic, after everything we've learned? You know to believe all will be well, take action, and hold the positive outcome (see S1 2.2, S1 3.3). The message said the technology "will not work the way they are intending… it will be disrupted"; it may be allowed for a while, so that people still asleep start questioning; and the new frequency from the galactic centre will help those who hold the heart.

You coexist with the negative structure only because we chose to experience and learn from this reality. You are not bound to it.

From the booksDP prints this message (11/18): "5G. Yes. But it will not work the way they are intending… It will be disrupted", and, to "all loving and positive humans that chose to hold energy from the heart structure", the new frequency from the galactic centre "will change your DNA, increase your upgrades in your photonic structure."
Added note

These are channelled messages and claims. Health agencies have found no evidence that 5G or mobile phones harm health at normal exposure levels. Claims in the talk linking phone masts to cancer cases, and the long research playlist the talk presented (political, medical and legal material from outside sources), are left out.

#11.2The download: perception creates the new hologram

The second message: your awareness and perception of it will lead you into action, expressed as a sonic whirlpool of expansion through your thoughts (see S2 2.5), projecting through "platforms of fractal imagery for a new outcome." Change your understanding, and you can project a new frequency reality in which that manipulation no longer exists; it simply collapses, moment to moment, to non-existence. This happens methodically and slowly, with the level of awakening, individually and then collectively: when enough people build "a proper mass", it expands into the collective.

#11.3The trinity of manifestation

The message gives the order: first, knowledge and awareness: research and know what is really happening. Second, the desire to change. Third, to project it into matter, take action. "Awareness, thoughts and action: this is the trinity structure for manifestation process, 1 + 1 = 3." Then use the quantum manifestation protocol (see S1 1, Method). This is the alchemy of creation: the code for you to create your own channel instead of staying on someone else's platform.

From the booksDP prints this message in full: "Your awareness and perception of the 5G platform will lead you into an action… Awareness, thoughts and action this is the trinity structure for manifestation process 1+1 = 3."

#11.4Changing the channel from the heart

Words and thoughts create a whirlpool, a cone of frequency expanding to the zero point of your holographic structure (see S2 2.4, S2 2.5). Once your perception changes, you see through the veil: this hologram is not the only channel. Project from the heart (thoughts, emotions and deeds), and the new sonic whirlpool changes the frequency; you create a new holographic imprint, a new timeline, and "literally change the channel." From there, keep projecting from the heart, with awareness of what is happening.

#11.5Why traces of the old still appear

"I'm meditating, but I still see things happening here and there." Every hologram is interconnected, so when it splits, traces of the previous hologram remain. That's because we chose a linear experience: if you popped into a completely new world, you wouldn't know where you are, so you need traces to know what to do next. And we chose a collective ascension, in which not everyone is awake yet.

Your job: hold your vibration whatever traces you see, globally or next door. When a situation appears, ask what you can do to make it better, then use the protocol: pull out, go to zero point, give gratitude, change the frequency (see S1 1 to S1 3). Thank the experience: it shows your location, and whether you need to stay more in the heart. Keep overriding the negative; whatever you hold more becomes your permanent timeline (see S2 10.10). The leaking will continue until humanity's whole ascension structure is complete.

#11.6Change comes fractally, so it can be smooth

A major positive change was predicted within two years. But change must come one step at a time, or the system collapses. Imagine a law announced overnight that no one may eat meat: there would be an uproar, because people need to arrive at such changes by realizing them personally. So it is happening, fractally, moment to moment, for a smooth transition. And you won't be told every move: "if you play a chess game", you don't tell your opponent your next move (see S2 9.3).

#11.7Honesty and self-check

There will be no awakening until we are honest with ourselves about what we do. The talk's example: we are troubled that some consciousness may look at humanity the way we look at cattle, yet we do the same to animals, who are "another version of us"; honesty means pulling out of the ego and admitting it, then changing step by step (it is still okay to eat meat while transitioning).

And self-check how you receive warnings: no matter how intelligent we think we are, we can dismiss something until it hits us personally. Some people only wake up when a situation affects them directly. Better to look honestly first. Then take action, moment to moment, from the heart: one by one, the old particles are deleted, until your structure is clear.

#11.8One particle, every dot a universe

The one particle expands to the infinite and collapses back, pulsing like elastic, and imprints itself even into your little dot of a hologram (see S1 1.1). That is why zooming into a fractal shows the same pattern again and again; yet each is slightly different, with its own vibrational geometry: a universe for itself. So what you do here matters for the whole universe, not only the planet.

Each dot is a hologram, a situation. Holding the heart cancels it out: each time you see a situation and switch your emotions, thoughts and deeds to the positive, its negative collapses, until you reach zero point in the positive (see S2 7.6). The result is a fractal timeline with nothing to do with the negative structure: "a different channel, think of TV." Those who sacrifice for humanity do it from the heart, collectively, and the collective includes the universe.

#11.9Act, and give without conditions

"Nobody is outside of you" (see S2 9.6): what you see on a screen is part of your hologram, so knowing isn't enough; you are here on the ascension protocol, so take action. When you see someone struggling, help: that is energy philanthropy, with no expectation. A condition ("I'll help you, but I want something") is a frequency, and through infinite probabilities someone will put a condition on you: a mirror image (see S1 4.2). If you want full abundance without conditions, help without them. If you see your child being harmed, act to protect them; each parent decides how.

#11.10Face the facts first, then shift

The talk presented a long playlist of research videos, put "in order" so people can wake up step by step, with the quantum manifestation series placed in the middle: the Freedom Awakening playlist on her Harmonic Reactor YouTube channel, alongside Season 1 and Season 2 of Quantum Manifestation, the interviews and the detox video. Her instructions: press "play all" and go in order, one video then the next, "because they're connected"; make a goal to watch one or two videos every day; you'll get angry, "but keep it in a positive". Other sources she named: the film Thrive, Nassim Haramein ("8 hours, incredible information"), Gregg Braden, and Dolores Cannon ("I strongly suggest you buy her books"); and research how to get cheap or free land. The teaching behind it: you can't shift a reality you refuse to look at; the first thing the extraterrestrials did was show the teacher where she was. But don't go into fear: once you know, study the alchemy of creation and escape reality by creating your own. The quantum manifestation work is "the antidote": go at your own pace, and above all practise.

Technology isn't the enemy either: it can free time and help us, and AI can be positive or negative, depending on its use (for example, helping read and rebuild the body's tissue). Simpler, low-cost homes and local communities are possible too.

Added note

"Do your own research" is good advice only with reliable sources. Much of the playlist (on vaccines, fluoride, cancer cures, taxes and the courts) contradicts established evidence or law; following it could harm your health or bring legal trouble. Talk to qualified professionals before acting on such claims.

#The method — From Fear to a New Channel

When something in the news or around you frightens you:

  1. Become aware. Find out what is actually happening, from sources you can trust. Don't look away.
  2. Pull out to zero point. Don't take on the fear (see S1 1, Method).
  3. Give gratitude. The situation is a trace that shows your location.
  4. Choose the change you desire. Picture the reality where the problem no longer exists.
  5. Switch your emotions, thoughts and words to the positive, from the heart: the new whirlpool.
  6. Take action in the physical world: help, protect, support those working for the change.
  7. Repeat every time it reappears, until the new channel is permanent.

#Questions answered

One viewer left, upset that the talk focused on the negative. How do you respond?
You can't change what you won't face: the first step to change is realizing where you are. Respect each person's choice; no one is upset with them. Once you know, use the quantum manifestation protocol to shift: it's the antidote. Take in difficult material at your own pace, and apply what you learn (see 11.10).
Can we study reiki online and heal someone at a distance?
Yes: you read a person's frequency because they are part of your hologram. Put in the emotion. But only with permission, and only if they need it. Everyone vibrates differently, so one method won't suit everyone, "like one pill for all diseases". It works best if you are connected telepathically with your higher self, so you're guided. You can always send positive thoughts. Research a teacher before learning their method.
Since 2013 I shake whenever I meditate or receive healing. Why?
You were in the wrong meridian, and the frequency wasn't coming properly. Sit in the centre of the house, aligned with the four directions.
If letters and words hold frequency, would changing my name change my timeline?
It depends on perception. The sound of each letter opens a specific portal depending on how they combine; that knowledge is complex and isn't known here. Ancient naming systems exist and are worth exploring, but be very careful where you learn it, because some words were designed to keep a structure in place (see S2 2.10).
Will the currency revaluation and the release of hidden technologies, such as healing beds, happen within the two years?
In 2028 there will be a global check of the level of consciousness we've reached, and a decision on next steps (see S2 10.6). Within two years, a new platform of the hologram, for more positive expansion; you'll see a lot happen. Stay in the heart: your hologram can hold situations designed for your own awakening. Keep overriding the traces (see 11.5). (Comments on current political and health news are left out.)
An antenna was put on a small school, and no one could tell me its frequency. How can I help the children?
Take action: raise awareness, talk to the parents, do something. The teacher would personally take her children out of the school; each parent decides.
Added note

You can ask the school or local authority for the antenna's details and radio-frequency measurements. Public exposure limits are set well below levels known to cause harm, and the cancer claims in the answer are not supported by evidence.

What about a subscription service that uses quantum entanglement to turn interference into a positive frequency?
It isn't one frequency but many, a subtonality, and the best one is individual. No information about that service; the guidance won't put anyone's technology down. Research who is behind it and "follow the money": real help is done in the name of humanity, not for big profit. Proceed with caution.
How does the reversal of the magnetic poles relate to us? Sometimes I feel airy and can't focus.
If you want it to relate to you, you'll create that reality. It is happening very, very slowly and smoothly, not as a disaster; things have always shifted through history. The planet is raising its frequency to the next level, and we choose whether to go with it. Don't take it from fear.
What about other well-known channelled teachings?
Everyone has a place in awakening humanity. If their information helps you, learn from it; most people pass through such channels before they are ready for the quantum material. How each teacher shares (through money or energy philanthropy) is their choice, and choices can change at any moment; hope they stay in the heart.
What about a device said to kill viruses, bacteria and parasites?
Not known; worth researching. It is possible in principle. (Medical claims in the answer about strep throat, antibiotics and coeliac disease are left out; take antibiotics as your doctor prescribes.)
Is a "dove frequency" coming on a particular date?
That is your own belief and projection. Since it's positive, believing it creates a whirlpool that shifts you into a more positive platform; nothing wrong with that.
Any thoughts on a well-known arrest this week?
"The game of war, it's a chess game": think about what a legally arrested person might then reveal. (Details left out.)
You said not to do the juice cleanse with metal in the body. Is there a partial protocol?
It depends on the metal. The tissue must accept it (a matter of frequency, like a metal allergy), and the metal must not break down with acidity, from juice or any food. Find out what metal you have, ask about an allergy or compatibility test and alternatives, and watch your body's pH.
Added note

If you have implants or other metal in your body, talk to your surgeon or doctor before starting any cleanse or major change in diet.
