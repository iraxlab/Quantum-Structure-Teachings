The big idea

Much of this talk pointed listeners to outside research on legal systems, which this book doesn't reproduce (see the note). The lasting teachings: words carry frequency, so the way a sentence is built can loop you back into what you meant to leave; within any collective storm you can hold your own hologram, with shelter, water and food; and manifestation needs an open program: no expectations of how, when or through whom, because trying too hard and doubting shut it down, while steady projection builds until it expands "like an avalanche".

Added note

This talk recommended outside videos claiming that common laws, taxes and mortgages are invalid. Those claims are not included. Refusing to pay taxes or a mortgage on that basis has led people to fines, lost homes and prison; for any legal or financial decision, consult a qualified lawyer or adviser. Political predictions are also left out.

#Key teachings

#15.1Words can loop you back

Each word has a sonic, vibrational whirlpool that builds reality (see S3 7.1), so sentence structure matters. The example given: "disengage" sounds like moving away, but may carry "engage"; to move away, the teacher suggested saying "off". Be precise in what you say and affirm, so the words carry you where you mean to go. And change in a system comes "from within", from those who understand it.

#15.2Your own hologram inside the collective

"You cannot reject the collective; you're part of the structure, but you can represent your own self within it" by broadcasting your own frequency. So even if people are fighting somewhere, "you're always going to have a shelter, you always can create water, food" through the fluctuation. You also carry it everywhere: people begin asking you about what you know. Be "the calm within the storm": those who practise it become a stable foundation for others. Not everyone is at the same level of awareness; that's why experiences differ.

#15.3Hard truth needs the structure

"It takes bravery to face the truth and move forward", because many beliefs will collapse; then you need consistency and a strong mind to convert it. Without the quantum structure, frightening information can loop a person "deeper and deeper in fear" until they create a war zone for themselves. That's why the structure comes first: practice S1 1–4 until it's potent.

#15.4Trying too hard blocks it

Every action is right in the end, even an error, because it makes you question and grow. "Sometimes when you try too hard, you have the opposite effect": telling yourself you're not good enough projects exactly that. Study without expectations of when, how or from where, and let the collective field put the jigsaw together. When it doesn't work, don't say "I tried so hard"; that shuts the program. Say, "that wasn't the resonance; I'll try again", and leave it open (see S3 14.6).

#15.5It builds, then expands like an avalanche

The teacher spent about two years helping non-stop without seeing results, until "you collected enough frequency, and now it's going to expand", and it came all at once. And it came from people she didn't expect: the person she was sure could help didn't; the opening came through a chance phone call. "It usually happens exactly from the people you don't expect." The more you study, the more you hear in the same material, because your brain's resonance is changing.

#15.6Share without being walked over

Give your knowledge "when it's needed, when it's wanted". Say openly who you are and what you share, and the right people find you; every train has its passengers. Let others hold their own views on their own platforms, without pointing fingers. But respect works both ways: "Nice is good, love is good. But when somebody is stepping on top of you and is disrespectful toward your sovereignty, take a stand and say no more." Power can destroy or protect; use it to protect.

#15.7You can rise while unwell

Being ill lowers the body's frequency span, but not your timeline: "the real sequence of the vibration is your heart", and the structure mirrors your heart's resonance, not the body's. Bring the body back to health in its own way; the body matters for mutation later, for contact above 5D.

#15.8Train joy if you've rarely felt it

If happiness is unfamiliar, you first need to recognize its frequency: watch something genuinely funny for 10–15 minutes every day. Once it crystallizes, you can recall it at will for manifestation. When you drop, lift it physically: dress up, dance, put on music, go out. "The illusion that you have to go to a club or drink to be happy" is just that: "I can have a grape juice and pretend that it's wine." You can ferment grape juice for two days in a dark brown bottle: it becomes very fermented, "good for your gut flora", and that can be your wine (see S3 38.4).

#The method — Training the Frequency of Joy

  1. Each day, watch 10–15 minutes of something that truly makes you laugh.
  2. Notice the feeling in your body while you laugh: that's the frequency.
  3. Recall it later, without the video, for a few seconds.
  4. Use it when you meditate on what you want (15.8).
  5. When you drop, move: music, dance, dress up, go out.
  6. Repeat daily until joy is easy to find.

#Questions answered

Will the currency change? Should I pay off my student loans?
A new system, digital and hard to manipulate, was said to be in place for when the right people are in office. (For loans and money decisions, see the note above: ask a financial adviser.)
Others see angels and elementals; I don't. Am I not ready?
"Look at your wording: I don't see it." Feel and know that you see it, even before you do, to project it.
Is climate change natural or man-made?
Both: a natural cycle, and resources were used without regard.
How long should I listen to the Bach?
As much as you like, on and off; the earlier protocol is no longer needed (see S3 2).
How do I know I'm on the right course for ascension?
See 15.4 and 15.5.
Will the economy recover soon? I may lose my job.
About three years globally, step by step; some small businesses will close. Meanwhile, lay low, follow the rules calmly, and don't fight: things come to fruition.
Is there a minimum number of people needed to change a country?
It depends; but in the field, one person meditating carries potency "by the power of ten" over one acting negatively.
People online attack me when I share. Am I wasting my time?
See 15.6.
Should we push back against new networks, or neutralize them in meditation?
Stay neutral and under the radar; it will not work the way some intend.
Where is my higher self, and what shape?
Everywhere: you are the one particle, expanding into every layer and collapsing to the smallest (see S1 1.1).
I can't balance my weight despite eating well.
Ask your doctor about a thyroid antibody test, which can show the thyroid being attacked before standard tests do; repair through diet only with a doctor and nutritionist.
Can I raise my vibration while I'm unwell?
Yes: see 15.7.
Should I study other channelled teachings?
If you wish; the teacher kept her material clear of others' so it came through without interference.
I find it hard to feel happy.
See 15.8.
I stopped my blood-pressure tablets with distilled water and get dizzy. Is that okay?
"I'm not a doctor, so I cannot comment." Whenever you do something like this you need a doctor's supervision: take your vitals and blood work, because you need to know your levels. "Whatever I was doing, I never did it by myself, I always had a check-up by a doctor." "Don't ever just do it like that without checking your body frequently."
Added safety note

Please don't stop blood-pressure medication on your own: it can cause a dangerous rise in blood pressure or a stroke. Dizziness needs a doctor's check. Talk to your doctor before any change.

What role should I play in the bigger picture?
"What do you wish to do?" Whatever excites you, from love and giving, even painting a neighbour's house: consciousness needs it.
My adult son has struggled since a psychosis and stopped his medication.
"You've done what you can possibly do."
Support

Please keep his doctors involved; family support groups can help too. In a crisis in the US, call or text 988.

How can I help my daughter's nightmares, where something pulls her away?
Know she's already okay, even while you see otherwise, and at the same time look for practical help: someone qualified to work with her fears.
