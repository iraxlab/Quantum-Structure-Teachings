Controlling and Shifting Parallel Timelines

The big idea

Every moment you shift into a new parallel reality, a new Earth, reprinting yourself and everyone in it. To control that shift, three things must work as one unit: your consciousness must know the quantum structure so completely that no other possibility exists, because a single doubt splits off a sub-reality; your body must be cleared gradually so its molecules can take a higher frequency; and your heart and behaviour must match, because the heart holds the codes of the portals that shift timelines. You never go back or forward in time: you create a new version of yourself in a similar vibration, moment by moment. Between 3D and 5D lies the transition, a stillness where both realities mix.

Before you read

This is a master class: it assumes S1 1–3 (the quantum manifestation protocol). If those aren't familiar, read them first.

#Key teachings

#25.1Why controlling the shift matters

Every moment you shift into another parallel reality by reprinting yourself and every other version of everybody into it: "every moment is a new Earth" (see S1 2.7). This knowledge belongs to the fifth-dimensional and extraterrestrial platforms. As the molecules of your body begin vibrating at a higher frequency, your geometry in the quantum field changes: you "shift to another channel", and the more you shift, the more you construct a platform where you can connect with your guidance. Telepathic contact can come first, or contact through lucid dreaming or out-of-body experience; physical contact with beings above the fifth dimension needs the body itself to change. How it happens depends on your level, what you've learned and how you've acted with people. And, as always: "Do not trust anybody, even me"; let it be embedded only if it resonates.

#25.2You will be tested

As your vibration rises, you will be tested on the weakest link in your personality and behaviour: have you forgiven people? Something will hit you, and exactly how you react shows the true vibration you hold. Until you pass those lessons, you may not reach certain levels (see S2 14.4).

From the booksHR: "Once you start to learn this material, you will be tested from your teachers and guidance of a higher order. It is always about how you act towards people", and "when you least expect it."

#25.3Step one: consciousness that knows the structure

First your consciousness must understand the quantum manifestation knowledge: shifting, creating new realities, moving between platforms. A short review: you fluctuate between realities according to your actions, thoughts and deeds (see S1 3.3); between two realities a third is created, 1 + 1 = 3 (see S1 2.2), and as you fluctuate they collapse into one outcome. Whether it's positive or negative depends on which you implemented more, daily and fractally. Four, between 3D and 5D, "is the transition" (25.10).

#25.4Neutralize until every particle is cleared

When the version of someone you project behaves negatively, don't answer with negativity: "Thank you very much, I see what you're doing, but I'm going to subside my emotion to zero point." That shuts the program of that hologram; then shift your frequency with a new positive emotion to a brand-new timeline, so the collapse ends in a positive outcome. If they give you problems a hundred times, neutralize a hundred times: it keeps coming because there's a lesson, and the code keeps running until every particle of that frequency is cleared (see S2 24.11).

#25.5Step two: a body cleared gradually

Once your consciousness can shift parallel realities, the diet lets the spinning of your molecular structure change. It must go with a detox, as given step by step: about a year of detox and a year of elimination diet, removing one thing every two weeks while juicing. Before juicing, see your doctor for an allergy test; start with no more than two cups at a time, because it's potent and can cause nausea until the body adjusts. Flushing removes the chemicals blocking the pineal gland and molecules, so they can take the new frequency: "mutation cannot come properly if you have a lot of chemicals in the body." Consciousness and body are not separate: they're one hologram, one unit (see S2 4.3–4.5).

#25.6Step three: know it so fully that nothing else exists

Embed the knowledge so well that your thoughts, emotions and deeds vibrate in synchronicity with it, and any other possibility is pushed aside. Why? Because doubt creates a sub-reality: "maybe it's not going to happen" splits you into two parallel timelines, one where it happens and one where it doesn't, and you fluctuate. Be a steady unit: in the now, this is the only possibility. Then you construct it as you go, by acting, because it already exists and you experience it into being. Only then can your consciousness clearly direct the new structure: through thoughts, actions, diet and the quantum knowledge, you can mutate every cell and the DNA of your body.

From the booksDP: "If you have any doubt know that you are creating your own and projecting your own holographic reality that will mimic your own frequency."

#25.7The danger: never rush it

"This is very dangerous if you're not fully prepared." It is the combination of conscious understanding and a healthy diet. For breatharianism and detox she points to Akahi Ricardo and Camila Castillo ("very good with breatharianism and detox"), but first do the elimination diet the way she gives in her videos. Living without food cannot be reached in seven days: "you don't do that until you're completely connected already", because "you really can die from something like this." Once connected, your guidance tells you what to stop, how much to take, how much to juice. And it isn't about appearance: dieting while greedy or unkind does nothing, and neither does connection without the diet. Body, consciousness and behaviour are one unit.

Added warning

Do not attempt to live without food (breatharianism or "living on prana"). People have died trying it. Any detox, elimination diet or fast should be supervised by a doctor, especially if you take medication or have a health condition.

#25.8The heart holds the timeline codes

When body and consciousness both rise, the frequency of the heart changes, and "the heart is the one that holds the vibrational sequence for the new portals to shift timelines", not the mind alone (see S2 23.10). That's why you do positive things: positive frequency goes into the heart structure. This class only plants the seed: once you know the knowledge exists, study it and act, and you'll be given opportunities through synchronicity, personally guided by your higher self or guidance.

#25.9A blueprint built in similar vibrations

Action and information create the structure, the magnetic and gravitational field; with enough potency it pops into matter. Implemented, it becomes a blueprint in your matrix, stored in your quantum field. Each moment you shift to a new reality of a similar vibration to the last, and that chain of similar sequences is your timeline (see S1 1.3).

You never travel back into the past or forward into the future: every moment you create a new version of yourself in a new timeline, in a similar vibration. Infinite parallel imprints exist in just one moment, because the one particle is infinite (see S1 1.1). Beings vibrating at much higher speeds know how to shift: they can reprint another version of themselves down through different sequences to experience Earth. "So can you."

#25.10The fourth dimension is the transition

We don't skip the fourth dimension: we transition through it. Between two timelines there is a space for the switch, "almost like a no time", a stillness like the zero point. Between 3D and 5D, the vibrational sequences of both are literally mixed: particles of one merge into the other, a mutation, until the new reality becomes permanent. Drawn as two overlapping circles, the overlap is the transition, and its centre is the zero point (this new information came through a question).

From the booksThe same two-circle image appears in S1 8.3 (the Vesica Piscis), with stillness at the centre. SD mentions "the 3D 4D brain frequency" as unable to register higher sequences until the body's vibration rises.

#25.11Choose which reality is your truth

"Everything is true in a quantum structure." A reality with a craft and one without both exist in the now; you choose which you wish to be your truth. If you see it in your mind, it already exists, but if you keep doubting, more of you was in the version where it doesn't. What others say comes from their own parallel reality and what they can reach. So don't take on someone else's belief system: a person who is unkind to others can be the nicest person to you. "Create your own. That's the mastery." "I'm another version of you: if I can do it, so can you" (see S2 23.7).

#25.12When you keep meeting blocks

Believing that something is "not allowing me to progress" holds that vibration, and you shift into a reality that holds it too. Challenges come so you learn: to become a better personality, to get stronger, or to hear what helps you do better. Imagine that you've already succeeded, and continue even when you don't see it: "even if you fail 100 times, you're going to continue the 101st time with a different approach every time" (see S2 23.1). It's the hide-and-seek game: you know you have it, you construct it, and you look for it at the same time (see S1 1.7).

#25.13Only the now exists

Every moment is its own holographic imprint; two seconds later you've reprinted another version of yourself and everyone. "Nothing else exists except what I feel right now, what I do right now, what I'm projecting right now." Someone who yelled at you two minutes ago is literally in another reality: stop talking about it, neutralize it with gratitude, and its frequency stops coming into the now.

#The method — Controlling the Shift

  1. Know the structure: study S1 1–3 until it is your only possibility (25.3, 25.6).
  2. Neutralize every negative program to zero, as often as it returns (25.4).
  3. Switch to a positive emotion at once, so the collapse ends positive.
  4. Remove doubt: notice "maybe not" and return to knowing (25.6).
  5. Clear the body gradually, under medical supervision, never rushing (25.5, 25.7).
  6. Act from the heart with people; your reactions are your tests (25.2, 25.8).
  7. Live in the now, building a blueprint of similar vibrations moment by moment (25.9, 25.13).

#Questions answered

I charge my distilled water with a set of plasma products and want to transition to living on prana. Is the water good for me, and can I stop my supplements?
Such plasma devices must be built as a secure platform; otherwise there can be a leakage, "almost like a radiation leakage", and she couldn't guarantee how well yours were made. Her suggestion: distilled water only.
Added warning

Don't stop supplements or medication, or try to live without food, on your own: see the warning at 25.7 and talk to your doctor.

I keep moving toward a positive outcome in my projects, but I keep meeting blocks and interference.
See 25.12; and study S1 1–3 again.
By the time we give meaning to what we experience, we're no longer in the now. How can we be in the now and still make sense of it?
See 25.13.
Breathing in deeply from the belly releases stress for me. Can you add anything?
She pointed to the meditation she was given by the Council of Nine (see S2 16) and couldn't vouch for other methods.
My cousin took his own life long ago. Did his soul go back to source, or reincarnate?
"He is moving forward." She sensed depression and drugs, with others involved in the drug use, but couldn't pinpoint more.
Support

Losing someone to suicide is a particular kind of grief. Support groups for suicide-loss survivors can help. If you're struggling yourself, in the US call or text 988; elsewhere, your local crisis line.

Some teachers say the fourth dimension is a consciousness we skip, going from 3D to 5D. Is that so?
No: we transition through it. See 25.10.
I sensed a huge ship over the city, and a channel later described one. Was what I felt true, or am I in a false timeline?
See 25.11: both realities exist; which one do you choose as your truth, and where is the doubt coming from?
When the heart's vibration increases, does it affect pulse, heart rate and blood pressure?
"All of the above, yes."
Added note

Any new or lasting change in your heart rate or blood pressure should be checked by a doctor.

I feel a lot of fluctuation, and old patterns of behaviour are coming back. Any advice besides S1 1–3?
Hold the vibration and learn to switch your emotion from negative to positive. Set your phone to buzz every half hour; when it does, check your emotion and thoughts, and if they're negative, switch them with action. Give gratitude to what's happening, so the previous reality's imprint shuts down. If it keeps coming, keep shutting it down: "do it again, and again, infinitely, until you get it." (The host added: it's a practice, like a sport; it becomes consistent as you practise.)
Is there guidance for my soul?
The person couldn't be reached on the call; personal guidance needs a specific question.
I feel someone or a group hits me with energy that depletes me. How can I protect my outcome?
This isn't her area; her guidance keeps her away from tuning into it. If it's physical, it's harder to control your emotions, thoughts and deeds, "however, help will be given". (She referred the person to someone else, not named here.)
Added note

If you feel targeted or watched, or in distress, please talk to a doctor or someone you trust; support is available.

Where do I go next with my healing business?
"Expand": you go wherever you create. Nothing is constructed yet, so start: feel in the now that you already have the business and are successful, use the meditation (see S2 16) with your business as the focus, then work on it daily. "You cannot be two days happy about it and the next day doubting."
I'm mostly raw vegan, but pathogens keep returning. Why, if I'm not feeding them?
It was read as candida overgrowth: look at your diet, as some vegetables carry it. Do occasional preventive cleanses, such as herbal teas for the gut flora.
Added note

Recurring infections should be checked by a doctor, who can test for candida and other causes.

Is there a message for me?
It needs a specific question: otherwise the information "goes everywhere".
I don't remember dreams, and my husband says I cry out in my sleep. Why?
Your sleep frequency isn't relaxed enough to hold the dream's memory, read as accumulated stress. Relax: a day at a spa, a caffeine-free relaxing tea such as chamomile. Practise dream recall: as you wake, before you even open your eyes, look for what you dreamed and write down even a sentence or image. The crying was read as frequencies leaking from a painful simultaneous reality; stabilize here to shut it down: detox, and lots of relaxation.
Added note

Shouting or crying out during sleep can have medical causes; if it continues, mention it to your doctor.
