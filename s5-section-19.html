New Message To All Groups

A coded message from the Council of Nine and the Federation for specific groups; why goodness is not weakness and love without action leaves you open; why everyone has a role; and questions on the seedlings, 4D, 5D matter, ascension alone or together, children, contact and dolphins (live talk)

The big idea

Some messages she receives are meant for specific groups, including factions that split from those who controlled the planet and now work to preserve humanity; she is given only the message, for protection, and is told the right people will find it. The core teaching for everyone: the outcome is positive, but nothing comes "boom" overnight; change is slow, with testing grounds. Love must be backed by action and the power to say no. "Goodness does not mean weakness." Every soul has a role; without your one particle, consciousness cannot be fulfilled.

#Key teachings

#19.1Why she receives group messages

Your consciousness was dropped from the extraterrestrial platform into the 3D matrix as a new "reality avatar" while holding the same structure above. Because of a commander-type role and work with councils, she sometimes receives messages for groups she doesn't know. "Whatever I can achieve, so can you"; she asks not to be taken as more than anyone else. The secret societies split: most were for power and money, not destroying humanity; others went into dark practices. Now they war with each other, and "everything has been predetermined" by consciousness. In a war there are casualties, usually those who blindly followed orders.

The message: to all groups (brief)

"All they're doing will go south... It is a law." Evil "splits in absolute power" and destroys itself. "In the next couple of months, big changes... full exposure, global outrage. Do not travel until August"; watch which countries go along. "The collective consciousness is not awake yet to full capacity, but [it is] enough percentage to move forward." New technologies (trains, gas) will change slowly, by treaties, in testing-ground countries. "America is holding the trajectory for ascension; all the world is watching." Scandinavia will "break through the veil" and be pushed to act; South Africa will rise to take its freedom back.

Coded lines for named groups (Rising Sun, Black Dragon, Black Sun): "The stargate of the left side of the moon is secure... The galactic sectors of interstellar fleet is already in position... Signals are in sequence 31371147... See that in the motherboard... back to federation quarter four... liberation protocol engage." "Do not try to reclaim power again over the humans... equal, fair exchange... You will all have new banking system soon. Remember to share... Do not touch the seeds... Leave the seedlings and their families free." She doesn't know more.

#19.2Love them enough to let them choose

Everyone receives what they consented to. Help those who ask, but you can't change a mind that doesn't wish it: love them enough to let them go their way (see the remission pockets, S5 18). Study the quantum structure, given free, so you create your own platforms toward ascension.

#19.3Goodness is not weakness

Those who believe they need only "spread love" without acknowledging truth can be easily overtaken. Compassion makes you stronger: say no, "I will not consent to this manipulation... I am going to create my own." Hold your ground and power, acting in the name of preservation and love; be a warrior when you must. Love and meditation without physical action leave your platform wide open; you can't meditate away what is being done to you. "Physical action is absolutely the key." Find private associations, doctors, lawyers and other systems: they appear when you start looking.

#19.4Every soul has a role

Every particle adds potency to consciousness's expansion; all are pushed between pockets and trajectories until everything collapses positive, "like a magnet". "You are significant as God itself in a collective, and God itself in a fractal as you." Earth now is the toughest schoolyard, a one-in-a-million chance: graduate, and you'll be given opportunities elsewhere (to lead a fleet, decide for a planet). Staying on the trajectory, you'll meet the right people and be grouped to create something in the name of humanity.

#Questions answered

Who are the seedlings, and the seeds?
Specific avatars, lightworkers seeded into the matrix to download information and help liberate humanity, some working in very dark places; not the star-seed children. The groups were warned not to touch them. She can't say more.
How do we let our cosmic family know we want to help?
It is already known; that is why this information and action are coming. Many were manipulated by the system and deny truth through ego, importance or fear.
Do we visit the motherboard in sleep to get the rest of the code?
No; it is for specific groups and is physical. "We already have an interstellar fleet."
Are we out of the 3D grid, in 4D, able to drop back through fear?
Yes (see S5 15): constant negativity creates a lower timeline. Don't consent to manipulation; research, find the right people, avoid speculation; synchronicity shows the path. Staying put out of fear keeps you in their system.
Is the fifth dimension material or energetic?
Both. Everything is energy at different densities. Higher resonance is more fluid and fast; particles merge quickly; energy is so clear and precise that structures pass through each other, unlike moving through muddy water.
Do we ascend alone or as a group?
Both at once: one particle expands to the collective and collapses back. Keep shifting negative emotion to positive. A negative situation fluctuates, but consciousness pushes its outcome positive.
Do humans have a real role, or only ETs?
Every soul has a role (see 19.4). "Do not put yourself down."
I try hard and still can't manifest the positive. Is negativity crushing me?
No, you are being challenged, to push you to different decisions. Hold your ground; looking back you'll see every challenge was right for the trajectory to collapse positive.
Will we see the white hats act soon?
You already have, in alternative media. Don't fear if mainstream media raises an ET threat; it is possible in a reality within the reality, so don't be afraid if anything happens.
How do we pass this to children?
Start with diet and nutrition. Children's brains are sponges: teach proper words, math, behaviour. The child's soul already knows what it wants; let it experience (she unschooled her children, using programs they liked). Be the parent first, monitor what they learn, teach that no one tells them what their reality is. Hands-on learning: geometry, Fibonacci, visual videos, then build something together.
I can't connect with my galactic family. How will I know?
You need about 30% more for a clearer signal; practise. Before bed, enter the alpha state, let go of sound, ask, and write the answer immediately.
Can dolphins help with childbirth by sonar?
Yes. At birth, mother and child synchronize in a sub-tone that dolphins recognize and can guide with sound, making birth easier.
Added safety note

Please plan any birth with qualified medical care; births with dolphins carry real risks.

Unanswered: whether the Black Sun and Black Dragon are the same (she declined). Left out: political commentary and videos; named people.
