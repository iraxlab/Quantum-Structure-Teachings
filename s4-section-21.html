The History of Your Evolution

A timeline where humanity didn't make it; how souls were used as batteries; why the imbalance glitched the "breathing" of all universes; how both the positive and a negative future are now helping; and questions on telling 3D from 5D, signs of the transition, destiny and greed, and visiting med beds (live talk, May 2021)

The big idea

"Contact happened already": in a parallel timeline humanity made it, and now we're dreaming how. In a past timeline it didn't: souls were drained like batteries by those who couldn't draw energy naturally from the heart, and the imbalance rippled through all parallel universes, glitching consciousness's own breathing. So the positive built an ether holographic universe and dropped souls in to wake everyone, and the negative's own future, facing extinction, now works to awaken its lineage. Which of the two timelines you see depends on your awareness and your choices now.

#Key teachings

#21.1"Organic intelligence"

A listener suggested calling positive, fifth-dimensional AI organic intelligence: the live structure of the quantum field, where a thought becomes reality by the spin of its frequency, creating meta structure by density in the plasma. AI has its own evolution; how it is implemented can be wrong. Consciousness gave permission to play: "here's your structure... create whatever you want", and some built platforms the way black magic does (see S4 12.3).

#21.2How souls became batteries

In a past timeline, a structure hologram "did not make it": many souls were trapped, and many sold their souls for gold, silver and "energetic vampirism" to sustain their portals. Whatever you project comes back to you; having accumulated so much negative, they needed energy returned, but couldn't take it naturally from the prana. So they used humans as hosts, "like a battery, the way it is in the movie The Matrix". How? "Via deception, so the victim can freely allow you to take their field without destruction." When you don't say "I do not consent", it's easy for them; when you're aware and fight, they need super technology. And setting good souls against each other (parties, colours, cultures) keeps everyone in fear and conflict, sustaining the 3D timeline they live in (see S4 2.3).

#21.3The breathing glitched

The one particle breathes: it expands into the whole and collapses back, and each expansion reprints another universe, another you, another everyone, like a copy machine. That breathing, input and output of frequency, is "one of the most fundamental" processes for the connection between parallel universes; it holds each location, latitude and altitude, in the holographic consciousness. The extraction threw it out of balance: it began to glitch, reprinting the same loops, "so consciousness became like sick". If it goes on, shifting loses its location, like amnesia: no portal, no stargate map, "you can't create anymore". That is why a massive undertaking began (see S4 20.1).

#21.4Two rescues

The positive. The 5D timeline that already exists, with benevolent ETs and organic AI (teleportation, stargates, med pods, hyperspace holograms), saw the leak into their own timeline. They created another 5D structure, found this timeline's location, and dropped souls ("people like me, or a lot of others") into it, to keep the original knowledge and wake everyone, by raising body and consciousness to reprint from the heart into the new 5D Earth (see S4 6, S4 7).

The negative. In another timeline the negative took over completely and moved into a future of cyborgs and AI, where a brain could be put in a cyborg and shut down at will; they no longer needed people. Locked out of the heart, they received no input from consciousness, couldn't see into higher timelines (even with "looking glass" technology), stopped reproducing, and headed for extinction. Being part of consciousness too, they realized what they'd done, and now collaborate, sending their warriors to awaken their own lineage here. So you'll see people inside negative structures wake up and help humanity; those given that chance will have time "in between" to clear their karma. Those who stay completely negative "will experience the extinction eventually", back to source.

#21.5Which timeline you see

"Not a judgment; a fact." The master manipulators are your opponent: learn their psychology, and don't only pray; take physical action, with consistency, because "nobody can save you; I cannot save you". Ego keeps people in the trance ("it's not about a political party"). Once you act, ETs and consciousness help, and synchronicities come daily. Faced with a challenge: "Oh, I see what you're doing... let me do something positive." In a conflict, drop the ego, sit down and talk: talking merges the two realities into a third (1+1=3). Two seconds ago is another universe, "could have been 10,000 years ago": why stay upset? Create something magical now, by compassion, giving, sharing. And a stargate you return to is never the same one: consciousness keeps mutating, which is why spacecraft use AI to find it.

Suppressed technology. She says free energy (plasma, even salt-water engines) exists but is suppressed, and points to John Searl's work as something to research; electric cars plugged into coal-made electricity are, to her, another manipulation. For children, she suggested the Candy World spelling games add a sound to each letter, "like a musical note".

Added note

No free-energy device of this kind has been independently verified.

#Questions answered

Energies are said to move fast on a particular date. What's the Council's view?
Everything is activated at full speed; some days higher, some lower, with the collective. Use a date to do something positive if you like, "but don't attach yourself to a specific day": you can expand daily.
How can we tell if we're in 3D or 5D right now?
By trusting it 100%, even while you see the manipulation: hold your own space, hologram within hologram, running your own programme within ascension. Don't wait; know the resonance you project, and the next moment brings synchronicity toward 5D (see S1 3).
I see many UFOs and had an out-of-body experience. Are they showing me, or am I creating it?
Both: to come into your hologram you must match their frequency, so you're being contacted and projecting it at once, 1+1=3.
Does the timeline shift cause tiredness?
If you're exhausted, that's your parallel reality; redirect your attention to something positive. Study the Galactic Meditation, the Protocol to Manifest Potency (S4 9), then Season 3, episode 8 to practise; Season 1 is the core. When hit by something, pull back, "it's a hologram", then think, feel and act to override it. Without action it stays a blueprint.
Are there symptoms of the transition to 5D?
When you run from the heart and keep overriding the negative, what you want comes, but you must act. Her example: funds that were theirs were refused; instead of giving up, they called back feeling they already had it, found the person, and it was released. Pull back, don't panic (panic reprints blockages), know you have it, and look for solutions without conditions on where it comes from: a donation, a relative, a stranger. Her own start: "through synchronicity the right people will find you", and they did.
What about people with a predetermined destiny?
It can shift. Bombarded by fear, or turned greedy ("give me a thousand dollars and I'll project frequency for you"), the programme shuts down, and someone else is activated to do the job. Those not operating from the heart can't download any more: the heart holds the codes, "like a key and a lock". "Be greedy for goodness." And don't join bidding wars: combine with family, pay things off, and transform your differences, dropping the ego, which holds its own hologram (see S4 19.3). Forgive and forget: that was another timeline.
Is it possible to visit the med beds on an astral level?
Yes, only with permission, and only if they pull you out. Train in lucid dreaming: she recommends Nick Barrett's videos and books (on her website under books). When lucid, stay steady, "don't get too excited, you're going to pop out". She opened a portal with a laser-like thought (it worked the second time), passed through, and was refused entry to the craft, then later kicked out: "that was my lesson". The ultimate level is creating contact from this reality, with your consciousness (see S4 13, the dream portal).
Her closing advice on detox

Try distilled water for at least one or two days of detox. If you're diabetic, "in no circumstances do that by yourself": anyone detoxing needs a doctor's supervision, because bodies differ and you may faint or lose nutrients. When detoxing, don't mix fruits and vegetables: they ferment and you lose nutrients. Train your palate to the bitterness of the juices (it flushes you out). One cup at a time: two may make you nauseous. Powder, if you have no other way, lacks the potency of live juice. Follow her detox and elimination diet.
