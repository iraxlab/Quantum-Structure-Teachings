The big idea

The Council of Nine said the collective is over 73% and counting, enough to open stargates toward the fifth-dimensional platform. An event will bring shock, despair and disbelief, which quickly becomes awareness and a trajectory of "miracles"; our reaction decides which parallel outcome we get. Actions against humanity become incompatible with the new frequency and collapse on themselves. Timelines of dark ages exist, but tuning in to them feeds them; a positive timeline is spinning stronger. Consciousness always pushes you forward, so change now: do something positive every day and be informed briefly, not 24/7.

#32.1Those acting against humanity

Officials acting against humanity are not compatible with the collective's intervention. They are pushed out of the field into their own timeline, where their actions create dark forces against themselves and collapse. After the leap, the structures that controlled the world for centuries will be repelled. Anyone who tries something predominantly evil gets an "electroshock" in their own hologram: their frequency drops to a low murmur and they can't think clearly enough to go after you.

#32.2When it spins out of control

Some things may not be stopped, and some regions will see division, protests and drama, designed to make people fight each other (civil war) instead of seeing who manipulates them. But at the moment things seem about to explode, "something out of the blue" will make people pull back. The same may happen in personal life (a friend, a marriage, the bank): it rises, then subsides, and you can transition out of it. With 73% and counting, the negative can't penetrate your hologram unless you let it confuse you.

#32.3Don't feed the dark-age timeline

Many people download a timeline of dark ages or worse. If you believe it and keep tuning in, you resonate with it and feed it. A positive timeline is spinning more now: there may be a transition of a couple of months or a banking change, but if one thing closes, something else opens. Those behind the squeeze are about 3% of the population and can't conceive of another outcome; you have enormous power. They promote collapse so your belief locks you into their reality.

#32.4Why details are never given

Every download is a probability. The Council gives the overall picture, in words that won't cause fear, to keep your frequency in the fifth-to-sixth-dimensional sound wave so your emotion is more potent than the negative. Your daily behavior is what feeds the field; if you keep tuning into negative belief, you may experience it individually. Before pointing fingers, look in the mirror.

#32.5Why evil is allowed to show

There is always yin and yang. Some things must be allowed so you can see what evil is and what it plans; otherwise, like someone raising dark topics at a family barbecue, no one would listen. It is a laboratory of consciousness: a creation in the making, so you learn to restructure and become a master of manifestation and quantum self-healing.

#32.6Consciousness keeps pushing

Consciousness is omnipotent, omnipresent and always moving; you are never in the same space-time. Stillness is only a fractal moment. If ego or ideology makes you stay, you will be "put to your knees" until you learn, in this life or another. A mind stuck like swimming in muddy water can't move its charge, so it creates off-balance realities. Change now.

#32.7The one-week practice

Pull away from negative social media and do something positive every day, moment to moment: a positive film, something good for yourself. Try it for a week and you will start seeing beauty around you. Be aware, but what is happening "somewhere" may be a different world; your reality is what you experience now.

#32.8Stay in the solution (her closing advice)

Fear-based "maybe this, maybe that" fluctuates between two negative probabilities, so it can only collapse negative (the 1 + 1 = 3 principle). When you hear a solution, stay in it; keep learning and listening to uplifting things so the positive reprints and collapses positive. See it once, acknowledge it ("I'm a hologram, it doesn't have to be a negative outcome"), then tune into what you want to help with. Don't underestimate the other side; they are clever.

Added safety note

Added safety note: she describes her own detox (one type of fruit every four hours, otherwise water, for seven or eight days, then juices toward water only) and herbs. Fasting and mono-diets can be risky for some people, including those with diabetes, eating disorders, pregnancy or medication needs; check with a doctor first.

#The method — Converting what you see (her closing steps)

  1. Be informed: see it once and acknowledge it.
  2. Pull back: "I'm a hologram; this doesn't have to be a negative outcome."
  3. Don't watch it ten times or follow people who repeat only the negative.
  4. Ask what you can do to help and tune into that all day.
  5. Listen to solutions and uplifting material so the positive reprints.
  6. For a week, do something positive every day away from negative social media.

#Questions answered

Is the event worldwide or only for the US?
Worldwide, and no more will be given, to avoid fear. They plan "order through chaos"; in chaos, observe, pull back, analyze and use the knowledge. Stay on that and you ride the frequency of the positive reality that already pre-exists for you.
When will we master our reality? I find it hard to rewire childhood values.
You are on a timeline that pushes toward mastery, but they can't make the horse drink: your reaction decides your trajectory. Study how reality works and repeat it. Feeling different as a child was an activation that made you ask questions; what seemed negative was designed by you, your higher self and guides to push you toward ascension. The road is rocky, the summit air is crystal clear. Helping you is in everyone's interest, since we are all interconnected.
How can I prepare body, mind and frequencies for ascension?
The quantum manifestation protocols taught over the years are the specific knowledge, the alchemy of creation. Yoga or exercise that makes the body feel good is fine too, but the protocols are the key.
Any advice for connecting with my galactic family?
(A personal health reading for the asker is left out.) Practice automatic writing (it needs full trust) or, faster, lucid dreaming, where you can ask to see your galactic family; you'll learn whether direct contact is allowed. First you connect telepathically, then get personal instructions. Few psychics can give exact details, and some information is blocked; be careful when someone says you're connected to this or that. Become the conduit yourself: study manifestation and lucid dreaming, practice daily, without expectations. New information takes about 15 minutes to bridge into neurons; ask what you want to learn today and research it. Contact can be delayed by unhelpful choices. Direct channeling, aware with eyes open or closed, is the hardest way. Don't compare yourself with others.
How can I have stronger, clearer communication with my higher self?
Keep your body a clean frequency: detox, no drinking, no smoking. Intimacy merges two fields into a double structure, so for precise contact it should be with the right person through love, and at times you may pause for a week or two while detoxing. Not forever; your own resonance just needs to collect everything precisely.
