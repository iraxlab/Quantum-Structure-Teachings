The big idea

You are first light and frequency; geometry condenses into a body, with each organ holding its own geometric pattern and resonance (the heart's is the chestahedron, which opens a tunnel when spun). Each time you shift realities you carry and slightly change that pattern, and guides can locate and help you by it. To restore the original pattern, your own soul frequency in the DNA must become more potent than the old charge; then the angles of your organs' geometry realign, lock in like steel, and shifting to the positive timeline becomes easy. Cells need purity (clean water, living food) so frequency can pass through them.

#Key teachings

#51.1Light to body

Cymatics shape sound into geometry in the field; as density increases, geometry becomes a body with organs, brain, vessels and blood. Consciousness can locate you by your pulsation's sequences. In 3D the light dimmed and DNA partly shut down: not junk DNA, but unactivated DNA. Reactivating needs new patterns in your synapses and electrical charge, above the 3D structure.

#51.215 minutes and 21 days

She was told one concept crystallizes after about 15 minutes of nonstop repetition, while an action takes about 21 days to manifest permanently in your field (as with 21 days of exercise or fasting): cells, memory and synapses need that long to rebuild as a lasting mutation. See S6 47 (morphogenetics), S6 48 (the laboratory meditation) and S6 49 (diet and the etheric body).

#51.3Why purity matters

Every chemical puts a resonance like a shield around cells, so incoming frequency from the ether or your guides can't penetrate, and depleted cells lack life force. Fresh juice has more life force than powder. Pure, distilled-like water is a conductor that recognizes and flushes what isn't natural, carrying frequency cell to cell: the body becomes lighter. Living food is potent: sip new juices slowly (a large one drunk fast made her nauseous). Friends may leave as you change; true friends accept it, and new ones come.

Added safety note

Added safety note: she again told her own story of fasting on fruit and then water to treat a fatty liver and a gallbladder stone, calling it "a fiction story" and adding "I'm not telling you to do this." Don't try it: gallstones and liver disease need medical care, and water fasting can be dangerous. Any detox only under medical supervision with regular blood tests.

#Questions answered

What if you don't have an organ (no gallbladder)?
In the etheric body the remaining organs and DNA will mutate their geometry to hold the missing organ's pattern, "like you don't miss it".
Is there an alternative for people with health issues whose doctors say they can't fast?
The process will be slower, and must be fully under a doctor's supervision: fasting clinics exist where it's monitored with blood and organ checks. Diabetics especially must be careful. "Never do anything like this without a doctor's supervision."
Will this protocol eliminate parasites?
Some are hard to remove; different parasites have different resonances. Some skin and mouth organisms are necessary and protective. Signs of overgrowth: acidic or fungal-smelling breath, damp, sticky skin. Balance pH, detox properly, and look to herbalism (with professional guidance).
How often should we get blood work?
She had it every month to six weeks in the beginning, with an open-minded medical doctor and a nutritionist (including a thyroid antibody test); less often once in a pattern.
